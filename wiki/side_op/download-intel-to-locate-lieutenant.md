Download Intel To Locate Lieutenant is the Side Op at a Lieutenant Hunt tower: you climb the tall lattice tower, use the laptop or upload station once you reach the top, and the game then marks the general area of the nearest active Lieutenant [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. It is the free way to start a Lieutenant hunt ([[lieutenants]]); the paid way is Boss Board intel ([[paid-intel]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 every Side Op pays 2,000 DMZ Cash [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How do you find and use a tower?

Lieutenant Hunt towers are tall grey lattice masts with yellow ladders, marked with a skull; the short mast on a box is a UAV Tower instead ([[uav-towers]]) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]]. Ping Lieutenant Hunt on the map to find the nearest one [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported). The press counted five on the official map, but players have used towers that are not among those five [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] (reported).

1. Clear the ground around the base first: you cannot shoot while you are on the ladder [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
2. Climb to the platform and use the terminal [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
3. Read the marked area on the Tac-Map and decide whether to go after the Lieutenant [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

The intel only shows a rough location, not an exact one [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Lieutenant Hunts are optional; their rewards can include rare keys to special loot rooms [[src-gamespot-from-the-ward-ep7-2026-10-05]].

## Which Orders use it?

Hunter (Human Intel 1/2) wants exactly this: climb a tower and locate the nearest Lieutenant, no kill required ([[human-intel-orders]]) [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-game8-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]]. Triangulation (Underground 1/2) uses two of these towers for its transmitters [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] (reported). It also appeared on screen in the official Dynamic Ops trailer [[src-fpsjp-dynamic-ops-trailer-2026-10-07]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]], and CODMunity lists it among the three Side Ops it tracks [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]].
