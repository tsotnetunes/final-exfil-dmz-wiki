Clear the Military Compound is the Side Op at one of Hajin's fortified enemy compounds: kill the defenders until the clearing counter fills, and the Side Op completes [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]. Compounds hold better loot, intel and armoured vehicles such as the LTV [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 a Side Op pays 2,000 DMZ Cash; before that this one paid 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]].

## How do you clear one?

Compounds are lettered on the map; Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo and Foxtrot have all come up [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]. The Side Op tracks progress as a percentage [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

- Go in quietly if you can. If you open fire and do not take the compound quickly, more of its defenders join the fight at once [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- If the bar stops at about 90 percent, one defender is still alive; check cabins and behind containers [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- A solo player cleared Compound Delta in 12 kills [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported). Compound Alpha is High threat [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported).

The XP for a full clear was 225 in early access [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported).
