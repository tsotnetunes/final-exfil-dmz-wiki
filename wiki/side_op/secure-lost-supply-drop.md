Secure Lost Supply Drop is the Side Op for a supply crate that went astray in Hajin: find it by its red smoke, open it and keep what is inside [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-patchcrazy-crashed-crate-2026-10-22]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. Finding lost supply drops was one of Infinity Ward's first Side Op examples [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]], and the name was on screen in the official trailer [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 a finished Side Op pays 2,000 DMZ Cash [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How do you find one?

Look for red smoke rising; an alert also appears on the left of the screen when you are close, and the drop then shows on your minimap [[src-patchcrazy-crashed-crate-2026-10-22]] (reported). Drops land in odd places: one hung on the front of a hospital building and had to be knocked down with an explosive (a frag, C4 or a grenade launcher) before it could be opened [[src-patchcrazy-crashed-crate-2026-10-22]] (reported). Enemy reinforcements can turn up soon after you find one, so loot quickly [[src-patchcrazy-crashed-crate-2026-10-22]] (reported).

One can also fall during a Story Mission: in [[operation-critical-extraction]] a crate landed on red smoke in the hospital atrium while players held the lifts, and securing it gave 150 XP [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## Which Orders need it?

Crashed Crate on page 10 asks for three lost supply drops plus a Loot Radio Tower side op ([[dmz-orders-pages]]) [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]] [clm_a847abe87f0f]. Only the red-smoke drops count; crates from SAM site kills or finished Dynamic Ops do not [[src-patchcrazy-crashed-crate-2026-10-22]] (reported). Multi-Talented on page 19 asks you to open 12 supply drops of any kind [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## How is it different from other crates?

A Dynamic Op's Supply Drop is a reward that lands when you finish the Op and improves with each Op in the same deployment ([[dynamic-operations]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; a SAM site can bring down a cargo helicopter whose crate you can loot ([[capture-sam-site]]) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. A lost supply drop is already on the map, waiting to be found [[src-patchcrazy-crashed-crate-2026-10-22]] (reported).
