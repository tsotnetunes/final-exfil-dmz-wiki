Paid Intel is the Side Op you get when you buy intel on a Lieutenant at the FOB's [[boss-board]]: in your next deployment the Lieutenant is marked, and the Side Op asks you to reach them and then kill them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 a finished Side Op pays 2,000 DMZ Cash [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How does it work?

Lieutenant intel costs DMZ Cash, is unlocked by challenges in earlier deployments, and lasts for one deployment only [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. In the official trailer the Side Op read Paid Intel with two steps, reach Bale and kill Bale, and finishing it showed a 500 cash badge (before launch) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]] [[src-fpsjp-dynamic-ops-trailer-2026-10-07]]. CODMunity lists it as Paid Intel: Kill Bale [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]]. A Lieutenant kill pays DMZ XP and DMZ Cash and drops their Dog Tag Case; carrying the case marks you to other players, but extracting with it is what advances the Boss Board ([[lieutenants]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What are the free Kill side ops?

If you run into a Lieutenant without buying intel, a matching Kill side op can start on its own, for example Kill Roze in [[operation-grid-assault]], Kill the Doctor at [[mirae-general-hospital]] and Kill Chemist in [[operation-fallen-ember]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. Early-access extraction summaries paid 500 for such a side op plus 10,000 for a First Lieutenant Takedown [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## Which Orders need it?

Information Power on page 11 asks you to buy Lieutenant intel at the Boss Board and kill a Lieutenant ([[dmz-orders-pages]]) [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. Keep Out, How Bulletproof? and Unstoppable later ask for three, five and fifteen Lieutenant kills [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].
