## What does a safe look like and how do you open it?

A safe is a green metal box with a red electronic lock panel. Hold interact until the ring fills; the panel turns blue and the door swings open [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported). Contents seen include intel documents such as Nuclear Program Files and Ballistic Flight Logs, valuables and cash [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported). One early-access crack paid 225 XP [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] (reported).

## Where are safes?

Often inside compounds and locked rooms. Spots players have used: the watchtower office at Compound Bravo, the office building at Compound Charlie, a tent at Compound Delta and an office at Compound Foxtrot ([[clear-the-military-compound]]); the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] has a Crack Safe side op of its own [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).
