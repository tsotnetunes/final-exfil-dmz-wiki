Repair the Vehicle is the Side Op at a broken-down radar truck: you fill its empty repair slots with vehicle parts, and once it is fixed the truck's radar marks enemies around you while you drive it [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]]. Truck repairs were among Infinity Ward's first Side Op examples [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 a Side Op pays 2,000 DMZ Cash [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What parts do you need?

Four: a Car Battery, an Alternator, a Spark Plug and a Vehicle Water Pump [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported). The truck's repair counter has four slots [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]]. Search Gas Stations and auto repair shops; a station can hold all four, but none is guaranteed [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]].

## How do you repair one?

1. Find a damaged radar truck on the Tac-Map; some sit beside Gas Stations [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]].
2. Go to its repair point at the front; the screen shows which parts are missing [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]].
3. Fit every missing part. A half-repaired truck does not count [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).

Parts you extract stay in your Stash for a later deployment, so collect spares [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported). The official vehicle list includes the D50 Radar Flatbed, a truck with a mounted UAV ([[vehicles]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; whether the repairable trucks are that vehicle is not confirmed.
