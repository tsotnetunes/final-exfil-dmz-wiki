Capture SAM Site is the Side Op at an enemy surface-to-air missile site: you hack the launcher's control computer and hold the site until the capture bar fills, and the launcher then fires on enemy aircraft by itself while you stay near [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. It is the easiest answer to a Deathstalker [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 a Side Op pays 2,000 DMZ Cash [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How do you capture one?

1. Clear the soldiers around the launcher; anyone left alive can push you out of the zone [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]].
2. Use the laptop or computer on the launcher. A siren or alarm sounds, and trucks can bring reinforcements [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]].
3. Stay inside the capture area until the bar fills, a little over 30 seconds. Leaving pauses it; come back to resume [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]].
4. The completion banner appears and the launcher starts firing [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]].

If you wander too far off, enemies can retake the site [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]]. The Survivalist Trait Air Superiority keeps SAM Sites captured longer ([[air-superiority]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_c3633bb72757].

## What uses a SAM site?

- Orders: Eye in the Sky (bring down a cargo helicopter and loot its crate), Multi-Talented (capture six) and Stalking the Stalker (down three Deathstalkers with one) [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]]. For Eye in the Sky only a cargo helicopter counts, and its crate can land far from the wreck [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]].
- Dynamic Ops: [[ghost-shipment]] and some routes of [[sky-heist]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]].
- Story Missions: [[operation-iron-citadel]] ends with a SAM capture [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]].

The XP seen for a capture was 150 in early access [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
