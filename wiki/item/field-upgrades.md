A Field Upgrade is the deployable gadget in your loadout's Field Upgrade slot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. DMZ launched with 16, all printable at the [[3d-printer]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]; Season 1 added a seventeenth, the Hazmat Box (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-08]], and on 8 Nov 2026 the same database listed three more Season 1 boxes, the Consumable Box, Medical Box and Utility Box, bringing the count to 20 (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-09]]. In Multiplayer every Field Upgrade recharges [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]; in DMZ one guide says they do not recharge during a deployment, so each is one use per match (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-armor-backpacks-guide-2026-10-24]].

## Which ones matter most?

- **Armor Box**: apart from Traits, it is the only way to start a fight with more plates than your vest gave you at infil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[armor-vests]].
- **Rebreather**: one database gives its recipe as 15 Synthetics and 15 Chemicals (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]; there is loot underwater, such as the Reactor DRC Locker in the flooded pool under Reactor 02 [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]; whether you need a Rebreather to reach it is not confirmed (see [[keys]]).
- **Fulton Drop Marker**: worth 2,500 on its card, the most valuable launch Field Upgrade with the Revive Pistol; of the Season 1 boxes, the Utility Box (3,000) is listed higher and the Medical Box level with it (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- The Trophy System's card value is 900 [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]]; most others are 100 to 300 [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
