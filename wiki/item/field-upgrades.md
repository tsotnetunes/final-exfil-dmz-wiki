A Field Upgrade is the deployable gadget in your loadout's Field Upgrade slot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. DMZ has 16, all printable at the [[3d-printer]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. In Multiplayer every Field Upgrade recharges [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-equipment-2026-10-19]]; in DMZ one guide says they do not recharge during a deployment, so each is one use per match (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-armor-backpacks-guide-2026-10-24]].

## What does each Field Upgrade do?

| Field Upgrade | Rarity | What it does | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ammo Box | Uncommon | refills ammunition and equipment for you and your squad | recipe at DMZ level 12; Restock Kit |
| Armor Box | Rare | gives you and your team armour plates | recipe at level 14; Basic Armor Kit, Restock Kit |
| Portable Radar | Uncommon | regular pings show enemies nearby | printable |
| Seeker Drone | Uncommon | hunts nearby enemies and snapshots them | Recon Equipment Kit |
| Inflatable Decoy | Uncommon | a dummy pops up when enemies come close, or on command | recipe at level 52 |
| Razor Wire Trap | Uncommon | snares enemies in razor wire | Trap Equipment Kit |
| Riot Shield | Uncommon | a shield you plant for cover; it can be destroyed | printable |
| Smoke Wall | Uncommon | a fast rocket that leaves a long wall of smoke | printable |
| Rebreather | Uncommon | lets you breathe underwater | printable |
| DDOS | Rare | briefly jams electronics and enemy sensors nearby | printable |
| Recon Drone | Rare | remote drone that marks targets by hand or by itself | Recon Equipment Kit |
| SAM Airburst Turret | Rare | portable turret that shoots down air threats | printable |
| Trophy System | Rare | destroys up to three incoming projectiles | printable |
| Defibrillator | Uncommon (official) | revives a downed Operator at once | DMZ level 67; see [[medical-items]] |
| Revive Pistol | Legendary | revives or heals teammates from a distance | DMZ level 64 |
| Fulton Drop Marker | Ultra | marks a spot and calls in a Fulton extraction | Vault Edition kit; printable; see [[fulton-drop-marker]] |

The Restock Kit in the official table holds a Munitions Box, the Multiplayer name for an ammo and equipment box; players who opened one found an Ammo Box [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-allthings-how-large-backpack-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].

## Which ones matter most?

- **Armor Box**: apart from Traits, it is the only way to start a fight with more plates than your vest gave you at infil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[armor-vests]].
- **Rebreather**: one database gives its recipe as 15 Synthetics and 15 Chemicals (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]; there is loot underwater, such as the Reactor DRC Locker in the flooded pool under Reactor 02 [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]; whether you need a Rebreather to reach it is not confirmed (see [[keys]]).
- **Fulton Drop Marker**: worth 2,500 on its card, the most valuable Field Upgrade with the Revive Pistol [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- The Trophy System's card value is 900 [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]]; most others are 100 to 300 [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
