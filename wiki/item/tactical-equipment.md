Tacticals are the non-lethal throwables and gadgets that go in your loadout's Tactical slot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. DMZ has ten [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]; the official launch list describes the same equipment for Multiplayer and says DMZ shares many of its items [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Your equipped Tacticals are topped up when you deploy (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## What does each Tactical do?

| Tactical | Rarity | What it does | Recipe or other source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Smoke Grenade | Common | smoke screen that also blocks automatic targeting | recipe at DMZ level 4 |
| Concussion Grenade | Common | slows the target's reactions and blurs their vision | recipe at level 4 |
| Snapshot Grenade | Common | briefly shows enemies near where it lands | recipe at level 9; Recon Equipment Kit |
| Flash Grenade | Common | blinds and deafens | printable |
| Decoy Grenade | Common | fakes gunfire, footsteps and radar blips | printable |
| Screamer Mine | Common | proximity alarm that suppresses and marks enemies close to it | printable |
| Shock Stick | Common | sticks to surfaces, shocks enemies, wrecks equipment, disrupts vehicles | recipe at level 17 |
| Heartbeat Sensor | Uncommon | tablet with limited use that shows roughly where nearby enemies are | Recon Equipment Kit |
| Stim | Uncommon | starts health regeneration, refreshes Tactical Sprint, eases flash and stun | printable; see [[medical-items]] |
| Spotter Scope | Rare | scope for marking enemy Operators and threats | recipe at level 12 |
