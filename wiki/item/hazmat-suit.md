The Hazmat Suit is a Legendary piece of gear that protects you from radiation, and you lose it when you exfil [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [clm_f28fa8554173]. Players report that wearing it lets you explore the irradiated area of the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] without taking damage [clm_f655e133d8dd]; the official guide rates that reactor High with radiation [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Where do you find a Hazmat Suit?

It cannot be printed [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. A community database says it comes from crashed helicopters that carry soldiers in chemical suits: kill the soldiers, the helicopter goes down, and the suit is often in the wreck (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. Other spawn spots are not confirmed yet.

## Do you keep it after exfil?

No. The suit is discarded when you extract, so it never reaches your [[stash]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [clm_f28fa8554173]. Use it in the deployment where you find it, for example to loot the reactor's irradiated rooms.

## How does it compare with a gas mask?

Both protect against radiation; gas masks wear out in strong radiation, but you can print them and keep them in your Stash between deployments [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] (see [[gas-masks]]). Whether the suit wears out, and whether you still need a gas mask with it, is not confirmed yet. Radiation Blockers, a consumable, weaken radiation effects for a minute (see [[medical-items]]) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. For the storm that spreads over the map, see [[radiation-storm]].
