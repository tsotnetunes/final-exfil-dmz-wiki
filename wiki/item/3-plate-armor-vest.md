The 3-Plate Armor Vest is the standard plate carrier for three armour plates, and the surest way to get one is to reach DMZ level 37, where the progression track hands you one [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. It has no special effect beyond the third plate; the Ballistic Gel and Helmet versions add one (see [[armor-vests]]).

## How do you get a 3-plate vest?

- **DMZ level 37.** The official reward table lists it as an Epic consumable at that level [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- **3D Printer.** It is printable once you hold the recipe [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]; one community database gives the inputs as 2 Ballistic Fabric, 5 Nylon Cloth and 10 Reactive Metals (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. See [[3d-printer]].
- **Loot.** A guide's run of the Reactor DRC Locker, an Ultra key room under Reactor 02, gave a 3-plate vest as its Ultra item (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]; see [[keys]].

## What happened to the level 37 reward?

At launch the level 37 reward did not arrive. The developers fixed it on 28 Oct 2026, but the fix only worked for players who had not reached level 37 yet; on 29 Oct they said they were working on granting the vest to everyone who had passed the level earlier [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Whether those players have received it is not confirmed yet.

## How rare and how valuable is it?

The official table calls it Epic, and codmunity.gg agrees; dmzhub.gg, wzstats.gg and dmzcodex.com list it as Legendary [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzcodex-armor-backpacks-guide-2026-10-24]]. Its loot card value is 5,000 on two databases and 6,000 on a third, which ranks it among the best items to carry [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]].
