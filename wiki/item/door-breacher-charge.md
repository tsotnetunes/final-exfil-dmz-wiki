The Door Breacher Charge is a single-use consumable that blows open a locked door, so you can enter a key room without its key: stand at the door and hold the interact button [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]] [[src-gfinity-door-breacher-2026-10-27]]. It is an Ultra item, the top rarity [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]].

## How do you get a Door Breacher Charge?

- **DMZ level 62**: the official reward table gives one Door Breacher Charge [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- **The 3D Printer**, in the Consumables category, once you have found the recipe in Hajin: 1 Experimental Compounds, 5 Stock Rods and 3 Volatile Explosives [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. See [[3d-printer]].
- **The Vendor**, now and then: it turns up in the daily stock [[src-gfinity-door-breacher-2026-10-27]], and players have seen it for 50,000 DMZ Cash [clm_13dd447ee402]; one guide says it can also turn up in loot [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]]. See [[weapon-vendor]].

## Is it worth using instead of a key?

A key opens one room and is then used up, and keys sell for only about 100 DMZ Cash [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]. The charge is also used up, but it does not care which door it is, which makes it the way into rooms whose key you do not have. Its card value is 600 [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]], while one guide's runs of a Legendary room at the prison turned up a Gold Skull and an item worth 10,000 [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]. The rooms and their keys are listed on [[keys]].

## Which doors can it not open?

Keycard doors, gun lockers and the Supreme Leader's Bunker under [[chang-san-air-base]], according to one guide (reported) [[src-gfinity-door-breacher-2026-10-27]]. For those you still need the card or key: the bunker's Access Card comes from [[operation-iron-citadel]] ([[keys-west-and-north]]). A Brazilian guide says the charge works on any locked door on the map [[src-criticalhits-dmz-keys-2026-10-26]], so the exceptions are not settled; bring the key or card for those doors when you can.
