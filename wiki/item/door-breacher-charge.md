The Door Breacher Charge is a single-use consumable that blows open a locked door, so you can enter a key room without its key [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]]. A Brazilian guide says it works on any locked door on the map [[src-criticalhits-dmz-keys-2026-10-26]]. It is an Ultra item, the top rarity [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]].

## How do you get a Door Breacher Charge?

- **DMZ level 62**: the official reward table gives one Door Breacher Charge [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- **The 3D Printer**, in the Consumables category, once you have found the recipe in Hajin: 1 Experimental Compounds, 5 Stock Rods and 3 Volatile Explosives [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. See [[3d-printer]].
- **The Vendor**, now and then: players have seen it for 50,000 DMZ Cash [clm_13dd447ee402], and one guide says it can also turn up in loot [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]]. See [[weapon-vendor]].

## Is it worth using instead of a key?

A key opens one room and is then used up, and keys sell for only about 100 DMZ Cash [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]. The charge is also used up, but it does not care which door it is, which makes it the way into rooms whose key you do not have. Its card value is 600 [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]], while one guide's runs of a Legendary room at the prison turned up a Gold Skull and an item worth 10,000 [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]. Which doors it cannot open, if any, is not confirmed yet. The rooms and their keys are listed on [[keys]].
