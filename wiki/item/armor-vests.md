An armor vest (the game files them under Plate Carriers) sets how many armour plates you wear in DMZ: one, two or three. There are eight, from the Uncommon 1-Plate Armor Vest to the Ultra Tempered Armor Vest [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [clm_bc3d420f777c]. The vest has its own Plate Carrier slot in your loadout, and your plates fill the separate Armor slot when you deploy [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do armour plates work?

- You deploy with your plates filled; more than the vest holds at the start comes only from certain Traits or the Armor Box Field Upgrade, which drops plates for the squad [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-dmzcodex-armor-backpacks-guide-2026-10-24]].
- Plates do not grow back. You put new ones in by hand, except with a Ballistic Gel vest, which refills on its own [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-dmzcodex-armor-backpacks-guide-2026-10-24]]. How fast the gel refills has not been measured (reported).
- Spare plates come from the Ammo & Armor Caches marked on the [[tac-map]], which go on cooldown after use [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]], and from loot such as military bags in armories [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]. An Armor Plate is a Common item that stacks to six (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- There is no separate helmet item; the Helmet vests are the headshot protection [[src-dmzcodex-armor-backpacks-guide-2026-10-24]]. Since the 30 Oct 2026 fix, hitting a helmet without breaking it shows a blue armour marker instead of a white one [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- Weapon damage in DMZ is tuned with its armour and plate carriers in mind, separately from Multiplayer [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

An Operator killed in a deployment comes back from MIA without their gear, vest included [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], so keep a spare in the [[stash]]; see [[what-carries-over]].
