Valuables are loot whose only job is money or upgrades: you extract them and sell them at the FOB for DMZ Cash [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthings-how-solo-money-2026-10-21]]. Most of them are intel documents; the top two are the Gold Bar and the Gold Skull at 25,000 each [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. Before you sell, check whether a FOB upgrade needs them [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## What should you sell and what should you keep?

- Keep enough of each document for the upgrades you still want, above all the Bank: from Bank III to X every level asks for valuables, stepping up from Uncommon notes to ten Gold Bars at level X (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]]. Some orange and purple documents are needed this way, so items marked with a cash or diamond icon deserve a second look [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- Raise your Bank cap before big sales: cash sold past the cap is lost without a warning (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. See [[wallet]].
- Carry your best valuables in a Secure Slot, so they survive a failed exfil [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[backpacks]].
- There are no barter items in MW4: the 3D Printer replaced the barter recipes of the 2022 DMZ [[src-codmunity-dmz-whats-new-2026-10-05]].

## Where do valuables come from?

Safes in compounds and key rooms hold them [[src-keengamer-best-loot-2026-10-22]], and bunkers hold sellable files that upgrades need [clm_c712a62e76ae]. Guides found Nuclear Program Files, Encrypted Archives and Gold Skulls in the Counting Room Vault, and WMD valuables in the Supreme Leader's bunker [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-keys-2026-10-27]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]; Regional Combat Notes turn up in civilian and military areas alike [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. Operation Silent Ledger pays a Gold Bar [[src-gfinity-story-mission-rewards-2026-10-23]]. Rooms and keys are on [[keys]].

## Do Dog Tags count?

Yes. Lieutenant Dog Tags are Legendary, there are eight (Bale, Gorani, Iskra, Jin-Tae, Roze, the Scavenger, The Chemist, The Doctor), and one guide was paid 25,000 into the Bank for extracting one [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthings-how-solo-money-2026-10-21]]. The Order Unstoppable asks for five of them (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. See [[legendary-dog-tag]] and [[dog-tags]].
