Night Vision Goggles (NVGs) let you see in the dark parts of Hajin. DMZ has no night mode or day-night cycle at launch, so the developers put NVGs in the loot pool for darker areas instead [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. You need them in the unlit underground shopping level below [[nurigo-mall]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## How do you get NVGs?

- **Operation Shopping Hacks**, a Story Mission, gives Night Vision Goggles among its rewards [[src-gfinity-story-mission-rewards-2026-10-23]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]; players also report it unlocks their printer recipe [clm_3086f62d37c7]. See [[operation-shopping-hacks]].
- **The 3D Printer** makes all three once you hold the recipe; NVGs are in its Gear category [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. One database lists the inputs: Weak, 25 Metals and 5 Control Units; standard, 50 Metals, 3 Printed Circuit Boards and 10 Control Units; Elite, 2 Power Regulators, 3 CPUs and 5 Electronic Sensors (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. See [[3d-printer]].
- **Loot**: they are part of the loot pool in Hajin [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

Season 1 (6 Nov 2026) corrects the unlock instructions the printer showed for the standard recipe, and lets you sell both the Weak and the standard pair from your Stash, which failed before [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

How long the goggles last, or whether they use a battery, is not confirmed yet.
