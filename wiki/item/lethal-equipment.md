Lethals are the explosives, traps and throwing weapons that go in your loadout's Lethal slot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. DMZ has eleven, all printable at the [[3d-printer]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]; the official launch list describes the same equipment for Multiplayer [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-equipment-2026-10-19]].

## What does each Lethal do?

| Lethal | Rarity | What it does | Recipe or other source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frag Grenade | Common | shrapnel grenade you can cook | recipe at DMZ level 7; Explosives Equipment Kit |
| Semtex (Sticky Grenade) | Common | sticks where it lands, goes off on a timer | recipe at level 23; Explosives Equipment Kit |
| Throwing Knife | Uncommon | kills on a hit to an unarmoured target or the head; pick it up again | recipe at level 7 |
| Drill Charge | Uncommon | a thermal lance bores into a surface, then explodes | printable |
| Molotov Cocktail | Common | bursts on impact into burning pools | recipe at level 36 |
| Thermite | Common | sticky incendiary, heavy damage to vehicles and armour | recipe at level 41 |
| C4 | Common | sticks to surfaces, set off remotely | Explosives Equipment Kit |
| Proximity Mine | Common | jumps and explodes when enemies come close; big vehicle damage | Explosives and Trap Equipment Kits |
| Claymore | Common | mine set off by proximity | Trap Equipment Kit |
| Trip Wire | Uncommon | wire trap; several can be chained | Trap Equipment Kit |
| Zip Gun | Common | one-shot printed pistol for close range | printable |

## What changed, and what are they good for?

- **Drill Charge vs the Colossus**: on 25 Oct 2026 the developers cut the Drill Charge's damage against the Colossus [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Players report killing it solo with seven or eight Thermite and two Shock Sticks [clm_d12acdac5305]. See [[commander-threats]].
- **Zip Gun recipe**: guides found it in the Counting Room Vault at the casino [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-keys-2026-10-27]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-keys-2026-10-22]]; see [[keys]].
- **Refills**: your equipped Lethals are topped up when you deploy (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]; the Trait Equipment Professional recharges them slowly [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and Auto Fill refills them [clm_6aac9d9c7dc0] (see [[auto-fill]]).
- On loot cards Lethals are worth 100 to 250 DMZ Cash [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]].

Tacticals are on [[tactical-equipment]].
