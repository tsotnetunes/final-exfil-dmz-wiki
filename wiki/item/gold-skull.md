The Gold Skull is an Ultra valuable, the top rarity, with a card value of 25,000 DMZ Cash when you extract it and sell it at the FOB [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. Like the [[gold-bar]], it is also an upgrade material, so think before you sell it.

## What is the Gold Skull used for?

Players report that end-game FOB upgrades need five Gold Skulls [clm_b90e9b9a3636]. A community database names the upgrade: Stash Item Upgrade IV, which adds 18 item slots to your Stash and also takes 30 Alloyed Plating, 10 Experimental Compounds and 30 Turned Components (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]]. No other upgrade on that list uses Gold Skulls, so five is the number to keep. See [[stash]].

## Where do you find Gold Skulls?

In high-tier key rooms. Guides found them in the Counting Room Vault on the main floor of the Heavenly Luck Casino, and in the South Block Armory deep inside The Citadel at the 14th Political Prison [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-keys-2026-10-27]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]. Both rooms need a key; see [[keys]]. Loot changes between deployments and no place guarantees a given rare item [[src-keengamer-best-loot-2026-10-22]], so a skull is never certain.

## How do you keep one safe?

Carry it out in a Secure Slot: a guide's rule of thumb is that every red (Ultra) item belongs there, because a failed exfil costs you the rest of your backpack [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. All other valuables are compared on [[valuables]].
