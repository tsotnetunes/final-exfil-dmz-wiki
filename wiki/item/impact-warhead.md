The Impact Warhead is one of the ten Fire Support items in DMZ, the Killstreak-style calls you carry in your loadout's Fire Support slot instead of earning with kills [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. It is rated Legendary [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].

## What does it do?
It drops a payload that bores down through several floors of a building before it goes off [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. That makes it the Fire Support item for squads holed up inside a building rather than out in the open. Damage figures and the number of floors it passes through are not published.

## How do you get one?
Print it at the [[3d-printer]]. The recipe is not a level reward: you find it as a recipe card in Hajin, extract it, and then each print costs 2 Turned Components, 3 Nitro Compounds and 5 Detonators [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. Turned Components and Detonators are printable ingredients themselves ([[turned-components]], [[detonators]]); Nitro Compounds are on [[materials]].

## How does it compare?
Of the other Legendary calls, the Artillery Beacon sends homing missiles, the Advanced UAV shows enemies and the Railgun Sentry guards a spot; the table is on [[fire-support]]. All recipes: [[recipes]].
