A gas mask protects you from radiation, both the storm that spreads across Hajin during a deployment and the permanent radiation at the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. DMZ has two: the Rare Gas Mask and the Epic Durable Gas Mask [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. Your squad panel shows whether each teammate has one on [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Do gas masks still break in radiation?

No longer instantly. At launch gas masks and parachutes had the wrong number of uses; the fix of 23 Oct 2026 stopped gas masks, the Durable one included, from breaking the moment you walk into a radiation zone [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. They still wear out: the reactor's red zone uses them up fast, so carry a spare (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. One database gives each mask a fixed time: the Gas Mask protects for 160 seconds in ordinary radiation and 80 seconds in the reactor's critical zone, the Durable Gas Mask for 240 and 160 seconds (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-24]].

One database lists the Gas Mask at 30 Chemicals and 3 Rigid Synthetics, and the Durable Gas Mask at 30 Chemicals, 1 High-Tenacity Fibers and 5 Biomaterials (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. Fire Stations, the red buildings on the map, stock radiation gear [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-keengamer-best-loot-2026-10-22]]. The same database adds industrial lockers for both masks, Fire Station storage rooms for the Durable one, and drops from enemy soldiers in hazmat suits, more often inside the reactor (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-24]].

## What else protects you from radiation?

- **Radiation Gear**: codmunity.gg lists it as a separate Gear item found in Fire Stations that, worn with a Gas Mask, gets you into the reactor (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]; other databases do not list it.
- **[[hazmat-suit]]**: a Legendary suit against radiation that you lose when you exfil [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- **Radiation Blockers**: a Rare consumable that weakens radiation effects for a minute (see [[medical-items]]) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

Since the evening of 25 Nov 2026 a Gas Mask stays on when you switch Night Vision Goggles on or off; before that fix, toggling NVGs could make the mask disappear [[src-infinityward-x-thanksgiving-fixes-2026-11-25]] ([[night-vision-goggles]]).

"Purple gas mask" is not a separate item: neither item database lists one [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. For the storm itself, see [[radiation-storm]].
