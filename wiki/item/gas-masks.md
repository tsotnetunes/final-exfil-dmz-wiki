A gas mask protects you from radiation, both the storm that spreads across Hajin during a deployment and the permanent radiation at the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. DMZ has two: the Rare Gas Mask and the Epic Durable Gas Mask [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. Your squad panel shows whether each teammate has one on [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Do gas masks still break in radiation?

No longer instantly. At launch gas masks and parachutes had the wrong number of uses; the fix of 23 Oct 2026 stopped gas masks, the Durable one included, from breaking the moment you walk into a radiation zone [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. They still wear out: the reactor's red zone uses them up fast, so carry a spare (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. How long each mask lasts is not published.

One database lists the Gas Mask at 30 Chemicals and 3 Rigid Synthetics, and the Durable Gas Mask at 30 Chemicals, 1 High-Tenacity Fibers and 5 Biomaterials (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. Fire Stations, the red buildings on the map, stock radiation gear [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-keengamer-best-loot-2026-10-22]].

## What else protects you from radiation?

- **Radiation Gear**: codmunity.gg lists it as a separate Gear item found in Fire Stations that, worn with a Gas Mask, gets you into the reactor (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]; other databases do not list it.
- **[[hazmat-suit]]**: a Legendary suit against radiation that you lose when you exfil [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- **Radiation Blockers**: a Rare consumable that weakens radiation effects for a minute (see [[medical-items]]) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

"Purple gas mask" is not a separate item: neither item database lists one [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. For the storm itself, see [[radiation-storm]].
