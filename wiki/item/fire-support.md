Fire Support is DMZ's name for Killstreak-style items: instead of earning them with kills, you carry one in the Fire Support slot of your loadout and call it in when you need it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. There are ten, all printable once you hold the recipe [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].

## What does each Fire Support item do?

| Item | Rarity | What it does | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UAV | Epic | shows enemies on your minimap; since 10 Nov 2026 for a much shorter time over a much smaller area, and not at all in [[x01]] [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] | DMZ level 13 and 65 rewards; recipe at level 31 |
| Care Package | Rare | a supply drop | recipe at level 28; level 58 reward |
| Combat Drone | Epic | remote-controlled drone that attacks people | DMZ level 59 reward |
| Bomb Glider | Epic | hand-launched glider carrying explosives; since 6 Nov 2026 its target markers reach less far, so rivals are not highlighted from so far away [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] | printable |
| Bombing Run | Epic | bombs a spot you choose | printable |
| Advanced UAV | Legendary | orbital UAV that cannot be shot down and shows which way enemies face, live; since 10 Nov 2026 shorter and smaller in reach, and not at all in [[x01]] [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] | printable |
| Artillery Beacon | Legendary | heat-seeking missiles home in on a beacon you throw | printable |
| Impact Warhead | Legendary | payload that drills down through several floors | printable |
| Railgun Sentry | Legendary | deployable railgun that fires on its own | printable |
| Crossfire | Ultra | two airstrikes from opposite directions; since 6 Nov 2026 stronger against vehicles, tanks most of all, and strikes land where you aim [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] | DMZ level 66 reward |

## What else should you know?

- **Killed Operators drop theirs.** Until a fix on 23 Oct 2026, eliminated Operators sometimes dropped extra Killstreaks; the same day a bug carrying Multiplayer Killstreak indicators into DMZ was fixed [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- **No UAVs in X01.** On 10 Nov 2026 Infinity Ward first cut the duration and range of UAVs and Advanced UAVs a lot, then the same day disabled both in [[x01]], so they cannot be called in there; elsewhere in Hajin they still work at the reduced strength. No figures were given [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- **UAV Traits.** The Scout Trait UAV Upgrade gives your UAVs more range and duration and turns them into Advanced UAVs; Thermal Padding hides you from enemy UAVs [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The UAV Towers on the map are a different thing: see [[uav-towers]].
- **Value.** UAV cards show 1,000 to 2,500, so spare Fire Support sells well compared with grenades [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]]. Sources differ on the Bombing Run: one list gave 4,500 in October, while DMZHub's card has shown about 1,100 since 4 Nov 2026 (reported) [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-05]].
- **Looting a Bombing Run.** Besides printing, it is reported in weapon lockers and crates, weapon backpacks, supply drops, safes, ordinary lockers and some boss drops (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-05]].
- Multiplayer-only streaks such as the Counter UAV, Wheelson, Gunship and Argonaut do not appear in the DMZ item databases [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
