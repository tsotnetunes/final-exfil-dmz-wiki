The Tempered Armor Vest is the top plate carrier in DMZ: an Ultra vest built heavy, using enhanced plates [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzcodex-armor-backpacks-guide-2026-10-24]]. It sits at the top of the eight vests in the Plate Carriers tab [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [clm_bc3d420f777c], and players have found it in Hajin as loot [clm_7182ba17a6b0]. How many plates it holds and how much tougher its plates are is not confirmed yet.

## How do you get the Tempered Armor Vest?

Print it at the [[3d-printer]] once you own the recipe. The recipe takes 2 Military Composites, 4 Ballistic Fabric and 10 Rigid Synthetics [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. Military Composites are a Legendary ingredient that a community database says the Colossus drops (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]; killing a Commander pays prized printer ingredients [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Where is the recipe?

Guides found it in the Lieutenant crate inside The Doctor's Command Office, an upper-floor room of [[mirae-general-hospital]] (grid F5) [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-keys-2026-10-27]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-keys-2026-10-22]]. The room's key is Legendary and drops from The Doctor, the Lieutenant who holds the hospital [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-keys-2026-10-27]] [[src-criticalhits-dmz-keys-2026-10-26]]. One site says the recipe turned up there once and is not a guaranteed drop [[src-dmzcodex-armor-backpacks-guide-2026-10-24]]. A looted recipe is used up at exfil, added to your printer and pays 5,000 DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[keys]] for the room.
