Dog Tags are collectible items tied to player bounties and base progression in DMZ. Players can store and manage them through the Dog Tag Case station located in the FOB.

* Extracting with the Dog Tags of Wanted players rewards DMZ Cash.
* The Dog Tag Case station is located in the FOB.
* Players reported that the Dog Tag Case unlocks at DMZ Level 20.
* On 21 October 2026, an error when opening the Dog Tag Case station in the FOB was confirmed to make it inaccessible for some players.
* Later on 21 October 2026, it was confirmed that players can access the Dog Tag Case in the FOB, superseding the earlier issue, with players reporting that the case in DMZ was fixed in the latest update to Modern Warfare 4.

Collect from Lieutenants or rival Operators; exfilling with them pays but makes you trackable [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. Every Operator wears their own Dog Tag, which rises from Steel I to Onyx III as they earn DMZ XP (one Trait Point per level, [[trait-points]]) and shows their Bounty and Operator kill count; another player who kills you can take it, and a recovered MIA Operator gets theirs back [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Extracting a Wanted player's tag pays their Bounty ([[bounty-system]]).

A Lieutenant drops a **Dog Tag Case**: after the kill the tag shows on every Operator's Tac-Map, picking the Case up also broadcasts your position, and extracting with it advances the [[boss-board]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Tags you took from other players are shown at the FOB's [[dog-tag-case]] station.
