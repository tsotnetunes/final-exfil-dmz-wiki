A Dog Tag is two things in DMZ: the tag every Operator wears, which levels up and carries their Bounty, and the tags you take from the Operators and Lieutenants you kill [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## What is your Operator's Dog Tag?

Each Active Duty Operator has their own tag with 18 levels in six tiers (Steel, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Damascus, Onyx, each I to III); every level gives one Trait Point ([[trait-points]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. It rises with the DMZ XP you earn with that Operator [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-level-up-fast-2026-10-21]]; XP per level is on [[dmz-progression]]. The tag also shows the Operator's Bounty and how many Operators they have killed, and another player who kills you can take it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What do you get for extracting someone's tag?

- **Their Bounty.** A Wanted player's tag pays DMZ Cash once you extract with it [clm_1dda32204130] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; the kill alone pays nothing [[src-cod-dmz-bounty-systems-video-2026-10-08]]. A tag can carry a Bounty of 0 (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- **XP.** About 1,000 DMZ XP per extracted tag, Operator or Lieutenant (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-xp-strategies-2026-10-30]].
- **A trophy.** Tags you extract go to the FOB's [[dog-tag-case]] from DMZ level 20 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_f8c43f6e8da8].

The victim loses their Bounty when you extract their tag [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Carrying tags out makes you trackable, so plan a quick exfil [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. A Steel Dog Tag, the Common kind, records the victim's Bounty, killer and time of death (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]].

## What do Lieutenants drop?

Every Lieutenant drops a Lieutenant Dog Tag Case [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_8954c5cec1aa]. Picking the Case up marks you on every Operator's Tac-Map. Leaving it still pays the kill's XP and cash, but only extracting with the Case, from your own kill or taken from another player, advances the [[boss-board]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Boss tags are Legendary ([[legendary-dog-tag]]) (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. Extracting two Lieutenant tags in one deployment, 50 times, is the Ripple Tide camo challenge ([[dmz-camos]]) [[src-callofduty-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
