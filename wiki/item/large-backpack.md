The Large Backpack is the biggest plain backpack in DMZ: it has two Secure Slots and room for one extra weapon [[src-dmzcodex-backpacks-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzradar-backpacks-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. Anything in those two slots comes home even if you die; the rest of the backpack does not [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-xboxplay-large-backpack-2026-10-23]].

## How do you get the Large Backpack?

- **DMZ level 34.** The official reward table gives one at that level [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- **The FOB Vendor**, when it is in the rotation. Prices seen differ: two guides paid 50,000 DMZ Cash under the Gear tab, while a Windows Central writer bought one for 10,000 [[src-xboxplay-large-backpack-2026-10-23]] [[src-allthings-how-large-backpack-2026-10-21]] [[src-windowscentral-dmz-backpacks-2026-10-22]]. The Vendor's stock refreshes regularly, so check it often [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **The 3D Printer**, once you hold the recipe; one database lists 2 Nylon Laminate, 5 Structural Alloy and 10 Rigid Synthetics (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]].
- Loot it from players you kill, like any backpack [[src-dmzcodex-backpacks-2026-10-31]].

## How rare and how valuable is it?

The official table calls it Epic, as does codmunity.gg; most other community sites list it as Legendary [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-xboxplay-large-backpack-2026-10-23]]. Its card value is 8,000 on two databases, while two guides say it sells for 5,000 from the Stash [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]] [[src-xboxplay-large-backpack-2026-10-23]] [[src-allthings-how-large-backpack-2026-10-21]]. Either way, selling one returns less than any Vendor price seen.

## How much does it hold?

Not confirmed. Its card shows a capacity of 200 against 50 for the starting pack, but no source says what that figure counts [[src-xboxplay-large-backpack-2026-10-23]]. For more Secure Slots, look at the Secure family on [[backpacks]].
