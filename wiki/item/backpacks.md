Your backpack decides how much loot you can carry out of Hajin and how much of it survives if you die: items in its Secure Slots are kept after a failed exfil, the rest is lost [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-dmzradar-backpacks-2026-10-20]]. Without one you carry the Basic Pack with 8 slots [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. DMZ has ten real backpacks above it, in four families: plain, Weapon (an extra weapon slot), Scavenger (more room for resources) and Secure (more Secure Slots) [[src-dmzcodex-backpacks-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].

## What can go in a Secure Slot?

Keys, recipes, Weapon Manuals and other rare items can; weapons cannot [[src-windowscentral-dmz-backpacks-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]. Players also call this protected part of the backpack the Safe Pocket, and report that a Weapon Manual carried out in it does not count toward the War Spoils camo [clm_4f7ad24e07bb]; see [[war-spoils-camo]]. The Medium Backpack is the cheapest printable backpack with one [[src-windowscentral-dmz-backpacks-2026-10-22]] [clm_0a8ddc7fe171]. See [[secure-slots]].

## What else should you know?

- Every backpack has its own pouch for the four Resources, which take no item slots, and mission items go into a separate Mission Item slot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Each Active Duty Operator keeps their own backpack and loadout between deployments [[src-cod-dmz-progression-video-2026-10-07]]. The Free Loadout comes without a backpack (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- Backpacks also come from Survival Kits and from players you kill, whose backpacks you can loot [[src-windowscentral-dmz-backpacks-2026-10-22]] [[src-dmzcodex-backpacks-2026-10-31]]. The inventory's Sort button tidies a full backpack [clm_8f5d9e799304].
- Fixed on 28 Oct 2026: weapons dropping when moved from the primary slot into the backpack, weapons vanishing after repeated swaps with a weapon-storage backpack, and printing items into the backpack mid-deployment costing players their Free Loadout gear [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
