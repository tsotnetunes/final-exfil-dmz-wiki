The Sedative Inhaler is an Uncommon consumable that keeps you hidden for a while: it takes you off radar, stops you being marked and hides you from thermal (the effects are reported by a community database) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. It sits with the other healing and boost items on [[medical-items]].

## How long does it last?
Since 2 Nov 2026 the effect runs for 120 seconds; before that it was 60 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Since Season 1 you can use it with a Knife in hand [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## What does it hide you from?
Ordinary radar and UAV pings, and, after two Season 1 fixes, the upgraded scans that used to see through it: since 6 Nov 2026 rival UAV Towers upgraded by the [[uav-upgrade]] Trait, and since 12 Nov 2026 enemy UAVs boosted by that Trait. The Advanced UAV still shows you [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. The [[thermal-padding]] Trait gives the same protection all the time; the inhaler is the cheap, temporary version ([[uav-towers]], [[fire-support]]).

## How do you get one?
Print it at the [[3d-printer]]: the recipe unlocks at DMZ level 3 and costs 15 Synthetics and 15 Chemicals [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. Its card value is listed as 100 or 250 DMZ Cash, depending on the database (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. All recipes: [[recipes]].
