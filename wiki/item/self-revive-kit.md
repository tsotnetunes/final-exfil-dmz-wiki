The Self-Revive Kit lets you pick yourself up when you are downed, without waiting for a squadmate [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. It is a Legendary consumable with its own Self-Revive slot in your loadout [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. One guide adds that getting up this way leaves you without a wound (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## How is it different from the tourniquet?

Every Operator can use the [[tourniquet]], which brings you up from Last Stand at 100 health with a wound; the June intel described it as the way back when you have no self-revive equipped [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. The tourniquet only works again once you are back at full health [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]], so a Self-Revive Kit is what gets you up a second time in the same fight. The Possum Trait is a separate once-per-match revive (see [[possum]]).

## How do you get a Self-Revive Kit?

- **Loot** in Hajin [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
- **The 3D Printer**: its recipe unlocks at DMZ level 48 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]; one database lists 1 Stabilized Reagents, 3 Binding Catalyst and 5 Biomaterials (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. See [[3d-printer]].
- **Kits**: the Triage Kit at DMZ level 27 and the Advanced SERE Kit at level 70 contain Self Revives [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]; a SERE Kit holds a Medkit and a Self-Revive Kit (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. See [[kits]].

## What is it worth?

Its card value is 4,000 on two databases and 2,500 on a third, and one tier list puts it in its top tier [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-loot-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. It stacks to one per slot [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. Other healing items are on [[medical-items]].
