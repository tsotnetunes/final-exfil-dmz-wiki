## Where do you find medical items?

Clinics, the green buildings on the map, and hospitals hold medical loot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]], and a guide says clinics marked with a cross nearly always have a Medkit (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]; see [[loot-placement]]. Medkits and Bandages are the first recipes you own, at DMZ level 1, and you can pin the Medkit recipe at the [[3d-printer]] to track its ingredients [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [clm_3fc45e2c36d0]. Triage kits bundle several of these items (see [[kits]]).

## Which Traits change them?

The Survivalist Trait Consumable Duration makes consumables last longer, and Numb the Pain gives revived players the effects of Painkillers and Smelling Salts [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
