Work through DMZ Orders. Each chapter of Orders ends with a Story Mission, and some missions stay locked until you meet their prerequisites [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-features-2026-10-09]]. Six Story Missions were listed at launch [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]].

## In what order do they open?
| Tier | Missions | Opens when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Operation Critical Extraction, Operation Silent Ledger, Operation Shopping Hacks | after your first Dynamic Ops deployment on Orders page 1 |
| 2 | Operation Iron Citadel, Operation Grid Assault | after all three tier 1 missions |
| 3 | Operation Fallen Ember | after both tier 2 missions |

Reported by a community guide (reported) [[src-dmztracker-story-missions-2026-10-20]]. Before launch, Infinity Ward described Story Missions opening as you complete DMZ Orders, including Orders done in Free Roam [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]] [[src-fpsjp-story-missions-trailer-2026-10-09]].

## What do they pay?
Each pays 25,000 DMZ Cash plus items, and four unlock paid infil options: Silent Ledger the Polaris Dagor A1, Iron Citadel the LTV, Grid Assault the helicopter and Fallen Ember the cargo plane [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-story-missions-2026-10-20]]. Prices of those infils: [[can-you-choose-where-you-infil]].

## How do you start one?
At the Deploy station choose Story Missions, read the briefing and Ready Up; a random-mission option is there too [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. You can run one Story Mission per match (reported) [[src-dmztracker-story-missions-2026-10-20]].

## Can you do them again?
Yes; see [[can-you-replay-story-missions]]. Every mission: [[story-missions]]. The Orders that lead to them: [[dmz-orders]].
