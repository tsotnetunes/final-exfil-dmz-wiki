No. Every DMZ deployment, Story Missions included, shares Hajin with rival Operators as well as AI enemies, and there is no PvE-only playlist [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [clm_5925122b4bac]. The developers said the Single Squad Mission option seen in a pre-launch screenshot was for internal testing, not for release [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-thexclusiveace-bounty-video-2026-10-12]] [clm_cb80a507198b].

## Will a PvE mode be added?
Nothing is announced. Before launch, press reported Infinity Ward saying no PvE-only mode was planned (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-things-to-know-2026-10-03]], and the Season 1 post of 28 Oct 2026 adds none [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## Why did Story Missions feel like PvE at launch?
At launch a Story Mission queue tended to put you in a sparse lobby with other Story Mission squads. After the 28 Oct 2026 update, press reported these queues going into fuller Free Roam and Dynamic Ops lobbies, a change the patch notes do not mention [[src-detonated-dmz-story-matchmaking-2026-10-29]] [[src-ixbt-dmz-pvp-matchmaking-2026-10-30]]. Players then reported Story Mission matchmaking back on launch settings around 30 and 31 Oct (reported) [clm_2738483c8aa0]. Infinity Ward has not commented. Details: [[pvp-matchmaking-prioritization]].

## How do you meet fewer players?
You cannot switch PvP off, but you can lower the odds: queue solo so matchmaking looks for other solos, keep the [[stealth-meter]] white, and offer a truce on the Ping Wheel [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Aggressive players are marked: four player kills make an Operator Wanted, and nearby Operators are alerted when one comes close [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A solo guide adds: stay out of Critical and Extreme areas early on [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]]. See [[dmz-solo-guide]] and [[bounty-system]].
