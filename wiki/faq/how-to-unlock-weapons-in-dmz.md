Extract its Weapon Manual. Once you exfil with a weapon's Manual, you can buy that base weapon at the Gunsmith for DMZ Cash as often as you like [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The Manual is used up at exfil and also pays 5,000 DMZ Cash, and the Gunsmith opens at DMZ level 10 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. There are 32 launch weapons in eight classes [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and community data lists 32 Weapon Manuals, one for every weapon except the melee knife (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]]. See [[weapon-manuals]] and [[gunsmith]].

## Where else do weapons come from?
- Your Free Loadout, kept only if you extract [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Your Stash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- The Gunsmith, after extracting the Manual; any weapon you extract can also be customised there as a single copy [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- The Vendor, from DMZ level 5; its stock rotates and improves with your level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]].
- Enemies and Lieutenants (a Lieutenant's own weapon is a rare drop), players you kill, and military containers in police stations and compounds [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Blueprints, each usable once for free and bought back at a discount if lost [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What does a gun cost?
A base M4 costs about 5,000 DMZ Cash, and each attachment slot and attachment costs extra [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A weapon you pick up in Hajin may come with as many as eight attachment slots, while Gunsmith builds take up to five plus the Apex slot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The Vendor's best guns cost over 100,000 (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-money-2026-10-26]].

## Anything new in Season 1?
From 6 Nov 2026 the Vendor sells more weapons and gear for DMZ Cash to players with the Season 1 BlackCell, and the Wager Match Event can award the Hyeon Auto and Kastov 545 Apex Attachments for DMZ [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## Is there a hidden reward for Manuals?
Yes, a camo. Players report that extracting three Weapon Manuals of the same weapon unlocks its War Spoils camo, seen so far on guns such as the Pila, the Patriot XMR and the Hyeon Burst [clm_ce6b0de65759] [clm_4c4b5f9891b5] [clm_2b05fa148d3c] [clm_e153c3eb028f]. A Manual extracted from a backpack's secure pocket reportedly does not count toward it (reported) [clm_4f7ad24e07bb]. Which guns are worth it: [[best-weapons-in-dmz]].
