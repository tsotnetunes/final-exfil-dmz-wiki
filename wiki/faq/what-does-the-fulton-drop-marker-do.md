The Fulton Drop Marker is a Field Upgrade that marks a spot and calls a Fulton extraction to it, according to three community databases [[src-codmunity-fulton-drop-marker-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmztracker-fulton-drop-marker-2026-10-15]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. Activision has not described it: the official FOB guide only lists two in the Vault Edition Deployment Kit [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do you get one?
- Two come in the Vault Edition Deployment Kit [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- You can print one at the 3D Printer, under Field Upgrades, from 1 Prototype RF Module, 3 Comms Modules and 5 Control Units [[src-dmztracker-fulton-drop-marker-2026-10-15]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]].
- It is an Ultra item with a listed value of 2,500 that does not stack [[src-codmunity-fulton-drop-marker-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].

## Does it lift you out, or only loot?
Not confirmed yet. The databases describe a Fulton extraction at the marked spot, but not whether it takes you, your squad or only items, or how long it takes [[src-dmztracker-fulton-drop-marker-2026-10-15]]. Try your first one somewhere quiet before you rely on it in a fight [[src-dmztracker-fulton-drop-marker-2026-10-15]].

## How is it different from a normal exfil?
Every match has exfil sites marked by green smoke, where you call the Twin Heli and hold the area until it lands; a Fulton balloon or a Flare are the official alternatives [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The Drop Marker lets you choose the spot instead. See [[how-to-exfil-in-dmz]] and [[exfil]].

## Which slot does it take?
Your loadout has one Field Upgrade slot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], so carrying a Fulton Drop Marker means leaving another Field Upgrade, such as the Armor Box, at home [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
