Yes. DMZ Cash goes into a Bank with a capacity limit, and Bank upgrades raise it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Activision's guide gives no figures [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], but community data lists ten Bank Upgrades, from a 250,000 cap at level I to 250 million at level X [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]], and other guides confirm the early steps [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-money-2026-10-26]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## What does each Bank Upgrade give?
| Upgrade | Bank holds up to | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 250,000 | 20,000 DMZ Cash |
| II | 500,000 | 50,000 |
| III | 1,000,000 | 100,000 and 5 Infantry Invasion Plans |
| IV | 2,000,000 | 200,000 and valuables |
| V to X | 5 million, then 10, 25, 50, 100 and 250 million | 500,000 each and higher-rarity valuables; X needs 10 Gold Bars |

Levels I to X from dmzhub (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]]. The 500,000 and 1,000,000 steps and the Upgrade III price were also seen in play [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-money-2026-10-26]], and the 2,000,000 step is in a second database [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Before launch, players reported a starting cap of 100,000 (reported) [clm_06c3db0d22f9].

## What happens to cash over the cap?
It is reported to be lost without a warning (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. If you are close to the cap, spend before a big run: upgrades, the Gunsmith, the Vendor or an MIA recovery.

## How do you raise the cap?
Buy the next Bank Upgrade and keep the valuables it asks for, such as Infantry Invasion Plans for level III, instead of selling them [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-money-2026-10-26]]. DMZ levels also give Bank capacity rewards [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[wallet]] and [[3d-printer]].

## Is the Bank shared?
Yes: every one of your Active Duty Operators draws on the same Bank, Stash, Resources and Ingredients [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Earning more: [[how-to-make-money-in-dmz]].
