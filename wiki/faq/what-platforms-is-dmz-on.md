DMZ is on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC through Battle.net, Steam and Xbox on PC, and Nintendo Switch 2, the same platforms as the rest of Modern Warfare 4 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-2026-06-12]]. The official platform lists name no PlayStation 4 or Xbox One version [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How does DMZ run on PS5 and Xbox?
Performance Mode in Campaign and DMZ needs VRR switched on; the official Call of Duty Updates account said so on 20 Oct 2026, and without VRR the game runs at 60Hz on those consoles [[src-gamerfuzion-vrr-performance-mode-2026-10-22]]. If you are stuck at 60Hz, check that your display supports 120Hz and VRR, then enable VRR [[src-gamerfuzion-vrr-performance-mode-2026-10-22]].

## How does DMZ run on Switch 2?
Digital Foundry measured DMZ at 40 frames per second on Switch 2, but only with a 120Hz display connected, against 60 for Multiplayer and 30 for the Campaign (reported) [[src-nintendolife-df-switch-2-2026-10-30]]. Docked play runs at a dynamic 900p in the DLSS mode and handheld at a dynamic 720p; the report does not cover DMZ in handheld mode (reported) [[src-nintendolife-df-switch-2-2026-10-30]].

## What does a PC need?
TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot, an Intel or AMD processor with AVX, DirectX 12 and 115 GB on an SSD; ray tracing works in DMZ [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]]. Full specs: [[does-dmz-need-tpm-2-0-and-secure-boot]].

Yes; see [[is-dmz-cross-play]]. Whether you need a subscription on each platform: [[does-dmz-need-ps-plus-or-game-pass]].
