Yes. Every PC player of Modern Warfare 4 needs TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot enabled, and Activision says more security measures may be enforced; the RICOCHET anti-cheat uses them together with server-side detection, and accounts get two-factor sign-in and email checks [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]]. The developers list them among DMZ's anti-cheat measures, with server culling and stronger account security (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## What else does a PC need?
| Tier | Processor | Memory | Graphics card (video memory) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum | Ryzen 5 1400 or Core i5-6600 | 8 GB | Radeon RX 470, GeForce GTX 970 or 1060, or Arc A580 (3 GB) |
| Recommended, 90 fps in Multiplayer on High | Ryzen 5 3600 or Core i7-8700K | 16 GB | Radeon RX 6600 XT, GeForce RTX 3060 Ti or Arc B580 (8 GB) |
| Ray tracing, 4K upscaled | Ryzen 7 3700X or Core i7-10700K | 32 GB | Radeon RX 9070 XT or GeForce RTX 5070 Ti (16 GB) |

All figures from Activision's PC post [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]]. Every tier needs 115 GB on an SSD plus room for updates, DirectX 12 and broadband; only Intel or AMD processors with the AVX instruction set are supported, and Intel Arc cards need Resizable BAR [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]]. Minimum is Windows 10 64-bit; recommended is Windows 11 64-bit [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]].

## Does DMZ support ray tracing and upscaling?
Yes: ray tracing works in Campaign, Multiplayer and DMZ, with DLSS 4.5, FSR Redstone, XeSS 3 and Filmic SMAA T2X for upscaling, and there is a built-in benchmark [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]].
