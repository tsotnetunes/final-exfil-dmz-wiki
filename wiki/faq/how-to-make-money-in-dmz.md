Objectives pay best. Since the update of 30 Oct 2026 a Dynamic Operation pays 10,000 to 20,000 DMZ Cash, a Side Op 2,000 and each completed mission step 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Story Missions pay 25,000 each [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-money-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmztracker-story-missions-2026-10-20]], and each completed page of DMZ Orders pays 20,000 [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-money-2026-10-26]].

From the official patch notes [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Story Mission replays were also raised on 29 Oct, with no amount given [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What else pays?
- Extracting a Weapon Manual or a looted Recipe pays 5,000 each [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Bounties: kill a Wanted player and extract with their Dog Tag; the payout follows their Bounty level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_1dda32204130].
- The first kill of each Commander pays DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and a Lieutenant kill pays cash and XP [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; community data lists 50,000 per Hajin Commander and 10,000 per Lieutenant (reported) [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]].
- Valuables you carry out and sell: a Weapon Manual card showed 5,000 and Infantry Invasion Plans 3,000 (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-money-2026-10-26]].

## When does cash count?
Cash and valuables you carry count only when you exfil, and the After Action Report moves the cash to your Bank [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Dynamic Op payouts go straight to the Bank, so you keep them even if you die later [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]] [clm_f17f3ff635f7].

## Is there a limit?
Yes, the Bank has a cap that upgrades raise [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[is-there-a-bank-limit-in-dmz]]. Cash on a death: [[do-you-lose-dmz-cash-when-you-die]].
