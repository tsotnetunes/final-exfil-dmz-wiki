Yes, if you pay for the cargo plane: it drops you at a spot you choose for 100,000 DMZ Cash, while going in on foot is free but starts you at a random spot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. In between are vehicle drops and a helicopter that one of your squad flies from the FOB [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What are the options?
| Infil | Price in DMZ Cash | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On foot | free | random spot |
| Polaris Dagor A1 drop | 5,000 | carries a full squad |
| LTV drop | 25,000 | optional mounted .50 cal |
| Helicopter from the FOB | 50,000 | one of you pilots |
| Cargo plane drop | 100,000 | you choose the spot |

Prices from the FOB guide [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; vehicle notes from the Hajin guide [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Do you have to unlock them?
Some options need progression [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Community databases tie four of them to Story Missions: Silent Ledger unlocks the Polaris Dagor A1, Iron Citadel the LTV, Grid Assault the helicopter and Fallen Ember the cargo plane [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-story-missions-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-vehicles-2026-10-31]]. See [[how-to-unlock-story-missions]].

## When can you use paid infil?
On Dynamic Operations and Free Roam deployments [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Activision sums it up as going in quietly on foot or fast and loud by helicopter or plane [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## Anything to know about vehicles?
DMZ vehicles have limited armour and fuel, and you repair and refuel them at Gas Stations [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Details: [[paid-infil]] and [[vehicles]].
