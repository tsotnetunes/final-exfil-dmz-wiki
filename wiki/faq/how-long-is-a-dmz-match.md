About 40 to 50 minutes. That is the length Infinity Ward gave in a pre-launch interview, up from about 30 minutes in the 2022 beta (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-things-to-know-2026-10-03]]. You do not have to stay that long: exfil whenever your objective is done [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What sets the clock?
Radiation spreads across Hajin during a deployment, and the Time Remaining timer on the HUD shows when the storm gets worse [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Players report just under 15 minutes between the timer reaching zero and the radiation covering everything (reported) [clm_a15e34798a66]. See [[radiation-storm]].

## How long do activities take?
- A Story Mission takes about 10 to 15 minutes (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]].
- A DMZ Order asks you to finish a Dynamic Operation in under 8 minutes, so a quick Op fits in that time [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].
- Calling an exfil takes about 90 seconds until the helicopter is down (reported) [clm_0654da754a95].

## Does a short match cost you anything?
No. The XP you earn counts even if you do not extract [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and a 2XP token counts down only while you are in a match [[src-cod-2xp-game-time-2026-09-28]]. Leaving early with what you have is often the safer choice: see [[how-to-exfil-in-dmz]].

## Will Season 1 change it?
Nothing announced. X01 adds its own pressure: its exits shut once the facility goes into lockdown [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].
