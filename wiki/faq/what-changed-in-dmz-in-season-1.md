The Season 1 patch notes of 5 Nov 2026 went live with the season at midnight ET on 6 Nov. They hold about 110 DMZ lines: one new place, a few balance changes and many fixes [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. A small update followed on 6 Nov 2026 with about a dozen more DMZ lines, a third on 7 Nov 2026 with about fifteen, all fixes, a fourth on 10 Nov 2026 with about fifteen more, among them a cash payout for failed exfils and much weaker UAVs, and a fifth on 11 Nov 2026 with a game-wide hitscan change and five DMZ lines, a sixth on 12 Nov 2026 with six DMZ fixes, a seventh on 13 Nov 2026 with four DMZ fixes and the Resupply Run event, and an eighth on Monday 16 Nov 2026 with thirteen DMZ lines, among them shared Lieutenant tag cash and a weaker UAV Upgrade Pro, a ninth on 19 Nov 2026 whose only DMZ lines start the War Chest event, and a tenth on Monday 23 Nov 2026 with DMZ's first weapon tuning and six DMZ fixes [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## What did the 10 Nov update change?
- Failed exfils now pay something: if you die or fail to get out, you receive cash worked out from the value of what you looted and how long you were deployed. Story Missions are left out, and the notes give no formula ([[death-and-loss]], [[dmz-cash]]) [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- UAVs and Advanced UAVs from [[fire-support]] now last a much shorter time and cover a much smaller area, with no figures given. The official post called it X01 tuning; later the same day both were switched off in [[x01]] altogether [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- The Mini Tracker Minor Trait has a slightly smaller radius ([[scout-tree]]) [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- Fixes: the Crude Diethyl Ether no longer floats in [[chemical-denial]]; AI no longer stays stuck in a passenger seat ([[vehicles]]); explosions under a departing helicopter no longer hurt players already aboard ([[exfil]]); in [[x01]] the crank gate elevator shaft exfil in the Power Generators area works again, the Vault Elevator no longer traps players on an upper floor, and bodies no longer drop through the Deep Vault floor; the Wheelson Commander's turret stops tracking after a DDoS ([[commander-threats]]) [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- Camos and menus: Platinum challenges for the Cronen Osprey and CSG-35 track, Sniper BlackCell headshots count, and Map Voting works when only two maps are offered ([[dmz-camos]]) [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## What did the 11 Nov update change?
- Gunfights, in every mode including DMZ: the true hitscan range of all bullet weapons is 3.91 times longer, about 35 meters on average, and a new simulation makes long-range bullets land 34 ms sooner ([[movement-and-tuning]]) [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- Solo players and duos build a Bounty slightly faster ([[bounty-system]]) [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- The Bombing Run from [[fire-support]] can no longer be used in [[x01]] [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- Fixes: claiming a Battle Pass reward from DMZ no longer fails ([[is-there-a-battle-pass-in-dmz]]), spectator and downed HUD elements such as Calling Cards no longer overlap, and a new Operator Skin picked in the FOB shows correctly straight away [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- Later that day Activision began looking into intermittent delays when firing semi-automatic weapons [[src-codupdates-x-semi-auto-delay-2026-11-11]].

- Some BlackCell camo challenges track properly in DMZ ([[dmz-camos]]).

The same evening Activision said it had granted the level 37 3-Plate Armor Vest to every player who passed that level before the 28 Oct fix ([[3-plate-armor-vest]]) [[src-codupdates-x-level-37-armor-grant-2026-11-12]].

## What did the 16 Nov update change?
Two balance changes and a round of fixes [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]:
- The cash for extracting a Lieutenant's Dog Tag now goes to every squad member ([[dog-tags]], [[lieutenants]]).
- The [[uav-upgrade]] Pro Trait zooms the minimap out 1.5 times instead of 3 times.
- The Wheelson in [[x01]] no longer targets players protected by [[viper-sense]].
- Plate Pouch and Plate Organization stacked show ten plates, not nine ([[warfighter-tree]], [[survivalist-tree]]).
- Loot from a destroyed [[deathstalker]] can be pinged again.
- A weapon exfilled in the Backpack Weapon slot equips properly on the next deployment after you make it your Primary or Secondary.
- Bomb sites in Dynamic Operations no longer stay usable after they should close ([[dynamic-operations]]).
- Screen fixes: hidden weapon stats in the DMZ Gunsmith and weapon select, wrong Blueprint names in the Backpack, Operators seeming to fall out of the exfil helicopter, wrong rarity colours for [[threat-briefing-battle-tank]] rewards, and a zero-kill requirement shown on the "Chained" camo challenge.
- For all modes, the [[joint-fire]] end time is shown correctly, and the notes repeat that Joint Fire runs 24 hours every Friday for the rest of Season 1. The Drill Charge range cut is listed for Multiplayer only.

The same day the [[natural-concealment]] camo event began; its class challenges count in DMZ [[src-infinityward-x-natural-concealment-2026-11-16]].

## What did the 19 Nov update change?
For DMZ, one new event and no fixes [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]:
- [[leaderboard-war-chest]] started: containers score 5 points and safes 50 on a leaderboard that lasts 3 hours, with a new one every 4 hours; rewards such as Fulton Exfils, Tempered Armor Vests and UAVs can each be won once [[src-infinityward-x-war-chest-live-2026-11-19]].
- For all modes, camo tracking screens and Natural Concealment completion notices were fixed, and the notes repeat that [[joint-fire]] returns every Friday.
- Fourteen weapons and Apex Attachments were tuned, mostly with more range or damage, among them a [[mosin-nagant]] that keeps its top damage much further out. All of it is listed under Multiplayer; whether DMZ gets the same values is not stated, and DMZ has its own tuning ([[movement-and-tuning]]).

## What did the 23 Nov update change?
DMZ's first weapon tuning of its own, a round of fixes and a cross-mode event [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]:
- Weapons: eighteen assault rifles, SMGs and LMGs deal 4 to 15% less damage in DMZ, among them the [[han-86]], [[m4]], [[kastov-762]], [[ppsh-41]] and [[x-58-nyx]], while the [[signal-50]] gains mid and long-range damage and a 1.6 headshot multiplier. The full list is on [[movement-and-tuning]].
- Missions: the keycard objective in [[operation-iron-citadel]] no longer sticks when another team picks up the card, and the [[colossus]] in [[operation-fallen-ember]] drops the Boathouse Key again.
- Traits: [[possum]] and Possum Pro no longer stack.
- Gear: Weapon Insurance works in back-to-back matches ([[what-do-rescue-beacons-and-weapon-insurance-do]]), and a Lieutenant tag you cannot pick up because your Backpack is full no longer marks you on the map ([[dog-tags]]).
- Exploits: several closed, among them mantling from a vehicle through a ceiling ([[vehicles]], [[exploiting-glitches]]).
- For all modes, the [[thanksgiving-duty-roster]] kill leaderboard started; a DMZ kill is worth 2 points against 10 in Multiplayer and 20 in Warzone.
