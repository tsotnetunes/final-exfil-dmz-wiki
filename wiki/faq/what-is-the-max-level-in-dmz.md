DMZ level 70 is the top of the DMZ track, with an unlock at every level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Two other tracks run beside it: each Operator's Dog Tag has 18 levels, Steel I to Onyx III, and your Modern Warfare 4 player level runs from 1 to 55, which DMZ XP also fills [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What does each track give?
| Track | Range | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DMZ level | 1 to 70 | station unlocks and upgrades, an extra Active Duty slot, Recipes, Operators, Survival Kits [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] |
| Operator Dog Tag | Steel I to Onyx III, 18 levels | one Trait Point per level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] |
| Player level | 1 to 55 | shared with Multiplayer; DMZ XP counts toward it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] |

## What do you get at level 70?
A community database lists 18 more item Stash slots, a 100% higher bounty payout and an Advanced SERE Kit at level 70 (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-progression-2026-10-19]]. Players note that the DMZ level has no prestige of its own (reported) [clm_70618c5686b4].

## Can you prestige?
There are two kinds. Operator Prestige: once an Operator has all 18 Trait Points and is not MIA, you give up set gear and DMZ Cash to reset their Traits for a permanent Prestige Trait, up to three, with a swap offered from the fourth Prestige on [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[operator-prestige]]. Player Prestige can also be entered from the DMZ FOB; on 28 Oct 2026 the patch notes listed a known issue where doing so from DMZ menus blocks new Multiplayer classes until older ones with relocked items are deleted [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How fast can you get there?
No official XP-per-level table exists; see [[how-to-level-up-fast-in-dmz]] and [[dmz-progression]].
