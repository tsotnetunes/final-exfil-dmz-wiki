Yes. Every Operator deploys able to use a tourniquet, a self-revive from Last Stand that brings you to 100 health with a wound (developers' Q&A, as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. It works again only once you are back to full health; if you go down again while wounded, you need a squadmate or another revive [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Activision describes it as healing you to a wounded state when you have no self-revive equipped [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## What else gets you back up?
| Option | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Self-Revive kit | looted in Hajin or printed at the 3D Printer, carried in its own loadout slot [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] |
| Possum (Warfighter Trait) | once per match, from death back into Last Stand with full health [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] |
| Die Hard (Survivalist) | no bleed-out timer in Last Stand, and reaching an exfil calls it for you [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] |
| Free Revive (Survivalist) | your first Wound is repaired automatically [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] |

Since 28 Oct 2026 Possum cannot be used while the squad that killed you is nearby or before four minutes have passed [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How do you clear the wound?
A Bandage lifts you to 150 health for a short time before it drops back to 100; a Medkit repairs the wound and keeps you at 150 [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Maximum health is 150 [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## Where can you get Self-Revives?
Loot and the 3D Printer [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. A community database lists the Self-Revive Kit as a Legendary print from 1 Stabilized Reagents, 3 Binding Catalyst and 5 Biomaterials, and the SERE Kit bundle as holding a Medkit and a Self-Revive Kit (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. From Season 1 the Resupply Run event also trades for Self Revives [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. More: [[tourniquet]] and [[health-and-healing]].
