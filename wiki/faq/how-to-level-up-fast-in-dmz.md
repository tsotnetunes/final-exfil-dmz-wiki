Do objectives, not just fights: DMZ Orders, Story Missions and Dynamic Operations give the most XP, and you add more from loot containers, Side Ops, Lieutenants, Commanders and combat [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Rival Operators are worth more XP than AI [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. All of it counts even if you do not exfil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_43eb9661dfec].

## Which activities pay most?
| Activity | XP seen in press runs |
| --- | --- |
| Completed Dynamic Operation | 3,000 to 4,575 |
| Lieutenant kill | about 632, plus 500 for its Side Op |
| Unlocking a key door | 300 |
| Enemy kill | up to 120 with a headshot |
| Opening a safe | 75 to 82 |

These are press observations relayed by a community guide, not an official table (reported) [[src-dmztracker-level-up-fast-2026-10-22]]. A few Ops plus kills can pass 8,000 XP in one deployment (reported) [[src-dmztracker-level-up-fast-2026-10-22]].

## Do 2XP tokens work in DMZ?
Yes. A token counts down only while you are in a match, not in menus or at the FOB [[src-cod-2xp-game-time-2026-09-28]] [[src-dmztracker-level-up-fast-2026-10-22]], and you can use at most one hour of each token type a day [[src-activision-mw4-bonus-content-2026-10-23]]. Whether a token also speeds up your Operator's Dog Tag is being checked: [[does-double-xp-work-on-operator-dog-tags]]. Token rules: [[double-xp-tokens]].

## What else helps?
- Aim for an S rating or better in the After Action Report, which earns the best rewards [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]; see [[after-action-report]].
- Chain Dynamic Ops in one deployment: each completion also sends a better Supply Drop [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- The Fast Learner and Faster Learner Traits slightly raise XP gain (reported) [[src-dmztracker-level-up-fast-2026-10-22]].
- Multiplayer and Warzone XP never raise your DMZ level, but DMZ XP raises your MW4 player level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
