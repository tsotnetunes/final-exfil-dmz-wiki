No wipe has been announced. Since the post of 2 Nov 2026, one reset is known: Player Level resets on 6 Nov, with optional Prestige across Multiplayer, DMZ and Warzone [[src-activision-warzone-season-1-blog-2026-11-02]] [clm_ec947898aaaf]; details are on [[does-your-level-reset-in-season-1]]. Neither that post nor the Season 1 post of 28 Oct mentions resetting the Stash, Operators, FOB or Traits [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Before launch, press reported Infinity Ward saying there would be no hard wipes and that Operators could be retired instead, but that came from a video and was never confirmed in writing (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-things-to-know-2026-10-03]].

## What persists between deployments now?
Your FOB upgrades and Stash, your Operators' Traits, weapon unlocks from extracted Weapon Manuals, your Bounty Leaderboard standing, and your XP and levels all carry over from one deployment to the next [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Details: [[what-carries-over]].

## Is anything reset on purpose?
- Operator Prestige is a reset you choose: it clears one Operator's Traits in return for a permanent Prestige Trait [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[operator-prestige]].
- A Japanese report of the official Dynamic Ops trailer says the Boss Board is filled again each season (reported) [[src-fpsjp-dynamic-ops-trailer-2026-10-07]].
- The Bounty Leaderboard updates weekly [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Why do players expect a wipe?
The 2022 DMZ beta was a separate free mode with its own rules [[src-windows-central-dmz-reveal-2026-06-06]]. Modern Warfare 4's DMZ was built around a permanent Stash and FOB, which the developers named among the biggest changes from the beta (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## When will we know for sure?
Season 1 starts at midnight ET on 6 Nov 2026 [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]; read the Season 1 patch notes that day. See [[where-to-find-dmz-patch-notes]] and [[open-questions]].
