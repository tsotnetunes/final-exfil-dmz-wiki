It depends on how much a faster start is worth to you: for DMZ the Vault Edition gives a third Active Duty slot from the start, a one-time Deployment Kit of gear, the Signature Weapon Collection Blueprints and one season of BlackCell [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]]. Standard owners unlock the third slot by levelling instead, and can buy BlackCell separately on the same platform [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Before launch the Vault Edition was listed at 99.99 USD [[src-windows-central-dmz-reveal-2026-06-06]].

## How do you claim the DMZ items?
Players report that you claim the DMZ Vault Edition bonus at the Survival Kits station, which moves it into your Stash (reported) [clm_d79723694987]. A Blueprint can be used once for free in DMZ; if you lose it, you can buy it back at a discount [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Are the Signature weapons good in DMZ?
Three of them, the ISO Nightshade, Kastov 762 and Rezi 12, sit high on community tier lists [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]] [[src-playhub-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-21]]. Tier lists are opinion and move with patches; see [[best-weapons-in-dmz]].
