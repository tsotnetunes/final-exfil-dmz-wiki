Pay for a recovery: an MIA Operator comes back for DMZ Cash taken from your Bank, keeping every Trait but none of their gear, and the price rises with their Trait Points [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The alternative is to dismiss them and recruit a fresh Operator [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Where do you do it?
At the Operators (Active Duty) station in the FOB, which shows each Operator's status [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. An MIA Operator cannot deploy until you choose [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[mia-system]] and [[active-duty]].

## How much does it cost?
There is no official price list: the fee grows with the Operator's Trait Points [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and press add that your own stats play a part (reported) [[src-windows-central-dmz-reveal-2026-06-06]]. Permanent FOB upgrades give MIA recovery discounts [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; community data lists four MIA Recovery Discount levels, each 5% off, paid in valuables and Ingredients (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]]. Rescuing a squadmate's MIA Operator is reported to cost more the more they have invested in them (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## Recover or recruit?
Compare the fee with the build you would lose: a recovered Operator keeps all their Traits, while a new recruit starts with none and a Steel I Dog Tag [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Your Bank, Stash, Resources and Ingredients are shared by all Operators, so recruiting costs you no stored items [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What are Operator Rescue Beacons?
The Vault Edition Deployment Kit includes two, but the official guide does not say what they do [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Not confirmed yet.

## Can a bug put an Operator in MIA?
It did: until a fix on 24 Oct 2026, a party leader could drag teammates out of a deployment against their will, leaving their Operators MIA [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Disconnects: [[what-happens-if-you-disconnect-in-dmz]].
