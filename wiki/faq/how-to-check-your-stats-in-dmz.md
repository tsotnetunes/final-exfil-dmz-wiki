Not that we can find. We know of no official text or guide showing a lifetime K/D or extraction rate for DMZ in the game, and that is an absence of evidence, not a statement from Activision. One guide to the Career menu lists K/D, win rate and recent matches under Multiplayer Stats; for DMZ it points only to your DMZ level in Progression and to DMZ's own progress and resource screens (reported) [[src-u4n-check-stats-2026-11-04]].

## What can you see in the game?
- **After Action Report:** after each deployment, what you brought back, a rating from F to S++, the AI you killed, the loot containers you opened and the XP from each [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. See [[after-action-report]]. It covers one match, so it does not add up to a rate.
- **Dog Tag:** a Bounty on your Operator shows here with your Operator kill count [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[bounty-system]].
- **Weapons:** level and camo progress sit on the Gunsmith's Progression tab (reported) [[src-u4n-check-stats-2026-11-04]].

## Can you track it yourself?
Yes, by hand: count deployments and extractions from your After Action Reports. We do not vouch for third-party stat trackers, and we have not checked whether any reads DMZ data. This is listed on [[open-questions]] until there is a clear answer.
