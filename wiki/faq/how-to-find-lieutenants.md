Two ways. Buy a Lieutenant's intel at the Boss Board before you deploy, or climb a Lieutenant Hunt tower in Hajin and use the terminal at the top, which marks the general area of the nearest active Lieutenant [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Climbing radio towers as a Side Op also reveals a nearby Lieutenant's general location [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What does Boss Board intel cost?
DMZ Cash; it is unlocked by challenges in earlier deployments and lasts one deployment [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A community guide reports 25,000 (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. The Boss Board opens after your second DMZ Order [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A bug that sometimes blocked buying intel, and with it the Information Power Order, was fixed on 30 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What happens when you find one?
Lieutenants work their territory with escorts whether you hunt them or not [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. A kill pays DMZ XP and DMZ Cash, can drop their weapon and the key to their loot room, and always drops a Dog Tag Case; picking the Case up shows your position to every Operator, and extracting with it moves the Boss Board on [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Is there an Order for this?
The first Human Intel page asks you to climb a Lieutenant Hunt tower and use its terminal, and a later Order asks you to buy intel and kill a Lieutenant (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## Are new Lieutenants coming?
Season 1 adds Tae-Hyun ("Knuckle") and Chol-Min ("The Wrench"), with intel on the Boss Board [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Known Lieutenants: [[lieutenants]]. The board: [[boss-board]].
