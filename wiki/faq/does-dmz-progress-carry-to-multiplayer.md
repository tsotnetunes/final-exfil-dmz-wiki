Partly. DMZ XP counts toward your Modern Warfare 4 player level (1 to 55), and weapon levels and shared attachment unlocks count across Multiplayer and DMZ, so a gun you level in one mode is levelled in the other [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. It does not work the other way for the DMZ level: XP from Multiplayer and Warzone never raises it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What stays inside DMZ?
Your DMZ level, your Operators and their Traits, the Stash, DMZ Cash and FOB upgrades belong to DMZ [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. DMZ weapon builds are not for Multiplayer: a fix on 24 Oct 2026 stopped them showing as usable in Core Multiplayer [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. DMZ has its own camos and camo grind [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; see [[dmz-camos]].

## Does the Campaign give anything for DMZ?
Yes: finishing Campaign missions unlocks eight base Operators, a calling card, an emblem, a loading screen and Double XP tokens that you can use in DMZ [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]]. See [[does-the-campaign-unlock-anything-for-dmz]].

## What about Warzone from Season 1?
From 6 Nov 2026 Modern Warfare 4 is fully integrated with Warzone [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]; the post does not say how that touches DMZ progress. See [[is-dmz-part-of-warzone]].

## Any known problems?
Entering player Prestige from the DMZ FOB has upset Multiplayer loadouts: a fix on 28 Oct 2026 made it reset them correctly, and a known issue remains for players who prestiged from DMZ menus [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Until a fix on 23 Oct, DMZ could show Killstreak indicators left over from your last Multiplayer match [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
