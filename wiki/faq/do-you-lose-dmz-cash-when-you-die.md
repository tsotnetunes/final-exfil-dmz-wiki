Only what you carry. The DMZ Cash in your Bank stays safe at the FOB, but cash you pick up during a match sits in your loadout and reaches the Bank only when you exfil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Your loadout's DMZ Cash slot is empty at the start of every deployment [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Do you get anything back when you fail to exfil?
Yes, since 10 Nov 2026. A player who dies or otherwise fails to exfil now receives a cash payout based on the value of the items they looted and how long they were deployed. It does not apply in Story Missions, and Infinity Ward has not published the formula or any minimum time [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. It softens a loss but does not return the cash you carried.

## Which cash is safe?
- Your Bank balance, shared by all your Operators and never taken into Hajin [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Dynamic Operation payouts, which go straight to the Bank when the Op is done [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]] [clm_f17f3ff635f7].

A death can still cost money afterwards: recovering an MIA Operator is paid from the Bank [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[how-to-get-operators-back-from-mia]].

## Where do you see how much you carry?
The squad panel shows each Operator's DMZ Cash, armour plates, gas mask and parachute [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. After a deployment the After Action Report lists the cash you brought out, and it goes to the Bank [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Is the Free Loadout really free?
It costs nothing to take, but you keep it only if you extract [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and losing your Operator with it still means a recovery fee (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## How do you protect your cash?
Exfil when your pockets are full instead of pushing one more fight, and spend before your Bank hits its cap: [[is-there-a-bank-limit-in-dmz]]. Earning more: [[how-to-make-money-in-dmz]]. Everything else you lose on a death: [[what-happens-when-you-die-in-dmz]].
