Being checked: no official source says yet whether a Double XP token makes an Operator's Dog Tag level up faster. Two things are known. 2XP tokens do run in DMZ matches, and the Dog Tag levels from DMZ XP; how the two combine has not been stated [[src-dmztracker-level-up-fast-2026-10-22]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How does a Dog Tag level up?
Each Operator has a Dog Tag with 18 levels, Steel I to Onyx III, and every level gives one Trait Point [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Re-read on 31 Oct 2026, Activision's guide ties the Dog Tag to the DMZ XP that each successful exfil awards, and a strong deployment can raise it by more than one level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The Operator's level is separate from your DMZ player level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[trait-points]].

## How do 2XP tokens work in DMZ?
You switch a token on from the menu at the FOB; tokens are split into Rank XP and Weapon XP, and they count down only while you are in a match (reported) [[src-dmztracker-level-up-fast-2026-10-22]] [[src-cod-2xp-game-time-2026-09-28]]. Each token type can run for at most one hour a day [[src-activision-mw4-bonus-content-2026-10-23]]. Monster Energy codes give 2XP tokens that players report work in DMZ (reported) [clm_7be12d197052], and the Campaign gives three Double XP tokens and two Double Weapon XP tokens [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]].

## What is not known yet?
- Whether a Rank 2XP token doubles the XP that feeds the Dog Tag, or only your DMZ and player levels [[src-dmztracker-level-up-fast-2026-10-22]].
- Whether XP from a failed exfil moves the Dog Tag at all; the guide only says a failed exfil keeps your DMZ level XP [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How can you check it yourself?
Note your Operator's Dog Tag progress in the After Action Report after an exfil without a token, then make a similar run with a Rank 2XP token active and compare the two bars. See [[after-action-report]] and [[double-xp-tokens]].
