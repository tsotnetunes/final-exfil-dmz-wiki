No. DMZ is one of Modern Warfare 4's core modes, and you get it by buying Modern Warfare 4, not by downloading Warzone [[src-windows-central-dmz-reveal-2026-06-06]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-things-to-know-2026-10-03]]. The 2022 DMZ beta did ship with free Warzone 2.0, which is where the confusion comes from [[src-windows-central-dmz-reveal-2026-06-06]]. The developers put it this way in their 9 Oct Q&A: DMZ lives in Modern Warfare 4, not Warzone, so Operators from older games do not appear (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## What changes with Season 1?
From 6 Nov 2026 free-to-play Warzone fully joins up with Modern Warfare 4; Hajin and Zodiac join Warzone on day one, and Verdansk and Rebirth Island return later in the season [[src-activision-warzone-season-1-blog-2026-11-02]]. Hajin becomes a battle royale map that stays in the live seasons, per Infinity Ward's The Ward Room Ep. 02 as reported [[src-dotesports-hajin-warzone-season-1-2026-10-16]]. Neither the 28 Oct post nor the Warzone post of 2 Nov says that DMZ joins Warzone or becomes free [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [[src-activision-warzone-season-1-blog-2026-11-02]].

## Is Warzone's Hajin the same as DMZ's?
It is the same place with different rules. DMZ vehicles need fuel and repairs at Gas Stations, while Warzone vehicles do not [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Activision's post of 2 Nov 2026 lists more: the Edge Circle and gas move differently, the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] has no radioactive cloud in Warzone, vehicles differ, and interactable objects and loot caches sit in different places [[src-activision-warzone-season-1-blog-2026-11-02]]. So routes and loot spots learned in one mode will not all hold in the other. Warzone's Season 1, named Hajin Conflict, is free from 6 Nov [[src-cod-x-hajin-conflict-2026-11-01]].

## Do Warzone and DMZ share progress?
Only one way: DMZ XP counts toward your Modern Warfare 4 player level, but Multiplayer and Warzone XP never raise your DMZ level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[does-dmz-progress-carry-to-multiplayer]].
