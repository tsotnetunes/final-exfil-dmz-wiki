A squad holds up to three Operators, and a Hajin match up to 60 players: 20 squads of three (developers' Q&A, as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]]. Squad Fill tops your squad up with two other players if you want a full team [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Can a squad have four players?
Yes, but only by assimilation: squads can join up during a match, and an assimilated squad is capped at four (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-things-to-know-2026-10-03]]. The Ping Wheel lets you ask for a truce or to join another squad [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; see [[how-to-team-up-with-other-squads]].

## Does matchmaking group squads by size?
It tries to put solos with solos, duos with duos and trios with trios, without a guarantee [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Players are also grouped by what they queued for, Story Missions, Dynamic Operations or Free Roam, and there is no separate party finder [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. More: [[matchmaking]].

## How many Operators can you own?
That is a different number: two Active Duty slots at the start and a third by DMZ level, or three from the start with the Vault Edition [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[how-to-get-more-active-duty-slots]].

## Is the match size changing in Season 1?
Not announced. Season 1 adds the X01 Black Site, reached through Hajin, but the official post gives no new player count [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].
