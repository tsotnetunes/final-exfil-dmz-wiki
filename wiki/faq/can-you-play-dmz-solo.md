Yes. Switch Squad Fill off on the Ready Up screen and you deploy alone [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]]. Infinity Ward says a solo player can do all of the content [[src-destructoid-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].

## How do you turn Squad Fill off?
Squad Fill is an option at Ready Up, just before Deploy; when it is on, it fills your squad with two other players [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Guides give the button as 1 on keyboard or the left trigger on controller [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]].

## Will you only meet other solo players?
Not always. Matchmaking aims to put solos with solos, duos with duos and trios with trios, with no guarantee, and up to 60 players share a match (developers' Q&A, as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]]. There is no solo-only playlist: the Single Squad Mission option seen in a screenshot was an internal test tool [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [clm_cb80a507198b]. Other squads may truce with you, betray you or invite you in [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; see [[how-to-team-up-with-other-squads]].

## What helps a solo player?
Every Operator deploys with a [[tourniquet]] that gets you up from Last Stand without a teammate, and the [[stealth-meter]] warns you before AI spot you; the developers say both were added with solos in mind [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Since the 28 Oct 2026 update the Possum Trait cannot revive you while the squad that killed you is nearby or before four minutes have passed [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Cash from a completed Dynamic Operation goes straight to your Bank, so you keep it even if you die later [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]] [clm_f17f3ff635f7].

## Can you do Story Missions solo?
Yes, players run them alone, and only your squad has your mission active [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]] [clm_a6788c608374]. Full advice: [[dmz-solo-guide]].
