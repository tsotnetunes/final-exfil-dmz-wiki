Yes, from Season 1. The Battle Pass opens from a new Battle Pass and Store Station beside the Survival Kit Station at the FOB, and its rewards include DMZ Survival Kits [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Season 1 starts at midnight ET on 6 Nov 2026 [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## What does it cost and give?
- 1,100 COD Points, or 2,400 as a bundle; 100 tiers with over 100 rewards [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].
- Instant rewards include a 10% Battle Pass XP boost and a DMZ Survival Kit; finishing it gives another DMZ Survival Kit, 200 COD Points and the Tier 100 Bellum Mosin-Nagant Blueprint [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].
- If you buy late, you receive every reward up to the season's current day, as far as day 50 [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## Does the Battle Pass claim rewards by itself?
Partly. If you own the paid pass, tiers 1 to 50 are guaranteed: one tier unlocks on its own each day of the season until day 50, unless you are already at or past that day's tier [[src-activision-support-battle-pass-2026-11-10]]. Tier Skip Tokens from the Battle Pass Bundle (20 of them) let you take a reward up to 10 tiers ahead of your position, or 20 with BlackCell, without moving your position; any tokens you have not spent when the season ends are used for you on your lowest unclaimed rewards and do not carry over [[src-activision-support-battle-pass-2026-11-10]]. Activision's support article does not mention a setting that turns these automatic unlocks off, and we have not found one in any other source.

## Does DMZ count towards it?
Yes. XP from DMZ, Multiplayer and Warzone all moves the same Battle Pass, and your progress and rewards belong to your Activision account, so they follow you across platforms; COD Points earned from the pass can be spent only on the platform where you unlocked them [[src-activision-support-battle-pass-2026-11-10]].

## What is BlackCell?
The premium tier. It includes the XERO Operator, 1,100 COD Points, a DMZ Survival Pack and a clan tag [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Players add weapon blueprints, Survival Kits, bonus Vendor slots and a free daily ingredient refresh, and a reported price of $29.99 (reported) [clm_10685d3b70d8] [clm_7ecc64c071c8]; Activision's posts have not listed these for DMZ. In DMZ, Season 1 BlackCell owners can buy extra weapons and gear from the Vendor for DMZ Cash [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. The Vault Edition includes one season of BlackCell, and buying BlackCell after the Battle Pass returns 1,100 COD Points [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]].

## What is a DMZ Survival Kit?
A themed bundle of gear, such as Deployment, Triage, Armor, Equipment or SERE kits, that you open into your Stash; unopened kits stack at the station [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[survival-kits]].

## Is the Battle Pass needed for DMZ?
No: DMZ levels, Orders, missions and events all give Survival Kits and gear without it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Vault Edition details: [[is-the-vault-edition-worth-it-for-dmz]]. Season 1 overview: [[season-1]].
