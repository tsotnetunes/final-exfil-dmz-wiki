Yes. In Modern Warfare 4 you add friends by their Activision ID, and squads can party up across platforms with crossplay, using the game's own party and friend tools [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]]. That covers every platform DMZ is on: PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Battle.net, Steam, Xbox on PC) and Nintendo Switch 2 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Can you turn crossplay off?
The crossplay settings sit in the settings menu under Account & Network. On PlayStation, Xbox and Switch 2 each one can be On, Off or Console Only, which keeps PC players out of your lobbies; on PC the option to switch crossplay off is not offered (reported) [[src-dexerto-mw4-crossplay-2026-10-31]]. The settings that guide lists are named for Multiplayer and Warzone Ranked, none for DMZ [[src-dexerto-mw4-crossplay-2026-10-31]], so it is not confirmed whether they change who you meet in DMZ.

## What does the FOB show when you party up?
In a party the FOB looks like the party leader's progress, while each station's menu shows your own progress and items (developers' Q&A, as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. A fix on 24 Oct 2026 stopped party leaders pulling teammates out of DMZ without their consent, which had left those teammates' Operators MIA [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Is Switch 2 on equal terms?
It plays with everyone else [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]]. Digital Foundry judged the Switch 2 version faithful enough for fair cross-platform play, with DMZ at 40 fps on a 120Hz display (reported) [[src-nintendolife-df-switch-2-2026-10-30]]. Platform details: [[what-platforms-is-dmz-on]].

## How many friends can join you?
Up to two in your squad, three in all; see [[how-many-players-in-a-dmz-squad]].
