No. There is no respec, because every Trait you pick is permanent [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthings-how-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. You can start a build over in only two ways: Prestige the Operator, or replace them with a new one [[src-allthings-how-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]].

## How does Prestige reset Traits?
Once an Operator has earned all 18 Trait Points and is not MIA, Operator Prestige resets their Traits and adds one random permanent Prestige Trait that stays with them; an Operator can hold three, and from the fourth Prestige on you can swap a new one in or keep the three [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. It costs gear and cash:

| Prestige | Gear you give up | DMZ Cash |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | two weapons (Rare or better, three attachment slots), a Rare plate carrier and backpack, four Rare Ingredients | 200,000 |
| Second | two weapons with four slots, an Epic plate carrier and backpack, four Epic Ingredients | 300,000 |
| Third and later | the same at Legendary rarity | 500,000 |

All from Activision's FOB guide [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Prestige consumes the required items, so move anything you want to keep out of the loadout and backpack first [[src-allthings-how-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. More: [[operator-prestige]].

## How does a new Operator help?
A newly recruited Operator starts with no Traits [[src-allthings-how-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. Dismissing an MIA Operator instead of paying for a recovery is one way to get one [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[how-to-get-operators-back-from-mia]].

## How do Traits work?
There are three trees, Warfighter, Scout and Survivalist, with 22 Traits each; the first point in a tree buys a random Minor Trait, and an Operator's 18 points are enough to complete two trees or spread over three [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Plan before you spend: [[trait-points]].
