DMZ is live now. Early access opened worldwide by 10 AM PT (1 PM ET, 17:00 UTC) on Tuesday 20 Oct 2026, and the full game, DMZ included, launched on 23 Oct 2026 [[src-iw-dmz-early-access-time-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. If you own Modern Warfare 4, there is nothing left to wait for.

## Who had early access?
Players who digitally pre-ordered or pre-purchased the Standard Digital or Vault Edition on Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, PS5, Battle.net, Steam or Switch 2 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Campaign early access came first, on 16 Oct, and both ran until launch [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]]. You cannot get early access any more: it ended when the game launched.

## Why was the hour unclear?
Activision's posts gave the date but no time; Infinity Ward confirmed the 10 AM PT start only on the evening of 19 Oct, the day before [[src-iw-dmz-early-access-time-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]].

## What time did the full game unlock?
On PC the full game unlocked at midnight local time on 23 Oct 2026, after a preload that started on 15 Oct at 9 AM PT [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]]. Campaign rewards became usable in DMZ from full launch [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]].
