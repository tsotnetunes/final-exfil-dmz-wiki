Yes. Completing Campaign missions unlocks rewards, in order, that you can use in Multiplayer and DMZ from full launch [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]]. They are eight base Operators (Cho, Dallas, Luca, Oh-Ji, Kal, Dunn, West and Park), the calling card No More Rules, the loading screen Ready Line, the emblem Gone Dark, three Double XP tokens and two Double Weapon XP tokens [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]].

## Do you need to play the Campaign first?
No. DMZ is its own mode, launched alongside the Campaign and Multiplayer [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]. The Campaign has 16 missions [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]].

## How does the story connect?
DMZ is set after the Campaign: a power vacuum in Hajin and a new threat to identify and take apart [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-features-2026-10-09]]. Infinity Ward says MW4's single-player team wrote the Story Missions (reported) [[src-windows-central-dmz-reveal-2026-06-06]]. See [[story-missions]].

## How should you use the tokens?
Double XP tokens count down only during matches, and each token type can run for at most one hour a day [[src-cod-2xp-game-time-2026-09-28]] [[src-activision-mw4-bonus-content-2026-10-23]]. Whether they also speed up Operator Dog Tags is being checked: [[does-double-xp-work-on-operator-dog-tags]].

## Where do the Operators go?
They are recruits for your Active Duty slots at the Operators station [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[active-duty]] and [[how-to-get-more-active-duty-slots]].
