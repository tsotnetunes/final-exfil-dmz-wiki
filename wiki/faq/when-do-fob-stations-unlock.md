Most stations are open from your first visit; five unlock as you play: the 3D Printer at DMZ level 1, the Boss Board after your second DMZ Order, the Vendor at level 5, the Gunsmith at level 10 and the Dog Tag Case at level 20 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].

## Which station opens when?
| Station | Unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| Operators (Active Duty), DMZ Orders, Stash and Loadout, Firing Range, Survival Kits, Bounty Leaderboard, DMZ Progression, Events, Deploy | from the start |
| 3D Printer | DMZ level 1 (level 2 in the 9 Oct guide) |
| Boss Board | after your second DMZ Order |
| Vendor | DMZ level 5 |
| Gunsmith | DMZ level 10 |
| Dog Tag Case | DMZ level 20 |

From Activision's FOB guide of 9 Oct [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], except the 3D Printer: that guide said level 2, but Activision's newer launch table of 19 Oct lists the station under level 1 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]], as does a community database [[src-codmunity-dmz-progression-2026-10-19]]. ## How do stations get better?
You upgrade them with prints from the 3D Printer, for example Bank capacity, Stash space, MIA recovery discounts, Gunsmith discounts and more Vendor offers, and DMZ levels add rewards of their own [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Each upgrade needs several prints [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What changes in Season 1?
On 6 Nov 2026 a Battle Pass and Store Station arrives next to the Survival Kit Station, and the 3D Printer gets an upgrade that has not been revealed [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].
