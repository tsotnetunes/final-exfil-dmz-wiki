Probably yes on the current consoles, but we have no official word for DMZ. Spain's Vandal lists split screen for two players on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in the online modes, and names DMZ alongside Multiplayer (reported) [[src-vandal-split-screen-2026-10-21]]. Brazil's Game Overdrive also describes two players per console, for Multiplayer, and warns that DMZ is not officially confirmed [[src-gameoverdrive-split-screen-2026-11-02]]. Activision's DMZ guides, as far as we have read them, do not settle it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Which platforms?
PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Vandal rules out PC and Switch 2 [[src-vandal-split-screen-2026-10-21]]; Game Overdrive only says those two are not confirmed [[src-gameoverdrive-split-screen-2026-11-02]].

## How do you set it up?
Per Vandal: each player needs an own Activision account and the console must be online. Open the mode's lobby, sign in on a second controller, and have the second player press the join button [[src-vandal-split-screen-2026-10-21]]. It also says both accounts need the platform's online subscription [[src-vandal-split-screen-2026-10-21]]; see [[does-dmz-need-ps-plus-or-game-pass]], which covers that in full.

## Does it work well?
Final Exfil's known-issues tracker has one player report, from 30 Oct 2026, of stability trouble and inactivity kicks in split screen on PS5 (reported, unverified). Squads and party rules: [[squads-and-solo-play]].
