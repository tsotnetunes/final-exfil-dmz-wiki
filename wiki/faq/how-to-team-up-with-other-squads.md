Open the Ping Wheel: it can request a Truce or ask to join up with another squad, and proximity chat is the quickest way to talk to Operators near you [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The developers say squads can truce, betray or assimilate, and an assimilated squad is capped at four players (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## Truce or assimilation?
A truce is an agreement not to fight; players report the truce function lets two squads work through a deployment together (reported) [clm_3f9c4cb39462]. Assimilation means joining the other squad after you have spawned, up to the cap of four [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [clm_5b76bd4676ec]. A fix on 29 Oct 2026 lets you pick up a hostage after assimilating, which used to fail [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Can they turn on you?
Yes: betrayal is part of the mode (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. It has a cost, though: killing players earns a Bounty, and four player kills make an Operator Wanted, which alerts nearby Operators [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[bounty-system]].

## How does proximity chat sound?
Voices fade with distance, come from the speaker's direction and echo with the surroundings [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. When talking is not wise, the Ping Wheel carries the same requests without a word [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. More: [[proximity-chat]].

## Is there an Order for it?
Yes: the DMZ Order Friendly Face asks you to request a truce with the Ping Wheel (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. See [[dmz-orders]].

## Does matchmaking help?
It tries to match squads of the same size, so a solo usually meets other solos first [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. See [[how-many-players-in-a-dmz-squad]].
