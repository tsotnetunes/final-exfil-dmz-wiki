No. Hajin has no night mode or day-night cycle at launch (developers' Q&A, as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Darkness comes from places instead: dark interiors and underground areas, where Night Vision Goggles from the loot pool help [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## What weather is there?
Sun, cloud, rainstorms, fog and mist, with snow possible (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Sudden downpours cut visibility and dense fog changes how you cross an area [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. Weather also changes how well AI soldiers see and hear you [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]]. See [[dynamic-weather]].

## How do you get NVGs?
NVGs are in the loot pool [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]], and they sit in the 3D Printer's Gear category beside gas masks and parachutes [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The Night Vision Goggles recipe comes through DMZ Orders, and players tie it to Operation Shopping Hacks, which closes an Orders chapter [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]] [clm_3086f62d37c7]. Weaker and Elite goggles exist too; their recipes have to be found in a match (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]].

## Where are they useful?
Underground areas and dark interiors such as the [[casino]] and the mall under [[nurigo-mall]]; the Codex pages for those places have the detail. From Season 1, the X01 Black Site adds tunnels and underground labs [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## Will night come later?
Nothing is announced: the Season 1 post of 28 Oct 2026 adds no night mode [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].
