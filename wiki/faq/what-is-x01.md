X01 is Season 1's new DMZ location, which Activision calls a Black Site: a weapons facility nobody knew about, believed to be a GRANIT Arms black site, now held by North Korean forces [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [clm_64b8588379af]. It arrives with Season 1 at midnight ET on 6 Nov 2026, which is 9 PM PT on 5 Nov [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## Where is it, and how do you get there?
It is a facility that used to be Russian, at the edge of Hajin near North Korea's northern border, and you deploy to it through the Hajin Exclusion Zone [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [clm_88179a380fbb].

## What is inside?
Cargo bays, vehicle depots, underground labs, production lines and a sealed Deep Vault, linked by tunnels and routes that climb several floors, with hazards that change [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. It holds equipment you cannot find anywhere else [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## Who will you meet?
Troops and specialists of the 100 Spirits faction, rogue Operator squads, tougher patrolling enemies and new enemy types not yet shown; the Commander The Captain, a Juggernaut with an elite guard, lives there [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. The Threat Briefing: The Captain event asks you to defeat him and extract pieces of his helmet [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## How is getting out different?
X01's exits shut once the facility goes into lockdown, so set steps must be done before you can leave, and it pays to know where your nearest exfil is [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Ghost hands out new DMZ Orders for it across several pages [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## Is it like Building 21?
Before launch, the developers said X01 fits DMZ the way Building 21 did in the 2022 beta (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]], and From The Ward Ep. 7 described a Season 1 map in that style [[src-gamespot-from-the-ward-ep7-2026-10-05]]. More: [[x01]] and [[season-1]].
