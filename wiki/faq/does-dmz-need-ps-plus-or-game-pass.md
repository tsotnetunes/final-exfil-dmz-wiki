On consoles, yes; on PC, no. Guides report that Modern Warfare 4's online modes need PlayStation Plus on PS5, a Game Pass tier that includes online play on Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch Online on Switch 2, while PC needs no subscription (reported) [[src-timesaver-mw4-subscriptions-2026-09-18]]. The same guide notes that free-to-play Warzone does not need these memberships, but a paid game's online play does (reported) [[src-timesaver-mw4-subscriptions-2026-09-18]]. Activision has published no rule written for DMZ alone.

## Is DMZ included in Game Pass?
No. Modern Warfare 4 did not join Game Pass at launch, so a Game Pass Ultimate or PC Game Pass membership does not give you DMZ [[src-gfinity-mw4-game-pass-2026-06-17]] [[src-windows-central-dmz-reveal-2026-06-06]] [clm_517e1fd9f5b2]. Press report it should be added around the end of 2027 (reported) [[src-gfinity-mw4-game-pass-2026-06-17]].

## Can you play DMZ offline?
No. A DMZ match puts up to 60 players on the same Hajin server (developers' Q&A, as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]], and every online mode needs an internet connection; only the Campaign plays offline once installed (reported) [[src-timesaver-mw4-subscriptions-2026-09-18]].

## What do PC players need instead?
No subscription on Battle.net, Steam or Xbox on PC (reported) [[src-timesaver-mw4-subscriptions-2026-09-18]], but TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot must be switched on and accounts get two-factor sign-in [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]]. Details: [[does-dmz-need-tpm-2-0-and-secure-boot]].

## Do you need to buy the game as well?
Yes: DMZ is part of Modern Warfare 4, not a free download [[src-windows-central-dmz-reveal-2026-06-06]]; see [[is-dmz-free]].
