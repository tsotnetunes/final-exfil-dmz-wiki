Rejoin quickly: after a crash or a disconnect you can get back into the same match as long as it is still running (developers' Q&A, as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. If you never make it out, the normal rule for a failed exfil applies: anything outside a Secure Slot is lost [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_b83a508a7fac]. Players report losing an Operator after being kicked during an exfil (reported) [clm_b6179531b351].

## Did Activision ever give gear back?
Once, for one bug. On 21 Oct 2026, during early access, Activision said that players who had dropped out of matches because of a specific disconnect issue would keep the equipment they took in and the progress they made afterwards, but not the loot they had found in that match [[src-codupdates-dmz-disconnect-gear-2026-10-21]]. No general refund for gear lost to crashes has been announced. Players report losing loot when servers crash (reported) [clm_004ae083916b].
