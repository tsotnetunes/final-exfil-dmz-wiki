The Air Base Bunker under Chang-san Air Base and the Nenshin Tech Complex in The Grid carry the top threat rating, Extreme; the rest of The Grid, the Air Base exterior and The Citadel are Critical [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. These are the expected threat levels Activision published before launch [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How does each area rate?
| Threat | Areas |
| --- | --- |
| Extreme | Air Base Bunker ([[chang-san-air-base]]), Nenshin Tech Complex ([[the-grid]]) |
| Critical | Air Base Exterior; The Citadel ([[prison-complex]]); The Grid, DBN Headquarters, Hanex Bank, Minsok Art School and the Construction Zone |

| Low | [[deadtown]] and [[imjin-farmland]] exteriors, Jeong Loading Dock, [[cheongun-village]] and its outskirts, [[mirae-general-hospital]], [[songdo-wharf]], Oamsik Turtle Island, Shoreline Station |

All ratings from Activision's Hajin guide [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What changes the danger during a match?
Each area's Threat Level sits under your compass: noise and violence raise the AI response, while stealth, cover, distance and breaking line of sight calm it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Other players are not counted in it, so a Low area can still hold a hostile squad [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The radiation spreads over time too [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Where should a new player go?
A solo guide suggests skipping Critical and Extreme areas early, since Low and Medium areas are enough to level and earn (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]]. See [[threat-escalation]], [[hajin-exclusion-zone]] and [[dmz-beginner-guide]].
