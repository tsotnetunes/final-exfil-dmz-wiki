Yes. The FOB has an Events station for limited-time challenges and unlocks, and Season 1, which starts on 6 Nov 2026, brings the first DMZ events. Threat Briefing: Battle Tank is the one the patch notes list as live from the start, and Wager Match is marked coming soon [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. Events also hand out Survival Kits [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

| Event | What you do | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[threat-briefing-battle-tank]] | destroy a North Korean battle tank in Hajin and extract its armor fragments, one part per deployment | better rewards the more parts you bring |

All from the official Season 1 post [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] and the patch notes [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. When one squad member extracts a Commander part, the whole squad gets credit [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].
