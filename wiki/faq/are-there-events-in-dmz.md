Yes. The FOB has an Events station for limited-time challenges and unlocks, and Season 1, which starts on 6 Nov 2026, brings the first DMZ events; the announcement gives no start date for each one [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Events also hand out Survival Kits [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Which DMZ events are announced?
| Event | What you do | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Threat Briefing: Battle Tank | destroy a North Korean battle tank in Hajin and extract one part per deployment | better rewards the more parts you bring |
| Threat Briefing: The Captain | defeat The Captain in X01 and extract pieces of his helmet | bigger rewards the more you extract |
| Resupply Run | collect set items around Hajin's points of interest and trade them | gear such as Self Revives and 3-Plate Carriers |
| Wager Match Event (Multiplayer) | its rewards also count in DMZ | Hyeon Auto and Kastov 545 Apex Attachments |
| Joint Fire Event, later in Season 1 | applies to DMZ | a DMZ Survival Kit |

All from the official Season 1 post [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. When one squad member extracts a Commander part, the whole squad gets credit [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## Was there a Halloween event in DMZ?
No DMZ Halloween event was announced. Halloween 2026 in Modern Warfare 4 meant two Multiplayer modes, Possession and Infected [[src-gamerfuzion-launch-roadmap-2026-10-14]] [clm_beb7bce73ff1]; see [[halloween-2026]].
