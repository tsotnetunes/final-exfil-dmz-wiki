No official or community source describes a pulse that comes from your own Operator on a timer. As of 23 Nov 2026 the Codex knows four things that send out a pulse or put you on the map, plus two display bugs that were fixed in early November, and one of them is the most likely explanation. Check what you have equipped and whether you hold a tower.

## Something you equipped
- **Combat Scanner**, a Minor Trait in the Warfighter tree: each time you down an enemy Operator it sends out one UAV pulse, which marks enemies for you [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] ([[warfighter-tree]]).
- **Kastov ARC**, an Apex Attachment for the Kastov: a disruption barrel with a built-in optic that sends out an RF pulse [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] ([[kastov-762]], [[apex-attachments]]).

## A UAV Tower you activated
An active tower pulses nearby Operators and enemies onto the map [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. With the Overwatch Trait a tower you activate never runs out, so its pulses keep coming for as long as you hold it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[overwatch]], [[uav-towers]]).

## Other players finding you
If you have killed four or more players you are Wanted: Operators nearby get an alert as you approach, paid intel at a Bounty Station draws a shrinking circle around you, and at the top level your general area can be shown to everyone in Hajin [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[wanted-status]], [[bounty-system]]). A rival's UAV Tower also shows you while you are in its range [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Bugs that looked like this
- Until the fix of 5 Nov 2026, some players received UAV pings they should not have while an enemy UAV was up [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- Until the same fix, towers held with Overwatch could show enemy marks on the compass with no players nearby [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

If you still see a repeating pulse after 5 Nov 2026 with none of the causes above, it is not explained by any source yet. Since 10 Nov 2026 UAV and Advanced UAV Killstreaks cannot be used in [[x01]] at all, so a pulse there is not a Killstreak [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
