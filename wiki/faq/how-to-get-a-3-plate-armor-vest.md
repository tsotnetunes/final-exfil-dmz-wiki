The surest way is DMZ level 37, which rewards a 3-Plate Armor Vest [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-progression-2026-10-19]]. Some pages of DMZ Orders also give one, and from Season 1 the Resupply Run event lets you trade for 3-Plate Carriers [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## What went wrong with the level 37 reward?
The vest was not being granted at level 37 until a fix on 28 Oct 2026. The fix helped players who had not yet reached the level; on 29 Oct Activision said it was working on granting the vest to players who had already passed it [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. If you passed level 37 before 28 Oct and have no vest, that is the known problem.

## Which Orders give one?
Orders pages 12 (The Grid, part 1) and 20 list a 3-Plate Armor Vest among their rewards (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. All Orders: [[dmz-orders]].

## Can you print one?
Yes, once you have the recipe: a community database lists the 3-Plate Armor Vest as a Legendary print from 2 Ballistic Fabric, 5 Nylon Cloth and 10 Reactive Metals (reported) [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. How that recipe unlocks is not documented. The first recipes you get cover the 1-Plate and 2-Plate vests; the 2-Plate needs 50 Synthetics and 5 Reactive Metals [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Two Ultra versions also exist: a 3-Plate Ballistic Gel Vest whose plates regenerate and a 3-Plate Helmet Armor Vest that softens headshots (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. See [[3d-printer]].

## Why does it matter?
Your armour plates are filled at infil, and you can only add more through Traits or the Armor Box field upgrade, so the vest decides how many plates you start with [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Community pages suggest a 3-Plate vest for the hardest Story Missions (reported) [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]].
