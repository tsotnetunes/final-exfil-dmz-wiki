They are two consumables that soften a failed run. Activision's guide lists them without explaining them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], so what follows comes from the in-game tooltips and one creator's testing as written up by GamerFuzion on 28 Oct 2026 (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-rescue-beacon-insurance-2026-10-28]]. A guide of 21 Oct still called them unexplained [[src-allthingshow-dmz-death-rules-2026-10-21]].

## What does each one do?
| Item | Effect by its tooltip | Where you switch it on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operator Rescue Beacon | Your Operator comes back at no cost if you fail to exfil, so there is no paid MIA recovery | The number beside EDIT OPERATOR on the deploy screen |
| Weapon Insurance Contract | Weapons you are holding when you die return to your Stash | The number beside EDIT LOADOUT on the deploy screen |

All of this is from [[src-gamerfuzion-rescue-beacon-insurance-2026-10-28]].

## Are they used up?
Yes, each use spends one. The creator also found that a contract is gone even when you exfil safely, and that it does not stay on, so you switch it on again for every deployment (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-rescue-beacon-insurance-2026-10-28]]. Save them for runs where the weapons or the Operator's Traits are worth protecting.

## Where do you get more?
- DMZ Orders rewards: [[dmz-orders-pages]] lists them on several pages.
- Survival Kits: the Advanced SERE Kit holds both ([[kits]]) [[src-gamerfuzion-rescue-beacon-insurance-2026-10-28]].
- The Vault Edition Deployment Kit: two of each [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What do they not cover?
Nothing here says they protect your backpack or armor, so plan on losing what sits outside Secure Slots as usual ([[death-and-loss]]). Paid recovery without a beacon: [[how-to-get-operators-back-from-mia]].
