Your Player Level does. Activision's post of 2 Nov 2026 says that on 6 Nov Player Level resets, so you can level up again and, if you want, Prestige, across Multiplayer, DMZ and Warzone [[src-activision-warzone-season-1-blog-2026-11-02]] [clm_ec947898aaaf]. One account's summary says levels go back to 1, which the post does not spell out (reported) [clm_03bac0765ec2].

## Does the DMZ level reset too?
Not stated. MW4 has a shared Player Level from 1 to 55 that DMZ XP feeds, and a separate DMZ level from 1 to 70 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The post names only Player Level, so do not read it as a reset of the 1 to 70 track until the Season 1 patch notes say so.

## Do you lose your Stash, Operators or Traits?
Neither Season 1 post mentions it [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [[src-activision-warzone-season-1-blog-2026-11-02]]. Operator Prestige is a different system, chosen per Operator ([[operator-prestige]]). The wider question is on [[does-dmz-wipe]].
