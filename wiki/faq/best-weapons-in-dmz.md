## Why might a list be wrong for you?
DMZ has its own damage tuning, built around its armour and plate carriers, so Multiplayer rankings do not carry straight over (developers' Q&A, as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Lists also move with balance patches; the weapon changes of 28 Oct 2026 were listed under Multiplayer [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Can you get these guns?
You need a weapon's Manual to buy it at the Gunsmith, or you find it in Hajin [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[how-to-unlock-weapons-in-dmz]]. A base M4 costs about 5,000 DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The Kastov 762, ISO Nightshade and Rezi 12 are also Vault Edition Signature Blueprints [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
