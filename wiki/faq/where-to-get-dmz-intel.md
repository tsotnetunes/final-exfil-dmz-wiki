In DMZ, intel means three things you can buy or read: Lieutenant intel at the Boss Board, Wanted-player intel at Bounty Stations in Hajin, and free Commander information on the Boss Board [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. For news about the mode, the official sources are Activision's Call of Duty blog, the patch notes and Infinity Ward's Trello board [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [[src-iw-mw4-trello-2026-10-19]].

## What intel is there in the game?
| Intel | Where | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Lieutenant's location | Boss Board at the FOB | DMZ Cash, lasts one deployment [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] |
| The nearest Lieutenant | Lieutenant Hunt tower terminal in Hajin | free [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] |
| Wanted players in your match | Bounty Station in Hajin | the list is free; a tracking circle costs more for higher Wanted levels [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] |
| Commanders | Boss Board | free; no intel can be bought [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] |
