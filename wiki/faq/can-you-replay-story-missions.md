Yes. Story Missions can be selected and played again [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and replays pay DMZ Cash, which Activision raised on 29 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. A community guide reports that the cash and items pay out again on each replay, while one-time unlocks such as infil vehicles do not repeat (reported) [[src-dmztracker-story-missions-2026-10-20]].

## What is the random mission option?
The Deploy station has a random-mission option [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], which picks only from missions you have finished (reported) [[src-dmztracker-story-missions-2026-10-20]]. A weekly Random Mission Deploy challenge counts toward the Philanthropist Order on page 19 [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]; a fix on 24 Oct 2026 made that Order count weekly challenges completed before it unlocked [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Who will you meet on a replay?
At launch your squad was matched with players who queued for the same mission (reported) [[src-dmztracker-story-missions-2026-10-20]]. After the 28 Oct 2026 update, press reported that replays of finished missions go into regular lobbies with more players [[src-ixbt-dmz-pvp-matchmaking-2026-10-30]] [[src-detonated-dmz-story-matchmaking-2026-10-29]], and players then reported Story Mission matchmaking back on launch settings (reported) [clm_2738483c8aa0]. None of this is in the patch notes. Details: [[pvp-matchmaking-prioritization]].

## Was there a replay exploit?
Yes: a fix on 20 Oct 2026 stopped players getting double completion credit for Story Missions [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
