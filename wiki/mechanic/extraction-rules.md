You keep only what you carry out of Hajin: a successful exfil sends your gear to the Stash and your DMZ Cash to the Bank, while failing to get out costs you everything except what is in Secure Slots [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Where the exfils are and how to call them: [[exfil]].

## What do you get when you extract?

- Gear and weapons go to your [[stash]]. Each weapon you bring out is kept as one copy that you can modify at the [[gunsmith]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- The DMZ Cash you carry goes into the Bank, as the After Action Report shows [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The Bank has a cap, and codmunity warns that cash beyond it is lost without a warning (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- Recipes and Weapon Manuals are used up at exfil: each becomes a permanent unlock and pays 5,000 DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[weapon-manuals]], [[3d-printer]]).
- A Lieutenant's Dog Tag Case only advances the [[boss-board]] once you extract with it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- A Bounty is paid only after you extract with the target's Dog Tag [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-cod-dmz-bounty-systems-video-2026-10-08]]; see [[bounty-system]].
- If you went in with the Free Loadout, you keep it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What do you lose if you do not get out?

Everything outside [[secure-slots]] is lost and your Operator goes MIA ([[mia-system]]), but the DMZ XP you earned still counts [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The full list is on [[death-and-loss]].

## How does boarding work?

- Call the helicopter, then hold the site against the rising enemy response until it lands, and board it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Players report about 90 seconds from the call to touchdown (reported) [clm_0654da754a95].
- Stepping aboard starts a countdown: about 5 seconds for a solo; for a squad it runs 30 to 45 seconds until everyone is on, then drops to 5 [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Another guide says the helicopter leaves 30 seconds after landing, or 5 seconds after the whole squad is aboard [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]].
- You are not safe until it leaves: players report being killed inside the exfil helicopter (reported) [clm_cf56ba61e6f8], and other squads ambush exfil sites [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- The Die Hard Trait calls the exfil for you if you reach it while in Last Stand [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Is Dynamic Operation cash banked without an exfil?

Not confirmed. Some players report that the cash from a finished Dynamic Operation goes into your Bank at once, with no exfil needed (reported) [clm_f17f3ff635f7], but other reports dispute it. Footage shown before launch had Op payouts going to the Bank [[src-fpsjp-dynamic-ops-trailer-2026-10-07]]. See [[dynamic-operations]].
