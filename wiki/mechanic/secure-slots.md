A Secure Slot is a protected space in a backpack: whatever is in it survives a failed exfil, while everything else you carry is lost [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_b83a508a7fac]. Players also call it the safe pocket [[src-skycoach-dmz-beginner-guide-2026-10-19]] [clm_4f7ad24e07bb].

## Which backpacks have Secure Slots?

Better backpacks add slots, weapon slots and Secure Slots [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The 8-slot Basic Pack you carry without a backpack has none, and the Medium Backpack has one (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [clm_0a8ddc7fe171]. You can print a Medium Backpack from DMZ level 2 [[src-callofduty-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]; the 9 Oct guide gives its recipe as 50 Synthetics and 5 Rigid Synthetics [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Larger backpacks come later ([[dmz-progression]]).

## What should go in a Secure Slot?

- **Keys**, so you keep them if you die before reaching their room (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]; see [[keys]].
- **Mission items** you must bring back. Since a fix of 28 Oct 2026 you can upgrade the Airbase Access Card in its Story Mission even while it sits in the Secure Slot [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- **Your most valuable small find**, such as a Recipe or a [[weapon-manuals]] page. Players report that a Weapon Manual extracted from the safe pocket does not count toward the War Spoils camo ([[war-spoils-camo]]) [clm_4f7ad24e07bb].

## What do you still lose?

Everything outside the Secure Slot: weapons in hand, armor, the rest of the backpack and carried cash. The Operator also goes MIA ([[mia-system]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Full list: [[what-carries-over]].
