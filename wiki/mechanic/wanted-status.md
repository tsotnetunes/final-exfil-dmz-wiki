Wanted is the Bounty state you reach after four player kills, counted across deployments [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_8c1bbf558597]. One report puts the same moment at a 25,000 Bounty (reported) [clm_0873af25017e]. There are several Wanted levels, and the highest are called Most Wanted [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How the Bounty itself works: [[bounty-system]].

## What happens when you are Wanted?

- Operators near you get an alert when you come close [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Any player at a Bounty Station sees you on the free list of Wanted Operators in the deployment. Paid intel puts a circle around you that shrinks and updates faster the higher your Wanted level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- At the top level your general area can be shown to every Operator in Hajin [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- Your Dog Tag pays more to whoever extracts it, with an extra step at the maximum Wanted level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- You can appear on the weekly Most Wanted board at the FOB ([[bounty-leaderboard]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do you stop being Wanted?

You lose Wanted status when you die, when another player extracts with your Dog Tag, or when you pay off your Bounty with DMZ Cash at a Bounty Station [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-thexclusiveace-bounty-video-2026-10-12]]. If you want a quiet deployment, paying it off before you head for a busy area removes the alerts and the circle.

## Is it the same as the AI wanted level?

No. The meter that rises at the top right of the HUD as you fight AI is a separate thing (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. AI kills fill the Hostiles Kill Count, which sends a Hunt Squad after you ([[marked-for-death]]), and each area has its own Threat Level ([[threat-escalation]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
