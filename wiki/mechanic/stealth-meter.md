The Stealth Meter warns you before an AI enemy spots you: a HUD indicator moves from white to yellow to orange as detection gets closer, and a sound plays, so you have a moment to hide before the area is alerted [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-solo-play-2026-10-03]].

## Who is it for?

Solo players. In their 9 Oct 2026 Q&A the developers named it, with the [[tourniquet]], as one of the tools added for people playing alone [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]], and before launch they told Screen Rant it is meant entirely for solos [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-solo-play-2026-10-03]]. codmunity describes it as the solo player's gauge of how visible they are [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. It reacts to AI soldiers [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-solo-play-2026-10-03]]; whether squads see it too is not confirmed. See [[squads-and-solo-play]].

## How do you stay unseen?

- When the meter starts to fill, go prone or get behind cover; that can make the soldier lose you [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-solo-play-2026-10-03]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].
- Kill quietly. Melee, throwing knives and suppressed shots let you cross dangerous areas without raising the alarm [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Before launch, suppressed kills were said not to raise your star level [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-solo-play-2026-10-03]].
- Keep to shadows, move quietly and get out of sight and earshot; quiet rural ground also calms an area's [[threat-escalation]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- Weather changes how well AI soldiers see and hear [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]]; see [[dynamic-weather]].
- Traits: [[viper-sense]] keeps you off thermal scopes and out of AI targeting, and Thermal Padding keeps you off UAV sweeps [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What happens if you are seen?

Being spotted or fighting in an area brings retaliation and reinforcements, and killing enough AI in one deployment fills the Hostiles Kill Count and sends a Hunt Squad after you ([[marked-for-death]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Before launch a developer said internal testers tried to finish all DMZ content without being seen, and that full stealth runs are possible [[src-destructoid-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].

## Fixes

On 29 Oct 2026 Infinity Ward stopped the enemy called the Scavenger from spotting players the moment they deployed [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What is not known yet?

How fast the meter fills, what fills it besides being seen, and whether noise counts.
