The After Action Report appears after every deployment, whether you extracted or not, and has four pages [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What is on each page?

- **Report:** what you brought back (valuables, 3D Printer Ingredients, rare finds) and the DMZ Cash you picked up or earned, which goes into your Bank ([[wallet]]); the Backpack and Loadout contents are listed too [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- **Rating:** a grade from F to S++ built from Objectives, Combat and Looting, with totals for AI kills, missions completed, loot containers opened and Side Ops completed, each with its XP [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- **Level:** the XP added to your DMZ Player Level and to the Operator's Dog Tag level ([[dmz-progression]], [[dog-tags]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- **Summary:** the DMZ Orders you completed and their rewards; weapon level-ups follow back at the [[fob]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What earns a good rating?

An S rating or better earns the best rewards [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]. One fan site's logs show these lines on the Rating page (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-xp-strategies-2026-10-30]]: a Deployment Bonus of 450 XP for every minute in the match, an Extracted Bonus of 500 XP plus a fifth of the Deployment Bonus, 12,500 for a Story Mission, 4,000 to 8,000 for a Dynamic Op, 1,000 for each extracted Dog Tag and about 75 for each kill or loot container. The same site saw the Rating page mark Double XP as active while a token ran ([[double-xp-tokens]]).

## Do you get XP if you die?

Yes. XP is awarded even with an F rating or a failed exfil [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; only the Extracted Bonus is lost (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-xp-strategies-2026-10-30]]. Cash and gear you carried are a different story: [[what-carries-over]].
