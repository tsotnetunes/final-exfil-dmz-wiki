<!-- human:start -->
Every DMZ match runs against a radiation storm: the HUD's Time Remaining counts down from about 30 minutes after you land, then radiation spreads across Hajin and closes exfils until only the [[final-exfil]] is left [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]].

## How long do you have?

Players saw the countdown start at 30:00, 29:57 and 28:49 on landing, so plan on about 30 minutes [[src-gamerfuzion-underground-orders-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-orders-page-14-2026-10-30]] [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]]. At zero the HUD shows a Radiation Spreading timer, and the radiation takes just under 15 more minutes to cover the whole map [clm_a15e34798a66] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]]; one run saw 8:55 on that second timer when it appeared (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-orders-page-14-2026-10-30]]. So a full match lasts about 45 minutes at most.

## Where does the radiation come from?

The [[tac-map]] shows where it is spreading [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. It starts from a circle marked at the start of the match and grows out toward the map edge (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]; in one early access match it spread from the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] [[src-allthingshow-hajin-gameplay-2026-10-21]]. Exfil sites close as it reaches them, the nearest first, and the final exfil appears in white once the rest are gone [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]] [clm_c772bf67ab75].

## How do you survive radiation?

- A gas mask slows the damage [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]]. Since a fix of 23 Oct 2026, gas masks, Durable ones included, no longer break the moment you enter a radiation zone [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- In the reactor's radiation a purple mask lasted about three minutes and a blue one about a minute and a half (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-reactor-02-2026-10-27]].
- Radiation Blockers, printed at the 3D Printer, reduce its effects (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-gamerfuzion-reactor-02-2026-10-27]]. Players say a Hazmat Suit lets you walk the reactor area unharmed but is lost on exfil [clm_f655e133d8dd] [clm_f28fa8554173].
- Fire Stations hold radiation gear ([[fire-stations]]), and the squad panel shows who carries a gas mask [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

The reactor's own radiation is permanent and separate from the storm; its red zone eats gas masks fast [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. See [[exfil]].
