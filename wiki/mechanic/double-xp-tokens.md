**What they are.** A 2XP token doubles the XP you earn while it runs; a Dual 2XP reward gives two separate tokens, one for player XP and one for weapon XP [[src-activision-mw4-bonus-content-2026-10-23]]. You switch a token on when you choose, and once its time is used it is gone.

**When it runs.** In Modern Warfare 4 a token counts down in game time, so menus and lobbies do not use it up, and tokens held from earlier games carry over [[src-cod-2xp-game-time-2026-09-28]]. Players report that 2XP tokens work in DMZ [clm_7be12d197052].

**Limits.** At most one hour of each token type can be used a day, and an account can hold at most 40 hours of each token type across all promotions; bonus content can take up to 24 hours to show up in game [[src-activision-mw4-bonus-content-2026-10-23]]. No expiry date for unused tokens has been published.

**Where they come from.** Campaign rewards ([[active-duty]]), Monster Energy codes (one code a day; the first six redemptions give 15-minute tokens) [[src-activision-mw4-bonus-content-2026-10-23]], edition bonuses, and giveaways on X. The launch giveaway asked players to post their Activision ID with a tag of the Call of Duty account and the keyword MW4 2XP by 26 Oct 2026 at 10 AM PT; that date is the deadline to enter, and the post does not say how much 2XP it gives or when it arrives [[src-cod-x-mw4-2xp-giveaway-2026-10-23]]. The same giveaway for Black Ops 7 in November 2025 gave two one-hour tokens (reported, unverified for MW4).

**Not to confuse with.** A Double XP event or weekend raises XP for everyone without a token; none had been announced for MW4 by 24 Oct 2026 (reported, unverified).
