DMZ moves and shoots like the rest of Modern Warfare 4, with two differences the developers point out: tactical sprint is always on, and weapon damage has its own tuning for DMZ's armor [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How does movement work?

- **Tac sprint** is on by default in DMZ [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. In Multiplayer, a 21 Oct change made the Blood Rush perk start tactical sprint whenever you press sprint [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- **Stamina:** energy drinks, stim shots and smelling salts refill or extend it ([[health-and-healing]]) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
- The Adrenaline Burst Trait resets tactical sprint after a kill ([[adrenaline-burst]]).

## Is weapon damage the same as in Multiplayer?

No. In the 9 Oct Q&A the developers said DMZ has its own weapon damage tuning, made with its armor and plate carriers in mind [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; a creator quoting the same answer says it is close to Warzone's [clm_34cf7998ef77]. The 23 Oct patch notes repeat that Multiplayer and DMZ each have bespoke weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. There are no armor-piercing or Stopping Power rounds as field upgrades for now [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
