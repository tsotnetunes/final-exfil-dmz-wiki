DMZ moves and shoots like the rest of Modern Warfare 4, with two differences the developers point out: tactical sprint is always on, and weapon damage has its own tuning for DMZ's armor [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How does movement work?

- **Tac sprint** is on by default in DMZ [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. In Multiplayer, a 21 Oct change made the Blood Rush perk start tactical sprint whenever you press sprint [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- **Stamina:** energy drinks, stim shots and smelling salts refill or extend it ([[health-and-healing]]) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
- The Adrenaline Burst Trait resets tactical sprint after a kill ([[adrenaline-burst]]).

## Is weapon damage the same as in Multiplayer?

No. In the 9 Oct Q&A the developers said DMZ has its own weapon damage tuning, made with its armor and plate carriers in mind [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; a creator quoting the same answer says it is close to Warzone's [clm_34cf7998ef77]. The 23 Oct patch notes repeat that Multiplayer and DMZ each have bespoke weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. There are no armor-piercing or Stopping Power rounds as field upgrades for now [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## What did DMZ's own weapon tuning change on 23 Nov 2026?

On 23 Nov 2026 Infinity Ward added the first weapon tuning section for DMZ to the Season 1 notes, listed under the DMZ heading and with DMZ's own damage figures [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. It slows the time to kill with automatic weapons and rewards long shots:

- Nine assault rifles lost 4 to 14% of their damage at the ranges listed: [[axion]], [[cronen-osprey-assault-rifle]], [[han-86]], [[hyeon-burst]], [[kastov-762]], [[m4]], [[cronen-squall]], [[oden]] and [[patriot-xmr]]. The Hyeon Burst, Kastov 762 and Cronen Squall lost the most.
- Six SMGs lost 4 to 14%: [[csg-35-smg]], [[iso-nightshade]], [[ppsh-41]], [[vel-46]], [[wz-55-striga]] and [[x-58-nyx]].
- Three LMGs lost 10 to 15%: [[apoc-338]], [[finn-lmg]] and [[type-73]].
- The [[signal-50]] went the other way: first middle damage from 145 to 180, minimum damage from 140 to 168 and the head multiplier from 1.3 to 1.6.

Shotguns, pistols, marksman rifles and the other snipers had no line. Each weapon page has its table.

## Where do shots go, and how fast?

In Modern Warfare 4 a shot leaves from the weapon's barrel instead of landing at random inside a spread, both aimed and from the hip; a laser attachment shows where shots travel (official, 29 Sep, for the whole game) [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]]. No bullet velocity figures for DMZ are published; the only velocity change for one weapon so far is a cut to the Oden's Sentinel Apex on 28 Oct, listed under Multiplayer ([[oden]]) [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What changed with hitscan on 11 Nov 2026?
Since 11 Nov 2026 every bullet weapon hits instantly, with no travel time, out to 3.91 times the old distance. Infinity Ward puts the new true hitscan range at about 35 meters on average, 288% further than at launch, so close fights register more directly. Past that range bullets still travel, but a new long-range simulation makes them connect 34 ms sooner, which should make rifle fights at mid and long range feel steadier. The change is listed under Global, so it applies in DMZ as well as Multiplayer and Warzone [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## Is there a problem with semi-auto weapons?
Yes, for now. On the evening of 11 Nov 2026 Activision said it is investigating intermittent delays when firing semi-automatic weapons in Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone; no fix date or workaround was given [[src-codupdates-x-semi-auto-delay-2026-11-11]].
## Did diving change on 2 Nov 2026?

Yes, in every mode: Infinity Ward removed a temporary exploit fix, which gives back the 23% of dive speed it had cost, and closed a way to stand up from prone with no animation. Small visual bugs remain until a later update [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
