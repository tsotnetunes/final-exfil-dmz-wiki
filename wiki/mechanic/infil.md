Infil is how you get into Hajin. At the FOB's [[deploy]] station you choose what to play and ready up, and a heavy transport helicopter takes you in; you land on foot at a random spot for free, or pay for a vehicle drop, a helicopter of your own or a plane jump to a spot you pick [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What do you choose before you go in?

1. The deployment: a [[story-missions]] mission, a [[dynamic-operations]] Op or [[free-roam]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]. The matchmaker groups players who chose the same thing [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
2. Ready Up: take the Free Loadout or edit your own loadout, swap your Active Duty Operator, and set Squad Fill [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[squads-and-solo-play]]).
3. For Dynamic Operations and Free Roam, the way in [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]:

| Infil | DMZ Cash | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On foot | free | lands you at a random spot |
| Polaris Dagor A1 drop | 5,000 | a squad off-roader ([[vehicles]]); listed as a reward of the Story Mission Operation Silent Ledger (reported) [[src-dmzhub-vehicles-2026-10-31]] |
| LTV drop | 25,000 | unlocked by the Story Mission Operation Iron Citadel [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-vehicles-2026-10-31]] |
| Helicopter from the FOB | 50,000 | one of your squad flies it; unlocked by the Story Mission Operation Grid Assault [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-vehicles-2026-10-31]] |
| Cargo plane jump | 100,000 | you choose where to drop; listed as a reward of the Story Mission Operation Fallen Ember (reported) [[src-dmzhub-vehicles-2026-10-31]] |

Some of these need progression first [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; the Story Mission unlocks above come from community sources, not an official list. Prices and changes are tracked on [[paid-infil]].

## What does the Free Loadout give you?

A basic kit at no cost: one weapon, a Lethal, a Tactical, a melee weapon and one armour plate, with no backpack (only the basic 8 slots) and no parachute [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Another guide describes it as a 1-plate vest, a rifle, a pistol and a couple of grenades [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]]. You keep it if you extract [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What happens when you land?

- All squads drop in at the same time, at least for now [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-60-players-2026-10-12]].
- Infil tops up the plates, ammo, Lethals and Tacticals you have equipped, but not what is in your backpack (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- Each Story Mission has its own spawn points (reported) [clm_94e4a795c9a7].
- One guide says a vehicle waits at every spawn (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]].
- Without an equipped parachute a long fall can kill you, and one carried in your backpack does not count as equipped (reported) [clm_b9d5f0d9bab7] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## What can stop you deploying?

- Items left in the Overflow Stash: since 25 Oct 2026 you must sort them before your next deployment [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- Fixed on 29 Oct 2026: you could not choose the Free Loadout while your Stash was full, even with an empty Operator [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- Fixed on 28 Oct 2026: adding items to your Backpack at the 3D Printer could send you in without your Free Loadout weapons and gear [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Which infil problems were fixed?

On 20 and 21 Oct 2026 Infinity Ward stopped enemy AI vehicles from running players over during infil, including in the NK Wilderness, and on 29 Oct the Scavenger stopped spotting players the moment they deployed [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
