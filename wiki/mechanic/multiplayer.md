Multiplayer is Modern Warfare 4's competitive mode. It is separate from DMZ, but the two share some progress: weapon levels and shared attachment unlocks count in both, and DMZ XP raises the 1 to 55 player level, while Multiplayer XP never raises your DMZ level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What do the two modes share?
- Weapon levels and shared attachment unlocks [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- The player level from 1 to 55, which DMZ XP also fills [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Campaign rewards, such as the eight base Operators and the Double XP tokens, usable in both from launch [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]].

## What stays apart?
- DMZ weapon builds: a fix on 24 Oct 2026 stopped them showing as usable in Core Multiplayer [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- Tuning: DMZ has its own damage tuning built around its armour, and tactical sprint is on by default in DMZ while Multiplayer ties it to a perk (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
- Camos: DMZ has its own camos and camo grind [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## Which launch bugs crossed between the modes?
- Prestiging from the DMZ FOB did not reset Multiplayer loadouts properly until a fix on 28 Oct; a known issue remains for players who entered Prestige from DMZ menus [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- Killstreak indicators from a Multiplayer match could carry into DMZ until 23 Oct [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- Match completion events were not recorded properly in Multiplayer and DMZ until 21 Oct [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What changed in Multiplayer after launch?
XP for a kill in Search and Destroy and Collateral rose from 100 to 300 on 25 Oct, and XP rates rose across several modes on 29 Oct [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Shipment returned on 27 Oct [clm_31e5b4ec0040], and Possession and Infected came for Halloween [[src-gamerfuzion-launch-roadmap-2026-10-14]].
