DMZ's first week was hit by server crashes and disconnects, and players who dropped out of a match often lost the loadout and gear they had with them [[src-gfinity-dmz-tech-issues-2026-10-22]] [clm_004ae083916b] [clm_9863414622be]. Infinity Ward released server-side changes against the disconnects on 21 Oct 2026, and almost every patch-note update since has listed more stability and crash fixes [clm_2e81dbaa4c19] [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What happens to your gear if you crash?

If you crash or lose connection, the developers said you should be able to rejoin while that match is still running [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-60-players-2026-10-12]]. If you cannot get back in, players report losing the run's loot, as in any failed exfil [clm_004ae083916b] [[src-gfinity-dmz-tech-issues-2026-10-22]]; what a failed run costs is on [[death-and-loss]]. We have found no announcement of compensation or restored items for gear lost to crashes (31 Oct 2026).

## What is still open?

- On PC, some players hit a network error as they extracted carrying a Dog Tag they had looted: a known issue in the 24 Oct notes [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]], acknowledged by the developers on 27 Oct [clm_6155f83bd127].
- Final Exfil's known-issues tracker on 31 Oct 2026: general instability in DMZ acknowledged by the developers on 30 Oct; server crashes with loot loss and unstable connections confirmed by several players since 21 Oct; single reports of crashes at exfil, during Story Missions and while loading in, some with lost gear (reported, unverified).

## Which settings affect performance?

- PS5 and Xbox Series X|S: DMZ's Performance Mode needs a display with Variable Refresh Rate [clm_bf0928b1d51a], and players add that 120 FPS also needs a 120 Hz display (reported) [clm_31dd8c9184fe].
- PC: DMZ supports ray tracing and the DLSS 4.5, FSR Redstone and XeSS 3 upscalers [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]]. Requirements and security settings: [[modern-warfare-4]] and [[ricochet-anti-cheat]].
