DMZ gives you two ways to communicate: voice, which nearby players outside your squad can hear through proximity chat, and the Ping Wheel, which sends silent visual commands such as a truce request [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What does the Ping Wheel do?

It gives you visual commands for when voice is not available or would give you away. From it you can ask another squad for a truce or to join up with them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. What happens after a truce or a join: [[squads-and-solo-play]].

## What can you ping?

- Places on the full Tac-Map: you set and clear pings there, and you can ping DMZ Order objectives so your squad sees them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[tac-map]]).
- Loot containers. Items you need for an Order are marked for you automatically; a bug that wiped those markers after you pinged a container was fixed on 28 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- On PC the inventory's Split key follows your custom Ping key; until a fix on 24 Oct 2026 the prompt did not show that, so players thought they could not split item stacks [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How does voice work?

- Proximity chat is the quickest way to reach nearby Operators [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; voices fade with distance, come from a direction and echo off the surroundings ([[proximity-chat]]) [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. Players use it to team up or to betray each other [[src-destructoid-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].
- Party voice works across platforms, and you add friends by Activision ID [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]].

## Which alerts does the game give you?

- Your Team Leader warns you when a Commander is close [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Operators near a Wanted player get an alert when that player comes close, and at the top Wanted level a Killer's general area can show to everyone [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; before launch the developers also described a banner for a nearby bounty target [[src-gamingtrend-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].
- Picking up a Lieutenant's Dog Tag Case marks you on every Operator's Tac-Map [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- Players report a distinct sound when a downed player gives up (reported) [clm_05f439dded08].

What the rest of the screen shows is on [[hud]].
