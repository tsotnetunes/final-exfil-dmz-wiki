Free Roam is the deployment with no assigned objective: you drop into Hajin and decide for yourself what to do [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]. It is one of the three deployment types, alongside [[story-missions]] and [[dynamic-operations]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]].

## What can you do in Free Roam?
- Loot, fight and exfil whenever you like [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].
- Start a Dynamic Operation from a Dynamic Ops phone in the field [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- Take Side Ops: lost Supply Drops, truck repairs, radio towers, UAV towers and SAM sites [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- Hunt Lieutenants through a Lieutenant Hunt tower or Boss Board intel [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; see [[how-to-find-lieutenants]].
- Work on DMZ Orders; before launch the developers said Orders done in Free Roam earn progress toward Story Missions [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]].

## How do you get in?
On foot for free at a random spot, or by paid infil: Polaris Dagor A1 drop 5,000, LTV drop 25,000, helicopter 50,000, or a cargo plane drop at a spot you choose for 100,000 DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[can-you-choose-where-you-infil]].

## Who will you meet?
Matchmaking groups players who queued for the same kind of deployment (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]], and press reported that Free Roam squads top up the lobbies of other activities (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-things-to-know-2026-10-03]]. After the 28 Oct 2026 update, Story Mission queues were reported landing in Free Roam and Dynamic Ops lobbies [[src-detonated-dmz-story-matchmaking-2026-10-29]]; see [[pvp-matchmaking-prioritization]].
