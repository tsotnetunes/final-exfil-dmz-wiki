When you are killed in DMZ your Operator goes MIA and the gear you took in stays in Hajin, except what sits in Secure Slots; your DMZ XP, your DMZ level, the Stash and the Bank are safe [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-death-rules-2026-10-21]] [clm_15b35293c60f].

## What happens when you go down?

- You fall into Last Stand. A squadmate can revive you, and so can a Self-Revive Kit, a Revive Pistol or a Defibrillator, none of which leaves a wound [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- With no self-revive you can use the [[tourniquet]]: you get up at 100 health with a wound, and it works again only after a Medkit repairs you [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Details: [[health-and-healing]].
- Last Stand has a bleed-out timer, which the Die Hard Trait removes [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Enemies can stop you getting up: before launch the developers said a melee hit interrupts a tourniquet or self-revive, and that a downed player can be dragged away from their squad [[src-gamingtrend-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].
- Players report a distinct sound when a downed player gives up (reported) [clm_05f439dded08].
- The [[possum]] Trait brings you back into Last Stand once per match after you are killed; since 28 Oct 2026 it stays locked while your killers' squad is close by, or until 4 minutes pass [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What do you lose and what do you keep?

| You lose | You keep |
| --- | --- |
| The loadout you took in, Free Loadout included | DMZ XP and your DMZ level, even on a failed exfil |
| Backpack contents outside Secure Slots | Items in [[secure-slots]] |
| Any Blueprint you carried in (you can buy it back at a discount) | The [[stash]], the Bank and your crafting resources |
| Your Bounty and Wanted status | The Operator's Traits, if you pay to recover them |
| The Operator, until you recover or dismiss them | |

## What happens to your body?

Your gear stays where you fell for anyone to loot [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]], and vultures circle over Operators who died without getting out, so others can spot the place from afar [[src-cod-dmz-bounty-systems-video-2026-10-08]]. Your killer can take your Dog Tag, and if they extract with it they collect any Bounty on you ([[dog-tags]], [[bounty-system]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. codmunity adds that a body normally turns into a loot bag; one that stays a body, with no vultures overhead and plates you cannot take, may belong to a Possum user about to get up (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## How do you get your Operator back?

Pay DMZ Cash at the FOB to recover the MIA Operator with all their Traits but none of their gear, or dismiss them for free and recruit a new one, whose Dog Tag starts again at Steel I [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-death-rules-2026-10-21]]. The price rises with the Operator's Trait Points; codmunity reports figures from a few hundred DMZ Cash up to about 90,000 at 18 points (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Recovery discounts can be printed at the [[3d-printer]], and the Vault Edition kit includes Operator Rescue Beacons and Weapon Insurance Contracts [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], whose exact rules are not published. Everything about recovery: [[mia-system]].

## What if the game crashes?

You can rejoin a match that is still running [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Players who could not get back report losing the run's loot [clm_004ae083916b]; see [[server-and-performance-issues]]. Every deployment, extracted or not, ends with an After Action Report [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[after-action-report]]).
