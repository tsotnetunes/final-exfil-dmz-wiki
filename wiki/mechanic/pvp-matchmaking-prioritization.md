A matchmaking change that players and press reported, and Infinity Ward has not confirmed. After the update of 28 Oct 2026, Story Mission queues stopped putting squads in sparse lobbies with other Story Mission squads and sent them into fuller Free Roam or Dynamic Ops lobbies, with UAV towers switched on early [[src-detonated-dmz-story-matchmaking-2026-10-29]]. Charlie Intel, citing its own sources, reported that Infinity Ward had given PvP more weight in DMZ matchmaking and that replays of finished Story Missions now go to regular lobbies (as relayed) [[src-ixbt-dmz-pvp-matchmaking-2026-10-30]] [clm_bbaff371a6b3].

## Is it confirmed?
No. The patch notes of 28 to 30 Oct list no DMZ matchmaking change [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]], and Infinity Ward had not commented by 30 Oct [[src-detonated-dmz-story-matchmaking-2026-10-29]] [[src-ixbt-dmz-pvp-matchmaking-2026-10-30]].

## Was it reverted?
Players reported on 30 Oct that an update appeared to reset Story Mission lobbies, and on 31 Oct that Story Missions were back on launch settings (reported) [clm_1c0c758f3464] [clm_2738483c8aa0]. There is no official word either way, and reports still disagree on 2 Nov: replays reportedly go to regular lobbies [clm_2fb9802e1cca], while a first solo attempt at a Story Mission is reported to be private (reported) [clm_0b415b70b5df].

## How did matchmaking work at launch?
You pick Story Missions, Dynamic Operations or Free Roam when you queue and are grouped with players who picked the same; squads are matched by size where possible (developers' Q&A, as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. A community guide reports that at launch your squad shared a Story Mission match with others who queued for it and no other squad ran the same mission (reported) [[src-dmztracker-story-missions-2026-10-20]].
