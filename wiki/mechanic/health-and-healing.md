**As the developers explained in their 9 Oct Q&A, via a video recap [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].** Maximum health is 150. Every Operator deploys able to use a [[tourniquet]]: from Last Stand it gets you back up at 100 health with a wound, and it cannot be used again until you are back at full health, so going down again while wounded needs a squadmate's revive. A Bandage lifts a wounded Operator to 150 for a short while before the wound pulls health back to 100; a Medkit repairs the wound and keeps you at 150. Self-revive kits can be looted in Hajin and printed at the [[3d-printer]].

**What the official guide adds** [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The Medkit's recipe text: it repairs wounds, restores full health and lets you use the tourniquet again; the Bandage's: it temporarily restores max health when wounded. Health still regenerates, since the Quick Fix Trait shortens the regeneration delay ([[quick-fix]]).

**Stamina.** Energy drinks, stim shots and smelling salts refill or extend it ([[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]).
