Your maximum health in DMZ is 150, and the system turns on wounds: once you have used the [[tourniquet]] to get up from Last Stand, you are wounded and capped at 100 until a Medkit repairs it [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do wounds work?

- **Tourniquet.** Every Operator deploys able to use it; it gets you up at 100 health with a wound and cannot be used again until you are back at full health, so going down again while wounded needs a squadmate or a revive item [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
- **Bandage.** It lifts a wounded Operator to 150 for a while, then the wound pulls health back to 100 [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; one database puts the effect at 180 seconds (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- **Medkit.** It repairs the wound, restores full health and lets you use the tourniquet again [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Its recipe can be pinned at the [[3d-printer]] [clm_3fc45e2c36d0].
- **Revive items.** Self-Revive Kits can be looted in Hajin and printed [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; one guide says they, the Defibrillator and the Revive Pistol get you up without a wound (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Every item is compared on [[medical-items]].

## Does health regenerate?

Yes. The Quick Fix Trait shortens the delay before regeneration starts after a kill [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[quick-fix]]), and a Stim starts regeneration at once [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-equipment-2026-10-19]].

## Which Traits change healing?

From the Survivalist tree: Free Revive repairs your first Wound automatically; Faster Revive is in the same tree; Numb the Pain gives revived players the effects of Painkillers and Smelling Salts; Consumable Duration makes consumables last longer; Die Hard removes the Last Stand bleed-out timer and calls an exfil automatically if you crawl into one [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. From the Warfighter tree, Possum revives you into Last Stand once per match [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; since 28 Oct 2026 it cannot fire while the squad that killed you is nearby, or before four minutes have passed [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] ([[possum]]).

## What about stamina?

Energy drinks, stim shots and smelling salts refill or extend it [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; one database gives the Energy Drink and Smelling Salts 60 seconds of unlimited stamina each (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
