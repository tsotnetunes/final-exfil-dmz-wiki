The Tac-Map is DMZ's full map of Hajin: it shows your objectives, the POIs, dangerous zones and enemy Compounds, useful buildings and caches ([[locations-of-interest]]), where radiation is spreading ([[radiation-storm]]), and you, nearby enemies and your squad's pings. From it you ping, cancel your current Op and read your [[dmz-orders]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How does the grid work?

Letters run west to east and numbers north to south; D7, for example, is [[imjin-farmland]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Every key's description names the grid square of its door ([[keys]]). Key cards place the west of the map in columns A and B ([[chang-san-air-base]] around B5 and B6), the casino at F4, [[mirae-general-hospital]] at F5 and the reactor coast at I4 [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-key-doors-2026-10-21]]. Community maps do not always follow the game's grid: one puts Imjin Farmland around D9 [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]], another is a square off near some edges [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]], so trust the key card.

## What do the markers mean?

- Buildings are colour-coded: Fire Stations are red, Police Stations blue and Clinics green [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; police stations carry a white badge marker [[src-gameshorizon-police-stations-2026-10-21]].
- Exfil sites carry an exit-door icon and green smoke in the world [[src-gfinity-exfil-2026-10-20]]; the [[final-exfil]] turns white [clm_c772bf67ab75].
- The [[silo-bunkers]] are unnamed circles in the west (reported) [[src-techwiser-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]].

## What else is on the HUD?

Time Remaining; Objectives (Story Missions, Dynamic Ops and Side Ops on the left, DMZ Orders on the right); a compass naming the POI you are in; the area's Threat Level under it ([[threat-escalation]]); the Hostiles Kill Count ([[marked-for-death]]); a squad panel with each Operator's plates, DMZ Cash, gas mask and parachute; the Backpack menu; the Ping Wheel ([[proximity-chat]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
