UAV Towers are guarded towers marked on the Hajin map: activating one completes the Activate UAV Tower Side Op ([[side-ops]]), pays DMZ Cash and sends out a pulse that shows Operators and enemies nearby [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 a Side Op pays 2,000 DMZ Cash [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How many UAV Towers are there?

The map shows 14, by one guide's count (reported) [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]]. Most major points of interest have at least one, and [[imjin-farmland]] and [[mirae-general-hospital]] have two each on a community map [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]].

## How do you activate one?

Clear the guards first; towers are usually defended [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Then use the laptop at the base of the tower. The tower is a camouflaged equipment box with a short mast and an open laptop; do not mix it up with the tall lattice tower marked with a skull, which is a Lieutenant Hunt tower ([[download-intel-to-locate-lieutenant]]) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. Getting close adds the Activate UAV Tower side op to your HUD, and your Team Leader mentions it over the radio [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported). One site counts 14 towers on the map [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] (reported).

## What does a tower do once active?

It marks enemies, AI and players alike, around the tower for a while [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]], and players report that popping towers is one of the most common things squads do [clm_0b7cfd4fc000]. Two Traits change how long it lasts: Overwatch (Scout) keeps towers you activate running for good, and Air Superiority (Survivalist) keeps UAV Towers and SAM Sites captured longer ([[air-superiority]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_c3633bb72757]. A minor update on 22 Oct 2026 made it possible to take a UAV Tower back when enemies control it; the patch notes list the change under 23 Oct [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [clm_ae67c70adc88].
