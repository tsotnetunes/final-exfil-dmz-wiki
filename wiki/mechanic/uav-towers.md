UAV Towers are guarded towers marked on the Hajin map: activating one completes the Activate UAV Tower Side Op ([[side-ops]]), pays DMZ Cash and sends out a pulse that shows Operators and enemies nearby [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 a Side Op pays 2,000 DMZ Cash [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How many UAV Towers are there?

Fewer than at launch, and no longer in fixed places. Since 2 Nov 2026 towers appear at random and 40% less often; a point of interest is not sure to have one, and two will not turn up in one area [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. [[deadtown]] is the exception and keeps its tower, because a DMZ Order needs it [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Before that, one guide counted 14 on the map (reported) [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]], and a community map pins 21 spots, which now shows where a tower can be, not where one will be [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]]. Check your map each deployment.

## How do you activate one?

Clear the guards first; towers are usually defended [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Then use the laptop at the base of the tower. The tower is a camouflaged equipment box with a short mast and an open laptop; do not mix it up with the tall lattice tower marked with a skull, which is a Lieutenant Hunt tower ([[download-intel-to-locate-lieutenant]]) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. Getting close adds the Activate UAV Tower side op to your HUD, and your Team Leader mentions it over the radio [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## What does a tower do once active?

It marks enemies, AI and players alike, around the tower for a while [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]], and players report that popping towers is one of the most common things squads do [clm_0b7cfd4fc000]. Two Traits change how long it lasts: Overwatch (Scout) keeps towers you activate running for good, and Air Superiority (Survivalist) keeps UAV Towers and SAM Sites captured longer ([[air-superiority]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_c3633bb72757]. A minor update on 22 Oct 2026 made it possible to take a UAV Tower back when enemies control it; the patch notes list the change under 23 Oct [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [clm_ae67c70adc88]. A fix of 2 Nov 2026 corrected the status a tower could show after a recapture or with the Trait the notes call Extended Duration [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How do you hide from one?

Since 2 Nov 2026 the Sedative Inhaler lasts 120 seconds, up from 60, partly to help you stay off tower pulses ([[medical-items]]) [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. It does not yet hide you from a tower switched on by a rival with [[uav-upgrade]]; Infinity Ward plans to fix that in a Season 1 update [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
