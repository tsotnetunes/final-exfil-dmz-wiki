In DMZ you build weapons at the FOB [[gunsmith]] and pay DMZ Cash for the base gun, for each attachment slot and for each attachment; cosmetics are free except Blueprints [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_55b26f37e758]. What you may fit depends on weapon levels, which are shared with Multiplayer [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Slot and part prices: [[gunsmith]].

## How do attachments unlock?

- **Weapon levels.** Each gun has its own level track (the Han 86 has 72 levels, the Warlord 60, the Signal 50 74), and levels earned in Multiplayer or DMZ count in both [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **Shared Attachments.** An attachment unlocks once and is then available on every gun that can take it; a level that would grant one you already own gives a Bonus Level Up instead [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]].
- **Apex Attachments** are the last reward of each track and take their own slot ([[apex-attachments]]).
- **Weapon Prestige** puts a maxed gun back to level zero and relocks its attachments in exchange for exclusive camos [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]; a 24 Oct fix made attachments reset properly when you prestige in the Gunsmith [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Which slots are there?

Optic, Laser, Barrel, Muzzle, Underbarrel, Stock, Rear Grip, Comb, Ammunition and Magazine; some Apex Attachments also block a Trigger Action slot [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Pistols take full attachments; launchers and the knife only change their look [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Optic Rail Placement lets you slide an optic along the rail to set your sight picture [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How many attachments can a DMZ gun carry?

- **Gunsmith build:** five, plus the Apex [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-gunsmith-weapons-2026-10-21]].
- **Found in Hajin:** up to eight [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; one guide says boss weapons can go past that (reported) [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].
- **Damaged guns:** loot includes weapons with broken attachments [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]; they still fire but perform worse than clean ones [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]], and stripping a broken attachment at the Gunsmith costs 1,000 each (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].

## What do weapon rarities mean?

Loot runs Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Ultra [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]], and DMZ Orders ask for Rare-or-better and Ultra weapons (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapons-2026-10-31]]. Rarity belongs to each copy, so the same model turns up at different tiers [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]; Rare weapons are marked blue [clm_ccfae152bab3]. A fully built Rezi 12 with its Apex showed as Legendary (reported) [[src-allthingshow-rezi-12-enforcer-build-2026-10-21]], and Lieutenants carry named Legendary versions ([[weapons]]) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. How many slots each rarity gives is not confirmed yet.

## How do saved builds work?

Gunny can suggest a build from the parts you own [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Since 21 Oct you save a build by hand in the Gunsmith, and a build edited during a match saves itself; these notes are listed under Multiplayer [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Fixes for DMZ builds: saved Blueprint changes could not be bought on Nintendo Switch 2 (20 Oct), DMZ builds showed as usable in core Multiplayer (24 Oct), and saved builds from modified Blueprints were charged the original Blueprint's full price (25 Oct) [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. See [[blueprints]].
