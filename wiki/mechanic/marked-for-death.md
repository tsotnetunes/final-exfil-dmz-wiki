The Hostiles Kill Count is a HUD meter that counts the enemy combatants you eliminate in the current deployment. When it fills, the enemy sends a Hunt Squad after you and you are Marked for Death [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How is it different from a Bounty or the Threat Level?

It counts AI kills only. Killing other players builds a Bounty and Wanted status instead ([[bounty-system]], [[wanted-status]]). The Threat Level under the compass belongs to the area you are in, rises with noise and violence and ignores human Operators ([[threat-escalation]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The game tracks your activity and escalates its response as you draw attention [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. codmunity.gg describes an AI wanted level at the top right of the HUD that rises as you fight and is separate from the Bounty (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]; whether that is this meter is not confirmed.

## How do you avoid a Hunt Squad?

Fight less. Stealth, cover, distance and breaking line of sight calm enemy forces, while noise and violence raise the response [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Take only the fights your objective needs, and leave an area once its job is done.

## What is not known yet?

How many kills fill the meter, how big the Hunt Squad is, and whether the count resets during a deployment are not published. Not to confuse with the Hunt Operators Dynamic Op, which sets a full rival squad against you on purpose [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
