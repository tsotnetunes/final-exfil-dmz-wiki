Since 15 Nov 2026 the official updates account has said the team is investigating reports that players may, in rare cases, lose their customized Loadouts, either at the end of a match or when they pick up a Wager Match ticket [[src-codupdates-x-loadout-loss-2026-11-15]]. No fix or date has been given yet. This is about saved Loadouts (your custom classes), not the gear an Operator carries into a deployment.

## When does it happen?
The two moments named are the end of a match and getting a Wager Match ticket, which ties it to the Season 1 Wager Match Event ([[wager-match]]) [[src-codupdates-x-loadout-loss-2026-11-15]]. The post covers Modern Warfare 4 as a whole; it does not single out DMZ.

## Is this the same as losing gear after a disconnect?
No. In the first week of DMZ, players who dropped out of a match through crashes or disconnects often lost the gear they had taken in [[src-gfinity-dmz-tech-issues-2026-10-22]]. That is described on [[server-and-performance-issues]] and [[what-happens-if-you-disconnect-in-dmz]].

## What can you do meanwhile?
Nothing official has been suggested. Until a fix is announced, note down any Loadout you rely on so you can rebuild it quickly.
