Loot in Hajin is placed where it would logically be: police stations hold tactical gear, hospitals medical supplies, and homes crafting resources [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. So you can plan a run by building type, but no spot guarantees a particular rare item, and contents change from one deployment to the next [[src-keengamer-best-loot-2026-10-22]] [[src-destructoid-best-loot-2026-10-25]].

## What containers are there?

Guides name duffel bags, jackets on coat racks, military bags, lockers, footlockers, tool chests, rifle cases, weapon lockers, safes and suitcases on the train [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-keys-2026-10-22]]. Opening containers pays DMZ XP [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Hidden caches such as a military duffel can hold a Field Upgrade or a throwing knife (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Players report a loot route where every stop is a weapon locker [clm_5d04f7dd5bb2]. Ammo & Armor Caches on the map refill your ammunition and plates and then cool down; one guide times the cooldown at 60 seconds (reported) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## What else shapes your loot?

- Junk breaks down into the four Resources as you pick it up, and Resources take no slots [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Weapons can be damaged, with broken attachments; Lieutenants carry customised weapons [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].
- Traits add loot: Container Looter can put an extra item in a container, Adaptive Restock makes AI drop an extra item, Hostile Looter makes AI drop printer ingredients [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- The [[3d-printer]] suggests where an ingredient you need is likely to be [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Each completed Dynamic Operation brings a Supply Drop, better with every Op done in the same deployment [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- Fixed on 21 Oct 2026: some Dynamic Ops asked you to find keypad codes in safes that sat inside locked rooms [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
