Every point of interest in Hajin has its own threat level, shown under the compass when you enter: Low, Medium, High, Critical or Extreme. The higher it is, the more enemies there are and the tougher they get; loud play raises the response and stealth calms it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## What are the threat levels?

| Low | [[imjin-farmland]], Deadtown exterior, [[cheongun-village]], [[mirae-general-hospital]], [[songdo-wharf]] |
| Medium | Re-Education Center, Rice Mill, [[cheonwang-peak-array]], [[wolves-stadium]], [[hajin-river-heights]], NuriGO East |

All from [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; players also report Extreme in the tech complex [clm_0d5aa093524d]. Human Operators do not count toward the level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. On the DBN Headquarters roof, guides playing at launch read Extreme on the compass where the guide had Critical [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-roze-2026-10-22]]. Bosses sit at every level: [[the-doctor]] in a Low hospital, [[jang-ui]] and [[roze]] in Extreme towers ([[lieutenants]]).

## How does the threat go up?

The game tracks what you do and escalates the response [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]: being seen moving or fighting brings retaliation, and the patrols that ignored you start searching or attacking [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. At high threat, attack helicopters and Deathstalker helicopters arrive as reinforcements [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] ([[deathstalker]]). Every AI kill also fills the separate Hostiles Kill Count, which sends a Hunt Squad when full ([[marked-for-death]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How do you calm it down?

Kill quietly (melee, throwing knives, suppressed shots), stay in cover or shadow, move away to quieter rural ground and break the enemy's line of sight and hearing [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The [[stealth-meter]] shows how close you are to being spotted [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]], and weather changes what the AI can see and hear [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]] ([[dynamic-weather]]).

## Threat level, kill count and Wanted: what is the difference?

Three separate things. The threat level belongs to a place and has nothing to do with other players [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The Hostiles Kill Count follows you and fills with AI kills; a community guide calls it a wanted level that brings search parties (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. The Bounty's Wanted status comes only from killing Operators [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[wanted-status]]). The enemy itself is described on [[north-korean-forces]].
