**Levels.** Every point of interest has its own Threat level, shown under the compass when you enter: Low, Medium, High, Critical, Extreme. The higher it is, the more and the tougher the enemies; human Operators are not counted in it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Each POI page lists its expected levels (for example, [[chang-san-air-base]] bunker Extreme, [[the-grid]] Critical).

**Escalation.** The game tracks what you do and escalates the response [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]: being seen moving or fighting brings retaliation and reinforcements at higher levels ([[commander-threats]]). **Calming it:** stealth kills (melee, throwing knives, suppressed shots), taking cover, moving away to quieter rural ground and breaking line of sight and hearing. See [[stealth-meter]] and, for the separate kill counter, [[marked-for-death]].
