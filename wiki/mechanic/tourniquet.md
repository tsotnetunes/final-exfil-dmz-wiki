The tourniquet is the self-revive every Operator deploys with: when you are downed into Last Stand it gets you back up, but only to a wounded state at 100 health out of 150 [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. A guide describes it the same way: using it takes 50 off your maximum health and marks you Wounded [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## When can you use it again?

Only once you are back at full health, which in practice means a Medkit: the Medkit repairs the wound, restores full health and lets you use the tourniquet again [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. If you go down again while still wounded, you need a squadmate, a Self-Revive Kit or another revive item [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. A Bandage only lifts your maximum health for a while; it does not fix the wound [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## How does it fit with other revives?

The June intel framed it as the way up when you have no self-revive equipped [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. The [[self-revive-kit]], the Defibrillator and the Revive Pistol get you up without a wound, according to one guide (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]; all of them are on [[medical-items]]. The developers named the tourniquet, together with the [[stealth-meter]], as something added with solo players in mind [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Item databases list it among the consumables, without a rarity [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. The full rules are on [[health-and-healing]].
