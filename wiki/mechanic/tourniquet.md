Activision: the tourniquet lets you heal to a wounded state if you have no self-revive equipped [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. Per the developers' 9 Oct Q&A, relayed by [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]: every Operator deploys able to use it; it gets you up from Last Stand at 100 health with a wound (maximum is 150), and it works again only once you are back at full health. A Medkit repairs the wound and re-enables it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Developers also named it, with the [[stealth-meter]], as a help for solo players.
