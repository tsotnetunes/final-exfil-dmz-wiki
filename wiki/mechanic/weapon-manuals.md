A Weapon Manual is a loot item for one gun. Extract with it and you can buy that gun in the [[gunsmith]] for DMZ Cash as often as you like; the Manual is used up at exfil and pays 5,000 DMZ Cash, as a Recipe does [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_9ee7db0a613a]. It is the only permanent way to unlock a weapon in DMZ [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and the unlock carries over between deployments ([[what-carries-over]]) [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## Do you need three Manuals to unlock a gun?

No. One extraction unlocks the weapon [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-gunsmith-weapons-2026-10-21]]. The number three belongs to the War Spoils camo: extracting three Manuals of a weapon counts toward that weapon's War Spoils camo ([[war-spoils-camo]]) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]] [clm_ce6b0de65759]. Some players report extractions that did not count, one of them for a Manual carried out in the backpack's safe pocket [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]] [clm_4f7ad24e07bb].

## Where do you find Weapon Manuals?

They have no fixed spawns [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]]. Look in:

- **Military containers**, most often in police stations and compounds [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-gfinity-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-27]].
- **Safes and locked rooms** behind keys or keycards; a Door Breacher Charge opens rooms that need no card [[src-gfinity-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-27]] ([[keys]]). One guide names police station armories, the Gun Store Weapons Locker and Gangbyeon Apartment B [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]].
- **Everyday containers** such as duffel bags, footlockers, toolboxes and medicine cabinets [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]], and the backpacks of enemy Operators [[src-allthingshow-dmz-gunsmith-weapons-2026-10-21]].
- **Irradiated Containers** processed at Reactor 02 in [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] give one almost every time (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]].
- **A farming spot:** the barracks by the perimeter wall at [[chang-san-air-base]], opened with no gear during the Operation Iron Citadel mission, gave Manuals on many runs (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-weapon-manual-farm-2026-10-22]].

## How do you keep one until exfil?

Put it in a backpack's Secure Slot as soon as you pick it up ([[secure-slots]]); if you die with it anywhere else, it is lost [[src-gfinity-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-27]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]]. Only the player who extracts with it gets the unlock, so hand it to the squadmate who needs it before exfil [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## What does the unlock give you?

The base gun with no attachments, bought in the Gunsmith from DMZ level 10; you pay again for every copy [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]]. Without its Manual a gun stays locked in Buy New Weapon even if you maxed it in Multiplayer [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]], and a DMZ Manual does not unlock the gun in Multiplayer (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. Looted guns, Vendor copies and [[blueprints]] let you use a gun without its Manual, but none of them unlocks it [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]]. A DMZ Order on page 24 asks you to extract two Manuals (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]].
