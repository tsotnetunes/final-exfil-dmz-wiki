The DMZ HUD puts the whole deployment on one screen: the Tac-Map, the radiation clock, your objectives, a compass with the area's Threat Level, a counter of the AI you have killed, your squad's state and your own gear [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What does each part show?

| Element | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Tac-Map | Objectives, key places, danger zones, enemy bases, useful buildings, ammo and armour caches, spreading radiation, you and your facing, nearby enemies and pings ([[tac-map]]) |
| Time Remaining | When the radiation storm will intensify ([[radiation-storm]], [[match-length]]) |
| Objectives | Your squad's operations and Side Ops on the left, DMZ Orders on the right |
| Compass | Your heading from 1 to 360 and the name of the place you are in |
| Threat Level | Under the compass at top centre: the area's AI danger, from Low to Extreme, updated as you enter a new place ([[threat-escalation]]) |
| Hostiles Kill Count | AI enemies killed this deployment; when it fills, a Hunt Squad comes for you ([[marked-for-death]]) |
| Squad panel | Each squadmate's portrait and colour, name, health, plates and armour, whether they are Downed, Wounded or Dead, and temporary health from painkillers |
| DMZ Cash | The cash each Operator is carrying |
| Equipment capacity | Spare plates, and whether a gas mask and a parachute are equipped |
| Backpack | Opens the backpack menu, with a key to sort it |
| Weapons and gear | Ammo, fire mode, rarity and attachments of your weapon; your secondary; melee, Tactical and Lethal counts; Field Upgrades and Fire Support |

All from the official guide [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. codmunity adds that the stamina bar sits at bottom centre and turns red when you are too tired to tactical sprint (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## What can you do from the HUD?

- In the Backpack menu you can move items into your loadout or use consumables on the spot, such as bandages, spare plates and field upgrade crates [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- The Ping Wheel sends visual commands when talking is not possible or not wise, including a truce request ([[pings-and-communication]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- On the full Tac-Map you can set and clear pings, cancel your current operation and ping DMZ Order objectives for your squad [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Which warnings pop up?

Your Team Leader warns you when a Commander is near [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; before launch a screen splash was described for a nearby Lieutenant [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-solo-play-2026-10-03]]; and the [[stealth-meter]] warns you before an AI spots you.
