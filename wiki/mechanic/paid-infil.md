For Dynamic Operations and Free Roam you choose how to go in: on foot for free, or pay DMZ Cash for a vehicle, a helicopter or a plane [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The June intel put it as going in quietly on foot or fast and loud by helicopter or plane [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## What does each infil cost?

| Infil | DMZ Cash | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On foot | free | you start at a random spot |
| Polaris Dagor A1 drop | 5,000 | a vehicle dropped to you that seats the whole squad |
| LTV drop | 25,000 | a vehicle that can carry a mounted .50 cal, dropped to you |
| Helicopter | 50,000 | you fly in from the FOB, one of your squad at the controls |
| Cargo plane | 100,000 | you jump at a spot you pick |

Prices and options from the 9 Oct guide [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; the vehicles themselves are on [[vehicles]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How do you unlock the paid infils?

Some options need progression first [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: each vehicle or air infil opens with a Story Mission ([[story-missions]]), and an air infil needs a parachute (reported) [[src-skycoach-dmz-beginner-guide-2026-10-19]]. codmunity.gg ties the LTV to Operation Iron Citadel and the helicopter to Operation Grid Assault [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. A community database lists a vehicle among Story Mission rewards: the Polaris Dagor A1 for Operation Silent Ledger, the LTV for Iron Citadel, the helicopter for Grid Assault and the cargo plane for Operation Fallen Ember (reported) [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]].

## Is a paid infil worth it?

Paying buys you a start point and a ride. Vehicles in DMZ have limited armor and fuel, so plan a stop at a Gas Station to repair and refuel [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The cash comes from your Bank ([[wallet]]); compare it with what a deployment pays ([[dmz-cash]]): a Dynamic Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 since 30 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Known problems

Fixed 20 and 21 Oct 2026: enemy AI could run players over during the infil, including in the NK Wilderness [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
