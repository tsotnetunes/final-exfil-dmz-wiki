## What does Rescue do?

* Paying the rescue cost returns that Operator.
* It was confirmed on 7 Oct 2026 that paying the rescue cost retains the Operator's progressed Skill Tree.
* The mechanic preserves any experience earned in the deployment before the Operator died, confirmed on 7 Oct 2026.
* Players report that rescuing an Operator costs 50.
* Players report that rescuing a dead Operator recovers their Traits.
* Players report that players can kit up the rescued Operator from their stash without entering the game again.
