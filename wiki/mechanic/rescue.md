Rescue is a mechanic that allows players to recover a dead Operator along with their earned progress. Paying the rescue cost preserves an Operator's development after death rather than losing their progression entirely.

## What does Rescue do?

* Paying the rescue cost returns that Operator along with their progressed Skill Tree, confirmed on 7 Oct 2026.
* Rescuing an Operator also returns any experience earned in the deployment before they died, confirmed on 7 Oct 2026.
* Players report that rescuing an Operator costs 50.
* Players report that rescuing a dead Operator allows you to recover their Traits.
* Players report that you can kit out the rescued Operator from your stash without entering the game again.
