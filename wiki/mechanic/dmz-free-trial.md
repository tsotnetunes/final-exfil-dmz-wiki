The DMZ Free Trial lets anyone play DMZ without buying Modern Warfare 4 for a few days at the start of Season 1. It opened with the season on 6 Nov 2026 and ran until 11 Nov at 10 AM PT, so it is over now [clm_c03d62d1bd39] [clm_00eb4f492414] [clm_0e0655be5769]. The main Call of Duty account repeated the end date on 7 Nov and again on 9 Nov 2026 [[src-cod-x-camo-tracking-2026-11-07]] [[src-cod-x-dmz-free-trial-2026-11-09]].

## What could you play?
The trial is not a cut-down mode. Official posts said trial players could deploy to both Hajin and the X01 black site and take on the new Orders added in Season 1 [[src-cod-x-dmz-free-trial-2026-11-09]]. See [[what-is-x01]] and [[dmz-orders]] for how those work.

## Is it on every platform?
Yes, the trial was open on all platforms DMZ runs on [clm_0e0655be5769]. See [[what-platforms-is-dmz-on]].

## What happens when it ends?
Since 11 Nov 2026 at 10 AM PT you need a copy of Modern Warfare 4 to play DMZ again ([[is-dmz-free]]). No new trial date has been announced. Activision has not said whether levels, Stash items or Operators earned during the trial carry over to a bought copy, so treat that as unknown until it does.
