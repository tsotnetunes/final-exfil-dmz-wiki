An Operator killed in a deployment goes MIA (missing in action) and cannot deploy again until recovered [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. At the Operators station ([[active-duty]]) you either pay DMZ Cash from your Bank to recover them with all their Traits, or dismiss them and recruit someone new. The price rises with the Operator's Trait Points, and a recovered Operator comes back without gear [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The 3D Printer can print MIA recovery discounts ([[3d-printer]]). The June intel described it as deploying rescue teams, paid from the [[wallet]] [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].
