The MIA system designates an Operator as missing in action when that Operator dies.

## What happens when an Operator dies?

* An Operator is marked MIA upon death, confirmed on 7 Oct 2026.

<!-- human:start -->
An Operator who dies in a deployment goes MIA (missing in action) and cannot deploy again until you recover them or dismiss them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_15b35293c60f]. Meanwhile you can swap in another Active Duty Operator at Ready Up ([[active-duty]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do you get an MIA Operator back?

At the Operators station, pay DMZ Cash from your Bank ([[wallet]]). The Operator returns with all their Traits but without the gear they died with, so give them a new loadout [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-operator-guide-2026-10-20]]. Paying also keeps the XP that Operator earned in the deployment before dying [clm_ec258e1f0cc1]. If the price is too high, dismiss the Operator and recruit a new one instead [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The June intel described this as sending a rescue team [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## How much does recovery cost?

The price rises with the Operator's Trait Points [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; Activision has published no figures. One player reported a price of 270,000 for an Operator lost during an exfil [clm_5765e08bf423]. The [[3d-printer]] prints permanent MIA recovery discounts [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: four MIA Recovery Discount levels of 5% each, 20% in all, paid in printer ingredients and valuables rather than cash (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]]. Costs per level: [[fob-upgrades]]. The guides mention no timer and no free recovery.

## Can you protect an Operator before you deploy?

- The Vault Edition's Deployment Kit holds two Operator Rescue Beacons and two Weapon Insurance Contracts [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; Activision has not explained how they work.
- The round recovery slots beside the Ready button each protect one Operator or a set of weapons for a single deployment, and are used up even when you extract (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- A Free Loadout protects nothing: your own Operator still goes in, and a death still means paying to recover them (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- A Blueprint weapon can be bought back at a discount if you lose it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What else do you lose when you die?

Everything outside [[secure-slots]] and your Bounty ([[bounty-system]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Your DMZ level XP is kept, and the [[stash]] at the FOB is safe [[src-skycoach-dmz-beginner-guide-2026-10-19]]. Full list: [[what-carries-over]].
