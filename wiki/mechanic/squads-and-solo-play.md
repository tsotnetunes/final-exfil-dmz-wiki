You can play DMZ alone or in a squad of up to three. A match holds up to 20 squads, 60 players in all, and a squad can grow to four by taking in another player during the match [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-60-players-2026-10-12]] [clm_30653443233a].

## How do you play solo?

There is no solo playlist or button. At Ready Up, switch Squad Fill off and deploy alone (key 1 on keyboard, left trigger on controller); with Squad Fill on, the game adds two other players to your squad [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [clm_d5a1dc3ae648]. The matchmaker tries to put solos with solos, duos with duos and trios with trios, but promises nothing, so a solo can still meet a full squad [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; queue details are on [[matchmaking]]. Story Missions can be done alone [clm_a6788c608374], and before launch a developer said a solo player can do all of DMZ's content [[src-destructoid-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].

## What helps when you are alone?

- Infinity Ward added the [[tourniquet]] and the [[stealth-meter]] with solo players in mind [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-60-players-2026-10-12]].
- Nobody can revive you, so carry a Self-Revive Kit or take the [[possum]] Trait [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- A solo's exfil countdown is about 5 seconds once you are aboard, against 30 to 45 seconds for a squad that is still boarding [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] ([[extraction-rules]]).

## Can you team up with other squads?

Yes. The Ping Wheel lets you ask nearby players for a truce or to join up, and proximity chat lets you talk it through ([[pings-and-communication]], [[proximity-chat]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Squads can agree a truce, betray each other or assimilate, which means joining another squad after the match has started; an assimilated squad tops out at four players [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-solo-play-2026-10-03]] [clm_5b76bd4676ec]. A bug that stopped assimilated players picking up hostages was fixed on 29 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How do parties work?

In a party the FOB shows the party leader's progress, while each station's menu shows your own progress and items [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Parties can mix platforms, and you add friends by Activision ID [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]]. Until a fix on 24 Oct 2026, a Party Leader could take squadmates out of a DMZ match against their will, and their Operators were then marked MIA [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What can squadmates do for each other?

- Revive you from Last Stand without the wound a tourniquet leaves [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]; see [[health-and-healing]].
- Drag you to safety while you are down (developers, before launch) [[src-gamingtrend-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].
- Follow your health, plates, armour, DMZ Cash, gas mask and parachute on the squad panel of the [[hud]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
