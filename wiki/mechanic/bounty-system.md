Killing other players earns you a Bounty, and enough kills make you Wanted, so other squads can hunt you for cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_162bac1414e5]. Only player kills count toward it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; killing AI fills a separate meter ([[marked-for-death]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How do you get a Bounty?

The first player kill puts a Bounty on you straight away. It shows on your Dog Tag ([[dog-tags]]) with your Operator kill count and grows with every player kill, across deployments; who fired first and being outnumbered are among the factors [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. At four player kills you become **Wanted** ([[wanted-status]]) and nearby Operators are alerted when you come close; higher thresholds raise your Wanted level, up to **Most Wanted** [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_8c1bbf558597]. The bounty trailer shows 100,000 after a sixth kill and 250,000 after an eighth (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## How do you get rid of your Bounty?

- **Die.** Dying loses your Bounty and Wanted status [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **Lose your tag.** If another player takes your Dog Tag and extracts with it, your Bounty is gone, even if you were revived and got out [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **Pay it off** with DMZ Cash at a Bounty Station [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-skycoach-dmz-beginner-guide-2026-10-19]]. The trailer shows a Reduce Bounty button costing 5,000 (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

Otherwise the Bounty stays with you from one deployment to the next [[src-skycoach-dmz-beginner-guide-2026-10-19]].

## How do you hunt a Bounty?

At a Bounty Station, a rugged laptop on the map, the list of Wanted Operators in your deployment is free. Paid intel, dearer for higher Wanted levels, draws a circle on your Tac-Map that shrinks and updates faster the more Wanted the target is; at the top level the Killer's area may be shown to everyone [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. To collect, kill the target and extract with their Dog Tag; the payout follows their Bounty, with extra at maximum Wanted level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_1dda32204130], and it reaches your Bank after the exfil (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. The kill alone pays nothing; the tag has to come home [[src-cod-dmz-bounty-systems-video-2026-10-08]].

Payouts grow with the [[dog-tag-case]] rewards at DMZ levels 30, 50 and 70 (10%, 50% and 100% more) [[src-callofduty-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. The Bounty Intel and Bounty Hunter Minor Traits make intel cheaper and rewards bigger (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]].

## What do circling vultures mean?

Vultures circle the bodies of Operators who died in Hajin, so you can spot from far away where someone fell [[src-cod-dmz-bounty-systems-video-2026-10-08]].

## Does hunting lower your own Bounty?

Not known. The June intel said hunting bounty targets could lessen the target on your own back [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]; the 9 Oct guide does not say how ([[open-questions]]). The FOB's [[bounty-leaderboard]] ranks the top hunters and killers.
