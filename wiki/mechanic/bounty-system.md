In a deployment, **Bounty Stations** (rugged laptops on the map) let you review the bounties on human Operators before hunting them, or pay off your own bounty with DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The FOB's [[bounty-leaderboard]] tracks the most successful bounty hunters and killers. Full Bounty rules were promised for the guide's Part 2 (not out on 9 Oct).
