**Killers** [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Killing another player earns a Bounty at once; it shows on your Dog Tag with your Operator kill count and grows with every player kill across deployments; the guide says factors such as who fired first and being outnumbered count. At four player kills you become **Wanted** ([[wanted-status]]), and nearby Operators are alerted when you come close. Higher thresholds raise your Wanted level; the top ones are **Most Wanted**. Dying loses your Bounty and Wanted status. Other ways to shed a Bounty: another player kills you and extracts with your Dog Tag (even if you are revived and get out), or pay it off with DMZ Cash at a **Bounty Station** [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

**Hunters** [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. At a Bounty Station (rugged laptops on the map) the list of Wanted Operators in your deployment is free; paid intel, dearer for higher Wanted levels, puts a circle on your Tac-Map that shrinks and updates faster the more Wanted the target is. At the top level the Killer's area may be visible to everyone. To collect, kill the target and extract with their Dog Tag; the payout follows their Bounty, with extra at maximum Wanted level.

**Vultures** circle the bodies of Operators who died in Hajin, so you can see from a distance where someone fell [[src-cod-dmz-bounty-systems-video-2026-10-08]].

The FOB's [[bounty-leaderboard]] lists the top 50 of each; the [[dog-tag-case]] keeps the tags you took. Hunting players raised notoriety in the June intel too, which added that hunting bounty targets "can lessen the target on your own back" [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]; the 9 Oct guide does not say how, so whether killing a Wanted player lowers a hunter's own Bounty is still open ([[open-questions]]). Not the same as being [[marked-for-death]] by AI forces.
