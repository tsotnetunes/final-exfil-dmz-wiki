War Spoils is a weapon camo that players report earning in DMZ by extracting the same gun's Weapon Manual three times [clm_ce6b0de65759] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]]. Activision has not mentioned it yet, so treat the details below as reported.

## How do you unlock War Spoils?

Extract three Manuals of one weapon. Players describe it as three more extractions after the first one has unlocked the gun [clm_ce6b0de65759]; one guide says the three Manuals must belong to the weapon you have equipped, and that some players saw extractions that did not count [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]]. Each Manual you extract still pays 5,000 DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]].

## Does a Manual in the safe pocket count?

Reportedly not: a Manual carried out in the backpack's safe pocket ([[secure-slots]]) did not count toward War Spoils [clm_4f7ad24e07bb]. The catch is that a Secure Slot is what keeps a Manual if you die [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]], so for camo progress you take the risk of carrying it in the main backpack.

## Which weapons have it?

Players have seen it on the Pila, the [[patriot-xmr]] and the [[hyeon-burst]] [clm_4c4b5f9891b5] [clm_2b05fa148d3c] [clm_e153c3eb028f]. Whether every gun with a Manual has one is not confirmed yet.

## Is it official?

Not yet. The official launch post on progression lists the DMZ Mastery camos (Chiral, Ripple Tide, Helio) but not War Spoils [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. How Manuals unlock guns: [[weapon-manuals]]. All DMZ camos: [[dmz-camos]].
