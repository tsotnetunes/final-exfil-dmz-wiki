DMZ keeps your long-term progress between deployments: the FOB, your Stash and Bank, Operators and their Traits, and your DMZ level. What is at risk in each deployment is what you carry in [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What do you keep between deployments?

- **FOB and storage.** Station unlocks and upgrades, the [[stash]] and the Bank ([[wallet]]); Stash, Bank, Resources and Ingredients are shared by all your Active Duty Operators [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **Operators.** Each keeps their Traits, Dog Tag level, backpack and loadout [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-cod-dmz-progression-video-2026-10-07]].
- **Unlocks.** Recipes and Weapon Manuals become permanent once extracted ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **Levels.** DMZ XP counts even when you fail to exfil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_43eb9661dfec]. Weapon levels and attachments are shared with Multiplayer, and anything unlocked in either mode can be bought at the Gunsmith with DMZ Cash [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]].
- **Standing.** Your place on the [[bounty-leaderboard]], and your own Bounty until you die [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What do you lose when you die?

- Everything you carry outside [[secure-slots]], including weapons, armor and backpack contents [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Cash you picked up reaches the Bank only if you extract (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-level-up-fast-2026-10-21]]; Dynamic Op rewards are paid into the Bank straight away (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [clm_f17f3ff635f7].
- The Operator goes MIA. Paying to recover them keeps their Traits and the XP they earned before dying, but not their gear ([[mia-system]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_ec258e1f0cc1].
- Your Bounty and Wanted status ([[bounty-system]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- The Extracted Bonus XP; everything else earned stays (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-xp-strategies-2026-10-30]].
- Looted Recipes and Weapon Manuals, which only count once you extract with them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Keep keys in a Secure Slot (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

Your FOB Stash is never at risk [[src-skycoach-dmz-beginner-guide-2026-10-19]].

## Did early access progress carry into launch?

Yes for weapons: players report that weapon levels, attachments and camos from early access (20 Oct) carried into the full launch on 23 Oct [clm_ab8878f43ec0].

## Does DMZ wipe at a new season?

Not announced. Season 1 starts on 6 Nov 2026 [[src-insider-gaming-season-1-date-2026-10-14]], and neither Activision nor Infinity Ward has said whether the Stash, Bank, Operators or DMZ levels reset with it; community guides give no wipe schedule [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. A Japanese report of the official Dynamic Ops trailer says the [[boss-board]] is filled again each season [[src-fpsjp-dynamic-ops-trailer-2026-10-07]]. Tracked in [[open-questions]].
