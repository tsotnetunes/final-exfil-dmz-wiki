DMZ pits you against other players as well as the AI: up to 60 players in 20 squads share Hajin, and every other squad is a possible threat or ally [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. There is no PvE-only playlist at launch: the single-squad option seen in a screenshot was an internal test playlist [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-60-players-2026-10-12]] ([[matchmaking]]).

## Why fight other players?

- Rival Operators give more XP than AI enemies [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- You can loot their gear and take their Dog Tag; a tag can carry a Bounty you collect by extracting with it ([[dog-tags]], [[bounty-system]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- The Hunt Operators Dynamic Operation sets a full rival squad as your target [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; its missing timer was fixed on 30 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What does killing players cost you?

Your first player kill puts a Bounty on your own Dog Tag, four make you Wanted, and nearby Operators are then warned when you come close ([[wanted-status]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Before launch the developers said a banner also tells players when a bounty target is near, even without paid intel [[src-gamingtrend-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].

## Can you avoid a fight?

Ask for a truce or to join up through the Ping Wheel or proximity chat, though a truce can be broken and squads do betray each other ([[squads-and-solo-play]], [[pings-and-communication]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## Where do players fight?

- At exfils: other squads lie in wait at exfil sites, and exfil flares are loud and seen from far away [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Players report campers at exfils and more fights around good loot and exfils by the third day after early access opened (reported) [clm_4821865b60e5] [clm_20b125dbeea0].
- Around the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]], where players report frequent fights because many squads infil nearby (reported) [clm_b9735f36bb0b].
- Around anyone who picks up a Lieutenant's Dog Tag Case, which marks them on every Operator's Tac-Map [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How do UAVs affect PvP?

UAV Towers are one of the Side Ops on the map [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[uav-towers]]). Several Traits shape the fight over them: Overwatch keeps the towers you activate running for good, [[air-superiority]] makes towers and SAM sites last longer, UAV Upgrade turns your UAVs into Advanced UAVs, and Thermal Padding hides you from UAVs [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Since 23 Oct 2026 you can take a UAV Tower back from enemy control [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [clm_ae67c70adc88]. Some players complain of too many UAVs and want Overwatch removed (reported) [clm_9bddae4cc5ea].

## Which combat rules matter?

- DMZ has its own weapon damage tuning, made around its armour and plate carriers [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
- There are no armour-piercing or Stopping Power rounds as field upgrades for now, and tactical sprint is on by default [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
- Explosives often knock players down or back instead of killing outright (developers, before launch) [[src-gamingtrend-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].
- [[possum]] cannot bring a player back while the squad that killed them is close by (since 28 Oct 2026) [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What is not allowed?

Fighting other players is the mode working as intended. Griefing, meaning deliberately spoiling other people's games, and cheating are against Activision's policy [[src-activision-security-enforcement-policy-2026-07-31]]; see [[ricochet-anti-cheat]].
