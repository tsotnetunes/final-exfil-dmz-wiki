## What do the other markers do?

- **Ammo & Armor Caches** refill ammo and plates, often inside compounds, then go on cooldown [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; a community guide puts the refresh at 60 seconds (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- **UAV Towers** are usually guarded; activating one completes a Side Op, pays DMZ Cash and pulses nearby Operators and enemies onto your map [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Since 23 Oct 2026 you can take a tower back from enemy control [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [clm_ae67c70adc88]. Most major POIs have one or two [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]]. More on [[uav-towers]].
- **SAM Sites**: hack the control computer and hold the site while a siren sounds; while you stay near, it shoots enemy aircraft, Deathstalkers included [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Enemies retake it once you leave [[src-allthingshow-hajin-map-2026-10-21]]. Known sites: a tent camp on South Island in [[hajin-river-heights]], a roof in Compound Echo and one at [[cheongun-village]] [[src-gamerfuzion-underground-orders-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-compound-echo-farm-2026-10-23]] [[src-allthingshow-hajin-map-2026-10-21]]. Pilots should avoid downtown SAM sites and the air base (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. The Air Superiority trait keeps SAM sites and UAV towers yours for longer [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_c3633bb72757].
- **Lieutenant Hunt towers** are tall radio towers: climb them and use the laptop on top to mark roughly where the nearest Lieutenant is ([[lieutenants]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. A fix of 29 Oct 2026 restored their missing interaction prompt during Side Ops [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Mapped at [[deadtown]], [[mirae-general-hospital]], [[wolves-stadium]] and [[hajin-river-heights]] (reported) [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]].
- **Bounty Stations**: a rugged laptop to see bounties on Operators or pay off your own ([[bounty-system]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; one sits on a roof near NuriGO East (reported) [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]].
- **Gun stores**: two, in west [[cheongun-village]] and north [[songdo-wharf]]; each Gun Store Weapons Locker key opens one locker [[src-allthingshow-gun-store-key-2026-10-22]] [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]], and players find three lockers together, so three keys empty a store [clm_2faa69935f53].
- **Blackmarket weapon lockers**: three mapped in the west, opened by a key from the Recon Equipment Kit (reported) [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]].
- **Dynamic Ops phones** start a Dynamic Operation in the field ([[dynamic-operations]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- **Hidden caches** sit off the beaten track, such as a cache in Deadtown's sewers, a highway service tunnel and an old shack in the north (reported) [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]]; a community guide also lists a military duffel under a stairwell and an irradiated pond [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- **Chemical lockers** (yellow, often labelled Chemical Storage) cluster at the air base, the reactor, the hospital and Cheongun Village; some hold an Irradiated Container, which is cleaned at the reactor's Decontamination Station (reported) [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]] ([[clinics]]).
- **Keypad-locked doors** open with a code: eight are pinned, several beside bank vaults (reported) [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]], and one Dead Man's Cache Op gave its door code in the objective text [[src-allthingshow-hajin-gameplay-2026-10-21]].
