Proximity chat lets you talk to any player near you, not only your squad: voices fade with distance, come from the speaker's direction and take on the echo of the surroundings [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. It is the fastest way to talk to nearby Operators; when voice is unavailable or best kept quiet, the **Ping Wheel** gives visual commands, including requesting a Truce or joining up with another squad [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The developers confirmed it returns in MW4's DMZ (Q&A of 9 Oct, as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## What is it for?
- Talking your way out: Infinity Ward says a solo player can use proximity chat with other squads or stay silent and stealthy [[src-destructoid-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]].
- Truces and joining up: squads can truce, betray or assimilate, and an assimilated squad is capped at four (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Players report that the truce function lets squads work through a deployment together (reported) [clm_3f9c4cb39462].
- An Order, Friendly Face, asks you to request a truce with the Ping Wheel (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## What should you watch for?
Betrayal is allowed (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. A player who keeps killing others builds a Bounty, and four player kills make them Wanted, which alerts Operators nearby [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How to team up: [[how-to-team-up-with-other-squads]].
