**From the developers' 9 Oct Q&A, via a video recap [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; most points were also reported by other accounts on X.**

- **Players.** At launch a Hajin match holds up to 60 players: 20 squads of three ([[hajin-exclusion-zone]]).
- **Queueing.** There is no separate party finder: you choose [[story-missions]], [[dynamic-operations]] or [[free-roam]] when you queue, and the matchmaker groups players who chose the same.
- **Solos and duos.** Matches aim to put solos with solos, duos with duos and trios with trios, but there is no guarantee. Squads can still truce, betray or assimilate, and an assimilated squad is capped at four players. The [[tourniquet]] and the [[stealth-meter]] were added with solos in mind. Squad Fill adds two allies if you want a full squad [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **Disconnects.** After a crash or disconnect you can rejoin while that match is still running.
- **Parties.** In a party the [[fob]] looks like the party leader's progress, while each station's menu shows your own progress and items.
- **Operators.** No skin toggle: DMZ lives in MW4, not Warzone, so Operators from older games do not appear.
- **No solo-only or PvE playlist** at launch: the "Single Squad Mission" option seen in a promotional screenshot was for internal testing, as the developers said in the Q&A (also relayed by [[src-thexclusiveace-bounty-video-2026-10-12]]).

**Crossplay.** Squads can party up across platforms, and friends are added in game by their Activision ID (official, 16 Oct) [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]].
