Keys open locked loot rooms in Hajin, and each key's description starts with the grid square of its door on the [[tac-map]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. ## How do keys work?

- **One door, then gone.** Most keys are used up when their door opens, and keys do not stack [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]] [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]]. Only the Air Base Access Card - Multi Use is reusable by name [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]].
- **Unlocking pays.** A locked door shows a padlock; with the key you get an unlock prompt, and opening it pays 300 XP [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-keys-2026-10-27]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-keys-2026-10-22]].
- **Protect it.** A key in a Secure Slot returns to your Stash if you die before using it; anywhere else it stays on your body, and it is lost on a failed exfil [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-keys-2026-10-27]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. The Medium Backpack is the cheapest with a Secure Slot; see [[backpacks]]. Keys in your [[stash]] must go into your loadout before you deploy (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]].
- **Do not sell keys.** They sell for about 100, so opening the room is nearly always worth more [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]. The Scout Minor Trait Key Cash adds DMZ Cash for each locked room you open, more for rarer keys ([[scout-tree]]) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]].
- **No key?** A [[door-breacher-charge]] opens a locked door once [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]]. A door locked from the inside has another way in, and rural areas hide underground entrances [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Where do keys come from?

- **Lieutenants** can drop the key to their own locked room [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[lieutenants]]. Infinity Ward said before launch that Lieutenant Hunt rewards include rare keys [[src-gamespot-from-the-ward-ep7-2026-10-05]].
- **Story Missions** each give one; the list is further down [[src-gfinity-story-mission-rewards-2026-10-23]].
- **Kits**: the Explosives and Trap Equipment Kits (DMZ levels 16 and 42) hold a Gun Store Weapons Locker Key; the Recon Equipment Kits hold the Police Station Key (level 19) and the Black Market Weapons Locker Key (level 46) [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. See [[kits]].
- **Containers and enemies**: duffel bags, jackets on coat racks, military bags, lockers, footlockers, tool chests and suitcases on the train; key rooms often hold another key [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-keys-2026-10-22]]. Players name [[hajin-river-heights]] as a good area to farm keys [clm_8dae00f30bfd].

## Which keys come from missions and bosses?

- **Story Missions**: Operation Critical Extraction gives the Mirae Electrical Room key, Silent Ledger the Counting Room Vault key, Shopping Hacks the Coin Mining Den key, Iron Citadel the Supreme Leader's Bunker Access Card, Grid Assault Roze's Highrise Armory key, and Fallen Ember the Reactor DRC Locker key [[src-gfinity-story-mission-rewards-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]]. The Air Base Access Card - Multi Use also comes only from Iron Citadel (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]]. See [[story-missions]].
- **Lieutenants**: Jin-Tae, Jang-Ui, The Chemist and The Doctor drop the Legendary keys to their own rooms, and rooms named after Gorani, Kang, Roze and Iskra are Legendary too [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]] [[src-dexerto-jang-ui-power-room-2026-10-24]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-keys-2026-10-27]]. The Chemist is also reported to drop a Black Keycard whose door is unknown [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]].
- **Ultra DRC lockers**: the three red keys open DRC weapons lockers at the reactor, under NuriGO Mall and in the air base bunker; they tie in with a DMZ Order that needs a DRC weapons locker opened [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]] [clm_2595c71db2ff]. See [[dmz-orders]].
