Operator Prestige in Modern Warfare 4 DMZ is an upgrade system intended to grant permanent prestige traits to an operator. Players care about this system because it offers long-term benefits across deployments, though multiple issues have been reported during the process.

## How does Operator Prestige work?

* Modern Warfare 4 DMZ features 12 possible prestige traits, as reported by players.
* Prestiging an operator in DMZ is supposed to award a retained trait, according to player reports.

## What issues affect Operator Prestige?

* Players report that prestiging an operator in DMZ resets traits, fails to provide the third perk, and removes all items.
* Reaching prestige 3 was reported by a player to reset the skill tree and consume requirements and cash without granting a permanent trait point.
* Using an ultra item to prestige an operator past prestige 3 is reported by players to reset the operator level to 1 without applying the prestige.
* Attempting to prestige an operator in DMZ can cause the game to time out while loading the instance inventory, as reported by players.

Operator Prestige lets you reset one Operator who has all 18 Trait Points (a Dog Tag at Onyx III) in exchange for rare gear and DMZ Cash; the Operator loses their Traits and gains a random permanent Prestige Trait [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. It is optional and per Operator, and it is separate from the 1 to 70 DMZ level ([[dmz-progression]]) and from the player Prestige of the 1 to 55 level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What do you need to Prestige an Operator?

The Operator must have earned 18 Trait Points and must not be MIA ([[mia-system]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The points must be earned but need not all be spent (reported) [[src-dmztracker-operator-prestige-2026-10-20]]. The gear has to be equipped on that Operator [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-operator-guide-2026-10-20]].

| Prestige | Weapons | Plate carrier and backpack | 3D Printer Ingredients | DMZ Cash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | 2, Rare or better, 3 attachment slots | Rare | 4 Rare | 200,000 |
| Second | 2, 4 attachment slots | Epic | 4 Epic | 300,000 |
| Third and later | 2, Legendary | Legendary | 4 Legendary | 500,000 |

Costs from the 9 Oct guide [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The live Prestige screen after launch asked for the same first-Prestige price: 18 Trait Points, eight Rare items (two weapons, a plate carrier, a backpack and four printer ingredients) and 200,000 DMZ Cash in the Bank (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. Keep the cash in your Bank before you start ([[wallet]]).

## What happens when you Prestige?

The gear and the cash are consumed and the Operator's Traits reset [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The Backpack is emptied too, so move valuables to the [[stash]] first [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-operator-guide-2026-10-20]]; the screen warns that whatever is left on the Operator is used up (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. You earn the Trait Points again by levelling the Dog Tag ([[dog-tags]]) [[src-dmztracker-operator-prestige-2026-10-20]], and players report the tag starting over [clm_a92a2067495e].

In return the Operator gets one random permanent Prestige Trait. It stays with them if you dismiss them, move them to another slot or change their skin; they can hold three. These Pro Traits sit in three Prestige slots of their own, outside the three trees, and cost no Trait Points (reported) [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]]. From the fourth Prestige you are offered a new one and either swap it for one of your three or keep the three [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_f979ff654d78]. Prestiging several Operators also earns calling cards and other rewards Activision has not revealed [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. One player says Min-Hee's Hajin Hound skin comes after five Prestiges (reported) [clm_201fd282270b].

## Does Double XP help you Prestige faster?

Not confirmed; see [[double-xp-tokens]]. A full Dog Tag takes about 450,000 Operator XP (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-xp-strategies-2026-10-30]]. The Fast Learner and Faster Learner Traits add Operator XP [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmztracker-level-up-fast-2026-10-22]]. ## Known problems

- **Listed as resolved since 6 Nov 2026:** Activision's known issues page, updated that day, moved DMZ Operator Prestige to its resolved list with one line saying the Prestige problems were addressed [[src-activision-known-issues-2026-11-07]]. Until then it listed two faults under investigation: a Prestige could take the items from your Stash and give no Prestige Trait, and some Operators could not Prestige at all even when they qualified [[src-activision-known-issues-2026-11-04]]. The page does not say which faults the fix covers or whether lost gear and DMZ Cash come back, and no patch note lists it [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. Press on 4 Nov 2026 had reported the same loss with no date for a fix (reported) [clm_7a1e93c85a77].
- On 21 Oct 2026 Infinity Ward acknowledged that Prestige failed for some Active Duty Operators. The workaround is to use Rare (blue) weapons that have only ever held three attachments [clm_ccfae152bab3] [[src-activision-known-issues-2026-11-04]].
- Players report Prestige taking the gear and cash and resetting the tag without the reward [clm_a92a2067495e]. One player reported losing a fourth-Prestige Operator after being kicked from a match during exfil [clm_b6179531b351].
- Reports from 1 and 2 Nov 2026, each from one player: Prestige still fails to start, which also blocks the last mastery camo and calling card [clm_0c8f2f5cab3c]; it can time out while loading the inventory [clm_eae0d58ce1e1]; and a Prestige reset the Traits and took the items and cash without granting the Trait it should give, including at a third Prestige [clm_4e48d9ed9499] [clm_698aa2d409ca] [clm_8541e0433130]. These came before the fix Activision listed on 6 Nov 2026; if a Prestige still fails after that date, report it to Activision support.
- Not the same as player Prestige: starting Classic Prestige from the DMZ menus blocked new Multiplayer classes (known issue of 28 Oct) [clm_6e5b27f5a3c3], and the same day a fix made Prestige from the DMZ FOB reset Multiplayer Loadouts correctly [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
