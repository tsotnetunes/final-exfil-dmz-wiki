DMZ has its own player level from 1 to 70, separate from the 1 to 55 player level, and every DMZ level gives a reward [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Each Operator also has a Dog Tag with 18 levels that pay Trait Points [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How does DMZ XP work?

DMZ XP comes from Story Missions, Dynamic Operations, Side Ops and other activities [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]: opening loot containers, DMZ Orders, Lieutenants, Commanders and combat, where rival Operators pay more than AI [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. It also counts toward the 1 to 55 player level, but Multiplayer and Warzone XP never raise the DMZ level [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [clm_5153b4480340]. You keep it even when you fail to exfil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_43eb9661dfec]. One fan site saw no XP from Orders (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-xp-strategies-2026-10-30]], against the official list.

## How much XP does a level take?

About 15,050 XP for level 2, rising to 18,634 for level 70, and 1,165,573 XP from 1 to 70: roughly 11 to 12 hours in matches at the best rates (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-xp-strategies-2026-10-30]]. A second fan capture agrees on 15,000 to 18,600 a level [[src-dmztracker-level-up-fast-2026-10-22]].

## What counts if you do not exfil?

All DMZ XP except the extraction bonus (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-xp-strategies-2026-10-30]]. An Operator you pay to recover keeps the XP they earned before dying [clm_ec258e1f0cc1]. Cash and gear: [[what-carries-over]].

## How do Dog Tag levels work?

Each Operator's Dog Tag rises through six tiers of three levels, and every level gives one Trait Point ([[trait-points]]); a good deployment can give more than one level. Operator level and DMZ level are separate [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. DMZ XP earned with an Operator raises their tag [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-level-up-fast-2026-10-21]].

| Tier | Levels | Trait Points by the end of the tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steel | I to III | 3 |
| Bronze | I to III | 6 |
| Silver | I to III | 9 |
| Gold | I to III | 12 |
| Damascus | I to III | 15 |
| Onyx | I to III | 18 |

The first level takes about 8,372 XP and each next one about 12% more, roughly 450,000 XP from Steel I to Onyx III; the Story Mission completion award does not count for the Operator (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-xp-strategies-2026-10-30]]. At Onyx III an Operator can Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]). The tag also shows your Bounty ([[dog-tags]]).

## What is the fastest way to level?

Start each deployment with a Story Mission (one per deployment), chain Dynamic Ops, take Side Ops on your route and extract every time [[src-dmztracker-level-up-fast-2026-10-22]]. One site measured about 105,000 XP an hour on the Iron Citadel Story Mission and 95,000 an hour from Dynamic Ops (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-xp-strategies-2026-10-30]]. An S rating or better earns the best rewards [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]. For Operators, the Fast Learner and Faster Learner Traits add XP [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]]; whether 2XP tokens help is not confirmed ([[double-xp-tokens]]).
