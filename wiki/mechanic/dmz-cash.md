DMZ Cash is the mode's currency. It sits in your Bank ([[wallet]]), which all of your Active Duty Operators share, and pays for nearly everything at the FOB [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Cash you find in Hajin counts only once you extract, while Dynamic Op rewards go straight to the Bank (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-level-up-fast-2026-10-21]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [clm_f17f3ff635f7].

## Can you sell or barter?

You can sell items at the FOB for DMZ Cash: the fixes of 20 Oct 2026 mention selling starting equipment [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]], and community databases list a cash value for valuables [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. Several valuables are also needed for [[fob-upgrades]], so check before selling, and cash beyond your Bank cap is lost (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Neither official guide mentions bartering; the Vendor takes DMZ Cash.
