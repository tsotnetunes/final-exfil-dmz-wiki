Hajin's weather changes from one match to the next: sudden rain cuts how far you can see, and thick fog changes which routes are safe [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. Players say it makes the map feel different each time they load in [clm_5716a58c7e46] [clm_762f70bd4aec].

## What weather is there?

The developers listed sun, cloud, rainstorms, fog and mist, and possibly snow. There is no night mode and no day-night cycle at launch; dark interiors are the reason NVGs are in the loot pool [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. In launch week one player crossed a foggy Pyonghwa Village (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-irradiated-container-2026-10-27]].

## Does weather change how enemies behave?

Yes: Infinity Ward told press that weather affects what AI soldiers can see and hear [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]]. Rain and fog that hide you from a rifle also hide you from patrols, which helps the [[stealth-meter]].

## What else moves on the map?

Enemy convoys drive the roads, helicopters cross the sky, and hostile forces move strategic weapons during a match [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. The closing hazard of each match is radiation, not weather ([[radiation-storm]]).

## Will it change?

A community guide says weather will change with the seasons (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. The Season 1 post of 28 Oct 2026 announces no weather change for Hajin [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].
