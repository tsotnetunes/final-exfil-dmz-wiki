DMZ Orders are the guided task list of DMZ: 24 pages of three Orders each that teach the systems, carry the story and send you across Hajin, and every finished page pays 20,000 DMZ Cash plus gear, kits or 3D Printer recipes [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. They are always available and do not expire [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Where do you see your Orders?

At the FOB's Orders station, on the Orders Tablet ([[orders]]) [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]; during a deployment, in the Orders menu of the [[tac-map]], where an Order's location can be pinged [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]]. The active Order sits on the right of the HUD, one at a time [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported). Items an Order needs are marked for you automatically [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Finished Orders and their rewards appear in the [[after-action-report]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How do pages and chapters work?

You must finish every task line on the active page before the next one opens [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. A page pays once all three of its Orders are done, not per Order [[src-game8-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. Tasks tick during the match; only tasks that say "extract" need a successful exfil [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]], and one player's page finished and paid without any exfil [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).

After page 1 you choose your first chapter from Human Intel, Underground and Counterfeit, and after page 11 between The Grid and Hornet's Nest; each chapter is two pages and ends with a Story Mission [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. Page numbers therefore depend on your choices [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]:

| Pages | What is there | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First Orders: Gear Up, Crackshot, First Op | [[dmz-orders-pages]] |
| 2 and 3, 5 and 6, 8 and 9 | The three first chapters, in the order you pick them | [[human-intel-orders]], [[underground-orders]], [[counterfeit-orders]] |
| 4, 7, 10, 11 | Fixed pages | [[dmz-orders-pages]] |
| 12 and 13, 16 and 17 | The Grid and Hornet's Nest, in the order you pick them | [[the-grid-orders]], [[hornets-nest-orders]] |
| 14, 15, 18 to 24 | Fixed pages; page 21 holds [[operation-fallen-ember]] | [[dmz-orders-pages]] |

After page 15 numbers can differ between accounts, and page 24 was the last page for the players who reached it [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] (reported). Players report an ending scene once all 24 pages are done [clm_34cd336f3bfa]; the story is set to continue with the Russian area that opens in Season 2 [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [clm_12a1524f7169].

## What do Orders unlock?

- The [[boss-board]] opens after your second completed Order [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]].
- The [[story-missions]] open after page 1 and your first deployment [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]; Infinity Ward described this link before launch [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]].
- Some Lieutenants on the Boss Board stay locked until you finish chapters [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- Recipes, kits and gear from page rewards; Orders also pay DMZ XP and are among the quickest ways to level ([[dmz-progression]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]].

## Do Orders reset or refresh?

No rotation has been reported: pages stay until you finish them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The only weekly link is the Philanthropist Order on page 19, which wants that week's Random Mission Deploy challenge [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Whether Orders progress resets with a new season is not confirmed yet.
