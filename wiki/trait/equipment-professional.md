Equipment Professional is a Major Trait in the [[warfighter-tree]]: after you use an equipped Lethal or Tactical, it slowly recharges [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]], up to what the slot can hold [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. How long a recharge takes is not published.

## Where is it in the tree?
It is one of the two Majors on row 3 of Warfighter, beside [[combat-resistance]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]]. You take one of the pair, and the choice is permanent for that Operator until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do you unlock it?
Spend Trait Points in Warfighter. Every pick costs one point and row 3 is the third pick, after the random starting Minor and one Minor from row 2 [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. So it takes three points spent in this tree; points come from the Operator's Dog Tag levels ([[trait-points]]).

## Is there a Pro version?
No. Only the Majors of rows 6 and 9 have Pro versions ([[pro-traits]]) [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]].

## Is it worth taking?
It helps players who throw a lot of equipment, because the slot refills without spares. Do not confuse it with [[auto-fill]], a Minor Trait that refills the slot from Lethals and Tacticals carried in your backpack [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. COD Central's solo build takes Equipment Professional with Viper Sense and Possum [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]; the creator build reported by GamerFuzion takes [[combat-resistance]] instead [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]].
