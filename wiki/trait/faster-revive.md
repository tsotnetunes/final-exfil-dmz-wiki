Faster Revive is a Major Trait in the [[survivalist-tree]] that shortens the time a revive takes [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. How much faster is not published, and the official text does not say whether it also speeds up your own self-revive.

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 3 of Survivalist, paired with [[consumable-duration]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
The third pick in Survivalist: the random starting Minor, one Minor from row 2, then this row, one Trait Point each [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. The choice holds until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How points are earned: [[trait-points]].

## Is there a Pro version?
No. Pro versions exist only for the Majors of rows 6 and 9 ([[pro-traits]]) [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## Is it worth taking?
In a squad, yes: COD Central and Playhub put it in their squad builds with [[impact-smokes]] and [[numb-the-pain]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]], and the creator's squad build reported by GamerFuzion goes into Survivalist for it [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. Solo, it does little, because revive Traits help when someone else is there to revive [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]]; solo builds take Consumable Duration on this row [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]. Reviving and Last Stand: [[health-and-healing]], [[tourniquet]]. The Minor Trait Rescuer, which speeds up dragging a downed teammate, fits the same role ([[survivalist-tree]]).

No patch note mentions it up to 31 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
