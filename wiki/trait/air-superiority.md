Air Superiority is a Minor Trait: UAV Towers and SAM sites that you activate stay active for longer [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]] [clm_5cb5d20deed5] [clm_c3633bb72757]. How much longer is not published.

## What do UAV Towers and SAM sites do?
Both are Side Ops on the map. Activating a UAV Tower pays DMZ Cash and pulses nearby Operators and enemies onto your map; at a SAM site you hack the control computer and hold the site, and while you stay near, its battery shoots at enemy aircraft such as Deathstalkers [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[locations-of-interest]], [[uav-towers]], [[commander-threats]]). Since 23 Oct 2026 UAV Towers can be stolen back from enemy control [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Is it a Major or a Minor Trait?
Minor. It was the Survivalist example of the random Minor bought by a tree's first point, listed next to the tree's six Majors in the official guide [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and the trait databases file it as a Minor [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]].

## How do you get it?
It costs one Trait Point like every Trait, but Minor Traits come from one pool of 48 and sit in random slots that differ between Operators [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]]; guides have seen it in Survivalist [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]]. The pick stays until the Operator Prestiges ([[operator-prestige]]). See [[survivalist-tree]] and [[trait-points]].

## Is it worth taking?
COD Central rates it situational [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. If the same Operator has [[overwatch]] (Scout, row 9), its towers never expire anyway, so only the SAM site part of Air Superiority adds anything (our reading of the two descriptions).
