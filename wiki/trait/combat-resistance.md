Combat Resistance is a Major Trait in the [[warfighter-tree]]: explosives deal you less damage, and enemy Tacticals have a weaker effect on you [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. The size of either reduction is not published.

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 3 of Warfighter, paired with [[equipment-professional]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. Picking one locks out the other for that Operator, and picks stay until the Operator Prestiges ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do you unlock it?
It is the third pick in Warfighter. Each pick costs one Trait Point, so it takes three points in this tree: the random starting Minor, one Minor from row 2, then this row [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. Points come from Dog Tag levels ([[trait-points]]).

## Is there a Pro version?
No. Pro versions exist only for the Majors of rows 6 and 9 ([[pro-traits]]) [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## Is it worth taking?
It is the row 3 choice for players who expect to take fights. The creator build reported by GamerFuzion pairs it with Quick Fix and Possum for solo play [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]], and Playhub's walkthrough of the tree puts it ahead of Equipment Professional (reported) [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]. COD Central's solo build takes Equipment Professional on this row instead [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. DMZ has four launchers among its 32 launch weapons [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and weapon damage in DMZ has its own tuning ([[movement-and-tuning]]).

No patch note mentions it up to 31 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
