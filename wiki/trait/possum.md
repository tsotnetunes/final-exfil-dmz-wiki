<!-- human:start -->
Possum is the top Major Trait of the [[warfighter-tree]]: once per match, after you are killed, it puts you back in Last Stand with full health [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. It works after you have been fully killed, not only downed [[src-dexerto-possum-complaints-2026-10-21]]. From Last Stand you get up the usual ways, such as the [[tourniquet]] or a squadmate's revive ([[health-and-healing]]).

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?
Infinity Ward limited it. You can no longer trigger Possum while the squad that killed you is nearby, and the patch note adds a 4-minute limit, which guides read as the point after which it works even if they stay [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [clm_2c5d28cefa18]. The developers say both the distance and the time can be tuned later [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [clm_0d5fa6dad722]. The same update fixed a bug where Possum brought back players who had already given up on their Operator, leaving them stuck, and a black screen that appeared when every member of a squad died while one of them carried Possum [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Why did players call it overpowered?
Before the change it had no time limit, made no sound, and every squad member could carry it [[src-dexerto-possum-complaints-2026-10-21]] [[src-destructoid-possum-debate-2026-10-24]]. Killed players could come back in the middle of a fight, or wait, revive unnoticed and exfil, and some used it to avoid paying the MIA rescue fee ([[mia-system]]) [[src-destructoid-possum-debate-2026-10-24]]. Players suggested a longer activation, a sound cue or a wait of about 30 seconds (reported) [clm_4d662dbb6a40]. Supporters said it makes DMZ more forgiving, especially for solo players [[src-destructoid-possum-debate-2026-10-24]]. Its intent is described as a second chance for a well-levelled Operator (reported) [clm_9d97c2d998df].

## How do you unlock it?
It sits on row 9 of Warfighter opposite [[adaptive-restock]], so it takes all nine Warfighter picks at one Trait Point each [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. Choosing it rules out Adaptive Restock on that Operator until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. With 18 points in all, a full Warfighter tree leaves nine for one other tree ([[trait-points]]).

## What does Possum Pro add?
It also removes the tourniquet's health penalty [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. COD Central ranks it the best Pro Trait [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. See [[pro-traits]].

## Is it worth taking?
Dexerto calls it the best Trait in DMZ, and most build guides include it, solo builds above all [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. To deny an enemy's Possum, Playhub suggests trapping the body or staying near it (reported) [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]; since 28 Oct staying near also blocks the revive, at least until the 4-minute limit runs out [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
