Pro Traits are the permanent Traits that Operator Prestige hands out: each Prestige grants one at random, an Operator keeps up to three, and they stay with that Operator for good [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Guides that read the in-game Prestige Traits menu list twelve, four per tree, each an upgraded version of a Major Trait from row 6 or row 9 of its tree [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]; players count twelve as well [clm_cb769aa684e4]. The official patch notes name two of them, Impact Smokes Pro and Numb the Pain Pro [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How do you get a Pro Trait?
Prestige an Operator who is not MIA and has collected all 18 Trait Points ([[operator-prestige]]). The Prestige uses up the required gear and DMZ Cash and clears the regular Traits, and the Operator receives a random permanent Trait. Up to three are kept; from the fourth Prestige on you are offered a new one and either swap it for one of your three or keep what you have [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_f979ff654d78]. You cannot pick which one you get [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. An Operator keeps them through a dismissal, a move to another Active Duty slot or a new skin [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. They sit in three Prestige slots of their own, apart from the three trees, and cost no Trait Points (reported) [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## Which Pro Traits are best?
COD Central puts Possum Pro, Overwatch Pro, Quick Fix Pro and Viper Sense Pro at the top, and Numb the Pain Pro, Die Hard Pro, Impact Smokes Pro and Thermal Padding Pro at the bottom [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. Since the first three are random, a ranking matters most when the fourth and later Prestiges offer a swap.

## What has changed?
Impact Smokes Pro did not trigger when a player was downed until a fix on 20 Oct 2026, and the descriptions of Numb the Pain and Numb the Pain Pro were corrected on 23 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What is not known yet?
