Overwatch is a Major Trait in the [[scout-tree]]: once you activate a UAV Tower, it never runs out [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. A player report of a "permanent UAV" Trait [clm_24b10c8643de] most likely means this one. Activating a tower is a Side Op that pays DMZ Cash and pulses nearby Operators and enemies onto the map [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[uav-towers]], [[locations-of-interest]]).

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 9, the top of Scout, opposite [[thermal-padding]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
Fill the Scout tree: nine picks at one Trait Point each [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. It is permanent until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How points are earned: [[trait-points]].

## What does Overwatch Pro add?
Towers you hold also cover a wider area [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. COD Central ranks it among the four best Pro Traits [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. See [[pro-traits]].

## Has it changed?
In the first days some players complained of too many UAVs and asked for Overwatch to be removed (reported) [clm_9bddae4cc5ea]. Destructoid wrote on 24 Oct that its unlimited UAV coverage had been reined in by a quick patch [[src-destructoid-possum-debate-2026-10-24]]; the official notes of 23 Oct 2026 say UAV Towers can now be stolen back from enemy control [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Whether a stolen tower keeps running for its new owner, and for how long, is not stated.

## Is it worth taking?
Squad and intel builds favour it, often with [[uav-upgrade]] [[src-destructoid-best-operator-traits-2026-10-20]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]. Solo players usually take Thermal Padding on this row ([[thermal-padding]]). A cheaper, temporary option is the Minor Trait [[air-superiority]], which keeps towers and SAM sites running for longer.
