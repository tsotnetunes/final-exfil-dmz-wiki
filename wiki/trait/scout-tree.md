Scout is the looting and intel tree of the three Trait trees: its six Major Traits add loot from AI and containers, stronger UAVs, crafting in the field, cover from UAVs and towers that never switch off [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]]. Every pick costs one Trait Point and nine picks fill the tree ([[trait-points]]).

## How is the tree laid out?
Nine rows read from the bottom, one pick per row: row 1 is a random Minor bought by your first point in the tree; rows 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8 offer three hidden Minor Traits; rows 3, 6 and 9 offer two fixed Major Traits [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Picks are permanent until the Operator Prestiges ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

| Row | Major Trait | What it does | Pro version adds |

| 3 | [[hostile-looter]] | AI can drop an Uncommon printer ingredient now and then, a Rare one seldom | no Pro version |

| 9 | [[overwatch]] | towers you switch on never time out | towers cover a wider area |
