Hostile Looter is a Major Trait in the [[scout-tree]]: AI enemies can drop an Uncommon 3D Printer ingredient now and then, and on rare occasions a Rare one [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. Drop rates are not published. Ingredients are what the [[3d-printer]] needs, beside the four basic Resources, for better gear and FOB upgrades [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 3 of Scout, paired with [[container-looter]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
Three Trait Points in Scout: the random starting Minor, one Minor from row 2, then this pick, one point each [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. The choice between the two looting Traits is permanent until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How points are earned: [[trait-points]].

## Is there a Pro version?
No. Pro versions exist only for the Majors of rows 6 and 9 ([[pro-traits]]) [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## Is it worth taking?
Dexerto prefers it to Container Looter and says it stacks with the Minor Trait Combat Scavenger, which lets AI occasionally leave an ingredient or a valuable (reported) [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]]. COD Central and Destructoid pick Container Looter for steadier loot [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-destructoid-best-operator-traits-2026-10-20]], and the creator build reported by GamerFuzion took Hostile Looter without having tested it [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. It pays off most for players who fight a lot of AI, since every kill is a roll.
