Adrenaline Burst is a Minor Trait: when you kill an enemy, your tactical sprint is refilled [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]] [clm_cac10a9e9be1]. Tactical sprint is on by default in DMZ, so the Trait helps anyone who pushes on after a kill ([[movement-and-tuning]]) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## Is it a Major or a Minor Trait?
Minor. The official guide listed it among the Warfighter examples, which were the tree's six Major Traits plus the random Minor that the first point bought [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; the trait databases and guides file Adrenaline Burst as that Minor [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. Warfighter's Majors are on [[warfighter-tree]].

## How do you get it?
Minor Traits are placed at random, from one pool of 48 that can land in any tree, and you take one from each Minor row you pass [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]]. Guides have seen Adrenaline Burst in Warfighter [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]]; in the official example it was the random Minor bought by the first Warfighter point [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Where it shows up differs between Operators, so look at each Operator's tree. Like every Trait it costs one Trait Point ([[trait-points]]), and like every pick it stays until the Operator Prestiges ([[operator-prestige]]). Minor Traits have no Pro versions ([[pro-traits]]).

## Is it worth taking?
COD Central rates it situational [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. It suits players who chain fights. Killing Spree, another Warfighter Minor, works on kills too, by stretching effects already running on you up to double their length ([[warfighter-tree]]).
