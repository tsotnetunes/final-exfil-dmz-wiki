Die Hard is a Major Trait in the [[survivalist-tree]] with two effects while you are in Last Stand: there is no bleed-out timer, and coming close to an exfil calls the extraction automatically [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. How exfils work: [[exfil]].

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 9, the top of Survivalist, paired with [[numb-the-pain]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
All nine Survivalist picks at one Trait Point each [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. The choice holds until the Operator Prestiges ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. With 18 points in all, a full Survivalist tree leaves nine for one other tree ([[trait-points]]).

## What does Die Hard Pro add?
Faster movement while in Last Stand [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. COD Central ranks it among the weaker Pro Traits [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. See [[pro-traits]].

## Is it worth taking?
For solo players it is one of the two most valuable Traits, with [[possum]], because nobody else can revive them [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]. With no bleed-out you can crawl to an exfil at your own pace, but rival players and AI can still finish you on the way, and you still have to survive until the helicopter lands ([[exfil]]). In a squad, Numb the Pain is the more common pick on this row [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]].
