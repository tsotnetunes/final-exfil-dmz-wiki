Consumable Duration is a Major Trait in the [[survivalist-tree]]: the effects of consumables last longer [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. The extra time is not published, and the official text does not list which consumables count. DMZ's consumables include stamina items such as energy drinks, stim shots and smelling salts [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] ([[movement-and-tuning]]).

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 3 of Survivalist, paired with [[faster-revive]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
Three Trait Points in Survivalist, one per row up to row 3 [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. It is permanent until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How points are earned: [[trait-points]].

## Is there a Pro version?
No ([[pro-traits]]) [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## Is it worth taking?
It is the usual solo choice on this row, since Faster Revive needs a teammate [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]. Destructoid rates it because consumables matter in fights [[src-destructoid-best-operator-traits-2026-10-20]]. A related Minor Trait, Consumable Stacker, lets a consumable you use again while it is active add time, up to double [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] ([[survivalist-tree]]); whether the two combine is not confirmed.
