Impact Smokes is a Major Trait in the [[survivalist-tree]]: when you go down, smoke is released around you [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]], which hides you while you wait in Last Stand for a revive or reach for your [[tourniquet]] ([[health-and-healing]]). The size and length of the smoke are not published.

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 6 of Survivalist, paired with [[free-revive]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
Six Trait Points in Survivalist, one pick per row, including a row 3 Major ([[faster-revive]] or [[consumable-duration]]) [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. It stays until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How points are earned: [[trait-points]].

## What does Impact Smokes Pro add?
A bigger smoke cloud that lasts longer [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. COD Central ranks it among the weaker Pro Traits [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. See [[pro-traits]].

## Has it changed?
A bug that stopped Impact Smokes Pro from triggering when a player was downed was fixed on 20 Oct 2026, the first day of DMZ early access [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Is it worth taking?
Squad builds take it to cover a downed player until a teammate gets there [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]. Solo builds usually take Free Revive on this row [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]].
