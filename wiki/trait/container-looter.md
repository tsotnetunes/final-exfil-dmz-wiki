## How do you unlock it?
The third pick in Scout, one Trait Point per pick, after the random starting Minor and a Minor from row 2 [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. Picks are permanent until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How points are earned: [[trait-points]].

## Is it worth taking?
Destructoid calls it an early investment because loot is the centre of DMZ, and both of COD Central's builds, solo and squad, start Scout with it [[src-destructoid-best-operator-traits-2026-10-20]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. Dexerto prefers Hostile Looter instead [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]]. Opening loot containers also earns DMZ XP [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[dmz-progression]]).
