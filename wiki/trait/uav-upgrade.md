UAV Upgrade is a Major Trait in the [[scout-tree]]: UAVs you use get more range and a longer duration, and they are upgraded to Advanced UAVs [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. Exact range and time are not published, and the official text does not say whether it changes [[uav-towers]]; the Traits for towers are [[overwatch]] and [[air-superiority]].

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 6 of Scout, paired with [[field-crafting]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
Six Trait Points in Scout, one per row up to row 6, including one of the two row 3 looting Majors ([[hostile-looter]] or [[container-looter]]) [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. The pick lasts until the Operator Prestiges ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What does UAV Upgrade Pro add?
A larger minimap, on top of the stronger UAVs [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. See [[pro-traits]].

## Is it worth taking?
Destructoid and COD Central pair it with Overwatch for constant intel, and COD Central's squad build takes both [[src-destructoid-best-operator-traits-2026-10-20]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]; it is also in the solo build reported by GamerFuzion [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. Counterplay to know: rivals with [[thermal-padding]] are hidden from normal UAVs, but an Advanced UAV still shows them the way a normal UAV would [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Has it changed?
Not the Trait itself, but what it boosts did: on 10 Nov 2026 the UAV and Advanced UAV Fire Support items lost much of their duration and range, and both were switched off in [[x01]], so this Trait does nothing for Killstreak UAVs there ([[fire-support]]) [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. Earlier, since 6 Nov 2026 a tower upgraded with this Trait to show direction no longer sees players using [[thermal-padding]], Thermal Padding Pro or a Sedative Inhaler; until then it did, which the notes of 2 Nov had called a bug to fix in Season 1 [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. The same update reworded the FOB descriptions of UAV Upgrade and UAV Upgrade Pro without changing them [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. A change to UAV health on 21 Oct 2026 was for the Multiplayer killstreak [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. One player reported on 2 Nov that Advanced UAVs do not show elevation, so a marked enemy could be on another floor (reported) [clm_ab817680481b].
