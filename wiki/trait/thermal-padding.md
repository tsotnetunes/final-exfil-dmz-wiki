<!-- human:start -->
Thermal Padding is a Major Trait in the [[scout-tree]]: you are hidden from UAVs, and an Advanced UAV reveals you only the way a normal UAV would [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. Despite the name, the official text is about UAVs, not thermal scopes; hiding from thermal optics is [[viper-sense]] in Warfighter.

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 9, the top of Scout, paired with [[overwatch]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
All nine Scout picks at one Trait Point each, so the whole tree [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. It rules out Overwatch on that Operator until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. With 18 points in all, a full Scout tree leaves nine for one other tree ([[trait-points]]).

## What does Thermal Padding Pro add?
A vehicle you ride in is also kept off UAVs [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. COD Central nonetheless ranks it among the weaker Pro Traits [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. See [[pro-traits]].

## Is it worth taking?
For solo play several guides make it the key Scout pick, since a lone Operator has nobody to cover them once a UAV shows where they are [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. Squads that hold [[uav-towers]] often take Overwatch instead. Since 6 Nov 2026 the patch notes say it hides you from UAV Towers too, including towers a rival has upgraded with [[uav-upgrade]] to show direction; the AUAV fire support still finds you [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] ([[fire-support]]).

## Has it changed?
Yes, on 6 Nov 2026. A small Season 1 update made Thermal Padding, Thermal Padding Pro and the Sedative Inhaler hide you from UAVs and from towers upgraded by [[uav-upgrade]]; before that, such towers could still show you, a gap the notes of 2 Nov had promised to close [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. The same update reworded the FOB description of Thermal Padding Pro; the effect itself did not change [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. A player report of 2 Nov, of being seen on a rival's UAV with Thermal Padding Pro under an Inhaler, came before this fix (reported) [clm_531b123e8546]. The AUAV fire support is the one sensor it does not beat [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
