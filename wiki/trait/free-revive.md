Free Revive is a Major Trait in the [[survivalist-tree]]: the first Wound you take is repaired for you, with no medical item [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. A Wound is what the [[tourniquet]] leaves you with: it gets you up from Last Stand at 100 health instead of the full 150, and it cannot be used again until you are back at full health; normally a Medkit repairs the Wound [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Whether "first" means once per match is not spelled out.

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 6 of Survivalist, paired with [[impact-smokes]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
Six Trait Points in Survivalist, one per row up to row 6 [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. It is permanent until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How points are earned: [[trait-points]].

## What does Free Revive Pro add?
You may also use the tourniquet while Wounded [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]], which the normal rule forbids until you are back at full health. See [[pro-traits]].

## Is it worth taking?
Solo builds favour it on this row, because it saves you a Medkit after your first tourniquet [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]; squads often take [[impact-smokes]] instead [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. COD Central rates Free Revive Pro as a good Pro Trait [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. Full rules on health: [[health-and-healing]].
