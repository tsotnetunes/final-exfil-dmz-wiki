Warfighter is the combat tree of the three Trait trees: its six Major Traits cover equipment, damage resistance, concealment, health regeneration and a self-revive, and it ends with [[possum]] or [[adaptive-restock]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]]. Every pick costs one Trait Point and nine picks fill the tree ([[trait-points]]).

## How is the tree laid out?
Nine rows read from the bottom, one pick per row [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]:

| Row | What it holds | You take |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | one random Minor Trait, bought by your first point in the tree | that Minor |
| 2, 4, 5, 7, 8 | three Minor Traits, hidden until you reach the row | one of the three |
| 3, 6, 9 | two fixed Major Traits | one of the two |

Picks are permanent until the Operator Prestiges ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What are the Warfighter Major Traits?
| Row | Major Trait | What it does | Pro version adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | [[equipment-professional]] | a used Lethal or Tactical slowly comes back | no Pro version |
| 3 | [[combat-resistance]] | less explosive damage, weaker enemy Tacticals | no Pro version |
| 6 | [[viper-sense]] | thermal scopes and AI targeting cannot see you | a warning when an enemy aims at you |
| 6 | [[quick-fix]] | a kill shortens the wait before health regenerates | regeneration starts at once |
| 9 | [[possum]] | once per match, come back from death in Last Stand at full health | no tourniquet health penalty |
| 9 | [[adaptive-restock]] | AI drop one extra item | the extra item can be rarer |

## Which Warfighter Traits are best?
[[possum]] is the most recommended Trait in DMZ [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. On row 6 guides split between [[quick-fix]] and [[viper-sense]]; on row 3 between [[combat-resistance]] and [[equipment-professional]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]. Solo builds often pair a full Warfighter tree with Scout [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. Among the Minors, COD Central rates Plate Speed and Plate Pouch as must-takes and the Popcorn and ammo Minors as skips [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]].
