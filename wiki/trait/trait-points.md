Trait Points are how an Operator buys Traits: each Dog Tag level gives one, up to 18 at Onyx III, and every Trait costs one point in one of three trees, the [[warfighter-tree]], the [[scout-tree]] and the [[survivalist-tree]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. Picks are permanent; only an Operator Prestige resets them. Players also call the trees skill trees [clm_2ecafe15d1ab].

## How do you earn Trait Points?
Each Operator has its own Dog Tag with 18 levels: Steel, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Damascus and Onyx, each in I, II and III. Every level gives one Trait Point, and a strong deployment can raise the tag more than one level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The Operator banks the XP for it when you exfil successfully [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]; XP comes from missions, objectives, opening containers and combat ([[dmz-progression]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-cod-dmz-progression-video-2026-10-07]]. If the Operator dies and you pay the rescue, they come back with their tree and the XP they earned before dying [clm_ec258e1f0cc1]. The Dog Tag level is separate from your 1 to 70 DMZ level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and points belong to the Operator who earned them [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [clm_2ecafe15d1ab]. The XP needed per level is not published.

## How many Trait Points do you get?
18 per Operator, one per Dog Tag level; Onyx III is the cap [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. That fills two trees of nine picks or spreads across all three [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Common splits in guides are 9/9/0 for two full trees and 6/6/6 to reach the first two Major rows of every tree [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]].

## How do the trait trees work?
Each tree holds 22 Traits: 6 Major Traits that you can always see and 16 Minor Traits that are random and stay hidden until you reach them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Your opening point in a tree gets you a random Minor; after that you take one Trait per branch, Majors in pairs and Minors in threes, nine picks at most per tree [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Guides map this to nine rows: row 1 the random Minor, rows 3, 6 and 9 the Major pairs, the other rows three Minors each, one point per pick [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. The Majors are the same on every Operator; the Minor Traits come from a shared pool of 48 and land in different trees and rows on different Operators (reported by several guides) [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]]. One build planner adds that each pick must link to a pick in the row below it (reported) [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]]. You spend points at the Operators station of the FOB ([[active-duty]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Can you reset or respec Traits?
No. A Trait you pick is locked in, and there is no refund [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]]. The two ways to start over are an Operator Prestige, which clears the regular Traits, or dismissing the Operator and recruiting a new one, who starts with none [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]].

## Do Traits survive MIA, dismissal or Prestige?
- **MIA:** paying DMZ Cash to recover a killed Operator keeps all their Traits; the price rises with their Trait Points, and they return without gear ([[mia-system]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **Dismissal:** the replacement Operator starts without Traits [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]; permanent Prestige Traits stay with the dismissed Operator [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Whether regular Traits return if you recruit the same Operator again is not confirmed.
- **Prestige:** open once the Operator has earned all 18 points and is not MIA. It consumes gear and DMZ Cash, clears the regular Traits so you earn the points again, and adds one random permanent Prestige Trait, up to three ([[operator-prestige]], [[pro-traits]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **Seasons:** whether Season 1 (6 Nov 2026) resets Traits is not confirmed yet.

**Solo.** [[possum]] and [[die-hard]] are the picks guides agree on, because nobody else can revive you [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]].
