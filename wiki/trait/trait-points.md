Each Operator earns Trait Points by levelling their Dog Tag: 18 levels from Steel I to Onyx III, one point per level, sometimes more than one level after a good deployment [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Points go into three trees, Warfighter, Scout and Survivalist, 22 Traits each and 66 in all; a tree has 6 Major Traits (always visible) and 16 Minor Traits (random, revealed as you go). The first point in a tree buys a random Minor Trait, then you pick one Trait per branch, up to nine per tree; picks are permanent [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. With 18 points you can finish two trees or spread over three [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Traits persist per Operator [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]], and more Traits make an MIA Operator dearer to recover ([[mia-system]]).

**Examples from the 9 Oct guide** [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Warfighter: [[adrenaline-burst]], Equipment Professional, Combat Resistance, [[viper-sense]], [[quick-fix]], Possum, Adaptive Restock. Scout: Investor, Hostile Looter, Container Looter, Thermal Padding, UAV Upgrade, Overwatch, Field Crafting. Survivalist: [[air-superiority]], Faster Revive, Consumable Duration, Impact Smokes, Free Revive, Numb the Pain, Die Hard. Permanent Traits: [[operator-prestige]].
