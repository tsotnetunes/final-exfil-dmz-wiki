Numb the Pain is a Major Trait in the [[survivalist-tree]]: a revive also applies the effects of Painkillers and Smelling Salts [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. Smelling salts are one of the items that refill or extend stamina [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] ([[movement-and-tuning]]); what Painkillers do in DMZ is not described in the official guides. The official text also does not say whether the reviver, the revived player or both get the effects.

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 9, the top of Survivalist, paired with [[die-hard]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
Fill the Survivalist tree: nine picks at one Trait Point each [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. It rules out Die Hard on that Operator until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How points are earned: [[trait-points]].

## What does Numb the Pain Pro add?
The Painkiller and Smelling Salts effects last longer [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. One trait database still shows the same text for the Pro version as for the base Trait [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]], so check the in-game description. See [[pro-traits]].

## Has it changed?
The in-game descriptions of Numb the Pain and Numb the Pain Pro did not match what the Traits do; the text was corrected on 23 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. The notes do not say which part was wrong.

## Is it worth taking?
It is a squad Trait: Destructoid says it makes a large difference in team fights, and COD Central and Playhub put it in their squad builds [[src-destructoid-best-operator-traits-2026-10-20]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]. Solo players take Die Hard on this row, since nobody revives them [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]].
