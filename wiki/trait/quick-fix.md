Quick Fix is a Major Trait in the [[warfighter-tree]]: when you kill an enemy Operator or an AI enemy, the wait before your health starts to regenerate gets shorter [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. Players sum it up as faster health regeneration [clm_a2e0e2cd58fd]. How much shorter is not published. Health, wounds and the 150 maximum: [[health-and-healing]].

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 6 of Warfighter, paired with [[viper-sense]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
Six Trait Points in Warfighter, one per pick: the random starting Minor, a Minor from row 2, a row 3 Major, Minors from rows 4 and 5, then this row [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. Taking it rules out Viper Sense on that Operator until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How points are earned: [[trait-points]].

## What does Quick Fix Pro add?
Regeneration starts right away after a kill instead of after a shorter delay [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. COD Central ranks it among the four best Pro Traits [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. See [[pro-traits]].

## Is it worth taking?
Destructoid recommends it for players who like to fight, and it is in the solo build reported by GamerFuzion [[src-destructoid-best-operator-traits-2026-10-20]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]; Dexerto and COD Central prefer Viper Sense on this row ([[viper-sense]]). The Minor Trait Quick Regen, which makes health start regenerating a little sooner in general, does a similar job without needing a kill ([[warfighter-tree]]).

## Has it changed?
Not in DMZ up to 31 Oct 2026. Multiplayer has a Perk with the same name, and the patch lines of 28 Oct 2026 about the Quick Fix Perk (damage not interrupting its regeneration, a new hitmarker icon) are listed under Multiplayer only [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
