Survivalist is the staying-alive tree of the three Trait trees: its six Major Traits speed up revives, stretch consumables, drop smoke when you go down, repair your first Wound and make Last Stand safer, ending with [[numb-the-pain]] or [[die-hard]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]]. Every pick costs one Trait Point and nine picks fill the tree ([[trait-points]]).

## How is the tree laid out?
Nine rows read from the bottom, one pick per row: row 1 is a random Minor bought by your first point in the tree; rows 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8 offer three hidden Minor Traits; rows 3, 6 and 9 offer two fixed Major Traits [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Picks are permanent until the Operator Prestiges ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Which Survivalist Traits are best?
It depends on whether you play alone. Solo builds take [[consumable-duration]], [[free-revive]] and [[die-hard]], because revive Traits need someone else to revive; squad builds take [[faster-revive]], [[impact-smokes]] and [[numb-the-pain]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]] [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. Among the Minors, COD Central rates Plate Organization, Objective Oriented and Expedited Extraction as must-takes [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]].

## What has changed?
Impact Smokes Pro did not trigger when a player was downed until a fix on 20 Oct 2026, and the descriptions of Numb the Pain and Numb the Pain Pro were corrected on 23 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
