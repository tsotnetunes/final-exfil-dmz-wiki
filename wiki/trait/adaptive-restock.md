Adaptive Restock is a Major Trait in the [[warfighter-tree]]: AI enemies drop one additional item [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. The official text does not say what the extra item can be, for example ammo, plates, consumables or equipment.

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 9, the last row of Warfighter, opposite [[possum]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. You can hold only one of the two on an Operator until it Prestiges ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do you unlock it?
It costs the ninth Trait Point spent in Warfighter, so you fill the whole tree: one pick per row, one point per pick [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. With 18 points in all, a full Warfighter tree leaves nine for one other tree ([[trait-points]]).

## What does Adaptive Restock Pro add?
The extra item that AI drop can be of a higher rarity [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. See [[pro-traits]].

## Is it worth taking?
Few guides take it, because Possum sits on the same row and is the most recommended Trait in DMZ [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. Adaptive Restock suits players who clear a lot of AI for supplies. Other Traits that add AI drops exist as Minors, such as Hostile Ammo and Combat Scavenger ([[warfighter-tree]]), and the Scout Major [[hostile-looter]] adds printer ingredients to AI drops; whether these combine with Adaptive Restock is not confirmed.
