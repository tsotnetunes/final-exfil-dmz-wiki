Field Crafting is a Major Trait in the [[scout-tree]] that lets you craft items in the middle of a deployment from Common 3D Printer resources [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-destructoid-trait-effects-2026-10-20]]. Without it, crafting happens at the FOB's [[3d-printer]], which turns the four Resources (Synthetics, Electronics, Chemicals, Metals) and Ingredients into gear [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Which recipes can be made in the field, and from which menu, is not published yet.

## Where is it in the tree?
Row 6 of Scout, paired with [[uav-upgrade]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]].

## How do you unlock it?
Six Trait Points in Scout, one per pick up to row 6, including a row 3 looting Major [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-traits-2026-10-21]]. It stays until a Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. How points are earned: [[trait-points]].

## What does Field Crafting Pro add?
The same crafting for half the resources [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-best-traits-2026-10-23]]. See [[pro-traits]].

## Is it worth taking?
Destructoid likes being able to craft during a live run, and COD Central's solo build takes it [[src-destructoid-best-operator-traits-2026-10-20]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. Dexerto and Playhub take UAV Upgrade on this row instead [[src-dexerto-best-operator-traits-2026-10-31]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]]. Minor Traits that raise how many resources you carry or gain, such as Pack Rat, Resource Hoarder and Resource Pockets (each +20%), suit a crafting build ([[scout-tree]]).
