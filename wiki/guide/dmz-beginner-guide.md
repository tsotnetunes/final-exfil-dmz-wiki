DMZ rewards patience: take objectives, loot what you can carry, and leave alive. This guide walks you from your first visit to the FOB to a steady routine. Words you do not know are on [[dmz-terms]].

## 1. Start at the FOB
The FOB is your home between deployments. From the start you have the Operators, DMZ Orders, Stash and Loadout, Firing Range, Survival Kits, Bounty Leaderboard, DMZ Progression, Events and Deploy stations; the 3D Printer opens at DMZ level 1 by the official launch table (the 9 Oct guide said level 2), the Vendor at 5 and the Gunsmith at 10 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[when-do-fob-stations-unlock]].

## 2. Follow the first DMZ Orders
The first Orders page is a tutorial: equip a weapon, a backpack, an armour vest and a Medkit from the Stash, knock down targets at the Firing Range, then deploy into a Dynamic Op (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. Orders pay resources, loot and cash, and they also carry the story [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; each chapter ends with a Story Mission [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. The game also has an onboarding mission, Breaking New Ground [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## 3. Pick your Operator and loadout
You have two Active Duty Operators to start, three with the Vault Edition [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. At Ready Up you can take the Free Loadout, which you keep only if you extract, or build your own; the game suggests a plate carrier, a backpack, a weapon and a Medkit [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Leave Squad Fill on for two teammates or switch it off to go solo [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## 4. Choose how to deploy
Pick a Story Mission, a Dynamic Operation or Free Roam [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]. Going in on foot is free; paid infils cost 5,000 to 100,000 DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A Dynamic Op is a good first run: each completed Op pays XP and cash and sends a Supply Drop [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]], and since 30 Oct 2026 it pays 10,000 to 20,000 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## 5. Play the match
- Read the Tac-Map: objectives, points of interest, threat areas, caches and radiation, on a grid of letters (west to east) and numbers (north to south) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- Watch the Threat Level under the compass: noise and violence raise it, stealth and distance calm it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Low and Medium areas are enough to level and earn at first (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]].
- Keep the [[stealth-meter]] white; it turns yellow and orange as AI notice you [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].
- Do not kill every AI in sight: when the Hostiles Kill Count fills, a Hunt Squad comes for you [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- Open containers; looting raises your XP and your rating [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
- Grab Weapon Manuals and Recipes: each pays 5,000 at exfil and unlocks something [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Put keys and valuables in Secure Slots; they survive a failed exfil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## 6. Get out
Go to an exfil site with green smoke, call the Twin Heli and hold the spot until it lands [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Leave before the radiation storm closes in; the Time Remaining timer shows when it gets worse [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. See [[how-to-exfil-in-dmz]].

## 7. If you die
You lose what you carried outside Secure Slots and your Operator goes MIA, but you keep the XP [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Pay DMZ Cash to recover the Operator with their Traits, or recruit another [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[what-happens-when-you-die-in-dmz]].

## 8. Spend at the FOB
The After Action Report banks your cash, rates the run from F to S++ and applies XP to your DMZ level and your Operator's Dog Tag [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Spend Trait Points with care, because picks are permanent [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Keep valuables that upgrades ask for, such as Infantry Invasion Plans for Bank Upgrade III (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-money-2026-10-26]].
