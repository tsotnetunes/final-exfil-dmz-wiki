## What does it need on PC?
TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot are required, only Intel or AMD processors with AVX are supported, and every spec tier needs 115 GB of storage on an SSD; ray tracing is supported in DMZ [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]]. Minimum: Ryzen 5 1400 or Core i5-6600, 8 GB RAM and a 3 GB card such as a GTX 1060; recommended (90 fps in Multiplayer on High): Ryzen 5 3600 or Core i7-8700K, 16 GB RAM and an 8 GB card such as an RTX 3060 Ti [[src-activision-pc-specs-2026-09-29]].
