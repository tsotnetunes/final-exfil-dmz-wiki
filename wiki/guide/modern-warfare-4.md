Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is a first-person shooter featuring a single-player campaign, multiplayer modes, and an extraction-based DMZ mode. It launched worldwide on October 23, 2026.

## Release and Platforms

* Confirmed launch date: Modern Warfare 4 launched worldwide on October 23, 2026, across platforms, and is confirmed to be live.
* Campaign Early Access: Began on October 16, 2026, for players who pre-ordered. It launched on Steam that same day. Players reported the unlock time was 10am PT / 1pm ET / 6pm UK.
* Pre-installation: Pre-installation officially began on Wednesday, October 14, 2026, at 9 PM PT. A player report on October 13 previously suggested pre-loading would begin earlier that day at 9am PT.
* Custom installation: During the setup process, players can choose which content to download, including whether to install Warzone alongside Modern Warfare 4.
* Nintendo Switch 2: Pre-orders were confirmed available on Nintendo Switch 2, and players report the game is available on the platform.
* Store rankings: Players reported the Vault Edition reached the #2 pre-ordered game on the PS5 store behind GTA VI, and the game reached #1 on Steam ahead of Campaign Early Access.
* Updates: The game received an official minor update to correct issues in the credits, as well as an official minor PC update addressing stability and crash fixes. A confirmed attestation issue identified AMD TPM software versions in the 3.x.0.x range as the most common cause.

## Editions and Promotions

* Vault Edition: Includes a third Active Duty Operator slot, a Vault Edition Deployment Kit, and one-time access to Signature Weapon Collection Blueprints.
* Promotional tie-ins: The track "C.R.E.A.M." was officially reloaded for the game. Players reported an advertisement aired on the Joe Rogan Experience podcast, and Discord Nitro subscribers reportedly receive an exclusive weapon blueprint.
* Twitch Badge: A Global Twitch Badge is available for subscribers of creators streaming the game between October 23 and November 13, 2026.

## Campaign

* Structure: Players report the campaign consists of 16 missions, takes roughly 6 hours to complete, and includes an open-world mission as well as a mini DMZ-style mission.
* Story and tone: Players describe the campaign as intense, brutal, and among the darkest Call of Duty entries tonally.
