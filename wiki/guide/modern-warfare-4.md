Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is a first-person shooter featuring a single-player campaign, multiplayer modes, and an extraction-based DMZ mode. The game scheduled its full launch for October 23, with campaign early access opening October 16 and DMZ early access rolling out ahead of the main release.

## Launch and Access Schedule

* Pre-orders grant access to both Campaign Early Access and DMZ Early Access.
* Pre-installation began on Wednesday, October 14 at 9 PM PT. Players reported an alternate pre-load timing of October 14 at 9 AM PT.
* During setup, players can select which content to download, including whether to install Warzone alongside the base game.
* Campaign Early Access officially launched on October 16, including a release on Steam. Players reported the access time as 10 AM PT (1 PM ET / 6 PM UK).
* An update for DMZ Early Access was set for all platforms on October 19 at 9 AM PT. Players reported DMZ Early Access going live on October 20 at 10 AM PT.
* A season is currently active, with the fight confirmed to continue into Season 1. Players report Season 1 launches in November with Warzone integration.
* The game is available for pre-order on the Nintendo Switch 2.

## Campaign

* The campaign officially features a reloaded version of the song "C.R.E.A.M."
* A minor update was released to fix issues in the campaign ending credits.
* Players report the campaign contains 16 missions, takes roughly 6 hours to finish, and concludes the Modern Warfare story arc.
* Players describe the campaign tone as brutal, intense, and among the darkest in the franchise, rating it 10/10.
* Player reports note an open-world mission, a mission featuring Makarov, a fist fight between Price and Makarov, and a mini DMZ-style mission.
* Players noted that the ending credits pay respects to Vince Zampella.

## Multiplayer and DMZ

* The DMZ mode features an active countdown, and its early access phase is active.
