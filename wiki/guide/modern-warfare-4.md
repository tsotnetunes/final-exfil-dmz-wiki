Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is an entry in the Call of Duty series. Pre-ordering the game grants early access to both its Campaign and DMZ modes, and setup options allow players to choose whether to install Warzone alongside the game.

## Release and Early Access Schedule

* Modern Warfare 4 officially launches on October 23.
* Pre-orders are available on Nintendo Switch 2.
* Pre-installation began on Wednesday, October 14 at 9 PM PT. Players reported a pre-load time of October 14 at 9 AM PT earlier that day on October 13.
* Campaign Early Access opened on October 16, launching on Steam. Players reported the access time as 10:00 AM PT (1:00 PM ET / 6:00 PM UK).
* An update for DMZ Early Access arrived across all platforms on October 19 at 9:00 AM PT, with the DMZ mode entering early access. Players reported that early access went live on October 20 at 10:00 AM PT.
* Season 1 continues the fight. Players report that Season 1 arrives in November with Warzone integration, after which Tactical Sprint will be active by default in Warzone.
* A season is confirmed active.

## Editions and Pre-Order Bonuses

* Modern Warfare 4 Vault Edition includes a Vault Edition Deployment Kit, a third Active Duty Operator slot, and one-time access to Signature Weapon Collection Blueprints.
* Players reported that Vault Edition reached the #2 pre-ordered game spot on the PlayStation 5 store behind GTA VI, and that the game ranked as the #1 pre-ordered title on Steam leading up to Campaign Early Access.

## Campaign

* The campaign concluded the modern warfare narrative, according to player reports.
* Players report the campaign spans 16 missions, takes roughly 6 hours to finish, and features an open-world mission, a mini DMZ mission, and a mission involving Makarov that culminates in a fist fight between Price and Makarov.
* Tone and reception were described by players as brutal, intense, among the darkest in the franchise, and rated 10/10.
* Players reported that the credits pay respects to Vince Zampella.
