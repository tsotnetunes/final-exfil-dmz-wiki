Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is a first-person shooter game featuring a campaign, multiplayer, and DMZ mode.

## Release and Pre-Order Information

* Modern Warfare 4 officially launches on October 23, 2026.
* Pre-ordering provides access to Campaign Early Access and DMZ Early Access.
* Pre-installation began on Wednesday, October 14 at 9 PM PT. Players reported a pre-loading start date of October 14 at 9am PT on October 13, superseded later that day by the confirmed 9 PM PT schedule.
* Players can choose whether to install Warzone alongside Modern Warfare 4 during the setup process.
* The game is available for pre-order on Nintendo Switch 2.
* Players reported that Modern Warfare 4 was the #1 pre-ordered game on Steam ahead of Campaign Early Access, and the Vault Edition was the #2 pre-ordered game on the PlayStation 5 store behind GTA VI.
* Modern Warfare 4 had an advertisement on the Joe Rogan Experience podcast, according to players.

## Vault Edition

* Includes a Vault Edition Deployment Kit.
* Includes a third Active Duty Operator slot.
* Includes one-time access to Signature Weapon Collection Blueprints.

## Campaign

* Campaign Early Access officially began on October 16, 2026, launching on Steam and other platforms. Players reported an exact start time of 10am PT (1pm ET / 6pm UK).
* The song "C.R.E.A.M." was reloaded for the game.
* A minor update was released to correct issues in the credits.
* Players reported that the campaign includes 16 missions, takes about 6 hours to complete, and concludes the Modern Warfare story.
* Players reported that the campaign is brutal and intense, ranks as one of the darkest Call of Duty games in tone, features a 10/10 rating, includes an open-world mission, features a Makarov mission, includes a fist fight between Price and Makrov, and pays respects to Vince Zampella in the ending credits.
* Players reported that the Call of Duty app peaked at 108,500 concurrent players on Steam during the campaign early access release.

## DMZ Mode

* The DMZ mode features an active countdown and an early access period.
* An update for DMZ Early Access was set for all platforms on October 19 at 9 AM PT.
