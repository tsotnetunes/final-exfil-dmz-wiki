Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is an entry in the Call of Duty franchise scheduled to launch on October 23, 2026. A season is currently active, and the ongoing storyline continues into Season 1, which integrates with Warzone and adds default Tactical Sprint in November.

## Release and Access Dates
* Pre-installation officially began on Wednesday, October 14 at 9 PM PT, with setup giving players the option to install Warzone alongside the game. Players also reported a pre-load time of October 14 at 9am PT.
* Campaign Early Access officially opened on October 16, following countdown milestones confirming 5 days remaining on October 11, 4 days on October 12, and 3 days on October 13. Players reported the exact start time as 10am PT (1pm ET / 6pm UK).
* An update for DMZ Early Access is scheduled across all platforms on Monday, October 19 at 9 AM PT. On October 18, players reported DMZ Early Access starting in two days.
* The game is available for pre-order on Nintendo Switch 2. Players also reported it as the #1 pre-ordered title on Steam ahead of Campaign Early Access and the #2 pre-ordered title on the PS5 store behind GTA VI.

## Editions and Features
* The Vault Edition includes a Vault Edition Deployment Kit, a third Active Duty Operator slot, and one-time access to Signature Weapon Collection Blueprints.
