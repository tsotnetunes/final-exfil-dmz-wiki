Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is a first-person shooter featuring a story Campaign, Multiplayer, and an extraction-focused DMZ mode. Following pre-installation starting October 14, 2026, Campaign Early Access launched on October 16, and the game officially released worldwide on October 23, 2026.

## Launch and Availability

* Modern Warfare 4 officially launched worldwide on October 23, 2026. Pre-installation opened on Wednesday, October 14 at 9 PM PT, with players given the option during setup to choose whether to install Warzone alongside the game.
* Campaign Early Access became available on October 16, 2026, and was included alongside DMZ Early Access for players who pre-ordered.
* DMZ Early Access received a platform update on October 19 at 9 AM PT and entered early access testing around October 20.
* The game is available on PC, PlayStation, and Nintendo Switch 2 (confirmed available for pre-order and brought to the console).
