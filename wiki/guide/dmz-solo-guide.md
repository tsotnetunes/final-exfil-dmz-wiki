You can play all of DMZ alone; Infinity Ward says a solo player can do all of the content [[src-destructoid-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]]. The skill is choosing your fights and leaving with what you have. The short answer to "can I?" is on [[can-you-play-dmz-solo]].

## Queue as a solo
Switch Squad Fill off at Ready Up, with 1 on keyboard or the left trigger on controller [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]]. Matchmaking tries to put solos with solos, but up to 60 players share a match and nothing is guaranteed; there is no solo-only playlist (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [clm_cb80a507198b].

## Stay unseen
The stealth meter goes from white to yellow to orange as AI notice you, and the developers built it with solos in mind [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Noise raises an area's Threat Level and stealth calms it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Avoid the Critical and Extreme areas early, such as The Grid, the Chang-san bunker and The Citadel [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]], and do not kill every AI: a full Hostiles Kill Count sends a Hunt Squad [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Plan for going down
The tourniquet gets you up from Last Stand without help, then works again only once you are back at full health [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Carry a Self-Revive kit in its loadout slot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Possum still revives you once a match, but since 28 Oct 2026 not while your killers are nearby or before four minutes have passed [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. More: [[can-you-revive-yourself-in-dmz]].

## Earn without big fights
Dynamic Ops pay 10,000 to 20,000 since 30 Oct 2026 and go straight to your Bank, so you keep the cash even if you die later [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]]. Side Ops pay 2,000 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Story Missions pay 25,000, and only your squad has your mission active [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]].

## Talk, or slip away
Use proximity chat or the Ping Wheel to ask for a truce or to join a squad; an assimilated squad is capped at four, and betrayal is allowed [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Operators with four or more player kills are Wanted, and you get an alert when one comes close [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Exfil carefully
Watch the exfil from cover before calling it, because a flare or helicopter draws other squads (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-solo-2026-10-26]]. Solo, the countdown once aboard is about 5 seconds (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Leave before the radiation closes in [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Can you take on bosses alone?
Lieutenants have escorts, so pick your moment [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Players report killing the Colossus solo with two Shock Sticks and seven or eight Thermite charges (reported) [clm_718706a12b71] [clm_d12acdac5305]; Drill Charges do less damage to it since 25 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
