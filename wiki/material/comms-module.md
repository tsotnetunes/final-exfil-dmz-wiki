The Comms Module is an Epic 3D Printer ingredient that spawns by chance, not in fixed spots [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]]. It matters early because two FOB upgrades available from the start, Uncommon Ingredient Printing and Stash Weapon Upgrade I, need five each [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## Can you print Comms Modules?

Only after Epic Ingredient Printing: 100 Electronics and 3 CPUs each [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. Uncommon Ingredient Printing cannot make the five it costs, so your first ones must be looted [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]]. See [[cpu]] and [[3d-printer]].

## Where do you find Comms Modules?

- Computers on the top floors of the buildings east of Deadtown, a good early spot [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]]; one list names Deadtown's hillside buildings [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. See [[deadtown]].
- Computers and server hardware at [[cheonwang-peak-array]] and in the Nenshin Tech Complex in [[the-grid]], a high-threat area [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- The Chemist's cache at the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).

It is valued at 1,500 DMZ Cash [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
