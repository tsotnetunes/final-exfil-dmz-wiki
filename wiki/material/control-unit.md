The Control Unit is an Uncommon 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]], one of the publisher's example ingredients [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Electronic gear, drones and most Fire Support use it.

## Where do you find Control Units?

- Desktop computers and tech bags [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]].
- Server blade containers on the Data Farm's second floor at [[cheonwang-peak-array]], which can also hold CPUs or a Printed Circuit Board [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] (reported).
- The Chemist's cache at the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- DMZ level 38 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- Print them after Uncommon Ingredient Printing: 20 Electronics and 20 Metals each [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]].

Databases disagree on their value: 400 DMZ Cash [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] or 2,000 [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
