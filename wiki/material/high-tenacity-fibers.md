High-Tenacity Fibers are a Rare 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] that no recipe can print [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]]. You need ten to build Uncommon Ingredient Printing, so they gate the printing of every Uncommon ingredient [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]].

## Where do you find High-Tenacity Fibers?

- Mostly at [[chang-san-air-base]] (armory and barracks) and on the roof of La Grande Vie at [[songdo-wharf]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-patchcrazy-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-28]].
- Guides disagree on how they lie: as loose coils of rope at military sites rather than in containers [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported), or in weapon lockers, including the one on that rooftop and those in small military camps marked with tent icons [[src-patchcrazy-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-28]] (reported). Check both.
- A blue duffel bag in a barber shop in Hajin City at F8, south of Wolves Stadium, held some on one run [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- DMZ level 53 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]], and a later page of [[dmz-orders]] (page 22) pays two [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]].
