Rigid Synthetics are an Uncommon 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] and one of the most used: almost every backpack above Small, the Tempered Armor Vest, gas masks, parachutes and three early FOB upgrades need them [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]]. The Medium Backpack, a starter recipe, already asks for five [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Where do you find Rigid Synthetics?

- In homes, not in shops or banks: residential buildings and civilian duffel bags [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].
- In red tool boxes; one on the outside stair landing of DBN Headquarters held two [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported). The Train also gave some [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- DMZ level 6 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- Print them after Uncommon Ingredient Printing: 20 Synthetics and 20 Metals each [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]].

They sell for only 200 DMZ Cash [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]], so keep them.
