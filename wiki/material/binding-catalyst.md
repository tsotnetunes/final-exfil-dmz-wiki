Binding Catalyst is a Rare 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]], one of the publisher's example ingredients; the starter Medkit recipe needs one [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. No printer recipe makes it, because there is no Rare Ingredient Printing tier [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]].

## Where do you find Binding Catalyst?

- Medical bags at [[mirae-general-hospital]] and in the blue and white medical tents around it; not every container has one [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] (reported).
- DMZ level 49 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- Ingredients in general show up mostly in certain kinds of building; the printer's Ingredient Locations button points you to them [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]].

Databases disagree on its value, 1,200 DMZ Cash [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] or 500 [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]], but you need a lot of it.
