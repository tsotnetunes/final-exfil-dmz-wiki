Reactive Metals are an Uncommon 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] that goes into most armor vests, the Armor Box and two FOB upgrades [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [clm_cde331b22aa0]. Like every ingredient, they take inventory space and count only once you exfil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Where do you find Reactive Metals?

- In industrial areas and toolboxes [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- DMZ level 11 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- From the [[3d-printer]] once you build Uncommon Ingredient Printing: 20 Chemicals and 20 Metals each [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]].

Pin them at the printer and they are marked while you loot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. They sell for 200 DMZ Cash [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]], so keep them.
