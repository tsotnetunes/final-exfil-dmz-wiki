Detonators are an Uncommon 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] and one of the publisher's example ingredients [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. They go into explosive Fire Support, a few field upgrades and Smelling Salts.

## Where do you find Detonators?

- Military and industrial areas [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] (reported).
- The Train gave some on one writer's runs [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- DMZ level 29 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- Print them after Uncommon Ingredient Printing: 20 Electronics and 20 Chemicals each [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. One database lists 20 Electronics and 20 Metals instead [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]; check the card in game.

Values disagree: 200 DMZ Cash in two lists [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]], 900 in a third [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]].
