The [[3d-printer]] uses two kinds of material: four Resources that looted junk breaks down into, and Ingredients, named parts you extract whole [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. There are 26 Ingredients in four rarities [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. Some FOB upgrades also take valuables. Materials also level up the FOB [clm_763090fe4512].

## What are Resources?

Synthetics, Electronics, Chemicals and Metals. Looted items are broken down into them automatically; they take no Stash slots and every backpack has a separate pouch for them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Residential districts are rich in them [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]], and the Train is reportedly a strong source [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]]. Like Ingredients, they count only once you exfil [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]] [[src-gfinity-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-22]]. The Scout Trait Resource Pockets adds 20% to what you scrap [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]], and Field Crafting lets you craft from Common resources in the field [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

How many Resources you can hold is capped: the Scout Traits Resource Hoarder and Pack Rat each raise the cap by 20%, and Resource Management sends part of any overflow to your lowest Resource [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-traits-2026-10-31]]. The Scavenger backpacks carry extra resources [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported). The size of the cap is not confirmed yet.

## How do you print Ingredients?

Uncommon Ingredient Printing (50,000 DMZ Cash) opens the six Uncommon ones, each from 20 of two Resources; Epic Ingredient Printing (100,000) opens the eight Epic ones, each from 100 of a Resource and 3 Rare ingredients. There is no Rare tier [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]].

## Where should you farm?

- Hospital run: Binding Catalyst, Biomaterials and Stabilized Reagents all point to [[mirae-general-hospital]] and medical tents [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] (reported).
- Server run: CPUs, Comms Modules, Control Units and PCBs sit in [[cheonwang-peak-array]], the Nenshin Tech Complex and the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]].
- Fabric run: both Nylons come from the Citadel, Deadtown's underground and the Mujin Nightclub [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]].
- Commanders for the Legendary parts [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-bosses-2026-10-23]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] ([[commander-threats]]).

Pin what you need at the printer and it is marked while you loot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; the Ingredient Locations button names the areas [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]. Recipes: [[recipes]].
