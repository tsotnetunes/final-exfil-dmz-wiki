Biomaterials are an Uncommon 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] named by the publisher as an example ingredient; the starter Medkit recipe needs five [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. They take inventory space and count once you exfil [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]].

## Where do you find Biomaterials?

- Hospitals and chemical lockers [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] (reported); see [[mirae-general-hospital]].
- A fire station first aid kit and the Chang-san Air Base barracks gave some on one writer's runs [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- DMZ level 39 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- Print them after Uncommon Ingredient Printing: 20 Synthetics and 20 Chemicals each [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]].

Databases disagree on their value: 800 DMZ Cash [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] or 200 [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]; check the sell screen at the FOB.
