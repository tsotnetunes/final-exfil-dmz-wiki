Turned Components are an Epic 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. Players count them, with Stock Rods, among the key parts for FOB upgrades and top-tier crafts [clm_0217c096a2ca], and the late Stash upgrades bear that out.

## How do you get Turned Components?

- **Print them** after Epic Ingredient Printing: 100 Metals and 3 [[stock-rod]] each [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]].
- **Loot them**: players report finding three or more in a deployment without needing a key [clm_6c7b8163cfc9]. One list places them at the rice mill at Imjin Farmland, the construction zone in Hajin City and Namjjog Station [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]; another database gives those same spots for Stock Rods [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. Either way, those are the places to search. See [[imjin-farmland]].

One database values them at 1,500 DMZ Cash [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported).
