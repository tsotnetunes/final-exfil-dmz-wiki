The CPU is a Rare 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]]. ## Where do you find CPUs?

- Server blade containers on the second floor of the Data Farm at [[cheonwang-peak-array]]; one blade can hold two CPUs, a Control Unit or a Printed Circuit Board [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] (reported).
- Server rooms in the Nenshin Tech Complex in [[the-grid]] (stronger enemies), electronic containers in Deadtown's hillside buildings, and the Coin Mining Den key room in NuriGO West [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] (reported). The same server areas hold Comms Modules [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]].
- An IT bag in a Cheongun Village office, west of the D50 Radar, gave one [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- DMZ level 44 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].

Its value is given as 500 DMZ Cash [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] or 5,000 [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]; check in game.
