The Prototype RF Module is a Legendary 3D Printer ingredient that drops from the Deathstalker, the helicopter-sized drone Commander [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-bosses-2026-10-23]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]. No printer recipe makes it [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## How do you get one?

Bring the Deathstalker down and loot the wreck: it breaks into nose, body and tail sections, each its own loot container, so search all three [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [clm_d162a2f6766a]. Players report that two RPG rockets or four well-placed Astaroth .57 shots bring it down [clm_2709a2129c64] [clm_855a0d332b7b], and that finishing the FOB upgrades takes several kills [clm_33a4b2ec6e55]. It flies over the whole map, most often over Hajin City and the east [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-bosses-2026-10-23]]. Commanders resist many ordinary weapons [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]], and killing one and extracting pays valuable ingredients [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; one database says the Deathstalker also drops [[experimental-compounds]] [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]] (reported). See [[commander-threats]].
