Nylon Laminate is an Epic 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]] used only in big backpacks [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]]. It may not appear even after you search every known spot several times [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]].

## How do you get Nylon Laminate?

- **Print it** once Epic Ingredient Printing is built (100,000 DMZ Cash, 10 Binding Catalyst, 10 Printed Circuit Boards, 5 Military Composites): each costs 100 Synthetics and 3 [[nylon-cloth]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. After that you can print as many as your materials allow [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]].
- **Loot it** in the wings of the Citadel's main building at the 14th Political Prison, the underground holding area in Deadtown and the Mujin Nightclub of the Heavenly Luck Casino [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. See [[prison-complex]], [[deadtown]] and [[casino]].

The same spots hold Nylon Cloth, so one run can serve both [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. One database sums up its spots as military holding sites and upscale civilian places [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported).
