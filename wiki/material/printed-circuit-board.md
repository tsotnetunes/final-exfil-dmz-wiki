The Printed Circuit Board (PCB) is a Rare 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]]. No printer recipe makes it [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]], and the Epic Ingredient Printing upgrade alone asks for ten [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]].

## Where do you find Printed Circuit Boards?

- The [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] and electrical substations, where the printer's ingredient hint points [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]]. The reactor is irradiated, so bring a gas mask [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[gas-mask-recipe]]).
- Loose in the Chang-san Air Base bunker, and in server blades at [[cheonwang-peak-array]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] (reported).
- DMZ level 56 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].

Amounts from [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] and [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. Power Regulators in turn feed the Gunsmith upgrade, the Revive Pistol, the Railgun Sentry and Elite NVGs [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]], so PCBs sit under a lot of late crafting ([[materials]]).

Dog Tag Case Upgrade III comes from one database, which also values a PCB at 500 DMZ Cash [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported). Epic Ingredient Printing is what lets you print Power Regulators, at 100 Electronics and 3 PCBs each [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]], and Vendor Item Upgrade I adds more Vendor offers [[src-gamerfuzion-3d-printer-upgrades-2026-10-23]] (reported).
