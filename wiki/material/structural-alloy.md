## Where do you find Structural Alloy?

- Mostly the hangar and repair bay at Chang-san Air Base, and Bubun Yard [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported). Another list gives the same spots for Alloyed Plating, which is made from Structural Alloy [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. See [[chang-san-air-base]].
- The underground bunker of the Operation Iron Citadel Story Mission: armory cabinets, weapon cases, crates and side rooms [[src-patchcrazy-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-28]] (reported). See [[story-missions]].
- DMZ level 43 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]], and a page of [[dmz-orders]] (page 14) pays two [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]].

One database values it at 5,000 DMZ Cash [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported). The Avionics Order asks you to extract one before printing a UAV [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]].
