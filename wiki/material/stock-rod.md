The Stock Rod is a Rare 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] that no recipe can print [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. Players count it, with Turned Components, among the key parts for FOB upgrades and top-tier crafts [clm_0217c096a2ca]; Stash Weapon Upgrade I alone takes ten [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]].

## Where do you find Stock Rods?

- The rice mill at Imjin Farmland, the construction zone in Hajin City and Namjjog Station [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported). Another list gives the same spots for Turned Components, which are made from Stock Rods [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. See [[imjin-farmland]].
- Players report finding eight or more in a single deployment without needing a key [clm_6fc0235c2541].
- DMZ level 51 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]], and a page of [[dmz-orders]] (page 14) pays two with two Structural Alloy [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]].

It sells for 500 DMZ Cash [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. Pin it at the [[3d-printer]] to have it marked while you loot.
