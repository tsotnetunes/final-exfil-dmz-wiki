Electronic Sensors are an Uncommon 3D Printer ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] that you need early: Stash Item Upgrade I, the usual first FOB upgrade, takes ten [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]].

## Where do you find Electronic Sensors?

- Tech bags in commercial buildings [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-patchcrazy-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-28]].
- A grey IT bag on an office desk in the building just west of the D50 Radar in [[cheongun-village]] (F4); clear the guards first. The same bag also gave a CPU [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- Police stations, fire stations, downtown offices, and the office floors of the Tech Tower in Operation Critical Extraction [[src-patchcrazy-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-28]] (reported).
- Print them after Uncommon Ingredient Printing: 20 Synthetics and 20 Electronics each [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]].

Their value is given as 200 DMZ Cash [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] or 600 [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. The level 33 reward is written as Electronics x2 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]; whether that means Electronic Sensors is not confirmed.
