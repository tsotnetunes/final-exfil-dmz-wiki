DMZ is the extraction mode of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4: you deploy into Hajin from your FOB, loot, take on missions, fight AI and rival players, and keep only what you carry out [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. It is live: digital pre-orders of the Standard Digital or Vault Edition got early access on Tuesday 20 Oct 2026, worldwide by 10 AM PT (1 PM ET, 17:00 UTC) [[src-iw-dmz-early-access-time-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and every owner has had it since the full launch on 23 Oct 2026, on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Switch 2 [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-features-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-2026-06-12]]. Season 1 starts at midnight ET on 6 Nov 2026 [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## How does it play?
Three ways to deploy: [[story-missions]], [[dynamic-operations]] and [[free-roam]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]. A match holds up to 60 players in squads of up to three (as relayed) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Exfil and you keep your loot; die and you lose what you carried outside Secure Slots, and your Operator goes MIA until you pay to recover them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Between deployments your Stash, FOB upgrades, Operators and their Traits, and your DMZ level from 1 to 70 all persist [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; see [[dmz-progression]].

## What is the story?
It is set after the Campaign: a power vacuum in Hajin and a new threat to unmask [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-features-2026-10-09]].

## Is it free, or part of Warzone?
Neither: DMZ comes with Modern Warfare 4, unlike the free 2022 beta [[src-windows-central-dmz-reveal-2026-06-06]]. See [[is-dmz-free]] and [[is-dmz-part-of-warzone]].

## What changed since the 2022 beta?
The developers name the FOB, the permanent Stash and DMZ's own progression as the biggest changes. They say DMZ has been years in the making, the early seasons are being tested, and they plan to support and evolve the mode, without sweeping commitments yet (Q&A of 9 Oct, via a video recap) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
