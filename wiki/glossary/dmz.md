DMZ is a game mode set in the location of Hajin. Modern Warfare 4 launches on October 23, and Early Access for DMZ begins on October 20.

## Overview and Gameplay Details

* DMZ is confirmed to be set in Hajin.
* DMZ Early Access begins on October 20, and MW4 officially launches on October 23.
* Players report that DMZ will be available on day 1 for Modern Warfare 4 owners.
* Players report that dying in DMZ no longer wipes your Operator following a DMZ Beta update.
* The mode currently features only PvPvE, according to player reports.
* Players report that DMZ lobbies are not solo-only, though matchmaking will attempt to place solo players against other solos, and duos against duos.
* Infinity Ward stated that "Single Squad Missions" were intended strictly for internal use rather than public release, as reported by players.
* Players report that DMZ has its own weapon damage tuning, which is close to Warzone.
* Weapon unlocks for the mode require acquiring the weapons directly within DMZ, according to player reports.

Digital pre-orders and pre-purchases (Standard Digital or Vault Edition) get early access to all of DMZ from Tuesday 20 Oct 2026, four days after Campaign early access (16 Oct); the full game, DMZ included, launches on 23 Oct 2026 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-features-2026-10-09]], on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Switch 2 [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-2026-06-12]]. Three ways to deploy: [[story-missions]], [[dynamic-operations]], [[free-roam]]. Its own level track: [[dmz-progression]].

**What changed since the 2022 beta.** The developers name the FOB, the permanent stash and DMZ's own progression as the biggest changes. On the future they say DMZ has been years in the making, the early seasons are being tested, and they plan to support and evolve the mode, without sweeping commitments yet (Q&A of 9 Oct, relayed by [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]).
