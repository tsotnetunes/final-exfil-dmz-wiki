DMZ is an extraction game mode set in Hajin, released in Early Access on October 20, 2026, ahead of the full Modern Warfare 4 launch on October 23, 2026. Access to the early period required a digital pre-order, with preloads made available beforehand.

## Confirmed Details

* Early Access launched globally on October 20 at 10 AM PT, with deployments taking place in Hajin.
* Modern Warfare 4 officially launches on October 23.
* PC requirements for the Early Access launch include TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot enabled.
* Performance Mode on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S requires Variable Refresh Rate (VRR).
* The mode includes a progression system offering 70 levels of rewards.
* The three DMZ Mastery Camos are Chiral, Ripple Tide, and Helio. On October 10, 2026, players reported these three camos, and the list was confirmed on October 19, 2026.
* Players who took part in the MWII DMZ Beta receive the Beta On Record emblem and Beta Squad calling card.

## Player Reports and Gameplay Features

* Players describe DMZ as a looter extraction shooter centered on PvPvE encounters.
* Lobbies are reported not to be solo-only. When queuing, solo players are matched against other solos where possible, with the same rule applied to duos.
* Squad formation is supported, and proximity chat is reportedly available for talking to other squads.
* Players note that DMZ has its own weapon damage tuning, which is close to Warzone.
* The mode is reported to feature an unskippable opening mission, dynamic weather, and smooth movement mechanics tailored specifically to the mode.
* Players report that DMZ levels cannot be raised while playing multiplayer.
* Running at 120 FPS on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S is reported to require a display supporting both 120Hz and VRR.
* Modern Warfare 4 is reported to feature a DMZ mission or appearance within the campaign, and an official blog post was reported to contain campaign spoilers.
* Infinity Ward reportedly noted that "Single Squad Missions" were intended strictly for internal testing rather than public release.
* A separate PvE mode has been suggested by players, who propose it should have its own progression, stash, and operators if added.
* Season 1 of Modern Warfare 4 is reported to begin on November 6.
