DMZ is a game mode located in Hajin that is available in Early Access starting October 20, ahead of the Modern Warfare 4 launch on October 23. Playing the Early Access release requires a digital pre-order as well as TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot enabled on the system.

## Details

* DMZ Early Access begins on October 20.
* Modern Warfare 4 launches on October 23.
* The mode is set in Hajin.
* Accessing Early Access requires a digital pre-order.
* Early Access requires both TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot to be enabled.
* Players report that DMZ is listed alongside multiplayer and the campaign in the game menu.
* Players report that DMZ currently features only PvPvE.
* Players report that the mode allows players to form squads.
* Players report that DMZ lobbies are not solo-only, though solos queuing will attempt to match against other solo players, and duos will attempt to match against other duos.
* Players report that Infinity Ward stated Single Squad Missions were intended for internal use rather than public release.
* Players report that DMZ has its own weapon damage tuning close to Warzone.
* Players report that DMZ levels cannot be leveled up while playing multiplayer.
* Players report three Mastery Camos in the mode: Chiral, Ripple Tide, and Helio.
* Players report that Modern Warfare 4 contains a one-off DMZ mission within the campaign.
* Players report that a DMZ blog post included spoilers for the Modern Warfare 4 campaign.
* Players report that if a PvE mode were added, it should be a separate mode featuring its own progression, stash, and operators.

Digital pre-orders and pre-purchases (Standard Digital or Vault Edition) get early access to all of DMZ from Tuesday 20 Oct 2026, four days after Campaign early access (16 Oct); the full game, DMZ included, launches on 23 Oct 2026 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-features-2026-10-09]], on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Switch 2 [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-2026-06-12]]. Three ways to deploy: [[story-missions]], [[dynamic-operations]], [[free-roam]]. Its own level track: [[dmz-progression]].

**What changed since the 2022 beta.** The developers name the FOB, the permanent stash and DMZ's own progression as the biggest changes.
