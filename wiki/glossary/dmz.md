DMZ is a looter and extraction game mode featured in Modern Warfare 4, taking place in Hajin. Early access opened globally on October 20 at 10 AM PT ahead of the full Modern Warfare 4 release on October 23.

## Access and Requirements

* Early access required a digital pre-order, and preloads were made available prior to release.
* Launching DMZ Early Access requires TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot to be enabled.
* Modern Warfare 4 includes RICOCHET Anti-Cheat protections targeting Direct Memory Access (DMA) cheats.
* Performance Mode on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S requires Variable Refresh Rate (VRR). Players report that running at 120 FPS also requires a 120Hz display with VRR support.

## Mode Features and Progression

* The mode features a standalone progression system with 70 levels of rewards. Players report that DMZ levels cannot be raised by playing standard multiplayer.
* DMZ features three Mastery Camos: Chiral, Ripple Tide, and Helio. Players report that extracting the same weapon manual three additional times per weapon unlocks a hidden camo.
* Players who took part in the MWII DMZ Beta receive the Beta Squad calling card and Beta On Record emblem.
* Infinity Ward confirmed an official promotional collaboration with Monster Gaming in DMZ.
* An animation was disabled across all platforms as a temporary fix for a PlayStation issue.

## Gameplay and Player Reports

* Players report that DMZ functions as a squad-based PvPvE extraction shooter with dynamic weather and smooth, dedicated movement mechanics.
* Damage tuning for weapons in DMZ is reported by players to be close to Warzone.
* Players report that lobbies are not solo-only, though matchmaking attempts to place solo players against solos and duos against duos.
* Infinity Ward stated that Single Squad Missions were designed solely for internal testing and were not intended for public deployment.
* Players report the inclusion of grindable DMZ Orders, usable gun drones, and a campaign mission that features DMZ elements.
* Players reported server crashes causing total loot loss shortly after early access began, as well as occasional errors occurring post-exfil.
* Reports noted increasing player microphone usage and escalating PvP conflicts around high-tier loot locations and extraction points over the first few days of play.
* Season 1 of Modern Warfare 4 is reported by players to start on November 6.
