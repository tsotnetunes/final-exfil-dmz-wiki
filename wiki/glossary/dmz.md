DMZ is a game mode located in Hajin. Early Access begins on October 20, making the mode available to play ahead of the Modern Warfare 4 launch on October 23.

## Mode Details

* DMZ is confirmed to be located in Hajin.
* Early Access begins on October 20, confirmed ahead of the Modern Warfare 4 launch on October 23.
* Players report that DMZ supports squad play and allows players to form squads.
* Players report that lobbies will not be solo-only.
* Players report that matchmaking tries to match solo players against other solos, with the same applied to duos.
* Players report that the mode currently features only PvPvE.
* Infinity Ward reportedly stated that Single Squad Missions were intended for internal use rather than public release.
* Players report that if a PvE mode is added, it should be a separate mode featuring its own progression, stash, and operators.
* Players report that DMZ uses its own weapon damage tuning close to Warzone.
* Players report that DMZ levels cannot be leveled up while playing multiplayer.
* Players report three Mastery Camos in the mode: Chiral, Ripple Tide, and Helio.
* A DMZ blog post reportedly contained spoilers for the Modern Warfare 4 campaign.

DMZ is the extraction mode of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4. Digital pre-orders and pre-purchases (Standard Digital or Vault Edition) get early access to all of DMZ from Tuesday 20 Oct 2026, four days after Campaign early access (16 Oct); the full game, DMZ included, launches on 23 Oct 2026 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-features-2026-10-09]], on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Switch 2 [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-2026-06-12]]. The story is set after the Campaign: a power vacuum in Hajin and a new threat to unmask [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-features-2026-10-09]]. Three ways to deploy: [[story-missions]], [[dynamic-operations]], [[free-roam]]. Its own level track: [[dmz-progression]].

**What changed since the 2022 beta.** The developers name the FOB, the permanent stash and DMZ's own progression as the biggest changes. On the future they say DMZ has been years in the making, the early seasons are being tested, and they plan to support and evolve the mode, without sweeping commitments yet (Q&A of 9 Oct, via a video recap [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]).
