DMZ is a game mode located in Hajin that launches early ahead of the Modern Warfare 4 release. It allows players to form squads and features dedicated systems separate from standard multiplayer.

## Release and Access

* Early Access for DMZ begins on October 20.
* Modern Warfare 4 officially launches on October 23, allowing DMZ to be played ahead of the full game launch.
* Players report that Early Access begins at 10am PT / 1pm ET / 6pm UK.
* DMZ Early Access requires a digital pre-order.
* The Early Access launch requires TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot to be enabled.
* Players report that Season 1 of Modern Warfare 4 begins on November 6.

## Gameplay and Matchmaking

* DMZ is confirmed to be located in Hajin.
* Players report that the mode currently features only PvPvE.
* Lobbies will not be solo-only, as reported by players.
* Players report that matchmaking will attempt to match solo players against other solos, and duos against other duos.
* DMZ allows players to form squads, according to player reports.
* Players report that weapon damage tuning in DMZ is distinct and close to Warzone.
* Player progression in DMZ levels cannot be leveled up while playing multiplayer, according to player reports.
* Players report that there are three Mastery Camos in DMZ: Chiral, Ripple Tide, and Helio.
* A PvE option, if added, should be a separate mode with its own progression, stash, and operators, according to player reports.
* A DMZ map is reported to be coming eventually.

## Additional Details

* DMZ is listed alongside multiplayer and the campaign in the game menu, according to player reports.
* Players report that MW4 features a one-off DMZ mission within the campaign.
* A blog post about DMZ reportedly contained spoilers for the Modern Warfare 4 campaign.
* Infinity Ward stated that "Single Squad Missions" were intended only for internal use and not for public release, according to player reports.
