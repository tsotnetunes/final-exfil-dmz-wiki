**Apex Attachment:** a part made for one weapon that changes how it plays (for example an akimbo pair, a full-auto conversion or a built-in launcher), fitted in its own slot so it uses none of the five normal attachment slots [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Before launch it was described as the sixth slot of the DMZ Gunsmith: up to five attachments plus one Apex per weapon [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

**How you get one.** It is the final unlock of a weapon's level track, and weapon levels are shared between Multiplayer and DMZ [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Launchers and the knife have none, so there were 28 at launch; Season 1 (from 6 Nov 2026) adds more, including two that were in the beta but not at launch [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]] [clm_53d761688e7f]. Prestiging a weapon no longer shows its Apex as locked again since a 23 Oct fix [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

**Cost in DMZ.** Guides disagree: one says the Apex is free once unlocked [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]], while a post-launch cart charged 52,000 DMZ Cash for one (reported) [[src-allthingshow-rezi-12-enforcer-build-2026-10-21]].
