The final exfil is the last helicopter out of a match: once the spreading radiation has swallowed every other exfil site, one site remains, marked in white [clm_c772bf67ab75] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]].

## When does the final exfil appear?

Each match starts with a radiation countdown of about 30 minutes [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]] [[src-gamerfuzion-orders-page-14-2026-10-30]]; the official HUD calls it Time Remaining and says the storm intensifies when it runs out [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. At zero the HUD switches to a Radiation Spreading timer, and the radiation needs just under 15 more minutes to cover the map [clm_a15e34798a66] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]]. One run read 8:55 on that second timer at the switch (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-orders-page-14-2026-10-30]]. Exfils close as the radiation reaches them, and the final one leaves at a fixed time [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]].

## Where is it?

It changes every match. The radiation starts from a circle marked at the start of the match and spreads outward to the map edge, so the far side from that circle hints where the last exfil will be (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. In one early access match the radiation spread from the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] [[src-allthingshow-hajin-gameplay-2026-10-21]]; in a launch-week match the final exfil sat just south of [[songdo-wharf]] [[src-gamerfuzion-orders-page-14-2026-10-30]]. A community map pins ten possible final exfil spots, most near the edges of the map: the northwest corner, the west edge and the east coast, plus one around Cheongun Village (reported) [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]].

## Does the Final Intel Trait help?

Yes. Final Intel, a Minor Trait that can appear in the Warfighter and Survivalist trees, shows the final exfil on the Tac-Map from the start of the match [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-traits-2026-10-23]]. Players go further and describe it as a free extra exfil you can use to call a helicopter at any point in the match (reported) [clm_f1f95e1b6f10] [clm_51f7e1c5f386]; the guides only say it marks the final exfil early, so whether it gives a ride out before the radiation is not settled ([[survivalist-tree]], [[warfighter-tree]]).

## What are the risks?

Every squad still alive heads for the same spot, and players describe the final exfil as a place where squads hunt each other [clm_233daf06dc99]. Arrive early, hold cover and wear a gas mask in case the radiation edge reaches you. Some DMZ Orders ask you to exfil after the radiation has started, and one, Astronaut, to leave on the white final exfil helicopter three times ([[dmz-orders]]) [[src-gamerfuzion-orders-page-14-2026-10-30]] [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]].
