To keep what you carry you must exfil: go to an exfil site marked by green smoke, call the helicopter, hold the spot until it lands and board [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Other ways out are a Fulton extraction, an exfil flare and, late in a match, the [[final-exfil]]. If your whole squad dies first, only Secure Slot items come home and the Operator goes MIA ([[secure-slots]], [[mia-system]]) [[src-allthingshow-exfil-guide-2026-10-21]].

## How does a helicopter exfil work?

1. Pick an open site on the [[tac-map]]. Sites change every match and come from a fixed pool; about three are open at a time (reported) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-allthingshow-hajin-map-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]]. A community map pins 36 exfil spots, 10 of them possible final exfils [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]].
2. At the smoke, use the prompt to call the helicopter. Nearby players and AI can hear the call [[src-gfinity-exfil-2026-10-20]].
3. Hold the site for about a minute while enemy reinforcements, sometimes a helicopter, push in [[src-allthingshow-exfil-guide-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]]. Players also report about 90 seconds from call to touchdown [clm_0654da754a95].
4. Board. A squad gets 30 to 45 seconds to climb in, cut to 5 seconds once everyone is aboard; a solo player gets 5 seconds [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]]. You can shoot from the ramp, and you can still be killed inside [[src-allthingshow-exfil-guide-2026-10-21]] [clm_cf56ba61e6f8].

A used site reopens after a cooldown (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]].

## What can go wrong?

Squads camp exfil sites and fight near them more than anywhere [clm_4821865b60e5] [clm_dea77ea2af4d] [clm_20b125dbeea0]. The radiation closes the sites in its path, nearest first ([[radiation-storm]]) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]]. Picking up a Lieutenant's Dog Tag Case shows you on every Operator's map, so leave soon after [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

Fixed bugs: on 24 Oct 2026 the exfil helicopter could vanish with players inside; on 29 Oct enemy helicopters could crash into it and kill the squad [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. A network error that hit some PC players extracting with a looted Dog Tag, a known issue on 24 Oct, was resolved for all PC players by 27 Oct [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [clm_6155f83bd127].

## Tips

Reload, plate up and ping a backup exfil before you call; cover every approach [[src-allthingshow-exfil-guide-2026-10-21]]. The Survivalist trait Die Hard calls an exfil for you if you reach it in Last Stand [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Some rewards need no exfil at all: XP stays after a failed run, Dynamic Op cash goes straight to the bank, and Orders count when completed (reported) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_f17f3ff635f7] [[src-gamerfuzion-underground-orders-2026-10-23]].
