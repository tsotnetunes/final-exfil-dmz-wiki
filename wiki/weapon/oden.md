The Oden is a hard-hitting 12.7x55mm bullpup rifle with a low rate of fire; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 39 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 65 weapon levels (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. The three published DMZ tier lists rank it low: the press list calls it unsuited to DMZ, a community list puts it in C and a stats site in D [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Its DMZ price is not confirmed yet.

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Oden Sentinel:** turns the rifle into a semi-automatic sniper with a bipod, a suppressor and a scope; it takes the place of the Barrel and Muzzle [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

Six values, listed under Multiplayer; whether they apply in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

| Value | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neck damage multiplier | 1.2 | 1.1 (-8%) |
| Upper torso damage multiplier | 1.2 | 1.1 (-8%) |
| Lower torso damage multiplier | 1.1 | 1 (-9%) |
| Sentinel: maximum damage range | 870 | 820 (-6%) |
| Sentinel: first middle damage range | 1810 | 1740 (-4%) |
| Sentinel: muzzle velocity | none | -0.07 |

In short, body shots without the Apex hit a little softer, and the Sentinel sniper setup lost some range and bullet speed.
