The Han 86 is a South Korean service rifle built to be steady at close and middle range; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 4 and it has 72 weapon levels [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Every published DMZ tier list puts it in the top tier [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The base gun reads 6,000 DMZ Cash in the menu (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]; pre-launch footage showed 4,750 [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-prices-2026-10-08]]. Attachments add up fast: one guide's Han 86 with an optic, a suppressor and a laser came to 42,750 (reported) [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]].

Beta players own the **Beta Forged** blueprint: the first build is free, and later ones cost 88,312 DMZ Cash [[src-allthingshow-dmz-gunsmith-weapons-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. See [[blueprints]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Han X20 Skybreaker:** a targeting optic and a 20mm airburst launcher; it takes the place of the Optic, Stock and Barrel [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

Three values, listed under Multiplayer; the notes do not say whether they apply in DMZ, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Why do players rate it?

The press list calls it the safest choice outside close range, with good range and easy recoil [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]]. After the 28 Oct changes one stats site still ranks it first in DMZ while marking it as nerfed [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].
