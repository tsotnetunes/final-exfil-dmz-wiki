The Hyeon Burst is a North Korean prototype rifle that fires three-round bursts; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 21 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 68 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. DMZ lists disagree on it: one community list puts it in its top tier, while a press list and a stats site put it in their B tier [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: 5,250 DMZ Cash in the menu after launch (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]], 4,750 in pre-launch footage [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-prices-2026-10-08]]. The Lieutenant Jang-Ui carries one [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. One player reported getting a Hyeon Burst blueprint after killing a squad that was hunting them; not confirmed [clm_a2c25299e29f]. Players also report a War Spoils camo for it ([[war-spoils-camo]]) [clm_e153c3eb028f].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Hyeon Hyperburst:** a three-round burst followed by slower, controlled automatic fire [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

All three changes hit the Hyperburst Apex and are listed under Multiplayer; whether they apply in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

| Value (Hyperburst) | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full-auto maximum damage | 37 | 34 (-8%) |
| Full-auto maximum damage range | 970 | 1220 (+26%) |
| First middle damage range | listed | removed |

## What comes in Season 1?

From 6 Nov 2026 [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]]: the **Hyeon Auto** Apex, which makes the rifle fully automatic, as a Wager Match event reward (it was in the beta but not at launch [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]), and the **Blue Viper** blueprint for finishing all 100 premium Battle Pass tiers.
