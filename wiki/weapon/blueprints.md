A Blueprint is a named, ready-made version of a gun with its own look and parts. In DMZ every Blueprint you own can be built once for free in the [[gunsmith]]; it is the one cosmetic that otherwise costs DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-free-blueprints-2026-10-21]].

## How do Blueprints work in DMZ?

- **Where:** Gunsmith, Buy New Weapon, then the Builds column and its Blueprints tab; the Builds screen also has Saved and Store tabs [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-free-blueprints-2026-10-21]]. Claiming one puts the gun in your Stash [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-free-blueprints-2026-10-21]]. Blueprints from the Vault Edition and from store bundles both show up there [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- **No Manual needed:** you can build a Blueprint without the gun's Weapon Manual, but it does not unlock the base gun ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]].
- **After the free build:** a Blueprint lost in a deployment is gone, and you can buy it again for less than building the same gun from scratch [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-free-blueprints-2026-10-21]]. Prices vary by Blueprint: the Beta Forged Han 86 costs 88,312 DMZ Cash to rebuild [[src-allthingshow-dmz-gunsmith-weapons-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]], the Voltspine Kastov 762 136,687 (reported) [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].
- **Changing parts:** you can modify a Blueprint and save the result as a build. Launch fixes: such builds could not be bought on Nintendo Switch 2 (20 Oct) and were charged the full price of the original Blueprint (25 Oct) [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
