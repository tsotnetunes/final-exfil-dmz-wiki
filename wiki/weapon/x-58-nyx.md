The X-58 Nyx is a suppressed 5.8x21mm SMG made for stealth; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 34 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 53 weapon levels (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. All three published DMZ tier lists put it in their top two tiers, and the press list calls it the easiest SMG for beginners [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Pre-launch footage priced it at 2,850 DMZ Cash [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-prices-2026-10-08]]; the launch price is not confirmed yet. The Lieutenant called the Warden ([[the-warden]]) carries one [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]; one guide lists his drop as an X-58 Nyx blueprint [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]]. His named version, the Incarcerator, is an Ultra X-58 NYX valued at $8,500 and a rare drop [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**X-58 Void:** a subsonic setup for very quiet shots and hits that are hard to trace; it takes the place of the Barrel and Ammunition [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].
