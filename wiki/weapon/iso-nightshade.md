The ISO Nightshade is a fast-firing 9x19mm SMG that suits most roles; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 4 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 64 weapon levels (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. A press list ranks it with the best DMZ SMGs and a stats site has it third overall, while a community list puts it in B [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Pre-launch footage priced it at 2,850 DMZ Cash [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-prices-2026-10-08]]; the launch price is not confirmed [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. In one post-launch cart its first attachment slot cost 1,000 and a Pulse-MW Laser 1,900 more [[src-allthingshow-dmz-gunsmith-weapons-2026-10-21]]; another guide's ISO Nightshade with a single muzzle came to 13,500 in all (reported) [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]].

- **Blueprint:** the Vault Edition includes one, **La Sombra Venenosa** [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-free-blueprints-2026-10-21]]; a 24 Oct fix stopped bullets appearing to float in its part-used magazines [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. See [[blueprints]].
- **Lieutenant:** Jin-Tae carries one [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]; his named version, High Roller's Fortune, is a Legendary with a gold camo and a rare drop (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**ISO Trace:** tracking rounds that show a hit target's movement in your optic; it takes the place of the Optic and Ammunition [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. On 20 Oct a fix made the ISO Apex work as intended in DMZ [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

Hand, lower arm and upper arm damage multipliers on both sides went from 0.95 to 1 (+5%), part of a pass the notes say aims at more even damage across hit locations. The change is listed under Multiplayer; whether it applies in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What comes in Season 1?

A BlackCell ISO Nightshade blueprint, from 6 Nov 2026 [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].
