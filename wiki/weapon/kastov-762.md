The Kastov 762 is a fully automatic 7.62x39mm rifle with a heavy hit per bullet; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 34 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 68 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. All three published DMZ tier lists put it in their top two tiers; the press list warns that its recoil takes work to control [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: 5,500 DMZ Cash in the menu after launch (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]], 4,750 in pre-launch footage [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-prices-2026-10-08]].

The Vault Edition's Signature Weapon Collection includes a Kastov 762 blueprint [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_b92ce0a1725f], named **Voltspine** [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-free-blueprints-2026-10-21]]. Its first DMZ build is free; later builds cost 136,687 DMZ Cash (reported) [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. See [[blueprints]].

One guide says the Lieutenant called the Chemist carries a Kastov 762 (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]; another lists an Oris 8.6 blueprint for him instead [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Kastov ARC:** a disruption barrel with a built-in optic that sends out an RF pulse; it takes the place of the Barrel [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

Its maximum damage range went from 950 to 1050 (+11%), listed under Multiplayer; whether this applies in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What comes in Season 1?

From 6 Nov 2026 [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]: the **Kastov 545** Apex, which fires 5.45x39mm rounds for smoother handling and faster follow-up shots, as a Wager Match event reward (it was in the beta but not at launch [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]), and a BlackCell Kastov 762 blueprint.
