The Astaroth .57 is a 14.5mm sniper rifle, made to end a fight with one clean hit. Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 10 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]; one guide gives level 25 instead and counts 54 weapon levels (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]].

## How good is it in DMZ?

DMZ lists place it in the middle or near the bottom: B in a press list, C in a community list and D on a stats site [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]]. Against you it hurts: a boss guide says one shot from it takes off most of your armour and pushes you back (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

- **Weapon Manual:** listed by a community database [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]]; extract with it, then buy the gun in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], at 24,000 DMZ Cash, the highest launch price we have seen (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].
- **From [[roze]]:** the Lieutenant on the DBN Headquarters roof uses it and can drop a Rare one, valued at 6,400, or The Kraken instead, depending on the match [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]]. Another guide calls the drop a blueprint (reported) [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Astaroth .57 Infiltrator:** shows enemies through cover and walls within a limited range, cuts the magazine to one round and takes the place of the Optic [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].
