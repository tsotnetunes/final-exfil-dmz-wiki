The KG-7 Vulcan is a bolt-action sniper rifle whose bolt cycles quicker than most rifles of its class. Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 4, the earliest of the three launch snipers [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. It has 75 weapon levels, the longest track of any launch weapon, so its Apex comes last of all (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]].

## How good is it in DMZ?

Three of four DMZ lists we read put it in their A tier [[src-playhub-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-21]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]]; the press list notes it trades some damage for a faster rate of fire [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]]. A community list puts it in C [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]]. See [[best-weapons-in-dmz]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Its Weapon Manual is on the community list of Manuals [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]]: extract with it, then buy the gun in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], at 16,000 DMZ Cash in the menu after launch (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. One opened Gun Store locker held a ready KG-7, though locker contents vary (reported) [[src-allthingshow-gun-store-key-2026-10-22]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**KG-7 Drillcharge:** rounds loaded from the side that drill into a surface, then explode [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Players report that the Drillcharge rounds cannot be refilled during a DMZ match (reported) [clm_e384ad820ccc]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What comes in Season 1?

From 6 Nov 2026, the KG-7 is one of seven weapons with a BlackCell blueprint [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. See [[blueprints]].
