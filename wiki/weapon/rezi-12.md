The Rezi 12 is a semi-automatic 12-gauge shotgun for close range; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 4 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 60 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. All three published DMZ tier lists put it in their second tier, and the press list calls it the best shotgun in the game [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The base gun costs 4,000 DMZ Cash [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-rezi-12-enforcer-build-2026-10-21]]. The Vault Edition includes a Rezi 12 blueprint, **Shattered Code**, which builds once for free [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-free-blueprints-2026-10-21]] ([[blueprints]]).

## What does a full build cost?

One post-launch cart, reported by a guide site [[src-allthingshow-rezi-12-enforcer-build-2026-10-21]]:

| Part | DMZ Cash |
| --- | --- |
| Rezi 12 (base) | 4,000 |
| Haebang Bulsae-10 (muzzle) | 3,250 |
| ZLR Rogue 90 25" (barrel) | 6,000 |
| XRK Bygone-39 (rear grip) | 6,000 |
| No Stock | 6,000 |
| Rezi 12 Enforcer (Apex) | 52,000 |
| Total, paid at once | 118,250 |

The finished gun showed as a Legendary Rezi 12 in the Stash, with a sell value of only 4,300 [[src-allthingshow-rezi-12-enforcer-build-2026-10-21]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Rezi 12 Enforcer:** switches it to pump-action cycling and adds a side saddle of explosive slugs [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## Did it change after launch?

No tuning on 28 Oct [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. A 21 Oct fix stopped the Firing Range showing a time to kill of 0.00 for the Rezi 12 ([[firing-range]]) [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
