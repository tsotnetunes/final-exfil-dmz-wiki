The PPSh-41 is a close-range SMG with a very high rate of fire and a large magazine; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 30 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 62 weapon levels (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. All three published DMZ tier lists put it in their top tier; the press list singles out how well it strips armor [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Pre-launch footage priced it at 2,850 DMZ Cash [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-prices-2026-10-08]]; the launch price is not confirmed yet. One writer found its Manual in the barracks at Chang-san Air Base, and its pickup card showed the usual 5,000 payout (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-weapon-manual-farm-2026-10-22]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**PPSh-41 Ember:** an underbarrel launcher that fires incendiary rounds; it takes the place of the Barrel, Underbarrel and Magazine [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].
