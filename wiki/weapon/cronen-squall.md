The Cronen Squall is an open-bolt bullpup assault rifle with range close to a marksman rifle; Multiplayer unlocks it last of the launch rifles, at player level 55 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. One guide counts 67 weapon levels for it (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. Do not confuse it with the [[cronen-osprey-assault-rifle]], the Season 1 rifle from the same maker.

## Is the Cronen Squall good in DMZ?

It is a solid middle pick: a press list, a community list and a stats site all put it in B [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]]. One boss guide suggests it, or a PPSh-41, for shooting the [[colossus]] in the upper body from the bed of a parked truck (reported) [[src-allthingshow-colossus-2026-10-21]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Two community databases list the Manual item, and one shows it worth 5,000 DMZ Cash (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. The Gunsmith price list we use gives no price for the rifle itself [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Cronen Maelstrom:** fires a missile that you steer while aiming down sights; it takes the place of the Laser and Underbarrel [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].
