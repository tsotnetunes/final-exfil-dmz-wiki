The MAR-9 is a semi-automatic marksman rifle chambered in 7.62 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. One guide counts 73 weapon levels for it (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]].

## Is the MAR-9 good in DMZ?

Most lists rate it well. A press list, a stats site and a second community list put it in A, the press list noting that semi-auto fire keeps its kick easy to manage [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]] [[src-playhub-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-21]]. One community list puts it in C with most other marksman and sniper rifles [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]]. All date from late October.

## How do you get it in DMZ?

- **Gunsmith:** extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it ([[weapon-manuals]], [[gunsmith]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; the menu price read in one database is 6,500 DMZ Cash (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. A community database lists the Manual item without a value (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]].
- **From a Lieutenant:** [[gorani]], the marksman at Mujin Pass, carries one [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]]. One guide says he drops either a Rare MAR-9 valued at $1,700 or, rarely, the Incisor, a Legendary MAR-9 valued at $6,900 (reported) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**MAR-9 Auto:** converts it to fully automatic fire at a higher rate; it takes the place of the Trigger Action [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].
