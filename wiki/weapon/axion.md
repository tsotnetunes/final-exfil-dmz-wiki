The Axion is an assault rifle firing subsonic .300 BLK rounds, built for players who land their shots; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 52, the second-last rifle in the class [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. One guide counts 69 weapon levels for it (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]].

## Is the Axion good in DMZ?

Opinions split more than for any other rifle. A community tier list puts it in A next to the M4 and Kastov 762, and a stats site also rates it A [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]]. A press list puts it in its lowest group, the guns it calls unsuited to DMZ [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]]. All three lists date from late October, before the Season 1 tuning. See [[weapons]] for the full lists.

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A community database lists a Manual: Axion item but shows no value for it (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]], and the Gunsmith price list we use gives no price for the Axion [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Axion Clearcut:** a chainsaw-style rear grip with a handlebar foregrip and a laser for hip fire, made for moving and shooting; it takes the place of the Optic, Laser, Underbarrel, Stock and Rear Grip [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. One guide says the game names it Axion Reaver (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## Has it been changed?

On 13 Nov 2026 the Axion was one of the single weapons named in a flinch tuning pass, listed under the Multiplayer heading only; whether it applies in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] ([[movement-and-tuning]]). It had no tuning line on 28 Oct or 19 Nov 2026.
