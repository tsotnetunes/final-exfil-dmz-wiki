The Signal 50 is a .50 caliber anti-materiel sniper rifle with a barrel that soaks up recoil; the official launch guide puts its Multiplayer unlock at player level 35 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. One guide gives level 50 instead and counts 74 weapon levels (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]].

## Is the Signal 50 good in DMZ?

Most lists rate it highly: a press list, a stats site and one community list put it in A [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]] [[src-playhub-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-21]], while another community list puts it in C with most sniper rifles [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]]. Two boss guides suggest it for the [[colossus]] at the Citadel, firing from high ground or from the Armory stair landing (reported) [[src-allthingshow-colossus-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-colossus-2026-10-22]]. All of these lists predate 23 Nov 2026, when DMZ's own tuning made it hit harder past close range while assault rifles, SMGs and LMGs lost damage; it was the one gun that update strengthened (see below) [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The menu price read in one database is 14,000 DMZ Cash, among the dearest guns after the Astaroth .57 and KG-7 Vulcan (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. A community database lists the Manual item without a value (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Signal Inferno:** incendiary rounds that deal extra damage to vehicles and Killstreaks; it takes the place of the Ammunition [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed in DMZ on 23 Nov 2026?

On 23 Nov 2026 Infinity Ward published the first weapon tuning written for DMZ itself, and the Signal 50 was its only buff: it keeps more of its damage past close range and rewards headshots more [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] ([[movement-and-tuning]]).

| Value (DMZ) | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First middle damage | 145 | 180 (+24%) |
| Minimum damage | 140 | 168 (+20%) |
| Head damage multiplier | 1.3 | 1.6 (+23%) |

Its maximum damage was not changed. At the same time every assault rifle, SMG and LMG on the list lost damage, so the update tilts DMZ toward long-range sniping. These are DMZ figures; no earlier patch line had tuned the Signal 50 in any mode.
