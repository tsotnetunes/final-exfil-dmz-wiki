## How do you get weapons in DMZ?

- **Weapon Manual, then Gunsmith:** the only permanent unlock. Extract with the Manual, then buy the base gun at DMZ level 10 or later ([[weapon-manuals]], [[gunsmith]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A community database lists a Manual for every launch gun except the knife (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapons-2026-10-31]], while the game's Manual board counted 28 [[src-allthingshow-dmz-gunsmith-weapons-2026-10-21]].
- **Loot:** enemies, Lieutenants, killed players and military containers in police stations and compounds; a looted gun you extract stays in your Stash until you lose it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]].
- **Vendor:** a rotating stock for DMZ Cash, which can include guns you have not unlocked ([[weapon-vendor]]) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].
- **Blueprints:** each one you own builds once for free ([[blueprints]]).
- **Free Loadout and Stash** are the other routes the official guide lists, and each [[operator-prestige]] pays out two weapons [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

You can carry two primaries, two secondaries or one of each; some Weapon Backpacks hold more [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]].

Not stated. The 28 Oct weapon changes sit under Multiplayer in the patch notes, and on 23 Oct the developers reminded players that Multiplayer and DMZ have separate weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. See [[movement-and-tuning]].

## Which weapon bugs were fixed in DMZ?

From the launch patch notes [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]:

- **20 Oct:** the ISO Nightshade's Apex did not work as intended in DMZ.
- **21 Oct:** on PC, performance suffered when a Heartbeat Sensor was held while a weapon was equipped.
- **28 Oct:** guns could drop when you moved them from the primary slot into a Backpack, or vanish after repeated swaps with a Backpack that stores weapons; and adding items to your Backpack with the 3D Printer during a deployment could send you in without your Free Loadout weapons.

Gunsmith fixes are on [[gunsmith]].
