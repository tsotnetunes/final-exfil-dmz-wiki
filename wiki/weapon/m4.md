The M4 is an adaptable fully automatic service rifle; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 14 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 72 weapon levels [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. It is the official guide's example DMZ weapon, and all three published DMZ tier lists put it in their top two tiers [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]). The official guide prices a base M4 at about 5,000 DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; one guide read 6,250 in the menu after launch (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]], and pre-launch footage showed 4,750 [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-prices-2026-10-08]]. A fully kitted M4 with five attachments came to 99,987 after a 5% discount (reported) [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]].

Digital pre-orders include the **Inner Circuit** M4 blueprint, which builds once for free in DMZ (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-free-blueprints-2026-10-21]]. See [[blueprints]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**M4 Hurricane:** a top-loading 5.7x28mm bullpup-style conversion with a bigger magazine and a faster rate of fire; it takes the place of the Barrel and Magazine [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

Its maximum damage range went from 990 to 1020 (+3%), listed under Multiplayer; whether this applies in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. A 24 Oct fix stopped a wrong attachment being fitted to the M4, which had caused a visual bug on its muzzle [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What comes in Season 1?

From 6 Nov 2026: an **M16 Burst Attachment** for controlled burst fire as an event reward, and a BlackCell M4 blueprint, **Perimeter Lance** [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]].
