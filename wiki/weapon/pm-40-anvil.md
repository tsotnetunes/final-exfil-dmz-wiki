The PM-40 Anvil is a pump-action launcher that lobs 43mm grenades. It is the fourth and last launcher on the Multiplayer unlock track, at player level 54 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and like the other launchers it has 46 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. Unlike the PILA, RPG-8 and JOKR, it is not described as a vehicle weapon: the official list presents it as a grenade launcher rather than an anti-vehicle rocket [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract its Weapon Manual and buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. One community database lists a Manual: PM-40 Anvil item but shows no sale value (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]]. No Gunsmith price for any launcher has been published.

## Can you customize it?

No. Launchers take no attachments and have no Apex Attachment; the Gunsmith changes only how they look [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Is it worth carrying?

Not by the published DMZ tier lists. Two put the PM-40 Anvil in their lowest tier, one calling the launchers unsuitable for DMZ fights [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]], and a third leaves launchers unranked [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]]. No patch note has tuned it since launch, as of 28 Nov 2026 [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
