The APOC-338 is a belt-fed .338 machine gun with a slow cyclic rate and heavy recoil; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 44 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 56 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. The DMZ lists split widely: a press list puts it in its second tier for its very high damage but warns the recoil is harsh for beginners, a community list puts it in C and a stats site in D [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Its Weapon Manual is a lootable item [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]]: extract with it, then buy the gun in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], at 7,750 DMZ Cash in the menu after launch (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**APOC-338 Praetor:** a ten-round belt that locks it to semi-automatic fire; it takes the place of the Magazine [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].
