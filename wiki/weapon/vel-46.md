The VEL 46 is an agile 4.6x30mm SMG whose recoil is easy to predict; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 5 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 60 weapon levels (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]].

## Is it good in DMZ?

The lists split. A community list on 28 Oct put it in its top tier, but a press list on 22 Oct called it unsuited to DMZ and a stats site on 31 Oct ranks it in C [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]]. None of them states its method, so test it in the [[firing-range]] before you spend on it.

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Pre-launch footage priced it at 2,850 DMZ Cash [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-prices-2026-10-08]]; the launch price is not confirmed yet.

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**VEL 46 Snap:** a tactical sling that makes weapon swaps faster and allows Quick Draw with Overkill; it takes the place of the Stock [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Overkill is a Multiplayer perk [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]; whether the sling's Overkill bonus does anything in DMZ is not confirmed yet. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

Hand, lower arm and upper arm damage multipliers on both sides went from 0.95 to 1 (+5%), six values in all. The change is listed under Multiplayer; whether it applies in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
