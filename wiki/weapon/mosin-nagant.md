<!-- human:start -->
The Mosin-Nagant is a bolt-action marksman rifle that hits hard at long range; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 36 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 69 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].

## Is it good in DMZ?

A press list puts it in its second tier because it can kill an enemy without armor in one shot, though it reloads slowly; a community list puts it in C and a stats site in D [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: 7,500 DMZ Cash in the menu after launch (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. The same guide saw a Rare Mosin-Nagant offered at the [[weapon-vendor]] for 32,750 (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]; a Vendor copy is lost if you die with it, and it does not unlock the gun [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Mosin Harpoon:** a spring-loaded bayonet you can fire off and pick back up; it takes the place of the Barrel and Muzzle [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## Did it change after launch?

No tuning so far. A 28 Oct fix in the Multiplayer notes corrected its firing pin sometimes showing in the wrong place after the last round [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What comes in Season 1?

From 6 Nov 2026: the **Bellum** Ultra Mosin-Nagant blueprint at Battle Pass tier 100, and a BlackCell Mosin-Nagant blueprint [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]]. See [[blueprints]].
