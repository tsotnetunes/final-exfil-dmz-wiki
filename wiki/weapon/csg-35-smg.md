The CSG-35 is a new SMG for Season 1, which starts on 6 Nov 2026 [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [clm_1c8be2a1f8bc]. It is not in the game before then, so it is not in DMZ yet.

## How do you unlock it?

Reach tier 36 of the Season 1 Battle Pass. A Battle Pass blueprint of it sits at tier 73, and the Season 1 BlackCell pass adds another CSG-35 blueprint [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## What are its stats?

From the Season 1 announcement [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]:

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Weapon levels | 42 |
| Magazine | 28 rounds |
| Reserve | 112 rounds |
| Rate of fire | 1,200 rounds per minute |

That is faster than the other new Season 1 rifle, the Cronen Osprey, at 882 ([[cronen-osprey-assault-rifle]]).

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**CSG Crossfire:** you carry a CSG-35 in each hand, trading accuracy for a wide spray of fire downrange [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. At launch every Apex unlocks at the top of its gun's level track; the post does not say how this one unlocks ([[apex-attachments]]).

## Will it be in DMZ?

Not confirmed yet. The Season 1 post names no DMZ weapons and says nothing about Weapon Manuals [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Every launch gun except the knife has a Manual you must extract before the [[gunsmith]] sells it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-dmzhub-weapons-2026-10-31]], so look for a CSG-35 Manual in Hajin after 6 Nov ([[weapon-manuals]]). A blueprint you own can be built once for free in DMZ even without the Manual [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]] ([[blueprints]]). The five launch SMGs are in [[weapons]].
