The CSG-35 is a new SMG for Season 1 [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [clm_1c8be2a1f8bc], in the game since the season went live on 6 Nov 2026 [[src-cod-x-season-1-live-2026-11-06]].

## How do you unlock it?

Reach tier 36 of the Season 1 Battle Pass. A Battle Pass blueprint of it sits at tier 73, and the Season 1 BlackCell pass adds another CSG-35 blueprint [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## What are its stats?

From the Season 1 announcement [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]:

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Weapon levels | 42 |
| Magazine | 28 rounds |
| Reserve | 112 rounds |
| Rate of fire | 1,200 rounds per minute |

That is faster than the other new Season 1 rifle, the Cronen Osprey, at 882 ([[cronen-osprey-assault-rifle]]).

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**CSG Crossfire:** you carry a CSG-35 in each hand, trading accuracy for a wide spray of fire downrange [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. At launch every Apex unlocks at the top of its gun's level track; the post does not say how this one unlocks ([[apex-attachments]]).

## Does its camo grind work?

Not for everyone yet. Since 7 Nov 2026 Call of Duty has been looking into reports that its Platinum camo challenges do not track, the same fault as on the [[cronen-osprey-assault-rifle]], with no fix date [[src-cod-x-camo-tracking-2026-11-07]]. Weapon levels and camo progress are shared between Multiplayer and DMZ ([[dmz-camos]]).

## Can you use it in DMZ?

Likely, through a Weapon Manual like the launch guns. On 6 Nov 2026 a community database added a "Manual: CSG-35" item, filed as a Season 1 Weapon Manual, but it shows no value and no place to find it yet (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-07]]. The Season 1 post itself names no DMZ weapons and says nothing about Weapon Manuals [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Every launch gun except the knife has a Manual you must extract before the [[gunsmith]] sells it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-dmzhub-weapons-2026-10-31]], so extract a CSG-35 Manual before you can buy the gun ([[weapon-manuals]]). A blueprint you own can be built once for free in DMZ even without the Manual [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]] ([[blueprints]]). The five launch SMGs are in [[weapons]].
