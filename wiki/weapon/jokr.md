The JOKR is a launcher that fires a guided missile which climbs and then strikes its target from above. Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 43 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and like every launcher it has 46 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]].

## Is the Infrared mode working?

No. Since 2 Nov 2026 Infinity Ward has kept the JOKR's Infrared mode switched off in all modes because of an exploit, until a full fix ships [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [clm_6ca698ed62e3]. No patch note since has turned it back on, as of 28 Nov 2026 [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. On 5 Nov 2026 a DMZ fix let the JOKR fire in the FOB [[firing-range]] again [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

The same way as other guns: extract its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. One community database lists a Manual: Jokr item but shows no sale value for it (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]], and no Gunsmith price for launchers has been published.

## Can you customize it?

No. Launchers take no attachments and have no Apex Attachment; the Gunsmith changes only their look [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What is it good for in DMZ?

Vehicles. Commanders such as the [[main-nk-battle-tank]] need launchers, though guides name the RPG and the PILA rather than the JOKR [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]]. Against other squads it is weak: both DMZ tier lists that rank launchers put it in their lowest tier [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]]. Players report that its vehicle and killstreak destruction challenge, shared with the PILA and RPG-8, tracks in DMZ and needs 50 instead of 30 (reported) [clm_9e05ae7aa090].

The other launchers: [[pila]], [[rpg-8]]; every gun: [[weapons]].
