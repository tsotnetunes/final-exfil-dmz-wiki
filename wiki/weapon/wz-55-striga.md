The Wz.55 Striga is a mobile 9x18mm SMG with a low rate of fire; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 18 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 60 weapon levels (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. DMZ tier lists place it in the middle or lower: B on a community list, C on a press list that calls the C group unsuited to DMZ, and C on a stats site [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Pre-launch footage priced it at 2,850 DMZ Cash [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-prices-2026-10-08]]; the launch price is not confirmed yet.

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**WZ.55 Striga Rampage:** turns it into an akimbo pair; it takes the place of the Optic, Stock and Underbarrel [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].
