The PILA is one of the four launchers in Modern Warfare 4: it fires missiles at vehicles that you can lock on and guide, or shoot straight without a lock. Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 4, the first launcher on the list [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 46 weapon levels, like every launcher [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. The Warzone Season 1 notes add that its missiles arm only after 11 m of flight but then explode on contact with a vehicle at any range; they do not say the same holds in DMZ [[src-warzone-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Like other guns, by extracting its Weapon Manual and then buying it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Two community lists include the Manual: Pila item, and one shows it paying 5,000 DMZ Cash if you sell it instead (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. No Gunsmith price for launchers has been published yet. Extracting further Manuals of the same gun counts toward its hidden War Spoils camo, and players have seen it on the PILA [clm_ce6b0de65759] ([[war-spoils-camo]]).

## Can you customize it?

No. Launchers take no attachments and have no Apex Attachment; the Gunsmith changes only their look [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. On 6 Nov 2026 a fix let players buy some Rocket Launcher Blueprints in the Gunsmith that had been stuck [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## What is it good for in DMZ?

Vehicles and Commanders. Guides name it, with the RPG-8, as the tool for the [[main-nk-battle-tank]], fired from cover; one solo player needed about 25 rockets for one tank, so bring ammo (reported) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. It does nothing to [[colossus]], whose suit shrugs off explosives [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-colossus-2026-10-22]]. Against players it is a poor pick: both DMZ tier lists that rank launchers put the PILA in their lowest tier [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## Do its challenges count in DMZ?

Players report that the vehicle and killstreak destruction challenge shared by the PILA, JOKR and RPG-8 tracks in DMZ and asks for 50 instead of 30 (reported) [clm_9e05ae7aa090]. The other launchers: [[rpg-8]], [[jokr]]; every gun: [[weapons]].
