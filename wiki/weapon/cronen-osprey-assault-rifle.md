## How do you unlock it?

Reach tier 16 of the Season 1 Battle Pass. A Battle Pass blueprint of it waits at tier 87, and the Season 1 BlackCell pass adds another Cronen Osprey blueprint [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]].
