Reach tier 16 of the Season 1 Battle Pass. A Battle Pass blueprint of it waits at tier 87, and the Season 1 BlackCell pass adds another Cronen Osprey blueprint [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Cronen Helix Mk. II:** it fires both when you pull the trigger and when you release it, giving high damage at a quick semi-automatic rate [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. At launch every Apex unlocks at the top of its gun's level track; the post does not say how this one unlocks ([[apex-attachments]]).

## Does its camo grind work?

Not for everyone yet. Since 7 Nov 2026 Call of Duty has been looking into reports that its Platinum camo challenges do not track, the same fault as on the [[csg-35-smg]], with no fix date [[src-cod-x-camo-tracking-2026-11-07]]. One player says 140 headshots in DMZ earned no Platinum progress (reported) [clm_e1f3c5801f26]. Weapon levels and camo progress are shared between Multiplayer and DMZ ([[dmz-camos]]).

Likely, through a Weapon Manual like the launch guns.
