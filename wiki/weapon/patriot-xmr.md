The Patriot XMR is a fully automatic rifle with heavy recoil that pays off if you control it; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 27 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 72 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. All three published DMZ tier lists put it in their B tier [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual (the Manual is a lootable item [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]), then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: 6,500 DMZ Cash in the menu after launch (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. The Lieutenant called the Doctor carries one [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]; one guide lists his drop as a Patriot XMR blueprint [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]]. Players report a War Spoils camo for it ([[war-spoils-camo]]) [clm_2b05fa148d3c].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Patriot XMR Volt:** an underbarrel launcher that fires electric stun darts; it takes the place of the Barrel [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

Two attachments now cost less recoil control, listed under Multiplayer; whether this applies in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

| Attachment | Recoil control penalty before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EXP Haste 80L 14" (light barrel) | 8% | 6% |
| FJX Fury IV Grip (skeleton grip) | 5% | 4% |

The same day the developers listed a known issue: the Patriot XMR Platinum camo challenge does not show its progress correctly, though it still counts [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
