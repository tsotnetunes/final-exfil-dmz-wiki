The Patriot XMR is a fully automatic rifle with heavy recoil that pays off if you control it; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 27 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 72 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. All three published DMZ tier lists put it in their B tier [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual (the Manual is a lootable item [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]), then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: 6,500 DMZ Cash in the menu after launch (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. The Lieutenant called the Doctor ([[the-doctor]]) carries one [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]; one guide lists his drop as a Patriot XMR blueprint [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]]. His named version, Doctor's Salve, is a Legendary Patriot XMR valued at $6,900 and a rare drop [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]]. Players report a War Spoils camo for it ([[war-spoils-camo]]) [clm_2b05fa148d3c].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Patriot XMR Volt:** an underbarrel launcher that fires electric stun darts; it takes the place of the Barrel [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed in DMZ on 23 Nov 2026?

On 23 Nov 2026 Infinity Ward published the first weapon tuning written for DMZ itself, and the Patriot XMR was one of nine assault rifles whose damage went down at every range listed, by 10 to 12% [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] ([[movement-and-tuning]]).

| Value (DMZ) | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum damage | 43 | 38 (-12%) |
| First middle damage | 39 | 35 (-10%) |
| Minimum damage | 34 | 30 (-12%) |

These are DMZ's own damage figures, kept separate from Multiplayer's, so changes listed under Multiplayer elsewhere on this page are not DMZ values. The same update made the Signal 50 hit harder ([[signal-50]]).

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

Two attachments now cost less recoil control, listed under Multiplayer; whether this applies in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

| Attachment | Recoil control penalty before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EXP Haste 80L 14" (light barrel) | 8% | 6% |
| FJX Fury IV Grip (skeleton grip) | 5% | 4% |

The same day the developers listed a known issue: the Patriot XMR Platinum camo challenge does not show its progress correctly, though it still counts [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What changed on 19 Nov 2026?

Its minimum damage went from 24 to 25 (+4%), so it loses a little less at long range. The change is listed under Multiplayer; whether it applies in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
