The Type 73, made in Korea, is a 7.62x54r machine gun for middle and long range; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 9 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 65 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. A press list and a stats site put it in their second tier (the press list cites good damage and range held back by poor handling); a community list puts it in C [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]]: 7,500 DMZ Cash in the menu after launch (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].

The Lieutenant Bale carries one [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Bale's named version is the **Dreadnought**, a Legendary LMG found at the Tank Depot: one guide says it has a 100-round box and is a Type 73, another that it comes with five attachments fitted [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-gunsmith-weapons-2026-10-21]]. It shares its name with a vehicle, the Aston Martin Dreadnought ([[vehicles]]).

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Type 73 Assault:** a magazine mounted on top that holds explosive rounds; it takes the place of the Magazine and Ammunition [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

With the Assault Apex fitted, its minimum damage went from 19 to 20 (+5%). The change is listed under Multiplayer; whether it applies in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
