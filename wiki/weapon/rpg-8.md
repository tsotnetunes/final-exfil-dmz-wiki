The RPG-8 is a shoulder-fired rocket launcher that fires one unguided rocket meant for vehicles. Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 23 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and like every launcher it has 46 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Two community lists include the Manual: RPG-8 item, and one shows it selling for 5,000 DMZ Cash (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapon-manuals-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. No Gunsmith price for launchers has been published.

## Can you customize it?

No. Launchers take no attachments and have no Apex Attachment; only their look changes [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A 6 Nov 2026 fix made some Rocket Launcher Blueprints buyable again in the Gunsmith [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## What is it good for in DMZ?

- **The tank.** Bullets do nothing to the [[main-nk-battle-tank]]; guides use rockets from an RPG or the PILA, from cover, and aim at the thinner armour at the back and on top (reported). Expect to need a lot of rockets [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
- **The Deathstalker.** Players report that two RPG rockets bring the [[deathstalker]] helicopter down (reported) [clm_2709a2129c64]; the Stalking the Stalker Order asks for one Deathstalker downed with a launcher (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].
- **Not players.** Both DMZ tier lists that rank launchers place the RPG-8 in their lowest tier for fights against other squads [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]].

## Do its challenges count in DMZ?

Players report that the vehicle and killstreak destruction challenge for the RPG-8, PILA and JOKR tracks in DMZ and needs 50 instead of 30 (reported) [clm_9e05ae7aa090]. The other launchers: [[pila]], [[jokr]]; every gun: [[weapons]].
