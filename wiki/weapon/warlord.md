The Warlord is a pump-action 12-gauge shotgun with a large tube magazine and a slow reload; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 32, and it has 60 weapon levels [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]].

## Is it good in DMZ?

Opinions are split: a community list puts it in its top tier, a press list in its second tier (it fires faster than the Rezi 12 but reloads slowly), and a stats site in its lowest tier [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]] [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]]. Compare it with the [[rezi-12]] in the [[firing-range]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: 3,500 DMZ Cash in the menu after launch, the lowest price on that guide's list (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. The Lieutenant Iskra carries one [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]; Iskra's named version, the Legendary **Widow Maker** blueprint, is a rare drop (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**Warlord Lumen:** a strobe that blinds when you aim and then needs to recharge; it takes the place of the Optic and Laser [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

## What changed on 28 Oct 2026?

With 12G Slug Rounds fitted, its second middle damage range went from 900 to 810 (-10%). The change is listed under Multiplayer; whether it applies in DMZ is not stated, and DMZ has its own weapon tuning [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
