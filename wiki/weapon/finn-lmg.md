The FiNN LMG is a belt-fed 5.56 machine gun with a high rate of fire and good control; Multiplayer unlocks it at player level 4 [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]], and it has 59 weapon levels [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. Two of the three published DMZ lists put it in their second tier and the third in B; the press list notes it is weak at range but makes up for it with control and damage [[src-destructoid-dmz-weapons-tier-list-2026-10-22]] [[src-wzstats-dmz-meta-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-tier-list-2026-10-28]].

## How do you get it in DMZ?

Extract with its Weapon Manual, then buy it in the [[gunsmith]] ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: 8,000 DMZ Cash in the menu after launch, the dearest LMG on that guide's list (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].

## What is its Apex Attachment?

**FiNN Adverse:** removes the cap on its rate of fire, so it can overheat and need to cool down [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. See [[apex-attachments]].

The **Fortune's Favor** FiNN LMG blueprint is an instant reward for buying the Season 1 Battle Pass, from 6 Nov 2026 [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]]. See [[blueprints]].
