Rogue Operators are the other players in your match: rival human squads who can fight you, call a truce or join up with you [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [clm_9acf7f2eb6df].

## How many are in a match?

Up to 60 players at launch, in 20 squads of three, according to the developers' Q&A as recapped on video [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. Matchmaking tries to put solos with solos, duos with duos and trios with trios, but does not promise it [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] ([[matchmaking]]).

## Fight, truce or team up?

- The Ping Wheel can ask another squad for a Truce or to join up, and proximity chat is the quickest way to talk to nearby Operators [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[proximity-chat]]).
- When squads merge, the group is capped at four players [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
- Killing a rival Operator pays more DMZ XP than killing AI [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What happens when you kill players?

Each player kill earns a Bounty at once, shown on your Dog Tag; four kills make you Wanted, and nearby Operators are warned when you come close [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[bounty-system]], [[wanted-status]]). A player who kills you can take your Dog Tag, and extracting it pays your Bounty to them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[dog-tags]]).

## Where will you run into them?

- **Around bosses.** A Lieutenant's Dog Tag Case shows whoever carries it on every Tac-Map, and Commander fights draw rival squads in [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[lieutenants]], [[commander-threats]]).
- **In the Hunt Operators Dynamic Op**, which sets a full rival squad against you [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[dynamic-operations]]); its missing mission timer was fixed on 30 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- **In X01** from Season 1, where rival squads roam alongside the 100 Spirits soldiers [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]] ([[x01]]).

The AI side is on [[north-korean-forces]].
