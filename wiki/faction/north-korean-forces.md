A secretive North Korean faction holds Hajin with fixed and roving patrols; depending on how much of a threat you are, they ignore you, search for you or attack [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

- **Soldiers:** Tier 1 grunts; Tier 2 armoured; Tier 3 armoured with gas masks; Tier 4 faceless armoured. All come in various ranks and equipment.
- **Machines:** autonomous drone swarms, autonomous LMG drones, robotic K9 quadrupeds (mostly underground).
- **Patrols:** attack helicopters and road convoys.
- **Bosses:** [[lieutenants]] and Commanders ([[commander-threats]]).

The response grows with the area's threat level ([[threat-escalation]]); killing too many sends a Hunt Squad ([[marked-for-death]]). Rival players: [[rogue-operators]].

**Name.** Japanese press at Infinity Ward's Tokyo media briefing call the armed group that every player fights in Hajin Hundred Spirits, and describe the [[lieutenants]] as fighters it hired; the same report says enemy soldiers come in three tiers plus a rare, much stronger fourth, and that weather affects the AI's sight and hearing ([[dynamic-weather]]) [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]].
