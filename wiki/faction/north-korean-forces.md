Japanese press at Infinity Ward's Tokyo briefing had already written the name as Hundred Spirits and described the Lieutenants as fighters it hired [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]]. Dexerto's wiki calls it the Korean People's Army (reported) [[src-dexerto-dmz-enemy-types-2026-10-31]].

## How do they react to you?

Fixed and roving patrols ignore you, search for you or attack, depending on how much of a threat you are [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; Activision describes squad AI that coordinates and reacts to what you do [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. Each area has its own threat level that rises with noise and violence ([[threat-escalation]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]], and weather changes what the soldiers can see and hear ([[dynamic-weather]]) [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]]. At high threat, attack and Deathstalker helicopters come as reinforcements [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]], and reinforcements can also arrive by elevator [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## What is a Hunt Squad?

Kill enough soldiers in one deployment and the Hostiles Kill Count fills: the faction sends a Hunt Squad after you and you are Marked for Death ([[marked-for-death]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. A community guide describes the same meter as a wanted level at the top right of the HUD that rises with AI kills, sends search parties and falls when you break line of sight (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## Which Traits help against the AI?

Viper Sense hides you from AI targeting ([[viper-sense]]), Adaptive Restock makes AI drop one extra item, and Hostile Looter sometimes adds an Uncommon printer ingredient to what AI drop [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
