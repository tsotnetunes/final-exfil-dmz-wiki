Operation Critical Extraction is a Medium-threat Story Mission at [[mirae-general-hospital]]: you find a high-value target recovering there, General Kang, and get him out on the roof helicopter [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]]. It is open from the start, alongside Silent Ledger and Shopping Hacks, and it closes the Human Intel chapter of Orders ([[human-intel-orders]]) [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]]. The official Story Missions trailer of 8 Oct 2026 played this mission [[src-fpsjp-story-missions-trailer-2026-10-09]].

## How do you start it?

Finish Orders page 1, then pick it under Deploy, Story Missions [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]]. The card suggests a 2-Plate Armor Vest and a Medkit [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]].

## How do you complete it?

1. Infil in the South Korean Border Zone, about 770 m out, and approach the hospital [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]].
2. Find Kang in the upper patient wards; players point to an intensive care room on the second floor [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]].
3. Sedate him. He stays in his hospital bed and you push it; you never carry him [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]].
4. Ride the first lift down to the lobby, roll the bed to the second bank of lifts and hold off attackers for about a minute while they arrive [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).
5. Go up to the roof and push the bed into the exfil helicopter. The mission completes as it lifts off, and you extract with your gear [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]].

The bed works as moving cover, and once Kang and his escort go up the lift locks behind them, so a player left below needs another way out such as a Fulton [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported). During the lift hold a lost supply drop can land on red smoke in the atrium ([[secure-lost-supply-drop]]) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported). The Lieutenant called The Doctor holds the hospital, with a Kill the Doctor side op [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]; a guide says his key opens a Command Office holding a 3D Printer recipe [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]] (reported).
