The Grid is one of the two chapters of [[dmz-orders]] you choose between after page 11 (the other is [[hornets-nest-orders]]): two pages about drones, Vendor weapons and fast Ops that end with the Story Mission [[operation-grid-assault]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. Site lists place it on pages 12 and 13 [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## What does each Order ask?

| Page | Order | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1/2 | I'm Over Here | Draw a drone off with an Inflatable Decoy, then destroy 3 Heavy Combat Drones and 8 Combat Drones. |
| 1/2 | Worth Every Penny | Purchase any weapon at the DMZ Vendor ([[weapon-vendor]]), then get 50 kills using weapons of Rare quality or higher. |
| 1/2 | No Time | Complete one Dynamic Op within 8 minutes ([[dynamic-operations]]). |
| 2/2 | Opulent | Finish six Side Ops during one deployment ([[side-ops]]). |
| 2/2 | Civil Service | Head into NuriGO Mall to City Hall, take the Financial Ledger Fragments from the second floor and get out with them. |
| 2/2 | Operation Grid Assault | Finish that Story Mission. |

## Tips

- The Inflatable Decoy for I'm Over Here comes from page 11's reward [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].
- No Time: on 23 Oct 2026 Infinity Ward fixed the under-8-minutes requirement being shown wrongly [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. One player's counter read 2 but it ticked after a single fast Op [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] (reported). A short Op such as Ignition Point ([[dynamic-operations]]) once ended about a minute after deploying [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- Opulent: UAV Towers, SAM Sites, Lieutenant Hunt towers, marked safes and military compounds all count as Side Ops, so plan a route that passes several of them [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]].
- A later Order on page 23 is also called No Time and asks for four Dynamic Ops in one deployment [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].
