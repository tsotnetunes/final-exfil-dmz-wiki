Story Missions are DMZ's scripted, replayable missions with their own story, picked from Deploy at the FOB [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Seven exist at launch: [[breaking-new-ground]], which plays once as onboarding, and six Operations that open in three tiers; each Operation's card lists 25,000 DMZ Cash plus gear, a key or an infil vehicle [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]].

## How do you start a Story Mission?

At the FOB choose Deploy, then Story Missions, then a mission; each card shows the place, a briefing, a threat rating, three main objectives and the rewards [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]]. Story Missions unlock once you finish page 1 of [[dmz-orders]] and come back from your first deployment [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]; before launch, Japanese press heard the same link to Orders [[src-fpsjp-story-missions-trailer-2026-10-09]] [[src-gamespark-tokyo-media-event-2026-10-01]]. A Story deployment drops you at that mission's own spawn point, close to its area [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [clm_94e4a795c9a7]; the casino, for example, has one [clm_5008eef40a70]. When you queue, the matchmaker groups you with players who picked the same deployment type [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]], and players report the missions can be done solo [clm_a6788c608374]. The active mission shows on the left of the HUD, with any Dynamic Op and Side Ops, while Orders sit on the right ([[tac-map]]) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]].

## How do Story Missions fit the Orders?

Each Orders chapter ends with an Order to finish one mission: Human Intel with Critical Extraction, Counterfeit with Silent Ledger, Underground with Shopping Hacks, The Grid with Grid Assault, Hornet's Nest with Iron Citadel, and page 21 asks for Fallen Ember [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. If you already finished that mission, its Order ticks by itself [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] (reported). Story Missions also unlock 3D Printer recipes, players say [clm_1373d27208f1].

## What do they pay, and can you replay them?

You can replay every mission [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]. The extraction summary adds a Story Mission Payout line, which was 5,000 per run in early-access walkthroughs [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported). Two guides say a replay pays the card cash again but never repeats a vehicle unlock [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]; Infinity Ward has not said how replays pay. On 29 Oct 2026 it raised the DMZ Cash for Story Mission replays a little, without amounts [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Squads wiped before exfil still saw their mission payouts in the summary [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported). Story Missions are among the best XP sources [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]], XP is kept even when you fail to exfil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and death costs no XP [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

**Random Story Mission.** The Deploy menu offers a random mission drawn from those you have finished [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported), and a weekly task asks for three random Story Missions [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]]. The Orders Surprise Me and Philanthropist use it ([[dmz-orders-pages]]) [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## What is the story?

DMZ's story follows the Modern Warfare 4 Campaign: a power vacuum in Hajin and a new threat to unmask [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-features-2026-10-09]]. Before launch, Infinity Ward described the missions as tightly scripted, with a CIA handler's lines and their own enemies [[src-gamingtrend-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]]. A Lieutenant often turns up near the end of a mission, players report [clm_c305b5549cf0]. Fallen Ember, the last mission so far, ends with a nuclear blast and no cutscene [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]; players report an ending scene once all 24 Orders pages are done [clm_34cd336f3bfa]. Before launch the developers said a Russian area of Hajin opens in Season 2 with its own story, missions and Dynamic Ops [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [clm_12a1524f7169].
