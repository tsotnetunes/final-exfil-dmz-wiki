Viral Reckoning is a Dynamic Op ([[dynamic-operations]]) in which you rescue a captive scientist from behind a keypad door, get him onto a helicopter, and then either leave for part of the reward or stay to bomb a biological weapon [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]].

## Where does it start?

Starts seen so far: a locker at the 14th Political Prison ([[prison-complex]]), a phone in the South Korean Border Zone, and [[songdo-wharf]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## How do you complete it?

1. Find the HVT who carries an enemy radio and kill him (in some runs you use a UAV Tower to find the scientist instead).
2. Take the radio to learn where the scientist is held.
3. Go there and open the keypad door with the code shown in your objective; it changes every match.
4. Free the scientist and take him to the landing zone.
5. Defend the zone until the helicopter lands, then put him aboard.
6. Either leave on that helicopter for partial credit, or collect an explosive and plant it on the biological WMD to finish the Op.

Steps: [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]; the leave-or-bomb choice is also on [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]].

## What does it pay?

In early access it paid 7,500 DMZ Cash and about 4,200 XP [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported: both sites show the same runs). Since 30 Oct 2026 each Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 and each step 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Risks

Waypoints sat 650 to 915 m apart in one run, so bring or find a vehicle, and the landing zone defence runs on a timer until the helicopter arrives [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).
