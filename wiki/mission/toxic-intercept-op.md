Toxic Intercept is a Dynamic Op ([[dynamic-operations]]) that ends with you hijacking an enemy fuel tanker and driving it to a marked spot, after you decrypt a stolen drive and call in a fuel resupply [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. Infinity Ward's patch notes name it the Toxic Intercept Op [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How do you complete it?

One run started in the South Korean Border Zone [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported). The steps [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]:

1. Loot an encrypted drive from a hidden cache (one was on Oamsik Turtle Island).
2. Decrypt it at an enemy computer (a tent in Compound Delta in that run) and hold the spot while it works.
3. Request a fuel resupply at a UAV Tower ([[uav-towers]]); NuriGO West in that run.
4. Hijack the marked yellow fuel tanker.
5. Drive it to the marked location to finish the Op.

Locations change from match to match, so follow the markers [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## What does it pay?

The Op panel showed about 4,500 XP in early access; no cash figure was recorded [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported). Since 30 Oct 2026 every Dynamic Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 DMZ Cash and 500 per finished step [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Risks

Enemies push the decryption tent while you defend it, and the tanker drive crosses open roads where you are easy to spot [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## Known bugs and fixes

On 21 Oct 2026 Infinity Ward fixed the Op failing after players had completed its Defend the Bomb objective [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [clm_3da0ec9f7cc5]. The guides do not list a bomb step, so some runs of this Op may include one that has not been written up yet; check in game.
