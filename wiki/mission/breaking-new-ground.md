Breaking New Ground is DMZ's onboarding mission: it starts by itself the first time you open DMZ, takes about ten minutes and teaches the basics alongside a dog called Rex [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [clm_a748c98e4921]. Infinity Ward's patch notes call it the onboarding mission [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. It is not in the Story Missions list, so you cannot replay it from a menu [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## What happens in it?

You cross three areas, and each step teaches one system [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]]:

| Area | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| North Korean wilderness | Track Rex, clear out the area, search a crashed drone, apply a Tourniquet ([[tourniquet]]), then bring Rex to safety. |
| [[deadtown]] | Head into the village for medical supplies, use a Medkit, follow a signal and scout from high ground. |
| Re-education facility | Slip in through the canal, find and open a drone black box, then make for exfil. |

The mission ends in a scripted capture instead of an extraction, and no reward screen follows [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## What comes after it?

You wake up at the FOB, called FOB Zulu, and recruit your first Operator before you can deploy [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]]. Next comes page 1 of [[dmz-orders]]: equip gear from the Stash, knock down three Firing Range targets, then deploy in a Dynamic Op; finishing that page and returning from your first deployment unlocks the [[story-missions]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]].
