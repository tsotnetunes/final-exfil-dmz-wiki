Chemical Denial is a Dynamic Op ([[dynamic-operations]]) and one of the two Ops in the official trailer of 6 Oct 2026, where it opened with looking into a power outage at the Tank Depot and inside the Chang-san Air Base bunker [[src-fpsjp-dynamic-ops-trailer-2026-10-07]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]]. In that footage its Side Ops sent the squad after [[bale]]: download intel, switch on a UAV Tower, then use Paid Intel to reach him [[src-fpsjp-dynamic-ops-trailer-2026-10-07]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]].

## How do you complete it?

Players have met two versions since launch [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]:

| Version | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| Chemicals | Loot the marked item from a car trunk (a sedan at Jayon Warehouses in one run), gather three chemical components, mix the compound at a workbench (Hanrim Village), drop it in the metal case, then get away before the site blows. |
| Data upload | At [[cheonwang-peak-array]]: restore power, hack the comms server, then protect the data transfer until it finishes. |

The chemical site explodes at the end, and staying too close fails the Op [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported). Infinity Ward's notes also place a route at [[nurigo-mall]] [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What does it pay?

Early access: 5,000 DMZ Cash and about 3,000 XP on the Op panel; the data-upload version paid 7,100 and 4,750 XP at extraction [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 each Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 and each step 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Risks

A Lieutenant called Iskra was met at the array during one run [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported), and the trailer's version led to Bale, so come ready for a boss fight ([[lieutenants]]).

## Known bugs and fixes

On 20 Oct 2026 Infinity Ward fixed a missing prompt that could stop players progressing this Op at NuriGO Mall on Nintendo Switch 2 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]].
