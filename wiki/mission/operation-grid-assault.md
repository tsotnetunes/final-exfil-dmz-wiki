Operation Grid Assault is a Critical-threat Story Mission in [[the-grid]]: a radio tower over the financial district passes orders to the enemy's drones, and you shut it down with a kill code [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]. It opens after the first three Story Missions and closes The Grid chapter of Orders ([[the-grid-orders]]) [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## What should you bring?

A 3-Plate Armor Vest and a Medkit, the card says [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]]. DBN Headquarters and Hanex Bank both read Extreme; Deathstalker drones attack the roof and the armoured boss Colossus walks the streets outside [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## How do you complete it?

The card lists three objectives: access the network, download its intel and upload the kill switch [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]]. On the HUD there are six steps [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]:

1. Find the drone control: hack the laptop in a server room on the upper floors of DBN Headquarters.
2. Get the first half of the code from an HVT; it was at Hanex Bank in one match and the Nenshin tech complex in another.
3. Get the second half by killing Lieutenant [[roze]] on the wrecked top floors of DBN Headquarters.
4. Compile the code on a laptop on floor 10.
5. Upload it at the roof antenna and hold the roof until the upload ends; a second Deathstalker arrives.
6. Climb the lattice tower on the roof and send the broadcast.

Killing Roze also completes a free Kill Roze side op [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]]. The mission ending does not extract you: walk out to an exfil with Colossus still around [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## What are the rewards?

The card lists 25,000 DMZ Cash, Roze's Highrise Armory Key and a Helicopter, which unlocks the helicopter insertion ([[paid-infil]]) [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]]. One early-access summary paid 5,000 for the mission, 500 for the side op and 10,000 for a first Lieutenant takedown [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).
