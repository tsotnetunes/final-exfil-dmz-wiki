Domino Protocol is a Dynamic Op ([[dynamic-operations]]) that chains three kills: two marked HVTs, each carrying intel, and then the Lieutenant that intel leads you to [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. In early access it was the best-paying Op on record [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## Where does it start?

One phone sat on a dresser in a house in Pyeonghwa Village; another was in a kitchen in NuriGO West, a Critical area [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## How do you complete it?

1. Find the first HVT and kill him; his guards stay close. Pick up the Classified Documents he drops.
2. Find the second HVT (Imjin Suburb in one run, a few hundred metres on), kill him and take his intel.
3. Go to the Lieutenant's location and defeat them ([[lieutenants]]).

Steps: [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]. The Lieutenant can be far away: the east side of the 14th Political Prison ([[prison-complex]]) in one run, and [[bale]] in [[deadtown]] in another [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]].

## What does it pay?

One full run paid 12,400 DMZ Cash and 8,000 XP at extraction, and the Lieutenant's Dog Tag paid another 25,000 on extract; unfinished runs paid only 1,500 to 1,750 XP [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported). Since 30 Oct 2026 each Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 and each step 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. A Lieutenant kill drops a Dog Tag Case, and picking it up marks you on every Operator's Tac-Map ([[lieutenants]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Risks

The Lieutenant fight is the hard part, and its location can send you across the map, so keep armor and a vehicle ready [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).
