Sky Heist is a Dynamic Op ([[dynamic-operations]]) in which you take an enemy helicopter carrying WMDs and fly it to an exfil point before its fuel runs out; staying on board extracts you with everything you carry [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]]. A phone at [[songdo-wharf]] started it in two guides [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]].

## How do you complete it?

The first half changes between matches; the second half is the same each time [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]:

1. Locate the intel: in a safe, in a hidden cache, or by rewiring and switching on the power.
2. Download the intel on the helicopter (Compound Charlie in one run). Some routes then send you to capture a SAM site first ([[capture-sam-site]]).
3. Go to the helicopter's landing spot. Either kill the pilot and take the keys, or hack the helicopter and defend it while the hack runs.
4. Fly it to the marked exfil within the fuel timer, about 3 minutes 45 seconds at take-off in one run.
5. Stay aboard to extract, or jump with one of the disposable parachutes inside and keep playing.

## Which helicopter is it?

A heavy enemy helicopter loaded with WMDs, not the light Heli you can buy as an infil ([[vehicles]], [[paid-infil]]). In the run one Hajin map guide describes, the squad captured the SAM site at [[cheongun-village]], then hacked and held the heavy helicopter at its landing spot in the South Korean Border Zone; once flown to the exfil marked on the Tac-Map it switched to autopilot, and the squad could ride it out or jump [[src-allthingshow-hajin-map-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]. The official vehicle list includes the Heavy Chopper, an armoured transport that players rarely fly, which fits the description, though no source names it in this Op [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What does it pay?

One full run paid 11,880 DMZ Cash and 6,500 XP at extraction [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported). Since 30 Oct 2026 each Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 and each step 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Because you leave by helicopter, it doubles as a private exfil [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]].

## Risks

Soldiers and drones attack the landing zone while you hack, enemy helicopters chase you in the air, and the exfil point can sit over the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]]. A Deathstalker appeared over [[the-grid]] during one flight [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).
