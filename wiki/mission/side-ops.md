Side Ops are short, optional tasks marked around Hajin that pay DMZ XP and DMZ Cash and often turn up resources or intel: activate a UAV Tower, download Lieutenant intel at a Hunt tower, capture a SAM Site, crack a safe, clear a compound, repair a truck, secure a lost supply drop or loot a radio tower [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 each one pays 2,000 DMZ Cash, up from 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How do Side Ops work?

They show as white markers on the [[tac-map]], while Dynamic Ops use green phone icons [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] (reported). Walk up to one, or start its interaction, and it joins the Side Ops list on the left of your HUD with its own counter [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]]. You can do them during any deployment, alongside a Story Mission or a Dynamic Op, and their XP counts toward your DMZ level even when the exfil fails [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The cash arrives at extraction on a Side Ops Payout line [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]].

## What do Side Ops pay?

2,000 DMZ Cash each since 30 Oct 2026, up from 500 at launch: the patch notes of 29 Oct announced a raise for Side Ops and Dynamic Ops, and the entry of 30 Oct gives the figures [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] [clm_2632001aa873]. The same entry raised Dynamic Ops to 10,000 to 20,000 and each finished mission step to 500 ([[dynamic-operations]], [[dmz-cash]]) [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. XP per Side Op was small in early access: about 150 to 225 [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported). Two Scout and Survivalist Traits extend towers: Overwatch keeps UAV Towers you activate running for good, and Air Superiority keeps UAV Towers and SAM Sites captured longer [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_c3633bb72757].

## Which Orders need Side Ops?

Opulent wants six in one deployment and Temp Job forty in all; Hunter, Execute Intel, Eye in the Sky, Crashed Crate, Mechanic and Multi-Talented each need a particular type [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]]. See [[dmz-orders]].
