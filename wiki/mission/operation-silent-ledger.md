Operation Silent Ledger is a Medium-threat Story Mission at the Heavenly Luck Casino ([[casino]]) in [[cheongun-village]]: you get into the casino vault and steal the plates the North uses to print money [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]]. It is open from the start and ends the Counterfeit chapter of Orders ([[counterfeit-orders]]) [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]]. Its name was first seen on the official trailer's mission screen [[src-fpsjp-story-missions-trailer-2026-10-09]].

## How do you complete it?

The card asks you to get into the casino, breach its vault and secure the plates, and suggests a 2-Plate Armor Vest and a Medkit [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]]. On the HUD that becomes eight steps, which come down to this [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]:

1. Approach the casino and find the lobby lifts.
2. Start the generator in the basement.
3. Go to penthouse security and use a parking-level camera (Camera 01) to open the vault fence.
4. Drill the vault in the basement and hold off the soldiers while it works.
5. Take the plate bag from the inner cage.
6. Extract the plates on the exfil helicopter.

Walkthroughs used the helicopter at Cheongun Outskirts, reached on foot or by driving a Grizzly or the D50 cargo truck [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported). Before launch Infinity Ward used this vault as its example of how scripted Story Missions are [[src-gamingtrend-dmz-interview-2026-06-06]]. Elite, heavily armoured guards defend the vault, so bring an ammo box and armor [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## What are the side tasks inside it?

Once the plates are out, a Cash Bags Secured counter appears: up to 12 bags worth 1,500 each, which count only once they sit on the bed of the D50 cargo truck in the garage [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]]; that is 18,000 in all [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]. A Repair the Vehicle side op on a disabled radar truck can run at the same time ([[repair-the-vehicle]]) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported). The same casino hosts the Eyes Everywhere Order, which wants ten enemies marked there with a Recon Drone [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-game8-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]].

## What are the rewards?

The card lists 25,000 DMZ Cash, a Gold Bar, the Counting Room Vault Key and the Polaris Dagor A1 vehicle [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]]. The Polaris insertion itself costs 5,000 and is open from the start, unlike the three dearer ones ([[paid-infil]]) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported). An early-access extraction summary also showed a 5,000 Story Mission Payout [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).
