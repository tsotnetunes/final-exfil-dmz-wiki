Hornet's Nest is one of the two chapters of [[dmz-orders]] you choose between after page 11 (the other is [[the-grid-orders]]): two pages of boss, explosive and prison fights that end with the Story Mission [[operation-iron-citadel]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. Site lists place it on pages 16 and 17, but one player saw it as pages 22 and 23 because of when it was picked [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]].

## What does each Order ask?

| Page | Order | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1/2 | Goliath | Damage Colossus ([[commander-threats]]) once with Thermite or a Drill Charge and once with a Shock Stick. |
| 1/2 | Click, Boom | Print five C4, kill 12 armoured enemies with C4, and kill five enemies with a single blast. |
| 1/2 | Navigate With Care | Buy the Polaris Dagor A1 insertion ([[paid-infil]]), wipe out a North Korean convoy, then extract with the Chemical Components it leaves. |
| 2/2 | Jail Break | Get 15 kills inside the 14th Political Prison ([[prison-complex]]), bring down a reinforcement helicopter, then get 10 kills in the Citadel. |
| 2/2 | Keep Out | Kill three Lieutenants ([[lieutenants]]), pick up one of their keys, then grab a Lieutenant's gun inside the room that key opens. |
| 2/2 | Operation Iron Citadel | Finish that Story Mission. |

Tasks: [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## Tips

- Goliath only asks you to land the hits, not to kill Colossus [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. Drill Charges were made weaker against Colossus on 25 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]], which matters if you want the kill as well ([[commander-threats]]).
- Navigate With Care needs the paid Polaris Dagor A1 insertion, which costs 5,000 DMZ Cash ([[paid-infil]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- For Keep Out, any Legendary key that carries a Lieutenant's name counts [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- C4 is a printer recipe from page 11's reward [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]].
