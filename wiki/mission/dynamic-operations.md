A Dynamic Operation (Dynamic Op) is a multi-step job generated for each match in Hajin: you pick one before you deploy or start one at a Dynamic Op phone, and finishing it pays DMZ Cash, DMZ XP and a Supply Drop [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 each Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 DMZ Cash [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Twelve named Ops have been seen so far [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]].

## How do you start a Dynamic Op?

- **At Deploy:** choose Dynamic Operations; you land with an Op already running [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]].
- **In the field:** activate a Dynamic Op phone, a ringing handset on a desk shown as a green phone icon on the [[tac-map]]. Phones move every match and beep as you get close [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-pcgamer-dynamic-op-phone-2026-10-21]].
- Only one Op runs at a time, so an Op handed to you at Deploy blocks a phone pickup; Orders that ask for a phone need a Free Roam deployment [[src-pcgamer-dynamic-op-phone-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]]. Your squad can vote to cancel an Op from the Tac-Map [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]].
- The running Op sits on the left of the HUD under the minimap, and the radiation countdown keeps going meanwhile [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]].

Both rows: [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. The notes of 29 Oct announced a small raise without figures; the entry of 30 Oct gives the figures above. The cash goes to your Bank as soon as the Op ends, without an exfil, even if your squad dies later [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-fpsjp-dynamic-ops-trailer-2026-10-07]] [clm_f17f3ff635f7]. Early-access runs paid about 3,000 to 8,000 XP per Op [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported). After the change players report about 20,000 for [[special-delivery]], 15,000 for [[dead-man-s-cache]] and 10,000 for [[broken-resupply]] [clm_4e9050dad7e9] [clm_fa5585a7795f] [clm_d1ca7e76878b].

Each finished Op also drops a Supply Drop, and each further Op in the same deployment raises its quality from Common to Uncommon to Rare [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]. Opening that care package gives 75 XP [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]]. The Survivalist Minor Trait Objective Oriented raises the crate one rarity tier, to Rare at most (reported) [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-traits-2026-10-30]], and the Scout Minor Cash Ops adds 20% to Op cash [[src-dmzmap-skill-tree-2026-10-29]] [[src-playhub-best-traits-2026-10-28]] ([[survivalist-tree]], [[scout-tree]]).
