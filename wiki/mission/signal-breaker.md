Signal Breaker is a Dynamic Op ([[dynamic-operations]]) in which you gather three bomb parts, put the bomb together at a workbench and plant it on three enemy radar trucks [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. It paid 10,000 DMZ Cash and 6,000 XP in early access [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]].

## How do you complete it?

One walkthrough started at a phone on a shelf in a greenhouse at [[imjin-farmland]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported):

1. Go to the target area; in that run, a wrecked tank in misty woods.
2. Collect the three bomb parts: a hidden cache beside the tank, a two-storey house in Imjin Farmland, and a safe upstairs in Compound Alpha.
3. Craft the bomb at the marked workbench (Imjin Farmland in that run; Namjin Riverside in another).
4. Climb each radar truck's ladder, use the panel on the roof and enter the code to plant the bomb. All three go off after the third.

## What does it pay?

Early access: +10,000 DMZ Cash and +6,000 XP [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 each Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 and each step 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Risks and tips

Compound Alpha is High threat, soldiers stand on the truck roofs and more arrive by truck while you plant, so a squad with one player covering from a roof helps [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported). Clearing Compound Alpha on the way also completes a Clear the Military Compound side op ([[clear-the-military-compound]]) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported).
