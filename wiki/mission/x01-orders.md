X01 is the chapter of [[dmz-orders]] that came with Season 1 on 6 Nov 2026. The Season 1 patch notes say the new underground facility brings its own DMZ Orders [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]], and Ghost, acting as the Watcher, hands them out [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Two fan guides list five pages of three Orders each [[src-dmzcodex-x01-orders-guide-2026-11-13]] [[src-dmzguides-orders-2026-11-13]]; DMZHub had pages 1 to 4 on 13 Nov 2026 [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-11-13]]. No official list exists yet, so everything below is reported until the game or Infinity Ward confirms it. One player says the X01 Orders only count while you are deployed into [[x01]] itself (reported) [clm_46b9cbca3827].

| Page | Order | What you do |

| 1 | Fresh Start | Get into X01, through the FOB deploy menu or the abandoned elevator shaft by the facility in the north west of Hajin. |

| 1 | First Win | Extract from X01 successfully. |
| 2 | Explorer | Find the hidden stash in Production Assembly and search five unopened containers in the Tech Lab. |
| 2 | Crafting Material | Visit the X01 [[3d-printer]] and any Keycard Encoder Station in X01. |
| 2 | Video Footage | Find the footage of Ri on the monitors of the surveillance room in the Admin Offices. |
| 3 | Streaming Service Ep. 1 | Tap Ri's video feed at the marked console in the Control Room, then kill ten enemies in that room; squad kills are said to count. |
| 3 | Streaming Service Ep. 2 | Tap the feed at the marked spot in the Medical Wing, then loot ten healing items there. |
| 3 | Streaming Service Ep. 3 | Pull Ri's video log from the marked terminal in the Data Center and kill three robot dogs anywhere in X01. |
| 4 | Racing the Hour | Stop the bioweapon release at the wall canister in the Bio Lab's canister chamber. |
| 4 | The Vault | Kill [[the-captain]] and pick up the X01 Vault Access Keycard lying by his body. |
| 4 | Complete Defusal | Go through the main gate of the [[x01-deep-vault]], neutralize the bioweapon at the computer in its core room, and kill [[blix]] inside the vault. |
| 5 | Door Opener | Destroy the Wheelson in the Logistics Tunnel and shut down the power at the generators. |
| 5 | Water Wading | With the power off, take the Super User Data Access Keycard from the flooded barracks under the Supply Depot and extract with it. |
| 5 | Source Code | Download the three parts of the AI source code in the Data Center dark room and extract in the same match. |
