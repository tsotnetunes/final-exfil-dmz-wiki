X01 is the chapter of [[dmz-orders]] that came with Season 1 on 6 Nov 2026. The Season 1 patch notes say the new underground facility brings its own DMZ Orders [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]], and Ghost, acting as the Watcher, hands them out [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. The Order names, tasks and rewards below come from one community database only, so they are reported until a second source or the game confirms them [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-11-11]] (reported). That database lists two pages so far, and one of its item entries ties the Hazmat Box recipe to a third X01 page, so the chapter is probably longer than what is written here [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-10]] (reported). One player says the X01 Orders only count while you are deployed into [[x01]] itself (reported) [clm_46b9cbca3827].

## What does each Order ask?

| Page | Order | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Fresh Start | Get into X01; the database points to the abandoned elevator shaft by the facility in the north west of Hajin. |
| 1 | Sight Seeing | Search five unopened containers in the Main Warehouse and five more in the Control Room. |
| 1 | First Win | Extract from X01 successfully. |
| 2 | Explorer | Find the hidden stash in Production Assembly and search five unopened containers in the Tech Lab. |
| 2 | Crafting Material | Visit the X01 [[3d-printer]] and any Keycard Encoder Station in X01. |

All of these come from [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-11-11]] (reported).

## What do the pages pay?

A page pays once every Order on it is done, as on the other Order pages [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-11-11]] (reported).

| Page | Rewards |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 20,000 DMZ Cash, a Medium Scavenger Backpack and two Encodable Blank Keycards |
| 2 | 20,000 DMZ Cash, a Medical Box and a Consumable Box |

Both rows come from [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-11-11]] (reported). For the backpack see [[backpacks]]; the boxes are Season 1 field upgrades on [[field-upgrades]].

Page 2 sends you to the X01 printer and an encoder station, so it pairs well with the Encodable Blank Keycards that page 1 pays out; see [[x01]] for the layout and [[x01-deep-vault]] for the sealed vault deeper inside.
