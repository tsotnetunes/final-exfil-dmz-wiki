Human Intel is one of the three chapters of [[dmz-orders]] you choose after page 1: two pages of three Orders that end with the Story Mission [[operation-critical-extraction]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. Pick it first and it fills pages 2 and 3; otherwise it lands on pages 5 and 6 or 8 and 9 [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]]. Guides suggest it first if you want early medical gear and UAVs [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## What does each Order ask?

| Page | Order | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1/2 | Hunter | Climb a Lieutenant Hunt tower and read the terminal up top, which shows where the closest Lieutenant is; no kill needed ([[download-intel-to-locate-lieutenant]]). |
| 1/2 | Triage Protocol | Visit a Clinic, then loot one Bandage and 30 Chemicals. |
| 1/2 | No One Left | Clear a Compound, then grab the Medical Extraction Intel that falls once it is cleared ([[clear-the-military-compound]]). |
| 2/2 | Blindness | Print four Flash grenades at the [[3d-printer]], then kill eight enemies while they are blinded. |
| 2/2 | Payment Due | Start an Op from a Dynamic Op phone in Hajin, then earn 11,500 DMZ Cash from [[dynamic-operations]]. |
| 2/2 | Operation Critical Extraction | Finish that Story Mission. |

Tasks: [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-game8-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]; Hunter needs no kill [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## Tips

- Check the ground before you climb a Hunt tower: you cannot shoot on the ladder [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- Go to a building marked as a Clinic. A visit to Mirae General Hospital did not count for one player [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- If a compound's progress stalls near 90 percent, one defender is still alive somewhere [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- Payment Due wants a phone: deploying straight into a Dynamic Op does not tick that task, so go in through Free Roam and walk to a green phone icon [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-pcgamer-dynamic-op-phone-2026-10-21]]. Several Ops are usually needed to reach 11,500 [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).
- Print the four flashes before you deploy and put them in your tactical slot [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).
