Operation Shopping Hacks is a Medium-threat Story Mission in and under [[nurigo-mall]]: you hack the network around Lieutenant Jang-ui's camp, break into a robotics lab under the mall and fly out with what you find [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]] [clm_c23dfbf1c4fe]. ## What should you bring?

Night vision: the parking garage and lab are dark. A pair of weak night vision goggles waits in a tent at the mall camp and another in the security annex [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]]. The card also suggests a 2-Plate Armor Vest and a Medkit [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]].

## How do you complete it?

The card's three objectives are network hacking, reprogramming a protocol and securing whatever evidence you find [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]]. On the HUD that runs to eleven steps, which come down to this [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]:

1. Hack the laptop at the camp in the mall shops and wait while your handler reads the data. You get a keypad code that changes every match, so do not copy one from a guide.
2. Enter it at the security door in the underground parking and search the security annex; start its generator.
3. Feed four intact K9 Battery Cores into the lab door terminal. One sits in an annex container; the rest come from robot dogs in the concourse and atrium.
4. Hack five computers in the robotics lab, then defend the lab during the breach countdown; restart any laptop that drops out.
5. Wait for the servers to reboot and loot the lab vault (a Bio-agent Sample).
6. Leave through the garage, climb the rope to the roof and use the Fulton on the marked crate.

Robot dogs blow up soon after they go down, and a destroyed core is useless, so shoot them at range or grab the core at once [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]]. Players recommend a squad for this one [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported). The later Order Abandoned sends you back underground for three intact K9 cores ([[dmz-orders-pages]]) [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].
