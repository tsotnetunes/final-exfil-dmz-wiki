Operation Fallen Ember is the last Story Mission at launch, at [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]]: you take three uranium fuel rods out of a flooded reactor, decontaminate them and escape by boat [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]. It opens after Iron Citadel and Grid Assault, its briefing rates it Extreme, and it is the Order that closes page 21 of Orders ([[dmz-orders-pages]]) [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## What should you bring?

Radiation protection. Guides list a 3-Plate Armor Vest, a Medkit, a Gas Mask and Radiation Blockers, which shield you for 60 seconds [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]. Players warn that gas masks rarely drop inside, so take spares from your Stash [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported). A Legendary Hazmat Suit ([[hazmat-suit]]) hangs in the Reactor 01 control room [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## How do you complete it?

The card's objectives are to breach the reactor, get the rods and extract them once decontaminated [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]]. The HUD runs to seventeen steps, which come down to this [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]:

1. Reach Reactor 01 and unlock it: the ground doors are shut, so climb to the damaged dome and use the ascender line to reach the lockdown override.
2. For each of three rods, start it at the console, then dive into the flooded pool and pull it from the hatch that opens. A wrong hatch shows an error, so one player on the console can call out the right one.
3. Carry the rods to Reactor 02 and decontaminate them one by one while you hold the room.
4. Secure the warhead and beat the reinforcements in the reactor yard.
5. Kill Colossus for the boathouse key, raise the boathouse door and take the armored patrol boat.
6. Meet the helicopter and escort it out over the water.

The Lieutenant called Chemist holds the Reactor 01 corridors, with a Kill Chemist side op; a Clear the Military Compound side op runs at Compound Delta near the road in ([[clear-the-military-compound]]) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## How does it end?

A nuclear blast lights the horizon and the mission completes on the water. There is no cutscene and no credits; the match goes on until you exfil, for example at Songdo Pier [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]].

## What are the rewards?

The card lists 25,000 DMZ Cash, the Reactor DRC Locker Key and the Cargo Plane, which unlocks that paid insertion ([[paid-infil]]) [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]]. One early-access summary paid 5,000 for the mission, 1,000 for two side ops and 10,000 for a first Lieutenant takedown [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).
