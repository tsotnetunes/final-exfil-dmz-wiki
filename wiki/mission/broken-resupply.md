Broken Resupply is a Dynamic Op ([[dynamic-operations]]): you hack and defend an enemy comms server, take a bomb, find a keypad code in a safe and blow up a weapon of mass destruction behind a locked door [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. Infinity Ward's patch notes use the name Broken Resupply [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]; players sometimes write Broken Supply [clm_d1ca7e76878b].

## Where does it start?

One run began at a phone behind the bar of the Heavenly Luck Casino ([[casino]]), which also counted for the Order that wants a Cheongun Village phone [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported); another began at a comms server in [[nurigo-mall]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## How do you complete it?

1. Hack the comms server and hold the area until the timer ends.
2. Loot the marked item, the bomb: from the trunk of a marked vehicle (open the trunk; getting in does not collect it) or from a hidden cache.
3. Find the keypad code in a safe; Compound Charlie and a back office in NuriGO West held it in two runs.
4. Enter the code at the locked door. The code changes every match and appears in your objective text, so never copy one from a guide.
5. Plant the bomb on the WMD (red barrels) and get clear.

Early access: 5,000 DMZ Cash and about 3,000 XP, with two crates of stims, an inhaler and a vest [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 each Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 and each step 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]; players report 10,000 for this one [clm_d1ca7e76878b].
