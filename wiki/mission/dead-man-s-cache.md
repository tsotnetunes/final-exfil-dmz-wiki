Dead Man's Cache is a Dynamic Op ([[dynamic-operations]]) in which you track down an enemy ammo and armor cache, blow it up with a bomb you collect on the way, and kill the HVT who guards the area [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]. It was one of the two Ops in the official trailer of 6 Oct 2026, where it opened with recovering an encrypted drive [[src-fpsjp-dynamic-ops-trailer-2026-10-07]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]]. Guides rate it Low threat [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## Where does it start?

From a phone or at Deploy. Phones that gave this Op were in [[cheongun-village]], in [[imjin-farmland]] and on the South Korean shoreline [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]]. The Cheongun Village phone also ticks the Operation Rewards Order ([[dmz-orders-pages]]) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]].

## How do you complete it?

The first step changes between matches: either an encrypted drive or hidden cache, or an enemy comms server [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. A typical run [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]]:

1. Get the intel: reach the comms server and download the cache location while you hold off attackers, or open a hidden cache (one was on Oamsik Turtle Island) and use a laptop.
2. Collect the bomb: from a bedroom safe at Mujin Pass, a dead drop, or a spot by a Hajin River bridge.
3. Find the ammo and armor cache (one sat under Songdo Pier) and destroy it.
4. Kill the HVT in the area; the Op then ends and a care package lands nearby.

## What does it pay?

In early access it paid 7,500 DMZ Cash and 4,575 XP [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]]; another run paid 9,900 [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported). Since 30 Oct 2026 every Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]], and players report 15,000 for this one [clm_fa5585a7795f]. Its care package held a Rare Medium Backpack in one run [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## Tips

The radiation countdown keeps running during the Op, so start it early [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. The route can cross several areas, so a vehicle saves time [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported).
