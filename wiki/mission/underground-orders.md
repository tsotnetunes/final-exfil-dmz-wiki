Underground is one of the three chapters of [[dmz-orders]] you choose after page 1: two pages about vehicles, towers and drones that end with the Story Mission [[operation-shopping-hacks]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. Its page numbers depend on when you pick it: lists show pages 8 and 9, early players saw 2 and 3, and one writer had it as page 5 [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]]. Guides suggest it first if you want the Night Vision Goggles recipe [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## What does each Order ask?

| Page | Order | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1/2 | Eye in the Sky | Take over a SAM Site ([[capture-sam-site]]), let it shoot down a cargo helicopter, then open the crate from the wreck. |
| 1/2 | Watch for Bumps | Take a vehicle from the enemy, fix and fuel it at a Gas Station, then drive over seven enemies ([[vehicles]]). |
| 1/2 | Triangulation | Put transmitters on top of two radio towers, then on the roof of the La Grande Vie building at [[songdo-wharf]]. |
| 2/2 | Dead Battery | Print the DDOS field upgrade, then use it to disable three drones or enemy vehicles. |
| 2/2 | Death From Above | Distract a drone using a Decoy Grenade, shoot down three drones and take a Drone Motherboard from the wreckage. |
| 2/2 | Operation Shopping Hacks | Finish that Story Mission. |

Tasks: [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-game8-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. A pre-launch build named the second Order Joyride [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]].

## Tips

- Any hostile aircraft will not do for Eye in the Sky: it has to be a cargo helicopter, and its crate can land far from the wreck, under its own care package marker [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. SAM sites sit at Deadtown, Imjin Farmland and north of NuriGO Mall, among others [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]].
- For Watch for Bumps, clear the crew of an enemy Grizzly or truck and get in; park under a Gas Station canopy until the repair bar fills [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported). Extraction is not needed for the page to count [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- The Triangulation towers are the tall lattice towers also used to hunt Lieutenants; South Island has two close together, and La Grande Vie's roof terminal is up its stairwell [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-orders-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).
- Shooting down Deathstalkers yields drone wreck loot, Drone Motherboards included [[src-allthingshow-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported).
