Ghost Shipment is a Dynamic Op ([[dynamic-operations]]) in which you kill a marked HVT for his Classified Orders, then take over a SAM site and wait for it to shoot down an enemy cargo helicopter [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]].

## Where does it start?

Starts seen: the South Korean Wilderness, and a radio on a workbench in Tong-il Village [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## How do you complete it?

1. Find the HVT and kill him.
2. Pick up the Classified Orders he carries.
3. Go to the target SAM site and capture it: use its control computer and stay inside the capture area until the bar fills ([[capture-sam-site]]).
4. Wait while the site downs the helicopter.
5. In some runs, loot the Lieutenant's cargo container from a netted crate near the site to finish.

Steps: [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]]. Leaving the capture area pauses the capture, and enemies can take the site back if you wander off [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]].

## What does it pay?

Early runs paid 7,500 DMZ Cash and 4,500 to 5,000 XP [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] (reported: both sites show the same runs). Since 30 Oct 2026 each Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 and each step 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Risks

The SAM site draws soldiers while you hold it. During one run the Lieutenant called The Doctor was active at [[mirae-general-hospital]], so check your route [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).
