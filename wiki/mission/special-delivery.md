Special Delivery is a Dynamic Op ([[dynamic-operations]]) in which you build a bomb from three parts and blow up an enemy mobile missile launcher [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzcodex-com-operations-2026-10-31]]. Players report it now pays about 20,000 DMZ Cash, the top of the official range [clm_4e9050dad7e9] [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## How do you complete it?

1. Collect three bomb parts. One route had the explosives in a dead drop at Cheongun Outskirts and the wiring in a safe at Compound Charlie; another used Songdo Wharf, a dumpster dead drop and a hidden cache in the Construction Zone [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]].
2. Craft the bomb at the marked workbench, in Cheongun Village or the Construction Zone depending on the route [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]].
3. Do the technical step: either upload intel at a laptop on top of a lattice tower near Cheongun Outskirts, or capture a UAV Tower ([[uav-towers]]), for example on South Island [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]].
4. Reach the Mobile Launch Platform: at Shoreline Station, or far north across the Hajin River near Hanrim Village and Revolution Dam [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]].
5. Plant the bomb, defend it while the countdown runs (about a minute), then get clear before it blows [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]].

The Op ends only when its completion banner appears; planting alone is not enough [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported). If you see no laptop marker, your route uses the UAV Tower instead [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## What does it pay?

Early access: 7,500 DMZ Cash and about 4,500 XP [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzmap-dynamic-ops-2026-10-31]]. Since 30 Oct 2026 Ops pay 10,000 to 20,000 and each step 500 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]; about 20,000 for this Op, players say [clm_4e9050dad7e9]. One crate held intel documents and a Medium Backpack [[src-codcentral-dmz-dynamic-ops-2026-10-21]] (reported).

## Risks

Guarded compounds on the parts route, a long drive if the launcher is in the north (take a vehicle), and soldiers who rush the launcher during the countdown [[src-dtgre-dmz-ops-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported). One guide saw it start near [[songdo-wharf]] [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] (reported).
