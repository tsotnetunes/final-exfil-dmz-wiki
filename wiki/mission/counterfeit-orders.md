Counterfeit is one of the three chapters of [[dmz-orders]] you choose after page 1: two pages that follow the North's money and end with the Story Mission [[operation-silent-ledger]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. Chosen first it takes pages 2 and 3; site lists that assume another order show it as pages 5 and 6 [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. Guides suggest it first if you want the Recon Drone recipe early [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## What does each Order ask?

| Page | Order | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1/2 | Hostile Force | Enter a Police Station, search three containers inside and kill five soldiers there. |
| 1/2 | Robbery | Open a safe ([[crack-safe]]), then extract with 5,000 of value in your backpack and loadout. |
| 1/2 | Execute Intel | Hack the UAV Tower in [[deadtown]] ([[uav-towers]]), kill five soldiers it marks and pick up the Counterfeit Cash they drop. |
| 2/2 | Suit Breach | Kill 15 enemies wearing hazmat suits, found mostly in radiation. |
| 2/2 | Eyes Everywhere | Print a Recon Drone, fly it over the Heavenly Luck Casino ([[casino]]) and mark ten enemies inside. |
| 2/2 | Operation Silent Ledger | Finish that Story Mission. |

Tasks: [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-game8-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzmap-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. The first page was already on screen in pre-launch footage [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]].

## Tips

- Police Stations are blue markers on the Tac-Map; desk drawers and filing cabinets count as containers [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).
- Safes are green boxes with a red lock panel; hold interact, no key or code needed. The 5,000 only counts after a successful exfil, and what you take from the safe counts toward it [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).
- The Deadtown UAV Tower is a camouflaged box with a mast and a laptop, not the tall lattice mast with a skull, which is a Lieutenant Hunt tower. Make sure the hack itself ticks before you leave [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).
- Eyes Everywhere is a different Order from Ears Everywhere on page 20 ([[dmz-orders-pages]]) [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].
