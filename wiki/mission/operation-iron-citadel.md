Operation Iron Citadel is a Critical-threat Story Mission at [[chang-san-air-base]], the base the briefing says Bale runs: you get into the armory, take its manifest and wreck the strike jets [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]]. It opens once you have finished Critical Extraction, Silent Ledger and Shopping Hacks, and it is the last Order of the Hornet's Nest chapter ([[hornets-nest-orders]]) [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## What should you bring?

The card suggests a 3-Plate Armor Vest, a Medkit and an Ammo Box [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]]. The base is Critical and its bunker Extreme [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]], and Colossus can meet you in the bunker garage or armory corridor [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## How do you complete it?

The card's objectives are infiltrate the base, recon the armory ordnance and disrupt the operation [[src-codmunity-dmz-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]]. The HUD splits that into fourteen steps, which come down to this [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]]:

1. Get into the base and search up to four buildings for an access card; picking it up raises the threat.
2. Upgrade the card at the reader in the server room of Admin Complex No. 2; it becomes a single-use Air Base Armory Card.
3. Enter the bunker through its garage and find the armory doors.
4. Put together the six-digit armory code from three paper slips (one turned up in the medical wing, another in the Control Room). The digits change every match.
5. Open the armory and collect the Armory Manifest.
6. Plant charges in the armory, then on three strike aircraft: one in the hangar and two outside.
7. Capture the SAM site ([[capture-sam-site]]) and extract the manifest on the exfil helicopter at the base fence.

## What are the rewards?

The card lists 25,000 DMZ Cash, the Supreme Leader's Bunker Access Card and the LTV, which unlocks that paid insertion ([[paid-infil]]) [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]]. One early-access run's summary showed a 5,000 Story Mission Payout and 1,500 for three Side Ops [[src-allthingshow-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-31]] (reported). Players also call it the Iron Citadel Story Mission [clm_aac399fada29].
