An Apex Attachment is a weapon's own special part that changes how it works (a new fire mode, a new role or built-in equipment) and sits in its own slot, so it uses none of the five normal attachment slots. Each weapon has one, except launchers and the knife: 28 at launch [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. The DMZ Gunsmith takes five attachments plus the Apex [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Term: [[apex-attachment]].

## How do you unlock an Apex Attachment?

Level the weapon to its maximum: the Apex is the last reward of that track [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Weapon levels count in both Multiplayer and DMZ [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Maximum levels run from 37 (.50 GS) to 75 (KG-7 Vulcan) (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. Since a 23 Oct fix, an Apex no longer shows as locked again after you prestige the weapon [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What does an Apex cost in DMZ?

Not settled. Two guide sites say the Apex costs no DMZ Cash once the weapon is fully levelled [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]], but a post-launch Gunsmith cart charged 52,000 DMZ Cash for the Rezi 12 Enforcer (reported) [[src-allthingshow-rezi-12-enforcer-build-2026-10-21]]. The official guide lists the gun, slots and attachments as paid and does not mention the Apex [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Check the cart before you buy.

## Do Apex Attachments work in DMZ?

Yes. A 20 Oct fix made the ISO Nightshade's Apex work as intended in DMZ [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]], and the Vault Edition blueprints come with an Apex fitted [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-free-blueprints-2026-10-21]]. The 28 Oct changes to the Hyperburst, Sentinel, Type 73 Assault and Void Apex Attachments are listed under Multiplayer; whether they reach DMZ is not stated [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Which Apex Attachments come with Season 1?

Season 1 starts 6 Nov 2026 [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]]:

- **Cronen Helix Mk. II** ([[cronen-osprey-assault-rifle]]): fires both when you pull and when you release the trigger.
- **CSG Crossfire** ([[csg-35-smg]]): akimbo CSG-35s, more volume and less accuracy.
- **JAK Barrage** (JAK 12, a later event reward): a salvo of explosives that stick before they go off.
- **Hyeon Auto** ([[hyeon-burst]]) and **Kastov 545** ([[kastov-762]]): Wager Match event rewards; full-auto fire, and 5.45x39mm rounds for smoother handling.

The same post lists an M16 Burst Attachment for the M4 as an event reward without calling it an Apex [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]].

## Why are some beta Apex Attachments missing?

Players reported Apex Attachments from the beta missing at launch [clm_53d761688e7f]; one guide names the Hyeon Auto and Kastov 545 as the two not in the launch game (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-mw4-weapons-2026-10-26]]. Both return in Season 1 as event rewards [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]].
