The Main NK Battle Tank is the ground Commander: a North Korean battle tank that patrols Hajin [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The [[boss-board]] calls it simply Tank and pays $50,000 the first time you destroy it [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]].

## Where is the tank?

It drives the roads and has no fixed spawn [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Guides met it on the streets by [[nurigo-mall]] and say it turns up most often in [[the-grid]], on the South Korean side of the map [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-dexerto-dmz-enemy-types-2026-10-31]]. The DMZHub map marks a spawn point in square G8 (reported) [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]]. Like every Commander it shows on the Tac-Map when you are close, and your Team Leader warns you [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do you destroy the tank?

- Bullets do nothing, grenades barely scratch it, and Thermite and Semtex are no help either [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]].
- Bring launchers (an RPG, the PILA or a Javelin) and fire from cover, out of the turret's line of fire [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Its armour is thinner at the back and on top (reported) [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]].
- A DDOS strips its reactive armour: the Massive Damage Order has you DDOS the tank, hit it with a rocket and then destroy it (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]].
- It takes a lot of rockets. One solo player needed about 25, so fight near an Ammo Cache or bring ammo boxes; in one test a Warhead killstreak bought from the Vendor did most of the damage (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].

## What does the tank drop?

Once it is destroyed, loot three spots: the front, the back and the top [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]]. The prize is the Legendary ingredient Nano-Structured Alloy, with other armour ingredients [[src-dexerto-dmz-enemy-types-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]; players also report Legendary loot from tanks [clm_1f94c720b316], and DMZHub adds Experimental Compounds (reported) [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]]. Extract to keep the ingredients, as with every Commander; the kill also pays a large amount of DMZ XP [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Two Legendary items, the Tank Alternator and the Tank Water Pump, are repair parts for heavy military tanks [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. A guide found them lying loose around [[chang-san-air-base]], where with a Tank Battery and Tank Plugs they fix the drivable tank in the Tank Depot (reported) [[src-xboxplay-dmz-tank-2026-10-28]]; whether this Commander's wreck also drops them is not confirmed ([[repair-items]], [[vehicles]]). Since 20 Oct 2026 the whole squad gets the XP and King Slayer calling card progress for a Commander kill [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
