**Named Commanders.** Colossus, a seven-foot armoured soldier with a minigun firing depleted uranium rounds; Deathstalker, a helicopter-sized autonomous drone with two machine-gun turrets, one of four enemy drone types (a captured SAM Site is especially useful against it); and the North Korean battle tank [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The June list also had drone swarms, Juggernauts and Elite Strike Teams; attack and Deathstalker helicopters arrive as reinforcements at high threat [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

**How they work.** Always present in Hajin, heavily armoured, able to shrug off many standard weapons, and they draw nearby enemies and rival squads into the fight [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. They show on your Tac-Map when close, and your Team Leader warns you. No intel can be bought for them, but the [[boss-board]] gives free information [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The first kill of each Commander pays DMZ Cash, and killing one and extracting yields valuable 3D Printer Ingredients; more rewards for multiple Commanders are classified [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
