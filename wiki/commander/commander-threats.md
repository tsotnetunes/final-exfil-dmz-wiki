## How do you find a Commander?

They show on your Tac-Map when you are close and your Team Leader warns you; a banner then names the Commander and the area [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-colossus-2026-10-21]]. You cannot buy intel on them: the [[boss-board]] gives their information for free [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Their fights draw in nearby enemies and rival squads [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What do Commanders pay?

- The first kill of each Commander pays DMZ Cash: $50,000 per Boss Board card at launch [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]].
- Killing one and extracting yields valuable 3D Printer ingredients, plus a large DMZ XP reward [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. DMZHub lists Experimental Compounds for all three, each with one more rare ingredient (reported) [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]].
- Commander kills count toward the King Slayer calling card; since 20 Oct 2026 the whole squad gets the XP and progress, not only the player with the final blow [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- Commanders drop no dog tags (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. More rewards for killing several Commanders are still classified [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Which Orders need Commanders?

Goliath (hit Colossus with Thermite or a Drill Charge, then a Shock Stick), Stalking the Stalker (several Deathstalker kills by SAM site, launcher and DDOS), Massive Damage (DDOS the tank, then rocket it) and Unstoppable (10 Commanders and 15 Lieutenants), all reported [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## What is new in Season 1?

The Captain and Wheelson join the Boss Board's Commanders [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]]. In the Threat Briefing events of the season's first part you hunt The Captain in [[x01]] for pieces of his helmet, with bigger rewards after a successful exfil, and take parts from the battle tank in Hajin [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]] ([[season-1]]). No place or reward has been announced for Wheelson.

By October drone swarms were listed with the regular enemies instead [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[north-korean-forces]]), and players report them hovering around buildings [clm_45a3d3d59bfc]. A Juggernaut arrives as The Captain in Season 1 [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]]. No launch source mentions Elite Strike Teams yet.
