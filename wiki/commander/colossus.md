Colossus is one of the three Commanders in Hajin at launch: a seven-foot soldier in heavy armour carrying a minigun that fires depleted uranium rounds [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. He roams instead of holding one place and shrugs off most standard weapons; the first time you kill him the [[boss-board]] pays $50,000 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-colossus-2026-10-22]].

## Where is Colossus?

He has no fixed spawn [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Guides playing at launch met him outside the wall of the 14th Political Prison and inside the Citadel ([[prison-complex]]), at the gate and in the office halls of [[cheonwang-peak-array]], in the Armory at [[chang-san-air-base]] during Operation Iron Citadel, and in the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] yard during Operation Fallen Ember [[src-allthingshow-colossus-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-colossus-2026-10-22]]. Other guides add [[cheongun-village]], [[songdo-wharf]], [[the-grid]] and [[wolves-stadium]] [[src-dexerto-dmz-enemy-types-2026-10-31]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. The DMZHub map marks eleven spawn points for him, in squares B7, C3, D8, F4, F6, F7, G7, H6, H7, I4 and I7, and ties him to Songdo Wharf, the 14th Political Prison and the Construction Zone in The Grid (reported) [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]]. He appears on your Tac-Map when you are close and your Team Leader warns you [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; a COMMANDER / COLOSSUS banner then names the area [[src-allthingshow-colossus-2026-10-21]].

## How does he fight?

Steady automatic fire down halls and through gates, an electric shock at close range that jumps through nearby metal and vehicles, and green gas that trails behind him and bursts from the canister on his back when he dies [[src-allthingshow-colossus-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-colossus-2026-10-22]]. Fights with him pull in nearby enemies and rival squads [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do you kill Colossus?

- **Skip grenades and launchers.** The game tells you his suit makes him immune to explosive attacks, and guides found grenades, launchers and breaching charges did nothing [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-colossus-2026-10-22]].
- **Thermite, Drill Charges and Shock Sticks do work.** The Goliath Order asks you to hit him with Thermite or a Drill Charge and then with a Shock Stick (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]]. On 25 Oct 2026 Infinity Ward lowered Drill Charge damage against Colossus [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]], so you need more of them than in early access [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Players report solo kills with two Shock Sticks and seven or eight Thermites [clm_d12acdac5305] (reported).
- **Use height and cover.** In the Armory, shoot down from the stair landing with a sniper rifle and stay out of the gas; in the Citadel, keep a parked truck between you and him, then fire at his upper body from the truck bed [[src-allthingshow-colossus-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-colossus-2026-10-22]]. He gets stuck walking around cars and pillars (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
- **Bring a big magazine.** High-capacity automatic weapons at close to medium range, or an LMG with armour-piercing rounds at a hallway corner; aim for the head and upper body, and keep firing to break his plates [[src-allthingshow-colossus-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-colossus-2026-10-22]].

## What does Colossus drop?

Killing a Commander and extracting gives valuable 3D Printer ingredients [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Colossus has dropped the Legendary ingredients [[military-composites]] and [[experimental-compounds]], each valued at $3,500, along with other materials [[src-allthingshow-colossus-2026-10-21]] [[src-dexerto-dmz-enemy-types-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]]. Commanders drop no dog tags (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. The kill banner reads ELIMINATED ARMORED COMMANDER, and an assist paid 1,075 XP [[src-allthingshow-colossus-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-colossus-2026-10-22]]. Commander kills count toward the King Slayer calling card [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
