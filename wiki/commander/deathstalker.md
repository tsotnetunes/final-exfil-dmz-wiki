The Deathstalker is the Commander that flies: an autonomous drone the size of a helicopter with two machine-gun turrets, one of four kinds of enemy drone in Hajin [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The quickest kill is a captured SAM site, and the first kill pays $50,000 at the [[boss-board]] [[src-allthingshow-deathstalker-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-deathstalker-2026-10-22]] [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]].

## Where is the Deathstalker?

Like every Commander it roams [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]: it patrols the sky and never stays in one building, so follow its icon on the Tac-Map and the COMMANDER / DEATHSTALKER banner that names the area as you get close [[src-allthingshow-deathstalker-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-deathstalker-2026-10-22]]. At launch it was seen over [[the-grid]], NuriGO West, Compound Delta and [[nurigo-mall]] along Expressway No. 1, the DBN Headquarters helipad and [[cheongun-village]] [[src-allthingshow-deathstalker-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-deathstalker-2026-10-22]]. It circles the DBN Headquarters roof during the fight with [[roze]] and attacks that helipad in Operation Grid Assault [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-roze-2026-10-22]]. Before launch Activision also listed Deathstalker helicopters among the reinforcements sent at high threat [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## How do you kill the Deathstalker?

| Method | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| SAM site | Ping it and the nearest SAM site, clear the guards, and hold the capture zone for a little over 30 seconds; the launcher fires one missile that brings it down. If it drifts out of range, shoot it to draw it back [[src-allthingshow-deathstalker-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-deathstalker-2026-10-22]]. |
| Sniper rifle | From the DBN Headquarters helipad, steady shots into the cockpit and hull took about 25 seconds [[src-allthingshow-deathstalker-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]]. Players report four well-placed Astaroth .57 shots [clm_855a0d332b7b] (reported). |
| Assault rifle or LMG | Bursts at the cockpit and hull took under a minute; a mounted LMG with armour-piercing rounds on a flat roof killed it over a few passes [[src-allthingshow-deathstalker-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-deathstalker-2026-10-22]]. |
| Launcher | Players report that two RPG rockets are enough [clm_2709a2129c64] (reported). |

Between its passes, take cover under roofs, in doorways or behind railings, and never reload or re-plate in the open [[src-allthingshow-deathstalker-2026-10-21]]. The 9 Oct guide already named a captured SAM site as the best tool against it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; SAM sites are on [[locations-of-interest]].

## What does it drop?

The wreck breaks into pieces that you loot one by one; guides describe a nose, body and tail, or containers marked Helicopter Piece [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-allthingshow-deathstalker-2026-10-21]] [clm_d162a2f6766a]. Inside you can find the Legendary Prototype RF Module (valued at $3,500), a Printed Circuit Board and Volatile Explosives, plus a Drone Motherboard for the Death From Above Order [[src-allthingshow-deathstalker-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-dexerto-dmz-enemy-types-2026-10-31]]. DMZHub also lists Experimental Compounds among its drops (reported) [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]]. Players say you need many Deathstalker kills to finish every FOB upgrade [clm_33a4b2ec6e55] (reported). The kill banner reads DESTROYED DEATHSTALKER COMMANDER [[src-allthingshow-deathstalker-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-deathstalker-2026-10-22]], and since 20 Oct 2026 the whole squad gets the XP and King Slayer calling card progress for a Commander kill [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
