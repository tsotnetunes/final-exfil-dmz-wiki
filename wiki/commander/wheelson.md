Wheelson is one of two Commanders added with Season 1 on 6 Nov 2026: an autonomous robot that patrols with turrets, listed with [[the-captain]] among the new Boss Board targets [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [clm_6f77b93c726e]. It is not the Multiplayer killstreak of the same name, which DMZ does not use ([[fire-support]]). The other Commanders are on [[commander-threats]].

## Where is it?
In the [[x01]] Black Site. The official fixes of 7 Nov 2026 place it there [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]], and its arrival banner names the Logistics Tunnel, the passage that leads down to X01's lower floor; one player met it there twice but could not pin a fixed spawn point, so walk the tunnel until the banner appears (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-x01-orders-page-5-2026-11-18]].

## How do you beat it?
- Its turrets can be shut off for a while with a DDoS or a Shock Stick. Since 7 Nov 2026 they stop turning while disabled, and since 10 Nov 2026 they no longer keep tracking their target after a DDoS [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] ([[field-upgrades]]).
- Since 16 Nov 2026 it no longer targets players protected by the [[viper-sense]] Trait, which hides you from AI targeting; before that fix it ignored the Trait [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].
- It takes a lot of damage. One solo player wore it down with a marksman rifle from a ledge above the tunnel stairs in about six minutes and advised bringing thermite (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-x01-orders-page-5-2026-11-18]].

## What do you get?
Disabling it gave 50 XP and destroying it 500 XP in that run, and the kill completes Door Opener, the page 5 Order on [[x01-orders]] that then sends you to cut the power at the generators (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-x01-orders-page-5-2026-11-18]]. No cash or item drop has been announced; other Commanders pay $50,000 for the first Boss Board kill, but nothing says Wheelson matches ([[commander-threats]]) [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].
