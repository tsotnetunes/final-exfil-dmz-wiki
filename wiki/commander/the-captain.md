The Captain is a new DMZ Commander, a Juggernaut who fights with an elite guard, found in the [[x01]] Black Site from Season 1 on 6 Nov 2026 [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [clm_93fc23c7a474]. The Boss Board gets new Commander intel with the season [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] ([[boss-board]]).

## Where is he?
Inside X01, which you reach through Hajin [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] ([[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]). His arrival banner names the Bio Lab, on X01's lower floor south of the Supply Depot: go through the Logistics Tunnel and down the stairs. One player met him in the gas tank room there, the room that also holds the canister chamber of the page 4 Order Racing the Hour (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-x01-orders-page-4-2026-11-19]]. Two other guides say he patrols the facility rather than holding one room, so look around the Bio Lab first (reported) [[src-lfcarry-x01-guide-2026-11-19]] [[src-gamingdeputy-x01-deep-vault-2026-11-07]]. Map of the areas: [[x01]].

## What is the Threat Briefing for him?
Since 24 Nov 2026 his Threat Briefing is live: killing him always drops a Prototype Helmet, more of them can be found around X01, and getting out with them earns rewards that include Argonaut Armor and Large Backpacks [[src-infinityward-x-captain-threat-briefing-2026-11-24]]. Bigger rewards come with more helmet pieces brought out, and one squad member getting out with a piece credits the whole squad [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Full details: [[threat-briefing-the-captain]].

## What does he drop, and how do you beat him?
He fights with a minigun in Juggernaut armour (reported) [[src-gamingdeputy-x01-deep-vault-2026-11-07]]. His body holds the orange Deep Vault 301 keycard, which three community sources agree on: it opens the gate of the [[x01-deep-vault]] without being used up, and door 301 inside, a room of red lockers worth 300 XP when opened [[src-gamerfuzion-x01-orders-page-4-2026-11-19]] [[src-lfcarry-x01-guide-2026-11-19]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-12]]. Picking the card up also completes its objective on page 4 of [[x01-orders]], and one player found that objective, and the Bio Lab one, still ticked in the next run, so you can kill him in one match and do the vault in another (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-x01-orders-page-4-2026-11-19]]. A headshot through a doorway finished him in that run; another guide suggests weapons with explosive rounds or a heavy shotgun against his armour (reported) [[src-lfcarry-x01-guide-2026-11-19]]. His Threat Briefing rewards are above. Other Commanders pay $50,000 for the first Boss Board kill and give printer ingredients on extraction, but nothing official says his rewards match ([[commander-threats]]). Since 7 Nov 2026 he no longer kills players in Last Stand who could still be revived, a fault he shared with [[colossus]] in the first days of the season [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. X01's other Commander, met in the Logistics Tunnel on the way down, is [[wheelson]].
