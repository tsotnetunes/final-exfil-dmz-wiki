The Warden is the Lieutenant who guards the 14th Political Prison ([[prison-complex]]). He fights behind a riot shield that stops bullets from the front, so the way to beat him is to flank him as a squad [[src-allthingshow-warden-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-warden-2026-10-23]].

## Where is the Warden?

In the mess hall of a prison block off the courtyard, where the compass reads High, or deeper inside the Citadel, where it reads Critical [[src-allthingshow-warden-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-warden-2026-10-23]] [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]]. A LIEUTENANT / WARDEN banner appears as you cross between the courtyard blocks [[src-allthingshow-warden-2026-10-21]]. Track him with Paid Intel: Reach Warden from the [[boss-board]], or with the terminal on a Lieutenant Hunt tower [[src-xboxplay-warden-2026-10-23]].

## How do you beat the Warden?

1. Bring the whole squad and clear the courtyard guards first, using oil drums and building corners as cover [[src-allthingshow-warden-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-warden-2026-10-23]].
2. Move together through the smoky corridors: armoured guards stay close to him and fire through the smoke [[src-allthingshow-warden-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-warden-2026-10-23]].
3. In the mess hall, do not waste ammo on the shield. One player holds his attention from the front while the others flank and shoot his exposed side at close range [[src-allthingshow-warden-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-warden-2026-10-23]].
4. Guides also report that two Thermites or a Drill Charge make him drop the shield, and that an LMG with armour-piercing rounds breaks through (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
