## Where is the Scavenger?

Guides met him in a car park between apartment blocks on South Island in [[hajin-river-heights]], at [[cheongun-village]] and at [[deadtown]] [[src-allthingshow-scavenger-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]]. Other guides say he spawns near players who were recently killed [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]], or that he drives an LTV around the map, so a fast-moving marker is usually him (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. At South Island he stood among parked vans with an LTV beside him [[src-allthingshow-scavenger-2026-10-21]]. Lieutenant Hunt towers and [[boss-board]] intel (the side op reads Paid Intel: Kill the Scavenger) both find him [[src-allthingshow-scavenger-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]].

## How do you beat the Scavenger?

Take cover as soon as his banner appears, then shoot through the gaps between the vehicles with a scoped rifle and reload only in cover, because he fires back from behind them [[src-allthingshow-scavenger-2026-10-21]]. If he is driving, disable the vehicle or wait for him to stop (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. One guide calls him the weakest Lieutenant (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Since 29 Oct 2026 he no longer detects players the moment they deploy [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. The side op paid 500 XP [[src-allthingshow-scavenger-2026-10-21]].
