## How do you find a Lieutenant?

- **Buy intel** at the [[boss-board]] for $25,000. It lasts one deployment and shows as a side op that reads Paid Intel: Reach, then Kill, with the Lieutenant's name [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]]. Some intel stays locked until you finish set challenges or a DMZ Orders chapter [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
- **Climb a Lieutenant Hunt tower** and use the terminal at the top: it marks the nearest active Lieutenant for free and completes a Side Op [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-hunter-order-2026-10-21]] ([[side-ops]]). The LT Hunter trait makes a tower reveal one more Lieutenant [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
- **Go to their territory.** Lieutenants are active whether or not anyone hunts them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Players report each one spawns once per match (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].

## What do Lieutenants drop and pay?

- Every Lieutenant drops a Legendary dog tag, the Lieutenant Dog Tag Case. Picking it up shows you on every Operator's Tac-Map until you extract, so leave fast [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-scavenger-2026-10-21]] [clm_8954c5cec1aa].
- Some drop a key to their own loot room; rooms hold lockers, a Lieutenant crate and Legendary weapon cases (reported) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. See [[keys]].
- Each Lieutenant tag you extract adds $25,000 to the After Action Report, and the first kill of each Lieutenant pays its $10,000 Boss Board reward [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]]. The kill side op paid 500 XP and the kill 632 XP in guides' tests [[src-allthingshow-warden-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-warden-2026-10-23]].
- The kill counts only once you extract with the tag, and it also counts if you took the tag from another player [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] ([[dog-tags]]).

## Which Orders and missions involve Lieutenants?

- **DMZ Orders:** Hunter (use a Lieutenant Hunt tower), Information Power (buy intel and kill a Lieutenant), Keep Out (kill three, loot a Lieutenant key and take a Lieutenant's gun from their room), How Bulletproof? (five Lieutenants among other kills) and Unstoppable (15 Lieutenants, 10 Commanders and five tags extracted), all reported [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]. The 30 Oct patch notes name Information Power [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]], and a guide confirms Hunter [[src-allthingshow-hunter-order-2026-10-21]].
- **Story Missions:** the Doctor in Operation Critical Extraction, Roze in Operation Grid Assault, Jang-ui's camp in [[operation-shopping-hacks]], the Chemist's reactor in Operation Fallen Ember [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamerfuzion-boss-board-explained-2026-10-12]] [[src-allthingshow-chemist-2026-10-21]].
- **Camos:** players report the DMZ Mastery Camos ask for two Lieutenant tags extracted in one match, 50 times [clm_f1b717014ef6] (reported).
