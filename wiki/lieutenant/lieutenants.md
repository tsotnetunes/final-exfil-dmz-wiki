## How do you find a Lieutenant?

- **Buy intel** at the [[boss-board]] for $25,000. It lasts one deployment and shows as a side op that reads Paid Intel: Reach, then Kill, with the Lieutenant's name [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]]. Some intel stays locked until you finish set challenges or a DMZ Orders chapter [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]]. Reported locks: Gorani and Bale until the Underground, Counterfeit and Human Intel chapters are done, Roze until Underground [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]], Jin-Tae until Counterfeit [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] ([[dmz-orders]]).
- **Climb a Lieutenant Hunt tower** and use the terminal at the top: it marks the nearest active Lieutenant for free and completes a Side Op [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-allthingshow-hunter-order-2026-10-21]] ([[side-ops]]). The LT Hunter trait makes a tower reveal one more Lieutenant [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
- **Go to their territory.** Lieutenants are active whether or not anyone hunts them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Players report each one spawns once per match (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].

## What do Lieutenants drop and pay?

- Every Lieutenant drops a Legendary dog tag, the Lieutenant Dog Tag Case. Picking it up shows you on every Operator's Tac-Map until you extract, so leave fast [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_8954c5cec1aa].
- Some drop a key to their own loot room; rooms hold lockers, a Lieutenant crate and Legendary weapon cases (reported) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Jin-Tae, Jang-Ui, the Chemist and the Doctor are known to drop theirs [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]] [[src-dexerto-jang-ui-power-room-2026-10-24]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-keys-2026-10-28]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-keys-2026-10-22]]; for the others no drop is confirmed yet. See [[keys]].
- Each Lieutenant tag you extract adds $25,000 to the After Action Report, and the first kill of each Lieutenant pays its $10,000 Boss Board reward [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]] ([[dog-tags]]).
- The kill counts only once you extract with the tag, even a tag you took from another player [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## Which Orders and missions involve Lieutenants?

- **DMZ Orders:** Hunter (use a Hunt tower), Information Power (buy intel, kill), Keep Out (three kills, a Lieutenant key and a gun from their room), How Bulletproof? and Unstoppable (15 Lieutenants among other targets), all reported [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]]; the patch notes name Information Power [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]] and a guide confirms Hunter [[src-allthingshow-hunter-order-2026-10-21]].
- **Story Missions:** the Doctor in Operation Critical Extraction, Roze in Operation Grid Assault, Jang-ui's camp in [[operation-shopping-hacks]], the Chemist's reactor in Operation Fallen Ember [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamerfuzion-boss-board-explained-2026-10-12]] [[src-allthingshow-chemist-2026-10-21]].
- **Camos:** players report the DMZ Mastery Camos ask for two Lieutenant tags extracted in one match, 50 times [clm_f1b717014ef6] (reported).
