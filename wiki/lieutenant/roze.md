Roze is the sniper Lieutenant who holds the roof of DBN Headquarters in [[the-grid]]. She can down you with one shot, so climb to her under cover and close the distance instead of trading shots [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-roze-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].

## Where is Roze?

On the top rooftop terraces of DBN Headquarters, where the compass reads Extreme (the 5 Oct guide had rated the building Critical) [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-roze-2026-10-22]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Enter on the ground floor, find the emergency stairwell and clear each floor on the way up; on the roof the side op changes from Reach the Lieutenant's Location to Kill Roze [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-roze-2026-10-22]]. One guide also met her in a top-floor machine room by the roof door, reached by elevator (reported) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]]. Her intel showed a padlock on the [[boss-board]] at launch, so it has to be unlocked before you can buy it [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]].

## How do you beat Roze?

- Her Astaroth .57 strips most of your armour in one hit and knocks you back, so stay away from the roof edge (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
- Take cover behind the first HVAC unit, then peek and shoot from behind the units and stair rails; flank along the terrace walkways [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-roze-2026-10-22]].
- Armoured guards hold every stair floor and the terraces, and the [[deathstalker]] patrols the same roof and strafes it: take cover on its runs [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-roze-2026-10-22]].
- If someone goes down, regroup in the inner stairwell under the roof rather than reviving in the open [[src-allthingshow-roze-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-roze-2026-10-22]].
