Iskra is the Lieutenant at [[cheonwang-peak-array]]. She fights in the stairwell of the compound's office building, shooting from an upper doorway and then running down, and she drops the Widowmaker shotgun [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-hunter-order-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].

## Where is Iskra?

In the office building of the Cheonwang Peak Array compound, a Medium threat area with radio towers on a hill [[src-allthingshow-hunter-order-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. When you reach her floor the banner reads LIEUTENANT ISKRA, CHEONWANG PEAK ARRAY and the side op becomes Kill Iskra [[src-allthingshow-hunter-order-2026-10-21]]. The Lieutenant Hunt tower on the hill beside [[wolves-stadium]] can point you to her, as in the Hunter Order example; she is a short drive to the north-west from there [[src-allthingshow-hunter-order-2026-10-21]]. Intel is also sold at the [[boss-board]] [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]].

## How do you beat Iskra?

Guards patrol the open-plan rooms and stairwells [[src-allthingshow-hunter-order-2026-10-21]]. Sensor traps on the stairs call in more soldiers without hurting you: defuse them, or shoot them with a suppressed weapon (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. She fires from the upper doorway and then runs down the stairs, so finish her on a landing [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Flanking from the hillside also works [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]]. One guide counts her among the easier Lieutenants (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
