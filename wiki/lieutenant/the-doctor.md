The Doctor is the Lieutenant who holds [[mirae-general-hospital]]: a bulky fighter in a blood-stained white protective suit and gas mask who rushes squads at point-blank range [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-the-doctor-2026-10-23]]. He drops the key to his Command Office, where a Lieutenant crate has held the [[tempered-armor-vest]] recipe [[src-dexerto-doctors-command-office-key-2026-10-22]] [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]].

## Where is the Doctor?

His spot changes from match to match: the hospital wards, the underground parking garage, or the grounds and street outside [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-the-doctor-2026-10-23]]. In Operation Critical Extraction he is in the wards; in free roam he is often in the garage [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-the-doctor-2026-10-23]]. The hospital reads Low on the compass, but he is a hard hitter [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-xboxplay-the-doctor-2026-10-23]]. Follow the side-op marker: it reads Reach the Lieutenant's Location, then Kill the Doctor, and a banner names him when you arrive [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]].

## How do you beat the Doctor?

- Clear the guards first: they hold the ward corridors and the nurses' station, or the street and the garage entrance [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-the-doctor-2026-10-23]].
- Spread out so he cannot reach everyone, back off from his rushes, plate up behind cover and aim for the head and chest with an assault rifle [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-the-doctor-2026-10-23]].
- In the garage, snipe the outside guards from range, then fight him among the pillars [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-the-doctor-2026-10-23]].
- Bring plates, bandages and a Medkit [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-the-doctor-2026-10-23]]. One guide rates him one of the easiest Lieutenants (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].

## What is in the Command Office?

The key opens a converted conference room in the upper-floor offices, on the 7th floor, behind wooden double doors; take the stairwell to the office floor [[src-dexerto-doctors-command-office-key-2026-10-22]] [[src-allthingshow-the-doctor-2026-10-21]].
