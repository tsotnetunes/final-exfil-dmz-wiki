The Chemist is the Lieutenant of the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]]. He waits inside the radiation in a warehouse beside the reactor tanks, guarded by soldiers in hazmat suits, so come with radiation protection [[src-allthingshow-chemist-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]].

## Where is the Chemist?

On the east side of the reactor, at the edge of the red core: a warehouse with blue and white panel walls and a single door, where he stands in the rear bay among forklifts and drums [[src-allthingshow-chemist-2026-10-21]]. The reactor's compass threat is High [[src-allthingshow-chemist-2026-10-21]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. With intel from the [[boss-board]] the side op reads Paid Intel: Reach Chemist, then Paid Intel: Kill Chemist [[src-allthingshow-chemist-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]].

## What gear do you need?

The outer yellow zone costs a little health, and the red core breaks gas masks quickly [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]]. Bring a gas mask and Radiation Blockers [[src-allthingshow-chemist-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]]; one guide gives each Blocker 60 seconds of protection, so take one going in and one coming out (reported) [[src-allthingshow-chemist-2026-10-21]]. Another says a [[hazmat-suit]] found in the reactor building lets you stay in the red zone, where Radiation Blockers only slow the damage (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. See [[radiation-storm]].

## How do you beat the Chemist?

Drive to the fence and cross on foot, clear the guards at the door (dark hazmat suits and gas masks), step back through the door to re-plate, then push into the rear bay: the Chemist is the one in the yellow suit and full mask [[src-allthingshow-chemist-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]]. He is not tough himself; the radiation and his armoured hazmat guards are the danger [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-allthingshow-chemist-2026-10-21]]. Loot quickly and leave the red zone.
