## How do you beat Bale?

He fires an LMG down long hallways and throws smoke, and guards fill the rooms and doorways around him [[src-allthingshow-bale-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Clear room by room, plate up in side offices and stay out of the long corridor he covers. In a squad, one player keeps him busy while another shoots from a different doorway; a scoped rifle from outside the hall wears him down, and an assault rifle finishes him up close [[src-allthingshow-bale-2026-10-21]]. Thermite thrown as he comes down the stairs also works (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
