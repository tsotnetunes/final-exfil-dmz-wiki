Jang-ui is the Lieutenant in the Nenshin Tech Complex in [[the-grid]], an Extreme area: he holds the server hall on floor 14 behind explosive robot dogs, sentry guns and laser defences [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-roze-highrise-armory-key-2026-10-24]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. His key opens a power room in [[nurigo-mall]] [[src-dexerto-jang-ui-power-room-2026-10-24]].

## Where is Jang-ui?

In the Nenshin Tech Complex (guides spell it Netshin), a twin high-rise in The Grid rated Extreme [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Take the interior stairwell to the upper floors and fight into the server hall marked 14 [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-gamingpromax-roze-highrise-armory-key-2026-10-24]]. Two launch guides and DMZHub's boss page also put him in the tech complex [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]]. Other sources point to [[nurigo-mall]] instead: before launch the official Story Missions trailer put his camp in the mall's shopping district, in the [[operation-shopping-hacks]] briefing [[src-gamerfuzion-boss-board-explained-2026-10-12]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]], and a community map and a key wiki list him at the mall, where his key room is [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]] [[src-dexerto-jang-ui-power-room-2026-10-24]]. Guides that fought him at launch met him in the tech complex, so start there and try the mall if your intel marker is elsewhere.

## How do you beat Jang-ui?

His room is fortified with explosive robot dogs (some carry EMP or freezing charges), trip mines, sentry guns and laser turrets that strip your plates [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]]. Rather than pushing into it, make noise so he comes out, and fight him in the open; Thermite destroys the sentry guns faster than bullets (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. One guide advises taking him on only once you have good gear and some levels (reported) [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]]. The kill side op paid 500 XP [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]].
