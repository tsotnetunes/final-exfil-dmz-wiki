Jin-Tae is the Lieutenant of the Heavenly Luck Casino ([[casino]]) in [[cheongun-village]]. He holds the upper floors with bodyguards and does his damage up close, so fight him from range [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]].

## Where is Jin-Tae?

In the Heavenly Luck Casino, which the compass rates High [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; guides place him on its upper floors [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]]. His intel was locked on the [[boss-board]] at launch [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]]. One guide says it opens with the Counterfeit chapter of the DMZ Orders (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]], the chapter that ends with [[operation-silent-ledger]] in the same casino (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]].

## How do you beat Jin-Tae?

The fight opens with heavy automatic fire across the casino floor: fall back into the back offices and fight from there [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]]. Clear his bodyguards first and keep your distance (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. If the elevators have no power, the generator in the underground parking brings them back, but it makes noise (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. The counting room door stays locked [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]]. When he dies, the side op shows Paid Intel: Kill Jin-Tae if you bought intel [[src-allthingshow-dmz-bosses-2026-10-21]].
