Threat Briefing: Battle Tank is a limited-time DMZ event in Season 1, listed in the patch notes of 5 Nov 2026 as running from the start of the season on 6 Nov [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. No end date is given.

## What do you do?
Destroy the battle tank that patrols Hajin, take its armor fragments from the wreck and extract with them [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. The Season 1 post allows one tank part per deployment, and rewards grow with the parts you bring back over several deployments [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. When one squad member gets out with a part, the whole squad gets credit [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## What are the rewards?
The official notes speak only of rewards, and the post of better ones for more parts [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. One community database names one of them: an Advanced SERE Kit for getting out with ten reactive armor fragments in total, and it says every tank always holds at least one fragment (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-10]]. What the kit holds is on [[kits]]. Running events show at the FOB's Events station [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
