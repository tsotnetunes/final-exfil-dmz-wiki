Thanksgiving Duty Roster is a Season 1 leaderboard event for all of Modern Warfare 4, marked new in the patch notes of 23 Nov 2026 [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. No end date has been given.

## How do you score points?

Get kills in any mode. A kill is worth 10 points in Multiplayer, 20 in Warzone and just 2 in DMZ, so DMZ is the slowest way to climb: one Warzone kill counts as much as ten DMZ kills [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. The notes do not say whether AI kills in DMZ count or only Operator kills.

## How long does a leaderboard last?

Each board runs for 3 hours and a new one starts every 4 hours, the same rhythm as the DMZ [[leaderboard-war-chest]] [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. The rank needed for each reward is not published.

## What are the rewards?

The notes name the "Bird Call" blueprint for the [[astaroth-57]] among the rewards. Each reward can be won only once, even after a board resets [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## Is it worth playing in DMZ?

For points, not really: at 2 points a kill, DMZ players who want the blueprint will climb faster in Multiplayer or Warzone. In DMZ, the War Chest board, which scores containers and safes, is the event built for the mode. Every dated event: [[events]].
