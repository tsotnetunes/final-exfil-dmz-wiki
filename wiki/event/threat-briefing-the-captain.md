Threat Briefing: The Captain is the second Threat Briefing event of DMZ's Season 1, after [[threat-briefing-battle-tank]]. Infinity Ward announced it as live in [[x01]] on the afternoon of 24 Nov 2026, New York time [[src-infinityward-x-captain-threat-briefing-2026-11-24]]. The Season 1 post of 28 Oct had already named it as the season's next briefing [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. No end date has been given.

## What do you do?
Kill [[the-captain]], X01's Juggernaut Commander, and take the Prototype Helmet he always drops; more Prototype Helmets can be found elsewhere in X01 [[src-infinityward-x-captain-threat-briefing-2026-11-24]]. Then get out with what you carry. The Season 1 post describes the briefing as bringing out pieces of his helmet, with bigger rewards the more you extract, and says one squad member getting out with a piece credits the whole squad [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. How to reach him: through Hajin into X01, then the Bio Lab on the lower floor ([[the-captain]]).

## What are the rewards?
Infinity Ward names Argonaut Armor, a heavy armour Fire Support item ([[fire-support]]), and Large Backpacks ([[large-backpack]]), and says there are more [[src-infinityward-x-captain-threat-briefing-2026-11-24]]. How many helmets each reward needs has not been published.

## Tips
His body also holds the Deep Vault 301 keycard for the [[x01-deep-vault]], so one kill serves both the event and the vault (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-x01-orders-page-4-2026-11-19]]. Guides suggest explosive rounds or a heavy shotgun against his armour (reported) [[src-lfcarry-x01-guide-2026-11-19]]. All events: [[are-there-events-in-dmz]].
