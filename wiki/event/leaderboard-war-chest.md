War Chest is a Season 1 live event for DMZ that turns looting into a race on a leaderboard. It started on 19 Nov 2026, listed as new that day in the patch notes [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] and announced as live by Infinity Ward and Call of Duty that afternoon, New York time [[src-infinityward-x-war-chest-live-2026-11-19]]. No end date has been given.

## How do you score points?
Open containers and safes while deployed in DMZ. An ordinary container is worth 5 points and a safe is worth 50, so one safe counts as much as ten containers [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. The Call of Duty post frames it as looting Hajin with your squad [[src-infinityward-x-war-chest-live-2026-11-19]]; neither source says whether containers in [[x01]] count, or whether points are counted for you alone or for the squad. Where safes are: [[loot-placement]].

## How long does a leaderboard last?
Per the patch notes, each leaderboard runs for 3 hours and a fresh one starts every 4 hours, so there is a new chance to climb several times a day [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. Infinity Ward's post says only that boards reset every 3 hours [[src-infinityward-x-war-chest-live-2026-11-19]]; the notes are the more detailed source. The rank you need for each reward is not published.

## What are the rewards?
The notes name Fulton Exfils, Tempered Armor Vests and UAVs among others, and the Call of Duty post adds Survival Kits [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] [[src-infinityward-x-war-chest-live-2026-11-19]]. Each reward can be earned only once, even after a board resets [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. What the rewards are: [[tempered-armor-vest]], [[fulton-drop-marker]] for the Fulton extraction, [[survival-kits]].
