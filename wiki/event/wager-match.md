The Wager Match Event is a Season 1 event across Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone, where Trophies are the currency and more of them earn better rewards [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. It went live on the evening of 11 Nov 2026, New York time, after the 5 Nov notes had listed it as coming soon and the 28 Oct post had placed it at launch [[src-infinityward-x-wager-match-live-2026-11-12]] [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. No end date has been given.

## How do you earn Trophies in DMZ?
Since the start, Trophies drop in every mode, DMZ included [[src-infinityward-x-wager-match-live-2026-11-12]]. On its first day the DMZ drops were bugged: on 12 Nov 2026 Infinity Ward confirmed the problem, and that day's update fixed Trophies not dropping at the intended rate in DMZ [[src-infinityward-x-dmz-trophy-bug-2026-11-12]] [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. The same day Trophy drop rates were raised in Multiplayer respawn modes; that post did not mention DMZ [[src-infinityward-x-trophy-drop-rates-2026-11-12]]. How many Trophies a DMZ run gives, and from what, is not published; this is one of Ask's open questions.

## Is there a free start?
Yes. Every player was given 30 Trophies at the start; if you were already in the game, restart it to collect them [[src-infinityward-x-wager-match-live-2026-11-12]].

## Where do you bet Trophies?
Only in designated Multiplayer wager modes, chiefly Gauntlet, a 1v1 Gunfight playlist [[src-infinityward-x-wager-match-live-2026-11-12]] [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. Nothing in DMZ asks you to stake Trophies, so a DMZ player only collects them. The 12 Nov update also fixed Multiplayer cases where earned Trophies dropped in Private Matches or the total reset between rounds [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## What are the rewards for DMZ?
The event rewards include Apex Attachments, Blueprints and camos [[src-infinityward-x-wager-match-live-2026-11-12]]; the Season 1 post names the Hyeon Auto and Kastov 545 Apex Attachments for DMZ [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] ([[apex-attachments]]). Trophy prices for each reward are not published.

## How does it fit with other events?
It runs beside the DMZ events on [[are-there-events-in-dmz]]. The other announced Multiplayer tie-in is the Joint Fire Event, later in the season, with a DMZ Survival Kit as a reward [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Dates: [[events]].
