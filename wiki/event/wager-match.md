The Wager Match Event is a Season 1 event across Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone, where Trophies act as the currency and more of them earn better rewards [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. The patch notes of 5 Nov 2026 mark it as coming soon, with no date, although the 28 Oct post placed it at launch [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## How do you take part?
Mostly through Gauntlet, a 1v1 Gunfight playlist in Multiplayer, plus other challenges in DMZ and Warzone [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. Which DMZ challenges count is not listed.

## What are the rewards for DMZ?
Several Weapon Attachments [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]; the post names the Hyeon Auto and Kastov 545 Apex Attachments for DMZ [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] ([[apex-attachments]]).

## How does it fit with other events?
It runs beside the DMZ events on [[are-there-events-in-dmz]], which you can play without entering the Multiplayer playlist. The other announced Multiplayer tie-in is the Joint Fire Event, later in the season, with a DMZ Survival Kit as a reward [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Dates: [[events]].
