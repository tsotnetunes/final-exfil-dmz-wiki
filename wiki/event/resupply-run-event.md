The Resupply Run is a Season 1 event for DMZ only. Activision's Season 1 post described it ahead of time: you gather a set list of items from points of interest around Hajin and hand them in for useful gear, Self Revives and 3-Plate Carriers among it [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. It began on the evening of 12 Nov 2026, New York time, according to several community accounts (reported) [[src-x-community-resupply-run-2026-11-13]], and the official notes for the 13 Nov 2026 update list it among the live events and fix several of its screens and steps so it flows better [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. No end date has been given.

## How does the Resupply Run work?
1. Check the event at the FOB; running events show at the Events station [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
2. Deploy, find the items each reward asks for, and extract with them; items count only once they are extracted (reported) [[src-x-community-resupply-run-2026-11-13]].
3. Back at the FOB, trade the items for the reward (reported) [[src-x-community-resupply-run-2026-11-13]].

## What are the rewards?
Officially, gear such as Self Revives and 3-Plate Carriers [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. Community posts count six rewards, with a SERE Kit for unlocking all six, and name Tempered armor vests and Large Secure Backpacks among the trades (reported) [[src-x-community-resupply-run-2026-11-13]]. One guide gives the Large Secure Backpack trade as 200 Chemicals, 10 Nitro Compounds and 20 Volatile Explosives (reported) [[src-x-community-resupply-run-2026-11-13]]. The full list of items for each reward is not published by Activision yet.

## Is it worth doing?
For a squad that already loots well, yes: the trades turn common loot into rare gear that otherwise takes a high DMZ level, a lucky print or a boss kill. One player reported finishing every reward within an hour (reported) [[src-x-community-resupply-run-2026-11-13]].
