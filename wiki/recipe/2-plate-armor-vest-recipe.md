The 2-Plate Armor Vest costs 50 Synthetics and 5 [[reactive-metals]] at the [[3d-printer]], and its recipe is among the first six you have [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The official launch post lists it as the level 2 reward [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. The printed vest is Rare [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]].

## Why print it?

The plate carrier you wear decides how many armor plates you deploy with: plates are filled at infil, and in the field you only get more through Traits or the Armor Box field upgrade [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The cheaper 1-Plate Armor Vest needs 25 Synthetics and 25 Metals [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_8b9dad98699a].

## Where do you get Reactive Metals?

Search industrial areas and toolboxes [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. DMZ level 11 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]], and after Uncommon Ingredient Printing you can print them from 20 Chemicals and 20 Metals [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## Which Order asks for it?

An early page of [[dmz-orders]] (page 4) asks you to print a Medium Backpack and a 2-Plate Armor Vest [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]]. Print both together; see [[medium-backpack-recipe]].

## Other ways to get one, and what comes next

The Basic Armor Kit, a reward at DMZ levels 6 and 21, contains 2-Plate Carriers and an Armor Box [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. The next step up are the 2-Plate Ballistic Gel Vest and 2-Plate Helmet Armor Vest, whose recipes you find in Hajin, and the 3-Plate Armor Vest from DMZ Orders [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]; see [[recipes]] and [[3-plate-armor-vest-recipe]].
