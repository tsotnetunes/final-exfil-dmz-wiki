The FOB's permanent upgrades are cards in the Upgrades category of the [[3d-printer]]: Bank, Stash item and weapon space, MIA recovery discounts, Gunsmith discounts, Vendor offers, Dog Tag Case space and the two Ingredient Printing tiers [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. One database counts 9 upgrades with 31 levels; each level costs DMZ Cash, specific items or both, never basic Resources, and needs the level before it [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]].

## What should you build first?

Stash Item Upgrade I: early storage runs out fast [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]] [[src-gamerfuzion-3d-printer-upgrades-2026-10-23]]. Track the next upgrade before each run so its items are marked while you loot [[src-gamerfuzion-3d-printer-upgrades-2026-10-23]].

## When can you build them?

Most first levels are available as soon as the printer opens; the Gunsmith, Vendor and Dog Tag Case upgrades need their station first [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]], which opens at DMZ level 10, 5 and 20 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. DMZ levels add space and discounts on top: 18 more Stash item slots at levels 22, 45 and 70, 6 more weapon slots at 35 and 60, a Gunsmith discount at 25 and Vendor upgrades at 18 and 32 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. See [[dmz-progression]].

## Which valuables should you keep?

Every Bank level from III on, and the higher MIA, Stash and Dog Tag Case levels, take valuables such as Infantry Invasion Plans, Regional Combat Notes, Degraded Execution Footage, Nuclear Program Files and Gold bars [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-3d-printer-upgrades-2026-10-23]]. Players warn in particular not to sell Gold Skulls and Gold Bars, which the end-game levels need [clm_b90e9b9a3636] [clm_4903d57a964d]. Check this table before selling.
