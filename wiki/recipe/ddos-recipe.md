The DDOS is a Rare field upgrade that costs 30 Electronics and 3 [[detonators]] at the [[3d-printer]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. One widely copied list gives 30 Detonators instead [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]]; check the card in game. Field Upgrades can be printed from DMZ level 12 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].

## How do you unlock the recipe?

Finish the Underground chapter of [[dmz-orders]]: the recipe comes with the reward for the Dead Battery, Death From Above and Operation Shopping Hacks Orders [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. Another guide lists the DDOS among the recipe rewards of the early Orders chapters [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]].

## What is the Dead Battery Order?

Print a DDOS, then use it to disable three enemy vehicles or drones [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]] [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]]. How the recipe can be printed for this Order before its chapter reward is paid is not explained by any source yet.

## What does a DDOS do?

It knocks out electronics and enemy sensors nearby for a short time [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. The Dead Battery Order shows its targets: enemy vehicles and drones ([[vehicles]]).

## Where do you get Detonators?

Military and industrial areas [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] (reported); DMZ level 29 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. After Uncommon Ingredient Printing you can print them from 20 Electronics and 20 Chemicals [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]]. Electronics is a basic Resource from looting. See [[detonators]].
