The Armor Box recipe unlocks at DMZ level 14, two levels after the printer starts making Field Upgrades [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. Each box costs 30 Metals and 3 [[reactive-metals]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]], and it is a Rare field upgrade [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## Why print one?

Your armor plates are filled when you infil. After that, more plates come only from Traits or the Armor Box field upgrade [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The map's Ammo and Armor Caches also refill you but go on cooldown after use [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. So the box is your main way to restock plates away from a cache, and it hands plates to your squad as well as to you [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported).

## Other ways to get one

The Basic Armor Kit (DMZ levels 6 and 21) and the Restock Kit (levels 8 and 24) each contain an Armor Box [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. See [[survival-kits]].

## Which Order asks for it?

A page of [[dmz-orders]] (page 10) asks you to print or extract three Reactive Metals and then print an Armor Box [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]].

## Where do you get Reactive Metals?

Industrial areas and toolboxes [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]; DMZ level 11 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]; after Uncommon Ingredient Printing you can print them from 20 Chemicals and 20 Metals [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]]. Metals is a basic Resource from looting. See [[reactive-metals]] and [[recipes]].
