The Large Backpack costs 2 [[nylon-laminate]], 5 [[structural-alloy]] and 10 [[rigid-synthetics]] at the [[3d-printer]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]. The recipe is a reward from [[dmz-orders]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]], and the printed bag is Legendary [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]].

## What does DMZ level 34 give?

One Large Backpack as an item, listed there as Epic; it does not unlock the recipe [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]. Which rarity is right for the printed bag is not settled.

## Which Order asks for it?

Hard Carry asks you to print a Large Backpack [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]]; its page number is not confirmed.

## Why carry one?

Bigger backpacks add item slots and may add weapon slots or Secure Slots; anything outside Secure Slots is lost on a failed exfil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. One database gives the Large Backpack a weapon slot and two Secure Slots [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported). For a Secure Slot early, print a [[medium-backpack-recipe]].

## Where do you get the ingredients?

- **Nylon Laminate** is Epic: print it from 100 Synthetics and 3 Nylon Cloth after Epic Ingredient Printing, or search the Citadel's main building, Deadtown's underground and the Mujin Nightclub [[src-destructoid-nylon-farming-2026-10-23]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].
- **Structural Alloy** is Rare and cannot be printed; level 43 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]], and one guide finds it in the Operation Iron Citadel bunker [[src-patchcrazy-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-28]] (reported).
- **Rigid Synthetics**: homes, or print them; see [[rigid-synthetics]].

Other large bags (Large Scavenger, Large Secure) are found recipes; see [[recipes]].
