The Gas Mask recipe unlocks at DMZ level 26 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]. Each mask costs 30 Chemicals and 3 [[rigid-synthetics]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]], and the printed mask is Rare [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## What changed on 23 Oct 2026?

Gas masks, Durable Gas Masks included, had wrong use counts and broke as soon as you walked into radiation. The fix of 23 Oct 2026 stopped that [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What about the Durable Gas Mask?

It is a separate, Epic recipe that you find in Hajin; it costs 30 Chemicals, 1 High-Tenacity Fibers and 5 Biomaterials [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. DMZ level 68 gives you one Durable Gas Mask, not the recipe [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. See [[high-tenacity-fibers]] and [[biomaterials]].

## When do you need a mask?

Radiation spreads across Hajin as the match goes on, and the Haneul Nuclear Reactor is irradiated [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; see [[radiation-storm]] and [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]]. Guides tell you to bring one when farming the reactor for Printed Circuit Boards [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] (reported). Your squad panel shows who has a gas mask [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Before level 26, Fire Stations are the place to find masks [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Where do you get the ingredients?

Chemicals is a basic Resource from looting; Rigid Synthetics come from homes or the printer ([[rigid-synthetics]]). The Disposable Parachute costs the same ([[disposable-parachute-recipe]]). The printer's Gear category covers gas masks, NVGs and parachutes [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
