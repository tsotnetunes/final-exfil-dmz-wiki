The Disposable Parachute recipe unlocks at DMZ level 55 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. Each one costs 30 Chemicals and 3 [[rigid-synthetics]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]], and the printed parachute is Rare [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. It has three uses: a fix on 23 Oct 2026 corrected the use counts of parachutes and gas masks [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Why do you need one?

In DMZ you have to find or craft a parachute [clm_7e1b19e52f1e], and it has its own loadout slot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The developers limited the uses of basic parachutes because a year of testing with unlimited ones made exploring Hajin feel too safe; they made them easier to find, and infinite-use parachutes exist as well [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. See [[parachute]].

## How do you get parachutes before level 55?

Fire Stations stock parachutes and radiation gear [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; see [[locations-of-interest]]. The Gas Mask recipe at level 26 costs exactly the same, so the same materials serve both ([[gas-mask-recipe]]).

## Where do you get Rigid Synthetics?

Homes and civilian duffel bags, or print them after Uncommon Ingredient Printing; see [[rigid-synthetics]]. Chemicals is a basic Resource that you gather by looting ([[materials]]).

The Legendary Parachute is a separate recipe; see [[parachute-recipe]].
