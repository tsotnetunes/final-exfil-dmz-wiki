The [[3d-printer]] prints 81 kinds of gear, each from its own recipe; with 14 ingredient recipes that makes 95 [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. Six come with the printer; the rest unlock through DMZ levels, DMZ Orders, a Story Mission or a recipe card you loot and extract [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. Ingredient recipes: [[materials]]. Upgrades: [[printer-upgrades]].

## How do you unlock a recipe?

- **Start**: the 1-Plate and 2-Plate Armor Vests, Small and Medium Backpacks, Bandage and Medkit come with the printer [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]].
- **Level N**: a DMZ level reward [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. Categories open by level too: Tacticals 4, Lethals 7, Field Upgrades 12, Fire Support 28.
- **Orders**: a reward for finishing a chapter of [[dmz-orders]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]].
- **Found**: loot the recipe card in Hajin and exfil with it; it joins the printer and pays 5,000 DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. Cards turn up in first aid kits, filing cabinets, duffel bags, footlockers and medicine cabinets [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]].

Inputs: three lists, agreeing except as noted below [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]; rarity is the printed item's [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]; levels are official [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
