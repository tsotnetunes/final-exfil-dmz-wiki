The Tempered Armor Vest recipe is in a Lieutenant crate inside The Doctor's office on the second floor of [[mirae-general-hospital]] (grid F5). The office is locked, and the key drops from The Doctor, the hospital's Lieutenant [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Extract with the recipe and it appears at the [[3d-printer]] [[src-patchcrazy-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-28]].

## What does it cost to print?

2 [[military-composites]], 4 Ballistic Fabric and 10 [[rigid-synthetics]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]. The printed vest is Ultra, one of eight Ultra recipes, all of them found in Hajin [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. It is a heavy carrier that takes enhanced plates [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported); for the vest itself see [[tempered-armor-vest]].

## Where do you get the ingredients?

- **Military Composites** are Legendary and cannot be printed. They drop from Colossus, a roaming boss [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]; see [[military-composites]].
- **Ballistic Fabric** is Epic: print it from 100 Synthetics and 3 High-Tenacity Fibers after Epic Ingredient Printing, or search the Chang-san Air Base armory and barracks and the La Grande Vie rooftop at Songdo Wharf [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- **Rigid Synthetics** are Uncommon and common in homes; see [[rigid-synthetics]].

## How do you reach the recipe?

Kill The Doctor at the hospital, take his key, then open his Command Office; see [[lieutenants]] and [[keys]]. Picking up a Lieutenant's Dog Tag Case broadcasts your position, so plan your exit [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[boss-board]]). Nothing confirms yet that the recipe is in the crate every time.
