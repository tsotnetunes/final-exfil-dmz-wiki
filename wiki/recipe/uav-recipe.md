The UAV recipe unlocks at DMZ level 31, three levels after the printer starts making Fire Support at level 28 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. Each UAV costs 30 Electronics, 2 [[structural-alloy]] and 5 [[electronic-sensors]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]; the printed UAV is Epic [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## Can you get a UAV without the recipe?

Yes: DMZ levels 13 and 65 each give one UAV (Epic Fire Support) [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. UAV Towers on the map are another way to scan an area [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] ([[uav-towers]]).

## Which Order asks for it?

The Avionics Order (page 19 of [[dmz-orders]]) asks you to print or extract five Electronic Sensors, extract one Structural Alloy, then print a UAV [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]].

## Where do you get the ingredients?

- **Structural Alloy** is Rare and cannot be printed; DMZ level 43 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]; a page of Orders (page 14) pays two [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]]; one guide finds it in the underground bunker of Operation Iron Citadel [[src-patchcrazy-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-28]] (reported).
- **Electronic Sensors** are Uncommon: tech bags and IT bags in offices [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]], or print them after Uncommon Ingredient Printing.

## What about the Advanced UAV?

A separate Legendary recipe found in Hajin: 2 Comms Modules, 3 Nitro Compounds and 5 Electronic Sensors [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. The Scout Trait UAV Upgrade turns your UAV into an Advanced UAV with longer range and duration [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
