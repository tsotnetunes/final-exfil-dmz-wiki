The Self-Revive Kit recipe unlocks at DMZ level 48 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. Each kit costs 1 Stabilized Reagents, 5 [[biomaterials]] and 3 [[binding-catalyst]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]], and the printed kit is Legendary [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]].

## Can you get one before level 48?

Yes. The developers said self-revive kits can be looted in Hajin as well as printed [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]], and the Triage Kit at DMZ level 27 and the Advanced SERE Kit at level 70 contain Self Revives [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. Your loadout has its own Self-Revive slot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Without one, every Operator can still use the [[tourniquet]] to get up from Last Stand with a wound [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. See [[health-and-healing]] and the [[possum]] Trait.

## Where do you get the ingredients?

- **Stabilized Reagents** are Epic: print them from 100 Chemicals and 3 Binding Catalyst once Epic Ingredient Printing is built [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]; one list places them at [[mirae-general-hospital]] and in medical tents [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] (reported).
- **Binding Catalyst** is Rare and cannot be printed; level 49 gives two, and medical bags around the hospital are the usual tip [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] (reported for the spot).
- **Biomaterials** are Uncommon and printable after Uncommon Ingredient Printing; see [[biomaterials]].

Since both rarer parts point to the hospital, one trip there can cover the whole kit. All recipes: [[recipes]].
