The Parachute is a Legendary piece of Gear that costs 2 [[turned-components]], 5 [[nylon-cloth]] and 10 [[rigid-synthetics]] to print [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. Its recipe is found in Hajin [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. DMZ level 69 gives you one Parachute (Legendary), not the recipe [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].

## Is it the infinite-use parachute?

The developers said infinite-use parachutes exist besides the limited basic ones [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]. One database describes the Legendary Parachute as reusable [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported); whether it is the infinite one, and how many uses it has, is not confirmed yet. The cheaper [[disposable-parachute-recipe]] has three uses.

## Where do you get the ingredients?

- **Turned Components** are Epic: print them from 100 Metals and 3 Stock Rods after Epic Ingredient Printing [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]]; players report finding three or more in a deployment without a key [clm_6c7b8163cfc9].
- **Nylon Cloth** is Rare and cannot be printed; look in Deadtown's underground, the Mujin Nightclub and the Citadel [[src-destructoid-dmz-ingredient-guides-2026-10-22]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- **Rigid Synthetics**: homes, or print them; see [[rigid-synthetics]].

One database values the Parachute at 5,000 DMZ Cash [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported). See [[parachute]] for how parachutes work and [[recipes]] for the full list.
