The 3-Plate Armor Vest costs 2 Ballistic Fabric, 5 [[nylon-cloth]] and 10 [[reactive-metals]] at the [[3d-printer]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. You unlock the recipe through [[dmz-orders]], not through a DMZ level [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]. The printed vest is Legendary [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## What about the level 37 vest?

DMZ level 37 gives you one 3-Plate Armor Vest as an item, not the recipe [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. That reward was not being granted until a fix on 28 Oct 2026; on 29 Oct Infinity Ward said players who had passed level 37 before the fix still lacked it and that it was working on granting it [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. See [[dmz-progression]].

## Where do you get the ingredients?

- **Ballistic Fabric** is an Epic ingredient. Print it from 100 Synthetics and 3 High-Tenacity Fibers once Epic Ingredient Printing is built [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]], or search the Chang-san Air Base armory and barracks and the La Grande Vie rooftop at Songdo Wharf [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].
- **Nylon Cloth** is Rare and cannot be printed [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]]. It turns up in Deadtown's underground, the Mujin Nightclub in the Heavenly Luck Casino and the Citadel's main building [[src-destructoid-nylon-farming-2026-10-23]]; level 57 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].
- **Reactive Metals**: industrial areas and toolboxes, or print them; see [[reactive-metals]].

## Other 3-plate vests

The 3-Plate Ballistic Gel Vest, 3-Plate Helmet Armor Vest and Tempered Armor Vest are Ultra, and their recipes are found in Hajin; each needs a part for which that guide found no printer recipe [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. See [[tempered-armor-vest-recipe]] and [[recipes]].
