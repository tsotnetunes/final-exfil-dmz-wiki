A Medkit costs 30 Chemicals, 1 Binding Catalyst and 5 Biomaterials at the [[3d-printer]], and the recipe is one of the six you get when the printer opens [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. The printed Medkit is an Epic consumable [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## What does a Medkit do?

It heals all missing health for good: it repairs your wound and keeps you at the full 150, where a Bandage only lifts you to 150 for a while (180 seconds by one database) before you drop back to 100 [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. The publisher's suggested loadout for a deployment includes one [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. See [[health-and-healing]] and [[tourniquet]].

## Where do you get the ingredients?

- **Chemicals** is a Resource: looted junk breaks down into it automatically [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **Binding Catalyst** is Rare and cannot be printed [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. DMZ level 49 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]; search medical bags at [[mirae-general-hospital]] and the medical tents around it [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] (reported). See [[binding-catalyst]].
- **Biomaterials** is Uncommon; DMZ level 39 gives two, and after Uncommon Ingredient Printing you can print it from 20 Synthetics and 20 Chemicals [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]]. See [[biomaterials]].

The Basic Triage Kit at DMZ level 11 also contains a Medkit [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].

## What is the Healer Order?

Order 03 on page 7 of [[dmz-orders]] asks you to track the Medkit recipe at the printer and then print a Medkit [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]]. Tracking it is a separate step from unlocking it [clm_3fc45e2c36d0]. If Track Recipe seems to do nothing, untrack an older recipe in the Tracked list and track the Medkit again; this helps some players but is not a fix [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] (reported). A separate bug, where Untrack Recipe with nothing tracked threw you out of the FOB, was fixed on 21 Oct 2026 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
