The Door Breacher Charge is an Ultra consumable that costs 1 [[experimental-compounds]], 5 [[stock-rod]] and 3 Volatile Explosives to print [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]]. Its recipe is found in Hajin and must be extracted before it appears at the [[3d-printer]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [clm_12f1ab2f7bf6]. DMZ level 62 hands you one charge, not the recipe [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].

## Where do you get the ingredients?

- **Experimental Compounds** are a Legendary ingredient [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]] [clm_0a8c285157c0] that cannot be printed; Colossus drops them [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]], and one database says the Deathstalker and the tank drop them too (reported).
- **Stock Rods** are Rare and cannot be printed; level 51 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]], and players report finding eight or more in a deployment without a key [clm_6fc0235c2541].
- **Volatile Explosives** are Epic: print them from 100 Chemicals and 3 Nitro Compounds after Epic Ingredient Printing [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]]; one list names the NuriGO Mall DRC lab, the Citadel prison yard's chemical buildings and Imjin Farmland's storage tents [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] (reported).

## What does it do?

It is a one-use charge that opens locked doors [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported). Which doors it works on, and whether it replaces a key, is not confirmed yet; see [[keys]] for locked rooms. See [[recipes]] for the other Ultra recipes.
