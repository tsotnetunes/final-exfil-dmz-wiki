Night Vision Goggles cost 50 Metals, 3 [[printed-circuit-board]] and 10 [[control-unit]] at the [[3d-printer]] [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]]; the printed goggles are Rare [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. The recipe is a [[dmz-orders]] reward, from the Underground chapter [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]].

## Does Operation Shopping Hacks give the recipe?

[[operation-shopping-hacks]] closes the Underground chapter, and players report that finishing it gives the recipe [clm_3086f62d37c7]. Story Mission lists show Night Vision Goggles among its rewards without saying item or recipe [[src-gfinity-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-22]] [[src-altchar-dmz-story-missions-2026-10-22]].

## Are there other goggles?

- **Weak Night Vision Goggles**: Uncommon, 25 Metals and 5 Control Units, recipe found in Hajin [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]. A pair sits on a shelf by the bunk beds in the objective tent outside NuriGO Mall during Shopping Hacks [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]] (reported).
- **Elite Night Vision Goggles**: Legendary, 2 Power Regulators, 3 CPU and 5 Electronic Sensors, recipe found in Hajin [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## Why would you need them?

Hajin has no night or day-night cycle at launch, but NVGs are in the loot pool for its darker areas [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## Where do you get the ingredients?

Printed Circuit Boards are Rare and cannot be printed: try the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] and electrical substations [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]]. Control Units come from computers and tech bags [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]], or from the printer after Uncommon Ingredient Printing.
