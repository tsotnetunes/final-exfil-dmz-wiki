The Medium Backpack costs 50 Synthetics and 5 [[rigid-synthetics]] at the [[3d-printer]]. Its recipe is one of the first six you have, and the backpack has Secure Slots [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_0a8ddc7fe171], one according to a database [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]] (reported). The official launch post lists it as a level 2 reward [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]; the printed bag is Rare [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## Why does it matter?

Without a backpack you carry the Basic Pack with 8 slots. Better backpacks add slots, weapon slots and [[secure-slots]], and a failed exfil loses whatever sits outside the Secure Slots [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The Small Backpack (25 Synthetics, 25 Metals) is cheaper but is not described with Secure Slots [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## Where do you get Rigid Synthetics?

Look in homes rather than shops or banks: residential buildings and civilian duffel bags [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. A red tool box held two on one run [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] (reported). DMZ level 6 gives two [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]], and after Uncommon Ingredient Printing they print from 20 Synthetics and 20 Metals [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]].

## Other ways to get one

The Restock Kit at DMZ levels 8 and 24 contains Medium Backpacks [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]], and the Vault Edition Deployment Kit holds two [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. An early page of [[dmz-orders]] (page 4) asks you to print one together with a 2-Plate Armor Vest [[src-techbloat-dmz-orders-2026-10-28]].

## What comes after it?

Found recipes for the Medium Scavenger, Medium Weapon and Small Secure Backpacks, then the Large Backpack from DMZ Orders [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. See [[recipes]] and [[large-backpack-recipe]].
