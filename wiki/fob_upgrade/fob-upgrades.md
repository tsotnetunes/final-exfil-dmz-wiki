FOB upgrades are permanent improvements to your base that you print at the [[3d-printer]]; each one takes several prints [clm_7b0306c946e3]. They cover the Bank, Stash item and weapon space, MIA recovery discounts, Gunsmith discounts, Vendor offers, Dog Tag Case storage and printing rarer ingredients [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## How do FOB upgrades work?

dmzhub.gg lists nine upgrades with 31 levels in all. A level costs DMZ Cash, items, or both, and needs the level before it (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]]. The items are mostly valuables and 3D Printer Ingredients, so check this page before you sell a valuable [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]; players also report needing specific items for Stash upgrades [clm_1d5b2eabae74]. The costs below come from that one community database, so treat them as reported until Activision publishes its own list. Player reports point the same way: five Gold Bars and five Gold Skulls for the last upgrades [clm_4903d57a964d] [clm_b90e9b9a3636], Military Composites and Experimental Compounds [clm_5f55c9bd182e], Stock Rods and Turned Components [clm_0217c096a2ca], and sellable files found in bunkers [clm_c712a62e76ae].

Some DMZ levels give the same kinds of bonus for free: Stash slots at levels 22, 35, 45, 60 and 70, a 5% Gunsmith discount at 25 and Vendor upgrades at 18 and 32 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. Whether level rewards and printed upgrades add together is not confirmed. See [[dmz-progression]].

## Bank Upgrade

Raises how much DMZ Cash your Bank ([[wallet]]) can hold [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]]. The Bank starts at about 100,000, by one guide and player reports [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]] [clm_06c3db0d22f9]. Levels I and II are confirmed by two more guides [[src-allthingshow-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-21]] [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]], level III by another [[src-gamerfuzion-3d-printer-upgrades-2026-10-23]], and a creator's footage after launch showed the 1,000,000 cap with 2,000,000 as the next step, which matches levels III and IV [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Levels V to X rest on the community database alone (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]]; what else is unsettled is on [[wallet]].

## Ingredient printing

Two single-level upgrades let the 3D Printer make rarer ingredients: Uncommon Ingredient Printing (50,000 DMZ Cash, 20 Biomaterials, 10 High-Tenacity Fibers, 5 Comms Module) and Epic Ingredient Printing (100,000, 10 Binding Catalyst, 10 Printed Circuit Board, 5 Military Composites) (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]].
