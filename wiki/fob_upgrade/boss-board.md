The Boss Board is the FOB station for boss hunting: it opens after your second DMZ Order, sells one-deployment intel on Lieutenants, gives free information on Commanders, and records each boss you kill and extract [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How do you unlock the Boss Board?

Complete your second DMZ Order [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-gamingpromax-dmz-boss-locations-2026-10-23]]. Some players report it after the first [clm_98e537e8f2e3]; the official guide says the second.

## How does Lieutenant intel work?

- It costs $25,000 [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] and lasts one deployment [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- It unlocks through challenges in earlier deployments [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; at launch Jin-Tae, Bale and Roze showed a padlock [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]]. Guides tie most unlocks to DMZ Orders chapters, such as Counterfeit for Jin-Tae (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
- In the match it becomes a side op that reads Paid Intel: Reach, then Kill, with the Lieutenant's name [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]] [[src-allthingshow-bale-2026-10-21]].
- It guarantees that Lieutenant in your next deployment and marks it from the start, and you can switch it for free before deploying (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].
- The free alternative is a Lieutenant Hunt tower in Hajin ([[side-ops]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; the LT Hunter trait makes a tower reveal one more Lieutenant [[src-dmzhub-bosses-2026-10-31]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]].

## How does a kill count?

Kill the boss, pick up its dog tag and extract alive; die first and the kill is not recorded [[src-allthingshow-boss-board-2026-10-21]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A Lieutenant tag counts even if you took it from another player [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Infinity Ward describes boss kills as checkpoints that only count on extraction [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. See [[dog-tags]].
