The Bounty Leaderboard is the FOB station that ranks the top player killers and bounty hunters in DMZ [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. It holds two boards, each viewable worldwide or among your friends [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]:

| Board | Who is on it | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most Wanted | the top 50 players with a Bounty | status (Wanted or Eliminated) and Bounty |
| Bounty Hunters | the top 50 hunters | bounties claimed and DMZ Cash earned |

Status values as described by codmunity.gg [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Since launch a target has sat at the top of the boards [clm_0a8db7cc19eb].

## When is it updated?

Weekly [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Players report that the boards take a snapshot of the top 100 on Thursday mornings and that accounts are checked by hand before publishing [clm_fc75f811681c] [clm_25cf22beb910]; the official guide speaks of a top 50.

## How do you get on it?

For Most Wanted, build a large Bounty by killing players and stay alive: dying, losing your tag to a player who extracts it or paying the Bounty off clears it ([[bounty-system]], [[wanted-status]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. For Bounty Hunters, kill Wanted players and extract with their Dog Tags ([[dog-tags]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Your standing carries over between matches [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## When does it unlock?

The 9 Oct guide lists it among the stations open from the start [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and the launch level table names no unlock level for it [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. Players report it needing DMZ level 17 [clm_84fd4d1811bc]; check it in game.
