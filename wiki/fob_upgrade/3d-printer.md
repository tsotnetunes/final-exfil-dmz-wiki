The 3D Printer is the FOB station where you craft: pick a Recipe, pay its Resources and Ingredients, and the item goes to your Stash or straight onto your Operator. It also prints the FOB's permanent upgrades [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_7b0306c946e3]. It opens at DMZ level 2 and has 95 recipes at launch, 81 for items ([[recipes]]) and 14 for ingredients ([[materials]]) [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzhub-recipes-2026-10-31]], plus the FOB upgrades ([[printer-upgrades]]).

## When does the 3D Printer unlock?

At DMZ level 2, usually after your first match [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-gfinity-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-22]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]. The official launch post lists the station under level 1 instead [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]. At first it prints Gear, Backpacks, Consumables and Upgrades; level 4 adds Tacticals, level 7 Lethals, level 12 Field Upgrades and level 28 Fire Support [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] ([[dmz-progression]]).

## How does crafting work?

- **Resources**: Synthetics, Electronics, Chemicals and Metals. Looted junk breaks down into them automatically; they take no Stash slots and ride in a separate pouch in every backpack [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **Ingredients**: parts such as Binding Catalyst, Biomaterials, Detonators and Control Units stay whole, take inventory slots and count only once you exfil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]. Lose the deployment and you lose them [[src-gfinity-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-22]]. See [[materials]].
- **The card**: each recipe has Print to Operator, Print to Stash, Track Recipe and Ingredient Locations buttons [[src-xboxplay-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-23]]. Amounts turn green when you have enough, and you can print several at once [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]].
- **Tracking**: pin a recipe or an ingredient and tracked ingredients are marked while you loot; Ingredient Locations names the kind of area to search, not an exact spawn [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]]. Players report that around 30 Oct 2026 the crafting menu gained a way to print missing ingredients while you craft [clm_8c33047a814e] (reported); how it works is not confirmed yet.
- **If Print is greyed out**: you lack an input; if Print to Stash fails, the Stash may be full, so try Print to Operator [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] (reported).

## Where do recipes come from?

DMZ levels, DMZ Orders and loot. A recipe card you find is used up when you exfil, added to the printer and pays 5,000 DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. In Part 1's story a Lieutenant's loot room held a plate carrier recipe ([[keys]]). Every recipe and its unlock: [[recipes]].

## What upgrades can you print?

Every level with its cost: [[printer-upgrades]]. DMZ levels add Stash slots and discounts on top ([[dmz-progression]]).

## What does Ingredient Printing do?

Uncommon Ingredient Printing lets you print Biomaterials, Reactive Metals, Electronic Sensors, Rigid Synthetics, Control Units and Detonators from 20 each of two Resources [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. It needs 5 Comms Modules that only the Epic tier can print, so loot them first [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]] ([[comms-module]]). Epic Ingredient Printing adds eight Epic parts, each from 100 of a Resource and 3 Rare ingredients [[src-destructoid-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-21]] [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]]. There is no Rare tier: Rare and Legendary ingredients cannot be printed [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-dtgre-dmz-crafting-guides-2026-10-24]].

## Which Traits help with crafting?

Scout Traits: Field Crafting lets you craft from Common printer resources while deployed; Hostile Looter makes AI sometimes drop an Uncommon ingredient, rarely a Rare one; Container Looter gives a chance of an extra item in containers [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Resource Pockets gives 20% more from scrapping [[src-codcentral-3d-printer-recipes-2026-10-20]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-items-2026-10-26]]. See [[skill-trees]].

## What should you build first?

Stash Item Upgrade I, because early storage runs out fast [[src-vgtimes-dmz-3d-printer-2026-10-27]] [[src-gamerfuzion-3d-printer-upgrades-2026-10-23]]. Track the next upgrade before each run, and keep valuables such as Infantry Invasion Plans and Regional Combat Notes for upgrades instead of selling them [[src-gamerfuzion-3d-printer-upgrades-2026-10-23]]. A Medium Backpack, a starter recipe with Secure Slots, is the cheapest printed way to protect loot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] ([[medium-backpack-recipe]]).
