Unlocks at DMZ level 2 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Crafts gear from Resources and 3D Printer Ingredients and is also how FOB stations are upgraded, given the right loot [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. It hints where specific ingredients are most likely to be found, and you can pin recipes and ingredients to track them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

**Recipes.** Most start locked. You get them from DMZ levels ([[dmz-progression]]), DMZ Orders and looting: a looted Recipe is consumed when you exfil, added to the printer and pays 5,000 DMZ Cash [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A Lieutenant's loot room held a plate carrier recipe in Part 1's story ([[keys]]).

**Materials.** Four Resources (Synthetics, Electronics, Chemicals, Metals) are broken down automatically as you loot; Ingredients such as Binding Catalyst, Biomaterials, Detonators and Control Units stay whole and must be extracted [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Commanders drop prized ingredients ([[commander-threats]]).

**First recipes (level 2)** [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: Bandage (15 Synthetics, 15 Chemicals); Medkit (30 Chemicals, 1 Binding Catalyst, 5 Biomaterials); 1-Plate Armor Vest (25 Synthetics, 25 Metals); 2-Plate Armor Vest (50 Synthetics, 5 [[reactive-metals]]); Small Backpack (25 Synthetics, 25 Metals); Medium Backpack (50 Synthetics, 5 Rigid Synthetics; it has Secure Slots).

**Upgrades.** Permanent FOB upgrades take several prints each: Bank, Stash item and weapon capacity, MIA recovery discounts, rarer ingredient printing, extra Dog Tag Case storage, Gunsmith discounts and more Vendor offers [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
