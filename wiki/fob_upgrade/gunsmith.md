The Gunsmith is the FOB station where you buy and build guns for DMZ Cash. It opens at DMZ level 10, when Gunny joins the FOB [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]] [clm_311263850855]. It works like Multiplayer's Gunsmith: weapon levels and shared attachment unlocks count in both modes [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Open it at the FOB, from the DMZ side menu, or from a weapon in your Stash [[src-allthingshow-dmz-gunsmith-weapons-2026-10-21]]. The daily rotating stock players mention belongs to the [[weapon-vendor]], not the Gunsmith [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What can you buy?

- **Base guns** whose Weapon Manual you have extracted, as often as you like ([[weapon-manuals]]); a gun you looted and extracted can be modified as a single copy [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Base guns come with no attachments [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]]. Buy New Weapon has a tab per class [[src-allthingshow-dmz-gunsmith-weapons-2026-10-21]]; prices per gun are in [[weapons]].
- **Attachment slots**, up to five, bought in any order, each dearer than the last [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] (table below).
- **Attachments**, each with its own price, once unlocked by weapon level [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]]; lasers alone run from 1,900 to 6,887 [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-prices-2026-10-08]] [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]].
- **The Apex slot**, which guides disagree on: free once unlocked, or 52,000 in one post-launch cart (see [[apex-attachments]]).
- **Cosmetics** are free, except [[blueprints]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

| Slot | DMZ Cash |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | 1,000 |
| 2nd | 5,000 |
| 3rd | 10,000 |
| 4th | 25,000 |
| 5th | 50,000 |
| All five | 91,000 |

Slot fees from two guide sites [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]; the third-slot figure comes from a single player report, and a creator's report of paying 50,000 for a fifth slot matches the last row [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. A finished gun quickly reaches six figures: an M4 with five attachments cost 99,987 after a 5% discount, and a Rezi 12 with four attachments and its Apex 118,250 (both reported) [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]] [[src-allthingshow-rezi-12-enforcer-build-2026-10-21]].

## How do you get Gunsmith discounts?

The official guide says discounts come from DMZ levels and from upgrades printed at the [[3d-printer]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

| Discount | What it takes |
| --- | --- |
| 5% at DMZ level 25 | reach the level [[src-codmunity-dmz-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]] |
| Gunsmith Upgrade I, 5% | 50,000 DMZ Cash, 20 Reactive Metals, 10 CPU, 5 Power Regulator [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]] [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]] |
| Gunsmith Upgrade II, 5% more | 100,000 DMZ Cash, 20 Nitro Compounds, 10 Comms Module, 10 Military Composites (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]] |

Two guides count the level 25 reward and Upgrade I as separate discounts [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]] [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]; whether they stack to 15% is not confirmed yet.

## What did the launch patches fix?

From the official notes [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]:

- **20 Oct:** the attachment menu could stay stuck on screen during a deployment; on Nintendo Switch 2, saved Blueprint modifications could not be bought.
- **24 Oct:** applying some camos or reticles in the DMZ Gunsmith took DMZ Cash, although cosmetics are meant to be free [clm_6ecd75a4da6f]; DMZ builds could show as usable in core Multiplayer.
- **25 Oct:** buying a saved Build made from a modified Blueprint charged the full price of the original Blueprint; weapon stats did not show for locked attachments.
- **28 Oct:** prestiging from the DMZ FOB did not reset Multiplayer loadouts. Still known that day: entering Classic Prestige from the DMZ menus stops you making new Multiplayer classes until the old classes with relocked items are deleted.

## What else is at the bench?

Gunny suggests a close-range, balanced or long-range build from the parts you own [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]]. You can also prestige a fully levelled weapon here, which relocks its attachments for exclusive camos [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]] [[src-dmzradar-gunsmith-2026-10-20]]. Several DMZ Orders send you here: fit a suppressor (page 4), buy a gun and build one with three or more attachments (page 11), and build a sniper rifle with five or more (page 24) (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]] ([[dmz-orders]]).
