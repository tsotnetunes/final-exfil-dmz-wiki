Always available: walk through the wire to the big transport helicopter that flies every deployment in [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Choose a deployment first: [[story-missions]], [[dynamic-operations]] or [[free-roam]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. For Dynamic Operations and Free Roam you also choose how to go in; see [[paid-infil]] for the options and prices [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

**Ready Up.** Take the Free Loadout (you keep it if you extract), edit your loadout (the guide suggests at least a plate carrier, a backpack, a weapon and a Medkit), swap your Active Duty Operator, and turn Squad Fill on to be matched with two other players [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
