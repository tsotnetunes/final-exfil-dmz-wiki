Deploy is always available: walk through the wire to the heavy-lift transport helicopter that takes every deployment into Hajin [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What can you deploy into?

Three kinds of deployment: [[story-missions]], [[dynamic-operations]] and [[free-roam]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Story Missions show a list to complete or replay, with a briefing, rewards (usually valuable gear and DMZ Cash), a random-mission option and milestone rewards [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]; some stay locked until you meet their prerequisites [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-features-2026-10-09]]. The matchmaker groups players who picked the same kind of deployment, and if you crash you can rejoin while your match is still running (the developers' Q&A via a video recap) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

## How do you go in?

For Dynamic Operations and Free Roam you also pick an infil: free on foot, or a paid vehicle, helicopter or plane drop ([[paid-infil]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The paid options open through Story Missions (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [[src-skycoach-dmz-beginner-guide-2026-10-19]].

## What happens at Ready Up?

Take the Free Loadout, which you keep if you extract, or edit your own loadout (the guide suggests at least a plate carrier, a backpack, a weapon and a Medkit). You can also swap your Active Duty Operator and turn Squad Fill on to be matched with two other players [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The round recovery slots beside the Ready button each protect one Operator or a set of weapons for a single deployment (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## What is in the Free Loadout?

It is offered whenever you have no gear [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-operator-guide-2026-10-20]]. It holds one gun, a melee weapon, a single Lethal and a single Tactical plus one armor plate, with no backpack beyond the 8-slot Basic Pack and no parachute (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Your own Operator still goes in, so dying on a Free Loadout still sends them MIA ([[mia-system]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

## What should you check before you deploy?

- Clear the Overflow Stash; since 25 Oct 2026 you cannot deploy with items left in it [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- Fixed 28 Oct 2026: printing items into your Backpack while deploying could send you in without the Free Loadout's weapons and gear [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
- Fixed 29 Oct 2026: the Free Loadout could not be picked when the Stash was full [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].
