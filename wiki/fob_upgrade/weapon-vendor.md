The Vendor is the FOB station that sells weapons and other gear for DMZ Cash, handy for refilling a loadout fast [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. It opens at DMZ level 5 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-progression-2026-10-19]] [clm_b5908fa8c1b3]; some players have reported level 10, but the official guide and the progression track say 5.

## What does the Vendor sell?

A rotating selection that includes pre-built weapons [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]], and the stock changes every day [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-dmztracker-gunsmith-2026-10-29]]. It can offer guns you have not unlocked yet, such as the Cronen Squall [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Offers players have reported:

- a Rare Mosin-Nagant for 32,750 DMZ Cash (reported) [[src-dmzcodex-gunsmith-2026-10-29]];
- a Door Breacher Charge, sometimes, for 50,000 [clm_13dd447ee402].

A gun bought here is one copy: you lose it if you die with it, and it does not unlock the weapon in the [[gunsmith]]; only a Weapon Manual does that ([[weapon-manuals]]) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-weapon-manuals-2026-10-28]].

## How do you get better offers?

Better gear appears as your DMZ level rises, and Vendor upgrades printed at the [[3d-printer]] add offers [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. One progression list puts the Vendor upgrades at DMZ levels 18 and 32 [[src-codmunity-dmz-progression-2026-10-19]]; a community database gives their materials (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]]:

| Upgrade | Gives | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vendor Item Upgrade I | more items for sale | 20 Electronic Sensors, 10 CPU, 10 Printed Circuit Board, 2 Prototype RF Module |
| Vendor Item Upgrade II | more items for sale | 20 Control Unit, 10 High-Tenacity Fibers, 10 Nylon Cloth, 2 Nano-Structured Alloy |

No DMZ Cash cost is listed for either. From Season 1 (6 Nov 2026), owners of the Season 1 BlackCell pass get extra weapons and gear at the Vendor, still paid in DMZ Cash [[src-activision-mw4-season-1-2026-10-28]].

## What else should you know?

A DMZ Order asks you to buy a weapon from the Vendor (reported) [[src-dmzhub-weapons-2026-10-31]] ([[dmz-orders]]). The time of day the stock changes is not confirmed yet.
