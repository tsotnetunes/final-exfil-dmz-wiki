The Orders station is the FOB terminal where you read your [[dmz-orders]]; it is open from your first visit to the FOB [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The 5 Oct guide calls it DMZ Orders and describes guided directives for onboarding, story and mastery [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]], and the PlayStation Blog says you review them on an Orders Tablet [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]]. The June deep dive already listed Orders and objective tracking among the FOB's features [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## What can you do there?

- Read the active page: its three Orders, the task lines under each with their counters, and what the page pays when all three are done [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]].
- Choose a chapter when the list branches: after page 1 (Human Intel, Underground or Counterfeit) and after page 11 (The Grid or Hornet's Nest) [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] [[src-codcentral-dmz-orders-2026-10-21]].
- See the rewards of each chapter before you pick it, on the chapter choice screen [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]] (reported).

You do not need to visit the station for Orders to progress: tasks tick during any deployment, and you can open the Orders menu from the Tac-Map in the middle of a match [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-ops-explained-2026-10-13]] (reported).

## Does the station unlock anything?

Your second completed Order opens the [[boss-board]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and finishing page 1 opens the [[story-missions]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. No 3D Printer upgrade for this station has been reported.
