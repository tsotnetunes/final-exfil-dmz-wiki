<!-- human:start -->
The Operators station, open from the start: manage your Active Duty Operators, check their status, spend their Traits and pick one to deploy [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. You start with two slots and unlock a third through DMZ levels; the Vault Edition starts with three [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. An Operator stays in their slot until you dismiss them, and the same Operator cannot fill two slots; skins can be changed [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

Each Operator has their own Dog Tag level and Trait Points ([[trait-points]]) and keeps a persistent backpack and loadout [[src-cod-dmz-progression-video-2026-10-07]], while the Bank, Stash and crafting materials are shared [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. An Operator killed in a deployment goes MIA until recovered or dismissed ([[mia-system]]); a fully levelled one can Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]).

**Operators from the Campaign.** Finishing Campaign missions unlocks eight base Operators (Cho, Dallas, Luca, Oh-Ji, Kal, Dunn, West and Park), plus three Double XP and two Double Weapon XP tokens, all usable in DMZ from full launch on 23 Oct [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]].
