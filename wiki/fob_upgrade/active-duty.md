The Operators station, open from the start: here you manage your Active Duty Operators, check their status, spend their Trait Points and pick who deploys [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [clm_eee0a1ff7205].

## How many Operators can you have?

Two Active Duty slots at the start and a third from DMZ level 15 [[src-callofduty-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Vault Edition owners start with three [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [clm_37cdae5b1cba]. A player reported having a fourth Operator [clm_fe6a53c7c828]; whether the level 15 slot adds to the Vault Edition's three is not confirmed.

## How do Active Duty Operators work?

You recruit an Operator into a slot and they stay there until you dismiss them; the same Operator cannot fill two slots, and you can change their skin [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Each Operator has their own Dog Tag level and Trait Points ([[trait-points]], [[dmz-progression]]) and keeps a persistent backpack and loadout [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-cod-dmz-progression-video-2026-10-07]]. The Bank, Stash, Resources and Ingredients are shared by all of them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. A killed Operator goes MIA until you recover or dismiss them ([[mia-system]]); one with 18 Trait Points can Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]). The Operator's profile shows any Bounty on them [[src-xbox-wire-dmz-operator-guide-2026-10-20]].

## Which Operators can you use?

- **From the Campaign:** Cho, Dallas, Luca, Oh-Ji, Kal, Dunn, West and Park, plus three Double XP and two Double Weapon XP tokens, all usable in DMZ from 23 Oct 2026 [[src-activision-campaign-early-access-live-2026-10-16]].
- **From DMZ levels:** Reece at 4, Coffey at 9, Simone at 15 and Hector at 19, usable in DMZ and Multiplayer [[src-callofduty-ready-for-launch-operators-2026-10-19]].
- Operators from older games do not appear, because DMZ is part of Modern Warfare 4 rather than Warzone (the developers' Q&A via a video recap) [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].
