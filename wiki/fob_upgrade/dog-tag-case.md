The Dog Tag Case is the FOB station that keeps the dog tags you extracted after killing other players; it unlocks at DMZ level 20 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [clm_d156c7a1a567] [clm_f8c43f6e8da8]. You can mark favourites to keep them [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-playstation-blog-dmz-2026-10-05]].

## What does it show?

Each tag shows its rarity, from Steel to Onyx, the Operator it belonged to, who killed them, and the time and date [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Storage is limited [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]; the starting number of slots is not published. How tags work in the field: [[dog-tags]].

## How do you upgrade it?

- **Bounty payouts.** DMZ levels raise the DMZ Cash you get from bounties: 10% more at level 30, 50% at 50 and 100% at 70 [[src-activision-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- **More slots.** 3D Printer upgrades add storage [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: four Dog Tag Case Upgrade levels of 20 slots each (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]].

| Upgrade | DMZ Cash | Items (reported) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 10,000 | 10 Rigid Synthetics, 10 Biomaterials |
| II | 50,000 | 20 Detonators, 10 Nylon Cloth, 5 Power Regulator |
| III | 100,000 | 10 Printed Circuit Board, 10 Structural Alloy, 5 Power Regulator |
| IV | 200,000 | 20 CPU, 10 Stabilized Reagents, 5 Experimental Compounds |

All FOB upgrades: [[fob-upgrades]].

## Is it the same as a Lieutenant's Dog Tag Case?

No. A Lieutenant drops a Dog Tag Case item when killed; extracting with that item advances the [[boss-board]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
