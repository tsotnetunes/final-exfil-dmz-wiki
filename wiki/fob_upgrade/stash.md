The Stash is your storage at the FOB, open from the start and kept between deployments; you also set your next loadout here [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. It is shared by all your Active Duty Operators [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], and what you extract is what builds it up [clm_fe49071395d4].

## How much does the Stash hold?

At first 40 items and 12 weapons, counted separately [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. DMZ levels add 18 item slots at levels 22, 45 and 70, and 6 weapon slots at 35 and 60 [[src-callofduty-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]; players reported 58 items and 12 weapons, which is one 18-slot step [clm_cd43cdd0d14f]. The [[3d-printer]] also prints Stash upgrades [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]: four Stash Item levels of 18 slots and three Stash Weapon levels of 6 slots, paid in cash and specific items (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]] [clm_1d5b2eabae74]. Costs: [[fob-upgrades]].

## What goes in the Stash?

Weapons, gear, consumables, 3D Printer Ingredients, keys and valuables; you can sort by type or rarity and merge stacks [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The four Resources (Synthetics, Electronics, Chemicals, Metals) are broken down the moment you loot them and take no Stash slots [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. After every exfil, move what you will need later into the Stash and set the loadout for your next infil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What is the Overflow Stash?

Items that land in the Overflow Stash have to be sorted out before your next deployment; a fix on 25 Oct 2026 stopped players deploying while items were still there [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Another fix, on 29 Oct, let you pick the Free Loadout when the Stash is full [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What do you carry into Hajin?

Without a backpack you carry the Basic Pack with 8 slots. Better backpacks add slots, weapon slots and [[secure-slots]]; anything outside Secure Slots is lost if you fail to exfil ([[what-carries-over]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Backpack recipes come with DMZ levels 1 and 2, the Large Backpack at 34 and the Medium Weapon Backpack at 61 [[src-callofduty-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]].

## What happens to duplicated items?

Players report that weapons made with duplication glitches are deleted from the Stash [clm_304434c0da1f]; see [[item-duplication-glitches]].
