Open from the start; persistent across deployments [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. At first it holds 40 items and 12 weapons, counted separately, shared by all your Active Duty Operators; it keeps weapons, gear, consumables, 3D Printer Ingredients, keys and valuables, sorted by type or rarity, with stacks you can merge [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The four Resources (Synthetics, Electronics, Chemicals, Metals) are counted apart and take no slots [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Capacity grows with DMZ levels and 3D Printer upgrades [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].

In the field: without a backpack you carry the Basic Pack with 8 slots; better backpacks add slots, weapon slots and [[secure-slots]], and anything outside Secure Slots is lost if you fail to exfil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. After every exfil, offload what you will need later and set your loadout for the next infil [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. See [[what-carries-over]].
