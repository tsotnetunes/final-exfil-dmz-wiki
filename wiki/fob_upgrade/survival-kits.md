Survival Kits are themed bundles of supplies, such as Deployment, Triage (medical), Armor, Equipment and SERE kits. The station is open from the start: opening a kit moves what is inside to your [[stash]], and unopened kits stack in the station [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How do you get Survival Kits?

From DMZ levels, DMZ Orders and missions, and Events [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The DMZ levels that give a kit [[src-callofduty-ready-for-launch-progression-2026-10-19]]:

| Kit | DMZ level |
| --- | --- |
| Basic Armor Kit | 6 and 21 |
| Restock Kit | 8 and 24 |
| Basic Triage Kit | 11 |
| Explosives Equipment Kit | 16 |
| Recon Equipment Kit | 19 and 46 |
| Triage Kit | 27 |
| Trap Equipment Kit | 42 |
| Advanced SERE Kit | 70 |

## What is inside?

A community database lists these contents (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-10-31]]:

| Kit | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Basic Armor Kit | a 2-plate carrier and an Armor Box |
| Basic Restock Kit | a Medium Backpack, an Armor Box and an Ammo Box |
| Basic Triage Kit | a Medkit, Pain Killers, Radiation Blockers and a Bandage |
| Explosives Equipment Kit | a weapons locker key, 2 C4, 2 Semtex, 2 Frag Grenades and 2 Proximity Mines |
| Recon Equipment Kit | a weapons locker key, a Heartbeat Sensor, a Seeker Drone, a Recon Drone and a Snapshot Grenade |
| SERE Kit | a Medkit and a Self-Revive Kit |

The Trap Equipment Kit, the Triage Kit and the Advanced SERE Kit are not described yet.

## What is the Vault Edition Deployment Kit?

The Vault Edition adds a Deployment Kit with two each of Weapon Insurance Contracts, Operator Rescue Beacons, Fulton Drop Markers, Medium Backpacks and 2-Plate Carriers [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Players claim it at this station, which adds it to the Stash [clm_d79723694987]. How the insurance contracts and rescue beacons work: [[mia-system]].

## When should you open a kit?

Opening a kit moves everything into the Stash at once, so make room first; until then, unopened kits stay stacked in the station [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
