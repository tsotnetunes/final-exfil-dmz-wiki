Survival Kits are themed bundles of supplies, for example Deployment, Triage (medical), Armor, Equipment and SERE kits; opening one moves its contents to your [[stash]], and unopened kits stack in the station [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. You earn them from DMZ levels, DMZ Orders and missions, and Events [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The Vault Edition adds a Deployment Kit with two each of Weapon Insurance Contracts, Operator Rescue Beacons, Fulton Drop Markers, Medium Backpacks and 2-Plate Carriers [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
