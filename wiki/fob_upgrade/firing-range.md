The Firing Range is the FOB's practice area, open from your first visit and at any time between deployments [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. It sits in a wooded grove between the [[gunsmith]] and the Vendor ([[weapon-vendor]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. Multiplayer has a range of its own; the DMZ range is this one, in the FOB [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]].

## What is on the range?

- Walls to crouch behind, go prone at or mount your weapon on [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
- Round and bottle-shaped targets from under 10 m out to beyond 100 m, useful for checking optics at distance [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [clm_71239444e8f1].
- A time-to-kill (TTK) figure: a 21 Oct fix, listed for the whole game, stopped the range showing 0.00 for the Rezi 12 shotgun [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## What is it good for?

Testing a build before you take it into Hajin. The official launch guide suggests a quick aim down sights on the range to check where you placed your optic with Optic Rail Placement ([[weapon-customization]]) [[src-activision-mw4-launch-weapons-2026-10-19]]. Some players try attachments in Multiplayer before paying for them in DMZ, since unlocks are shared [clm_4d2a6927d4f1]. Compare guns side by side here before you spend DMZ Cash on one ([[weapons]]). One of the first DMZ Orders, Crackshot, asks you to knock down three targets on the range (reported) [[src-dmzhub-orders-2026-10-31]] ([[dmz-orders]]).

## Can you upgrade it?

No Firing Range upgrade is known. A community database's list of FOB upgrades has none for the range (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]], and the official guide lists it among the stations that are simply open from the start [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]].
