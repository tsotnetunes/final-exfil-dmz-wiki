The Bank is where your DMZ Cash lives between deployments; it is shared by all your Active Duty Operators [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. The June intel called it the Wallet [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]]. How to earn and spend the cash: [[dmz-cash]].

## How does cash get into the Bank?

- **By extracting.** Cash you pick up in Hajin is carried in your loadout and listed in the After Action Report, then paid into the Bank [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; it only counts if you exfil (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-dmz-level-up-fast-2026-10-21]].
- **Straight from Dynamic Ops.** Completed Ops pay into the Bank without an exfil (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]] [clm_f17f3ff635f7].
- **Bounties.** Extracting a Wanted player's Dog Tag pays DMZ Cash [clm_1dda32204130]; see [[bounty-system]].
- **Selling** items at the FOB ([[dmz-cash]]).

## How much can the Bank hold?

Players reported a 100,000 cap before any upgrade [clm_06c3db0d22f9]. Bank upgrades printed at the [[3d-printer]] raise the cap [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]] [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. dmzhub.gg lists ten levels (reported) [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]]:

| Bank Upgrade | Cap | Costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 250,000 | 20,000 |
| II | 500,000 | 50,000 |
| III | 1,000,000 | 100,000 and valuables |
| IV | 2,000,000 | 200,000 and valuables |
| V to X | 5, 10, 25, 50, 100 and 250 million | 500,000 each and valuables |

A creator's footage after launch showed the 1,000,000 cap with a 2,000,000 next step, matching levels III and IV [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. The valuables for each level: [[fob-upgrades]].

## What happens to cash over the cap?

It is lost, with no warning, both when you sell an item and when you extract with more than fits (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Upgrade the Bank before a big haul.

## How much should you keep?

Recovery of an MIA Operator ([[mia-system]]) and Operator Prestige ([[operator-prestige]]) are paid from the Bank [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]]. A first Prestige needs 200,000 and a third 500,000 [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part2-2026-10-09]], so by the reported caps you need at least Bank Upgrade I for the first and Bank Upgrade II for the third [[src-dmzhub-upgrades-2026-10-31]].
