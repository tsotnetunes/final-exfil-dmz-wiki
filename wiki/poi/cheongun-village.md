Cheongun Village is the northernmost South Korean town in Hajin: Low threat in its streets and border posts, with the High-threat [[casino]] (Heavenly Luck Casino) at its centre [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. It packs many loot stops into a small area: a police armory, a gun store, a bank vault, a fire station and clinics.

## Where is it?

North Hajin, on the Hajin River, split by two border posts (South Korean to the west, Russian to the east), with the denser woodland of the Outskirts to the west and Expressway No. 1 along the south. The Russian side across the river is fogged in and closed for now [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
