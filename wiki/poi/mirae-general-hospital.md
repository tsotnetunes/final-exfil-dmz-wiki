Mirae General Hospital is Hajin's main hospital in the north centre of the map, rated Low threat; it is the home of the Lieutenant called The Doctor and the setting of the Story Mission Operation Critical Extraction [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-gfinity-lieutenant-locations-2026-10-22]] [[src-fpsjp-story-missions-trailer-2026-10-09]].

## Where is it?

North-central Hajin, former South Korean side, next to a train station, with [[the-grid]] and [[nurigo-mall]] to the east and Expressway No. 1 to the north. Snipers are expected in the woods of Mujin Pass to the south [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What is inside?

Triage tents and temporary barracks on the stepped grounds, a parking structure, and a gutted multi-level lobby leading to wards and emergency rooms colour-coded by floor; fights inside are close quarters [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Hospitals hold medical supplies [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] ([[loot-placement]]). An Ammo and Armor Cache, a Lieutenant Hunt tower, a gas station and two UAV towers are mapped around it (reported) [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]].

## Who is here?

The Doctor is met on the roof, the first floor or in the underground parking, with armoured guards [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]] [[src-gfinity-lieutenant-locations-2026-10-22]]. The Doctor can drop the key to the Command Office [[src-gfinity-lieutenant-locations-2026-10-22]] [[src-allthingshow-key-doors-2026-10-21]] ([[lieutenants]]).
