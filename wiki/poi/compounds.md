Compounds are small fortified enemy positions with better loot, intel and armoured vehicles such as the LTV; storm one loudly and you fight more of its defenders at once, so a quiet approach can pay [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Hajin has six, named Alpha to Foxtrot, each with a safe [[src-gamingpromax-compounds-2026-10-26]] [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]].

## How do you clear a compound and open its safe?

Crossing the perimeter starts the Clear the Military Compound Side Op [[src-gamerfuzion-compound-echo-farm-2026-10-23]] [[src-gamingpromax-compounds-2026-10-26]]. One guide says a meter tracks your progress against about 10 to 12 guards, and the safe unlocks at 100 percent; look in the main building, at the foot of the guard tower or in a command tent (reported) [[src-gamingpromax-compounds-2026-10-26]]. Each compound is also said to have a secret way in, and weapon lockers with valuables (reported) [[src-destructoid-best-loot-2026-10-25]].

## What loot do compounds hold?

Safes have paid FOB upgrade valuables such as Infantry Invasion Plans (most often at Charlie and Delta), Regional Combat Notes, Weapon Manuals, blueprints and cash; contents seem random [[src-gamingpromax-compounds-2026-10-26]] [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-compound-run-2026-10-26]]. Compounds also hold Ammo and Armor Caches and vehicles you can take [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Tips

Compounds stay in the same place every match, but another squad may have emptied one: open containers or open doors are the sign [[src-gamingpromax-compounds-2026-10-26]] [[src-gamerfuzion-compound-echo-farm-2026-10-23]]. Clear the tower snipers first. Safes count for Orders that ask you to open safes ([[dmz-orders]]).
