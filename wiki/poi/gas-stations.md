Gas Stations repair and refuel ground and air vehicles and refill the Gas Cans you carry; Marine Gas Stations, a small jetty with two pumps, do the same for boats [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. You need them because DMZ vehicles have limited armour and fuel, unlike Warzone's [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [clm_d8792d42a945]. Vehicle details: [[vehicles]].

## How do you refuel and repair?

Park at a pump and hold the interact button until the Refueling and Repairing bar fills (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-underground-orders-2026-10-23]].

## Where are the gas stations?

There are 18 Gas Stations and 6 Marine Gas Stations: one player counted 18 on the in-game map, and a community map pins the same number [[src-gamerfuzion-underground-orders-2026-10-23]] [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]].

| [[songdo-wharf]] | Two, one with a yellow canopy beside the gun store, plus a marine jetty [[src-allthingshow-gun-store-key-2026-10-22]] [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]] |

Marine Gas Stations are mapped near the prison, at the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]], at Cheongun Village, Songdo Wharf and [[hajin-river-heights]] (reported) [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]].

## Is there loot?

Gas stations are service points, not loot spots. One exception: the Rare Gas Station Electrical Room key opens an electrical room at one station in the city (reported) [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]].
