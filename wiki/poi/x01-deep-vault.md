The Deep Vault is the sealed vault at the heart of [[x01]], the Season 1 Black Site under Hajin. Infinity Ward's Season 1 post named it among the site's areas without saying what it holds [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. The patch notes since then confirm a Vault Door, a Vault Defend step that only becomes available once that door is open (since 7 Nov 2026), a Vault Elevator and a floor that bodies could fall through until a fix on 10 Nov 2026 [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## How do you get in?
You need a key whose name includes Deep Vault. One press guide says the easiest is the one [[the-captain]] drops near his body when he dies; he roams X01 with a minigun and Juggernaut armour, so bring a full squad or heavy weapons (reported) [[src-gamingdeputy-x01-deep-vault-2026-11-07]]. The same guide puts the entrance in the north west of X01, opened from a wall console. Pressing the elevator button inside closes the main door behind you, so another squad cannot follow you in (reported) [[src-gamingdeputy-x01-deep-vault-2026-11-07]].

## How do you open the core room?
Per that guide: ride the elevator down, take the left stairs to the top level, the core control room, and start the opening at its console. Tier 3 enemies then flood the area and must all be killed. Back on the ground floor a button opens the core room (reported) [[src-gamingdeputy-x01-deep-vault-2026-11-07]]. The official notes call the defending part the Vault Defend step [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

## What is inside?
The guide lists cabinets to search, Juggernaut armour you can put on and a bioweapon in the middle of the room that you neutralise; you leave by forcing a door to the elevator and riding a zipline up (reported) [[src-gamingdeputy-x01-deep-vault-2026-11-07]]. A community database also lists an Ultra key for a DRC weapons locker inside the vault (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-08]] ([[keys]]).

## Known problems
Fixed on 10 Nov 2026: players could get stuck in the Vault Elevator after closing its doors on an upper floor, and the bodies of eliminated players could drop through the Deep Vault floor, putting their loot out of reach [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. Since the same day UAV Fire Support cannot be used anywhere in X01 ([[fire-support]]) [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]].

Not confirmed yet: the full list of Deep Vault keys, the rewards in the core room, and whether the bioweapon step counts toward an Order ([[dmz-orders]]).
