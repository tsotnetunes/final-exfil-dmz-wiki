Imjin Farmland is a Low-threat spread of rice fields, waterways and rail lines in the south-southwest of Hajin, built around a big Rice Mill (Medium) that overlooks everything [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Where is it?

On the old border, former South Korean side; grid square D7 on the [[tac-map]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Compound Alpha lies to the east toward Daesong Ridge ([[compounds]]) [[src-gamingpromax-compounds-2026-10-26]] [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]].

## How dangerous is it?

| Area | Threat |

| Jeong Loading Dock | Low |

Flat floodplain, exposed fields and narrow roads give long sightlines and little cover, so vehicles and careful movement pay off; use the warehouses for cover on the way in and close-range weapons inside the mill [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What loot is there?

A fire station, a gas station, Ammo and Armor Caches and two UAV towers are mapped here (reported) [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]]. Players report a clinic with yellow chemical lockers and a keypad door whose code is unknown [[src-gamerfuzion-irradiated-container-2026-10-27]], and a bedroom safe [[src-allthingshow-hajin-gameplay-2026-10-21]]. For crafting, the 3D Printer points to the Rice Mill for Stock Rods and to the farm's storage tents for Volatile Explosives (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]].
