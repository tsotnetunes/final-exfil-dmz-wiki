Hajin City is the ruined urban east of the map: four major POIs, [[nurigo-mall]], [[the-grid]], [[songdo-wharf]] and [[wolves-stadium]], with the housing of [[hajin-river-heights]] to the southeast [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The June intel called it the Hajin City remains [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]].

## How dangerous is it?

Threat ranges from Low on the Songdo waterfront to Extreme in the Nenshin Tech Complex at the centre of The Grid [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The Commanders favour the city: Colossus and the Tank are met most often in The Grid and the Deathstalker over downtown (reported) [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. Downtown SAM sites shoot at helicopters, so pilots should give them room (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. Roze holds the DBN tower in The Grid [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]].

## How do you get around?

An elevated monorail, the Tram, loops around the city, and trains still run along the wharf ([[vehicles]]) [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## What loot is there?

Police stations hold tactical gear, hospitals medical supplies and residential districts crafting resources [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]] ([[loot-placement]]). Four of the five Hanex Bank vaults are in the city, at NuriGO West, Songdo Wharf and two at Wolves Stadium ([[bank-vaults]]) [[src-gamerfuzion-gold-bar-route-2026-10-29]]; police stations stand at NuriGO Mall and Wolves Stadium ([[police-stations]]) [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]], and a gun store at Songdo Wharf [[src-allthingshow-gun-store-key-2026-10-22]].

## Where do you exfil?

Loot routes through the city often end at a helicopter exfil inside it (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-gold-bar-route-2026-10-29]] [[src-gamerfuzion-irradiated-container-2026-10-27]]; there is also a rooftop Fulton on NuriGO Mall and a site on Songdo Pier [[src-allthingshow-exfil-guide-2026-10-21]].
