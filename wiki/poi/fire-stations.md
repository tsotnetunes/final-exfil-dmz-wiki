Fire Stations are the red buildings on the [[tac-map]] and the place to look for radiation gear and parachutes; expect enemies inside. On the old North Korean side they are brick, on the South Korean side modern [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The developers said limited-use parachutes were made easier to find, naming Fire Stations [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]].

A community map pins six fire stations in all, with a Fire Station Storage key room at the one by Imjin Farmland (reported) [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]]; the sixth station's place is not confirmed yet.

## What loot is there?

Radiation gear and parachutes are the official draw [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. At the Cheongun Village station one player found a Durable Gas Mask (purple) and a Radiation Blockers recipe, with the Rare Fire Station Storage key lying on a desk; storage rooms have yellow Chemical Storage lockers that can hold an Irradiated Container, and restrooms and a roof weapon crate sometimes pay well (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-irradiated-container-2026-10-27]]. Radiation Blockers are printed at the 3D Printer and protect you in irradiated areas [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## Why does it matter?

You need radiation protection and a gas mask at the [[haneul-nuclear-reactor]] and when the [[radiation-storm]] closes in. Since a fix of 23 Oct 2026, gas masks no longer break the moment you enter radiation, and Disposable Parachutes have three uses [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Item details: [[parachute]], [[purple-gas-mask]], [[hazmat-suit]].

## Tips

Guards upstairs may open doors on you as you climb (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-irradiated-container-2026-10-27]]. Check the HUD's squad panel to see who already carries a gas mask and a parachute before you split up [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
