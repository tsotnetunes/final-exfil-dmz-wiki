Police Stations are the blue buildings on the [[tac-map]]: expect guards and many rooms, sometimes a roof to watch from, military gear, and a locked Armory that needs a key [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. There are five on Hajin, a count a player and a community map agree on [[src-gameshorizon-police-stations-2026-10-21]] [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]].

## How do you open the Armory?

One Uncommon Police Station Armory key opens the armory of any station; look for it in the offices upstairs, where it is not always in the same spot [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]]. The Epic Police Weapon Depository key opens the auxiliary armory north of the Wolves Stadium station [[src-dmzhub-keys-2026-10-31]] [[src-gameshorizon-police-stations-2026-10-21]]. See [[keys]].

## What loot is there?

Rooms hold plates, weapons, ammo, gear and healing items; the armory holds better gear [[src-gameshorizon-police-stations-2026-10-21]]. One Cheongun armory gave a rifle case, a military bag of plates and a weapons locker, and a Wolves Stadium medicine cabinet held a Drill Charge recipe (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-vault-run-2026-10-25]] [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-compound-run-2026-10-26]].

## Tips

Clear the main floors before you loot and check corners for hidden guards; a door locked from inside means another way in by stairs or roof [[src-gameshorizon-police-stations-2026-10-21]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].
