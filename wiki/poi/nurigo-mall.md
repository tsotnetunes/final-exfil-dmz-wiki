NuriGO Mall is the shopping district in the northeast of Hajin City, Medium threat in NuriGO East and High in NuriGO West, built over a large unlit underground mall where you need NVGs [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. It is the stage of Operation Shopping Hacks and holds a bank vault, a police station and a rooftop Fulton exit [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]] [[src-gamerfuzion-gold-bar-route-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-exfil-guide-2026-10-21]].

## Where is it?

Northeast Hajin City, former South Korean side, north of [[the-grid]], with tenements on its west edge near [[mirae-general-hospital]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How dangerous is it?

| Area | Threat |

[[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. NuriGO West's bank branch is guarded by heavy AI with riot shields (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-gold-bar-route-2026-10-29]].

## What is there?

A police station, a clinic, City Hall and a monorail station on the tram loop, parking above and below ground, and a courtyard whose pavilion and two light wells (one with an elevator) lead down to the underground mall [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. After launch the community map adds Han-Go Korean BBQ, a rooftop Bounty Station near NuriGO East, a fire station, three gas stations, Ammo and Armor Caches, a UAV tower and a safe in a tent (reported) [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]]. The Hanex Bank branch in NuriGO West has a coded vault ([[bank-vaults]]) [[src-gamerfuzion-gold-bar-route-2026-10-29]].

## Who and which missions are here?

Operation Shopping Hacks has you hack a network in the mall and secure evidence; its briefing places the Lieutenant Jang-Ui's camp in the shopping district [[src-dmzhub-missions-2026-10-31]] ([[operation-shopping-hacks]]). Two guides meet Jang-Ui in the Nenshin Tech Complex in The Grid instead [[src-gfinity-lieutenant-locations-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. A fix of 24 Oct 2026 cut over-frequent AI reinforcements in the Shopping Mission, and one of 20 Oct fixed a missing prompt in the Chemical Denial Dynamic Op at the mall on Switch 2 [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]].

## Where do you exfil?

A green air-transport crate on the mall roof, reached by ascender cable and ladder, gives a Fulton exit about 10 seconds after you activate it (reported) [[src-allthingshow-exfil-guide-2026-10-21]]; helicopter sites are mapped nearby too [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]]. See [[exfil]].
