The Haneul Nuclear Reactor is the breached nuclear power plant on Hajin's northeast coast: High threat with permanent radiation, so you need radiation protection and a gas mask to go in [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. It is the June intel's "fallout reactor" [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]], home to the Lieutenant called The Chemist and the Story Mission Operation Fallen Ember [[src-gfinity-dmz-lieutenants-2026-10-22]] [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-compound-run-2026-10-26]].

## Where is it?

Northeast Hajin, former South Korean side, on the coast north of [[songdo-wharf]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Compound Delta sits beside it to the southwest, with a tunnel into the plant; the other way in is by water [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-guide-2026-10-26]] ([[compounds]]).

## How do you survive the radiation?

Wear a gas mask: since 23 Oct 2026 masks no longer break the moment you enter radiation [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]], but the red zone still eats them fast, so carry spares [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]]. In one run a purple mask lasted about three minutes and a blue one about half that; Radiation Blockers help, and players say a Hazmat Suit makes the area safe (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-reactor-02-2026-10-27]] [clm_f655e133d8dd]. See [[radiation-storm]] and [[fire-stations]].
