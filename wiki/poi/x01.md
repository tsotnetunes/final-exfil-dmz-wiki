X01 is a Black Site that opened with Season 1 on 6 Nov 2026 at 00:00 ET, the first new location since launch [[src-cod-x-season-1-live-2026-11-06]]. The Season 1 patch notes of 5 Nov 2026 call it a new Exclusion Zone: an underground GRANIT Arms facility beneath Hajin that you discover from inside Hajin, with its own new areas, threats, equipment and DMZ Orders [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]]. Infinity Ward also places it at the edge of Hajin, near North Korea's northern border, recently seized by enemy forces [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [clm_64b8588379af] [clm_c555c9265f3f] [clm_3bf7a7ace0aa]. Official teasers of 3 and 4 Nov 2026 add Ghost on site and high-tier rewards [[src-cod-x-x01-season-1-2026-11-04]]. Call of Duty's announcement adds that North Korean forces hold this former Russian facility in the northern reaches of the Exclusion Zone, heavily fortified [clm_88179a380fbb].

## How do you get to X01?

Two ways since 6 Nov 2026. At first you deployed into Hajin as usual and travelled to X01's infiltration point [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]; one player puts the entrance in the north west of the map (reported) [clm_a6959777efc7]. From 5 PM PST on 6 Nov 2026 Infinity Ward also opened a direct deployment from the FOB to X01, warning of heavy resistance [[src-cod-x-season-1-live-2026-11-06]] [clm_91beda4a2e39] [clm_335123e1ad31] ([[deploy]]). One player says the X01 Orders only count while you are deployed into X01 itself (reported) [clm_46b9cbca3827]. One outlet says the way in is an elevator, and using it starts a countdown: squadmates who miss it stay in Hajin (reported) [clm_a66709e01ef4] [clm_3d4c81c4b97c]. The post describes it as a facility that once belonged to Russia, sitting on Hajin's border [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]; Hajin's own Russian side stays closed until Season 2 [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] ([[hajin-exclusion-zone]]).

## What is inside?

A dim maze of passages, tunnels and linked rooms around a secret weapons assembly line, fully closed off from outside and spread over several floors; the post names cargo bays, vehicle depots, underground labs and production lines. A sealed Deep Vault waits inside. X01 holds equipment found nowhere else in DMZ, but the new items and enemy types are still secret [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. The patch notes confirm one thing players can plan around: X01 has a 3D Printer of its own inside the location, and everything else about the site stays marked classified [[src-mw4-season-1-patch-notes-2026-11-05]] ([[3d-printer]]). A community database names a Main Warehouse with a raised platform leading to a Superintendent's Office, opened by an Epic key ([[keys]]), and lists a Season 1 valuable found here, the Ceramic Pup of Loyalty, worth 6,000 ([[valuables]]) (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-07]]. Since 7 Nov 2026 the same database also lists an Ultra Vault DRC Locker Key for a DRC weapons locker inside the Deep Vault, and a Season 1 Field Upgrade found in X01, the Hazmat Box, which hands out radiation protection to a squad ([[keys]], [[field-upgrades]]) (reported) [[src-dmzhub-items-2026-11-08]].

## Who guards it?

Troops and specialists of the 100 Spirits faction, rival Operator squads and tougher patrolling entities. The new Commander The Captain, a Juggernaut with an elite guard, is found here, and the Threat Briefing: The Captain event asks you to collect sections of The Captain's helmet and exfil with them [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [clm_93fc23c7a474] ([[commander-threats]]). Ghost, acting as the Watcher, hands out the new DMZ Orders for X01 [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] ([[dmz-orders]]).

## How do you get out?

Exfil points close off as the facility progresses, and you must finish set steps to escape; the post advises always knowing where the nearest exfil is [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. See [[exfil]].

## Where does the name come from?

The developers first named X01 in their 9 Oct 2026 Q&A as the first map after launch, filling the role Building 21 had in the 2022 DMZ [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; Infinity Ward's own video described a Season 1 map in the style of Building 21 [[src-gamespot-from-the-ward-ep7-2026-10-05]] [clm_c063b7d057fd], and some sites wrote it as XL1 [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

Since 6 Nov 2026 Infinity Ward has been investigating a failed move from Hajin into X01 that can cost some players their Operator, loadout and Backpack; there is no fix date yet [[src-cod-x-season-1-live-2026-11-06]]. One player also reports a connection time-out while riding the elevator down to the Deep Vault (reported) [clm_1d86cd054a17].
