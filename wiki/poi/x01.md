X01 is DMZ's first new location, arriving with Season 1 on 6 Nov 2026 at 00:00 ET: a hidden weapons facility, probably a former GRANIT Arms black site, recently seized by enemy forces at the edge of Hajin near North Korea's northern border [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] [clm_c555c9265f3f]. Call of Duty's announcement adds that North Korean forces hold this former Russian facility in the northern reaches of the Exclusion Zone, heavily fortified [clm_88179a380fbb].

## How do you get to X01?

You deploy into Hajin as usual and travel to X01's infiltration point [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. The post calls it a former Russian facility on the edge of Hajin's borders [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]; Hajin's own Russian side stays closed until Season 2 [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]] ([[hajin-exclusion-zone]]).

## What is inside?

A dim maze of passages, tunnels and linked rooms around a secret weapons assembly line, fully closed off from outside and spread over several floors; the post names cargo bays, vehicle depots, underground labs and production lines. A sealed Deep Vault waits inside. X01 holds equipment found nowhere else in DMZ, but the new items and enemy types are still secret [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]].

## Who guards it?

Troops and specialists of the 100 Spirits faction, rival Operator squads and tougher patrolling entities. The new Commander The Captain, a Juggernaut with an elite guard, is found here, and the Threat Briefing: The Captain event asks you to collect sections of The Captain's helmet and exfil with them [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]] ([[commander-threats]]).

## How do you get out?

Exfil points close off as the facility progresses, and you must finish set steps to escape; the post advises always knowing where the nearest exfil is [[src-activision-season-1-announcement-2026-10-28]]. See [[exfil]].

## Where does the name come from?

The developers first named X01 in their 9 Oct 2026 Q&A as the first map after launch, filling the role Building 21 had in the 2022 DMZ [[src-westie-dmz-ama-recap-2026-10-11]]; Infinity Ward's own video described a Season 1 map in the style of Building 21 [[src-gamespot-from-the-ward-ep7-2026-10-05]] [clm_c063b7d057fd], and some sites wrote it as XL1 [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

Infinity Ward's Season 1 post also promises a whole new Exclusion Zone to explore in DMZ [clm_75bb19ee9527]; whether that means X01 itself or more is not confirmed yet. Also not confirmed: what the Deep Vault holds, the steps to escape, and whether X01 has its own matchmaking queue. See [[season-1]].
