The biggest bunker in Hajin is the one under [[chang-san-air-base]], an Extreme-threat network larger than the base above it [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]; smaller bunkers are spread over the west and around the reactor. The official guide's rule for all of them: a door locked from the inside has another way in, and the countryside hides underground entrances [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## How do you open locked bunker doors?

Each locked room takes its own key or card; the card's description names its grid square on the [[tac-map]] ([[keys]]). A Door Breacher Charge opens most locked doors but not keycard doors, gun lockers or the Supreme Leader's Bunker [[src-gfinity-door-breacher-2026-10-27]].

## Is it worth it?

The air base bunker is the most dangerous place on the map, so go in as a full squad with armour and spare ammo. The silo bunkers are the opposite: almost empty of enemies and of good loot, though players say bunkers hold sellable files that FOB upgrades need [clm_c712a62e76ae]. What the Supreme Leader's Bunker holds is not confirmed yet.
