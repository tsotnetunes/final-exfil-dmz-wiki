Chang-san Air Base is the North Korean air base in the west of Hajin: Critical threat on the surface and Extreme in the bunker beneath it, the most dangerous ground on the map [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. It is the large military base of the June intel [[src-activision-dmz-deep-dive-2026-06]], home to the Lieutenant Bale and the Operation Iron Citadel Story Mission [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]] [[src-destructoid-best-loot-2026-10-25]].

## Where is it?

West Hajin, former North Korean side, built into a granite hillside. Cliffs guard the east; the wooded high ground to the south is the approach the official guide points to [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. The [[prison-complex]] lies to the north, with Compound Foxtrot between the two [[src-gamingpromax-compounds-2026-10-26]] [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]].

## How dangerous is it?

Air Base Exterior is Critical; the Air Base Bunker is Extreme [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]]. On the surface: walls and watchtowers, a hospital, a helipad, a jet runway, barracks, supply depots and a command building; Admin Complex No. 2 is one of its fortresses [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]] [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]]. Behind big bunker doors on the south side, tunnels lead into a bunker network larger than the base [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. If you fly, keep away: its SAM sites fire on helicopters (reported) [[src-codmunity-dmz-guide-2026-10-24]].

## Who is here?

Bale is fought in the Tank Depot, a hangar inside the bunker [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]] [[src-allthingshow-hajin-map-2026-10-21]]; two guides place him upstairs in the Re-Education Center at [[deadtown]] instead [[src-gfinity-lieutenant-locations-2026-10-22]] [[src-conquestcapped-dmz-bosses-2026-10-28]]. See [[bale]].

## What missions are here?

Operation Iron Citadel is played here [[src-destructoid-best-loot-2026-10-25]]. It involves an Airbase Access Card, which a fix of 28 Oct 2026 lets you upgrade while it sits in a Secure Slot [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]], and ends with flying out the armory manifest from near the base fence after capturing a SAM site (reported) [[src-allthingshow-exfil-guide-2026-10-21]]. Its reward includes the Supreme Leader's Bunker Access Card [[src-dexerto-supreme-leaders-bunker-card-2026-10-24]]. During the mission, three houses near a marked entrance gave Weapon Manuals and blueprints almost every time (reported) [[src-destructoid-best-loot-2026-10-25]]. See [[story-missions]].
