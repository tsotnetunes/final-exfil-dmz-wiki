## How do you open a safe?

It depends on the safe. A compound's safe opens once the compound is cleared (reported) [[src-gamingpromax-compounds-2026-10-26]]; a bank vault needs a powered reader, a keycard and a code ([[bank-vaults]]). Some Dynamic Ops hide keypad codes in safes; a fix of 20 Oct 2026 stopped those safes from appearing inside locked rooms [[src-mw4-launch-patch-notes-2026-10-29]]. Early access players also met a Crack Safe task that drills a locked safe open (reported) [[src-allthingshow-hajin-gameplay-2026-10-21]].

## What do safes hold?

Reported so far: Infantry Invasion Plans, Regional Combat Notes, Nuclear Program Files and Encrypted Archives (valuables that feed FOB upgrades), Weapon Manuals, blueprints, a Drill Charge recipe, Gold Bars and cash [[src-gamingpromax-compounds-2026-10-26]] [[src-gamerfuzion-solo-compound-run-2026-10-26]] [[src-gamerfuzion-gold-bar-route-2026-10-29]] [[src-allthingshow-hajin-gameplay-2026-10-21]]. No loot table has been published. Safes also count toward safe-opening [[dmz-orders]].
