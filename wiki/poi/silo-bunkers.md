Hajin has six missile silo bunkers, all in the west of the map; their hatches are always open, a few guards at most watch them, and the loot inside is random and mostly common [[src-techwiser-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]] [[src-xboxplay-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]]. Before launch the official guide already mentioned hidden missile bunkers around [[chang-san-air-base]] [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Where are the silo bunkers?

The two guides agree on these six places; grid squares given by guides disagree, so none are listed here [[src-techwiser-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]] [[src-xboxplay-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]].

| 4 | Ridge west of the air base, outside its fence, facing the runway | [[chang-san-air-base]] |
| 5 | South of the air base, below the runway near fuel tanks | [[chang-san-air-base]] |
| 6 | Mountain pass southwest of Deadtown, at the western map edge | [[deadtown]] |

The map does not name them: look for a circle on the [[tac-map]] or minimap, with the entrance just outside it [[src-techwiser-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]] [[src-xboxplay-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]].

## How do you get in?

Walk in through the concrete hatch, which is never locked. Inside, one metal door says it is secured from the inside: take the first left off the main corridor and enter the same room through its open back door [[src-xboxplay-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]] [[src-techwiser-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]]. This matches the official rule that a door locked from inside always has another way in [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. At the end of the corridors a ladder and a button open the silo hatch, a quick way back to the surface (reported) [[src-techwiser-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]].

## What loot is inside?

Bags, cabinets, shelves, a cache and military lockers with weapons, backpacks and field upgrades. Most of it is common, with an occasional rare weapon, recipe or Weapon Manual [[src-techwiser-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]] [[src-xboxplay-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]]. Expect one AI enemy, rarely more.

## What is in the Silo Control Room?

A community map pins six silos and a Silo Control Room at three of them (reported) [[src-dmzhub-maps-2026-10-31]]. It is a locked room at the back that only a Door Breacher Charge of Ultra rarity opens [[src-xboxplay-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]] [[src-techwiser-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]]; players also say a red-rarity charge is the only way they know to open the bunker door by the air base [clm_17a343625b92]. Inside stands a nuclear missile with a control panel that does nothing, the "hidden nuke" players talk about [clm_97324b4d4a3d], and very little loot. Both guides advise keeping the charge for a locked room in a major POI.
