Deadtown is an abandoned North Korean farm town in the southwest of Hajin: Low threat in its streets and Medium in its two Re-Education Centers, where the enemy gathers [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]].

## Where is it?

Southwest Hajin, former North Korean side, between toxic rivers, with small hamlets running northeast along the railway [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. One of the [[silo-bunkers]] hides in the mountain pass southwest of town, at the edge of the map [[src-techwiser-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]] [[src-xboxplay-silo-bunkers-2026-10-27]].

## How dangerous is it?

| Area | Threat |
| --- | --- |
| Deadtown Exterior | Low |
| Re-Education Center | Medium |

The town has been taken over by the military: expect street fighting against lightly armoured soldiers and road convoys [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. In one launch-week run the Deathstalker's warning banner showed over Deadtown (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-irradiated-container-2026-10-27]]; see [[commander-threats]].

## What is there?

A sewer system under the main buildings, newer tenement blocks on the east side hiding military storage, a clinic and two gas stations, one at either end of town [[src-activision-dmz-guide-part1-2026-10-05]]. Maps made after launch add a fire station northeast of town by the railway, Ammo and Armor Caches, a UAV tower, a Lieutenant Hunt tower and an exfil site [[src-allthingshow-hajin-map-2026-10-21]] [[src-codmunity-hajin-map-2026-10-31]]. Deadtown also has a SAM site you can capture, one of the four that guides name ([[capture-sam-site]]) [[src-dmztracker-dynamic-side-ops-2026-10-20]] [[src-allthingshow-dmz-orders-2026-10-31]]. The clinic's locker room, behind a sign over a doorway off the main corridor, has yellow chemical lockers; one held an Irradiated Container (reported) [[src-gamerfuzion-irradiated-container-2026-10-27]] ([[clinics]]).
