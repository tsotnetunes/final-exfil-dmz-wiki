# ConquestCapped: MW4 DMZ keys

Captured: 2026-10-31 (updated 28 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Most keys are gone as soon as their door opens; keys do not stack.
- A key kept in a Secure Slot (a Medium Backpack has one) returns to your Stash if you die before using it; a key anywhere else stays on your body for others to take. Keys in the Stash do nothing until you move them into your loadout before deploying.
- Keys sell for about 100 DMZ Cash, so opening the room is usually worth more. A Scout-tree Trait named Key Cash pays DMZ Cash for each locked room you open.
- Where keys come from: Story Missions, Lieutenants, Survival Kits and random containers (duffel bags, jackets on coat racks, military bags, lockers, suitcases on the train); key rooms often hold another key.
- Counting Room Vault (Epic): the casino's main floor, F4; from Operation Silent Ledger or random loot; two stacked safes, two weapon containers and loose cash inside.
- Vault Armory (Epic): a caged room in the casino's underground car park, F4; random loot, found loose in the casino and in duffel bags; weapon lockers, a rifle case, military bags, a chance of a Weapon Manual.
- Gun Store Weapons Locker Key (Rare): gun store basements in west Cheongun Village (F4) and Songdo Wharf (I6); in the Explosives Equipment Kit (DMZ level 16) and random loot; one key opens one of the three lockers.
- Police Station Armory: the locked basement armory of any Police Station; its key lies on a desk or shelf in the same station; also in the Recon Equipment Kit (level 19).
- Fire Station Storage (Rare): second floor of the large fire stations; key on the ground floor of the same station.
- Mirae Electrical Room (Rare): lowest car park level under Mirae General Hospital, F5; Operation Critical Extraction or random loot.
- Coin Mining Den (Rare): third floor of an apartment east of the westernmost gas station by NuriGO Mall, G6; Operation Shopping Hacks or random loot.
- Bank vault access card (Rare): the vaults of Hanex Bank branches in Cheongun Village and by Wolves Stadium; found on the same bank's ground floor.
- The Citadel South Wing (Uncommon): the outer door of the South Wing, 14th Political Prison; prison guards carry it often.
- The Citadel Main Control Room (Uncommon): an officers' door in The Citadel; the key sits in the room behind the K9 Core Terminal in the East Wing, which K9 Battery Cores open.
- South Block Armory (Legendary): the inner armory of The Citadel; key on a counter in the Main Control Room; their runs found weapons from blue to Legendary, a Gold Skull and an item worth 10,000.
- Commandant Office (Epic): second floor of Admin Complex No. 2, Chang-san Air Base, B5; random loot.
- Air Base Access Card - Multi Use (Epic): used at the server room terminal in Admin Complex No. 2 (B5); only from Operation Iron Citadel; the terminal turns it into a single-use Air Base Armory Keycard; the armory also needs a six-digit code.
- Supreme Leader's Bunker Access Card (Legendary): the bunker under Chang-san Air Base, B6; reward of Operation Iron Citadel, and players report Bale dropping it; one use; private rooms, a locker corridor, a Lieutenant crate, a Legendary LMG in every run reported, and WMD valuables.
- The Chemist's Secure Armory (Legendary): the right-hand bunker door by the boathouse at the Haneul Nuclear Reactor, I4; dropped by The Chemist.
- Reactor DRC Locker (Ultra): a locker in the flooded pool under Reactor 02; reward of Operation Fallen Ember; one Ultra item (a 3-plate vest in their example) plus random loot, sometimes a Weapon Manual.
- The Irradiated Container works like a key; the Door Breacher Charge is a one-use door opener, not a key.
