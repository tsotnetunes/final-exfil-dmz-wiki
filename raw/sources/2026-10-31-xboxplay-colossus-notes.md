Facts taken, in our words:
- Met outside the 14th Political Prison wall and into the Citadel, near the Cheonwang Peak Array gate, in the Chang-san Air Base Armory (Operation Iron Citadel) and in the Haneul reactor yard (Operation Fallen Ember). Listed on the Boss Board under Commanders with the Death Stalker and the Tank.
- Armour suit: immune to grenades, launchers and breaching charges; its plates break under sustained fire.
- Attacks: steady automatic fire down corridors; a blue electric shock up close that travels through metal and vehicles; green gas clouds; its back canister bursts into toxic gas when it dies.
- Tactics: high-capacity automatic weapons at close to medium range; a Signal 50 from high ground; use a parked military truck for cover in the Citadel; fire down from the Armory stair landing; aim at the upper body.
- Reward $50,000; an assist paid 1,075 XP; banner ELIMINATED ARMORED COMMANDER.
