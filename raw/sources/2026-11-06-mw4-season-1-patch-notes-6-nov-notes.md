# Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 patch notes: the 6 Nov 2026 section
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes
Captured: 2026-11-07 (from the app's patch reader, read at 21:00 UTC on 6 Nov 2026)

Facts taken, in our words:
- A small update went live on 6 Nov 2026; Infinity Ward says more updates follow over the next days.
- UAV rule: Thermal Padding, Thermal Padding Pro and the Sedative Inhaler now hide you from UAVs and from UAV Towers that a rival with the UAV Upgrade Trait has made show direction. The AUAV fire support still finds players using any of the three.
- More DMZ exploits closed (no list).
- FOB Trait descriptions reworded for Auto Fill, Impact Smokes Pro, Thermal Padding Pro, UAV Upgrade and UAV Upgrade Pro (wording only).
- Progression fixes: Story Missions done in Hajin now count for the Upper Class Order; looting Lieutenant or player Backpacks now counts toward the Packing Calling Card Challenge; BlackCell Daily Rewards claimed in the FOB are now paid; exfils now count for the BlackCell Operator Challenges (Adder Xero skin, Exitium M4 Blueprint); the BlackCell Login Reward counter starts at Day 1, not Day 0.
- Gunsmith (listed under Multiplayer, applies to the DMZ Gunsmith): some Rocket Launcher Blueprints could not be bought; the Helix MK.II Apex on the Cronen Osprey Vanish Blueprint showed wrong costs and visuals.
- Live issues are tracked on the official Trello board and the CODUpdates account.
