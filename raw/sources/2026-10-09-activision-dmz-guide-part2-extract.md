## Early access and dates
- Campaign early access from Friday 16 Oct 2026; early access to all of DMZ from Tuesday 20 Oct 2026; full launch 23 Oct 2026.
- Both early accesses are for digital pre-orders or pre-purchases of the Standard Digital or Vault Edition on Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, PS5, Battle.net, Steam and Switch 2. Start times may vary by time zone and platform.

## FOB stations and when they open
- Open from the start: Operators (Active Duty), DMZ Orders, Stash/Loadout, Firing Range, Survival Kits, Bounty Leaderboard, DMZ Progression, Events, Deploy.
- 3D Printer at DMZ level 2; Boss Board after the second DMZ Order is completed; Vendor at level 5; Gunsmith at level 10; Dog Tag Case at level 20.
- The FOB grows into a full base as you deploy; stations are upgraded with the 3D Printer.

## Operators (Active Duty)
- Two Active Duty slots at the start, a third unlocked through DMZ levels; Vault Edition owners start with three.
- A recruited Operator stays in its slot until dismissed; the same Operator cannot be in two slots; skins can be changed.
- Bank, Stash, Resources and Ingredients are shared by all Active Duty Operators.
- Each Operator has a Dog Tag with 18 levels: Steel, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Damascus, Onyx, each I to III. Every Dog Tag level gives one Trait Point; a good deployment can give more than one level. Operator level and DMZ player level are separate.
- The Dog Tag shows the Operator's Bounty and Operator kill count. Another player can take it after killing you.

## MIA
- An Operator killed in a deployment goes MIA and cannot deploy until recovered.
- Recovery costs DMZ Cash from the Bank and keeps all Traits; the cost rises with the Operator's Trait Points; the Operator returns without gear. Or dismiss them and recruit a new one.

## Traits
- Three trees: Warfighter, Scout, Survivalist. 66 Traits in all, 22 per tree: 6 Major Traits (always visible) and 16 Minor Traits (random, shown as you unlock the Major Trait below them).
- The first point in a tree buys a random Minor Trait; then one Trait per branch (Major branches of two, Minor branches of three), up to nine per tree. Picks are permanent.
- 18 Trait Points per Operator: enough to complete two trees or spread over three.
- Examples (six Major plus the random first Minor per tree):
  - Warfighter: Adrenaline Burst (a kill resets tactical sprint); Equipment Professional (slowly recharges equipped Lethals and Tacticals); Combat Resistance (less explosive damage and weaker enemy Tactical effects); Viper Sense (hidden from thermal optics and AI targeting); Quick Fix (shorter health regeneration delay after killing an enemy Operator or AI); Possum (once per match, self revive into Last Stand from death with full health); Adaptive Restock (AI enemies drop one extra item).
  - Scout: Investor (slightly more cash when extracting); Hostile Looter (AI sometimes drop an Uncommon printer ingredient, rarely a Rare one); Container Looter (chance of an extra item in containers); Thermal Padding (hidden from UAVs, Advanced UAVs show you as a normal UAV would); UAV Upgrade (longer range and duration, upgraded to Advanced UAV); Overwatch (UAV towers you activate never expire); Field Crafting (craft from Common printer resources while deployed).
  - Survivalist: Air Superiority (UAV towers and SAM sites last longer); Faster Revive; Consumable Duration (consumables last longer); Impact Smokes (smoke when downed); Free Revive (your first Wound is repaired automatically); Numb the Pain (revives also give Painkillers and Smelling Salts effects); Die Hard (in Last Stand, reaching an exfil calls it automatically; no bleed-out timer in Last Stand).

## Operator Prestige
- Possible once an Operator has earned 18 Trait Points and is not MIA; optional; separate from the 1 to 55 player level, the 1 to 70 DMZ level and other MW4 prestige.
- First Prestige: two weapons (Rare or better, three attachment slots), a Rare plate carrier, a Rare backpack, four Rare 3D Printer Ingredients, 200,000 DMZ Cash. Second: two weapons with four attachment slots, Epic plate carrier, backpack and four ingredients, 300,000. Third and later: Legendary versions, 500,000.
- Prestiging consumes the gear and cash and resets the Traits; the Operator gains one random permanent Prestige Trait that stays with them even if dismissed, moved or reskinned. Maximum three.
- From the fourth Prestige on: a new permanent Trait is offered; swap it for one of the three or keep the three.
- Prestiging several Operators earns calling cards and other rewards not yet revealed.

## DMZ Orders
- Organised in pages; they onboard you, upgrade Operator and FOB, carry the story and set challenges; rewards are resources, loot and cash; always available.

## Stash and loadout
- Stash holds 40 items and 12 weapons at first (counted separately); keeps weapons, gear, consumables, ingredients, keys and valuables; sort by type or rarity, merge stacks.
- Four Resources (Synthetics, Electronics, Chemicals, Metals) are broken down automatically when looted and take no Stash slots.
- Without a backpack you carry the Basic Pack with 8 slots; every backpack has a separate pouch for Resources. Better backpacks add slots, weapon slots and Secure Slots; anything outside Secure Slots is lost on a failed exfil.
- Loadout slots: DMZ Cash (empty before infil), Armor (plates filled at infil; more only via Traits or the Armor Box field upgrade), Dog Tag, Headgear, Self-Revive, two weapon slots, Plate Carrier, Tactical, Lethal, Backpack, Field Upgrade, Fire Support, Mission Item (filled automatically during missions), Parachute, Melee.

## Firing Range
- Between the Gunsmith and the Vendor; walls for crouching, prone and mounting; round and bottle targets from under 10 m to over 100 m.

## Survival Kits
- Bundles such as Deployment, Triage, Armor, Equipment and SERE kits; opening one moves the contents to the Stash; unopened kits stack in the station.
- Earned from DMZ levels, DMZ Orders and missions, and Events.
- Vault Edition Deployment Kit: two each of Weapon Insurance Contracts, Operator Rescue Beacons, Fulton Drop Markers, Medium Backpacks and 2-Plate Carriers.

## Bounties
- Killers: killing players earns a Bounty at once, shown on the Dog Tag; it grows across deployments; factors include who fired first and being outnumbered. Four player kills make you Wanted; nearby Operators are alerted when a Wanted Operator comes close. Several Wanted levels; the highest are Most Wanted.
- Dying loses your Bounty and Wanted status. Other ways to lose it: exfil without your Dog Tag after another player took it and extracted, or pay it off with DMZ Cash at a Bounty Station.
- Hunters: at a Bounty Station in Hajin, the list of Wanted Operators in the deployment is free; paid intel (dearer for higher Wanted levels) shows a circle around the target that shrinks and updates faster the more Wanted they are. At the top level the Killer's area may be visible to everyone.
- Claiming: kill the target and extract with their Dog Tag; the payout follows their Bounty level, with an extra increase at maximum Wanted level.
- Bounty Leaderboard station: top 50 Most Wanted (status and Bounty) and top 50 Bounty Hunters (bounties claimed, DMZ Cash earned), updated weekly, global or friends.

## 3D Printer
- Unlocks at DMZ level 2. Crafts gear and some ingredients and prints FOB upgrades.
- Recipes come from DMZ levels, DMZ Orders and looting; a looted Recipe is consumed at exfil, added to the printer and pays 5,000 DMZ Cash (Weapon Manuals pay the same).
- Ingredient examples: Binding Catalyst, Biomaterials, Detonators, Control Units. Track (pin) recipes and ingredients; the printer suggests where to find an ingredient.
- First recipes at level 2: Bandage (15 Synthetics, 15 Chemicals); Medkit (30 Chemicals, 1 Binding Catalyst, 5 Biomaterials); 1-Plate Armor Vest (25 Synthetics, 25 Metals); 2-Plate Armor Vest (50 Synthetics, 5 Reactive Metals); Small Backpack (25 Synthetics, 25 Metals); Medium Backpack (50 Synthetics, 5 Rigid Synthetics; has Secure Slots).
- Permanent FOB upgrades, each needing several prints: Bank, Stash item capacity, MIA recovery discounts, Stash weapon capacity, printing rarer ingredients; also extra Dog Tag Case storage, Gunsmith discounts and more Vendor options.
- Categories: Upgrades, Consumables, Plate Carriers, Backpacks, Tacticals, Lethals, Field Upgrades, Fire Support, Gear (gas masks, NVGs, parachutes), Ingredients.

## Boss Board
- Opens after the second DMZ Order. Lieutenant intel costs DMZ Cash, is unlocked by challenges in earlier deployments and lasts one deployment. Commander information is free; no intel can be bought for Commanders.
- Lieutenants: unique loadouts, sometimes escorts; their weapon is a rare drop; they can drop a key to their own locked location; they always drop a Lieutenant Dog Tag Case, and picking it up broadcasts your position. Extracting with the Case (from your kill or taken from another player) progresses the Boss Board. A Lieutenant Hunt tower (Upload Station at the top) marks the nearest Lieutenant. More rewards for repeated Lieutenant kills, not yet revealed. The guide shows three Lieutenants in images without naming them.
- Commanders: always present; shown on the Tac-Map when close, and your Team Leader warns you; the first kill of each Commander pays DMZ Cash; killing one and extracting yields valuable ingredients. Named: Colossus (a seven-foot armoured soldier with a minigun firing depleted uranium rounds), Deathstalker (a helicopter-sized autonomous drone with two machine-gun turrets; one of four enemy drone types), and a North Korean battle tank.

## Vendor
- Level 5. Better gear as your DMZ level rises; Vendor upgrades printed at the 3D Printer add offers; stock refreshes periodically.

## Gunsmith
- Level 10. Weapon levels and shared attachment unlocks count across Multiplayer and DMZ; unlocked attachments carry over.
- DMZ costs DMZ Cash: base weapon, each attachment slot (dearer each time, any order) and each attachment; cosmetics are free except Blueprints.
- Buying a base weapon needs its Weapon Manual extracted first; then it can be bought as often as you like. Any extracted weapon can be customised as a single copy.
- Gunsmith builds take up to five attachments plus the Apex slot; weapons found in Hajin can have up to eight attachment slots.
- 32 launch weapons in eight classes: 8 assault rifles, 5 SMGs, 2 shotguns, 3 LMGs, 3 marksman rifles, 3 sniper rifles, 4 pistols, 4 launchers (no attachments). A base M4 costs about 5,000 DMZ Cash; the example M4 has 72 weapon levels.
- A Blueprint can be used once for free; if lost, it can be bought back at a discount. Vault Edition Signature Weapon Collection: Kastov 762, ISO Nightshade, Rezi 12, Oris 8.6 and a Combat Knife.
- Gunny can suggest a build. Weapon backpacks carry an extra weapon.
- Ways to get weapons: Free Loadout, Stash, Gunsmith, Weapon Manuals, Vendor, enemies and Lieutenants, slain players, military containers (police stations, compounds), Blueprints.

## Dog Tag Case
- Level 20. Shows each extracted tag's rarity (Steel to Onyx), the victim, the killer, and time and date. DMZ levels raise bounty payouts; 3D Printer upgrades add slots.

## Progression
- DMZ level 1 to 70; XP from Story Missions, Dynamic Operations, Side Ops and other activities; kept even on a failed exfil. DMZ XP also counts toward the 1 to 55 MW4 player level; Multiplayer and Warzone XP do not count toward the DMZ level.
- Rewards: station unlocks and upgrades (printer, an extra Active Duty slot, Bank capacity, Gunsmith discounts, Stash and weapon slots, Dog Tag Case payouts, Vendor offers), Recipes, Operators and skins, ingredients, Survival Kits, Fire Support.

## Events
- A station for limited-time challenges and unlocks when an Event is running.

## Deploy
- Story Missions: a list to complete or replay, briefing, rewards (usually high-value gear and DMZ Cash), a random-mission option, milestone rewards.
- Dynamic Operations and Free Roam infil options: on foot (free, random spot); Polaris Dagor A1 drop (5,000 DMZ Cash); LTV drop (25,000); helicopter from the FOB, one of you piloting (50,000); cargo plane drop at a spot you choose (100,000). Some need progression.
- Ready Up: Free Loadout (kept if you extract), edit loadout (suggested: plate carrier, backpack, weapon, Medkit), swap Active Duty Operator, Squad Fill on or off (fills your squad with two other players).
