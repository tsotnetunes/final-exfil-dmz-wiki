# TechWiser: CoD Modern Warfare 4 DMZ silo bunker locations

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 27 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Six missile silos, all on the west side of Hajin around Chang-san Air Base, not labelled on the map; look for white circular markers, the entrance sits just before each circle.
- Placement: on the north ridge of the 14th Political Prison overlooking Hanrim Village; on the prison's northeast edge by Revolution Dam; on a hillside just west of Tong-il Village; on the ridge west of the air base facing the runway; on a plateau southwest of the air base near fuel tanks; in a mountain pass southwest of Deadtown at the western map edge. (The article's grid squares are not used: they clash with other sources.)
- The entrance is always open. Inside, a door is locked from the inside; a back entrance into the same room is always open.
- Loot is random: equipment bags, cabinets, shelves, caches and military lockers with weapons, backpacks and field upgrades, mostly common. Few AI guards.
- A ladder and a button at the end open the silo hatch to the surface.
- The Silo Control Room needs a Door Breacher Charge; inside is a nuclear missile panel that does nothing.
