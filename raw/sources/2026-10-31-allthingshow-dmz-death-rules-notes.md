# All Things How: MW4 DMZ death rules, what you keep and lose when an Operator goes MIA
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=296285
Captured: 2026-10-31 (updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- When AI or another squad kills you, you return to the FOB and your Operator is marked Missing in Action.
- Kept: DMZ XP and your DMZ Player Level, the Stash, and items in Secure Slots; kept only if you rescue the Operator: their Traits, Trait Points and Dog Tag level.
- Lost: the loadout you took in (including a Free Loadout), backpack contents outside Secure Slots, and any Blueprint you carried into the match.
- An MIA Operator cannot deploy until rescued or dismissed; rescue costs DMZ Cash that rises with the Operator's Traits and returns them without gear; dismissing is free and the new Operator's Dog Tag starts again at Steel I.
- Squadmates can rescue an MIA teammate.
- Each Active Duty Operator has its own backpack and loadout; the Stash, DMZ Cash and crafting resources are shared by the account.
- An extracted weapon becomes a single Gunsmith copy; lose it and that weapon is locked again; extracting its Weapon Manual lets you buy it over and over.
- The Vault Edition kit includes Weapon Insurance Contracts and Operator Rescue Beacons; on 21 Oct 2026 nobody had published how they work.
