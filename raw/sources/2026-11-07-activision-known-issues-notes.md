Captured: 2026-11-07 (page shows the date 11/06/26, read as 6 Nov 2026)
Trust: official

Notes in our own words:
- Both DMZ entries now sit under resolved issues.
- DMZ Operator Prestige: marked resolved, with one line saying the Prestige problems have been dealt with. It does not say which faults, whether lost gear or DMZ Cash comes back, or when the fix shipped.
- DMZ Information Power Order: marked resolved; the Boss Board fault that stopped some players buying Lieutenant intel is fixed.
- Open issues, none DMZ only: Battle Pass Tier Skip Tokens that seem to do nothing (the total updates late), the Warfighter and Warfighter Prime skins disabled while investigated, a BlackCell clan tag that looks wrong, Steam crashes after the Season 1 update (verify files, then a clean reinstall), and a missing launch button in COD Home for Xbox on PC.
- Other resolved items: Campaign rewards, the Patriot XMR headshot challenge count, Multiplayer loadout saving, Combat Record and leaderboard stats.
