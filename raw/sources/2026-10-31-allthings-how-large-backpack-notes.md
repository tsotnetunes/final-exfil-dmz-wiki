Facts taken, in our words:
- Large Backpack: Legendary; 50,000 DMZ Cash at the FOB Vendor; sells for 5,000; its card shows 200 capacity against 50 for the basic pack; one weapon slot and two Secure Slots.
- Medium Scavenger Backpack: Epic, 25,000 at the Vendor, two Secure Slots, more resource room. Small Secure Backpack: Epic, 25,000, three Secure Slots.
- A Basic Restock Kit claimed at the Survival Kits station held two Medium Backpacks, an Armor Box and an Ammo Box.
- The Vendor opens at DMZ level 5 and shows a countdown to its next stock change.
