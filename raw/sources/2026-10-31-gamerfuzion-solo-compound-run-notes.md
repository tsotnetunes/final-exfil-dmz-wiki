# GamerFuzion: MW4 DMZ best solo loot run, compound safes and Weapon Manuals

Facts taken, in our words (one live solo run):
- Infil through Operation Fallen Ember for its spawn; the mission card lists the Haneul Nuclear Reactor, Extreme threat and 25,000 DMZ Cash.
- Route: Compound Delta next to the reactor (a car always spawns there; the safe is in a big canvas tent in the middle), Compound Echo in the River Heights apartments, the police station near Wolves Stadium (a key usually on an upstairs desk opens a room downstairs), Compound Bravo (by an exfil where players camp; safe in the tower office), Compound Alpha through the woods, Compound Charlie north past Imjin Suburb.
- Exfil at Cheongun Outskirts: helicopter to green smoke while guards push the site.
- Loot seen: Weapon Manuals (Hyeon Burst, M4) in compound safes, a Drill Charge recipe in a police station cabinet, a Reactor 02 Control Room key at Compound Bravo. Safe contents may be random.
- Radiation timer: about 12 minutes left at Compound Alpha, about 8 when heading to exfil.
