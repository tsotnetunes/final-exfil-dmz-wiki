# Windows Central: Welcome to Hajin, everything you need to know about Modern Warfare 4's new take on DMZ
URL: https://windowscentral.com/gaming/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-dmz-reveal
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 6 Jun 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- DMZ is a third core mode of MW4 that comes with buying the game; it is not a free part of Warzone. The 2022 DMZ beta, by contrast, came free with MW2 and Warzone 2.0.
- The base game and the Vault Edition both include DMZ; the Vault Edition was listed at 99.99 USD.
- MW4 was not expected in Game Pass Ultimate or PC Game Pass until at least a year after launch.
- Platforms named: Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, Battle.net, Steam, PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2; launch on Friday 23 Oct 2026.
- DMZ is PvPvE across story missions, dynamic missions and free roam; Squad Fill matches partial groups with players heading for the same task.
- Bringing back an MIA Operator costs cash that depends on the Operator's traits.
- Infinity Ward said MW4's single-player team wrote the story missions.
