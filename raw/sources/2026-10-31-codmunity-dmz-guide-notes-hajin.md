# CODMunity: MW4 DMZ beginner guide
URL: https://codmunity.gg/dmz/guide
Captured: 2026-10-31 (updated 24 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Radiation starts from a yellow circle marked at the start of a match and spreads out toward the map edge, which hints where the final exfil will be.
- Exfil boarding: alone, stepping on starts a 5-second countdown at once; in a squad the countdown runs 30 to 45 seconds until every member is aboard, then drops to 5 seconds.
- Flying a helicopter: stay clear of downtown SAM sites, Chang-san Air Base and any SAM site another squad has hacked; missiles hit almost instantly, so fire flares as soon as one launches.
- Ammo and armor caches refresh every 60 seconds. AI reinforcements can arrive by elevator.
- Radiation Blockers printed at the 3D Printer protect in irradiated areas; the reactor's red zone eats gas masks fast, so bring spares. Radiation gear and parachutes are found at fire stations.
- Hidden caches include a military duffel under a stairwell, chemical lockers and an irradiated pond.
- Season 1 adds a location compared to Building 21; Season 2 expands Hajin toward Russia; weather changes with the seasons.
