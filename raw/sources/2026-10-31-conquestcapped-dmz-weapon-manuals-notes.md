# Conquest Capped: MW4 DMZ Weapon Manuals
URL: https://conquestcapped.com/guides/call-of-duty/mw4-dmz-weapon-manuals/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 28 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Every launch gun has a Manual, launchers included (examples: Manual: Type 73, Manual: APOC-338).
- A Manual is used up when you extract; the weapon is then unlocked in the Gunsmith for good (its tooltip says it unlocks buying the weapon there) and you receive 5,000 DMZ Cash, as for a Recipe.
- Each copy you build still costs DMZ Cash; a base rifle such as the Han 86 or M4 costs roughly 5,000 to 6,000.
- Only the player who extracts with the Manual gets the unlock, so pass it to a squadmate before exfil if they need it.
- Extracting three Manuals of the weapon you have equipped counts toward its War Spoils camo; some players report extractions that did not count.
- No fixed spawns. Sources named: military containers (green crates and Weapons Lockers), safes, duffel bags, footlockers, toolboxes, medicine cabinets; Irradiated Containers processed at Reactor 02 in Haneul Nuclear Reactor reportedly give one almost every time; locked rooms such as police station armories, the Gun Store Weapons Locker and Gangbyeon Apartment B.
- A gun without its Manual shows a lock in Buy New Weapon even if it is maxed in Multiplayer.
- Temporary ways to have a gun: a looted gun you extract (lost when you die with it), a daily Vendor copy (also lost on death), a Blueprint (one free build, then discounted builds of that Blueprint; it does not unlock the base gun).
- Items in a Secure Slot survive death; the Medium Backpack has one.
