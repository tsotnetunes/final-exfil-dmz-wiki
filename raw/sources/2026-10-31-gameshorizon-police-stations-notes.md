Facts taken, in our words:
- The writer found five police stations on Hajin; they are marked on the Tac-Map with a white police-badge marker, and the buildings are blue.
- Sizes vary from small city stations to big multi-floor buildings with roof access; NuriGO Mall and Wolves Stadium are named as nearby areas.
- Rooms hold plates, weapons, ammo, gear and healing items; the locked armory, placed in the basement, holds better gear and needs a Police Station key or a related key such as the Police Weapon Depository key.
- Expect guards, including hidden ones; clear the main floors first. A door locked from inside can be reached by stairs, the roof or another entrance.
