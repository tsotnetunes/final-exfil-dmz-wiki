# GamerFuzion: Modern Warfare 4 needs VRR for Performance Mode on PS5 and Xbox
URL: https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-vrr-required-performance-mode-120hz-ps5-xbox-campaign-dmz/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 22 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- The site relays a post by the official Call of Duty Updates account on X, 20 Oct 2026: on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, Performance Mode in MW4 Campaign and DMZ needs VRR switched on; the same will apply to Warzone's Performance Mode from Season 1. Players stuck at 60Hz should check that their display supports 120Hz and VRR and turn VRR on.
- Relaying Digital Foundry (21 Oct): without VRR those consoles run at 60Hz; PS5 supports VRR only over HDMI 2.1.
