Facts taken, in our words:
- Four Operators unlock by DMZ Player Level and can be used in both DMZ and Multiplayer: Reece (Shadow Company) at level 4, Coffey (JSOF) at 9, Simone (Shadow Company) at 15, Hector (Valeria's Strike Cell) at 19.
- At the FOB Gunsmith, any weapon or attachment unlocked or levelled in Multiplayer or DMZ can be bought, given enough DMZ Cash.
- In DMZ a weapon is looted in a deployment, built from a Weapon Manual found in Hajin, or bought from the FOB Vendor.
- The page has nothing on DMZ camos or Double XP tokens.
