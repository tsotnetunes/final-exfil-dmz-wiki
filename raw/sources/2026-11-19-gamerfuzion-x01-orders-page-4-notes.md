# GamerFuzion: X01 Orders page 4 solo guide (10 Nov 2026)

Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- The Captain's banner names the Bio Lab, on the lower floor of X01 south of the Supply Depot, reached through the Logistics Tunnel and down a flight of stairs. The author found him in the gas tank room, the same room as the canister chamber, and killed him with a headshot through a doorway.
- His body holds the orange Deep Vault 301 keycard; picking it up completes the keycard objective. Door 301 inside the Deep Vault stays locked until the card is used; opening it gave 300 XP and a room of red lockers.
- The Bio Lab objective and the keycard stayed ticked after the run ended, so the author split the Captain and the vault into two runs.
- Core room: hold the red Unlock Core Room console (about 78 seconds under attack), then hold the Neutralize Bioweapon console on the round platform; Blix appears when the core room opens and drops a Dog Tag.
- Page 4 rewards: a 3-Plate Ballistic Gel Vest, an SR-16 Bio Agent Sample, the Staff Emergency Exfil Keycard recipe and 20,000 DMZ Cash.
