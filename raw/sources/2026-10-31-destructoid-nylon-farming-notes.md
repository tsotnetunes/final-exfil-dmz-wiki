Facts taken, in our words:
- Nylon Cloth turns up in Deadtown's underground, in bags in the Mujin Nightclub in the west wing of the Heavenly Luck Casino at Cheongun Village, and in the Citadel's main building at the 14th Political Prison, which the guide rates best for both nylons.
- Nylon Laminate is Epic, spawns rarely in the same places, and can be printed without limit after the Epic Ingredient Printing upgrade (100,000 DMZ Cash, 10 Binding Catalysts, 10 Printed Circuit Boards, 5 Military Composites); each unit takes 100 Synthetics and 3 Nylon Cloth.
