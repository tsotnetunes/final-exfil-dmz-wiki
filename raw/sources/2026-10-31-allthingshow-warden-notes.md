# AllThingsHow: How to Beat the Warden in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ (Location and Drops)
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=298188
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Lieutenant guarding the 14th Political Prison (High): met in a block's mess hall; can also be deeper in the Citadel (Critical).
- Uses a riot shield and fires around it; bullets into the shield are wasted.
- Armoured guards near him; clear the courtyard first using oil drums and corners; guards fire through smoke in the corridors.
- Squad tactic: one player keeps the shield turned toward them while the others flank and shoot his side at close range.
- Drops: Incarcerator (Ultra SMG on the X-58 NYX, $8,500) or a plain X-58 NYX, his riot shield, and a tag named Kang's Dog Tag.
- Side op Paid Intel: Kill Warden paid 500 XP; the kill showed 632 XP; the kill counts toward the Boss Board.
