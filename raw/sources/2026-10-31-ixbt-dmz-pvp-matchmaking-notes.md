# iXBT Games: Infinity Ward changed DMZ matchmaking in Modern Warfare 4, raising PvP priority
URL: https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/10/30/nu-zaplacte-infinity-ward-izmenila-sistemu-podbora-igrokov-v-dmz-dlia-modern-warfare-4-uveliciv-prioritet-pvp.html
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 30 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Relaying Charlie Intel, which cites its own sources: Infinity Ward raised the weight of PvP in DMZ matchmaking, so squads meet other squads more often whatever they queue for.
- Replaying a Story Mission you already finished now puts you in a regular lobby instead of a match with others on the same mission.
- Infinity Ward had not commented; whether the change is permanent or a test was not known.
