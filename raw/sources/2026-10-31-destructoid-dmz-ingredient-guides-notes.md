Facts taken, in our words:
- Comms Module: an Epic ingredient that spawns by chance, not in fixed spots. Look in computers on the top floors of buildings east of Deadtown (the writer's pick for early farming), and in computers and server hardware at Cheonwang Peak Array and the Nenshin Tech Complex in The Grid (the site writes Netshin). It can be printed from 100 Electronics and 3 CPUs once Epic Ingredient Printing is bought. Used by Uncommon Ingredient Printing, Stash Weapon Upgrade I (5), Advanced UAV (2) and the Fulton Drop Marker (3). This page gives Stash Weapon Upgrade I as 7 Reactive Metals, unlike the 20 in Destructoid's own recipe list.
- Nylon Cloth: called a fairly common find; Nylon Laminate: Epic, and may not appear even after several visits. Both turn up in Deadtown's underground, in bags in the Mujin Nightclub in the west wing of the Heavenly Luck Casino (Cheongun Village), and best of all in the Citadel's main building at the 14th Political Prison. Neither needs a Lieutenant fight or a radiation zone.
- Nylon Laminate prints from 100 Synthetics and 3 Nylon Cloth after Epic Ingredient Printing. Nylon Cloth goes into the 3-Plate Armor Vest (5), Medium Scavenger Backpack (3), Medium Secure Backpack (5) and Parachute (5); Nylon Laminate into the Large Backpack (2), Large Scavenger Backpack (2) and Secure Scavenger Backpack (4).
- Best loot: the moving Train is described as perhaps the best source of 3D Printer resources, though it passes through busy areas; Cheonwang Peak Array can hold technology ingredients such as Comms Modules. Loot is not fixed.
