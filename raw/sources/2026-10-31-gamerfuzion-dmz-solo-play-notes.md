# GamerFuzion: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ solo play, Stealth Meter, Tourniquet and star system explained

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 3 Oct 2026, last modified 20 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Draws on the official Call of Duty blog, the developers' 9 Oct 2026 Q&A on r/DMZ, an Xbox Wire interview and a Screen Rant interview and preview (the Screen Rant page itself could not be opened from our tools). No hands-on play; everything is from before launch.

Facts taken, in our words:
- Stealth Meter: a HUD indicator that moves from white to yellow to orange as an AI soldier gets closer to spotting you, with an audio cue before detection; going prone or getting behind cover stops the detection; Infinity Ward told Screen Rant the system is entirely meant for solo players. It tracks AI soldiers.
- Star (threat) level rises when you go loud and kill AI; suppressed kills do not count toward it; Screen Rant reports a maximum of six stars.
- Squads hold three players; assimilation lets you add one more player, for a maximum of four; up to 20 squads and 60 players a match; lobbies fill with squads that chose the same activity, then with Free Roam squads.
- Match length: about 30 minutes in the 2022 beta and 40 to 50 minutes now, with both numbers still being tuned (Infinity Ward to Screen Rant).
- A screen splash warns you when a Lieutenant is nearby.
- DMZ XP counts even if you fail to exfil; DMZ Cash reaches your Bank only after a successful exfil.
