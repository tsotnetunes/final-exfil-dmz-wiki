# Dexerto: Modern Warfare 4's new Server Culling feature (wall hacks)
URL: https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4s-new-server-culling-feature-will-make-wall-hacks-useless-3415049/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 2 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Infinity Ward announced Server Culling on 2 Oct 2026 in a video, and the official Call of Duty account posted about it the same day.
- Wallhacks work because a player's game is sent the location data of enemies; in MW4 every map has occlusion layers built into its environment, so walls and buildings keep an opponent's location from reaching your game until that opponent is in view.
- It is built into the map objects and changes nothing for players who do not cheat.
- It applies to every launch map, to DMZ and to maps released from Season 1 onward; the article says it covers Multiplayer and Warzone, and Shipment as well.
