Facts taken, in our words:
- The printer opens at DMZ Player Level 2 and is reached at the FOB or from the side menu. Troubleshooting: a padlock on a recipe means a level, Order or extraction requirement; a greyed-out Print button means missing Resources or Ingredients; if printing to the Stash fails, the Stash may be full, and printing straight to the Operator may still work; a recipe you found stays locked until you extract with it, and extracting it also pays 5,000 DMZ Cash.
- Uncommon Ingredient Printing costs 50,000 Cash, 20 Biomaterials, 10 High-Tenacity Fibers and 5 Comms Modules, and opens printing of six Uncommon ingredients: Biomaterials, Reactive Metals, Electronic Sensors, Rigid Synthetics, Control Units, Detonators. It cannot make the Comms Modules it needs; those come from the Epic tier.
- No Rare Ingredient Printing tier is known: CPUs, Printed Circuit Boards, High-Tenacity Fibers, Nitro Compounds, Binding Catalysts, Nylon Cloth, Stock Rods and Structural Alloy cannot be printed. Getting a Rare ingredient as a level reward does not unlock a recipe for it.
- CPU (Rare) and Control Unit (Uncommon): the second floor of the Data Farm at Cheonwang Peak Array has server blade containers that can hold two CPUs, one Control Unit or one Printed Circuit Board (not guaranteed); also server rooms in the Nenshin Tech Complex (stronger enemies), electronic containers in Deadtown's hillside buildings, and the Coin Mining Den key room in NuriGO West. Control Units also come from computers and tech bags. A Control Unit prints from 20 Electronics and 20 Metals after Uncommon Ingredient Printing.
- Printed Circuit Board (Rare): DMZ level 56 gives two; the printer's ingredient hint points to the Haneul Nuclear Reactor (radiation: bring a Gas Mask) and electrical substations; reported loose in the Chang-san Air Base bunker. CPU: DMZ level 44 gives two.
- Binding Catalyst: search Mirae General Hospital and the blue and white medical tents around it, medical bags first; not in every container.
- The Healer Order (Order 03 on DMZ Orders page 7) asks you to track the Medkit recipe at the printer and then print a Medkit. If Track Recipe seems to do nothing, untrack an older recipe in the Tracked category and track the Medkit again; the writer blames full tracking slots, and the workaround is not guaranteed.
