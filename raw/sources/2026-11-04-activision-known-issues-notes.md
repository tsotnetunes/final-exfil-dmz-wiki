# Activision support: Modern Warfare 4 known issues
URL: https://support.activision.com/content/atvi/support/web/en_gb/modern-warfare-4/articles/known-issues-in-modern-warfare-4.html
Captured: 2026-11-04 (page shows the date 30 Oct 2026)
Trust: official

Notes in our own words:
- Three DMZ entries, for all platforms, all under investigation.
- Operator Prestige can take the items from the Stash and give no Prestige Trait.
- Some Active Duty Operators cannot Prestige though they meet the requirements. Workaround: Rare weapons that only ever had three attachments.
- Some players cannot buy Lieutenant intel at the Boss Board, which blocks the Information Power Order. The patch notes of 30 Oct 2026 list a fix for this.
