# Gamingdeputy (France): how to get into the X01 Deep Vault in MW4 DMZ
URL: https://www.gamingdeputy.com/france/mw4-dmz-entrer-deep-vault-x01/
Published: 2026-11-07 (in French)
Captured: 2026-11-11
Trust: third-party (press guide, one author's walkthrough)

Facts taken, in our words:
- The main door of the Deep Vault opens with any key whose name includes "Deep Vault"; the guide says the simplest one to get is a Deep Vault key that The Captain drops when killed, lying near his body.
- The Captain moves around X01 rather than spawning in a fixed spot, carries a minigun and wears Juggernaut armour; the guide advises a full squad or heavy weapons.
- The entrance is in the north west of X01; a console on the wall opens the door. Pressing the elevator button inside shuts the main door behind you, so no one else can follow.
- Below, the guide climbs the left-hand stairs to the top level, the core control room, and starts the opening at a console. Tier 3 enemies then spawn and must all be killed.
- Back on the ground floor a button opens the core room. Inside: cabinets to search, Juggernaut armour to put on, and a bioweapon in the middle of the room to neutralise. The way out is to force a door to the elevator and ride a zipline up.
- The guide names a further enemy appearing in the vault; not used here because no other source agrees yet.
