# DMZ Radar: MW4 DMZ Gunsmith: How to Buy & Build Guns, All Costs
URL: https://dmzradar.com/guide-gunsmith.html
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 20 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- The Gunsmith opens at DMZ level 10, when Gunny joins the FOB.
- Buy a gun after extracting its Weapon Manual, then as often as you like; a looted gun you extracted can be built on once, and if you lose it that weapon stays locked until you have its Manual.
- Base guns have no attachments; a base M4 is about 5,000.
- Attachment slots, bought in any order: 1st 1,000; 2nd 5,000; 3rd 10,000; 4th 25,000; 5th 50,000; all five 91,000. Each attachment has its own price and must be unlocked by weapon level.
- Examples: ISO Nightshade with one muzzle 13,500; Han 86 with optic, suppressor and laser 42,750; the official M4 with five attachments 99,987 after a 5% discount.
- The page says the Apex is free once the weapon is fully levelled, with levels counting across Multiplayer and DMZ; launchers take no attachments and no Apex.
- Discounts: 5% at DMZ level 25; Gunsmith Upgrade I and II, printed at the 3D Printer, 5% each.
- An owned Blueprint is free once; buying it again costs less than building the gun; cosmetics cost nothing.
- Weapon Prestige can be done at the Gunsmith: a maxed weapon goes back to level 1 for Pyroclast camos and keeps its Apex Attachment.
- Gunny suggests close-range, balanced or long-range builds from weapons you own.
