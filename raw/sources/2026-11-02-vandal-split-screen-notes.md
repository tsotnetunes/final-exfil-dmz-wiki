Facts taken, in our words:
- Two players on one console, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only; not on PC or Switch 2.
- Online modes only, Multiplayer and DMZ named; the Campaign has none.
- Each player signs in with an own Activision account; the second joins from the mode's lobby with a second controller.
- Online play needs the platform subscription on both accounts, by this guide.
