# Call of Duty blog: Warzone Season 1, everything you need to know
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1
Captured: 2026-11-02 (published 2 Nov 2026)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- Season 1 goes live at midnight ET on 6 Nov 2026, 9 PM PT on 5 Nov, on all platforms.
- A Player Level reset lets players level up again and optionally Prestige across Multiplayer, DMZ and Warzone. The post does not give the range, what is kept, or whether the 1 to 70 DMZ level is touched.
- Warzone's Hajin has 13 major places. Compared with DMZ: the Edge Circle and gas move differently, the Haneul reactor has no radioactive cloud (the post spells it Hanuel), vehicles differ, and interactable objects and loot caches are placed differently.
- Hajin (battle royale) and Zodiac (Resurgence) are there at launch. Verdansk and Rebirth Island return later in the season, no date.
- All 33 Modern Warfare 4 launch weapons join Warzone. Weapons from earlier games move to a Legacy tab and cannot be edited in the Gunsmith.
- Older Operators and cosmetics carry forward into Warzone, and a skin toggle applies in Warzone only.
- Warzone arrives on Nintendo Switch 2 and leaves PlayStation 4 and Xbox One.
- A mid-season update and a Season 1 Reloaded post come later, no dates.
- Warzone's Ground Ops, Rally Point and Grind for Royalty are Warzone features; none is described as part of DMZ.
