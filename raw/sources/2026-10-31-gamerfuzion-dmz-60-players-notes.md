# GamerFuzion: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ, 60 players, 20 squads, no solo queue, Single Squad Mission
URL: https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-60-players-20-squads-no-solo-queue-single-squad-mission/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 12 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

A summary of Infinity Ward's developer Q&A on r/DMZ of Friday 9 Oct 2026 (23 studio replies plus the opening post), with earlier reporting. Pre-launch.

Facts taken, in our words:
- Up to 20 squads of 3, so up to 60 players in a match on Hajin.
- All squads currently drop into a match at the same time; the reply said "currently", so this may change.
- There is no dedicated solo queue; the matchmaker aims to group solos, duos and trios with their own size but does not guarantee it; turn Squad Fill off to go alone.
- Assimilation caps a squad at 4 players; truces, betrayals and assimilation are all possible.
- The Tourniquet and the Stealth Meter were added as tools for solo players.
- At infil you choose a Story Mission, a Dynamic Op or Free Roam, which helps match players with similar goals.
- A player who loses connection should be able to rejoin while the server is still running; the developers did not say whether gear is kept.
- The Single Squad Mission option seen in a screenshot was a development playlist, never meant for players and not a PvE mode.
- No in-game party finder replaces the 2022 beta's; the article points to looking-for-group channels on the official Call of Duty Discord.
