- A full squad wipe loses everything carried and sends the Operator MIA.
- Safes seen: a bedroom safe at Imjin Farmland (Encrypted Archive, Regional Combat Notes) and a wall safe in Compound Charlie (Infantry Invasion Plans, Nuclear Program Files). A Crack Safe side task drills locked safes open. Compound Charlie showed a Medium threat rating.
- The Dead Man's Cache Op included a basement bunker in Imjin Suburb with an ammo and armor cache.
- Smaller places named: Hanrim Village (spelled Hanrie), Revolution Dam, Mujin Pass, South Island, Nenshin Tech Complex; compounds Alpha, Charlie and Echo.
