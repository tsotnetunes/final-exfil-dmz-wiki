# dmzradar.com: MW4 DMZ Secure Slots, every backpack, stash and what you keep
URL: https://dmzradar.com/guide-backpacks.html
Captured: 2026-10-31 (the page says its data was verified on 20 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Basic Pack: 8 item slots, no Secure Slot, with a separate pouch for Resources.
- Secure Slots and weapon slots by backpack: Small 0; Medium 1; Medium Weapon 1 and 1 weapon slot; Large 2 and 1 weapon slot; Small Secure 3; Medium Secure 4; Large Secure 4 and 1 weapon slot; Secure Scavenger 4 with more resource room. A plain weapon backpack adds one weapon slot.
- What sits in a Secure Slot stays with you after a failed exfil; everything else in the backpack is lost.
- The Stash starts at 40 items and grows through DMZ levels and 3D Printer upgrades.
