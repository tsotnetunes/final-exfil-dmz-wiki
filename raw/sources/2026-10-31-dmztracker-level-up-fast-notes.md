# DMZTracker: MW4 DMZ: How to Level Up Fast (Every XP Source)
URL: https://dmztracker.gg/guides/how-to-level-up-fast/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 22 Oct 2026, modified 25 Oct 2026; no byline)
Trust: third-party (fan guide site; numbers are examples from runs, not an official table)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Example XP: a completed Dynamic Op 3,000 to 4,575; killing The Warden 632, plus 500 for its Side Op; a crack-the-safe Side Op 225; any safe 75 to 82; a door opened with a key 300; looting a Dynamic Op supply drop 75; an AI kill up to 120 with a headshot.
- Double XP tokens work in DMZ and are split into rank XP and weapon XP; the page does not say whether they raise the DMZ level or Operator XP.
- A successful exfil pays bonus XP and raises the Operator's Dog Tag; a death keeps DMZ level progress but loses the exfil bonus.
- The Fast Learner and Faster Learner Traits each add a little XP and stack.
- A fan capture suggests about 15,000 to 18,600 XP per DMZ level.
- Suggested order: a Story Mission first (one per deployment), then chain Dynamic Ops, take Side Ops on the way, and extract every run.
