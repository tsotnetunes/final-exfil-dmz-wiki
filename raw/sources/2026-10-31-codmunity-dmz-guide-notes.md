# CODMunity: MW4 DMZ beginner guide
URL: https://codmunity.gg/dmz/guide
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published updated 24 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Threat level belongs to a place (Low to Extreme) and has nothing to do with other players.
- A separate wanted level rises as you kill AI and sends search parties after you; stealth, cover and breaking line of sight bring it down; a creator reports the Stealthy trait slows it. Not the same as the Bounty Wanted status from killing Operators.
- AI reinforcements can arrive by elevator.
- Infinity Ward describes boss kills as small checkpoints: they count only if you extract.
- A creator paid $25,000 at the Boss Board to start a match with a Lieutenant's intel.
- Four AI soldier tiers: grunts, armoured, armoured with gas masks, faceless armoured; also drone swarms, LMG drones, robot dogs, attack helicopter patrols and road convoys.
