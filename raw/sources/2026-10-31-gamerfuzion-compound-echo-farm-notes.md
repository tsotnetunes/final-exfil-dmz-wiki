# GamerFuzion: MW4 DMZ best loot farm, Gangbyeon Apartment B key and the Compound Echo stronghold

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 23 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (one live run):
- Compound Echo sits by River Heights in the south, Medium threat; its side tasks shown were Clear the Military Compound and Capture SAM Site.
- The Uncommon Gangbyeon Apartment B key was in a desk drawer in an apartment of the first building; it opens a stronghold in the next building, which has a SAM site on its roof; unlocking gives 300 XP.
- Inside: apartments, footlockers, cabinets, duffel bags and roof crates; the run found Epic backpacks, Infantry Invasion Plans, Degraded Execution Footage and a recipe. If the glass doors are already open, the stronghold has been looted.
- About four minutes from landing to a full backpack in the best case. It is not known whether the key always spawns in the same apartment.
