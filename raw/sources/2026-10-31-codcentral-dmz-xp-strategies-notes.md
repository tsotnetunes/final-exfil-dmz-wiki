# COD Central: Best MW4 DMZ XP Strategies: How to Level Up Fast
URL: https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-xp-strategies/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 30 Oct 2026, by the site's founder)
Trust: third-party (a fan site's own measurements from 71 of its DMZ deployments, solo and in squads)

Facts taken, in our words:
- With a Double XP token running, the deployment Rating tab showed Double XP as active, yet the DMZ level rose at the usual rate; the token doubled the MW4 player rank (1 to 55) instead. The author did not test a token against Operator (Dog Tag) XP.
- Operator XP is fed by the same XP as the DMZ level, except the Story Mission completion award, which does not count for the Operator.
- Tokens stop counting down outside a match.
- XP lines seen in the Rating tab: Deployment Bonus 450 XP for every minute in the match (7.5 a second); Extracted Bonus 500 XP plus a fifth of the Deployment Bonus; Story Mission completed 12,500; Dynamic Op completed 4,000 to 8,000, or 250 for each finished step of an unfinished Op; boss and Lieutenant kills 500 to 1,500; an Operator or Lieutenant Dog Tag 1,000; a door opened with a key 300; a Side Op usually 150; a revive up to 100; each AI kill, Operator kill or loot container 75.
- Dying loses none of the XP already earned, only the Extracted Bonus. The author saw no XP from Orders.
- Measured rates: Iron Citadel Story Mission about 105,000 XP an hour over six runs; Dynamic Ops about 95,000 an hour; staying in the match and extracting about 32,000 an hour; farming kills about 70,000; loot routes 48,000 to 71,000.
- DMZ level 70 takes 1,165,573 XP in total; level 2 costs 15,050 and level 70 costs 18,634; roughly 11 to 12 hours of match time with the best methods.
- Dog Tag levels: the first costs 8,372 XP and each next one about 12% more; reaching Gold takes about 144,000 XP; Steel I to Onyx III about 450,000 XP for one Operator.
