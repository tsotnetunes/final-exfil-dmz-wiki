# KeenGamer: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ map, best loot locations
URL: https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/modern-warfare-4-dmz-map-best-loot-locations/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 22 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Mirae General Hospital: medical supplies and crafting resources in the wards, emergency rooms, medical storage and the triage tents outside.
- Police Stations (blue buildings): military gear and locked armories that need their key. Fire Stations: radiation gear and parachutes. Clinics (green): medical supplies.
- Military compounds: safes, military equipment, intel you can sell and Ammo & Armor Caches; some need keys; two open compounds at Daesong Ridge in the south need none and have fewer guards.
- Valuables from safes can be extracted and sold for DMZ Cash.
- No place guarantees a given rare item; contents change from one deployment to the next.
