# GamerFuzion: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ Best Traits: Solo and Squad Builds, Possum and What Prestige Costs
URL: https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-best-traits-solo-squads-possum-prestige-pro-traits/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 23 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (guide site; the Prestige screen and Prestige Traits menu as seen in the live game)

Facts taken, in our words:
- The Prestige screen asks for 18 Trait Points, eight Rare items (two weapons, a plate carrier, a backpack and four printer ingredients) and 200,000 in the Bank, and warns that whatever is left on the Operator will be consumed.
- The Prestige Traits menu lists 12 Pro Traits, four per tree:
  - Warfighter: Adaptive Restock Pro (the extra AI drop is rarer); Possum Pro (once a match, revive yourself into Last Stand, without the tourniquet health penalty); Quick Fix Pro (health starts regenerating at once after a kill); Viper Sense Pro (hidden from thermals and AI targeting, and warned when an enemy Operator aims at you).
  - Scout: Overwatch Pro (UAV towers you activate never expire and reach farther); UAV Upgrade Pro (longer UAV range and duration, Advanced UAV, bigger minimap); Thermal Padding Pro (a vehicle you sit in is hidden from UAVs); Field Crafting Pro (craft from Common resources in the field at half the cost).
  - Survivalist: Free Revive Pro (your first Wound is repaired and you can use a tourniquet while Wounded); Numb the Pain Pro (longer Painkiller and Smelling Salts effects on revives); Impact Smokes Pro (larger smoke that lasts longer); Die Hard Pro (exfil called automatically in Last Stand, no bleed-out limit, faster movement).
- The site says its earlier leak-based list was wrong and that this list comes from the live menu.
- A companion video describes choosing the trait at a fourth Prestige; the article calls that unconfirmed.
