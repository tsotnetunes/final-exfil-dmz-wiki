# PC Gamer: How to find a dynamic op phone in DMZ in Modern Warfare 4
URL: https://www.pcgamer.com/games/call-of-duty/dynamic-op-phone-dmz/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (press)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Dynamic Ops are marked on the map by green satellite-phone icons; you can ping one or follow its beeping.
- The page 4 Order wants a Dynamic Op phone collected in Cheongun Village (north-west corner of the South Korean side, near the casino and hospital), and the Op must then be finished; otherwise you repeat the first step.
- Go in through Free Roam so no Op or Story Mission is assigned at the start: you cannot start another Op until the current one is done.
- Page 4 tip: get a suppressor and print a throwing knife, a medium backpack and a 2-plate vest before you deploy, so the whole page can be done in one run; a suppressed pistol is the cheap option for the eight clean kills.
