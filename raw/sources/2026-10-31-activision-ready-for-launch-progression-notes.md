Read through a web-fetch tool, which returned the page's DMZ level reward table. Only rewards that are items, gear, kits, recipes, Fire Support or Field Upgrades are noted here; ingredient rewards, station upgrades and Operator skins are left to the progression pages.

- Level 3: Sedative Inhaler recipe. Level 4: Smoke Grenade and Concussion Grenade recipes.

- Level 7: Frag Grenade and Throwing Knife recipes.

- Level 11: a Basic Triage Kit (Uncommon) with Bandages, a Med Kit, Painkillers and Radiation Blockers.
- Level 12: Ammo Box and Spotter Scope recipes. Level 13: a UAV (Epic Fire Support). Level 14: Armor Box recipe.

- Level 17: Shock Stick recipe.
- Level 19: a Recon Equipment Kit (Uncommon) with a Seeker Drone, a Recon Drone, Heartbeat Sensors, Snapshot Grenades and a Police Station Key.
- Level 23: Sticky Grenade recipe. Level 26: Gas Mask recipe.
- Level 27: a Triage Kit (Epic) with Bandages, Med Kits, Self Revives and Painkillers.
