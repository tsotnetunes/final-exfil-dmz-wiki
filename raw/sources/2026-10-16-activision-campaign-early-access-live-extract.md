# Activision blog, "Modern Warfare 4: Campaign Early Access Now Live", 16 Oct 2026: facts in our words

URL: https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-campaign-early-access-live-now
Published: 16 Oct 2026 (Call of Duty Staff). Read in a browser on 19 Oct 2026.
Trust: the publisher's own post. Restated in our words; nothing quoted beyond names.

- DMZ early access starts 20 Oct 2026; full launch 23 Oct 2026. The post gives no start time for DMZ.
- Campaign early access (16 missions) is for digital pre-orders or pre-purchases of the Standard Digital or Vault Edition on Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, PS5, Battle.net, Steam and Switch 2, and lasts until launch.
- Campaign completion rewards, earned in order and usable in Multiplayer and DMZ from full launch: base Operators Cho, Dallas, Luca, Oh-Ji, Kal, Dunn, West and Park; the calling card "No More Rules"; the loading screen "Ready Line"; the emblem "Gone Dark"; three Double XP tokens and two Double Weapon XP tokens.
- Squads: friends can be added in game by Activision ID, squads can party up across platforms with crossplay, and the game has built-in party and friend tools. The post suggests using Campaign early access to level up, then regrouping for DMZ early access.
