Facts taken, in our words:
- Every key card shows a rarity, a value of 100 and a hint that begins with a grid square and names the POI.
- A locked door shows a padlock; with the key you get an unlock prompt, and unlocking pays 300 XP.
- Keys in ordinary backpack slots are lost if you fail to exfil.
- Keys come from containers, AI enemies, boss kills and some mission rewards; HIGH and EXTREME threat rooms call for a full squad.
- The Counting Room Vault and the Vault Armory are two separate doors at the Heavenly Luck Casino; a vault gate in the casino car park cannot be opened by any key.
- Keys and places: Mirae Electrical Room (F5, car park level B2; guaranteed from Operation Critical Extraction); Counting Room Vault (F4; guaranteed from Operation Silent Ledger, also seen in a Rice Mill industrial locker and a Haksan School military locker; the vault held a Zip Gun recipe, Nuclear Program Files, Encrypted Archives and Gold Skulls); Mechanic's Shack (F5, a hut south of the Cheongun Village clinic; random loot and enemy drops); Police Station Armory (basement of the Pyonghwa Village police station; a second door of the same name at Revolution Dam); Militia Arms Depot (G7, Gangbyeon Apartment B, Hajin River Heights; often in duffel bags in the Citadel East Wing cells); Field Depot (Chang-san Air Base hangar); Re-Education Center Annex; Underground Propaganda Store Room; Executive Arms Auction (DBN Headquarters; from elite AI and high-value targets); Rice Mill Bunker (Imjin Farmland, D7 to D8; not the Foreman's Office); The Doctor's Command Office (F5, upper floor of Mirae General Hospital; guaranteed from The Doctor; its Lieutenant crate held a Tempered Armor Vest recipe); Commandant Office; Shop Records Room (F7, Wolves Stadium); Coin Mining Den (G6; guaranteed from Operation Shopping Hacks); Vault Armory (its weapon locker can hold a Weapon Manual); Haksan School Armory (Deadtown, B to C, 6 to 7; weapons, a Medium Backpack, a Defibrillator, printer materials; a military locker there can hold a Counting Room Vault key); Gun Store Weapons Locker (gun stores in west Cheongun Village and north Songdo Wharf; one key per green locker; free at DMZ levels 16 and 42); Hazard Storehouse (Haneul Nuclear Reactor, I4 to I5; the Reactor DRC Locker is at the same place); Supreme Leader's Bunker (B5 to B6; needs the Bunker Access Card, a Story Mission reward; ordinary keys do not open it).
