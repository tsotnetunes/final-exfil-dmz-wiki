# AllThingsHow: How to Beat the Colossus Commander in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=297416
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Armoured Commander with a yellow canister on its back; patrols; a COMMANDER / COLOSSUS banner names the area when you get close.
- Met at the 14th Political Prison and into the Citadel (yard, East Wing), at the gate and office halls of Cheonwang Peak Array, in the Armory at Chang-san Air Base during Operation Iron Citadel, and in the Haneul reactor yard during Operation Fallen Ember.
- Attacks: automatic fire; a close-range electric shock that runs through nearby metal; a trail of green gas, and gas where it falls.
- The suit makes it immune to explosives in the site's tests; grenades and charges did nothing.
- Methods: keep a parked truck between you and it, then fire from the truck bed at the upper body (PPSh-41 or Cronen Squall); fire down from the Armory stair landing with a Signal 50 and rifles; hold a hallway corner with a Type 73 LMG (armour-piercing, 100-round box) and aim for the head.
- Drops seen: Experimental Compounds (Legendary, $3,500) with other ingredients at the Citadel; Military Composites (Legendary, $3,500) at Cheonwang Peak Array.
- Boss Board reward $50,000; an assist paid 1,075 XP and a headshot kill 1,198 XP.
