Facts taken, in our words:
- Tac-Map layout of the lower floor: Data Center in the south east corner, Supply Depot north west of it, Pump Station and Power Generators in the north east, Bio Lab in the centre, Production Assembly in the south.
- The Wheelson Commander appears in the Logistics Tunnel with a banner; the fight took about six minutes, and it gave 50 XP for disabling it and 500 XP for the kill.
- A side op leads to the generator platform, where holding a laptop prompt cuts the power. The flooded barracks are down the back stairs of Supply Depot, a room of bunk beds, and count only with power cut in the same match; the Super User Data Access Keycard lies beside a footlocker there.
- The Data Center dark room is a server hall with three source code monitors; all three took about 67 seconds.
- Page 5 paid Argonaut Armor, a Large Scavenger Backpack, a Parachute and 20,000 DMZ Cash. Exfil lifts were used at Production Assembly and by the Tech Lab.
