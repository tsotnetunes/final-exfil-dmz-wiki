# Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 patch notes: 23 Nov 2026 block
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes
Captured: 2026-11-24 (read from Final Exfil's parsed copy of the page, re-parsed at 19:30 UTC on 23 Nov 2026; the official updates account announced a small update at about 17:40 UTC, and Infinity Ward said the DMZ weapon tuning was added to the notes at about 19:28 UTC the same day)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- Live events, all modes: Thanksgiving Duty Roster, marked new on 23 Nov. Kills in any mode score leaderboard points: 10 per Multiplayer kill, 20 per Warzone kill, 2 per DMZ kill. A "Bird Call" Astaroth .57 Weapon Blueprint is among the rewards. A board runs 3 hours and a new one starts every 4 hours; each reward can be won once only.
- Global: stability fixes; event leaderboard profiles could show incomplete data; a store bundle was mislabeled as a tracer pack; after an update the menu could jump to the Battle Pass instead of explaining catch-up rewards. All fixed.
- Multiplayer only: Ground War: Combat Outpost set to 24 against 24; doubled Wager Match: Gauntlet 1v1 rewards; two fixes (Gun Game spawns, empty class after Classic Prestige).
- DMZ weapon tuning, the first listed under the DMZ heading. Damage per hit, before to after:
  - Assault rifles: Axion max 40 to 36, mid 34 to 30, min 30 to 28. Cronen Osprey max 31 to 28, mid 28 to 26, min 26 to 24. Han 86 max 36 to 33, mid 32 to 28, min 29 to 25. Hyeon Burst max 50 to 43, mid 42 to 37, min 38 to 33. Kastov 762 max 42 to 37, mid 37 to 32, min 34 to 30. M4 max 34 to 30, mid 30 to 28, min 25 to 24. Cronen Squall max 40 to 35, mid 34 to 30, min 29 to 25. Oden mid 55 to 48, min 45 to 39 (no max line). Patriot XMR max 43 to 38, mid 39 to 35, min 34 to 30.
  - SMGs: CSG-35 max 26 to 23, mid 24 to 21, min 21 to 19. ISO Nightshade max 34 to 30, mid 30 to 27, min 23 to 21. PPSh-41 max 28 to 24, mid 25 to 22, min 19 to 18. VEL 46 max 32 to 28, mid 28 to 25, min 23 to 20. Wz.55 Striga max 45 to 39, mid 41 to 36, min 33 to 29. X-58 Nyx max 35 to 31, mid 30 to 28, min 25 to 24.
  - LMGs: APOC-338 max 46 to 40, mid 41 to 36, min 37 to 32. FiNN LMG max 39 to 34, mid 35 to 31, min 33 to 28. Type 73 max 31 to 28, min 27 to 24 (no mid line).
  - Sniper rifle: Signal 50 mid 145 to 180, min 140 to 168, head multiplier 1.3 to 1.6 (a buff; no max line).
  - "Mid" above is the first middle damage step.
- DMZ gameplay: several exploits closed. Fixed: the Operation Iron Citadel keycard objective sticking when another team picked up the keycard; mantling from a vehicle to break through a ceiling; Weapon Insurance failing in consecutive matches; the intended Colossus near the boathouse not dropping the Boathouse Key in Operation Fallen Ember.
- DMZ Traits: Possum and Possum Pro could stack; fixed.
- DMZ UI: using a Lieutenant Dog Tag with a full Backpack could mark the player on the map though the tag was not picked up; fixed.
