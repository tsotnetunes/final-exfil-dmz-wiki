# DMZ Codex (dmzcodex.com, a separate fan site, not this wiki): MW4 DMZ Operations database
URL: https://dmzcodex.com/operations/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (site banner says updated 31 Oct 2026; no page date)
Trust: third-party (fan database, unrelated to finalexfil.com)

Facts taken, in our words:
- 21 entries: 12 Dynamic Operations and 9 Side Ops; no Story Missions.
- Side Ops listed: Activate UAV Tower; Capture a SAM Site (use the control computer, hold the site until the hack ends); Clear the Military Compound (kill defenders until the counter completes); Crack Safe (find the marked safe and open it); Download Intel To Locate Lieutenant (climb the Lieutenant Hunt tower and download intel at the laptop on top); Loot Radio Tower (climb the active tower's outside route and use the top interaction); Paid Intel (reach and kill the named Lieutenant; Cash +500); Repair a Truck (fill the radar truck's empty repair slots with parts); Secure Lost Supply Drop.
- Dynamic Ops listed with steps matching other sites: Broken Resupply, Chemical Denial (several alternative routes), Dead Man's Cache, Domino Protocol, Ghost Shipment, Hunt Operators, Ignition Point, Signal Breaker (Cash +10,000), Sky Heist (stay aboard or parachute at the end), Special Delivery, Toxic Intercept, Viral Reckoning (partial-exfil or bomb ending).
