# Press reports about MW4 DMZ, 1 to 18 Oct 2026: notes in our words

Collected 18 and 19 Oct 2026 by web search and page reads. Trust: third-party press; where a site describes official footage or an official video, that is said. Nothing quoted beyond names and numbers.

## FPSJP (EAA FPS News), on the official DMZ Dynamic Ops trailer, 7 Oct 2026 (updated 9 Oct)
URL: https://fpsjp.net/archives/566075 (Japanese)
- Two named Dynamic Ops: Dead Man's Cache (recover an encrypted drive) and Chemical Denial (investigate a blackout).
- In Chemical Denial a Side-Op sends the squad after Bale; the English interface labels him LIEUTENANT, the Side-Op shows complete after the kill, and his dog tag is Legendary. The same reading is in GamerFuzion's Boss Board explainer (12 Oct, https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-boss-board-explained-lieutenants-bale-gorani-commanders-dog-tag-case/).
- In-development footage pays 10,000 to 20,000 DMZ Cash into the Bank per completed Op; each completion sends a supply drop whose rarity rises.
- The FOB's Boss Board records the Lieutenants you have beaten and is filled again each season.
- Deadtown Side-Ops shown: downloading intel and switching on a UAV tower.

## FPSJP, on the official DMZ Story Missions trailer, 9 Oct 2026
URL: https://fpsjp.net/archives/566657 (Japanese)
- The trailer's mission is Operation Critical Extraction: search Mirae General Hospital for a high-value target who is recovering there, then extract them.
- Operation Silent Ledger and Operation Shopping Hacks also show on the mission select screen.
- Story Missions open up by completing the DMZ Orders page.

## Game*Spark, report of Infinity Ward's Tokyo media conference, 1 Oct 2026
URL: https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2026/10/01/173010.html (Japanese)
- The armed group every player fights in Hajin is called Hundred Spirits (in katakana); Lieutenants are fighters it has hired.
- Enemy soldiers come in three tiers plus a rare, much stronger fourth; weather affects the AI's sight and hearing.
- Completing DMZ Orders in Free Roam earns points that unlock Story Missions; Dynamic Ops grew out of the 2022 Contracts.

## GameSpot, on From The Ward Ep. 7 (official Call of Duty video, 5 Oct 2026), 5 Oct 2026
URL: https://www.gamespot.com/articles/dmz-returns-in-modern-warfare-4-with-deeper-progression-and-more-ways-to-play/ ; video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5VVW6Z6fZcY ("DMZ: Addition By Extraction", 15:58)
- In the episode Infinity Ward says Season 1 adds a new map in the style of Building 21, Season 2 expands Hajin's locations, and special events are planned. A fan site writes the Season 1 location as XL1; the developers' Q&A of 9 Oct called the first post-launch map X01.
- Lieutenant Hunts are optional high-value targets found through intel; rewards can include rare keys to special loot rooms.

## Insider Gaming, "Season 1 Launch Date Announced", 14 Oct 2026
URL: https://insider-gaming.com/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-launch-date-announced/
- Activision confirmed on 14 Oct that Season 1 for Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone starts Friday 6 Nov 2026, two weeks after launch; details to follow after launch. GamerFuzion (14 Oct) describes the official roadmap image: Shipment 27 Oct, Possession 29 Oct, Infected 30 Oct, Season 1 on 6 Nov, with no DMZ items besides early access and no times of day.

## Dot Esports, "All major changes coming in Modern Warfare 4 Warzone", 16 Oct 2026
URL: https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/guides/mw4-warzone-changes
- Relaying Infinity Ward's The Ward Room Ep. 02 (official video, 14 Oct, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Krwvj7TCkyY): Hajin becomes a large Warzone battle royale map with Season 1, playable alongside Verdansk, and stays in the live seasons.

## Early access start time (as of 18 Oct 2026)
- No official start time for DMZ early access. Dot Esports (11 Oct) sets its countdown to 9 AM PT as the usual update hour; PC Gamer (16 Oct) reports predictions of 10 AM PT. Official footnotes say start dates and platforms may change and time zones apply.
