# PlayStation Blog — "Everything you need to know about DMZ mode in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4" (5 Oct 2026) — extract

- URL: https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/05/everything-you-need-to-know-about-dmz-mode-in-call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4/
- Written by Daniel Noel (Activision). Captured 9 Oct 2026 by Claude, read in a browser. Trust: third-party (publisher-written). Facts in our words.

- DMZ launches alongside Campaign and Multiplayer when Modern Warfare 4 releases on 23 Oct 2026.
- Three deployment options: Story Missions (select and replay narrative objectives), Dynamic Operations (deploy straight into an Op; every completed Op brings a Supply Drop of rising rarity), Free Roam (deploy without a mission).
- DMZ Orders are reviewed at the FOB on an Orders Tablet. FOB stations as in the Activision guide; Vendor stock rotates daily.
- XP tips: Story Missions, Dynamic Ops and Orders pay the most; Lieutenants and Commanders roam and pay XP, their weapons and loot; an S-tier After Action rating or better earns the best rewards; repair and refuel vehicles at Gas Stations; each area has a Threat Level.
