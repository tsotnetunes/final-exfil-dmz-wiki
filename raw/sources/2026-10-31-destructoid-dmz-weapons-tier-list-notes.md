# Destructoid: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ weapons tier list: Best ones to use
URL: https://www.destructoid.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-weapons-tier-list/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 22 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- S: Han 86 (good range, easy recoil, safest pick outside close range), M4 (versatile, a little weaker than the Han 86 at range), PPSh-41 (short range, strong against armor, big drum), ISO Nightshade (as effective as the PPSh-41, for players who keep moving), X-58 Nyx (the easiest SMG for beginners).
- A: Kastov 762 (high damage, hard recoil), Rezi 12 (best shotgun), Type 73 (good damage and range, poor handling), MAR-9 (semi-auto makes recoil easy), FiNN LMG, Signal 50, KG-7 Vulcan (less damage, faster fire), Warlord (faster than the Rezi, long reloads), Mosin-Nagant (slow reloads, one-shots enemies without armor), APOC-338 (very high damage, harsh recoil).
- B: Patriot XMR, Hyeon Burst, Oris 8.6, .50 GS, Cronen Squall, Astaroth .57.
- C, called unsuitable for DMZ: Krait P68, Oden, Axion, VEL 46, Mojave .357, PM-40 Anvil, Wz.55 Striga, SANG 9mm, PILA, RPG-8, JOKR.
- The writer says B and C are incomplete and may change.
