# GamerFuzion: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ: what to upgrade first at the 3D Printer, every cost and the valuables not to sell

Facts taken, in our words:
- Effects: Stash Item Upgrade I and II each add 18 item slots; Stash Weapon Upgrade I adds 6 weapon slots; Bank Upgrade III raises the cap to 1 million; MIA Recovery Discount I and II each cut recovery cost by 5 percent; Gunsmith Upgrade I takes 5 percent off Gunsmith purchases; Vendor Item Upgrade I adds Vendor offers; Epic Ingredient Printing opens higher-rarity materials.
- Costs beyond Destructoid's list: Stash Item Upgrade II 50,000 Cash, 20 Detonators, 10 Nylon Cloth, 5 Power Regulators; Bank Upgrade III 100,000 Cash and 5 Infantry Invasion Plans; MIA Recovery Discount II 5 Nuclear Program Files, 5 Degraded Execution Footage, 10 Binding Catalyst, 10 Nitro Compounds (read from a player's screenshot).
- Suggested order: Stash Item I (then II), Bank III, MIA Recovery Discount I, Gunsmith I, Stash Weapon I, Vendor Item I, Epic Ingredient Printing. Track the next upgrade's recipe before each run.
- Valuables used by upgrades, so worth keeping instead of selling: Infantry Invasion Plans (Bank III), Regional Combat Notes (MIA Recovery Discount I), Nuclear Program Files and Degraded Execution Footage (MIA Recovery Discount II). A player tally on X also lists Ballistic Flight Logs, W.M.D. Manifest and Foreign Intelligence Targets (not verified by the article).
