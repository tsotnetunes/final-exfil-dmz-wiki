A short comparison page written before launch. Facts taken, in our words:
- MW2 DMZ (2022) carried cash and one special backpack over between matches; in MW4 DMZ everything you extract persists, and what you do not extract is lost.
- MW2 used insured weapon slots and one shared stash; MW4 uses persistent Operators, each with their own backpack, loadout and Traits, and an MIA recovery that costs more the more you have invested in the Operator.
- MW2 crafting was done through barter recipes at buy stations; MW4 replaces it with the 3D Printer.
- MW4 weapons take five attachments plus one Apex, share progression with Multiplayer, and can turn up damaged in loot.
- Gas masks are named as gear you need for the reactor in the Hajin Exclusion Zone.
