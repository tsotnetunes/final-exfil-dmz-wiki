# DMZTracker: MW4 DMZ Operator Prestige Requirements
URL: https://dmztracker.gg/guides/operator-prestige-guide/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 20 Oct 2026, last checked by the site 27 Oct 2026; no byline)
Trust: third-party (fan guide site)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Prestige needs all 18 Trait Points earned (they need not all be spent) and an Operator who is not MIA.
- Costs as in the official guide: 2 weapons with 3 attachment slots, Rare plate carrier and backpack, 4 Rare ingredients, 200,000 (first); 2 weapons with 4 slots, Epic gear, 4 Epic ingredients, 300,000 (second); Legendary gear, 4 Legendary ingredients, 500,000 (third and later).
- The Operator's whole loadout and backpack are consumed; all Traits are lost and the points must be earned again through Dog Tag levels. The page does not say whether the Dog Tag itself resets.
- There are 12 Prestige Traits, Pro versions of Major Traits (examples Overwatch Pro and Possum Pro).
- Known issue: Infinity Ward was investigating a bug that stopped some players from entering Prestige on Active Duty Operators (Trello, 21 Oct 2026). Workaround: use Rare (blue) weapons that have three attachment slots and have never carried four or more attachments.
- The fourth-Prestige swap is listed as not yet confirmed by the site.
