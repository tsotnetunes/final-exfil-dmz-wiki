# DMZTracker: MW4 DMZ, how to level up fast (every XP source)
URL: https://dmztracker.gg/guides/how-to-level-up-fast/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 22 Oct 2026, checked 25 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words. The site says no official XP table exists; its numbers come from press runs:
- Press-observed XP: a completed Dynamic Op 3,000 to 4,575; a Lieutenant kill about 632, plus 500 for finishing the Lieutenant Side Op; opening a safe 75 to 82; unlocking a key door 300; an enemy kill up to 120 with a headshot. A few Ops plus kills can pass 8,000 XP in one deployment.
- 2XP tokens: switched on from a menu at the FOB; the token list is split into Rank XP and Weapon XP; they count down only during a Multiplayer or DMZ match, not in menus or at the FOB; different token types can run together; each type is capped at one hour a day; no Double XP weekend was announced at launch.
- The site could not confirm which XP pools a Rank 2XP token boosts in DMZ (DMZ level, player level or the Operator Dog Tag).
- Fast Learner (Scout) and Faster Learner (Survivalist) Traits slightly raise XP gain; exact amounts unknown.
- A fan capture suggests roughly 15,000 to 18,600 XP per DMZ level; unverified.
