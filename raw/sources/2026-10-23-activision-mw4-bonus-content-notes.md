Paraphrased notes on the page, not its text:
- 2XP tokens come with promotions such as Monster Energy codes and edition or store bonuses; a Dual 2XP reward gives separate tokens for player 2XP and weapon 2XP.
- In game, at most one hour of each 2XP token type can be used per day, and an account can hold at most 40 hours of each token type across all promotions.
- Monster Energy: one code redemption per person per day; the first six redemptions give 15-minute 2XP tokens.
- Bonus content can take up to 24 hours to appear in game.
- The page gives no expiry date for 2XP tokens, says nothing about when a token's timer runs, and mentions neither DMZ nor giveaways on X.
