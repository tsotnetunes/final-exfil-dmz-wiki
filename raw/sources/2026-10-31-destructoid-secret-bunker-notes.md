# Destructoid: All secret bunker locations in Modern Warfare 4 DMZ

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 21 Oct 2026, updated 22 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- One unmarked bunker in the northwest, with the 14th Political Prison further northwest of it.
- Its main door needs no code; downstairs, the main hallway ends in a right turn and a locked bunker door.
- That door needs a key, which spawns at random; the writer found one in a nearby room. The best loot is behind it; the side rooms hold useful resources.
- A silo room at the same site is also locked. Only one AI enemy was met.
