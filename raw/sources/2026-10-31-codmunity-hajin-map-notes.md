# CODMunity: MW4 DMZ Hajin interactive map and its 13 location pages
URL: https://codmunity.gg/dmz/map (location pages at https://codmunity.gg/dmz/map/<poi-slug>)
Captured: 2026-10-31 (no update date shown)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words. The site's grid letters and numbers do not match the in-game Tac-Map grid (it places Imjin Farmland around D9, the official guide says D7), so grid squares from this site are not used.
- The map lists 13 major locations with their side of the old border: NK for 14th Political Prison, Chang-san Air Base and Deadtown; SK for the other ten. It lists 35 threat zones with levels, matching the official guide.
- Smaller places named: Jayon Warehouses, Russian Border Crossing (West and East), Hanrim Village, Revolution Dam, Tong-il Village, Cheongun Outskirts, Pyonghwa Village, Oamsik Turtle Islands, Mujin Pass, Imjin Suburb, Shoreline Station, Daesong Ridge, Songdo Pier, South Island, Namjin Riverside, Hajin River, The Epic, Hotel Lucera, plus sub-areas of the major POIs.
- Marker layers: exfils and infil points; Bounty Stations, buy stations, UAV towers, SAM sites, Lieutenant towers, gas stations, marine gas; Lieutenants, Commanders, fortresses; mission objectives, Side Ops, Dynamic Ops; caches, weapon cases, safes, police stations, clinics and hospitals, crafting resources, fire stations, compounds; keys, locked doors, intel; vehicles, boats; radiation and night vision areas.
- 14th Political Prison: The Citadel is a fortress pin; a police station, a clinic, a fire station, a gas station, a marine gas jetty, a cache and a UAV tower are mapped around it, with three exfil pins nearby.
- Chang-san Air Base: Admin Complex No. 2 (fortress), Compound Foxtrot, two clinics, three caches, a UAV tower; Lieutenant Bale is fought in the Tank Depot, a hangar in the bunker.
- Deadtown: Re-Education Center (fortress), a fire station, caches, a UAV tower, a Lieutenant Hunt tower, two gas stations and an exfil.
- Imjin Farmland: Rice Mill (fortress), Compound Alpha, a fire station, three caches, two UAV towers, a gas station.
- Cheongun Village: the casino (fortress) with its vault; a police station, two clinics, a fire station, five caches, a UAV tower, a gas station, a marine gas jetty, three exfil pins; a bank vault holding two safes behind a code puzzle and other building safes.
- Mirae General Hospital: fortress pin, a cache, two UAV towers, a Lieutenant Hunt tower, a gas station.
- Haneul Nuclear Reactor: Reactor 01 and Reactor 02 named; three caches, a UAV tower, a marine gas jetty, one exfil; reached by water or a tunnel past the South Korean Border Zone.
- NuriGO Mall: Han-Go Korean BBQ, City Hall and a rooftop Open Bounty Station near NuriGO East; a police station, a fire station, caches, a UAV tower, three gas stations, a safe in a tent, two exfil pins, Compound Delta nearby; Lieutenant Jang-ui.
- The Grid: two caches; a bank vault with two safes behind a code puzzle.
- Cheonwang Peak Array: a clinic, two caches, a UAV tower, two gas stations and several building safes.
- Songdo Wharf: La Grande Vie tower (an Order places a transmitter on its roof; bounty hunts can lead to its penthouse), two caches, a UAV tower, two gas stations, a marine gas jetty, a bank vault with two safes, one exfil.
- Wolves Stadium: Baseball Stadium B Station, Compound Bravo, a police station, a clinic, caches, a UAV tower, a Lieutenant Hunt tower, two gas stations, bank vault safes and a safe in the compound tower.
- Hajin River Heights: Compound Echo, a cache, a UAV tower, a Lieutenant Hunt tower, marine gas jetties, a rooftop safe reached by climbing and a safe down in the parking garage.
