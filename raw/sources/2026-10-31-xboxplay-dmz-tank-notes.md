# XboxPlay Games: How to get a tank in MW4 DMZ

Facts taken, in our words:
- A broken tank sits in the Tank Depot inside the Armory bunker at Chang-san Air Base; from the ground floor of the main armory bunker you head right to reach it.
- Once repaired it can be driven around Hajin; it seats two, a driver and a gunner.
- Its main cannon fires explosive shells that destroy ordinary enemy transport vehicles and Deathstalkers in one hit, but not other heavy tanks.
- Four parts are needed: Tank Alternator (underground bunkers in the North Sector of the base, inside the Armory), Tank Battery (the hangar area of the underground bunker), Tank Plugs (mechanic huts in the West Sector and under the grounded jets in the underground hangar) and Tank Water Pump (rooftops, outside shelves and wall corners of North Sector buildings).
- Parts are never in containers, duffel bags or supply boxes; they lie in the open in set areas with randomised exact spots; you can collect them over several runs by extracting them and keeping them in your Stash.
- To repair, use the prompt at the back of the tank and fit all four parts; no repair time is given.
- The tank uses fuel and is refuelled and repaired at any Gas Station.
- Choosing Operation Iron Citadel when joining Hajin sets the right spawn and gives access to the Armory bunker.
