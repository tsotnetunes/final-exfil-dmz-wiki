# Activision blog, "Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 PC Trailer, Tech Updates, and More", 29 Sep 2026: facts in our words

URL: https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/09/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-pc-trailer-tech-specs-features
Published: 29 Sep 2026 (Call of Duty Staff). Read in a browser on 19 Oct 2026.
Trust: the publisher's own post (PC work led by Beenox with Infinity Ward). Restated in our words.

- PC release 23 Oct 2026 on Xbox on PC, Battle.net and Steam; the full game unlocks at midnight local time. PC preload for digital pre-purchases began 15 Oct 2026 at 9 AM PT.
- TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot are required on PC, and other security measures may be enforced (RICOCHET Anti-Cheat uses them with server-side detection; accounts get 2FA and email verification). Only Intel or AMD processors with the AVX instruction set are supported; Intel Arc GPUs need Resizable BAR.
- Every spec tier needs 115 GB on an SSD, plus space for mandatory updates; DirectX 12 and broadband are required.
- Minimum: Windows 10 64-bit, Ryzen 5 1400 or Core i5-6600, 8 GB RAM, Radeon RX 470, GeForce GTX 970 or 1060, or Arc A580, 3 GB video memory.
- Recommended (90 FPS in Multiplayer on High): Windows 11 64-bit, Ryzen 5 3600 or Core i7-8700K, 16 GB RAM, Radeon RX 6600 XT, GeForce RTX 3060 Ti or Arc B580, 8 GB video memory.
- Ray tracing (high quality at 4K upscaled): Ryzen 7 3700X or Core i7-10700K, 32 GB RAM, Radeon RX 9070 XT or GeForce RTX 5070 Ti, 16 GB video memory.
- Ray tracing is supported in Campaign, Multiplayer and DMZ. Upscalers: DLSS 4.5, FSR Redstone, XeSS 3, Filmic SMAA T2X. A built-in benchmark tool; Advanced Shader Delivery precompiles shaders through the Xbox PC app.
- Weapon bloom: shots now leave from the weapon's barrel position instead of landing at random inside a spread, in ADS and hipfire; a laser attachment shows where they travel. Depth of field is split into World and Weapon settings.
- Game updates no longer need a restart at boot; an update that goes live during play still asks for a restart, between matches.
- Vault Edition: Hostile Alliance and Special Forces Operator Packs, the Signature Weapon Collection, one season of BlackCell and a DMZ Deployment Bonus.
