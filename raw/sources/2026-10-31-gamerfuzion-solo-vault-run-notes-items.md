# GamerFuzion: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ solo vault run (Counting Room key, Police armory, Door Breacher)
URL: https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-solo-vault-run-counting-room-key-police-armory-door-breacher/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 25 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Counting Room Vault key: Epic, sells for 100; a reward for finishing Operation Silent Ledger; consumed when the vault opens. Their vault run gave several weapons, a Medium Backpack, Regional Combat Notes and cash.
- Regional Combat Notes: an Uncommon valuable worth 1,000; extract it and sell it at the FOB.
- Police Station Armory key: Uncommon, 100; found in a filing cabinet in an upstairs office, the spot changes between runs; the armory gave a sniper rifle in a rifle case, armour plates in a military bag and an assault rifle in a basic weapons locker.
- Door Breacher Charge: an Ultra consumable used once to breach a locked door; printable under Consumables once the recipe has been found (1 Experimental Compounds, 5 Stock Rod, 3 Volatile Explosives); the writers say it can also turn up at the Vendor or in loot; a DMZ level 62 reward in the official table.
- Operation Silent Ledger also paid a Gold Bar, a Polaris Dagor and 25,000 DMZ Cash.
- The run's backpack held items worth 44,700 in all (item values, not sales).
