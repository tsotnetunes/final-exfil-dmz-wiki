Facts taken, in our words:
- The printer needs DMZ level 2, usually reached after the first match. Basic recipes such as Bandages, Medkits and Medium Backpacks come with it.
- More recipes are found in Hajin and kept if you exfil with them; then they show up at the printer. Missions can also reward recipes.
- If you lack a material, select it (or press the right stick) to see where it can be found. Materials gathered in a match count only after you exfil.
- Story Mission rewards: Operation Critical Extraction gives the Mirae Electrical Room Key, a Defibrillator and 25,000 DMZ Cash; Operation Shopping Hacks gives the Coin Mining Den Key, Night Vision Goggles and 25,000 DMZ Cash.
