# LFCarry: MW4 DMZ X01, what it is and how to get in

Facts taken, in our words:
- The Captain patrols inside X01 and is listed on the Boss Board.
- He drops helmet sections whose rewards grow, and a Deep Vault keycard (Vault 301) every time.
- The guide suggests the Type 73 with its 60-round explosive top-feed, or the Rezi 12, against his Juggernaut armour.
- X01 has 15 Orders over 5 pages; pages 4 and 5 each include a Commander kill.
