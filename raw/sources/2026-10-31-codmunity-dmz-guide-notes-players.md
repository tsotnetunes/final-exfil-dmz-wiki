A community database. The page says many figures come from trailers or launch-week streams and may not match the current game. Facts taken, in our words:
- To go solo, switch Squad Fill off on the deploy screen (key 1 on keyboard, left trigger on controller).
- Cash and valuables only count once you extract; killing Operators gives more XP than killing AI.
- Dynamic Operations pay 10,000 to 20,000 straight into the Bank when completed.
- Exfil: solo, standing on the exfil starts a 5-second countdown; with a squad it runs about 30 to 45 seconds until every member is aboard, then drops to 5 seconds.
- The radiation spreads from a yellow circle at the start toward the map edge, which hints at where the final exfil will be.
- Keep keys in a Secure Slot so they are not lost if you die before reaching the room.
- The Basic Pack has eight slots; the Medium Backpack has one Secure Slot.
- Rescuing a squadmate's MIA Operator costs more the more they have invested in that Operator.
- Bank cap reported after launch: 1,000,000, with the next upgrade at 2,000,000; cash over the cap is lost without a warning.
- Reported prices: Boss Board Lieutenant intel 25,000; reducing a Bounty 5,000.
- The Free Loadout is not free for your Operator if you die with it.
