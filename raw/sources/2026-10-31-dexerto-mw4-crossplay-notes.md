# Dexerto wiki: How to turn off crossplay in Modern Warfare 4

Captured: 2026-10-31 (no publication date shown)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Crossplay settings sit in the settings menu under the last tab, Account & Network, in the Online section.
- The page lists three crossplay settings: Multiplayer Unranked, Multiplayer Ranked and Warzone Ranked. None is named for DMZ.
- On PlayStation, Xbox and Switch 2 each can be On, Off or Console Only (Console Only keeps PC players out of your lobbies).
- On PC the option to turn crossplay off is not offered.
