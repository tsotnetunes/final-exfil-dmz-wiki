Facts taken, in our words:
- The missions list counts 58 entries: 7 Story Missions, 3 Side Ops and 48 DMZ Orders. It has no Dynamic Operations category. The Orders are grouped as Page 1, Counterfeit, Underground, Human Intel, Page 4, Page 7, Page 10, Page 11, The Grid, Page 14, Hornet's Nest, Page 15 and Page 18; chapter Orders that only ask you to finish a Story Mission are not counted.
- Story Missions listed: Operation Critical Extraction, Operation Silent Ledger, Operation Shopping Hacks, Operation Iron Citadel, Operation Grid Assault, Operation Fallen Ember, Breaking New Ground.
- Critical Extraction: Mirae General Hospital, threat Medium; card objectives are to find the HVT, deal with resistance and get the HVT out; suggested gear 2-Plate Armor Vest and Medkit; rewards a Defibrillator and 25,000 cash. Player tips (unverified): the HVT lies in an ICU room on the second floor; you wheel him on a gurney to the lifts and onto the roof helicopter; the lift locks once the HVT and escort go up; a doctor-type boss can appear near the hospital.
- Silent Ledger: Cheongun Village, threat Medium; card objectives are to get into the casino, breach the vault and take the plates; suggested gear 2-Plate Armor Vest and Medkit; rewards a Gold Bar and 25,000 cash. Player tips (unverified): 12 cash bags can be loaded onto a van; one player says the bag side op pays only 500.
- Shopping Hacks: NuriGO Mall, threat Medium; the briefing ties the mall to Lieutenant Jang-ui and the enemy weapons programme; card objectives are to hack the network, reprogram the protocol and secure evidence; suggested gear 2-Plate Armor Vest, Medkit, Night Vision Goggles; rewards NVGs and 25,000 cash. Player tips (unverified): a pair of night vision goggles sits in a tent outside; a locked door needs intact batteries from the robot dogs, taken before the dog explodes.
- Iron Citadel: Chang-san Air Base, threat Critical; the briefing says Bale runs the base; card objectives are to get into the base, scout the armory ordnance and disrupt the operation; suggested gear 3-Plate Armor Vest, Medkit, Ammo Box; rewards an LTV and 25,000 cash; unlock: finish the three earlier missions.
- Grid Assault: The Grid, threat Critical; a radio tower over the city relays enemy drone commands; card objectives are network access, downloading network intel and uploading a kill switch; suggested gear 3-Plate Armor Vest, Medkit; rewards a Helicopter and 25,000 cash; unlock: finish the three earlier missions.
- Fallen Ember: details "being verified"; one tip (unverified) says to bring gas masks because the radiation is heavy.
- Breaking New Ground, Gear Up, Paid Intel: Kill Bale, Activate UAV Tower and Download Intel To Locate Lieutenant pages carry no details yet.
- Beginner guide (24 Oct): Story Missions are their own deployment type and each card shows location, briefing, threat, objectives and rewards; a weekly task asks for three random Story Missions a week; Story deployments put you near the mission; missions can be replayed. Each Dynamic Op chains three or four objectives, choosing Dynamic Operations at deploy starts you at the map edge with an Op set, and a finished Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 into the Bank even if the squad dies later, plus a Supply Drop that improves from Common to Uncommon to Rare. Side Ops include truck repairs, supply drops, radio towers and SAM sites. In Chemical Denial the Side Ops lead to Bale: download intel, switch on a UAV Tower, then use Paid Intel. Orders are the main FOB directives; the Boss Board opens after the second Order; Orders unlock 3D Printer recipes.
- Stranded is mission 10 of the Modern Warfare 4 Campaign, an open sandbox in North Korea; the page does not connect it to DMZ.
