An interview with Infinity Ward's studio multiplayer creative director at the June reveal. Developer statements made before launch.

Facts taken, in our words:
- Explosives now tend to knock a player back or down instead of always dealing full damage, though a direct hit can still kill.
- You can drag a downed teammate to safety, or drag a downed enemy away so their squad cannot revive them.
- A melee hit can interrupt an opponent's tourniquet or self-revive.
- You can buy intel on a Wanted player's location, and a banner tells you when a bounty target is nearby even without intel.
- Exfil is by a large twin-rotor helicopter with several ways to board; during testing, enemy Little Bird helicopters chased the team as its star level rose.
- Parachutes can be printed at the 3D Printer; the test team drove vehicles between locations; a roaming Commander in a big tank chased them.
- DMZ's map is larger than the 2022 map Al Mazrah.
