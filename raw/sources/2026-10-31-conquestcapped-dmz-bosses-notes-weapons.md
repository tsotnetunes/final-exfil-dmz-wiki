Facts taken, in our words:
- Lieutenant weapons: Gorani a marksman rifle; The Chemist a Kastov 762; Jin-Tae High Roller's Fortune, a Legendary ISO Nightshade with a gold camo; The Doctor a Patriot XMR; Bale a Type 73; Iskra the Widow Maker, a Legendary Warlord blueprint and a rare drop; Scavenger an Oris 8.6; Roze an Astaroth .57; Jang-Ui a Hyeon Burst; the Warden an X-58 Nyx.
- Lieutenants usually drop an ordinary Rare or Epic copy of their weapon with a few attachments; the named Legendary or Ultra versions are rare.
- Commanders drop 3D Printer ingredients, not named weapons.
