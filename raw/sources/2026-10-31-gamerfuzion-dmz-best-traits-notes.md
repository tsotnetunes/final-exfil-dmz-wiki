# GamerFuzion: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ Best Traits, Solo and Squad Builds, Possum and What Prestige Costs

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 23 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (it builds on a creator's video that shows the live menus, and on the official guides)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Minor Trait slots show as question marks until reached and differ between Operators; the six Major Traits of a tree are fixed.
- The in-game Possum description: once per match, a self-revive from death into Last Stand with health back to maximum.
- The Prestige screen asks for 18 Trait Points, eight Rare items (two weapons, a plate carrier, a backpack and four printer ingredients) and 200,000 in the Bank, and warns that everything left on the Operator is consumed.
- Prestige grants a random permanent Prestige Trait. The twelve are Pro versions, four per tree, read from the live Prestige Traits menu: Warfighter Adaptive Restock Pro, Possum Pro, Quick Fix Pro, Viper Sense Pro; Scout Overwatch Pro, UAV Upgrade Pro, Thermal Padding Pro, Field Crafting Pro; Survivalist Free Revive Pro, Numb the Pain Pro, Impact Smokes Pro, Die Hard Pro. The page corrects an earlier leak post of its own; the Codex uses only the live-menu list.
- The creator's solo build: Warfighter plus Scout (Possum, Quick Fix, a plate speed Minor, Combat Resistance; UAV Upgrade, Thermal Padding or Overwatch, Hostile Looter, the last untested). Squad build: Warfighter plus Survivalist, for Faster Revive.
- The creator's Possum tip: wait for the enemy to leave your body before reviving.
- MIA screen: without enough cash the game tells you to dismiss the Operator.
