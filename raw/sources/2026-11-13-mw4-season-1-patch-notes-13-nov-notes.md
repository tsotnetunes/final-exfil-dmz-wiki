# Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 patch notes: 13 Nov 2026 block
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes

Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- Live events: Wager Match Trophies now drop more often in every Multiplayer mode and in DMZ.
- Live events: the Resupply Run is listed as a DMZ live event; several of its screens and steps were fixed so the event flows better.
- DMZ: Legendary ingredients found in X01 now sell for money; before the fix they were turned into materials when sold.
- DMZ: Weapon Manuals, Recipes and Dog Tags now convert correctly when a player deploys into X01 from Hajin.
- DMZ: 3D Printer recipes completed earlier at the FOB can now be untracked.
- Multiplayer only: aim assist is switched off when firing through Smoke Grenades and the Smoke Wall; flinch tuning for assault rifles, SMGs, LMGs, pistols and several single weapons (Axion, Hyeon Burst, Patriot XMR, Oris 8.6, Mar-9), launchers now cause flinch; the notes list these under the Multiplayer heading only.
