# GamerFuzion: MW4 DMZ irradiated container location, solo yellow locker route

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 27 Oct 2026, modified 28 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (live runs):
- The Irradiated Container (Ultra) is found in yellow lockers, often labelled Chemical Storage, inside clinics and fire station storage rooms; most yellow lockers do not hold one.
- Clinics visited on the route: Cheongun Village, one southwest of the village near Compound Charlie, Pyonghwa Village (medical room), Tong-il Village, Deadtown (locker room in a green building, sign over a doorway off the main corridor), Imjin Farmland (with a keypad door), next to Wolves Stadium; plus a pharmacy with a green cross on the Cheongun outskirts.
- The Cheongun Village fire station next to the clinic held a Durable (purple) Gas Mask and a Radiation Blockers recipe; the Fire Station Storage key lay on a desk; fire station guards upstairs; restrooms and a roof weapon crate can hold good loot.
- The Deathstalker's banner appeared over Deadtown during the run. Pyonghwa was foggy.
- Exfil by helicopter in Hajin City; another run's exfil was just south of Wolves Stadium. Each route took about 20 minutes.
