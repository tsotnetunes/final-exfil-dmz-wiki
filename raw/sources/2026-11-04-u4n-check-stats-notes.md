Notes in our own words:
- The Career menu has Progression (player level, DMZ level, Prestige), Multiplayer Stats (K/D, win rate, recent matches, weapons), Challenges and Profile.
- Weapon level and camos show in the Gunsmith on the weapon's Progression tab.
- DMZ is described through its own progression and resource displays; no DMZ K/D or extraction rate is listed.
