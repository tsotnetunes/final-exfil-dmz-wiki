# GamerFuzion: DMZ Orders page 10 guide

Facts taken, in our words:
- Page 10 holds Crashed Crate, Surprise Me and Healer; the third Order is named Healer in both the table and heading.
- Loot Radio Tower: the author used a tower at Imjin Farmland, climbing by ladders and beams, with the crate in red smoke at the top; heal first.
- Lost Supply Drops: one on a hill north of Deadtown beside an exfil, which the author says returns each infil; others near Operation Iron Citadel, in the hospital lobby and on the ocean side by the reactor.
- The random Story Mission asks for a full squad, so bring players or use fill.
- The Armor Box is listed under Field Upgrades in the 3D Printer.
