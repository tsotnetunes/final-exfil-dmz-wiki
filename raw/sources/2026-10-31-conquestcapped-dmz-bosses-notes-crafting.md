# ConquestCapped: MW4 DMZ bosses
URL: https://conquestcapped.com/guides/call-of-duty/mw4-dmz-bosses/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (updated 28 Oct 2026; first publication date not shown)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (crafting-related lines only):
- Colossus roams, often in The Grid, around Wolves Stadium and near SAM sites; it drops Military Composites, a Legendary ingredient used for late FOB upgrades.
- Deathstalker flies over the whole map, most often over downtown Hajin City; it drops a Prototype RF Module (Legendary). Its wreck breaks into nose, body and tail, each a separate loot container.
- The tank patrols roads, most often in The Grid; it drops Nano-Structured Alloy and other armor ingredients; players say that alloy goes into a 3-plate vest recipe.
- The first kill of each Commander pays about 50,000 DMZ Cash; Commanders drop no Dog Tags.
- The Doctor's key opens his Command Office at Mirae General Hospital (grid F5).
- Experimental Compounds are not mentioned.
