# Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 patch notes: the 10 Nov 2026 section
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes
Captured: 2026-11-11 (from the app's patch reader, which re-read the page at 18:00 UTC on 10 Nov 2026, and a direct read of the page; the official accounts announced the update at 16:13 UTC on 10 Nov 2026 and the X01 UAV change at 17:39 UTC)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- A fourth Season 1 update rolled out on all platforms on 10 Nov 2026; its lines sit under a "November 10, 2026" heading on the same page.
- Economy: a player who dies or otherwise fails to exfil now gets a cash payout worked out from the value of the items they had looted and how long they were deployed. It does not apply in Story Missions. No formula or minimum time is given.
- UAVs: the duration and range of the UAV and Advanced UAV Killstreaks (Fire Support) were cut a lot; no figures are given. The official post called this X01 UAV tuning. Later the same day the notes were edited: UAVs and Advanced UAVs can no longer be deployed in X01 at all.
- Traits: the Mini Tracker Minor Trait's radius is a little smaller.
- Fixes: the Crude Diethyl Ether item in the Chemical Denial Dynamic Operation could float; AI could stay stuck in a vehicle's passenger seat; explosions under a departing exfil helicopter could hurt players already aboard; players could be blocked from exfilling through the crank gate elevator shaft in X01's Power Generators area; players could get stuck in X01's Vault Elevator after shutting its doors on an upper floor; bodies of eliminated players could drop through the floor of the X01 Deep Vault; the Wheelson Commander's turret could keep following its target after a DDoS.
- Recently addressed: Platinum camo challenges for the Cronen Osprey and CSG-35 now track; Map Voting works when only two maps are on offer; headshots count for the Sniper BlackCell camo challenge.
- No new known issues were listed under 10 Nov; the page points to the Trello board and the update account.
