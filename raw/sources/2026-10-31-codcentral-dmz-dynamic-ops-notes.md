# COD Central: MW4 DMZ Dynamic Ops: Every Operation and Its Rewards
URL: https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-dynamic-ops/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 21 Oct 2026, updated 30 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (fan site; payouts are single runs from early access, 20 to 22 Oct, before the 30 Oct cash change)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Twelve Dynamic Ops seen. Start one from the deploy screen or from a phone in the field; phones move each match. Each step usually showed +250 XP or +300 cash on the Op panel. The menu card promised 10,000 to 20,000 per Op, but single Ops paid about 6,800 to 14,880. A squad can vote to cancel an active Op from the tac map. Locations, keypad codes and sometimes step order change between matches; the code appears in the step text.
- Viral Reckoning: starts vary (a locker at the 14th Political Prison, a phone in the South Korean Border Zone, Songdo Wharf). Find an HVT with comms access (or use a UAV Tower to locate the scientist), kill him, take his radio, reach the scientist, enter the keypad code, rescue the scientist, defend the landing zone until the helicopter arrives, put the scientist aboard, then either leave for partial credit or take an explosive and plant it on a biological WMD. Panel: +4,230 XP and +7,500. Waypoints were 650 to 915 m apart.
- Ghost Shipment: starts in the South Korean Wilderness or at a radio on a workbench in Tong-il Village. Find and kill a marked HVT, take his Classified Orders, reach and capture a SAM site, wait for it to down a helicopter; one version adds looting a Lieutenant's cargo container from a netted crate. About 4,500 to 5,032 XP; +7,500 in one run. Lieutenant The Doctor was seen at Mirae General Hospital during one run.
- Chemical Denial: two versions. Scavenger version: loot an item from a sedan's trunk (Jayon Warehouses), gather three chemical components, craft the compound at a workbench (Hanrim Village), drop it in a metal case, then the site explodes. Array version at Cheonwang Peak Array (Medium): restore power, hack the comms server, protect the data transfer. Panel +3,000 XP and +5,000; the array version paid 7,100 and 4,750 XP at extraction. Lieutenant Iskra appeared at the array.
- Toxic Intercept: from the South Korean Border Zone. Loot an encrypted drive from a hidden cache (Oamsik Turtle Island), decrypt it at an enemy computer (a tent at Compound Delta) and defend it, request a fuel resupply at a UAV Tower (NuriGO West), hijack the marked yellow fuel tanker, drive it to the marked spot. +4,500 XP on the panel. Enemies push the tent during the defence.
- Dead Man's Cache: from the South Korean shoreline. Hidden cache on Oamsik Turtle Island (a hard drive and explosives), code door to the HVT's room at Songdo Wharf, download the cache location on a laptop, take a bomb from a dead drop, find the ammo and armor cache under Songdo Pier, blow it up, kill the HVT. 5,146 XP and 9,900 in one run; +4,500 XP and +7,500 in a later one. Its care package held a Rare Medium Backpack and counted for the "loot a Dynamic Op crate" task.
- Signal Breaker: into the North Korean wilderness; three bomb parts, a workbench at Namjin Riverside, bombs on three radar trucks near Imjin Farmland. +6,000 XP and +10,000; it also unlocked a weapon.
- Broken Resupply: one run started at a phone behind the bar of the Heavenly Luck Casino (this ticked the Cheongun Village phone Order). Hack a comms server in Cheongun Village and defend it, loot the bomb (a hidden cache in the South Korean Border Zone), find the keypad code in a safe (Compound Charlie), enter it (different codes in two runs), plant the bomb on the WMD (red barrels) and clear out. +2,993 XP and +5,000; it unlocked the ISO Nightshade. Two crates held stims, an inhaler and a vest.
- Special Delivery: three bomb parts (Songdo Wharf, a dumpster dead drop, a hidden cache in the Construction Zone), craft at a workbench (Construction Zone), capture a UAV Tower (South Island), reach the mobile launch platform (Shoreline Station), plant the bomb and defend it during a countdown, take cover. +4,500 XP and +7,500; it unlocked the Mojave .357.
- Domino Protocol: starts at a dresser in a Pyeonghwa Village house or a kitchen in NuriGO West. Kill a first HVT and take his documents, kill a second (Imjin Suburb) and take his intel, go to a Lieutenant (east side of the 14th Political Prison, High) and beat them. 12,400 and 8,000 XP at extraction; the Lieutenant's Dog Tag added 25,000. In another run the Lieutenant was Bale in Deadtown.
- Sky Heist: first half varies (intel from a safe, a hidden cache, or by restoring power; some runs add a SAM site at Compound Alpha); second half: kill the pilot, take the keys, reach the helicopter, fly it to the exfil within about 3:45. 11,880 and 6,500 XP at extraction. Disposable parachutes are inside the helicopter. Enemy helicopters chase you; a Deathstalker appeared over The Grid.
- Hunt Operator: a Wanted Operator banner names a target with a 5,000 bounty; kill them to finish. +6,000 XP; 600 cash at extraction. Colossus roamed the bunker where the target was.
- Ignition Point: finished in Deadtown about a minute after deploying, on arriving in the area. +5,000 on the panel, 3,000 XP. Seen at low levels and at DMZ level 70.
- The Survivalist Trait Objective Oriented raises Op crates one rarity tier, up to Rare.
