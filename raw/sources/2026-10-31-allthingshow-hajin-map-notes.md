Facts taken, in our words:
- Threat levels and POI descriptions follow the official guide (Low, Medium, High, Critical, Extreme; building colours red for fire stations, blue for police, green for clinics).
- A fire station stands northeast of Deadtown beside the railway.
- Police stations: the one in Pyonghwa Village (spelled Pyeonghwa) has its Armory door in the basement by the holding cells; Revolution Dam has a police station with an Armory door; another station sits near Wolves Stadium. The locked Armory needs a key; some stations have roof access.
- SAM sites can be retaken by enemies once you leave the area.
- Bale is met at the Tank Depot; buying his intel turns the objective into a paid Kill Bale task, and he drops his own dog tag.
- Sky Heist Dynamic Op: capture the SAM site at Cheongun Village, hack and hold a heavy helicopter in the South Korean Border Zone, then fly it to the exfil marked on the Tac-Map, where it switches to autopilot; you can extract or jump.
- Exfil sites come from a fixed pool and show as green smoke; squads may wait in ambush there.
