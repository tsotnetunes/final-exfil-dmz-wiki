Facts taken, in our words:
- Token types: 2XP for the player level and Prestige; Weapon 2XP; Battle Pass 2XP, usable from Season 1; a Dual code gives one 2XP and one Weapon 2XP token.
- Tokens run only during matches.
- The Campaign's 3 Double XP and 2 Double Weapon XP tokens can be used in Multiplayer and DMZ from launch.
- As of 30 Oct 2026 no Double XP weekend had been announced for MW4; the site guesses 30 Oct to 2 Nov from earlier launches (its own projection).
- US Monster Energy codes run 1 Sep 2026 to 31 Mar 2027 with 15-minute tokens, one code a day, plus a launch bonus token for codes entered 23 to 25 Oct.
- Nothing on Operator or Dog Tag XP.
