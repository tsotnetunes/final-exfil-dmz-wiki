Facts taken, in our words:
- The site says the Legendary Roze's Highrise Armory key comes from completing the Operation Grid Assault Story Mission (with a Helicopter and $25,000), not from random loot; killing Roze in that mission gives the second code segment.
- The armory is in the Netshin Tech Complex next to DBN Headquarters, The Grid, grid G6, floor 14, east side, behind a plain teal door; no elevator reaches the top floors.
- Inside: one Lieutenant crate, two weapon lockers and loot bags; contents partly random.
- Jang-ui guards the floor 14 server hall; his drop is a Legendary or Ultra Paektu rifle worth up to $11,200.
- Operation Grid Assault unlocks after the three Tier 1 missions; The Grid and the Netshin Tech Complex are Extreme.
