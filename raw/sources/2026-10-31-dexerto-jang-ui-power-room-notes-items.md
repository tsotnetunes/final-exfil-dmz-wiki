- A Legendary key that Jang-Ui, a Lieutenant at NuriGO Mall (H6), drops.
- It opens the closed-off generator room on the mall's lowest floor, beside the robot-dog agility course.
- Inside: two weapon lockers, a loot chest and a Lieutenant crate with high-tier weapons and loot.
