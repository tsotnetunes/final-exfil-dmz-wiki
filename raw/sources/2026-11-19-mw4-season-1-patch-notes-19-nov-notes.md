# Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 patch notes: 19 Nov 2026 block
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes
Captured: 2026-11-20 (read from Final Exfil's parsed copy of the page, re-parsed at 19:30 UTC on 19 Nov 2026; the update was announced by the official updates account and by Infinity Ward at about 18:00 UTC on 19 Nov 2026)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- Live events: a new DMZ event, War Chest, starts on 19 Nov. Opening containers and safes during a DMZ deployment scores points on a leaderboard: 5 points for an ordinary container, 50 for a safe.
- War Chest rewards named: Fulton Exfils, Tempered Armor Vests and UAVs, among others. A leaderboard runs for 3 hours and a new one starts every 4 hours. Each reward can be won only once, however many leaderboards you play.
- Live events: Wager Match is listed as live in all modes; Joint Fire is listed as coming back on Friday, for 24 hours every Friday of Season 1.
- Global: stability fixes; the "Tracked and Near Complete" tab could show camo challenges as complete too early; Natural Concealment challenge completions did not always show a notification in matches or in the Event menu; the studio intro could replay after opening settings. All fixed.
- Multiplayer: more Weapon, Player and Match Bonus XP in Wager Match modes and Ground War: Combat Outpost; enemies killed by several players drop extra Wager Match Trophies.
- Weapon tuning, listed under the Multiplayer heading only (no DMZ section this day):
  - M4: head multiplier 1.3 to 1.35.
  - Cronen Squall: max damage range 27.94 m to 31.24 m; mid damage range 43.18 m to 47.24 m; min damage 22 to 24.
  - Oden: max damage range 33.02 m to 36.83 m; min damage 32 to 34.
  - Patriot XMR: min damage 24 to 25.
  - Hyeon Auto Apex (Hyeon Burst): max damage range 26.42 m to 33.53 m; min damage 29 to 30; head multiplier 1.3 to 1.4.
  - Kastov 545 Apex (Kastov 762): max damage range 11.02 m to 13.72 m; mid damage range 20.57 m to 27.69 m.
  - CSG-35: max damage range 10.41 m to 11.18 m; mid damage range 19.81 m to 22.61 m.
  - ISO Nightshade: max damage range 12.45 m to 13.97 m; leg and foot multipliers 0.95 to 1.
  - PPSh-41: max damage 18 to 19; upper torso, lower torso and neck multipliers 1 to 1.06.
  - X-58 Nyx: max damage range 11.94 m to 12.45 m.
  - FiNN LMG: max damage 30 to 31; max damage range 21.84 m to 20.32 m; lower torso multiplier 1 to 1.1.
  - Type 73: max damage 22 to 23; min damage 19 to 20; lower torso 1 to 1.1. With the Assault Apex: max damage 24 to 25; mid damage 23 to 24; mid damage range 43.13 m to 58.42 m; lower torso 1 to 1.1.
  - Mosin-Nagant: max damage 95 to 91; max damage range 14.99 m to 43.18 m; mid damage 93 to 86; mid damage range 44.96 m to 58.42 m; the second mid damage step removed; min damage 60 to 68.
  - Rezi 12 Enforcer Apex, Explosive Slug Round only: mid damage range 27.69 m to 29.59 m; muzzle velocity 220 to 245 m/s.
