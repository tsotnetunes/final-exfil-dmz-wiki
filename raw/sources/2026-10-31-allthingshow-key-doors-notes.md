# AllThings.How: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ key locations, which door each key opens

Facts taken, in our words:
- A key's item card names a grid square; find that square on the map, then the door with the matching name. Locked doors read LOCKED until you carry the key. Opening a door gives 300 XP (375 XP for a gun store locker). Keys can be lost with your backpack.
- Doors listed (19, not all keys): Mirae Electrical Room (Mirae General Hospital, F5, basement car park level B2); Counting Room Vault (Heavenly Luck Casino, F4, staff area behind the gaming floor); Mechanic's Shack (Cheongun Village, F5, south of the clinic); Police Station Armory (Pyonghwa Village station basement; also Revolution Dam station); Militia Arms Depot (Hajin River Heights, G7, lower floor of Gangbyeon Apartment B); Field Depot (Chang-san Air Base, hangar with a white door and red panel); Re-Education Center Annex and Underground Propaganda Store Room (Re-Education Center, Deadtown); Executive Arms Auction (DBN Headquarters); Rice Mill Bunker (Imjin Farmland, green metal door with round knob); The Doctor's Command Office (Mirae General Hospital); Commandant Office (Admin Complex No. 2, Chang-san Air Base); Shop Records Room (Wolves Stadium, F7); Coin Mining Den (NuriGO West, G6, landing marked 3); Vault Armory (Heavenly Luck Casino car park); Haksan School Armory (Deadtown); Gun Store Weapons Lockers (western Cheongun Village and northern Songdo Wharf; one locker per key); Supreme Leader's Bunker (Chang-san Air Base Bunker, B5 to B6, needs an access card); Hazard Storehouse (Haneul Nuclear Reactor, I4 to I5, shed with a light-blue door).
- Key sources named: Mirae key from Operation Critical Extraction; Coin Mining Den key from Operation Shopping Hacks; Counting Room key from Operation Silent Ledger (also seen in an industrial locker at the Rice Mill and a military locker at the Haksan School Armory); The Doctor's Command Office key from The Doctor, the Lieutenant at Mirae General Hospital; the Militia Arms Depot key once found in a duffel bag in the Citadel's east wing.
