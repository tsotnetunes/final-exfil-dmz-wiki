# Destructoid: MW4 DMZ interview with Infinity Ward
URL: https://www.destructoid.com/mw4-dmz-interview-infinity-ward/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 6 Jun 2026; interviews during a late-May 2026 studio visit)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (from the multiplayer creative director and a multiplayer technical designer):
- DMZ squads are teams of three.
- Solo players can drop in alone, use proximity chat or stay stealthy, and can do all of the content; the Tourniquet lets a solo player get back up without teammates.
- Matchmaking is based on squad size, so solos meet other solos (the team's explanation as reported).
- The base has a 3D printer, a gunsmith and a merchant; each Operator has a skill tree of Traits.
- Dynamic Operations grew out of watching players run contracts in the 2022 beta.
- More content will arrive through seasonal updates over the year.
