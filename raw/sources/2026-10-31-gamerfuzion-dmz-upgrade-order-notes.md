Facts taken, in our words. Costs were read from the 3D Printer's upgrade screens in the author's own footage at DMZ level 57, except MIA Recovery Discount II, which came from a player's screenshot:
- Stash Item Upgrade I: 18 more item slots for 10,000, 10 Rigid Synthetics and 10 Electronic Sensors. Upgrade II: 18 more for 50,000, 20 Detonators, 10 Nylon Cloth and 5 Power Regulators.
- Bank Upgrade III: cap of 1,000,000 for 100,000 and 5 Infantry Invasion Plans (the author's Bank showed a 500,000 cap).
- MIA Recovery Discount I: 5% cheaper recoveries for 5 Regional Combat Notes, 10 Control Units and 10 Rigid Synthetics, no cash. Level II: another 5% for 5 Nuclear Program Files, 5 Degraded Execution Footage, 10 Binding Catalyst and 10 Nitro Compounds.
- Gunsmith Upgrade I: 5% off Gunsmith purchases for 50,000, 20 Reactive Metals, 10 CPU and 5 Power Regulators.
- Stash Weapon Upgrade I: 6 more weapon slots for 50,000, 20 Reactive Metals, 10 Stock Rods and 5 Comms Modules.
- Vendor Item Upgrade I: more Vendor offers for 20 Electronic Sensors, 10 CPU, 10 Printed Circuit Boards and 2 Prototype RF Modules, no cash.
- Epic Ingredient Printing: 100,000, 10 Binding Catalyst, 10 Printed Circuit Boards and 5 Military Composites.
- The author's suggested order: Stash Item I and II, Bank III, MIA Recovery Discount I, Gunsmith I, Stash Weapon I, Vendor Item I, then Epic Ingredient Printing.
- Do not sell valuables that upgrades use, such as Infantry Invasion Plans (Bank III) and Degraded Execution Footage (MIA Recovery Discount II).
- The author paid about 50,000 to recover a high-rank Operator (a single observation).
