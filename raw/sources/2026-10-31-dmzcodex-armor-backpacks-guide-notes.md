# dmzcodex.com: MW4 DMZ armor and backpacks guide (plate carriers, Secure Slots)
URL: https://dmzcodex.com/guides/dmz-armor-backpacks-guide/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 17 Oct 2026, updated 24 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (fan site; not the DMZ Codex at finalexfil.com)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Eight plate carriers: 1-Plate Armor Vest (Uncommon, 1 plate); 2-Plate Armor Vest (Rare, 2); 2-Plate Ballistic Gel Vest (Epic, 2, armour that refills); 2-Plate Helmet Armor Vest (Epic, 2, less damage from headshots); 3-Plate Armor Vest (Legendary, 3); 3-Plate Ballistic Gel Vest (Ultra, 3, refilling armour); 3-Plate Helmet Armor Vest (Ultra, 3, headshot protection); Tempered Armor Vest (Ultra; heavily armoured, uses enhanced plates).
- The site says the gel-vest refill comes from reveal commentary and community recipe data, and that refill delay and speed are not measured; helmet vests cut headshot damage, but no immunity to any shot is established.
- There are no separate helmets besides the helmet vests.
- Plates fill your Armor slot when you infil; refill at Ammo & Armor Caches, which cool down after use; extra plates only through certain Traits or the Armor Box Field Upgrade, which drops plates for the squad. The site gives a community recipe for the Armor Box and warns that sources disagree.
- Field Upgrades do not recharge during a deployment (the site's statement).
- A Tempered Armor Vest recipe was seen once in the Lieutenant crate in The Doctor's Command Office at Mirae General Hospital; the site says it is not a guaranteed drop.
- Secure Slot counts per backpack: Small 0, Medium 1, Medium Weapon 1, Small Secure 3, Medium Scavenger 2, Large 2, Large Scavenger 3, Medium Secure 4, Large Secure 4, Secure Scavenger 4. Resource and weapon space is separate from the protected slots.
- The Secure action moves an eligible item into the protected section; mission items go into a separate Mission Item slot.
