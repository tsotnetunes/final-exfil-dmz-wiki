# Patch Crazy: Crashed Crate Challenge Guide MW4 DMZ

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 22 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (guide site)

Facts taken, in our words:
- The Crashed Crate Order asks for three Lost Supply Drops. Ordinary drops (from SAM site captures or finished missions) do not count.
- Lost Supply Drops give off red smoke, and an alert appears on the left of the screen when you are close. Any red-smoke drop counts.
- One drop sat on a small island in the north-east; one hung on the front of the General Hospital building and had to be knocked down with an explosive (frag, C4 or a grenade launcher) before it could be opened; a third was near the same hospital. Enemy reinforcements can arrive soon after you find a drop.
- Loot Radio Tower side op: find tower icons on the Tac Map (hovering or getting close brings the side op notice); towers are usually guarded; the ladder stops partway, so you jump across and keep climbing; at the top you open a Tower Drop crate. The guide suggests Free Roam rather than a Story deployment.
