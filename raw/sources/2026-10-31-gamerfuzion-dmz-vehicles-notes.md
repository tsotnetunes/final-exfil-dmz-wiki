Facts taken, in our words:
- Built from the Vehicle Intel section of the official DMZ Deep Dive Part 1 guide of 5 Oct 2026; no hands-on play.
- 14 vehicles: 8 ground (Camper, Sedan, Grizzly, Dreadnought, Polaris Dagor A1, LTV, Cargo Truck, D50 Radar Flatbed), 2 rail (Train, Tram), 1 water (RHIB), 3 air (Heli, Heavy Chopper, Monarch).
- The only seat count given is the Cargo Truck's two-seat cab; the LTV's optional .50 cal, the D50's vehicle UAV and the Heavy Chopper's flares are the only weapons or equipment named.
- DMZ vehicles have finite armour and fuel and need repairs and refuelling, which Warzone vehicles do not.
- Marine Gas Stations are small jetties with two pumps; Armored Patrol Boats and RHIBs gain most from them, and carefully parked ground or air vehicles can use them too; Gas Cans refill there.
- Exact vehicle stats and spawn locations had not been published.
