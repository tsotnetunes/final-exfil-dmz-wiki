# Gfinity Esports: Call of Duty DMZ plagued by game-breaking tech issues
URL: https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/call-of-duty-dmz-plagued-by-game-breaking-tech-issues
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 22 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- About MW4's DMZ during early access, which opened on 20 Oct 2026 (full launch 23 Oct).
- Frequent server crashes disconnected players from matches.
- Players who were disconnected lost their whole loadout and gear; CharlieINTEL reported that hundreds of DMZ players were affected.
- Infinity Ward was working on fixes; a note shared by the Call of Duty Updates account said further server-side changes had been released to deal with reports of server disconnects (no date given in the article).
- No error codes and no compensation are mentioned.
