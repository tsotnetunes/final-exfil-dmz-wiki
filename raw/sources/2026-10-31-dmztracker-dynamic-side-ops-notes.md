# DMZTracker: MW4 DMZ Dynamic Ops and Side Ops Explained
URL: https://dmztracker.gg/guides/dynamic-ops-side-ops-guide/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 20 Oct 2026, last checked by the site 30 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (community site)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Dynamic Ops: pick one at deploy or start one from a green phone inside a building; only one runs at a time; no cap per match was found. Dynamic Ops show as green phone icons, Side Ops as white markers. Cancel from the Tac Map. Deploying straight into an Op does not count for the Order that asks you to collect a phone; use Free Roam.
- Pay seen before the 29 Oct change: about 5,000 to 10,500 per Op although the card advertised 10,000 to 20,000; Dead Man's Cache 7,500 and 4,575 XP; Signal Breaker 6,000 XP and 10,000; Ignition Point 5,000 XP and it unlocked the Han 86. Each finished Op calls in a care package worth 75 XP when opened.
- Side Op types listed: activate a UAV Tower; download intel at a Lieutenant Hunt tower; Loot Radio Tower; Capture a SAM Site; secure a lost Supply Drop; Crack Safe; Clear the Military Compound; repair trucks; fallout zones; a crashed drone (reported as Loot Crashed Drone); kill a Lieutenant.
- UAV Tower: pays DMZ Cash and pings nearby players and AI; the map shows 14 towers. Lieutenant Hunt tower: marks the nearest Lieutenant; the press says five on the official map. Radio Tower: climb and loot the crate at the top. SAM Site: capture and hold it; it shoots down aircraft but you lose it if you go too far; a capture takes a little over 30 seconds; four named: Deadtown, Imjin Farmland, NuriGO West, the Citadel roof. Crack Safe: a safe marked on the Tac-Map, no key or code. Military Compound: kill every soldier. Kill a Lieutenant: a free Kill side op can start when you meet a Lieutenant (for example Kill Roze in Grid Assault), unlike Paid Intel.
- Side Op pay before 29 Oct: 500 each, paid at extraction on a Side Ops Payout line, plus XP (one run: three Side Ops for 1,500; Crack Safe 225 XP).
