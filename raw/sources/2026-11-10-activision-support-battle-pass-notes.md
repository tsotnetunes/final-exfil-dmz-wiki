# Activision Support: Modern Warfare 4 Battle Pass
URL: https://support.activision.com/modern-warfare-4/articles/modern-warfare-4-battle-pass
Captured: 2026-11-10
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- Owners of the paid Battle Pass are guaranteed tiers 1 to 50: one tier unlocks by itself each day of the season until day 50.
- That daily tier is skipped on a day when you are already at or past the expected tier for that day.
- Buying the paid pass partway through the season grants the tiers that would have unlocked by then; upgrading from the free pass lets you claim everything up to your current tier.
- The Battle Pass Bundle includes 20 Tier Skip Tokens. Bundle owners can spend them on rewards up to 10 tiers ahead of where they are, BlackCell owners up to 20 tiers ahead. Spending one does not move your position on the track.
- Tokens left at the end of the season are spent for you on the lowest rewards you have not claimed; they do not carry into the next season.
- Battle Pass progress and rewards belong to the Activision account, so they follow you to every platform. COD Points earned from the pass can be spent only on the platform where they were unlocked.
- XP from Multiplayer, DMZ and Warzone all moves the same Battle Pass.
- The article does not mention a setting that turns off automatic unlocks or claiming.
