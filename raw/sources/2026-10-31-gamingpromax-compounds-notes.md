# Gaming ProMax: MW4 DMZ all compound locations and how to clear them

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 26 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Six compounds: Alpha (east of Imjin Farmland toward Daesong Ridge), Bravo (south of Wolves Stadium), Charlie (east of Pyonghwa Village in the Cheongun Outskirts area), Delta (south of the Haneul Nuclear Reactor near Shoreline Station), Echo (Hajin River Heights), Foxtrot (south of the 14th Political Prison toward Chang-san Air Base, among the hardest).
- Entering the perimeter starts the Clear the Military Compound Side Op with a progress meter; about 10 to 12 AI guard each, sometimes a tougher named enemy. Clearing it to 100 percent unlocks the safe, usually in the main building, at the guard tower's base or in a command tent.
- Compounds are fixed but another squad may have cleared one already. Some spawn vehicles.
- Safe loot is random: crafting ingredients, Infantry Invasion Plans (most often Charlie and Delta), Regional Combat Notes (reported from Bravo), DMZ Cash, sometimes blueprints. Hitting compounds helps the Open a Safe Order.
