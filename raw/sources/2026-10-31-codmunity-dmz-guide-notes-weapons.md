# CODMunity: MW4 DMZ Beginner Guide: How to Play, Loot, Craft and Exfil
URL: https://codmunity.gg/dmz/guide
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 24 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- A Weapon Manual lets you buy that gun in the Gunsmith; it is looted, used up when you extract, and a squadmate can hand one to you during a match.
- Trailer prices before launch: M4 around 5,000, assault rifles 4,750, SMGs 2,850, each new slot 1,000, with a Gunsmith discount in the cart. After launch, a creator reported paying 50,000 to open a fifth attachment slot, on top of the attachment.
- The Vendor opens at DMZ level 5, rotates its stock daily and can offer guns you have not unlocked yet, such as the Cronen Squall (player level 55 in Multiplayer).
- Blueprints from the Vault Edition or store bundles appear under Buy New Weapon; the first build of each was free.
- Free Loadout: one weapon, one Lethal, one Tactical, a melee weapon and one plate, no backpack and no parachute.
- Loot rarity: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Ultra. Damaged weapons work but perform worse than clean ones.
- The Stash starts with 12 weapon slots, separate from the item slots.
