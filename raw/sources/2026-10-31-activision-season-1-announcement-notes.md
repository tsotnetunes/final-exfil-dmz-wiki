# Activision blog: Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 and Call of Duty: Warzone Season 1 announcement
URL: https://callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 28 Oct 2026, Call of Duty Staff)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- Season 1 for MW4 and Warzone starts at midnight Eastern on 6 Nov 2026, which is 9 PM Pacific on 5 Nov, on every platform. Warzone details follow in their own post on Monday 2 Nov.
- New DMZ location: X01, which the post calls a Black Site. It is a weapons plant nobody knew about, thought to have been a GRANIT Arms black site, now held by North Korean forces. It is a former Russian facility at the edge of Hajin, near the northern border of North Korea, and you deploy to it through the Hajin Exclusion Zone.
- Inside X01: cargo bays, vehicle depots, underground labs, production lines and a sealed Deep Vault; tunnels, routes that climb over several floors, and hazards that change.
- X01 enemies: troops and specialists of the 100 Spirits NK faction, rogue Operator squads and tougher patrolling enemies. New enemy types are kept secret.
- Ghost hands out new DMZ Orders, spread over several pages.
- In X01 the extraction points shut as the facility locks down; you have to finish set steps to get out, so keep track of the closest exfil.
- New bosses: Commander The Captain, a Juggernaut with an elite guard, in X01; Commander Wheelson, an autonomous Commander that patrols; Lieutenants Tae-Hyun ("Knuckle") and Chol-Min ("The Wrench"), with intel on the Boss Board.
- DMZ events in Season 1: Threat Briefing: Battle Tank (in Hajin, destroy a North Korean main battle tank and carry out one tank part per deployment; more parts over several deployments give better rewards); Threat Briefing: The Captain (in X01, defeat The Captain and collect pieces of his helmet; exfil with them for bigger rewards). When one squad member exfils with a Commander part, the whole squad gets credit. Resupply Run: collect set items around points of interest and trade them for valuable gear such as Self Revives and 3-Plate Carriers. More Threat Briefings come later in the season.
- Threat Briefings reward rare gear and DMZ Survival Kits; X01 holds equipment found nowhere else.
- FOB: a Battle Pass and Store Station next to the Survival Kit Station; the Vendor sells extra weapons and gear for DMZ Cash to players with the Season 1 BlackCell; the 3D Printer change is kept secret.
- Two Multiplayer events also count in DMZ: the Wager Match Event (its rewards include the Hyeon Auto and Kastov 545 Apex Attachments) and the Joint Fire Event (one reward is a DMZ Survival Kit).
- Battle Pass: 1,100 COD Points (bundle 2,400); 100 tiers and over 100 rewards; buyers who fall behind are caught up to the current day of the season, up to day 50; instant rewards include a 10% Battle Pass XP boost and a DMZ Survival Kit; completing it gives another DMZ Survival Kit, 200 COD Points and the Tier 100 Ultra Bellum Mosin-Nagant Blueprint. The Battle Pass is opened from a DMZ FOB station.
- BlackCell: price not given in the post; includes the XERO Operator, 1,100 COD Points, a DMZ Survival Pack and a BlackCell clan tag; further DMZ benefits to be explained before the season starts. The Vault Edition unlocks BlackCell; Standard owners can upgrade on the same platform; buying BlackCell after the Battle Pass returns 1,100 COD Points.
- Warzone: Hajin and Zodiac are new maps; Verdansk and Rebirth Island return; MW4 becomes fully part of free-to-play Warzone.
- Later in the season (no dates): Season 1 Reloaded with more maps and modes, the JAK 12 shotgun, the ZLR Talonhawk melee weapon, camo events, the Joint Fire Event and more DMZ Threat Briefings.
- Not in the post: any DMZ wipe or progress reset, new Story Missions, mid-season dates, a full list of weapons usable in DMZ.
