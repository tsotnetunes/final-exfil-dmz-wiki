# AllThingsHow: Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ: How to Beat the Deathstalker Commander
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=297388
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- An attack helicopter Commander that roams Hajin; a COMMANDER / DEATHSTALKER banner shows near it; tracked by its icon on the Tac-Map.
- Seen over The Grid (Critical), NuriGO West (Medium, with a SAM site), Compound Delta and NuriGO Mall on Expressway No. 1, DBN Headquarters (Extreme, helipad) and Cheongun Village (Low). In Operation Grid Assault it attacks the DBN helipad.
- SAM site: ping it and the nearest SAM site, clear the guards, hold the capture zone for a little over 30 seconds; the launcher fires one missile that downs it. Shoot it to pull it back if it drifts away.
- Assault rifle bursts at the cockpit and hull took under a minute; a scoped rifle from the DBN helipad about 25 seconds; a mounted Type 73 LMG with armour-piercing rounds killed it over a few passes.
- Wreck: containers labelled Helicopter Piece with ingredients such as a Printed Circuit Board, Prototype RF Module (Legendary, $3,500) and Volatile Explosives; also a Drone Motherboard used by the Death From Above Order.
- Reward $50,000 from the Commanders tab of the Boss Board; the kill showed DESTROYED DEATHSTALKER COMMANDER and 605 XP.
