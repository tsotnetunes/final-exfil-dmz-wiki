Facts taken, in our words:
- Operators earn XP from tasks, objectives and kills; after a successful exfil with enough XP the Dog Tag levels up and pays one Trait Point.
- An Operator can hold at most 18 Traits; points can go into one, two or all three trees.
- Major Traits are always visible; Minor Traits are random and appear once the Major before them on the path is unlocked.
- Effect descriptions for the 21 Traits named in the official guide, matching it, with no numbers.
