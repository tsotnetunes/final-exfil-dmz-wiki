# GamerFuzion: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ Dynamic Ops, Side Ops and Orders Explained
URL: https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-dynamic-ops-side-ops-orders-explained-rewards-dead-mans-cache-chemical-denial/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 13 Oct 2026, before launch)
Trust: third-party (guide site reading the official Dynamic Ops trailer of 6 Oct 2026 frame by frame; in-development footage)

Facts taken, in our words:
- On the trailer HUD, Dead Man's Cache opens with an objective to collect an encrypted drive, at Cheongun Outskirts; Chemical Denial opens with an objective to look into a power outage, shown at the Tank Depot and inside the Chang-san Air Base bunker.
- Side Ops shown on screen: Download Intel To Locate Lieutenant, Activate UAV Tower (banner: Deadtown), Paid Intel (reach Bale, then kill Bale), Secure Lost Supply Drop. Finishing Kill Bale showed a +500 cash badge. Bale dropped a Legendary Dog Tag and a Legendary LMG.
- The trailer's deploy screen showed a payout of 10,000 to 20,000 per Dynamic Op, paid into the Bank on completion. The writer's reading: because Op cash is banked at once, it may be safe even if the run fails.
- Orders sit on the right of the HUD; Story Missions, Dynamic Ops and Side Ops on the left. Location Orders put an objective on the Tac-Map that you can ping.
- An early Underground 1/2 screen showed Eye in the Sky and Triangulation; the second Order was named Joyride in that build (later lists call it Watch for Bumps).
