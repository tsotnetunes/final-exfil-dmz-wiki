Read through a web-fetch tool. The equipment lists describe Multiplayer; the page adds that DMZ has its own set of equipment to find in Hajin or make at the 3D Printer, with many of the same items in it. Multiplayer unlock levels and kill costs are not noted here.

Facts taken, in our words:
- Lethals (11): Frag Grenade (can be cooked); Sticky Grenade (attaches to surfaces, goes off on a timer); Drill Charge (a thermal lance bores into a surface, then fires an explosive round); Proximity Mine (springs up and blows; can cling to a passing vehicle); Zip Gun (a one-shot pistol made on a printer, for close range); Trip Wire (a wire trap; several can be linked); Thermite (sticky incendiary, heavy damage to vehicles and armour); Molotov Cocktail (bursts on impact and leaves burning pools); Claymore (a mine triggered by proximity); C4 (sticks, set off remotely); Throwing Knife (kills on a hit, can be picked up again).
- Tacticals (9): Flash Grenade (blinds and deafens); Smoke Grenade (cuts sight and automatic targeting; bullets and blasts still pass); Snapshot Grenade (briefly shows nearby enemies to your team); Concussion Grenade (slows reactions, blurs vision); Heartbeat Sensor (a tablet on battery that shows enemies close by); Shock Stick (clings to surfaces, shocks people, destroys equipment, disrupts vehicles); Decoy Grenade (fakes gunfire, footsteps and radar blips); Stim (starts health regeneration, refreshes Tactical Sprint, eases flash and stun effects); Screamer Mine (suppresses and marks enemies who come near).
- Field Upgrades (12), each with a recharge speed of fast, medium or slow in Multiplayer: Portable Radar (a regular ping shows enemies nearby); DDOS (a pulse jams nearby enemy electronics); Munitions Box (ammo and equipment for the team); SAM Airburst Turret (shoots down air threats); Riot Shield (a plantable shield that can be destroyed); Trophy System (stops up to three incoming projectiles); Seeker Drone (hunts nearby enemies and snapshots them); Inflatable Decoy (pops up near enemies or on command); Smoke Wall (a handheld rocket that lays a long smoke screen); Recon Drone (a remote drone that marks targets); Razor Wire Trap (snares enemies in barbed wire); Tactical Insertion (sets your next spawn point).
- Killstreaks (17): UAV; Bomb Glider (a hand-launched glider carrying explosives); Care Package (drops a random Killstreak); Impact Warhead (a payload that drills down through several floors); Artillery Beacon (missiles home on a planted beacon); Crossfire (two airstrikes from opposite directions); Combat Drone (a remote anti-personnel drone); Counter UAV; Wheelson; Missile Salvo; Railgun Sentry; Attack Helicopter; Deathstalker; Advanced UAV (shows which way enemies face in real time, cannot be locked on); Argonaut; Gunship; Air Lockdown.
