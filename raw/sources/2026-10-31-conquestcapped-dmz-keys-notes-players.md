# ConquestCapped: MW4 DMZ key locations and loot rooms guide

A community guide from a site that sells boosting; drop rates and loot are player reports. Facts taken, in our words:
- Keys come from Story Mission rewards (Silent Ledger, Iron Citadel, Fallen Ember; not on every completion, players say), from Lieutenants (the key to their own room, not every kill), from some enemies (Citadel prison guards often drop the South Wing key), from Survival Kits (the Explosives Equipment Kit at DMZ level 16 holds a Gun Store Weapons Locker key; the Recon Equipment Kit at level 19 a Police Station key) and from random containers such as duffel bags, jackets, military bags, lockers and suitcases. Key rooms often hold another key.
- Rarity colours: green and blue for storerooms and offices; purple for vaults and armories; orange (gold keys) for Lieutenant rooms and top armories; red (Ultra) for DRC lockers.
- Each key names a Tac-Map grid square in its description (letters west to east, numbers north to south).
- Most keys are used up when the door opens; the Air Base Access Card is labelled multi-use. Keys do not stack.
- A key in a Medium Backpack's Secure Slot comes back to the Stash if you die; anywhere else it stays on your body for others to loot. Move a key from the Stash into your loadout before deploying.
