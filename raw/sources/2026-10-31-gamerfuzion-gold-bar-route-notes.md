Facts taken, in our words (filmed solo runs):
- Five Hanex Bank branch vaults: Cheongun Village, NuriGO West, Songdo Wharf, Wolves Stadium north (the side facing The Grid) and Wolves Stadium south (next to a UAV tower).
- To open one: power the card reader with a Car Battery, use the Rare Bank Branch Vault Access keycard, read a paper note for the first three digits of the code and find the fourth on the keypad by trying.
- Contents seen: Infantry Invasion Plans, Regional Combat Notes, a Drill Charge recipe, cash and a Gold Bar (Ultra, 25,000 DMZ Cash). Gold Skulls and other Gold Bars reported elsewhere on the route.
- The route takes about 20 to 25 minutes and ends at a helicopter exfil in Hajin City. Heavy AI with riot shields at NuriGO West.
