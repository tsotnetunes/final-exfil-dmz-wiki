# GosuGamers: Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 will launch with over 70,000 hardware bans already in place

Facts taken, in our words:
- Team RICOCHET posted on 25 Sep 2026 that more than 70,000 new hardware bans had been issued.
- Existing hardware bans carry into Modern Warfare 4 at launch, so banned players cannot start over on the same hardware.
- About 5,000 accounts have been banned for cheating with Cronus Zen, XIM Matrix and similar devices; enforcement looks at how a device is used in matches, since some are sold as accessibility tools.
- TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot are mandatory for MW4 online play on PC; the Secure Attestation Wizard was updated; AMD 3.x.0.x firmware is the most common cause of failures; Lenovo owners report more problems than others and may need to ask Lenovo for a BIOS update; players who still fail can send their system details through Activision's TPM report form.
