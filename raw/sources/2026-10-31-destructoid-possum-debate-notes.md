# Destructoid: After PvE vs. PvP, MW4 DMZ players are arguing about a possibly overpowered operator trait
URL: https://www.destructoid.com/after-pve-vs-pvp-mw4-dmz-players-are-arguing-about-a-possibly-overpowered-operator-trait/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 24 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (news)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Possum is one of the two final Warfighter choices. Once per match it gives a free life usable even after full death, with no time limit at that point (the article's example: coming back after a ten-minute break). Every squad member can carry it.
- Complaints: dead players return mid-fight; bodies vanish before they are looted; some use it to avoid paying the MIA rescue fee. Supporters: it makes DMZ more forgiving, especially solo.
- The article recalls that unlimited UAV coverage from Overwatch was reined in by a quick patch; the official notes of 23 Oct added the ability to steal UAV Towers back from enemy control.
