# AltChar: All Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ story missions and rewards

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 22 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (press)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Six Story Missions in order: Critical Extraction, Silent Ledger, Shopping Hacks, Iron Citadel, Grid Assault, Fallen Ember. The first three are open at once; Iron Citadel and Grid Assault open after the first three; Fallen Ember after those two.
- Each pays 25,000 cash plus items: Critical Extraction a Mirae Electrical Room Key and a Defibrillator; Silent Ledger a Gold Bar, a Counting Room Vault Key and the Polaris Dagor A1; Shopping Hacks a Coin Mining Den Key and Night Vision Goggles; Iron Citadel the Supreme Leader's Bunker Access Card and the LTV; Grid Assault Roze's Highrise Armory Key and a Helicopter; Fallen Ember a Reactor DRC Locker Key and the Cargo Plane.
- Missions can be finished again for the rewards, but a vehicle insertion already unlocked by a Story Mission is not given again.
- The story so far ends on a cliffhanger; more missions with later seasons are expected but not confirmed.
