# AllThingsHow: What the Boss Board Is in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ (Rewards and Intel)
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=297691
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- The Boss Board is a wall screen in the FOB with boss portraits; your Bank balance shows top right.
- Two tabs: Lieutenants (The Chemist, Jin-Tae, The Doctor, Bale, Iskra, Scavenger, Roze, Jang-ui, Warden, Gorani) and Commanders (Colossus, Death Stalker, Tank).
- Each Lieutenant card offers a $10,000 reward; each Commander card $50,000. Commander cards have no intel to buy.
- Lieutenant intel costs $25,000 and shows in the match as a side op (for example Paid Intel: Kill Gorani). The free alternative is a Lieutenant Hunt tower.
- Cards count kills (skull) and extracted tags (dog tag). On launch week Jin-Tae, Bale and Roze showed a padlock on intel. A claimed card reads Claimed and Eliminated.
- Three Lieutenant challenges at the bottom of the tab: kill every Hajin Lieutenant once; extract tags from every Hajin Lieutenant twice; and five times.
- The card only completes when you extract alive with the dog tag; dying first means the kill is not recorded. Carrying the tag shows you to others until you extract.
