Facts taken, in our words:
- The key opens green weapon lockers inside gun stores, one locker per key; the other lockers stay locked. Opening one gives 375 XP.
- Two gun stores: northern Songdo Wharf (a low shop beside a fuel station with a yellow canopy; downstairs back room) and western Cheongun Village (around column E, row 4; counter, mascot poster, stairs to a back room). Both areas show Low threat.
- One opened locker held a KG-7 Vulcan, a Cipher Code Ledger valuable, a Control Unit and a Zip Gun; contents are an example, not fixed.
