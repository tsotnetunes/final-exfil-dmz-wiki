Facts taken, in our words:
- Restates the official 5 Oct guide: DMZ is set after the campaign in an exclusion zone split between former North Korean and South Korean ground; the westernmost POIs were North Korean; a third, Russian area opens in Season 2.
- The North Korean side is rural and military; the rest is urban and modern (hospital, shopping district, stadium, housing).
- Lists the 13 major POIs: 14th Political Prison, Chang-san Air Base, Deadtown, Imjin Farmland, Cheongun Village, Mirae General Hospital, Cheonwang Peak Array, Haneul Nuclear Reactor, NuriGO Mall, The Grid, Songdo Wharf, Wolves Stadium, Hajin River Heights.
- Says Hajin has about 60 minor POIs but names only the eight Infinity Ward revealed: Hajin River, Tong-il Village, Revolution Dam, Pyonghwa Village, Imjin Suburb, Mujin Pass, Daesong Ridge, Namjin Riverside.
- Hajin is in DMZ and some Ground War maps from 23 Oct and comes to Warzone with Season 1.
