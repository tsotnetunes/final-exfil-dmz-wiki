## FOB stations
- From the start: Operators (Active Duty: status, Trait upgrades, pick who deploys); Stash/Loadout (stores weapons, gear and 3D Printer ingredients; set the loadout for the next infil); DMZ Orders (guided mission directives for onboarding, story and mastery); Firing Range (targets from close up to past 100 m); Survival Kits (themed loot packs to open for gear or crafting). Deploy (a heavy-lift transport helicopter) is always available.
- Unlocked by playing: 3D Printer (crafts gear from Printer Resources and 3D Printer Ingredients, upgrades FOB stations, hints where materials are likely found); Gunsmith (DMZ Cash buys attachments for looted weapons and builds new weapons; extracted Weapon Manuals widen what can be built; attachments depend on weapon level); Vendor (weapons and items for DMZ Cash, stock rotates daily); Bounty Leaderboard (top PvP bounty hunters and killers); Boss Board (buy intel on Lieutenants; information on Commanders); Dog Tag Case (shows tags taken from defeated Operators; mark favourites); DMZ Progression (a track to level 70 that awards printer recipes, unlocks stations and other content).

## Progression
- DMZ Player Level runs 1–70 with an unlock at every level. XP comes from opening loot containers, DMZ Orders, Dynamic Operations, Side Ops, Lieutenants, Commanders and combat (rival Operators pay more XP than AI).
- After Action Report after every deployment, extracted or not: Report (items, resources, ingredients, DMZ Cash; the cash goes to the Bank), Rating (F to S++ from Objectives, Combat and Looting, with totals for AI kills, missions, loot containers and Side Ops), Level (XP applied to the DMZ Player Level; the Operator's Dog Tag levels from Steel to Onyx III over 18 levels, with an Operator Trait each time it rises), Summary (completed DMZ Orders and rewards, then weapon levels back at the FOB).

## In a deployment
- Keys unlock special loot rooms; every key's description names a grid square on the Tac-Map. A door locked from the inside has another way in; rural areas hide underground entrances.
- Dynamic Operations: pick one before infil or start one at a Dynamic Ops phone; completion pays DMZ XP and DMZ Cash and brings a Supply Drop whose quality rises with each Op completed in the same deployment (Common, Uncommon, Rare). The Hunt Operators Op sets a full rival squad against you.
- Side Ops: lower-intensity tasks such as finding lost Supply Drops, repairing trucks and climbing radio towers that reveal a nearby Lieutenant's general location; UAV towers and SAM sites are Side Ops too.
- Lieutenants: active in their territories whether hunted or not; a Lieutenant Hunt tower's terminal shows the general location of an active one. A kill pays DMZ XP and DMZ Cash, their weapon and a Dog Tag Case, and may drop a Key to their own loot room. Picking up the Dog Tag Case marks you on every Operator's Tac-Map; leaving it still pays the XP and cash, but the Case is what advances the Boss Board.
- Commanders: roaming bosses that shrug off many standard weapons and may need specific tactics; a kill pays prized 3D Printer Ingredients, large XP and DMZ Cash and updates the Boss Board. Named: Colossus, Deathstalker, Main NK Battle Tank.
- HUD: Tac-Map with objectives, POIs, threat areas, compounds, key buildings, Ammo and Armor Caches and the spreading radiation; a grid with letters west to east and numbers north to south (D7 is Imjin Farmland). Time Remaining shows when the radiation storm intensifies. Threat Level sits under the compass. When the Hostiles Kill Count fills, an enemy Hunt Squad is sent and you are Marked for Death. The squad panel shows each Operator's DMZ Cash, armour plates, gas mask and parachute. The Ping Wheel can request a Truce or join up with another squad; proximity chat is the fastest way to talk to nearby Operators.
- Threat levels per area: Low, Medium, High, Critical, Extreme. Noise and violence raise the response; stealth, cover, distance and breaking line of sight calm it. Human Operators are not counted.

## Enemies
Tier 1 NK grunts; Tier 2 armoured soldiers; Tier 3 armoured, gas-masked soldiers; Tier 4 faceless armoured soldiers; autonomous drone swarms and LMG drones; robotic K9 quadrupeds (mostly underground); attack helicopter patrols; road convoys; Lieutenants; Commanders; rival Operators.

## Vehicles
DMZ vehicles have finite armour and fuel, repaired and refuelled at Gas Stations (Warzone vehicles do not need this). Ground: Camper, Sedan, Grizzly, Dreadnought (Aston Martin tactical SUV), Polaris Dagor A1 (carries a full squad), LTV (optional mounted .50 cal), Cargo Truck, D50 Radar Flatbed (vehicle-mounted UAV). Rail, not player-driven: Train (route across former South Korean POIs), Tram (elevated monorail loop around Hajin City). Water: RHIB. Air: Heli (combat helicopter), Heavy Chopper (armoured transport with flares, rarely player-flown), Monarch (twin-rotor infil and exfil transport, not player-flown).
