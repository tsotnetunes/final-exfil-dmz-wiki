Facts taken, in our words:
- Three trees of 6 Major and 16 Minor Traits; Majors always visible, Minors random and revealed by unlocking the Major before them; at most 18 Traits per Operator.
- Its recommended Traits and reasons: Container Looter (loot is central, an early investment), Overwatch and UAV Upgrade (intel, they work together), Field Crafting (craft during a live run), Quick Fix (for players who like to fight), Possum (can prevent a death outright), Consumable Duration (consumables matter in fights), Numb the Pain (a big difference in team fights).
- It puts Scout first because of looting and intel.
