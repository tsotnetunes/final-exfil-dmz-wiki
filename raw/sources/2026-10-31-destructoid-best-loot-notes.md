# Destructoid: Best loot locations in Modern Warfare 4 DMZ
URL: https://www.destructoid.com/best-loot-locations-in-modern-warfare-4-dmz/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 25 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (the writer's own play since launch):
- Chang-san Air Base, Critical: three houses near a marked entrance gave Weapon Manuals and blueprints almost every time, but only during Operation Iron Citadel.
- Compounds across the map: guarded by high-threat AI; each has a secret entrance; weapon lockers hold valuables and the safe is the best prize.
- Cheonwang Peak Array, Medium: many regular AI but no high-tier ones; safes possible; tech ingredients such as Comms Modules.
- The Train: moves around Hajin, its track shows the next stop; the writer calls it the best source of 3D Printer resources.
