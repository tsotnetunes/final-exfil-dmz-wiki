Facts taken, in our words (one live solo run):
- The radiation countdown starts at about 30 minutes on landing (29:57 seen just after).
- Bank vaults opened with a car battery, the Bank Branch Vault Access keycard and a three-digit note: Cheongun Village (two safes and cash), a vault at the north tip of The Grid (two safes, one with a Gold Bar), Songdo Wharf.
- When the countdown reached zero the HUD switched to a Radiation Spreading timer, which read 8:55 at that moment.
- The player then took a UAV tower and drove to the final exfil, which that match was just south of Songdo Wharf, waited for the helicopter and extracted.
