# DTGRE: MW4 DMZ Radar Truck repair, all 4 parts and Mechanic Order guide
URL: https://www.dtgre.com/2026/10/mw4-dmz-radar-truck-repair-mechanic-order.html
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 25 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- A Radar Truck in Hajin can be repaired by fitting four parts: a Car Battery, an Alternator, a Spark Plug and a Vehicle Water Pump.
- The parts turn up mostly at Gas Stations and garages, on shelves and in storage areas; no single station is sure to have all four, and some trucks already have some parts fitted.
- Once repaired the truck can be driven and picks up nearby enemies while you drive it.
- The Mechanic DMZ Order (on page 15 for the writer, possibly a different page for others) asks you to repair a Radar Truck and then get 19 kills on enemies the truck has pinged; rewards are not given.
- The guide does not use the official name D50 Radar Flatbed.
