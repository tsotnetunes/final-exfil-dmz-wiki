# Nintendo Life: Digital Foundry delivers its tech verdict on Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 on Switch 2
URL: https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/10/digital-foundry-delivers-its-tech-verdict-on-call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-on-switch-2
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 30 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (Nintendo Life summarising Digital Foundry's analysis):
- On Switch 2, DMZ runs at 40 frames per second, but only with a 120Hz display connected; the article does not say how DMZ runs in handheld mode.
- Campaign runs at 30 fps; competitive Multiplayer targets 60 fps with dips into the high 40s and 50s.
- Campaign and DMZ have some extra input latency because of their locked, lower frame rates.
- Docked: a DLSS mode at a dynamic 900p (as low as 450p) or an SMAA T2X mode aiming at 1080p. Handheld: dynamic 720p, down to 360p in busy scenes.
- Digital Foundry judged the port a job well done that keeps the key elements for fair cross-platform play.
