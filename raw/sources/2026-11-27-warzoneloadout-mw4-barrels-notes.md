Trust: third-party (the site says it is not affiliated with Activision and names no source)

Facts taken, in our words:

- Each barrel carries the weapon level that unlocks it, from level 2 to level 68.
- Names follow the manufacturer-plus-length pattern seen elsewhere in the game (for example a Mosin K025 Barrel and a Mosin S762 Barrel for the Mosin-Nagant, and ZLR and Hanbit barrels in several lengths).
