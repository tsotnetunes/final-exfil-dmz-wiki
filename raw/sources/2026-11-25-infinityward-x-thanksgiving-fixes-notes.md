# Infinity Ward on X: fixes before the Thanksgiving holiday (25 Nov 2026)
URL: https://x.com/InfinityWard/status/2125764057819136028
Captured: 2026-11-27 (posted 26 Nov 2026 at 01:30 UTC, the evening of 25 Nov in the US; read from Final Exfil's collected posts, not on x.com)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words (the post's text is not stored here):
- Infinity Ward pushed a small round of fixes on the evening of 25 Nov 2026, before the Thanksgiving holiday. They are not on the Season 1 patch notes page as of 27 Nov 2026 (its newest block is 23 Nov).
- DMZ: vehicle kills now count toward the challenges that ask for them; the post names the Spinal Verge BlackCell camo challenge as one.
- DMZ: a Gas Mask no longer vanishes when you switch Night Vision Goggles on or off.
- DMZ: a consumable no longer vanishes when you cancel using it while mantling.
- DMZ: a weapon swapped through the Backpack no longer shows the wrong attachment capacity.
- Multiplayer (not DMZ): an AFK-kick fix in Search and Destroy spectating and a Recon Drone collision fix.
- The studio repeats that Season 1 Reloaded comes the following week (the week of 30 Nov 2026), with no day.
