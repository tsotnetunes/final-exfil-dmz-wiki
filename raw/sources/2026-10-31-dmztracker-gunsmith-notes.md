# DMZ Tracker: MW4 DMZ Gunsmith, Weapon Manuals and Prices
URL: https://dmztracker.gg/guides/gunsmith-weapons-guide/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 29 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Lasers cost between 1,900 and 6,887.
- DMZ level 25 gives a 5% Gunsmith discount; the 3D Printer's Gunsmith Upgrade I gives another 5% and costs 50,000 DMZ Cash, 20 Reactive Metals, 10 CPU and 5 Power Regulator.
- Rarity belongs to each copy of a gun, so the same model can be found at different tiers, up to Legendary; looted weapons can carry up to eight attachments and boss weapons can carry more.
- Blueprints sit under Buy New Weapon, Builds, Blueprints tab; the first craft of each is free, later crafts cost DMZ Cash that varies by Blueprint: Kastov 762 Voltspine 136,687, Han 86 Beta Forged 88,312. A Blueprint can be claimed without the gun's Weapon Manual.
- The Vendor opens at DMZ level 5, its stock changes daily and higher DMZ levels add offers.
