# Gaming ProMax: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ, all Story Missions and rewards
URL: https://gamingpromax.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-all-story-missions-rewards/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 23 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (guide site)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Tiers: Critical Extraction, Silent Ledger and Shopping Hacks are open from the start in any order; Iron Citadel and Grid Assault open after all three; Fallen Ember after both of those. Start them from Deploy at the FOB by choosing Story Missions.
- Orders links: Critical Extraction is the last Order of Human Intel (after Payment Due); Silent Ledger closes Counterfeit; Shopping Hacks closes Underground.
- Critical Extraction: Mirae General Hospital, Medium; find General Kang, clear enemies, take him to the roof helicopter; a radiation countdown runs. Optional: the Lieutenant called The Doctor drops a key to a Command Office that holds a 3D Printer recipe. Card rewards: Mirae Electrical Room Key, Defibrillator, 25,000.
- Silent Ledger: Heavenly Luck Casino in Cheongun Village, Medium; basement generator, vault fence opened from penthouse security (Camera 01), drill the vault, carry the plate bag to exfil; optional cash bags worth up to 18,000. Card rewards: Gold Bar, Counting Room Vault Key, Polaris Dagor A1 (paid infil vehicle), 25,000.
- Shopping Hacks: NuriGO Mall, Medium; hack a laptop at Lieutenant Jang-ui's camp for a parking keypad code that changes each match, go down to the Robotics Lab, hack five lab computers, collect four K9 cores, take the Bio-agent Sample, Fulton out from the roof. Card rewards: Coin Mining Den Key, Night Vision Goggles, 25,000.
- Iron Citadel: Chang-san Air Base, Critical; 14 objectives; three transfer slips make the armory code (changes each match); open the armory, plant explosives on the strike aircraft, leave with the armory manifest; Lieutenant Bale is the named enemy. Card rewards: Supreme Leader's Bunker Access Card, LTV (paid infil vehicle), 25,000.
- Grid Assault: The Grid (DBN Headquarters, Hanex Bank), Critical; two hash key halves, compile them, upload the kill code to stop the drone-command tower, then a rooftop broadcast. Roze is there (Kill Roze side op). Card rewards: Roze's Highrise Armory Key, Helicopter (paid infil vehicle), 25,000.
- Fallen Ember: Haneul Nuclear Reactor, Extreme; 15 objectives: breach the reactor, take uranium rods, cross a flooded fuel pool, escape by patrol boat; no cutscene; the match goes on to a normal exfil. Card rewards: Reactor DRC Locker Key, Cargo Plane (paid infil vehicle), 25,000.
- Replays: missions can be repeated without limit; the site says the 25,000 repeats on replays, while vehicle unlocks are one-time. Codes in Shopping Hacks, Iron Citadel and Grid Assault change every deployment.
