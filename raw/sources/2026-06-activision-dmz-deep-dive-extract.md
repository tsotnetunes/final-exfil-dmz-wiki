## Map & Regions
Hajin Exclusion Zone is the primary battleground. Key Points of Interest include: Fallout reactor (irradiated area); Prison complex; Hajin City remains; Military Base; Casino (with vault). The setting is "a contested exclusion zone where rival forces battle for control of abandoned military technology" following the MW4 Campaign.

## Factions & NPCs
Rogue Operators (rival human squads); enemy combatants with coordinated squad AI; Lieutenants (high-value targets with specialized units); Commander enemies including tanks, drone swarms, Deathstalker helicopters, Juggernauts, Elite Strike Teams.

## Missions & Operations
- Story Missions: "narrative-driven Story Missions built for replayability, squad-based action, and evolving storytelling"
- Dynamic Operations: "multi-step objectives generated for each match" with escalating tension
- Side Ops: truck repairs, supply drop location, radio tower scaling
- Free Roam: open exploration without structured objectives

## FOB (Forward Operating Base)
Central hub between deployments featuring: Orders/Objectives tracking; Stash (persistent inventory across deployments, expandable via ranking); Wallet (in-game currency for purchases and operator recovery); 3D Printer (crafts gear, backpacks, plate carriers, tacticals, lethals, consumables, field upgrades, fire support, tracked recipes, special items); Gunsmith (weapon attachment customization; up to 5 attachments plus 1 Apex Attachment per weapon); Weapon Vendor (rotating selection of pre-built weapons); Firing Range; Active Duty (Operator management); Boss Board (Lieutenant intel and tracking); Bounty Leaderboard (rival player monitoring).

## Operator Progression
- Trait System: "spend earned Trait Points on individual Operators" across three trait trees covering combat, scavenging, and other capabilities
- Active Duty Slots: multiple operators with persistent backpacks and loadouts
- XP sources: Story Missions, Dynamic Operations, Side Ops, enemy takedowns (no XP penalties for death)
- Operators earn individual Trait Points tied to their progression

## Infil/Exfil Mechanics
- Infil options: "Go in quiet on foot, or fast and loud in a helicopter or large plane" — vehicle drops available via Paid Infil System
- Exfil options: helicopters, skyhooks
- Recovery: Tourniquet "allowing you to heal to a wounded state if you don't have self-revive equipped"; MIA System: "players...deploy rescue teams to recover them and continue their progression"

## Loot & Progression
Loot placement is "grounded and logical within the world itself": police stations contain tactical gear; hospitals have medical supplies; residential districts offer crafting resources. Weapon variants: damaged weapons with broken attachments; Lieutenants carry "uniquely customized weapons featuring attachments and camouflage finishes". Materials auto-disassemble during missions. Recipes unlock advanced 3D Printer crafting.

## AI & Threats
Enhanced squad AI with coordination and reactive behaviors. Threat Escalation System: "tracks player activity and escalates enemy responses" as operators attract attention. Stealth Meter: audio cues and HUD indicators (white→yellow→orange) help solo players avoid detection. Reinforcements include attack helicopters and Deathstalker helicopters at high threat levels.

## Dynamic Systems
Weather: "Sudden downpours can reduce visibility," dense fog transforms routes. Vehicle convoys moving across roadways. Air traffic: choppers in skies. WMD repositioning: hostile forces move strategic assets.

## PvP Systems
Bounty System: hunting other players raises notoriety and puts a price on your head. Dog Tags: collect from Lieutenants or rival operators; exfilling with them provides rewards but makes you trackable. Proximity chat: "Distance-Based Vocal Falloff," directionality, environmental voice reverb.

## Progression carries over
Operator Traits and skill trees (persistent per operator); FOB upgrades and Stash contents; weapon unlocks from extracted Weapon Manuals; Bounty Leaderboard standing; XP and rank progression (no penalties for failed exfils).
