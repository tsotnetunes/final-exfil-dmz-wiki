# DMZTracker: MW4 DMZ Story Missions, objectives and rewards
URL: https://dmztracker.gg/guides/story-missions-guide/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (posted 20 Oct 2026, checked 30 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Unlock order the site reports: Critical Extraction, Silent Ledger and Shopping Hacks open after the First Op deployment on the first Orders page; Iron Citadel and Grid Assault open once those three are done; Fallen Ember opens after both of those.
- Rewards per mission card: every mission pays 25,000 DMZ Cash; Critical Extraction adds the Mirae Electrical Room key and a Defibrillator; Silent Ledger a Gold Bar, the Counting Room Vault key and the Polaris Dagor A1; Shopping Hacks the Coin Mining Den key (and, unconfirmed, Night Vision Goggles); Iron Citadel the LTV infil and the Supreme Leader's Bunker Access Card; Grid Assault Roze's Highrise Armory key and the Helicopter infil; Fallen Ember the Reactor DRC Locker key and the Cargo Plane infil.
- Replays: missions can be replayed as often as you like and the cash and items pay again; one-time unlocks such as infil vehicles do not repeat. On 29 Oct the cash for replays was raised (no amount given).
- One Story Mission per match; your squad is matched with players who queued for the same mission and no other squad in that match runs it (relaying GameSpot and PC Gamer).
- The Random Story Mission card only picks missions you have finished; three random missions in a week count toward a weekly goal.
- Unconfirmed: a press report from unnamed sources that replays of finished missions now go into regular lobbies.
