# Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 patch notes: 16 Nov 2026 block
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes
Captured: 2026-11-17 (read from Final Exfil's parsed copy of the page; the update was announced by the official updates account and by Infinity Ward at about 18:11 UTC on 16 Nov 2026)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- Global: stability fixes; the Joint Fire event showed a wrong end time, now fixed; the notes restate that Joint Fire runs for 24 hours each Friday for the rest of Season 1.
- Global: execution previews in DMZ and Warzone were missing parts; fixed. Some preview images looked low resolution on some platforms; fixed.
- DMZ Traits: with Plate Pouch and Plate Organization stacked, the plate count could read nine instead of ten; fixed.
- DMZ Traits: the Wheelson in X01 could target players covered by Viper Sense; fixed.
- DMZ gameplay: the cash from a Lieutenant's Dog Tags now goes to every member of the squad.
- DMZ gameplay: the UAV Upgrade Pro Trait now zooms the minimap out 1.5 times instead of 3 times.
- DMZ gameplay: loot dropped by a destroyed Death Stalker could not be pinged; fixed.
- DMZ gameplay: a weapon exfilled in the Backpack Weapon slot could fail to equip on the next deployment once it had been set as Primary or Secondary; fixed.
- DMZ gameplay: bomb sites in Dynamic Operations could stay usable when they should not; fixed.
- DMZ UI: weapon stats could be hidden in the DMZ Gunsmith and weapon-select screens; some Blueprints showed the wrong name in the Backpack; Operators could seem to drop out of the exfil helicopter on the way to the After Action Report; Threat Briefing rewards showed the wrong rarity colours; all fixed.
- DMZ progression: the "Chained" camo challenge showed a kill requirement of zero; fixed.
- Multiplayer only: Drill Charge impact damage at range cut by 75 percent (close range unchanged); Crossfire tablet direction now matches the strafe run; faster Battle Pass tier claim sequence (listed under Multiplayer UI); other Multiplayer fixes.
