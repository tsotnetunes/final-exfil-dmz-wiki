# ConquestCapped: MW4 DMZ guide
URL: https://conquestcapped.com/guides/call-of-duty/mw4-dmz/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (last updated 26 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

A guide on a site that also sells boosting services. Most of its points carry no attribution, so the Codex uses them only as reports, ideally beside a second source.

Facts taken, in our words:
- 60 players in 20 squads of three.
- The match clock runs about 30 minutes before radiation starts closing in; after it reaches zero, radiation needs roughly 15 more minutes to cover the map, and every exfil it reaches closes.
- Three exfil sites are active at once, marked with green smoke; after calling the Twin Heli you hold the site for about a minute until it lands; it leaves 30 seconds after landing, or 5 seconds after the whole squad is aboard; the site nearest the radiation closes first; a used site reopens after a cooldown; once radiation has taken the others, a final exfil appears.
- Infil prices match the official guide; the LTV infil is unlocked by Operation Iron Citadel and the helicopter infil by Operation Grid Assault.
- On death you lose everything in your loadout and backpack except Secure Slot items; extracted items go to your Stash.
- The Free Loadout is a 1-plate vest, a rifle, a pistol and a couple of grenades, at no cost.
- A vehicle is always waiting at your spawn.
- The insured and contraband weapon split of the 2022 DMZ is not used in MW4.
- Solo: switch Squad Fill off on the Ready Up screen; players' early solo runs met full squads only a handful of times over several hours.
