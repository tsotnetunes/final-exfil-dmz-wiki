Facts taken, in our words (Warzone mode only):
- 5 Nov 2026: Hajin arrives as a large Warzone map with 13 major points of interest across North and South Korea.
- Each Warzone match on Hajin starts with one of six weather conditions chosen at random.
- Hajin has a new Gulag with two arena variants; players can mantle obstacles there, spectate from ground level at some angles, and the local weather affects the arena.
- 7 Nov 2026: fixed map collision on Hajin that let players clip through geometry in places.
- 16 Nov 2026: fixed the final circles closing over open water on Hajin.
