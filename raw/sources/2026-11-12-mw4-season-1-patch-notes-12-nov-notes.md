# Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 patch notes: 12 Nov 2026 block
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes

Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- DMZ: Wager Match Trophies now drop at the rate intended; before the fix they dropped too rarely.
- DMZ: Operator and weapon insurance no longer stops working for players who die in X01 after going there from Hajin.
- DMZ: a Grizzly no longer keeps driving once its driver has been killed.
- DMZ: the capture prompt at SAM Sites and UAV Towers now shows the longer duration given by the Air Superiority Trait.
- DMZ: players with Thermal Padding or a running Sedative Inhaler no longer show up on enemy UAVs boosted by the UAV Upgrade Trait.
- DMZ: some BlackCell camo challenges now track reliably in DMZ.
- Global: general stability fixes; the Schlager Indigo-RF optic shows sniper glint again; attachment pips are orange again.
- Multiplayer only: Wager Match Trophy fixes in Private Matches and round-based modes, Fast Hands fix, Battle Pass quick equip and completion display fixes.
