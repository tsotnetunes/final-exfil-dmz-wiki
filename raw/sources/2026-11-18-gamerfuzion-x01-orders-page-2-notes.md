Facts taken, in our words:
- X01 has two floors joined by the Logistics Tunnel; stairs in the Main Warehouse lead down to the lower tunnel.
- The hidden stash is a Military Locker in the back storeroom of Production Assembly, up metal stairs and through a dark corridor; the X01 3D Printer is also in Production Assembly.
- Tech Lab, on the lower floor, has plenty of unopened containers and an exfil lift beside it. A Keycard Encoder Station stands on the east edge of the Data Center.
- The Ri footage console is in the surveillance room of the Admin Offices, upstairs and west of the Main Warehouse. The Medical Wing and Control Room were also spawn points.
- Page 2 paid the Consumable Box recipe, the Medical Box recipe and 20,000 DMZ Cash. When the final exfil starts, standard exfils close after 3 minutes.
