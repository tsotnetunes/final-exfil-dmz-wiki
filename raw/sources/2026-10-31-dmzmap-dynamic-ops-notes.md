# DMZMap: MW4 DMZ Dynamic Ops: Every Op, Steps and Phones
URL: https://dmzmap.com/guides/dynamic-ops
Captured: 2026-10-31 (last updated 25 Oct 2026; no publish date shown)
Trust: third-party (fan site; pay figures are single runs before the 30 Oct cash change)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Twelve Dynamic Ops "so far": Viral Reckoning, Broken Resupply, Ghost Shipment, Chemical Denial, Toxic Intercept, Dead Man's Cache, Signal Breaker, Special Delivery, Domino Protocol, Sky Heist, Hunt Operators, Ignition Point.
- A Dynamic Op phone is a ringing radio handset on a desk or table, shown as a Dynamic Op marker on the Tac-Map; phone spots change every match. You can also pick Dynamic Operations at Deploy. Each finished Op pays DMZ XP and DMZ Cash and drops a Supply Drop that improves with each Op finished in the same deployment (Common, Uncommon, Rare). Each step paid about 250 XP or 300 cash (site estimate).
- Viral Reckoning and Broken Resupply use keypad doors; the code is shown in your objective and changes every match. In Broken Resupply you find the code in a safe first.
- Steps listed agree with COD Central for every Op. Extra notes: Chemical Denial is tied to NuriGO Mall by the first patch notes, and the site blows up if you do not clear out; Sky Heist has a flight timer; Domino Protocol's Lieutenant can be at the 14th Political Prison or Bale at the Re-Education Center (unconfirmed).
- Pay seen in one run each: Viral Reckoning 7,500 and 4,230 XP; Broken Resupply 5,000 and 2,993 XP; Ghost Shipment 7,500 and 5,032 XP; Chemical Denial 5,000 and 3,000 XP; Dead Man's Cache 9,900 and 5,146 XP; Signal Breaker 10,000; Special Delivery 7,500 and 4,500 XP; Domino Protocol 12,400 and 8,000 XP; Ignition Point 5,000 and 3,000 XP. These match COD Central's runs, so the two sites may share one set of observations.
