# Dexerto: MW4 players already want 'OP' second life trait removed from DMZ
URL: https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/mw4-players-already-want-op-second-life-trait-removed-from-dmz-3420638/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (news; it summarises player posts, which the Codex does not quote)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Possum is the last Trait of the Warfighter tree. It gives one self-revive per match and, unlike an ordinary revive, works after the Operator has been fully killed, returning them to Last Stand at full health.
- Complaints the article sums up: killed players can come back and swing a fight that looked won; the revive makes no sound, so a player can wait, revive unnoticed and exfil. Fixes players suggested: a longer activation, an audio cue, a rework or removal.
- No developer comment when it was written.
