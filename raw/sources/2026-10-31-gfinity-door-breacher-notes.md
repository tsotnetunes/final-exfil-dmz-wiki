Facts taken, in our words:
- The Door Breacher Charge is a single-use Ultra consumable that opens a locked door instead of its key: at the door, hold interact to use the charge.
- It does not open gun lockers, keycard doors or the Supreme Leader's Bunker.
- Sources: the Vendor's daily stock, or the 3D Printer once its recipe is found in Hajin (1 Experimental Compounds, 5 Stock Rods, 3 Volatile Explosives).
