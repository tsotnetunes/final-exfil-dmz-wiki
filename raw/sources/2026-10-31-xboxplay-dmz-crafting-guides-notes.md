Facts taken, in our words:
- DDOS: a Rare Field Upgrade under Field Upgrades at the printer; costs 30 Electronics and 3 Detonators. Detonators can be printed under Uncommon Ingredients for 20 Electronics and 20 Chemicals once the 50,000 Cash Uncommon Ingredient Printing upgrade (20 Biomaterials, 10 High-Tenacity Fibers, 5 Comms Modules) is built. The DDOS recipe comes with the reward for finishing the Dead Battery, Death From Above and Operation Shopping Hacks Orders. Dead Battery asks you to print a DDOS, then disable three enemy vehicles or drones with it. The DDOS briefly knocks out electronics and enemy sensors nearby.
- Each recipe card at the printer has four buttons: Print to Operator, Print to Stash, Track Recipe and Ingredient Locations. Resources already in your base inventory count toward a cost; ingredients carried in a deployment count only if you extract.
- Starting limits: the Bank holds 100,000 Cash, the Stash 40 items and 12 weapons. Bank Upgrade I raises the cap to 250,000 for 20,000 Cash; Bank Upgrade II to 500,000 for 50,000 Cash and opens Bank Upgrade III. Stash Item Upgrade I adds 18 slots (58 in all) for 10,000 Cash, 10 Rigid Synthetics and 10 Electronic Sensors; Stash Weapon Upgrade I adds 6 (18 in all) for 50,000 Cash, 20 Reactive Metals, 10 Stock Rods and 5 Comms Modules.
- Night Vision Goggles: the printer recipe comes from completing the Underground chapter of DMZ Orders; it costs 50 Metals, 3 Printed Circuit Boards and 10 Control Units. A Weak NVG sits on a shelf beside the bunk beds in the objective tent at the military camp outside NuriGO Mall during Operation Shopping Hacks. Printed Circuit Boards mostly come from the Haneul Nuclear Reactor and electrical substations; Control Units from desktop computers and tech bags.
