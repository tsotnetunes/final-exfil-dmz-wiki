# AllThingsHow: Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ Bosses: Where Lieutenants Spawn and What They Drop
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=286632
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- 13 bosses: 10 Lieutenants and 3 Commanders; the site says more may exist.
- Boss Board Lieutenant cards: reward $10,000, intel $25,000; bought intel starts a side op that reads Paid Intel: Reach <name>, then Paid Intel: Kill <name>. A kill counts for the board only if you extract with the dog tag.
- Extraction summary: one Lieutenant dog tag pays $25,000; the first Lieutenant kill adds $10,000 (First Lieutenant Takedowns).
- Bale: Re-Education Center (Medium) or Chang-san Air Base Bunker (Extreme); fight on the upper floor through smoke and green gas. Drops Dreadnought (Legendary LMG on the Type 73, $8,000) or a Type 73 (Rare, $2,000), and Bale's Dog Tag.
- Gorani: Mujin Pass (Low) or a forest in the South Korean Wilderness; ghillie-suited guards and decoys around his camp, and killing a decoy does not count. Drops Incisor (Legendary marksman rifle on the MAR-9, $6,900) or a MAR-9 (Rare, $1,700), Gorani's Dog Tag, Infantry Invasion Plans ($1,000), Nitro Compounds.
- The Doctor: Mirae General Hospital (Low): the wards during Operation Critical Extraction, the parking garage, or the grounds; a heavy in a white suit and gas mask who fights up close. Drops Doctor's Salve (Legendary battle rifle on the Patriot XMR, $6,900), a key to his Command Office on the 7th floor, and a dog tag worth 25K.
- Jin-Tae: Heavenly Luck Casino (High); heavy automatic fire on the casino floor, fall back to the back offices; the counting room is locked. Drops an ISO Nightshade and Jin-tae's Dog Tag.
- Jang-ui: Netshin Tech Complex in The Grid (Extreme); interior stairs to the upper floor and a server hall marked 14. Drops Paektu (Ultra assault rifle on the Hyeon Burst, $11,200, five attachments) or a Hyeon Burst (Epic, $2,800), and a dog tag.
- Warden: The Citadel (Critical) or the 14th Political Prison blocks (High); stays behind a riot shield with waves of guards; flank and shoot the open side. Drops Incarcerator (Ultra SMG, $8,500) or a plain X-58 NYX, and Kang's Dog Tag.
- Scavenger: met at Cheongun Village, South Island or Deadtown (all Low). Drops an Oris 8.6 ($1,900), a Legendary dog tag and a cache with a Gas Mask.
- Roze: DBN Headquarters rooftop (Extreme), reached by the stairs; also met in a top-floor machine room by the roof door. Drops The Kraken or an Astaroth .57 (Rare sniper, $6,400) and a dog tag; the indoor cache held a Legendary Roze's Highrise Armory key and Degraded Execution Footage.
- Iskra: Cheonwang Peak Array (Medium); fires from an upper doorway in a stairwell, then runs down. Drops Widowmaker (Legendary shotgun, $3,700) or a Warlord (Rare, $900), a dog tag and a Small Secure Backpack.
- The Chemist: Haneul Nuclear Reactor (High), a warehouse by the reactor tanks; the yellow radiation zone hurts a little, the red core breaks gas masks fast; bring a gas mask and Radiation Blockers. Guards in dark hazmat suits, the Chemist in yellow. Drops Revenant (Legendary marksman rifle, $7,500) and the Chemist's Dog Tag.
- Tank: met on streets by NuriGO Mall (Medium); bullets do nothing, use rockets from cover; loot points at the front, back and top after it is destroyed.
- Deathstalker: attack helicopter over The Grid, NuriGO West, Compound Delta and DBN Headquarters; a captured SAM site downs it; a scoped rifle from the DBN helipad killed one in about 25 seconds.
- Colossus: met in the Armory (Extreme), at the 14th Political Prison and the Citadel, and at the Cheonwang Peak Array gate. The game tells you his suit makes him immune to explosive attacks. Banner on the kill: ELIMINATED ARMORED COMMANDER; an assist gave 1,075 XP. Drops Military Composites (Legendary ingredient, $3,500).
