Facts taken, in our words:
- A key card shows its rarity, a value (usually 100) and a hint with the grid square of its door.
- Keys open armories, vaults and secure rooms that hold high-value loot and 3D Printer recipes.
- Sources named: lockers, duffel bags, footlockers, tool chests and military bags; The Doctor drops the Command Office key; Operations Critical Extraction, Silent Ledger and Shopping Hacks give keys.
- Unlocking a door pays 300 XP; afterwards the door keeps an open prompt.
- 14 keys with their places: Mirae Electrical Room (F5); Counting Room Vault (F4; also from an industrial locker at the Imjin Rice Mill); Mechanic's Shack (F5); Police Station Armory (Pyonghwa Village station); Militia Arms Depot (G7); Field Depot (Chang-san Air Base hangar); Re-Education Center Annex; Underground Propaganda Store Room; Executive Arms Auction (DBN Headquarters); Rice Mill Bunker (column D, rows 7 to 8); The Doctor's Command Office (F5); Commandant Office (Admin Complex No. 2); Shop Records Room (F7); Coin Mining Den (G6).
- The Counting Room Vault held a Zip Gun recipe; The Doctor's Lieutenant crate held a Tempered Armor Vest recipe; opening the Police Station Armory shows a stronghold-unlocked banner.
- Not used by the Codex: the article's claim that items in the hotbar stay at base when you die; no other source says so.
