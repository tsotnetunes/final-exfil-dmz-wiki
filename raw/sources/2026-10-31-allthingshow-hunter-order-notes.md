# AllThingsHow: MW4 DMZ: How to Complete the Hunter Order at a Lieutenant Hunt Tower

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Hunter is Order 01 on DMZ Orders page 2 (Human Intel 1/2); it completes when the download at the top of a Lieutenant Hunt tower finishes; killing the Lieutenant is not needed.
- Towers show a skull icon; the example was the hill tower beside Wolves Stadium (Medium). The terminal marks the nearest Lieutenant.
- The marker led to Iskra in the office building at Cheonwang Peak Array (Medium); on her floor the banner reads LIEUTENANT ISKRA, CHEONWANG PEAK ARRAY and the side op becomes Kill Iskra.
- Iskra's drops: Widowmaker (Legendary shotgun on the Warlord, $3,700), Iskra's Dog Tag, a Control Unit. The kill gave 1,000 XP.
