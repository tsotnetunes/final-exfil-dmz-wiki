# ConquestCapped: MW4 DMZ guide
URL: https://conquestcapped.com/guides/call-of-duty/mw4-dmz/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (updated 26 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (stated without a test method):
- A match runs about 30 minutes before the radiation starts to close in; once that timer hits zero it takes roughly another 15 minutes to cover the whole map.
- Exfils inside the radiation close; the site nearest the radiation closes first.
- Three exfil sites are open at a time, marked with green smoke. Call the helicopter and hold for about a minute until it lands; it leaves 30 seconds after boarding, or 5 seconds once the whole squad is on board. A used site reopens after a cooldown (length not given).
- A final exfil appears once the radiation has taken the other sites, and it leaves at a fixed time.
- Fulton balloons and flares are other ways out; a flare can be seen by every nearby squad. The Sky Heist Dynamic Op gives the squad its own helicopter.
- Gas masks slow radiation damage; since the launch patch they no longer break on entering radiation.
- Compound Delta has a tunnel to the reactor.
- Infil prices: on foot free, Polaris Dagor A1 5,000, LTV 25,000, helicopter 50,000, cargo plane 100,000 DMZ Cash.
