Facts taken, in our words:
- Starting: at the FOB helicopter pad choose Deploy, then Story Missions, then a mission. Tier 1 (Medium threat, 25,000 cash each on the card): Critical Extraction, Silent Ledger, Shopping Hacks. Tier 2 (Critical, 3-Plate vest advised): Iron Citadel, Grid Assault. Tier 3 (Extreme): Fallen Ember. The Random Story Mission option draws from missions you have finished, runs from Medium to Extreme threat and uses squad fill; a weekly task asks for three random Story Missions.
- Breaking New Ground: plays the first time you open DMZ, with the dog Rex; not in the Story Missions list and not replayable from a menu; about ten minutes. Areas and steps: North Korean wilderness (follow Rex, clear the area, search a drone, use a Tourniquet, move Rex to safety), Deadtown (find and use a Medkit, follow a signal, scout from a high spot), the Re-education facility (enter by the canal, find and open a drone black box, reach exfil). It ends in a scripted capture, with no reward screen. Afterwards you arrive at FOB Zulu and recruit an Operator; a deployment kit holds a 2-Plate Armor Vest, Medium Backpack, Fulton Drop Marker, Weapon Insurance Contract and Operator Rescue Beacon.
- Critical Extraction: infil about 770 m away in the South Korean Border Zone. Six HUD steps: reach the hospital, find General Kang, move him to safety, wait for the lifts, take him to the roof, load him into the exfil helicopter. He stays in a hospital bed that you push; you sedate him first; you ride one lift down to the lobby, cross to a second bank and hold for about a minute. A lost supply crate can fall on red smoke in the atrium during the hold (+150 XP). Extraction summary: Story Mission Payout 5,000, although the card shows 25,000. The mission closes the Human Intel chapter; the card reward is the Mirae Electrical Room Key plus a Defibrillator.
- Silent Ledger: Heavenly Luck Casino, Cheongun Village; card threat Medium. Eight HUD steps: approach the casino, find the lobby lifts, start the generator, reach penthouse security, open the vault fence through a parking-level camera, breach the basement vault, take the printing plates, extract them by helicopter. Side tasks inside: up to 12 cash bags worth 1,500 each, counted only once they sit on a D50 cargo truck bed in the garage; a vehicle repair with four slots on a disabled radar truck. Card rewards: Gold Bar, Counting Room key, Polaris vehicle, 25,000; the extraction summary showed a Story Mission Payout of 5,000. It ends the Counterfeit chapter.
- Shopping Hacks: in and under NuriGO Mall (POI card High). Eleven HUD steps: hack a laptop at the mall encampment, wait for data analysis, enter the code you were given at the parking security door, explore the security annex, feed four robot cores into the lab door terminal, wait for it, hack five lab computers, hold during the network breach, wait for the servers, loot the lab vault (a Bio-agent Sample), escape to a rooftop Fulton exfil. The door code changes every match. Night vision goggles lie at the encampment and in the annex. Intact K9 Battery Cores come from robot dogs that self-destruct soon after going down; a destroyed core does not work. Payout line: 5,000.
- Iron Citadel: Chang-san Air Base (base Critical, bunker Extreme); card gear 3-Plate vest, Medkit, Ammo Box. Fourteen HUD steps from approaching the base to extracting the Armory Manifest: search up to four places for an access card, upgrade it at a reader in Admin Complex No. 2 (it becomes a single-use Air Base Armory Card), find the armory doors, assemble a six-digit armory code from three note fragments (digits change between matches), collect the manifest, plant charges in the armory and on three strike aircraft, capture a SAM site, extract. Commander Colossus can appear in the bunker. One run's summary: Story Mission Payout 5,000, three Side Ops 1,500. Card rewards include a key, an LTV and 25,000.
- Grid Assault: DBN Headquarters and Hanex Bank in The Grid (both Extreme). Six HUD steps: find the drone control (a server-room laptop), get the first code segment from an HVT (Hanex Bank or the Nenshin tech complex, varies), get the second by killing Lieutenant Roze on the broken upper floors, compile the code on floor 10, upload it at the roof antenna while defending it, broadcast from the top of the lattice tower. Deathstalkers attack the roof; Colossus roams the streets. Mission complete does not extract you. Summary: Story Mission Payout 5,000, Side Ops 500 (Kill Roze), First Lieutenant Takedowns 10,000.
- Fallen Ember: Haneul Nuclear Reactor (briefing Extreme); gear 3-Plate vest, Medkit, Gas Mask, Radiation Blockers (60 seconds of protection). Seventeen HUD steps: reach and unlock Reactor 01 (by the roof and an ascender line), start and withdraw three fuel rods from the flooded pool, decontaminate them in Reactor 02 while defending, secure a warhead, beat reinforcements, take the boathouse key from Commander Colossus, raise the garage door, meet and escort the helicopter by patrol boat. Lieutenant Chemist holds Reactor 01 (side op Kill Chemist); Compound Delta has a Clear the Military Compound side op. A legendary Hazmat Suit hangs in the Reactor 01 control room; an underwater DRC locker in Reactor 02 held an ISO Nightshade. A nuclear blast on the horizon ends the mission; no cutscene; the match goes on until you exfil. Summary: Story Mission Payout 5,000, two Side Ops 1,000, First Lieutenant Takedown 10,000. Card: 25,000.
- In Grid Assault and Fallen Ember a squad wiped before exfil still saw its mission payouts in the extraction summary.
