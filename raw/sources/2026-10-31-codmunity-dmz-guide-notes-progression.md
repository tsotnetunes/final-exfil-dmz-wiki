# codmunity.gg: MW4 DMZ Guide
URL: https://codmunity.gg/dmz/guide
Captured: 2026-10-31 (page shows "Updated 24 October 2026")
Trust: third-party (community database and guide site)

Facts taken, in our words:
- The Bank has a cap; Bank upgrades printed at the 3D Printer raise it. A content creator's Bank held 1,000,000 after launch and the next upgrade showed 2,000,000 (from footage).
- Cash over the cap is lost without a warning, whether it comes from selling items or from extracting.
- DMZ Cash pays for infils, Vendor and Gunsmith buys, Lieutenant intel, Bounty intel, the Reduce Bounty button (5,000 in the bounty trailer) and MIA recovery.
- Dynamic Ops pay 10,000 to 20,000 straight into the Bank.
- The price of recovering an MIA Operator is set by their Trait Points; gear does not come back; recovery discounts are 3D Printer upgrades.
- Recovery slots are the round slots next to the Ready button; each protects one Operator or a set of weapons for a single deployment and is used up even when you extract.
- Secure Slots: an item moved to the Secure Slot survives a failed exfil; keep keys there. The Medium Backpack has one Secure Slot; the 8-slot Basic Pack has none.
- On death, gear stays in Hajin, the Operator goes MIA and your Bounty is gone.
- Everything carried is persistent between deployments; the page gives no wipe schedule.
- Bounties: the first Operator kill starts a Bounty that grows with each kill; four kills make you Wanted, with several higher Wanted states; at the top one your general area can be shown to every Operator. The trailer shows 100,000 after a sixth kill and 250,000 after an eighth. A tag can carry a Bounty of 0. A hunter's payout reaches the Bank after the exfil. Bounty intel costs more for a higher Wanted level.
- The AI wanted level at the top right of the HUD is a different thing from the Bounty Wanted status.
- Most Wanted leaderboard: top 50, weekly, with status (Wanted or Eliminated) and Bounty; Bounty Hunters: top 50 by bounties claimed and DMZ Cash earned; global or friends.
- Infil: on foot free; Polaris Dagor A1 5,000; LTV 25,000, unlocked by Operation Iron Citadel; helicopter 50,000, unlocked by Operation Grid Assault; cargo plane 100,000 with a drop point you pick.
- Lieutenant hunt intel at the Boss Board: 25,000.
- Free Loadout: one gun, a melee weapon, a single Lethal and a single Tactical, plus one armor plate; no backpack beyond the 8-slot Basic Pack and no parachute. Your own Operator still deploys, so a death still means paying to recover them.
- Some valuables with a sell price are also ingredients for FOB upgrades; check before selling.
- Vendor at DMZ level 5; stock rotates daily; Vendor upgrades add offers and slots.
- AAR: rating F to S++; XP is given even on an F rating or a failed exfil.
