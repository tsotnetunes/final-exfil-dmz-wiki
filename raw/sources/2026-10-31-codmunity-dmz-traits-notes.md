# codmunity.gg: MW4 DMZ Traits
URL: https://codmunity.gg/dmz/traits
Captured: 2026-10-31 (the page shows "updated 30 October 2026"; per-Trait pages read the same day show 23 and 24 Oct)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- A community database with a Trait planner. It knows 97 Trait names: 65 placed in the three trees as live Traits and 32 in a separate list it calls leaks, not confirmed by the developers. The Codex uses only the 65.
- Each tree is drawn as nine tiers. The Major Traits sit in pairs at tiers 3, 6 and 9:
  - Warfighter: Equipment Professional or Combat Resistance (3); Viper Sense or Quick Fix (6); Possum or Adaptive Restock (9).
  - Scout: Hostile Looter or Container Looter (3); Field Crafting or UAV Upgrade (6); Overwatch or Thermal Padding (9).
  - Survivalist: Faster Revive or Consumable Duration (3); Impact Smokes or Free Revive (6); Numb the Pain or Die Hard (9).
- Adrenaline Burst (Warfighter), Investor (Scout) and Air Superiority (Survivalist) are filed as Minor Traits.
- Minor Traits placed in Warfighter (16): Killing Spree, Free Prep, Plate Pouch, Supplied Drops, Adrenaline Burst, LT Hunter, Final Intel, Bounty Intel, Bounty Hunter, Quick Regen, Combat Scanner, Plate Speed, Popcorn Tacticals, Popcorn Lethals, Hostile Ammo, Quick Knife.
- In Scout (15, one more not yet named): Cash Ops, Resource Pockets, Stealthy, Fast Learner, Mini Tracker, Key Cash, Auto Fill, Ammo Reserves, Field Medic, Investor, Resource Hoarder, Resource Management, Pack Rat, Combat Scavenger, Ammo Scavenger.
- In Survivalist (16): Rescuer, Consumable Stacker, Rad Blocker, Resupply, Expedited Extraction, Air Superiority, Lucky, Objective Oriented, Fuel Economy, Faster Learner, Mechanic, Metabolism, Deep Breath, Plate Organization, Strong Arm, Rampage.
- Numbers in its short effects: Cash Ops +20% cash from Ops; Resource Pockets +20% resources from scrapping; Resource Hoarder and Pack Rat +20% resource cap each; Plate Pouch and Plate Organization +2 spare plates each, and they stack; Consumable Stacker up to 2x; Mechanic 2x refuel and repair speed; Ammo Scavenger 2x ammo from packs.
- Other short effects: Free Prep adds a random consumable to the Free Loadout; Supplied Drops gives a chance of an extra container in supply drops; Combat Scanner sends a UAV pulse when you down someone; Stealthy makes the threat meter (shown as stars) rise more slowly; Auto Fill refills Lethals and Tacticals from the backpack; Field Medic revives allies faster; Resource Management sends overflow resources to your lowest resource.
- Its leak list includes Operator Tracker, which COD Central and dmzhub list as a live Minor Trait.
- The list page shows no Trait Point costs and no Pro versions. It says every pick is locked in and the first point in a tree goes to a random Minor.
- Per-Trait pages: Possum (24 Oct) repeats the in-game description; UAV Upgrade (24 Oct) names UAV Upgrade Pro as a Prestige Trait that also enlarges the minimap; Field Crafting and Overwatch (23 Oct) add nothing beyond the one-line effect.
