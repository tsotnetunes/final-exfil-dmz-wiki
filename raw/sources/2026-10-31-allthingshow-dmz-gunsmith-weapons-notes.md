# All Things How: How to Unlock the Gunsmith and Weapons in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=297919
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Reaching DMZ level 10 opens the Gunsmith bench; it does not unlock any weapon by itself.
- At the start, the Manual board in the FOB reads Gunsmith Weapon Manuals 0/28.
- Each base weapon needs its own Manual before it can be bought; one Manual unlocks one weapon, and the unlock counts only once you extract with it.
- Manuals came from ordinary loot containers and from the backpacks of enemy Operators.
- Buy New Weapon has a tab per class (assault rifles, SMGs, shotguns, LMGs, marksman rifles, sniper rifles, pistols, launchers); each tile shows the build cost, the weapon level (for example 1/72) and how many saved builds you have.
- Cart example on an ISO Nightshade: opening the first attachment slot cost 1,000 and the PULSE-MW Laser 1,900 on top.
- Owned Blueprints are ready-made builds; the first copy is free, after that the Beta Forged Han 86 has a build cost of 88,312.
- The Gunsmith card says discounts unlock through DMZ player progression; no amounts given.
- A Gunsmith build takes five attachments (shown as 0/5) plus a separate Apex slot; the station card says weapons found in Hajin can carry up to eight.
- Bale's Legendary Dreadnought LMG comes with five attachments fitted.
- The Gunsmith can be opened from the DMZ side menu or from an owned weapon in the Stash.
