Facts taken, in our words:
- Special Delivery: collect three bomb parts (Explosives from a dead drop at Cheongun Outskirts, Explosives Wiring from a safe at Compound Charlie, an Explosives Numpad), craft the bomb at a workbench (Cheongun Village around F4 in one route, the Construction Zone in another), then either upload intel at a laptop on a lattice tower near Cheongun Outskirts or capture a UAV Tower (South Island), reach the Mobile Launch Platform (north across the Hajin River near Hanrim Village and Revolution Dam, or Shoreline Station), plant the bomb, defend for about a minute, take cover before it blows. Several steps gave 300 XP each.
- Broken Resupply (one route): hack a comms server at NuriGO Mall, defend it, take the bomb from the trunk of a white camper (use the trunk, not the enter prompt), find the keypad code in a safe in a back office in NuriGO West, open the locked room and plant the bomb on the WMD. The guide prints one code as if fixed; other sites saw different codes, so codes vary.
- Capture SAM Site: SAM sites at Chang-san Air Base and Imjin Farmland among others; clear the area, use the control computer, stay in the capture area until the timer ends (leaving pauses it), defend until the side op shows complete. The captured site fires at hostile aircraft in range by itself, Deathstalkers included; enemies can retake it if you move too far away. Reward seen: 150 XP. For Eye in the Sky the downed aircraft must be a cargo helicopter.
- Radar truck repair: a damaged Radar Truck is marked on the tac map; its repair point lists four parts: Car Battery, Alternator, Spark Plug, Vehicle Water Pump. Gas Stations and garages are the best places to look. Partial repairs do not count. Parts can be extracted and brought into a later deployment. A repaired truck's radar pings nearby enemies while you drive it. The Mechanic Order (page 15 for the writer) needs a repair and then 19 kills on enemies the truck pinged.
