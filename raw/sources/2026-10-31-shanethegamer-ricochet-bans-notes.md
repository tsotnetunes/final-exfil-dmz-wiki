# Shane the Gamer: RICOCHET carries 70,000 hardware bans into Modern Warfare 4 and targets Cronus users
URL: https://www.shanethegamer.com/news/ricochet-carries-70000-hardware-bans-into-modern-warfare-4-and-targets-cronus-users/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 29 Sep 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Relays Activision's Team RICOCHET anti-cheat update from its "road to launch" series (posted 25 Sep 2026).
- More than 70,000 new hardware bans; hardware bans already in place carry into MW4 at launch and are not reset.
- A hardware ban targets the machine rather than the login, so making a new account on the same hardware does not get around it.
- About 5,000 accounts have been banned for cheating with Cronus Zen, XIM Matrix and similar devices; RICOCHET judges such devices by what they do in a match, not by brand; distributors have received cease-and-desist notices.
- MW4 needs TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot for online play on PC; some AMD and Lenovo systems fail Secure Attestation, and RICOCHET says it is working with Microsoft and hardware makers on fixes.
