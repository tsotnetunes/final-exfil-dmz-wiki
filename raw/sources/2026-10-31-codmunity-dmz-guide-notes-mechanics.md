A community database's beginner guide to MW4 DMZ. Some points are credited on the page to named YouTube creators; they are kept here as reports and the creators are not named. Read through a web-fetch tool, so the wording below is ours.

Facts taken, in our words:
- There is no solo button or playlist: you go in alone by switching Squad Fill off on the deploy screen (key 1 on keyboard, left trigger on controller).
- The Free Loadout is one weapon, one Lethal, one Tactical, a melee weapon and one armour plate, with no backpack (the basic 8 slots) and no parachute.
- Infil refills the plates, ammo, Lethals and Tacticals you have equipped; it does not refill what is in your backpack.
- When you die your gear stays in Hajin; what you extract is yours to keep; a run only counts once you exfil.
- Recovery slots (some earned from DMZ Orders) can protect an Operator or weapons for one deployment if set before deploying.
- The MIA recovery price rises with what the Operator has invested; reported figures run from a few hundred DMZ Cash to about 90,000 at 18 Trait Points.
- Solo players get a Stealth Meter that shows how visible they are.
- Exfil countdown: solo, 5 seconds as soon as you step on; squad, 30 to 45 seconds until every member is on, then 5 seconds.
- Ammo and armour caches refill 60 seconds after use.
- Helicopter pilots should avoid the SAM sites downtown and at Chang-san Air Base, and any SAM site another player has hacked; the missile arrives almost at once, so fire flares as it launches.
- A parachute carried in the backpack is not equipped; check before jumping from height.
- The stamina bar sits bottom centre; when it is red you cannot tactical sprint; an Energy Drink gives unlimited stamina for a while.
- Health is 150 and regenerates after a short delay; the Tourniquet leaves you Wounded with a 100 maximum; a Self-Revive Kit, a squadmate's revive, a Revive Pistol or a Defibrillator get you up without a wound.
- Selling past the Bank's cap loses the extra cash without a warning.
- Gas Stations repair and refuel ground and air vehicles and refill Gas Cans; Marine Gas Stations serve boats and aircraft; players fly the Heli, while the Heavy Chopper and the Monarch fly themselves; the Train and Tram run on their own.
- Signs that a dead player has the Possum Trait: no vultures over the body, plates cannot be looted, and the body stays a body instead of turning into a loot bag.
