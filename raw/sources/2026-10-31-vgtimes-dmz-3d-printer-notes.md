# VGTimes: MW4 DMZ 3D Printer: how to unlock it and get recipes

Facts taken, in our words:
- The printer stands in the FOB and needs DMZ level 2 (a couple of early runs). Bandages, Medkits, the 1-Plate and 2-Plate Armor Vests and the Small and Medium Backpacks are available from the start. Tacticals open at level 4, Lethals at 7, Field Upgrades at 12 and Fire Support at 28.
- The four Resources (Synthetics, Electronics, Chemicals, Metals) come from looted items converted automatically and take no backpack space; Ingredients such as Biomaterials and Binding Catalysts take inventory slots and show up mostly in certain kinds of building. Anything gathered is lost if you die before exfil.
- At a recipe you see what you need and what you have; numbers turn green when you have enough; you can print several at once and send them to the Stash or straight to your loadout. Pin a recipe or an Ingredient to spot it more easily while looting; a button shows where an Ingredient can spawn. Rigid Synthetics show up in homes, not in shops or banks.
- Recipe sources: some at the start; DMZ levels; DMZ Orders (the 3-Plate Armor Vest, Large Backpack, C4 and Night Vision Goggles recipes); and loot in Hajin, rare in crates, which unlocks once you extract and pays 5,000 DMZ Cash. The Large Backpack is also a level 34 reward.
- Prototype RF Modules drop from the Deathstalker (needed for the Fulton Drop Marker, Large Secure Backpack and Vendor Item Upgrade I). Military Composites drop from Colossus (Tempered Armor Vest, Secure Scavenger Backpack, Epic Ingredient Printing).
- The Tempered Armor Vest recipe is in a Lieutenant crate in The Doctor's office on the second floor of Mirae General Hospital (F5); the office is locked and The Doctor carries the key.
- Costs it lists match Destructoid's, including the Self-Revive Kit (level 48), Bank Upgrade III (100,000 Cash and 5 Infantry Invasion Plans) and both Ingredient Printing upgrades. Epic-tier ingredient recipes each take 100 of a basic Resource plus 3 rarer Ingredients (its example: Alloyed Plating from 100 Metals and 3 Structural Alloys). It advises building Stash Item Upgrade I first, since early storage is tight.
