The article gives key names in Portuguese only; below they are matched to the English names other sources use. No grid squares, sources or contents are given.

Facts taken, in our words:
- Keys come in four tiers here: Rare, Epic, Legendary and Ultra; some keys work at more than one place.
- Rare: Black Market weapons locker (a black-market basement in east Hanrim Village or south Deadtown); Coin Mining Den; Gas Station Electrical Room (a gas station basement south of the Construction Zone, southeast of The Grid); Gun Store weapons locker (a basement west of the Cheongun Village police station, or east of the Songdo Wharf gas station); Haejeok Radio Station (the northern relay station, Imjin Farmland); Haksan School Armory (a second-floor classroom north of Deadtown); Hanwoori Art Records Room (east wing of the Hanwoori Design Complex, The Grid); Hazard Storehouse (south of Reactor 02); Imjin Mill Foreman's Office (first floor of the Rice Mill); Mechanic's Shack (south of the Cheongun Village clinic); Militia Arms Depot (Gangbyeon Apartment B); Mirae Electrical Room; Old Barber Shop (beside the crab stand, southeast Songdo Wharf); the train depot storeroom (a basement between the gas station and the fire station, Cheongun Village); Shop Records Room (over the supermarket south of the Wolves Stadium clinic); Underground Propaganda Store Room (cell block basement, Re-Education Center); Weapons Mod Workshop (second floor of the motor pool, Chang-san Air Base).
- Epic: Commandant Office; Counting Room Vault; Executive Arms Auction; Field Depot (a supply warehouse northeast of Chang-san Air Base); Hanex Guard Station (the floor below the roof of Hanex Bank, between vault rooms); Hoesik Supply Room (south building of the Rice Mill); Lucera Suite 703 (12th floor of Hotel Lucera, south of Hanex Bank); Netshin IT Room (14th floor, Netshin Tech Complex); Police Weapon Depository (a garage east of the Wolves Stadium police station); Re-Education Center Annex; Vault Armory.
- Legendary: Iskra's Control Room (top floor of the south building, Cheonwang Peak); Jang-Ui's Converted Power Room; Jin-Tae's lounge (upper floor of the casino, reached by the basement lift and the roof); Kang's incinerator (central 14th Political Prison); Roze's high-rise armory (14th floor, east side, Netshin Tech Complex); Supreme Leader's Bunker Access Card; The Chemist's Secure Armory (by the north boathouse at the reactor); The Doctor's Command Office (7th floor of Mirae General Hospital, offices 701 to 712).
- Ultra: Air Base DRC weapons locker (the bunker armory, through Operation Iron Citadel); Reactor DRC Locker (the central pool inside Reactor 02); Shopping DRC Locker (the DRC Labs under the NuriGO Mall garage, during Operation Shopping Hacks); and the Door Breacher Charge, a consumable that opens any locked door.
