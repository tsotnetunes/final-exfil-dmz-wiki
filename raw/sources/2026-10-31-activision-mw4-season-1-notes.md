# Activision blog: Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 and Call of Duty: Warzone Season 1: All You Need to Know
URL: https://callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 28 Oct 2026)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words (weapons and DMZ weapon-related lines only):
- Season 1 starts 6 Nov 2026.
- Cronen Osprey, assault rifle: Battle Pass tier 16, a Battle Pass Blueprint of it at tier 87; arrives at the Season 1 launch on 6 Nov. 70 weapon levels, 30-round magazine, 90 in reserve, 882 rounds per minute.
- CSG-35, SMG: Battle Pass tier 36, Battle Pass Blueprint at tier 73; arrives at the Season 1 launch on 6 Nov. 42 weapon levels, 28-round magazine, 112 in reserve, 1200 rounds per minute.
- JAK 12, shotgun: an event reward later in the season; fully automatic; 8 rounds, 16 in reserve, 323 rounds per minute.
- ZLR Talonhawk, melee: an event reward later in the season; a combat axe with 10 levels and Mastery Badges.
- New Apex Attachments: Cronen Helix Mk. II for the Cronen Osprey (fires on trigger pull and on release, high damage, quick semi-auto rate); CSG Crossfire for the CSG-35 (akimbo pair, more volume, less accuracy); JAK Barrage for the JAK 12 (a salvo of sticky explosives that detonate after attaching).
- In-season Apex rewards from the Wager Match event: Hyeon Auto for the Hyeon Burst (fully automatic fire) and Kastov 545 for the Kastov 762 (5.45x39mm rounds for smoother handling and quicker follow-up shots). An event also rewards an M16 Burst Attachment for the M4 (controlled burst fire); the post does not call it an Apex.
- The post's summary mentions two new Apex Attachments while describing three for new weapons; it does not explain the difference.
- Blueprints: Battle Pass purchase gives a FiNN LMG blueprint "Fortune's Favor"; finishing all 100 premium tiers gives a Hyeon Burst blueprint "Blue Viper"; tier 100 gives an Ultra Mosin-Nagant blueprint "Bellum"; BlackCell gives an M4 blueprint "Perimeter Lance" with "Occultus" and "Exitium" variants from BlackCell challenges, and seven BlackCell blueprints for the Kastov 762, Axion, KG-7 Vulcan, ISO Nightshade, CSG-35, Cronen Osprey and Mosin-Nagant.
- DMZ: new map X01, a GRANIT Arms black site on Hajin's border; the post names no DMZ weapons and does not mention Weapon Manuals or the Gunsmith. With Season 1 BlackCell, the Vendor sells extra weapons and gear for DMZ Cash.
