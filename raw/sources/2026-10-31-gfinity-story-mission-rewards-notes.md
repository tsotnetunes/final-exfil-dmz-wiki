Facts taken, in our words:
- Each of the six Story Missions pays 25,000 DMZ Cash plus items:
  - Operation Critical Extraction (the article spells it Critical Execution): the Mirae Electrical Room key and a Defibrillator.
  - Operation Silent Ledger: a Gold Bar, the Counting Room Vault key and a Polaris Dagor A1.
  - Operation Shopping Hacks: the Coin Mining Den key and Night Vision Goggles.
  - Operation Iron Citadel: the Supreme Leader's Bunker Access Card and an LTV.
  - Operation Grid Assault: Roze's Highrise Armory key and a helicopter.
  - Operation Fallen Ember: the Reactor DRC Locker key and a cargo plane.
