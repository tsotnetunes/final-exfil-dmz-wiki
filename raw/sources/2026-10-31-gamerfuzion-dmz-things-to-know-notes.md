# GamerFuzion: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ, 10 things to know before launch
URL: https://gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-10-things-to-know-before-launch/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 3 Oct 2026, updated 9 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (the site marks some points as unconfirmed; so do we):
- You have to own MW4 to play DMZ; no free version was announced. The 2022 DMZ came with the free Warzone download.
- MW4 is not on Xbox Game Pass this year (the site points to Xbox Wire's edition notes).
- Prices: Standard 69.99 USD, Vault Edition 99.99 USD.
- 20 squads of three, so up to 60 players, from Screen Rant's interview with Infinity Ward; assimilation can add one enemy player for a squad of four.
- Match length: about 30 minutes in the 2022 beta, now 40 to 50 minutes (Screen Rant interview with Infinity Ward).
- Lobbies are filled with squads that picked the same activity and topped up with Free Roam squads (Screen Rant).
- From a video recap of Infinity Ward on the COD Pod, which the site could not confirm in writing: no hard wipes are planned, Operators can be retired instead, and no PvE-only mode is planned.
