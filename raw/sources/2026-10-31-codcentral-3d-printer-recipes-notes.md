# COD Central: MW4 DMZ 3D Printer Recipes: Every Craftable Item

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 20 Oct 2026, updated 22 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words. A guide site's full list of printer cards with the rarity of each printed item and how each recipe unlocks.
- Count: 107 cards: 12 Upgrades, 9 Consumables, 8 Plate Carriers, 10 Backpacks, 10 Tacticals, 11 Lethals, 16 Field Upgrades, 10 Fire Support, 7 Gear, 14 Ingredients.
- Unlock routes it uses: available at the start; found in Hajin (46 cards); a DMZ level (20); DMZ Orders (8: Decoy Grenade, Flash, C4, DDOS, Recon Drone, 3-Plate Armor Vest, Large Backpack, Night Vision Goggles); a Story Mission (1: Defibrillator, from Operation Critical Extraction); a station being open (Dog Tag Case, Gunsmith and Vendor upgrades); the previous tier (Bank II and III, MIA Recovery Discount III); an Ingredient Printing upgrade (the 14 ingredient cards).
- All eight Ultra recipes are found in Hajin: Tempered Armor Vest, 3-Plate Ballistic Gel Vest, 3-Plate Helmet Armor Vest, Large Secure Backpack, Secure Scavenger Backpack, Door Breacher Charge, Fulton Drop Marker, Crossfire. Each needs a part that cannot be printed (Military Composites, Experimental Compounds, Nano-Structured Alloy or a Prototype RF Module).
- The printer opens at DMZ level 2; Tacticals at 4, Lethals at 7, Field Upgrades at 12, Fire Support at 28.
- Input amounts match Destructoid's list for every card, except DDOS: 30 Electronics and 3 Detonators here.
- Rarity of printed items (examples): Energy Drink Common; Bandage, Pain Killers, Sedative Inhaler Uncommon; Radiation Blockers and Smelling Salts Rare; Medkit Epic; Self-Revive Kit Legendary; Door Breacher Charge Ultra; 1-Plate Uncommon, 2-Plate Rare, 2-Plate Ballistic Gel and 2-Plate Helmet Epic, 3-Plate Armor Vest Legendary, 3-Plate Ballistic Gel, 3-Plate Helmet and Tempered Ultra; Small Backpack Uncommon, Medium Rare, Medium Scavenger, Medium Weapon and Small Secure Epic, Large, Large Scavenger and Medium Secure Legendary, Large Secure and Secure Scavenger Ultra; all Tacticals Common except Heartbeat Sensor and Stim (Uncommon) and Spotter Scope (Rare); all Lethals Common except Drill Charge, Throwing Knife and Trip Wire (Uncommon); Field Upgrades Uncommon (eight basic ones), Rare (Armor Box, DDOS, Defibrillator, Recon Drone, SAM Airburst Turret, Trophy System), Legendary (Revive Pistol), Ultra (Fulton Drop Marker); Fire Support Rare (Care Package), Epic (Bomb Glider, Bombing Run, Combat Drone, UAV), Legendary (Advanced UAV, Artillery Beacon, Impact Warhead, Railgun Sentry), Ultra (Crossfire); Gear Uncommon (Weak NVG), Rare (Disposable Parachute, Gas Mask, NVG), Epic (Durable Gas Mask), Legendary (Elite NVG, Parachute).
- Level unlocks it gives: Sedative Inhaler 3; Smoke and Concussion 4; Frag and Throwing Knife 7; Snapshot 9; Ammo Box and Spotter Scope 12; Armor Box 14; Shock Stick 17; Semtex 23; Gas Mask 26; Care Package 28; UAV 31; Molotov 36; Thermite 41; Self-Revive Kit 48; Inflatable Decoy 52; Disposable Parachute 55. It also gives the Revive Pistol as level 25 but notes that this conflicts with the level 25 reward (a Gunsmith discount) and that the recipe was still locked at level 34.
- Upgrades: Bank I 20,000 Cash; Bank II 50,000; Bank III 100,000 and 5 Infantry Invasion Plans (after Bank II); Bank IV and Stash Item II exist, costs not captured; MIA Recovery Discount III 5 W.M.D. Manifest, 5 Foreign Intelligence Targets, 10 Power Regulators, 10 Comms Modules. Other upgrade costs match Destructoid.
- Rare Ingredients with no print card: Binding Catalyst, CPU, Nitro Compounds, Printed Circuit Board, Stock Rod, Structural Alloy, High-Tenacity Fibers, Nylon Cloth. Military Composites, Prototype RF Module and Nano-Structured Alloy come from Commanders; it names no source for Experimental Compounds.
- Where ingredients are found, as it lists them: Biomaterials in hospitals and chemical lockers; Control Unit in computers and tech bags; Detonators in military and industrial areas; Electronic Sensors in tech bags and commercial areas; Reactive Metals in industrial areas and toolboxes; Rigid Synthetics in residential areas and civilian duffel bags; Alloyed Plating at the Chang-san Air Base hangar and repair bay and Bubun Yard; Ballistic Fabric at the Chang-san Air Base armory and barracks and the La Grande Vie rooftop at Songdo Wharf; Comms Module in the server rooms of the Nenshin Tech Complex (the site writes Netshin) and Cheonwang Peak Array and the hillside facade buildings of Deadtown; Nylon Laminate in the wings of the Citadel's main building, the underground holding area of Deadtown and the Mujin Nightclub of the Heavenly Luck Casino; Power Regulator at the Haneul Nuclear Reactor and electrical substations; Stabilized Reagents at Mirae General Hospital and medical tents; Turned Components at the Imjin Farmland rice mill, the Hajin City construction zone and Namjjog Station; Volatile Explosives in the NuriGO Mall DRC lab, the chemical buildings of the Citadel prison yard and the storage tents of Imjin Farmland.
- Traits: the Scout Trait Resource Pockets gives 20 percent more printer resources from scrapped items.
