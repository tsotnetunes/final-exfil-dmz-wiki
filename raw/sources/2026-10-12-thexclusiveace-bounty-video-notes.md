## Rules it restates (all in the official sources)
- Killing players (not AI) makes you a Killer with a Bounty on your Dog Tag, which also counts your Operator kills; four player kills across deployments make you Wanted; other Operators get an alert when a Wanted Operator comes close.
- Several Wanted levels. A Bounty Station lists the Wanted Operators in your deployment with their Bounties; paid intel shows a circle around a target that shrinks and updates faster at higher levels; at the top level the Killer's area may be visible to everyone.
- To collect a Bounty: kill the target, take the Dog Tag, extract. Your own Bounty is lost if another player extracts with your tag, even if you were revived; you can also pay it off at a Bounty Station.
- Who fired first, being outnumbered and other factors count toward a Bounty.
- Vultures circle the bodies of dead Operators.
- The FOB's Bounty Leaderboard shows the top 50, worldwide or among friends.

## The creator's own views (not facts)
- The system should keep players who avoid fights away from PvP while keeping PvP possible, but Call of Duty players may still turn DMZ into a PvP fest.
- The June intel said hunting bounty targets can lessen the target on your own back; the creator reads the 9 Oct guide as not doing that and expects hunters to become Wanted like any killer. The guide does not say either way.
- He would like a PvE-only mode later; none is planned at launch (the developers' Q&A said the "Single Squad Mission" option seen in a screenshot was internal).
