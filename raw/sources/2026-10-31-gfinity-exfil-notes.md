# Gfinity Esports: Call of Duty MW4 DMZ, how to extract (exfil)

Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Standard exfil zones are marked on the Tac-Map with an exit-door icon; at the zone, by the smoke, choose the prompt to call extraction transport (usually a helicopter).
- Calling transport makes a sound that nearby players and AI can hear; a countdown runs, then board quickly when it lands; a short timer runs before take-off and the successful exfil screen.
- Extracted items go to the FOB Stash.
- Fulton extraction balloon: found as loot in high-threat POIs, crafted at the 3D Printer or given as a reward; deploy it in the open with nothing overhead and attach high-value items, which the balloon carries off to your Stash (this site's reading).
- Exfil flare: fire it in an open area to bring a transport helicopter to you; it is bright and can alert enemies.
