# DMZMap: MW4 DMZ Skill Tree, Traits Explained
URL: https://dmzmap.com/guides/skill-tree
Captured: 2026-10-31 (last updated 29 Oct 2026; its Trait list was checked 27 Oct 2026 on the launch version)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Three trees of 22 Traits, 66 in all. Each tree has nine rows read from the bottom: row 1 holds one random starting Minor; rows 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8 offer three Minor Traits, of which you take one; rows 3, 6 and 9 offer two Major Traits, of which you take one.
- Every pick costs one Trait Point. 18 points at most, one per Dog Tag level up to Onyx III; nine picks fill a tree.
- Minor Traits come from one pool that the trees share: the same Minor can sit in different trees on different Operators, and several have been seen in two or three trees. 33 of the 48 Minor Traits had been seen when the page was written.
- No respec: picks are permanent. Prestige clears the Traits and adds one random permanent Prestige Trait.
- 28 Oct update: Possum does not trigger while the squad that killed you is still near, unless 4 minutes have passed (the page points to the official patch notes).
- Major rows: the same pairs as the other sources (rows 3, 6 and 9).
- Pro (Prestige) Traits: twelve, upgraded versions of the row 6 and row 9 Majors, four per tree. What each adds: Adaptive Restock Pro, the extra item can be rarer; Possum Pro, no tourniquet health penalty; Quick Fix Pro, regeneration starts at once on a kill; Viper Sense Pro, a warning when an enemy Operator aims at you; Overwatch Pro, towers cover a wider area; UAV Upgrade Pro, a bigger minimap; Thermal Padding Pro, the vehicle you are in is also hidden from UAVs; Field Crafting Pro, half the resource cost; Free Revive Pro, the tourniquet can be used while Wounded; Numb the Pain Pro, longer effects; Impact Smokes Pro, a bigger smoke that lasts longer; Die Hard Pro, faster movement in Last Stand.
- Minor Traits it has seen, with the trees: Adrenaline Burst (Warfighter); Investor (Scout); Air Superiority (Survivalist); Ammo Reserves (Scout); Ammo Scavenger (Scout, 2x ammo from packs); Cash Ops (Scout, +20% Op cash); Combat Scavenger (Scout and Warfighter); Consumable Stacker (Survivalist, up to 2x); Expedited Extraction (Survivalist); Faster Learner (Scout and Survivalist, on top of Fast Learner); Final Intel (Survivalist and Warfighter, final exfil marked from the start); Free Prep (Warfighter); Hostile Ammo (Warfighter); Key Cash (Scout, rarer keys pay more); Killing Spree (Warfighter, up to 2x); LT Hunter (Warfighter, one more Lieutenant revealed); Mechanic (Survivalist, 2x); Metabolism (Survivalist and Warfighter); Mini Tracker (Scout, 50% chance of a radar pulse on an unopened container); Objective Oriented (Survivalist, Op crates one rarity higher, up to Rare); Pack Rat (Scout, +20% resource cap); Plate Organization (Survivalist, +2 spare plates, stacks with Plate Pouch); Plate Speed (Warfighter); Popcorn Lethals (Warfighter); Quick Knife (Warfighter and Survivalist); Rad Blocker (Survivalist); Rampage (Survivalist); Rescuer (Survivalist); Resource Hoarder (Scout, +20% resource cap); Resource Pockets (Scout, +20% from scrapping); Resupply (all three trees; Armor Boxes give 2 extra plates); Stealthy (Scout, more kills before a Hunt Squad comes); Strong Arm (Survivalist).
- It gives no DMZ Cash figure for Trait picks or for MIA rescue.
