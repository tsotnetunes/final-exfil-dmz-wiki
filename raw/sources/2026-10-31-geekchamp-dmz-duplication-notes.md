# GeekChamp: Infinity Ward is taking action against DMZ item duplication exploits
URL: https://geekchamp.com/infinity-ward-is-taking-action-against-dmz-item-duplication-exploits/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 27 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- The official Call of Duty Updates account said that item duplication glitches and deliberate abuse of exploits break the Terms of Service, and that enforcement against accounts found exploiting them was beginning.
- Activision's policy allows penalties from temporary bans up to licence termination, applied to the account and every linked device; the article does not say what any account received.
- No item rollback, wipe or count of actioned accounts had been announced.
- The launch patch notes of 25 Oct 2026 list closed DMZ exploits without naming them, so it cannot be checked whether every duplication method was covered; the 24 Oct notes fixed an out-of-bounds spot in the FOB and the exfil helicopter vanishing.
