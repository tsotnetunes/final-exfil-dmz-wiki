# Gaming ProMax: All DMZ Boss Locations in COD Modern Warfare 4 (Lieutenants & Commanders)
URL: https://gamingpromax.com/all-dmz-boss-locations-cod-modern-warfare-4/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 23 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- 13 bosses: 3 Commanders, 10 Lieutenants. Boss Board opens after the second DMZ Order; Lieutenant intel $25,000; Lieutenant bounty $10,000 after extracting the tag; Commander bounty $50,000; Commander intel free.
- A challenge to kill every Hajin Lieutenant once has its own reward; protect tags in a Secure Slot.
- Colossus: sighted at Cheongun Village, Songdo Wharf, Chang-san Air Base, the Citadel and the NK Wilderness; grenades barely hurt it; fight from height or cover with sustained fire; avoid its green gas. Drops Experimental Compounds and Military Composites.
- Deathstalker: NuriGO Mall area, DBN Headquarters, eastern Hajin; one SAM missile downs it; sniper fire from the DBN helipad takes about 25 seconds. Drops Prototype RF Module and a Drone Motherboard. It shares the DBN roof with Roze.
- Tank: roads on the South Korean side; armour thinner at the rear and top; use a Javelin, RPG or SAM launcher; Thermites and Semtex do not work; loot three spots (front, rear, top). Drops Nano-Structured Alloy.
- Gorani: Mujin Pass above Hanrim Village; ambushes from the treeline; approach from above; drops a MAR-9.
- The Chemist: radiation zone warehouse by the reactor tanks; needs Radiation Blockers; drops a Legendary Oris 8.6.
- Jin-Tae: Heavenly Luck Casino; drops a Legendary ISO Nightshade (Highroller's Fortune).
- The Doctor: Mirae General Hospital; armoured escorts; drops a Legendary Patriot XMR and the Command Office key; the room's LT crate holds the Tempered Armor Vest recipe.
- Bale: Re-Education Center in Deadtown; elite troops in close support; also in the Tank Depot of the Air Base Bunker during the Chemical Denial Dynamic Op; drops a Type 73.
- Iskra: Cheonwang Peak Array; flank from the hillside; drops a Warlord shotgun.
- Jang-Ui: Netshin Tech Complex, The Grid (Extreme); explosive robot dogs and laser systems; drops a Hyeon Burst.
- The Warden: the main yard or the Citadel; riot shield, flank him; drops an X-58 NYX (Incarcerator) and Kang's Dog Tag.
- Roze: DBN Headquarters roof terraces; sniper; HVAC units block sight lines; drops an Astaroth .57.
- Scavenger: no fixed place, spawns near recently dead players; trackable with Lieutenant Hunt towers; drops an Oris 8.6.
