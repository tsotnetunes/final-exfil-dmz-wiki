# Playhub: Best Traits in Modern Warfare 4 DMZ
URL: https://playhub.com/blog/call-of-duty/best-traits-in-modern-warfare-4-dmz-578591
Captured: 2026-10-31 (dated 28 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (the site also advertises account sales; only game facts and its build advice are used)

Facts taken, in our words:
- 18 points per Operator at Onyx III, per Operator and not per account. A tree takes nine points, one pick per row. Major Traits are fixed pairs at the 3rd, 6th and 9th point; Minor Traits are random, can differ between Operators, and some appear in more than one tree.
- Point splits it describes: 9/9/0 (two full trees), 9/6/3, and 6/6/6 (the first two Majors of every tree).
- Its picks: solo, Thermal Padding (it calls it essential), with Consumable Duration, Free Revive and Die Hard on the Survivalist rows; squads, Faster Revive, Impact Smokes and Numb the Pain, with Overwatch for teams that use towers. Its tree walkthrough lists Combat Resistance first on Warfighter row 3.
- Minor effects with numbers: Mini Tracker, a 50% chance of a radar pulse when you loot an unopened container; Resupply, two extra plates from Armor Boxes; Cash Ops, 20% more Op cash; Ammo Scavenger, double ammo from packs; Metabolism slowly restores health lost to the tourniquet.
- It suggests countering Possum with mines or by watching the body (its opinion), and expected a nerf before the 28 Oct change.
