Facts taken, in our words:
- After you finish DMZ Orders page 1 and come back from your first deployment, the FOB says Story Missions are unlocked. Your active chapter does not block Story Missions.
- Page 1 Orders: Gear Up (equip a weapon, backpack, vest and Medkit from the Stash), Crackshot (knock down three Firing Range targets), First Op (deploy in Dynamic Ops). Page reward: a Medkit, a 2-Plate Armor Vest and cash.
- Then you choose a chapter: Human Intel, Underground or Counterfeit. Each has two pages of three Orders and ends with its Story Mission (Critical Extraction, Shopping Hacks, Silent Ledger). The page number a chapter takes depends on the order you choose them (Counterfeit 1/2 showed as page 2 when picked first). To move on you must finish every task line on the active page.
- Chapter rewards in total: Human Intel: Flash recipe, cash, Basic Triage Kit, UAVs, Operator Rescue Beacon. Underground: DDOS, Decoy Grenade and Night Vision Goggles recipes, cash, Basic Restock Kit, Operator Rescue Beacon. Counterfeit: Recon Drone recipe, cash, explosives kit, Operator Rescue Beacon, Weapon Insurance.
- Counterfeit 1/2: Hostile Force (enter a Police Station, search three containers there, kill five soldiers inside; desk drawers and filing cabinets count), Robbery (open a safe; extract 5,000 of backpack and loadout value, which only ticks after a successful exfil), Execute Intel (hack the UAV Tower in Deadtown, kill five soldiers it marks, pick up the Counterfeit Cash they drop). Safes are green boxes with a red lock panel; hold interact, no key or code. Known safe spots include Compounds Bravo, Charlie, Delta and Foxtrot; the Haneul reactor has a Crack Safe side op. The UAV Tower is a camouflaged box with a mast and a laptop; the tall lattice mast with a skull is a Lieutenant Hunt tower instead. Page reward: Recon Drone recipe.
- Human Intel 2/2: Blindness (print four Flash grenades at the 3D Printer, then kill eight enemies while they are blinded), Payment Due (start an Op from a Dynamic Op phone inside a Hajin building, then earn 11,500 from Dynamic Ops; deploying straight into an Op does not tick the phone task; the Songdo Wharf phone started Sky Heist), Operation Critical Extraction (finish the mission; Lieutenant The Doctor holds the hospital with a Kill the Doctor side op). Page reward: three UAV Fire Support, 20,000, an Operator Rescue Beacon and a Weapon Insurance.
- Page 4, Operation Rewards: start an Op from a phone in Cheongun Village (Free Roam, ground infil; Cheongun Village cannot be picked as a Dynamic Operations deploy), finish it, open its crate. The village phone gave Dead Man's Cache, which went from Cheongun Village (comms server) to a bedroom safe at Mujin Pass (the bomb) and ended at Imjin Suburb; it paid +7,500 and 4,575 XP. The care package after the Op gave 75 XP when opened and held a Rigid Synthetics. Page 4 also has Preparation and Work Quietly; page reward Medium Backpack, Ammo Box and 20,000.
- Eye in the Sky (Underground 1/2): take a SAM Site, let it down a cargo helicopter, open the crate from the wreck. SAM sites seen: Deadtown (Low), Imjin Farmland (Low), north of NuriGO Mall (Medium). Activating the launcher laptop adds a Capture SAM Site side op with a capture bar; leaving the area pauses it; trucks bring reinforcements; once captured the SAM fires by itself. The crate has its own care package marker and can land far from the wreck.
- Watch for Bumps (Underground 1/2): get into an enemy vehicle after clearing its crew, refuel and repair it at a Gas Station (park under the canopy until the bar fills), run over seven enemies. While refuelling a separate side op called Repair the Vehicle can appear.
