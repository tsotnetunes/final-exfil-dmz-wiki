# Activision blog: Ready for Launch: All Modern Warfare 4 Operators, Weapons, Equipment, and Multiplayer Create-a-Class Features

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 19 Oct 2026)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- 33 weapons at launch: 24 primaries, 8 secondaries and 1 melee weapon.
- Assault rifles (8), with the player level that unlocks each in Multiplayer: Han 86 (4), M4 (14), Hyeon Burst (21), Patriot XMR (27), Kastov 762 (34), Oden (39), Axion (52), Cronen Squall (55).
- SMGs (5): ISO Nightshade (4), VEL 46 (5), Wz.55 Striga (18), PPSh-41 (30), X-58 Nyx (34).
- Shotguns (2): Rezi 12 (4), Warlord (32).
- LMGs (3): FiNN LMG (4), Type 73 (9), APOC-338 (44).
- Marksman rifles (3): MAR-9 (4), Oris 8.6 (11), Mosin-Nagant (36).
- Sniper rifles (3): KG-7 Vulcan (4), Astaroth .57 (10), Signal 50 (35).
- Pistols (4): SANG 9mm (4), Krait P68 (7), .50 GS (41), Mojave .357 (48).
- Launchers (4): PILA (4), RPG-8 (23), JOKR (43), PM-40 Anvil (54).
- Melee (1): FSS Carbon Edge 1095 (4).
- Short character of each weapon, restated: Han 86 a balanced South Korean service rifle for close and mid range; M4 an adaptable full-auto rifle; Hyeon Burst a North Korean prototype firing three-round bursts; Patriot XMR full-auto with heavy kick that rewards control; Kastov 762 full-auto 7.62x39mm with a strong hit per bullet; Oden a hard-hitting 12.7x55mm bullpup with a low fire rate; Axion a subsonic .300 BLK rifle that rewards accuracy; Cronen Squall an open-bolt bullpup with marksman-style range. ISO Nightshade a fast-firing 9x19mm all-rounder; VEL 46 a nimble 4.6x30mm SMG with predictable recoil; Wz.55 Striga a mobile 9x18mm SMG with a low fire rate; PPSh-41 a very fast-firing close-range SMG with a large magazine; X-58 Nyx an integrally suppressed 5.8x21mm SMG for stealth. Rezi 12 a semi-auto 12 gauge; Warlord a pump 12 gauge with a big tube and slow reload. FiNN LMG a belt-fed 5.56 with a high fire rate and good control; Type 73 a Korean 7.62x54r LMG for mid and long range; APOC-338 a belt-fed .338 with a slow cycle and heavy recoil. MAR-9 a semi-auto 7.62 DMR; Oris 8.6 a light 8.6 Blackout bolt action; Mosin-Nagant a hard-hitting bolt action. KG-7 Vulcan a bolt-action sniper that cycles faster than most; Astaroth .57 a single-shot 14.5mm rifle meant to kill with one clean hit; Signal 50 a .50 cal anti-materiel rifle with a recoil-reducing barrel. SANG 9mm a quick 9mm sidearm; Krait P68 a semi-auto .45; .50 GS a heavy .50 pistol; Mojave .357 a close-range revolver. PILA fires guided or unguided missiles at vehicles; RPG-8 an unguided anti-vehicle rocket; JOKR a guided top-attack missile; PM-40 Anvil a pump-action 43mm grenade launcher. FSS Carbon Edge 1095 a carbon steel combat knife.
- Apex Attachments: a new optional tier that changes how a weapon works (new roles, fire modes or built-in equipment) instead of small stat changes. Unlocked as the last reward for fully levelling a weapon; equipped separately and uses no attachment slot. Each supported weapon has its own; launchers and the melee weapon have none: 28 in all.
- Apex list (weapon: Apex, effect in our words, slots it blocks): Han 86: Han X20 Skybreaker, adds a targeting optic and a 20mm airburst launcher, blocks Optic, Stock, Barrel. M4: M4 Hurricane, a top-loading high-capacity 5.7x28mm bullpup-style conversion that fires faster, blocks Barrel, Magazine. Axion: Axion Clearcut, a chainsaw-style rear grip with a handlebar foregrip and a hip laser for mobile play, blocks Optic, Laser, Underbarrel, Stock, Rear Grip. Cronen Squall: Cronen Maelstrom, fires a missile you steer while aiming, blocks Laser, Underbarrel. ISO Nightshade: ISO Trace, tracking rounds that show a hit target's movement in the optic, blocks Optic, Ammunition. VEL 46: VEL 46 Snap, a tactical sling for faster swaps and Quick Draw with Overkill, blocks Stock. Wz.55 Striga: WZ.55 Striga Rampage, an akimbo conversion with paired frames, blocks Optic, Stock, Underbarrel. PPSh-41: PPSh-41 Ember, an underbarrel launcher with incendiary rounds, blocks Barrel, Underbarrel, Magazine. X-58 Nyx: X-58 Void, a subsonic setup for very quiet fire and hidden hits, blocks Barrel, Ammunition. Rezi 12: Rezi 12 Enforcer, pump-action cycling plus a side saddle of explosive slugs. - Gunsmith: in Multiplayer and in the DMZ FOB; shows Damage, Range, Accuracy, Mobility and Handling, with a Details view. Pistols take full attachments; launchers and the melee weapon change only their look.
- Shared Attachments: an attachment unlocks once and is then on every weapon that can take it; a level that would grant an owned attachment gives a Bonus Level Up.
- Attachment types: Optic, Laser, Barrel, Muzzle, Underbarrel, Stock, Rear Grip, Comb, Ammunition, Magazine.
- Optic Rail Placement: move an optic along the rail from the Optic menu; a quick ADS in the Firing Range shows the result.
- Weapon levels differ per weapon (examples: Han 86 72, Warlord 60, Signal 50 74); the top level unlocks the Apex Attachment and Weapon Prestige. Weapon Prestige gives exclusive camos and relocks the weapon's attachments, putting its level back to zero.
- Gunny: suggests builds from unlocked attachments in Create-a-Class and in the DMZ FOB Gunsmith.
- Firing Ranges: one for Multiplayer and one in the DMZ FOB, open at any time.
- DMZ: any weapon or attachment unlocked or levelled in Multiplayer or DMZ can be used in the FOB Gunsmith if you have the DMZ Cash to buy it; weapons can also be looted in a deployment, built from a Weapon Manual found in Hajin, or bought from the FOB Vendor.
- DMZ loadout: two primaries, two secondaries or one of each; more with certain Weapon Backpacks.
- Operators Reece, Coffey, Simone and Hector unlock at DMZ player levels 4, 9, 15 and 19.
