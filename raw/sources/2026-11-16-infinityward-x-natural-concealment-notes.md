- The Natural Concealment Event went live in Modern Warfare 4 on 16 Nov 2026.
- It is a camo event: each weapon class has challenges, and finishing them unlocks that class's event camo.
- The challenges can be done in Multiplayer, DMZ or Warzone; each challenge needs completing in one mode only.
