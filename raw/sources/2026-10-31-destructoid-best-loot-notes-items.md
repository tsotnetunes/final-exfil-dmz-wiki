# Destructoid: Best loot locations in Modern Warfare 4 DMZ
URL: https://www.destructoid.com/best-loot-locations-in-modern-warfare-4-dmz/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 25 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- No loot spot is guaranteed; what you find changes between rounds.
- Compounds across the map usually hold military items, often behind a hidden way in; weapon lockers and the safe are what to aim for, and strong AI may guard them.
- Cheonwang Peak Array has safes and technology ingredients.
- The train is among the best sources of 3D Printer resources; its route can be followed.
- At Chang-san Air Base the writer found Weapon Manuals and Blueprints almost every time, but only during Operation Iron Citadel.
