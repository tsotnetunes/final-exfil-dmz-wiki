A commercial boosting site; its platform rules are not sourced to Sony, Microsoft or Nintendo, so treat them as reported. Facts taken, in our words:
- On PS5, online multiplayer in MW4 needs a PlayStation Plus membership (the cheapest tier is enough); free-to-play Warzone does not.
- On Xbox Series X|S, online multiplayer needs a paid Game Pass tier that includes online play; Warzone does not.
- On Switch 2, Nintendo Switch Online is the subscription for paid online play.
- On PC (Battle.net, Steam, Xbox on PC) no subscription is needed.
- MW4 is not in Game Pass at launch.
- An internet connection is needed to install and update the game and for every online mode; only the Campaign plays offline once installed.
- The page does not mention DMZ by name.
