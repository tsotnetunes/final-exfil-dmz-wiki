# Secure Slot items after a looted backpack: hands-on test notes

Captured: 2026-11-08, by the Final Exfil team. A test in the game; there is no URL.
Trust: first-hand, one test.

- Why: the site's Ask answered "not known yet" to what happens to Secure Slot items when your backpack is looted and you are then revived.
- What was done: in a DMZ match the player was eliminated, an enemy looted the backpack that held a Secure Slot, and the player was revived and exfilled with no backpack that has Secure Slots.
- What happened: back in the FOB, the items that had been in the Secure Slot reappeared once a backpack with a Secure Slot was equipped again.
- Not tried: a failed exfil after a looting; exfilling with a different secure backpack picked up in the match.
