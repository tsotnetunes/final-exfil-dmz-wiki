# COD Central: MW4 DMZ Best Traits, Every Trait, Build and Prestige
URL: https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-traits/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 23 Oct 2026, updated 30 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Nine rows per tree: row 1 a random starting Trait; rows 3, 6 and 9 two fixed Major Traits, the same on every Operator; rows 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8 three random Minor Traits. One pick per row at one point each; a full tree is nine points; 18 points per Operator.
- Minor Traits are not tied to one tree. The page ranks 46 Minor Traits and expects two more (66 minus 18 Majors leaves 48).
- It says other sites name Field Medic and Supplied Drops, which it has not seen in game.
- It ranks Operator Tracker (follow enemy footprints) among the Minors; codmunity files that name among its leaks.
- Numbers in its effects: Plate Pouch and Plate Organization +2 spare plates each; Cash Ops +20%; Objective Oriented one rarity higher, up to Rare; Resupply 2 extra plates from Armor Boxes; Mini Tracker 50%; Resource Pockets +20%; Consumable Stacker and Killing Spree up to 2x; Pack Rat and Resource Hoarder +20% each; Ammo Scavenger 2x; Mechanic 2x.
- Its ratings of Minors: take Plate Speed, Plate Pouch, Plate Organization, Cash Ops, Objective Oriented, Expedited Extraction; skip the ammo Minors, Popcorn Lethals and Tacticals, Bounty Intel, Quick Knife, Rad Blocker, Fuel Economy, Mechanic, Deep Breath; Adrenaline Burst, Air Superiority and Final Intel situational; Auto Fill good.
- Twelve Prestige Traits, granted at random, up to three per Operator: Possum Pro, Overwatch Pro, Quick Fix Pro, Viper Sense Pro (top); Field Crafting Pro (50% lower craft cost), UAV Upgrade Pro, Free Revive Pro, Adaptive Restock Pro (good); Numb The Pain Pro, Die Hard Pro, Impact Smokes Pro, Thermal Padding Pro (hides occupied vehicles from UAVs) (weak).
- Solo build: Warfighter and Scout (Equipment Professional, Viper Sense, Possum; Container Looter, Field Crafting, Thermal Padding). Squad build: Scout and Survivalist (Container Looter, UAV Upgrade, Overwatch; Faster Revive, Impact Smokes, Numb The Pain).
- It reports, without a source, that other players can take over a tower held with Overwatch. The official notes of 23 Oct say UAV Towers can be stolen back from enemy control.
