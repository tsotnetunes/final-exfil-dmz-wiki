# Gaming Trend: interview with Infinity Ward on MW4 DMZ (story missions, mastering DMZ)
URL: https://gamingtrend.com/interviews/actual-story-missions-mastering-dmz-and-rpg-knockouts-highlight-our-modern-warfare-4-dmz-interview/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 6 Jun 2026, before launch)
Trust: third-party (press interview with Infinity Ward's Studio Multiplayer Creative Director)

Facts taken, in our words:
- The developer says Story Missions are far more curated than the missions of the 2022 mode, so each run plays out in a consistent way.
- His example is the casino mission: open the vault gate from the penthouse, restore power in the basement, return to open the gate, drill the vault and hold off waves while it opens; the goal is the plates the North uses to print money.
- The Story Mission version adds dialogue from a CIA handler and its own enemies; the same content can be played outside the Story Mission structure without that extra flavour.
- A test run he described had a hidden-cache phone objective and a roaming Commander in a tank.
