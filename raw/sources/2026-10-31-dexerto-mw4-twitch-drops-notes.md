Facts taken, in our words:
- Drops count from gameplay watched from 23 Oct at 10 AM PT. No end date given.
- Rewards: an Emblem, a Charm and a Calling Card; the watch time needed was not yet published.
- To link accounts: sign in to your Activision account on the Call of Duty website, open your profile menu at the top right, choose Linked Accounts, pick Link Account next to Twitch and sign in to Twitch.
- Watch drop-enabled MW4 streams (look for the game's Twitch tag) while signed in with the linked account, claim the rewards in the Twitch Inventory page, and they appear in game; a restart may be needed.
- The page lists six older rewards as expired, without dates.
