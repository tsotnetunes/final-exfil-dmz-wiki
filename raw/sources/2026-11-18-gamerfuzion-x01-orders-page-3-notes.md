# GamerFuzion: X01 Orders page 3 solo guide (10 Nov 2026)
URL: https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-x01-orders-page-3-control-room-medical-wing-data-center-solo/
Captured: 2026-11-18 (published 10 Nov 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- The Control Room console hold is broken by enemies, so clearing the room first helps; the wave that comes counts toward the ten kills.
- The Medical Wing feed spot is an office desk with an old CRT monitor; first aid kits and medical bags there supply the ten healing items (bandages and medkits counted).
- The Data Center lies south east of the Power Generators, with large Ri screens and the video log console; robot dogs arrived about 10 seconds after the log was pulled.
- The route from the Medical Wing to the Control Room ran through the Logistics Tunnel, the Bio Lab and the Power Generators; the Pump Station is a loot detour. The page took about 18 minutes in one run.
