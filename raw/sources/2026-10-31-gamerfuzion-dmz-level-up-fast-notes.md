# GamerFuzion: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ: How to Level Up Fast Solo, Level 4 to 10 in Two Story Missions
URL: https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-how-to-level-up-fast-best-xp-method-story-missions-xp-tokens/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (a guide site writing up a creator's solo runs; the numbers are his results)

Facts taken, in our words:
- Operation Critical Extraction took the player from DMZ level 4 to 7 for about 44,100 DMZ XP; Operation Silent Ledger from 7 to 10 for about 42,900; each run took about 30 minutes.
- A failed exfil keeps the DMZ XP earned; cash reaches the Bank only on a successful exfil.
- DMZ XP earned with an Operator raises that Operator's Dog Tag; every level of the tag is worth one Trait Point.
- Player or weapon XP tokens are switched on before deploying; the article does not say whether a token multiplies the DMZ level or Operator XP.
- The 20 Oct fixes removed a bug that gave double credit for a Story Mission.
