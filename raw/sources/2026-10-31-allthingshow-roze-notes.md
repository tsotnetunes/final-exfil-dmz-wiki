# AllThingsHow: MW4 DMZ: How to Beat Roze at DBN Headquarters (Location and Drops)
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=297639
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Lieutenant on the top rooftop terraces of DBN Headquarters (compass Extreme): white stepped terraces, HVAC units, metal stairs, a radio mast.
- Route: the emergency stairwell, clearing guards floor by floor; the side op changes from Reach the Lieutenant's Location to Kill Roze on the roof.
- She snipes from the terraces and can down you in one shot; guards move on the stairs; the Deathstalker patrols the same roof and strafes it.
- Tactic: peek from behind HVAC units and stair rails, flank through the terraces, revive in cover, regroup in the inner stairwell.
- Drops: Roze's Dog Tag (Legendary), an Astaroth .57 (Rare sniper, $6,400) or The Kraken depending on the match, Infantry Invasion Plans, a Drill Charge.
- In Operation Grid Assault she is on the broken upper levels of DBN Headquarters, and killing her gives the second code segment.
