# All Things How: How to finish the Watch for Bumps Order in MW4 DMZ
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=297995
Captured: 2026-10-31 (updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Enemy patrols drive Grizzlies; kill the soldiers inside and use the enter prompt to take the vehicle; sitting in the driver's seat completes the Order's steal task. Enemy military trucks also count once the soldiers nearby are cleared.
- Examples: a patrolling Grizzly in Deadtown (Low threat) and a military truck at the checkpoint by the elevated railway on South Island.
- The Order's other vehicles panel lists a Camper, a Sedan and a Dreadnought, without details.
- Gas Stations show on the Tac-Map with a fuel pump icon; examples on the south-west side of Deadtown, by a pharmacy on the main road at Songdo Wharf, and in Cheongun Village. To refuel and repair, drive under the canopy, stop next to the pumps and wait for the refuelling and repairing bar to fill.
- The points-of-interest panel also lists Marine Gas Stations, Fire Stations and Police Stations.
- Enemies shoot at vehicles; if yours catches fire, repair it at a Gas Station or switch to another enemy vehicle. Vehicle fights are common at Songdo Wharf, South Island and the Cheongun Village streets toward the Heavenly Luck Casino.
