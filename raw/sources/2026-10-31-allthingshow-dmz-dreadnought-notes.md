Facts taken, in our words:
- An Aston Martin Dreadnought waits in the basement garage of the Heavenly Luck Casino in Cheongun Village, north Hajin (a High threat area).
- You can drive it away at once: no key, purchase or unlock is needed.
- It has four seats.
- The casino is also where the Story Mission Operation Silent Ledger takes place, but the article does not say the mission is needed for the vehicle.
