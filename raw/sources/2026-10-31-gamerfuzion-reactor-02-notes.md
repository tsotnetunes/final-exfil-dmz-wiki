# GamerFuzion: MW4 DMZ Reactor 02, how to decontaminate the irradiated container
URL: https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-reactor-02-decontaminate-irradiated-container-legendary-gear/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 27 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (one live solo run):
- Approach by water: a RHIB waits by the bridge; go northeast under the bridge along the edge of the radiation and land on the beach below the reactor.
- Route into Reactor 02: past the fence until the reactor banner (High threat) shows, up short steps under a radiation sign, over a big pipe and wall into a rubble yard, to a small metal shed by the dome, through two doors and down stairs marked 2 into a dark hall.
- At the bottom of Reactor 02 the HNR machine processes an Irradiated Container (an Ultra item carried in the Secure Slot). One container gave an Ultra Warlord shotgun, a Kastov 762 Weapon Manual, an Epic valuable and a Legendary parachute; no official drop table.
- A gas mask is required: a purple mask lasted about three minutes in the radiation, a blue one about a minute and a half. Radiation Blockers mitigate radiation; the game warns if you deploy without them. A Hazmat suit is an option if found.
- About three and a half minutes in radiation from beach to beach. Colossus was seen around the reactor.
- Exit by boat down the coast and up the river to Hajin City; a Fulton Drop Marker is suggested for a quick exit after leaving the radiation.
