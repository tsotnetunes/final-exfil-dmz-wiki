Interview with Infinity Ward's multiplayer creative director and a multiplayer technical designer at the June reveal. Pre-launch.

Facts taken, in our words:
- DMZ supports squads of three; solo players can drop in alone and rely on proximity chat or stealth; matchmaking is based on squad size, so solos are meant to meet solos.
- The technical designer said a solo player can do all of DMZ's content.
- Players can disengage or hide from AI; a visual indicator warns you just before you are spotted, and going prone can help you avoid a fight.
- Internal playtest groups tried to finish all DMZ content in stealth, and the designer said full stealth runs are possible.
- The new Tourniquet lets you revive yourself instead of waiting for a teammate.
- Lost Operators can be bought back with cash through the M.I.A. feature; each Operator has its own skill tree.
- Proximity chat lets players team up with others or betray them.
- Dynamic Operations grew out of watching players run contracts in the 2022 beta.
