Facts taken, in our words:
- One Trait Point per Dog Tag level (Steel I to Onyx III, 18 levels). Every Trait, Major or Minor, costs one point. Points belong to the Operator.
- Major Traits come in pairs at the 3rd, 6th and 9th point spent in a tree; taking one locks out the other for that Operator.
- The first point in a tree buys a random Minor; the other Minors sit in hidden groups of three behind the Major before them.
- No respec or refund. The ways to start over are a new Operator or a Prestige. A rescued MIA Operator keeps their Traits; dismissing one means a new Operator with none.
- Minor examples it names: Popcorn Lethals, Adrenaline Burst, Killing Spree (up to twice the normal duration), LT Hunter, Pack Rat (+20%, stacks with Resource Hoarder), Investor, Deep Breath, Air Superiority, Metabolism.
