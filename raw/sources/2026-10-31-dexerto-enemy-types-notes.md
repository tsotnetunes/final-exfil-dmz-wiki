Facts taken, in our words:
- The page calls the hostile force the Korean People's Army.
- Groups: soldiers (grunts, armoured, armoured with gas masks, faceless armoured), robotic enemies (drone swarms, LMG drones, four-legged K9 robots), vehicle squads (attack helicopter patrols, road convoys) and bosses.
- Lieutenants are high-priority targets, each worth a 10,000 reward; its table of places and drops matches Gfinity's.
- Commanders roam rather than spawn in one place: Colossus around Cheongun Village and Songdo Wharf (Experimental Compounds, Military Composites); Deathstalker around NuriGO Mall and DBN Headquarters (Prototype RF Module); the tank around the South Korean side (Nano-Structured Alloy).
