# COD Central: MW4 DMZ Orders: Every Page, Task and Reward
URL: https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-orders/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 21 Oct 2026, updated 30 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party (fan site; the writer and a teammate played every page)

Facts taken, in our words:
- 24 pages of three Orders each (72 Orders). Every page pays 20,000 plus items, recipes or kits (480,000 cash in all).
- Five chapters of two pages each, each ending with a Story Mission Order: Human Intel, Underground and Counterfeit are picked in any order after page 1; The Grid and Hornet's Nest are picked after page 11. Pages 1, 4, 7, 10, 11, 14, 15 and 18 to 24 have no chapter.
- Page numbers up to 15 match for everyone apart from chapter order; after 15 they can differ by account (a teammate saw Hornet's Nest as pages 22 and 23). Page 24 was the last page on both accounts.
- Orders that ask for a Story Mission tick on their own if you already finished that mission.
- Some Boss Board Lieutenants stay locked until chapters are done: Gorani and Bale need Underground, Counterfeit and Human Intel; Roze needs Underground.
- Iron Citadel and Grid Assault open when the first three chapters are done; Fallen Ember opens after both.
- Page list (tasks shortened):
  - 1: Gear Up, Crackshot, First Op (no extraction needed). Reward: Medkit, 2-Plate Armor Vest.
  - Human Intel 1/2: Hunter, Triage Protocol, No One Left. Reward: Flash recipe, Basic Triage Kit.
  - Human Intel 2/2: Blindness, Payment Due, Operation Critical Extraction. Reward: UAV x3, Operator Rescue Beacon, Weapon Insurance Contract.
  - 4: Operation Rewards, Preparation, Work Quietly. Reward: Medium Backpack, Ammo Box.
  - Counterfeit 1/2: Hostile Force, Robbery, Execute Intel. Reward: Recon Drone recipe, Explosives Equipment Kit.
  - Counterfeit 2/2: Suit Breach, Eyes Everywhere, Operation Silent Ledger. Reward: Operator Rescue Beacon, Weapon Insurance Contract.
  - 7: Recognition, Radiation Station, Healer. Reward: UAV, Armor Box.
  - Underground 1/2: Eye in the Sky, Watch for Bumps, Triangulation (transmitters on two Lieutenant Hunt towers and on La Grande Vie). Reward: DDOS, Decoy Grenade and Night Vision Goggles recipes.
  - Underground 2/2: Dead Battery, Death From Above, Operation Shopping Hacks. Reward: Basic Restock Kit, Operator Rescue Beacon, Weapon Insurance Contract.
  - 10: Crashed Crate, Surprise Me, Healer (print or extract 3 Reactive Metals, extract 30 Metals, print an Armor Box). Reward: Large Backpack, Basic Triage Kit.
  - 11: Information Power, Arms Deal, Friendly Face. Reward: Defibrillator, Inflatable Decoy, C4 recipe.
  - The Grid 1/2: I'm Over Here, Worth Every Penny, No Time (a Dynamic Op under 8 minutes; its counter showed 2 but ticked after one fast Op). Reward: 3-Plate Armor Vest recipe, Triage Kit.
  - The Grid 2/2: Opulent, Civil Service, Operation Grid Assault. Reward: Recon Equipment Kit, Operator Rescue Beacon, Weapon Insurance Contract.
  - 14: Lethal Tools, Committed, Nothing Is Secure. Reward: Structural Alloy x2, Stock Rod x2.
  - 15: Fire Insurance (print 5 Thermite, stick 5 enemy vehicles with crews inside), Quiet, Mechanic. Reward: CPU x2, Nylon Cloth x2, Inflatable Decoy.
  - Hornet's Nest 1/2: Goliath, Click Boom, Navigate With Care. Reward: Large Backpack recipe, Weapon Restock Kit.
  - Hornet's Nest 2/2: Jail Break, Keep Out (any Legendary key carrying a Lieutenant's name counts), Operation Iron Citadel. Reward: Helmeted Armor Kit, Operator Rescue Beacon, Weapon Insurance Contract.
  - 18: How Bulletproof?, Abandoned, Locksmith. Reward: Nitro Compounds x2, SERE Kit.
  - 19: Avionics, Philanthropist, Multi-Talented. Reward: Binding Catalyst x2, Explosives Equipment Kit.
  - 20: Ears Everywhere (hack the broadcast system on the second floor of the DBN TV building at Cheonwang Peak Array, take the Encrypted Voice Logs), The Vault (a Hanex branch bank in Cheongun Village or at Wolves Stadium: restore power, get into the vault), Massive Damage (DDOS the tank three times to drop its reactive armor, hit it with a rocket, destroy it). Reward: 3-Plate Armor Vest, Bomb Glider.
  - 21: Hard Carry, Stalking the Stalker (three Deathstalkers with a SAM site, one with a launcher, DDOS one), Operation Fallen Ember. Reward: Triage Kit, Operator Rescue Beacon, Weapon Insurance Contract.
  - 22: DRC, Unstoppable, Astronaut (loot 21 containers in radiation without radiation damage; exfil three times on the white final exfil helicopter). Reward: High-Tenacity Fibers x2, Trap Equipment Kit.
  - 23: Rule of Three (print or extract 2 Ballistic Fabric, extract 5 Nylon Cloth, print a 3-plate carrier), Temp Job (40 Side Ops), No Time (4 Dynamic Ops in one deployment). Reward: Printed Circuit Board x2, Recon Equipment Kit.
  - 24 (last): High Quality Loot (extract 2 Weapon Manuals, 4 Recipes and an Ultra weapon), You Won't See Me (a sniper rifle with 5 or more attachments, 100 kills with it), Upper Class (earn 250,000 from Dynamic Ops or Story Missions). Reward: Gold Bar x2, Advanced SERE Kit.
- Glitches seen: after page 20 the terminal labelled the next page as page 3; after pages 10 and 11 the extraction summary showed Underground 2/2 Orders and rewards (check your Stash).
