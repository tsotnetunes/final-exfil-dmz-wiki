Facts taken, in our words:
- Each Op begins at a phone in a point of interest; activating it puts the Op on the left of the HUD under the minimap. A finished Op pays XP and cash and drops a crate. The Dynamic Operations deploy card advertised 10,000 to 20,000 cash and a Supply Drop of rising rarity. The radiation countdown keeps running during an Op.
- Dead Man's Cache (Low threat): paid 4,575 XP and 7,500. One route started at Cheongun Village and ended at Imjin Suburb (bomb from a bedroom safe at Mujin Pass). A second route from a phone in Imjin Farmland: reach an enemy comms server, download the cache location and defend the hack, take an explosive near a Hajin River bridge, find and blow up an ammo and armor cache, then kill the HVT in the area (+4,590 XP for that step). A care package dropped afterwards; opening it gave 75 XP.
- Ignition Point (Low, Imjin Farmland): go to the area, take a bomb from a dead drop (a white dumpster in a yard by the gas station), plant it on the WMD.
- Sky Heist: phone at Songdo Wharf. Steps seen: find the intel, download intel about the helicopter carrying WMDs (Compound Charlie), go to a SAM site (Cheongun Village), capture and hold it, go to the helicopter's landing spot (South Korean Border Zone), hack the helicopter and defend the hack, fly it to the exfil point before its fuel runs out, then either stay on board to extract with everything you carry or jump out with a disposable parachute and keep playing. Soldiers and drones attack the landing zone; the exfil point can be over the Haneul reactor (High).
- Signal Breaker: phone in a greenhouse at Imjin Farmland (Low). Steps: go to the target area (a wrecked tank in misty woods), collect three bomb parts (a hidden cache by the tank, a two-storey house in Imjin Farmland, a safe upstairs in Compound Alpha, which is High threat), craft the bomb at a workbench in Imjin Farmland, then plant it on three radar trucks by climbing each truck's ladder and entering a code. The bombs go off after the third. Pay: +6,000 XP and +10,000 cash. Clearing Compound Alpha completed a Clear the Military Compound side op worth 225 XP.
- Deathstalker guide: a Capture SAM Site side op appears when you go to a SAM site; clear the soldiers, stay in the zone, and the bar fills in a little over 30 seconds; the site then fires and can down a Deathstalker. A SAM site sits at NuriGO West (Medium).
- Deathstalker guide, Boss Board: the Commanders tab lists Colossus, the Deathstalker and the Tank with a 50,000 reward each; after a kill the card shows the reward as claimed. Deathstalker wrecks can hold a Prototype RF Module, Volatile Explosives and a Drone Motherboard.
