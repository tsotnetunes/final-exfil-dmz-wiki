# AllThings.How: Call of Duty Modern Warfare 4 DMZ exfil guide (helicopter and Fulton)
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=297309
Captured: 2026-10-31 (updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- If the whole squad is wiped before getting out, the screen reads Failed to Exfil and only Secure Slot items come back; a successful escape shows Extraction Successful and an extraction summary.
- Helicopter: at a green-smoke site, use the call prompt; an inbound banner appears and touchdown takes close to a minute, during which enemy reinforcements and an enemy helicopter may arrive. After landing an Exfil Departing timer of about 35 seconds runs; you can shoot from the ramp once in the air.
- Fulton: a green air-transport crate on the NuriGO Mall roof, reached by ascender cable and ladder; activating it starts a countdown of about 10 seconds to board with your squad, then the line lifts you off the roof. The NuriGO Mall card there shows High threat.
- Flare: fired into the sky; loud and visible across the map, so it gives your position away.
- Songdo Pier has an exfil site with guards on the boardwalk.
- Story Mission exits: Operation Silent Ledger sends a helicopter for the printing plates after you leave the Heavenly Luck Casino; Operation Iron Citadel ends with exfilling the armory manifest by a helicopter near the base fence after capturing a SAM site (a base alarm sounds); Grid Assault and Fallen Ember show Mission Complete and then need a normal exfil.
- Tips: check bag, route and a backup exit; ping a second exfil; cover approaches; replace plates before calling.
- The HUD shows a radiation countdown; when it runs out radiation closes the map.
