# Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 patch notes: 11 Nov 2026 block
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes

Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- Global, every mode: the true hitscan range of all bullet weapons was multiplied by 3.91, which the notes put at about 35 meters on average, 288% further than at launch, to make close fights more responsive.
- Global: a new long-range bullet simulation makes ballistic bullets land 34 ms sooner, meant to steady mid and long range rifle fights.
- DMZ: solo players and duos now build Bounties a little faster.
- DMZ: the Bombing Run Killstreak (Fire Support) is switched off in X01.
- DMZ fixes: spectator and downed-player HUD pieces such as Calling Cards no longer overlap; claiming a Battle Pass reward from inside DMZ no longer fails; a newly chosen Operator Skin in the FOB now shows correctly the first time.
