# dmzcodex.com: MW4 DMZ Gunsmith guide: weapon prices, unlocks & builds
URL: https://dmzcodex.com/guides/dmz-gunsmith-weapons-guide/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 29 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Up to five paid attachment slots, any order, each dearer than the last; the page says Apex slots are free once levelled; a 5% Gunsmith discount; cosmetics are free; found weapons can carry up to eight attachments.
- Weapon levels and attachment unlocks are shared across DMZ, Multiplayer and Warzone; a duplicate unlock becomes a Bonus Level Up.
- A Weapon Manual pays 5,000 DMZ Cash and unlocks the base weapon for good; a DMZ Manual does not unlock the gun in Multiplayer.
- Gunsmith menu prices: Han 86 6,000; M4 6,250; Hyeon Burst 5,250; Kastov 762 5,500; Patriot XMR 6,500; Rezi 12 4,000; Warlord 3,500; FiNN LMG 8,000; Type 73 7,500; APOC-338 7,750; KG-7 Vulcan 16,000; Astaroth .57 24,000; Signal 50 14,000; MAR-9 6,500; Oris 8.6 7,000; Mosin-Nagant 7,500. SMG prices are unconfirmed; Axion, Oden, Cronen Squall, pistols and launchers have none listed.
- Slot fees 1,000, 5,000, 10,000 (from a written player report), 25,000 and 50,000.
- Han 86 example: 60 Round Drum 15,000 and FSS IS300 suppressor 15,000 plus the first two slot fees, 36,000 in all before the gun.
- A Rare Mosin-Nagant was a Vendor offer at 32,750.
- Official Dynamic Ops footage shows a Legendary Type 73 called Dreadnought, with a 100-round box, looted at the Tank Depot.
- Removing a broken attachment from an extracted enemy weapon costs 1,000 each (from a written walkthrough); whether a second copy of a gun is charged for its slots again is disputed in player reports.
