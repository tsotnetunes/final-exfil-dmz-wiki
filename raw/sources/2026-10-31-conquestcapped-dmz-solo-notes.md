# ConquestCapped: MW4 DMZ solo guide

A community guide from a site that sells boosting; only the solo-matchmaking point is credited to the developers. Facts taken, in our words:
- Squad Fill is on by default; switch it off on the Ready Up screen before you deploy (L2 or LT on controller, 1 on keyboard).
- Matches hold 60 players; matchmaking tries to put solos with solos but cannot promise it.
- You can self-revive once a match for free with the tourniquet from Last Stand and come back Wounded; a Self-Revive Kit gives another revive.
- Traits suited to solos named by the site: Possum, Free Revive, Die Hard, Thermal Padding, Overwatch.
- Dynamic Operations cash goes to your Bank even if you die afterwards; a solo player can cancel an Op from the Tac-Map.
- Exfil: watch the site from cover first; a flare can be seen by nearby squads; the helicopter leaves 30 seconds after you board; the final exfil leaves at a fixed time.
- Story Missions take about 10 to 15 minutes; only your squad has your mission active.
- Avoid Critical and Extreme zones early; Low and Medium areas are enough to level and earn; switch on UAV Towers before missions; killing too many AI can bring a Hunt Squad.
