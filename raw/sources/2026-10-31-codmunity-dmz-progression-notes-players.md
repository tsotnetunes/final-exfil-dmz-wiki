# codmunity.gg: MW4 DMZ progression, level 1 to 70
URL: https://codmunity.gg/dmz/progression
Captured: 2026-10-31 (updated 19 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

A community database of the DMZ level rewards. Facts taken, in our words:
- Level 15 gives one more Operator slot (listed as a station upgrade).
- Station unlocks listed: 3D Printer at level 1 (the official guide says level 2), Vendor 5, Gunsmith 10, Dog Tag Case 20.
- Level 37: a 3-Plate Armor Vest (Epic).
- Level 55: a Disposable Parachute recipe; level 69: a Legendary Parachute.
- Level 70: 18 more item Stash slots, a 100% higher bounty payout and an Advanced SERE Kit (Legendary).
- No XP-per-level figures; DMZ XP also counts toward player levels 1 to 55, and Multiplayer and Warzone XP do not count toward DMZ.
