Facts taken, in our words:
- The printer opens at level 2. The Tempered Armor Vest recipe is in a locked room on the upper floor of the offices that The Doctor's key opens; once extracted, it appears at the printer.
- Electronic Sensors: in tech bags in commercial buildings; also police stations, fire stations and downtown buildings; the office floors of the Tech Tower in Operation Critical Extraction are a farming option. Printable from 20 Synthetics and 20 Electronics.
- High-Tenacity Fibers: scarce; mostly at the Air Base; also weapon lockers (good odds) and sometimes CPU racks in small military camps marked with tent icons; and a weapon locker on the roof of the tall building at Songdo Wharf, reached by the elevator and a back stairway. Used by the Medium Weapon Backpack (3), Large Scavenger Backpack (5), Durable Gas Mask (1) and Ballistic Fabric (3).
- Scrapping: junk from containers turns into the four Resources automatically as you pick it up; you cannot choose to keep it whole; the gain shows in a notification on the right of the screen; resources gathered in a match are lost if you fail to exfil. The Resource Pockets Trait raises the yield.
- Structural Alloy: in the underground bunker during the Operation Iron Citadel Story Mission, in armory cabinets, weapon cases, crates and side rooms, including a fenced area in the room with tanks.
