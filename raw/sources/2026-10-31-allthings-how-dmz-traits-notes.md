# All Things How: MW4 DMZ, all Traits and how to get them
URL: https://allthings.how/mw4-dmz-all-traits-and-how-to-get-them/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- There is no respec: Traits cannot be refunded once a point is spent.
- Ways to start a build again: use a new Operator (starts with no Traits), Prestige the Operator (resets Traits and adds a random permanent Prestige Trait), or dismiss an MIA Operator instead of paying for the rescue.
- Operator Prestige needs an Active Duty Operator at Onyx III with all 18 Trait Points spent and not MIA; it consumes the required gear, so empty the loadout and backpack of anything you want to keep first.
- Operator Prestige does not touch the 1 to 55 player level or the 1 to 70 DMZ level.
