# Gaming ProMax: All DMZ boss locations in COD Modern Warfare 4

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 23 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (crafting-related lines only):
- Colossus roams; often seen around Cheongun Village, Songdo Wharf, Chang-san Air Base, the Citadel at the 14th Political Prison and the NK Wilderness. It drops Military Composites and Experimental Compounds, both Legendary 3D Printer ingredients.
- Deathstalker roams the sky, often around NuriGO Mall, DBN Headquarters and the east of the map. It drops a Prototype RF Module (Legendary ingredient) and a Drone Motherboard.
- The North Korean battle tank roams the roads with no fixed spawn and drops Nano-Structured Alloy (Legendary ingredient).
- The first kill of each Commander is worth 50,000 DMZ Cash on the Boss Board; each Lieutenant 10,000 with Dog Tag extraction.
- The Tempered Armor Vest recipe is in the Lieutenant crate inside The Doctor's Command Office at Mirae General Hospital.
- A companion recipe list on the same site (26 Oct 2026, https://gamingpromax.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-all-3d-printer-recipes/) repeats Destructoid's recipe costs, gives Military Composites a sell value of 3,500 DMZ Cash, and says recipe cards turn up in enemy Operator backpacks, first aid kits, filing cabinets, blue duffel bags, footlockers and medicine cabinets in barracks and military outposts.
