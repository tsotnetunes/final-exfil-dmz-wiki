# DETONATED: DMZ silently removes near-PvE mode in Modern Warfare 4
URL: https://detonated.com/dmz-silently-removes-near-pve-mode-in-modern-warfare-4/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 29 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Before the 28 Oct update, queuing for a Story Mission put you in a sparse lobby with other Story Mission squads, so players could loot with little threat.
- After the 28 Oct update, Story Mission queues land in ordinary Free Roam or Dynamic Ops lobbies with more players, and UAV towers get switched on early.
- The change was not in the patch notes; Infinity Ward and Activision had not commented when the article was written.
