Facts taken, in our words:
- Human Intel 1/2: Hunter only asks you to find the Lieutenant from the tower terminal, not to kill them; Triage Protocol is a clinic visit, a Bandage and 30 Chemicals (a Mirae General Hospital visit did not tick the clinic task in their run); No One Left is clearing a compound (Compound Delta took 12 kills solo) and picking up the Medical Extraction Intel in the yard. The tower used was a tall grey lattice tower near Compound Delta, not one of the five on the official map. Page reward, paid once for the whole page: Flash recipe, 20,000, Basic Triage Kit. The Clear the Military Compound side op paid 500 at 100 percent; a stalled bar at 90 percent means a defender is still alive. You cannot shoot while on a tower ladder. Only one Order shows in the top-right HUD at a time.
- Underground 1/2 (page 5 for this writer): Eye in the Sky (a SAM site on South Island, a tent camp with a launcher vehicle), Watch for Bumps (any stolen enemy vehicle, a gas station repair and refuel, seven run-overs), Triangulation (two lattice radio towers on South Island with yellow ladders, then the roof terminal of La Grande Vie in Songdo Wharf). Extraction was not needed: the page completed and paid without an exfil. Page reward: DDOS, Decoy Grenade and Night Vision Goggles recipes and 20,000.
- Money guide: each Story Mission card the writer ran showed 25,000 plus an item; each of four finished Orders pages paid 20,000; Clear the Military Compound paid 500.
