# Xbox Wire: Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, How to Get Started as an Operator in DMZ
URL: https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/20/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-how-to-get-started-operator-dmz/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 20 Oct 2026, Mike Nelson)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Two Active Duty slots at the start and a third through DMZ Player Levels; an Operator keeps their slot until you dismiss them for someone else.
- Operators improve as they survive deployments and gain DMZ XP. Their Dog Tag improves as they progress and complete exfils; every Dog Tag level banks one Trait Point. Elsewhere the article shortens this to one point per successful exfil.
- At most 18 Trait Points per Operator and 66 Traits in all; picking a Trait locks the other choices on that branch; the more Traits an Operator has, the more recovery costs.
- An eliminated Operator is MIA and cannot deploy until recovered or dismissed. Recovery happens at the Active Duty station and is paid in DMZ Cash from the Bank; Traits stay, gear is gone. A Free Loadout is offered when you have no gear.
- Operator Prestige needs 18 Trait Points, the DMZ Cash and the required rare gear equipped. It is per Operator and apart from the 1 to 55 level. Trait Points reset and the Operator receives a random permanent Prestige Trait. The Backpack is emptied, so take valuables out first. Later Prestiges ask for better gear and more cash.
- The Operator's Loadout profile records any Bounty on them.
- No mention of 2XP or Double XP.
