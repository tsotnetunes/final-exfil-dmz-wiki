# Gfinity Esports: Call of Duty: MW4 DMZ: How to Find & Use Weapon Manuals

Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 27 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- There are 28 Weapon Manuals in Hajin.
- Extracting a Manual lets you buy that weapon in the FOB Gunsmith and customise it there.
- Military Containers, often in police stations and compounds, are the most common source; safes also hold them; so do locked high-tier rooms, usually behind a keycard, and a Door Breacher Charge opens rooms that need no keycard.
- Put a Manual in a backpack Secure Slot so it reaches the FOB; if you die with it in your inventory you lose it.
- Extracting uses the Manual up automatically.
