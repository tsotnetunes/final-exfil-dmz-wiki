# Skycoach: Modern Warfare 4 DMZ Guide: FOB, Missions and Progression Explained

Facts taken, in our words:
- The guide says Double XP tokens also apply to DMZ Player XP; it does not say which track that means or whether Operator XP is affected.
- Vehicle and air infils need the matching Story Mission unlocked first; an air infil needs a Parachute.
- Infil prices: on foot free, Polaris Dagor A1 5,000, LTV 25,000, helicopter 50,000, cargo plane 100,000.
- Bank capacity and MIA recovery discounts are 3D Printer upgrades.
- A Blueprint gives one free use in DMZ; if the weapon is lost it can be bought back at a discount.
- On death you lose the loadout and backpack except what is in Secure Slots; the Operator goes MIA; DMZ XP still counts; Operator levels are not reset; the FOB Stash is safe.
- A Bounty stays across deployments until cleared and can be paid off at Bounty Stations.
