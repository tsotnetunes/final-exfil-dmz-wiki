# Call of Duty blog: Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone Season 1, all you need to know
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 28 Oct 2026)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- Season 1 for MW4 and Warzone goes live on all platforms on 6 Nov 2026 at 00:00 ET (21:00 PT on 5 Nov).
- X01 is a new DMZ location: a weapons facility nobody had found before, probably a former GRANIT Arms black site, recently taken over by enemy forces.
- It is a former Russian facility at the edge of Hajin's borders, close to North Korea's northern border. Players reach it by deploying into Hajin and moving to an infiltration point.
- Inside: a dim maze of passages, tunnels and linked rooms around a hidden weapons assembly line, fully closed off from outside, with several floors to climb. The summary names cargo bays, vehicle depots, underground labs and production lines.
- A sealed Deep Vault can be infiltrated; nothing more is said about it.
- Extraction points close as the facility progresses, and players must finish required steps to get out; the post advises always knowing where the nearest exfil is.
- Enemies: troops and specialists of the 100 Spirits NK faction, rival Operator squads, and stronger patrolling entities; new enemy types are kept secret. X01 holds equipment not found elsewhere in DMZ.
- New Lieutenants: Tae-Hyun ("Knuckle") and Chol-Min ("The Wrench"). New Commanders: The Captain (a Juggernaut with an elite guard, met in X01) and Wheelson (an autonomous patrolling unit).
- Events: Threat Briefing: Battle Tank (in Hajin; collect one part of an NK main battle tank per deployment; squad credit when one member exfils with a part); Threat Briefing: The Captain (in X01; collect sections of his helmet and exfil); Resupply Run (find set items across POIs and trade them for rewards such as Self Revives and 3-Plate Carriers). More Threat Briefings later in the season.
- Warzone: Hajin and Zodiac join Warzone as new maps in Season 1; full Warzone intel was due on 2 Nov.
- The post lists no new Hajin POIs and no changes to weather, radiation or exfil.
