A community guide written after launch from the site's own play and the official guides. Read through a web-fetch tool.

Facts taken, in our words:
- With no backpack you carry the Basic Pack, 8 slots; anything not in a Secure Slot is lost on a failed exfil. The Medium Backpack has one Secure Slot.
- The Free Loadout gives one weapon, one Lethal, one Tactical, a melee weapon and one plate; no backpack and no parachute.
- Plates do not come back on their own; you insert them yourself. The Ballistic Gel vests are the exception and refill their plates.
- At infil your equipped plates, ammunition, Lethals and Tacticals are topped up; your backpack is not.
- The Plate Carriers tab shows eight vests, with the Tempered Armor Vest at the top.
- Health is 150; the tourniquet takes 50 off your maximum, down to 100, and leaves you Wounded.
- A Stim brings max health back for a while, then the wound returns.
- Clinics marked with a cross nearly always hold a Medkit.
- Rising with a Self-Revive Kit leaves no wound; the Revive Pistol and the Defibrillator also revive without a wound.
- Energy Drink: printed from 10 Chemicals and 10 Metals; unlimited stamina for a while.
- Radiation Gear and parachutes are found in Fire Stations; Radiation Blockers, printed at the 3D Printer, help in irradiated ground; the red zone at the Haneul reactor wears gas masks out fast, so bring spares.
- A parachute in your backpack is not equipped; check the Parachute slot before you jump.
- NVGs are needed in the dark underground part of NuriGO Mall.
- Gold Bar: Ultra; its loot card shows 25,000.
- Junk is broken down into Synthetics, Electronics, Chemicals and Metals automatically as you loot.
- Look twice at items marked with a cash or diamond icon before selling: some, such as Regional Combat Notes and some orange and purple documents, are needed for FOB upgrades.
- Keep keys, and red (Ultra) items, in a Secure Slot.
- Hidden caches such as a military duffel can hold a Field Upgrade or a throwing knife.
- Ammo and Armor caches refill every 60 seconds.
- Selling beyond the Bank's cap gives no warning, and the extra cash is lost.
