Facts taken, in our words:
- 18 Trait Points, enough to max two trees. No respec: Traits stay locked until you Prestige, which clears the regular Traits. Every Trait added makes an MIA Operator dearer to recover.
- Row partners: Hostile Looter with Container Looter; Thermal Padding with Overwatch; UAV Upgrade with Field Crafting; Viper Sense with Quick Fix; Equipment Professional with Combat Resistance.
- Equipment Professional refills up to the slot's capacity. Killing Spree extends active effects up to twice their length. Plate Speed and Combat Scavenger are random Minors that appear only if they roll.
- Its picks: if you take one Trait, take Possum, which it calls the best in DMZ; for solo play Possum and Die Hard matter most, because the revive Traits (Faster Revive, Numb the Pain) need teammates; Viper Sense over Quick Fix; Hostile Looter over Container Looter; Combat Scavenger stacks with Hostile Looter.
