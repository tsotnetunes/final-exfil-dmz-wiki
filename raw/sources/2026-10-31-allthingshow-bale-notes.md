# AllThingsHow: MW4 DMZ: How to Beat Bale (Location and Drops)
URL: https://allthings.how/?p=298177
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published updated 21 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Places: upper floor of the Re-Education Center (Medium), up the interior stairs; or inside the Chang-san Air Base Bunker (Extreme).
- Boss Board card: reward $10,000, intel $25,000, marked Purchased after buying. Side op: Paid Intel: Reach Bale, then Paid Intel: Kill Bale; without intel it reads Kill Bale.
- He fires an LMG down long hallways; guards fill the rooms and doorways on the upper floor.
- Tactic: clear room by room, plate up in side offices, stay out of the long corridor, split the squad across doorways; he fell on a balcony by the door at the end of the corridor.
- Drops: Bale's Dog Tag (Legendary, bounty 25K), Dreadnought (Legendary LMG on the Type 73, $8,000, 100-round magazine, five attachments) or a Type 73 (Rare, $2,000). The tag counts for the Boss Board only after you extract with it.
- An assist on the kill paid 575 XP.
