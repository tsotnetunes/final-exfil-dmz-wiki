# Activision: Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 and Call of Duty: Warzone Season 1, All You Need to Know
URL: https://callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 28 Oct 2026)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- Season 1 goes live at 00:00 ET on 6 Nov 2026 (21:00 PT on 5 Nov).
- X01 is a weapons facility near the northern border that enemy forces took over, reached through Hajin: tunnels, several floors, extraction points that close as you go deeper, and a sealed Deep Vault. New enemies and items there are kept classified.
- The enemy is called the 100 Spirits NK Faction. Its troops and specialists may be around X01; the post advises against fighting them head-on, and says tougher entities patrol inside.
- Ghost acts as the Watcher and hands out the new DMZ Orders for X01.
- Boss Board additions: Lieutenants Tae-Hyun "Knuckle" and Chol-Min "The Wrench" (described only as dangerous foes for later deployments); Commanders The Captain (a Juggernaut who fights with his elite guard) and Wheelson (an autonomous unit on patrol). No places or arrival days are given, except The Captain's event in X01.
- Threat Briefing events in the first part of the season: Battle Tank (Hajin): remove one part from a North Korean main battle tank per deployment; more parts over several deployments earn a bigger reward; the whole squad gets credit when one member exfils with a part. The Captain (X01): collect pieces of his helmet; bigger rewards after a successful exfil.
- Resupply Run event: gather set items around POIs and trade them for gear such as Self Revives and 3-Plate Carriers. More events and Threat Briefings follow later in the season.
- FOB: a Battle Pass and Store station beside the Survival Kit station; the Vendor sells gear for DMZ Cash to Season 1 BlackCell owners; 3D Printer changes are classified.
