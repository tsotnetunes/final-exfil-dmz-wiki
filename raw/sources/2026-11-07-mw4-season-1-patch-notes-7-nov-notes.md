# Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 patch notes: the 7 Nov 2026 section
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes
Captured: 2026-11-09 (from the app's patch reader, which re-read the page at 01:30 UTC on 8 Nov 2026; the official accounts announced the update the same hour)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- A further update rolled out on all platforms on 7 Nov 2026 (US evening); its lines sit under a "November 7, 2026" heading on the same Season 1 page.
- X01: player kills there had raised a player's Bounty Level more than intended; fixed. Several stability fixes for X01. The "Power On" interaction was moved so its marker no longer sits on top of the door objective. The Vault Defend interaction now only becomes available once the Vault Door is open.
- Infil: players who paid for a Light Helo Infil could be shot down by the SAM Sites at the Chang-san Military Base soon after spawning; fixed.
- Bosses: The Captain and Colossus could finish off players in Last Stand who could still have been revived; fixed. Wheelson Commander turrets kept turning after a DDoS or Shock Stick had disabled them; fixed, plus missing turret movement sounds for the Wheelson in X01.
- Missions: destroying a boat before the second-to-last objective of the Operation Fallen Ember Story Mission could block progress; fixed.
- UI: the DMZ Orders menu could hang on a waiting message once every Order was done; fixed. The Argonaut Killstreak could show the wrong Operator portrait in the squad widget; fixed.
- Progression: the BlackCell Camo Challenge for five kills without reloading, ten times, now tracks; players who finished the Season 1 Boss Board Challenge before Season 1 now get its trophy in the FOB.
