# Modern Warfare 4 launch patch notes: the 2 Nov 2026 section

Facts taken, in our words:
- Season 1 Patch Notes are promised later in the week; Season 1 goes live at 9 PM PT on Thursday 5 Nov.
- All modes: dive speed lost to a temporary exploit fix (23%) is back, and going from prone to standing without the animation is closed; small visual bugs remain for now. The JOKR's Infrared mode is switched off for now because of an exploit, until a full fix.
- DMZ UAV Towers: 40% fewer, placed at random, one per point of interest is no longer promised, and two no longer appear in one area. Deadtown keeps its tower because a DMZ Order needs it.
- Sedative Inhaler lasts 120 seconds, was 60. It still does not hide you from towers switched on by rival Operators who have UAV Upgrade; a fix is planned for a Season 1 update.
- Truce Flag cooldown is 60 seconds, was 180.
- DMZ fixes: tower status shown wrongly after a recapture or with the Extended Duration Trait; retired Active Duty Operators not saving; SAM Sites that could not be captured after another squad left or failed; Bounty progress resetting between deployments; several exploits closed.
- Recently updated section: a fix to the Apex the notes call the KG-9 Drill Charge.
