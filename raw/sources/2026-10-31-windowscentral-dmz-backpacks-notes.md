# Windows Central: How to get better backpacks in Modern Warfare 4 DMZ
URL: https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/call-of-duty/the-most-essential-gear-for-looting-in-modern-warfare-4-dmz-isnt-your-weapon-its-having-the-right-backpack
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 22 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- Small Backpack: the easiest to print, holds little and has no Secure Slot; the writer only uses it for ingredient runs.
- Medium Backpack: Rare, the cheapest printable backpack with a Secure Slot; recipe from DMZ level 2.
- Large Backpack: tied to DMZ level 34; the writer bought one at the FOB Vendor for 10,000 DMZ Cash.
- Medium Weapon Backpack: tied to level 61; weapon backpacks hold an extra weapon.
- Scavenger backpacks suit looting runs such as ingredient farming; Secure backpacks suit going after high-tier items.
- Ways to get backpacks: find them in Hajin, take them from players you down, buy them at the Vendor, open Survival Kits.
- Weapons cannot go into Secure Slots; recipes, Weapon Manuals and other rare items can. If you find a recipe, carry at least a Medium Backpack so it can get out.
