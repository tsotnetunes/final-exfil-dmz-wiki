# ConquestCapped: MW4 DMZ bosses
URL: https://conquestcapped.com/guides/call-of-duty/mw4-dmz-bosses/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (updated 28 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (the page relies partly on player reports from launch week):
- Gorani: forest of the South Korean Wilderness around a house between Cheonwang Peak Array and Mirae General Hospital; decoys and ghillied snipers; loot room on top of the Rice Mill (D8), reached by climbing.
- The Chemist: inside the radiation at Haneul Nuclear Reactor with armoured hazmat soldiers; loot room The Chemist's Secure Armory (I4), a secured bunker near the boat house.
- Jin-Tae: upper floors of the Heavenly Luck Casino with bodyguards; drops a Private Lounge key; lounge on the casino's top floor (F4).
- The Doctor: Mirae General Hospital (roof, first floor or underground parking) with armoured guards; Command Office on the hospital's top floor (F5).
- Bale: upstairs in Deadtown's Re-Education Center or in the Chang-san Air Base underground bunker.
- Iskra: a building in the Cheonwang Peak Array compound with sensor traps; control room on the server room's top floor (F6).
- Scavenger: no home area; drives an LTV around Hajin.
- Roze: roof of the DBN tower in The Grid (also seen near Wolves Stadium); Highrise Armory (G6).
- Jang-Ui: server room on the upper floors of the Nenshin Tech Complex, with robot dogs, mines, sentry guns and laser turrets; loot room in a generator room on NuriGO Mall's bottom floor (H6).
- The Warden: The Citadel, with many AI; riot shield and shotgun.
- Commanders: Colossus often in The Grid, around Wolves Stadium and near SAM sites; the Deathstalker flies over the whole map, most often over downtown Hajin City; the Tank patrols roads, most often in The Grid.
- Boss Board intel for a Lieutenant costs 25,000 DMZ Cash.
