# GamerFuzion: How to Never Lose Your Operator, plus solo tips

Facts taken, in our words:
- Operator Rescue Beacon: by its tooltip it brings the Operator back at no cost when you fail to exfil, so no paid MIA recovery. Turned on with the number beside EDIT OPERATOR on the deploy screen; each use spends one.
- Weapon Insurance Contract: by its tooltip, weapons you hold when you die go back to the Stash. Turned on with the number beside EDIT LOADOUT on the deploy screen; each use spends one.
- The host's findings: the contract is used up even after a good exfil and has to be switched on for every deployment.
- More come from DMZ Orders pages (seen on pages 3 and 6), Survival Kits (an Advanced SERE Kit gave a beacon) and the Vault Edition Deployment Kit (two of each).
