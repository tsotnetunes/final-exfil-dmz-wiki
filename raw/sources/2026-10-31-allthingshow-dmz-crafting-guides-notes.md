Facts taken, in our words:
- Upgrade effects and costs: Bank Upgrade I raises the Bank cap to 250,000 for 20,000 Cash; Bank Upgrade II to 500,000 for 50,000 Cash; Stash Item Upgrade I adds 18 item slots; Stash Weapon Upgrade I adds 6 weapon slots; MIA Recovery Discount I cuts recovery cost by 5 percent and costs no Cash; Dog Tag Case Upgrade I adds 20 tag slots. The two Ingredient Printing upgrades open higher-rarity materials. Costs match Destructoid's list.
- A red tool box on the damaged outside stair landing at DBN Headquarters held two Rigid Synthetics on one run. The printer's Ingredient Locations button shows where each ingredient tends to be found.
- Recipe cards found on the writer's runs: Pain Killers in an enemy Operator's backpack at the gas station exfil in Cheongun Outskirts; Large Scavenger Backpack in a first aid kit at the fire station in the North Korean Wilderness (C6); 2-Plate Ballistic Gel Vest in a grey filing cabinet in the Heavenly Luck Casino (F4); Zip Gun in a blue duffel bag and Combat Drone in a footlocker in the Chang-san Air Base barracks; Pain Killers, Energy Drink and Drill Charge in the barracks washroom medicine cabinets. The Train also gave Rigid Synthetics and Detonators, and the barracks gave Nylon Cloth. Container loot changes between deployments.
- Electronic Sensors (Uncommon, sells for 200): an IT bag on an office desk in the building just west of the D50 Radar in Cheongun Village (F4), after clearing the guards; the same bag gave a CPU (Rare, 500). Used by the Spotter Scope and UAV.
- High-Tenacity Fibers (Rare, sells for 500): a blue duffel bag in the back corner of a barber shop in Hajin City at F8, south of Wolves Stadium. Used by the Durable Gas Mask and Large Scavenger Backpack.
- The Chemist's cache at the Haneul Nuclear Reactor holds 3D Printer ingredients such as a Comms Module (Epic, valued at 1,500) and Control Units.
- The 3D Printer opens at DMZ level 2.
- Orders chapters: the recipe rewards of the early chapters include Flash, DDOS, Decoy Grenade, Night Vision Goggles and Recon Drone (not tied to single Orders). The first Order of Human Intel 2/2 (Blindness) asks you to print four Flash Grenades and then kill eight flashed enemies.
