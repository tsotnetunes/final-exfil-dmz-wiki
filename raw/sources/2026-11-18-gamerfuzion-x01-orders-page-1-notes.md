# GamerFuzion: X01 Orders page 1 solo guide (9 Nov 2026)

Captured: 2026-11-18 (published 9 Nov 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words:
- X01 shows as its own card in the deploy location menu and spawns you inside; the elevator shaft in north west Hajin was not needed. The menu rates X01 Extreme and lists it as Free Roam only; Hajin is rated Medium.
- Spawns seen: the Main Warehouse door and the Admin Offices on the west side. The Main Warehouse upper floor and storage room hold kit bags, duffels and boxes, and a Commander was met there.
- The Logistics Tunnel joins the Main Warehouse to the Control Room and the Motor Pool. Containers in a side office with a Control Room sign did not count for the Order; the real Control Room is further on, beside the Power Generators.
- The exfil lift sits just south of the Motor Pool. A wall button calls it; it took about 40 seconds to arrive and then counted down 25 seconds, around 1 minute 15 seconds in all, and enemies rushed the room meanwhile.
- The gas timer started at about 20 minutes. Page 1 paid 20,000 DMZ Cash, a Medium Scavenger Backpack (Epic, two secure slots, sells for 2,500) and two Encodable Blank Keycards; rewards land in the stash overflow row when the stash is full.
