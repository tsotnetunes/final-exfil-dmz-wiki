# GamerFuzion: MW4 DMZ Underground 1/2 Orders, SAM site, radio towers and La Grande Vie
URL: https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-underground-1-orders-sam-site-radio-towers-la-grande-vie-triangulation/
Captured: 2026-10-31 (published 23 Oct 2026)
Trust: third-party

Facts taken, in our words (two live solo runs):
- A SAM site on South Island, below River Heights in the southeast: a tent camp with bunks, a watchtower and a SAM launcher truck on the road. Capturing it runs a timer while you hold the area; the captured SAM can then shoot down a cargo helicopter whose crate you loot.
- Two grey lattice radio towers on South Island with yellow ladders; at the top you place a transmitter on a platform. After the second one a Lieutenant hunt Side Op appeared.
- La Grande Vie, the tall building in Songdo Wharf just north of South Island: climb the stairwell to the roof (tents, sandbags, a terminal under a camo tarp) to finish the Triangulation Order.
- Refuel and repair: park at a pump and hold the button; used the Rin Oil station by the Russian Border. The writer says the in-game map lists 18 gas stations.
- Radiation clock starts at 30:00.
