Paraphrased notes on the page, not its text. The page is one living document for the launch, newest day first;
Final Exfil reads it every 30 minutes and keeps every line with its day at finalexfil.com/patches.

28 Oct 2026, DMZ
- Possum (a Warfighter Trait that can revive an eliminated player): can no longer be activated while the squad
  that killed you is nearby, or before 4 minutes have passed; the developers say the distance and the wait can be
  tuned later.
- Fixed: Possum reviving players who had already abandoned their Operator, leaving them unable to play.
- Fixed: a black screen after the whole squad was eliminated while a teammate had Possum equipped.
- Fixed: using the 3D Printer to add items to the Backpack during deployment could send players in without their
  Free Loadout weapons or equipment.
- Fixed: weapons dropping when moved from the primary slot to the Backpack, and weapons disappearing after repeated
  swaps with a Backpack that stores weapons.
- Fixed: the 3-Plate Armor Vest reward for reaching DMZ Level 37 not being granted.
- Fixed: the Airbase Access Card could not be upgraded during a Story Mission when kept in the Secure Slot.
- Fixed: auto-marked Order items losing their ping markers after a loot container was pinged.
- Several exploits closed (not named).
- Weapon tuning the same day is listed under Multiplayer only.

29 Oct 2026, DMZ
- DMZ Cash rewards raised slightly for Dynamic Ops, Side Ops and Story Mission replays (no amounts given).
- Fixed: enemy helicopters crashing into the exfil helicopter and eliminating players during extraction.
- Fixed: extracting with a disconnected teammate's Dog Tag and storing it in the Dog Tag Case.
- Fixed: some squadmate actions not counting toward DMZ Mastery Camo challenge progress.
- Fixed: the Scavenger detecting players immediately on deployment; hostages not able to be picked up after
  assimilating; missing interaction prompts at Lieutenant Towers during Side Operations; Encrypted Voice Logs not
  spawning consistently for the Ears Everywhere Order; terrain where players got stuck on Hajin; vehicles
  overlapping near the map edge.
- Recently updated: players who passed DMZ Level 37 before the vest fix still lacked the 3-Plate Armor Vest; the
  developers said they were working on granting it.
