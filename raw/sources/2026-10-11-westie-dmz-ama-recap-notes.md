## From the 2022 beta to MW4
- The developers name the FOB, the permanent stash and DMZ's own progression as the biggest changes from the 2022 beta. MIA: progress stays with a killed Operator, who can be rescued.

## Parachutes
- After a year of testing with unlimited parachutes, exploring felt too safe; base parachutes now have limited uses and are easier to find, for example in Fire Stations. Infinite-use parachutes also exist. Both can be printed at the 3D Printer. (The video's guess, not the developers': the infinite one needs rarer materials. Rumours said "single use"; the video says limited use.)

## Anti-cheat
- Ricochet is active with MW4's improvements: server culling, TPM 2.0, Secure Boot and stronger account security.

## Players and map
- At launch Hajin supports up to 60 players per match: 20 squads of three (also on X). Proximity chat returns.
- No night mode or day-night cycle at launch; weather includes sun, cloud, rainstorms, fog and mist, possibly snow; NVGs are in the loot pool for darker areas.

## Matchmaking
- No separate party finder: you choose Story Missions, Dynamic Operations or Free Roam when you queue, and the matchmaker groups players with the same choice.
- Developers' answer as read out in the video: matches aim to put solos with solos, duos with duos and trios with trios, but there is no guarantee; players can still truce, betray or assimilate, and an assimilated squad is capped at four players; the tourniquet and the stealth meter were added with solos in mind (also on X).
- If you crash or disconnect, you can rejoin while that match is still running (also on X).
- In a party, the FOB looks like the party leader's progress; each station's menu shows your own progress and items (also on X).
- No skin toggle: DMZ lives in MW4, not Warzone, so older games' Operators do not appear.

## Health and healing
- Every Operator deploys able to use a tourniquet, a self-revive from Last Stand that brings you to 100 health with a wound. Maximum health is 150.
- The tourniquet cannot be used again until you are back at full health; downed again while wounded, you need a squadmate.
- A bandage lifts a wounded Operator to 150 for a short time, then health falls back to 100; a medkit repairs the wound and keeps you at 150.
- Self-revive kits can be looted in Hajin and printed at the 3D Printer.

## Controller and looting
- Beside mouse and keyboard drag and drop, the inventory has controller quick actions: equip, drop and stow with one button; item details never cover the inventory; the backpack sits on the right and much of the looting UI is see-through so you can watch your surroundings.

## Movement, damage, stamina
- Tac sprint is on by default in DMZ, unlike Multiplayer (also on X: in Multiplayer it comes with a perk).
- Weapon damage has DMZ's own tuning, made with its armor and plate carriers in mind. (A creator's quote of the same answer on X says the tuning is close to Warzone's.)
- No armor-piercing or Stopping Power rounds as field upgrades for now (also on X).
- Energy drinks, stim shots and smelling salts refill or extend stamina.

## Camos and post-launch
- DMZ has its own camos; the DMZ camo grind starts with DMZ early access (also on X; one community account names three Mastery Camos: Chiral, Ripple Tide and Helio, unverified).
- A successor to the beta's Serpentine camo hunt is planned for post-launch seasons.
- X01, a map arriving soon after launch, fits the lineup the way Building 21 did in the beta.
- A Russian landmass, already part of Hajin but closed at launch, opens in Season 2 with its own story, missions and Dynamic Ops; then Hajin becomes the largest Call of Duty map. More maps are planned.
- Support statement (summarised): DMZ has been in the works for years; big post-launch plans; early seasons are being tested; the team plans to support and evolve the mode and will follow player feedback, but makes no sweeping commitments yet.
