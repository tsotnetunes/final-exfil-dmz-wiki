# Modern Warfare 4 launch patch notes: the 30 Oct 2026 section
URL: https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-launch-patch-notes
Captured: 2026-10-31 (read in a browser at about 18:50 UTC; the app's patch reader holds the same lines from its read of the night before)
Trust: official

Facts taken, in our words:
- A smaller update before the weekend; Infected joins the playlist for Halloween weekend.
- DMZ cash changed to help the economy: a finished Dynamic Op pays 10,000 to 20,000 (was 5,000 to 10,000), a Side Op 2,000 (was 500), and each completed mission step 500 (was 300).
- Broken Resupply: the mission item could be missing from the marked vehicle's trunk (fixed).
- Boss Board intel sometimes could not be bought, which blocked the Information Power Order (fixed).
- Hunt Operator Dynamic Op: missing mission timer and objective text on the in-world marker (fixed).
- A hit that does not break a helmet showed a white icon instead of the blue armour icon (fixed).
- FOB: shadows shifting as players moved, and a crash from spamming knife attacks (fixed).
- Global: server crashes during play; Platinum camo progress for Marksman Rifles on PC (fixed).
