Facts taken, in our words:
- Six silos in the western half: north of the 14th Political Prison; northeast of the prison toward the main roads; west of Tong-il Village; west of Chang-san Air Base outside its fence; south of the air base below the runway; southwest of Deadtown.
- They show as circles on the Tac-Map; the concrete hatch nearby is always open.
- Inside, a metal door is locked from the inside; take the first left off the main corridor and use the open back door of the same room.
- Most silos hold one AI. Loot lies on the floor and in a weapon locker, usually common, with a chance of rare weapons, recipes and manuals.
- The Silo Control Room at the back needs an Ultra Door Breacher Charge and holds a stationary missile with nothing to interact with and little loot.
