Re-read on 31 Oct 2026 through a web-fetch tool to settle questions the earlier extract did not cover. Facts in our words:
- The Dog Tag: the guide ties an Operator's Dog Tag level to the DMZ XP that a successful exfil awards. It does not say whether XP from a failed exfil moves the Dog Tag; the line about failed exfils keeping XP refers to the DMZ Player Level.
- Double XP or 2XP tokens are not mentioned anywhere on the page.
- The third Active Duty slot: unlocked by raising your DMZ level; the page gives no level number. Vault Edition owners start with three slots.
- Fulton Drop Markers, Operator Rescue Beacons and Weapon Insurance Contracts appear only as contents of the Vault Edition Deployment Kit (two of each); the page does not say what they do.
- Nothing on retiring Operators, wipes or seasonal resets. Dismissing an MIA Operator and recruiting a new one is described; Operator Prestige is separate from the player level tracks.
- The Bank has a cap that Bank Upgrades raise; no figures are given.
